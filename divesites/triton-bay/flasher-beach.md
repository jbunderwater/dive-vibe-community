## Flasher Beach

Flasher Beach is a dive site on an island outside Triton Bay proper, named for the flasher wrasses (Paracheilinus) that display there in the late afternoon.

## Overview

Diveplanit places Flasher Beach on an island outside Triton Bay, about 30 minutes away by boat. Divernet says it lies outside the Iris Strait and describes hard coral growth in the shallows giving way to rubble with depth, which is the habitat flasher wrasses favor. Diveplanit and The Seven Seas both say males display in the late afternoon. The Seven Seas itinerary also mentions the site under the name "Secret Beach", which no second source confirms. Earlier versions of this page said the site lies northwest of Aiduma and cited a 2008 species description and Misool distribution; none of that could be re-confirmed in the sources fetched on 2026-10-30, so it has been removed.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Island outside Triton Bay, West Papua, Indonesia (about 30 minutes by boat per Diveplanit)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 15 meters
- **Current**: The Seven Seas describes it as gentle. No second source.
- **Best Time**: Late afternoon for the display. Season: October to April/May per regional sources.

## Marine Life

Reported at Flasher Beach itself:

- Paracheilinus nursalim (Nursalim flasher wrasse), yellowfin and blue flashers, sailfin anthias, and flasher hybrids (Divernet)
- Paracheilinus cyaneus, P. flavianalis, P. nursalim and hybrids between them (Reef Builders)

No other species have been confirmed for this site by the sources checked.

## Dive Profile

Plan the dive for late afternoon, when sources report the male display. Descend over the shallow hard coral and rubble and stay still and low; sudden movement is likely to interrupt the display (general behavior of the genus, not a site-specific source).

## Safety Considerations

The site is reported as shallow and gentle, but current has only one source. Confirm conditions with your guide.

## Photography

The display is the subject. The Seven Seas and Diveplanit both identify late afternoon as the time for it.
