## Broken Rock

Broken Rock is one of the best-known dive sites in South Ari Atoll, a thila inside Dhigurah Kandu — the channel between the islands of Dhigurah and Dhangethi — named for a canyon that splits the reef formation in two.

## Overview

The reef's top sits at around 12 metres, with dives working down to roughly 30 metres on the surrounding slopes. The signature feature is the canyon or "break" that divides the pinnacle into two halves, which divers can swim through, along with large fan corals projecting from the walls. Because the site sits inside a channel, current can be strong and variable — divers should stay clear of the fragile coral formations that line the passage when drifting through. Resident Napoleon wrasse, turtles, and scorpionfish are regularly reported, alongside numerous moray eel species and abundant soft coral growth.

## Site Information

- **Location**: South Ari Atoll, Maldives (Dhigurah Kandu, between Dhigurah and Dhangethi)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Depth**: Reef top ~12 m; slopes to ~30 m
- **Difficulty**: Advanced (channel currents)
- **Site Type**: Pinnacle/seamount

## Marine Life

Napoleon wrasse (resident), turtles, scorpionfish, multiple moray eel species, triggerfish, puffer fish, and dense soft coral growth. No independent source confirms sharks or rays specifically at this site.

| Condition | Details |

## Tips for Divers

Watch buoyancy carefully through the central canyon — the passage is narrow enough that poor trim can damage the coral on either side. Current direction and strength should be checked with the boat crew before the dive, since this is a channel-adjacent site. The large fan corals on the walls are best photographed on the down-current face.
