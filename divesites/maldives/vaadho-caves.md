## Vaadho Caves

Vaadho Caves (also documented as Vaadhoo Caves) is a group of underwater caverns and overhangs on the northwest outer reef of Vaadhoo island in South Male Atoll (not North Male), at depths between roughly 7 and 30 metres.

### Site Conditions

- **Depth range**: 7–30 m (primary cave entrances around 7–25 m; a larger overhang near 30 m)
- **Typical visibility**: 15–25 m
- **Currents**: Usually light — the site is calm enough to be popular for snorkelling as well as diving
- **Best season**: November–April

### What to Expect

The site sits along the reef wall of Vaadhoo Kandu and comprises a series of small caves, overhangs, and a primary cavity with entrance depths from around 7 to 25 metres. A larger overhang sits near 30 metres, and a narrow passage swim-through runs between roughly 16 and 24 metres. This is rated as a recreational-level site (PADI Intermediate) rather than a technical cave dive, thanks to the generally light current and the presence of ambient light through much of the system.

### Tips for Divers

Because currents are usually light, this is a comparatively relaxed cave/cavern dive by Maldivian standards, but divers should still maintain single-file passage through the narrower sections and monitor air carefully in any section where light disappears.
