## Grotta della Pastizza

Grotta della Pastizza is a semi-submerged cave and wall dive a few minutes by boat from Ustica's harbour.

## Overview

Mister Jump describes a semi-submerged cave just past the Grotta Azzurra, named for an emerged rock the islanders call 'Pastizza'. The outbound route follows a colourful vertical wall, and the return passes through interconnected corridors with two chambers above sea level; a statue of San Bartolomeo, the island's patron saint, stands in one of them. Orca Diving Ustica adds a zone of large stones and Posidonia around the rock and the four-cave Academia complex (maximum 12 m). Reported depths: 15-22 m and never deeper than 20 m (Mister Jump), wall to about 25 m (Divernet). Operators describe it as easy and open to all certification levels and as one of the best night dives on the island (Orca Diving Ustica, Mister Jump, Scubaportal, Enjoy Sicilia). Cave penetration still requires appropriate training.

Species reported at this site: pipefish, spider crab, brown grouper, amberjack, conger and moray eels, shrimp, octopus, cuttlefish, sea hares, nudibranchs and the night anemone Alicia mirabilis (Orca Diving Ustica); Mister Jump and Divernet corroborate cuttlefish, Alicia mirabilis, eels, octopus, sea hares and nudibranchs.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Ustica, Europe (near the harbour)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Cave/cavern
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 20 meters
