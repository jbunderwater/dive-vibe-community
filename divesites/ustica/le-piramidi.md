## Le Piramidi

Le Piramidi is a deep pinnacle-and-canyon dive on Ustica's western coast between Punta Spalmatore and Punta Cavazzi.

## Overview

Orca Diving Ustica describes two submerged mountains cut by narrow canyons, in Zone B of the reserve between Punta Spalmatore and Punta Cavazzi, with a depth range of 0-46 m, and says it is only for divers with Deep Diver certification. Scubaportal lists the dive as Spalmatore - Piramidi at about 15-45 m with connected caves and tunnels, for advanced-certified divers. Mister Jump lists it as Piramidi / Grotta dei Cirri, off the Spalmatore tower, over a seabed of boulders with flat tops. Per Orca's profile: seagrass at 8-10 m, a plateau of large boulders, a steep canyon whose walls carry yellow cluster anemone and orange coral, an opening at about 35 m with a ridge and wall, and a large cave whose sandy floor has fan mussels and fanworms; a smaller second cave lies nearby. Scuba Travel separately describes a Grotta dei Cirri entered by a chimney from 28 to 40 m, but it does not say whether this is the same feature. Cave penetration requires appropriate training and equipment.

Species reported at this site: groupers, Mediterranean red sea star, blue spiny starfish, yellow cluster anemone, fan mussels and fanworms (Orca Diving Ustica); pencil sea urchin, mantis shrimp, encrusting sponges and large groupers (Scubaportal). Earlier mentions of unicorn shrimps, meagre and brown hake were not supported by any source and have been removed.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Ustica, Europe (west coast)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Pinnacle/seamount
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 45 meters
