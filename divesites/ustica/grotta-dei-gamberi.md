## Grotta dei Gamberi

Grotta dei Gamberi (Shrimp Cave) is a deep cave dive at Punta Galera on Ustica's south coast.

## Overview

The cave is reached from the Punta Galera mooring, and its entrance lies on a sandy seabed at about 40 m (Orca; Scuba Travel 41 m). Orca describes an entrance just under 2 m high and 6 m wide, a circular chamber with a vault almost 10 m high and more than 30 m across, then a narrower corridor to an exit at about 24 m. Reported exit depths range from 18 m (Scuba Travel) to 24 m (Orca), 26-28 m (Mister Jump, Profondo Blu Diving) and 27 m (Scubaportal). Sources also disagree on route length: about 100 m (Scubaportal), about 130 m (Scuba Travel), about 320 m (Orca). Mister Jump notes possible current during descent and ascent and requires full cave certification; Orca and Scubaportal rate it among the most difficult dives on the island. Cave penetration requires proper training and equipment.

Species reported inside this cave: swarms of Plesionika narval (red/unicorn) shrimp on the walls, the nudibranchs Facelina coronata and Peltodoris atromaculata, fan mussel Pinna nobilis, conger eels, lobsters and melon urchins (Mister Jump, Scuba Travel, Orca Diving Ustica, Scubaportal). Groupers, wrasses and sea breams are reported on the boulder slope at the exit (Mister Jump).

## Site Information

- **Location**: Ustica, Europe (Punta Galera)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Cave/cavern
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters
