## Ustica

Volcanic Mediterranean island north of Palermo, home to Italy's first marine reserve (established 1986), with volcanic rock formations, caves, archaeological itineraries and a shoal with a shipwreck.

## Description

Ustica is the emerged part of a volcanic edifice, and its dive sites include caves, walls, pinnacles, tunnels and canyons. Operators list roughly 12-15 sites around the island (Orca Diving Ustica lists 15, Mister Jump 12), with maximum depths in the sources ranging from about 20 to about 46 metres. Sites are mostly dived from boats leaving the island's harbour; Orca Diving Ustica gives about 10 minutes by boat for Sutta a Za Lisa.

### Diving Opportunities

- **Boat Diving**: Most sites are reached by boat from the island's harbour
- **Cave Diving**: Grotta dei Gamberi (Shrimp Cave, entrance about 40 m, requires cave/advanced certification) and Grotta della Pastizza (easy cave and night dive)
- **Wall and Rockfall Diving**: Punta dell'Arpa (red gorgonians), Punta Omo Morto, Sutta za Lisa
- **Pinnacle Diving**: Secca della Colombara (shoal with the wreck of the motor ship ITA, grounded 21 February 2005) and Le Piramidi (deep canyon and cave dive)
- **Archaeological Diving**: Marked underwater archaeological itineraries exist at Punta Gavazzi (10-24 m) and near the Torre di Spalmatore, plus Punta Falconiera (not in this database). No source supports an itinerary at Punta Omo Morto, which earlier text of this overview claimed

### Practical Notes

- **Getting There**: Ferries and hydrofoils run from Palermo; schedules vary by season and are weather-dependent, so check with the operators (for example Liberty Lines and Siremar) before travel
- **Dive Operators**: Operators listed on the dive-site pages consulted include Orca Diving Ustica, Mister Jump, Lustrica Diving, Profondo Blu Diving and Evasioni Blu Diving
- **Marine Protected Area**: Ustica's marine reserve is divided into zones; Zone A is an integral reserve where bathing and snorkelling are permitted only in specific coves (Cala Sidoti and Cala Acquario per Spiagge.it), and fishing and collecting are prohibited. Check zone rules with a licensed operator before diving

### Marine Life
