## Grotta Azzurra

Grotta Azzurra (Blue Grotto) is a sea cave near Ustica's harbour. It is listed here as a cave site, but dive-specific details could not be verified.

## Overview

Re-audit on 2026-11-13 could not find a current dive-operator, forum or database source describing a dive at the Grotta Azzurra: the operator listings consulted (Orca Diving Ustica, Mister Jump, Enjoy Sicilia, Scubaportal, Scuba Travel) do not list it as a dive site. Mister Jump and Orca place the neighbouring Grotta della Pastizza close to it, and Mister Jump says Pastizza lies just past the Grotta Azzurra. Sebastiano Tusa's article for the Centro Studi Ustica names the Grotta Azzurra and the adjacent San Francesco Vecchio as locations of Copper Age traces (terracotta fragments). Earlier text on this page about the cave's dimensions, its connection to another cave and its suitability for all certification levels could not be confirmed and has been removed.
