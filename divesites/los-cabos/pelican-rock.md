## Pelican Rock

The most frequently dived site in Cabo San Lucas, located approximately 300 feet offshore on the bay side of Land's End. A sloping reef wall drops from about 6 meters and meets the canyon edge where the Sand Falls occur; most operators cite a working maximum around 27 meters, though some sources describe divers following the slope down toward 30–40 meters where it merges with the adjacent canyon. The sheltered bay position keeps conditions calm even when the Pacific side is rough. Sea fans cover the slopes, and the site is known for macro life at shallower depths alongside larger species patrolling the deeper reef.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Cabo San Lucas Marine Park, inside the bay, approximately 5 minutes from the marina
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 27 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 10–25 meters
- **Current**: Minimal — protected within the bay
- **Best Time**: Year-round; September to November for best visibility

## Marine Life

Green moray eels, guineafowl pufferfish, Cortez angelfish, and scorpionfish are resident. Whitetip reef sharks rest under ledges, particularly reported in summer months. Spotted eagle rays and mobula rays pass through. The shallow coral areas shelter juvenile fish — grunts, wrasses, and damselfish — alongside snapper and jack schools and occasional Moorish idols. Octopus inhabit the rocky crevices. Nudibranchs and small crustaceans are found on the rocky substrate.

## Dive Profile

Enter from the boat on the bay side of the rock. Explore the shallow reef and sea fan slopes first (6–15 m), then descend along the wall toward the canyon edge for more advanced terrain. The Sand Falls cascade over the canyon rim at 27 meters and are visible from the edge. The site links naturally with the Sand Falls dive for experienced divers. For beginners, stay on the reef slope above 20 meters.

## Safety Considerations

The bay provides excellent shelter, making this one of the calmest sites in the marine park. Watch depth near the canyon edge — the bottom drops sharply beyond 27 meters. Boat traffic within the bay requires an SMB on ascent.
