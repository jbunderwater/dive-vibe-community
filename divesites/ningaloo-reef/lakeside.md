## Lakeside

Lakeside Sanctuary is a calm, shore-access snorkel and shallow dive site within Cape Range National Park, located near the Milyering Visitor Centre about 52 kilometres from Exmouth.

## Overview

Lakeside sits inside the Ningaloo Marine Park sanctuary zone and is consistently described as one of the more beginner-friendly sites along the western cape. The site features a series of large coral bommies set in clear water along a roughly 700-metre stretch of reef, reached via a short, well-marked walk along the beach from the small lake/inlet behind the dunes. Depths range from about 1 to 5 metres, making it primarily a snorkel-focused site, though it is listed by some dive guides for introductory scuba.

Confirmed marine life reported at Lakeside includes sea turtles, rays, reef sharks, octopus, and a large resident grouper around the coral bommies, along with species specifically documented at the site: banded humbug, harlequin filefish, blackspot snapper (schooling), and Australian anemone (with anemonefish). A resident dugong has also been reported in the area. The site is notably less crowded than nearby Turquoise Bay and Oyster Stacks.

Access involves a 10–15 minute walk along the beach from the Milyering Visitor Centre car park to the four large yellow sanctuary zone markers. Snorkelling is recommended within the marked zone. Conditions are generally calm year-round, though visibility drops on windy days.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Cape Range National Park, Ningaloo Marine Park, Western Australia (~52km from Exmouth, near Milyering Visitor Centre)
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 5 meters
- **Access**: Short, well-marked beach walk to sanctuary markers (approx. 700m snorkel stretch); no 4WD required
