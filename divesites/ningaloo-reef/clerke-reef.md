## Clerke Reef

**Data integrity note: Clerke Reef is part of the Rowley Shoals, located approximately 260–300 kilometres west of Broome in the Indian Ocean (sources vary on the exact distance). It is not part of Ningaloo Reef and is misplaced in this destination dataset. The site is described here for completeness, but should be moved to a dedicated Rowley Shoals destination.**

Clerke Reef is one of three coral atolls that form the Rowley Shoals Marine Park, rising from 390 metres depth in the Indian Ocean and offering some of Australia's most spectacular wall diving.

## Overview

Clerke Reef forms the southernmost atoll in the Rowley Shoals chain, approximately 23 kilometres south of Mermaid Reef. The atoll is accessible exclusively by liveaboard from Broome, with the crossing taking approximately 24 hours. The Western Australian state permit system limits visitor numbers, preserving exceptionally pristine conditions.

The reef features a combination of sheltered lagoon sites and dramatic outer wall dives. The lagoon harbours calm, beginner-friendly dives — including The Aquarium (12–30m diving, 2–10m snorkelling) and Blue Lagoon, where divers meet resident whitetip reef sharks — while outer sites like Jimmy Goes to China involve a wall, covered in gorgonian fans and black corals, whose top sits at roughly 10–20 metres before dropping further; sources do not agree on an exact maximum depth for this site, so no figure below the confirmed 10–20m top is stated here. Grey reef sharks, silvertip sharks, and thick schools of five-lined snapper have been reported cruising the wall. The Rollercoaster (also called The Channel) is an expert-only tidal drift pushing divers through the Clerke Reef channel, requiring advanced buoyancy in strong flow.

Documented marine life across Rowley Shoals includes 233 coral species and 688 fish species per WA Department of Fisheries figures. Grey reef sharks, Maori wrasse, giant potato cod, and moray eels are commonly encountered. Visibility regularly reaches 50 metres on outer wall sections, with the outside walls dropping from a shelf into water approximately 400 metres deep.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Rowley Shoals Marine Park, Indian Ocean (~260km west of Broome, Western Australia) — NOT Ningaloo Reef
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters
- **Access**: Liveaboard from Broome; limited permit system applies
