## Junkpile-1

Junkpile-1 is a scattered field of general US-origin debris -- tires, bottles, batteries -- on the Kwajalein lagoon floor.

## Overview

Kwajalein Atoll's lagoon -- the largest lagoon by area of any atoll in the world -- is dotted with hundreds of named coral heads and debris sites logged by the local diving community over decades. Water stays a warm 28-30C year-round and lagoon visibility commonly reaches 20-30 meters, though it varies site to site.

## What to Expect

Junkpile-1 is a scattered debris field discovered by local divers exploring out from a nearby coral head. Items reported on the bottom include tires, batteries, bottles, plates, and glasses -- general refuse rather than identified WWII wreckage. Depth to the debris runs approximately 30-34 meters (100-110 feet).

## Practical Information

- **Depth**: approximately 32 meters
- **Visibility**: typically 20-30 meters in the lagoon (varies by site and season)
- **Water Temperature**: 28-30C year-round
- **Entry**: Boat dive
- **Currents**: Generally mild in the inner lagoon; can strengthen near passes
