## Junkpile-2

Junkpile-2 is a large debris field of US-origin junk, including at least two ship's propellers, close enough to Junkpile-3 that the two fields may merge.

## Overview

Kwajalein Atoll's lagoon -- the largest lagoon by area of any atoll in the world -- is dotted with hundreds of named coral heads and debris sites logged by the local diving community over decades. Water stays a warm 28-30C year-round and lagoon visibility commonly reaches 20-30 meters, though it varies site to site.

## What to Expect

Junkpile-2 is a large debris field reported to be entirely US-origin junk -- tires, 55-gallon drums, bottles, and at least two ship's propellers have been noted -- believed to have been dumped in the lagoon some years ago rather than sunk in combat. Depth runs approximately 30-34 meters (100-110 feet). Junkpile-2 and Junkpile-3 lie close enough together that they may in fact be part of the same debris field.

## Practical Information

- **Depth**: approximately 33 meters
- **Visibility**: typically 20-30 meters in the lagoon (varies by site and season)
- **Water Temperature**: 28-30C year-round
- **Entry**: Boat dive
- **Currents**: Generally mild in the inner lagoon; can strengthen near passes
