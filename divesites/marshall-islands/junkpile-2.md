## Junkpile-2

Junkpile-2 is a US military-surplus debris field on the Kwajalein lagoon floor — not a named vessel — the deepest and among the largest of the three Junkpile sites.

## Overview

Per Dave Fortin's Kwajalein dive log: "another large area with all sorts of junk! It appears to be all US junk that I suspect was just dumped years ago! Maybe tires, 55 gal drums, bottles, propellers (I've seen two), etc, etc." Depth is given as "about 100-110 feet" (~30-34m), with no good nearby anchor point, requiring boats to anchor deep with plenty of scope. The source notes this site sits close to Junkpile-3 and "these junk fields may in fact be all part of the same junkpile."

## Site Information

- **Location**: Kwajalein Atoll's inner lagoon, Republic of the Marshall Islands
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 33 meters

## Marine Life

No marine life reports exist for this site from the source consulted; the described content is entirely dumped hardware, not a colonized reef structure.
