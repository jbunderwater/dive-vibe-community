## Victor Buoy Coral Head

Victor Buoy Coral Head sits just inside Bigej Pass, where tidal currents can run fairly strong during large tide swings.

## Overview

Per Dave Fortin's Kwajalein dive log: "this coral head is just inside Bigej Pass. There is a buoy (maybe a pole?) right next to it. This is always a nice dive. Being near the pass I have felt some fairly strong currents at times, so be alert if you are out there during a big tide swing."

## Site Information

- **Location**: Kwajalein Atoll's inner lagoon, Republic of the Marshall Islands
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 25 meters
