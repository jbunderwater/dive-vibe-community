## K-9 Deep Coral Head

K-9 Deep Coral Head is a smaller, slightly deeper companion to the larger K-9 Coral Head, near a small-arms and artillery debris field and the "G-Buoy Wreck."

## Overview

Per Dave Fortin's Kwajalein dive log: "this coral head is not far from the larger K-9 coral head. It is smaller and a little deeper (about 20-25 feet) to the top which makes it slightly harder to see. There is some small arms ammo on the top of the coral head and numerous artillery shells can easily be found in the area around this coral head." The source notes it is a good anchor point for accessing the nearby "G-Buoy Wreck," about three minutes away.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Kwajalein Atoll's inner lagoon, Republic of the Marshall Islands
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 25 meters

## Marine Life

No marine life reports exist for this site from the source consulted.

## Safety Considerations

Small-arms ammunition and artillery shells have been reported on and around this coral head. Do not touch or disturb any munitions; treat all such objects in the lagoon as live.
