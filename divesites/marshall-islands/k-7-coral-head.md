## K-7 Coral Head

K-7 Coral Head is a fair-sized coral head close to the surface, with a couple of resident anemones.

## Overview

Per Dave Fortin's Kwajalein dive log: "I have dove this a couple times. It is a fair sized coral head and, if I remember correctly, there are a couple nice anemones to see. Nice area. Hard to miss the coral head as it is fair sized and pretty close to the surface."

## Site Information

- **Location**: Kwajalein Atoll's inner lagoon, Republic of the Marshall Islands
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 25 meters

## Marine Life

A couple of anemones are documented at this site. No fish species are named by the source.
