## S-Buoy Coral Head

S-Buoy Coral Head is a pole-marked coral head roughly straight out from North Loi, best dived in calmer weather per the source.

## Overview

Per Dave Fortin's Kwajalein dive log: "this is one coral head that I always enjoy. There is a pole marking it. It is roughly straight out from North Loi. I prefer this one in calmer weather."

## Site Information

- **Location**: Kwajalein Atoll's inner lagoon, Republic of the Marshall Islands
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 25 meters
