# Cathedral Beqa

Beqa Lagoon Resort's signature hand-fed shark dive, where divers hold position at a fixed reef ledge as bull sharks and, with high frequency, tiger sharks move through at close range.

## Overview

The Cathedral is Beqa Lagoon Resort's own shark dive site, developed by the resort as its answer to Pacific Harbour's Shark Reef Marine Reserve on the other side of the lagoon. It is a fixed-location dive to roughly 21 meters (70 feet) where divers are positioned on ledges around a reef feature while operator staff hand-feed sharks in a controlled setting. Up to nine shark species have been recorded here, and the site is particularly known for tiger sharks — Beqa Lagoon Resort staff have reported as many as 13 tiger sharks on a single dive, and operators describe sightings as highly likely rather than occasional. Bull sharks, whitetip reef sharks, and other species round out the roster. The dive runs on a fixed weekly schedule (Monday, Wednesday, Friday) and is restricted to more experienced divers given the proximity to large sharks during active feeding.

**Source conflict note:** Tourism Fiji's fiji.travel-affiliated site (fijitravel.org) describes a site by this same name as a natural swim-through cavern with sunlight beams and resident batfish, rather than a shark-feeding site. That description could not be corroborated by the operator's own materials or by other independent sources (Diveplanit, Scuba Diving magazine, JettyDive, Aquaventure Dive & Photo Center), which consistently and specifically describe "The Cathedral" as Beqa Lagoon Resort's hand-fed shark dive. This description follows the more heavily corroborated shark-dive characterization; the cavern/batfish version could not be confirmed as accurate for this specific site and has been removed rather than presented as fact.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Beqa Lagoon Resort's shark dive site, Beqa Island, Fiji
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef (fixed shark-viewing site)
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 20 meters (~70 feet)
- **Typical Visibility**: 15–25 meters (50–80 feet)
- **Current**: Light to moderate
- **Best Time**: Year-round; dive runs Monday, Wednesday, Friday

## Marine Life

Bull sharks and tiger sharks are the headline species, with tiger sharks reported at high frequency — the operator has documented as many as 13 individuals on a single dive. Whitetip reef sharks are also regularly present. Up to nine shark species total have been recorded at the site by operator accounts, though the full list beyond bull, tiger, and whitetip reef sharks is not broken out in available sources. Large groupers and bait balls of trevally are reported near the site. No site-specific reports of the batfish/cavern fauna claimed in the earlier version of this description were found; that claim has been removed.

## Dive Profile

Divers descend to a fixed reef ledge/viewing position at approximately 15–20 meters. Operator staff conduct a controlled hand-feed while divers hold position at designated viewing points, observing sharks pass at close range. The dive concludes with an ascent along the reef and a safety stop in shallower water.

## Entry and Exit

Operated exclusively by Beqa Lagoon Resort from Beqa Island; day-trip access is available from Pacific Harbour operators as well. Entry is by giant stride or back roll from the dive boat. A pre-dive briefing on shark-dive protocol (positioning, no loose gear, no reaching toward sharks) is mandatory.

## Tips and Recommendations

- **Follow the briefing exactly**: As with any hand-fed shark dive, strict adherence to positioning and behavior protocol is what keeps the dive safe.
- **Bring a wide-angle lens**: Sharks pass at close range; a wide-angle or fisheye setup captures full-body shots without needing to close distance.
- **Ask about the weekly schedule**: The dive runs a fixed three-day-a-week schedule — plan your Beqa Lagoon Resort stay around it if this is a priority dive.
- **Combine with a second lagoon dive**: Operators typically pair this with a reef or wall dive elsewhere in Beqa Lagoon.

## Safety Considerations

This is a hand-fed shark dive with large, sometimes numerous tiger and bull sharks in close proximity — it carries inherent risk and requires strict discipline. Stay at the designated viewing position, keep gear secure and close to the body, and never reach toward or attempt to touch a shark. Advanced certification and prior shark-dive or deep-diving experience are recommended before attempting this dive.

## Photography

Wide-angle or fisheye lenses (10–17mm) are best suited to the close-range shark encounters. Position at the designated viewing ledge and shoot as sharks pass through the feeding area; avoid extending strobe arms or camera rigs beyond the briefed positioning, as loose equipment near feeding sharks is a genuine hazard.
