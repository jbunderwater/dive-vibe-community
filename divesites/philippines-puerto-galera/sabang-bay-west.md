# Sabang Bay West

A sandy-bottomed bay dive with coral outcrops and a range of macro subjects, suitable for beginners; this site remains sourced primarily from one detailed original description.

## Overview

Sabang Bay West is a straightforward bay dive over a sandy bottom dotted with coral outcrops, on the western side of Sabang Bay. According to the original source description, two dumped aluminium boats on the seabed add artificial reef interest to the natural formations, and barrel sponges grow among the coral outcrops. A generically-matching PADI "Sabang Bay" site page (by coordinates) corroborates the reef/sandy-bottom setting, calm conditions, and macro-friendly visibility, but this audit could not find a third independent source that specifically confirms the aluminium boats or names this exact sub-location as distinct from the broader Sabang Bay/Sabang Wrecks cluster. Per the destination's data-quality standard requiring 3+ independent sources for full validation, this site is flagged as needing further sourcing rather than treated as fully cross-confirmed; the description below is worded to reflect that.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Sabang Bay (western side), Puerto Galera, Mindoro Island, Philippines
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 22 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: ~10 meters
- **Current**: Generally mild
- **Water Temperature**: ~28°C

## Marine Life

Barrel sponges are a prominent feature among the coral outcrops. Nudibranchs are found across the site, and moray eels peer out from holes in the coral formations, per the original source. Mantis shrimps occupy burrows in the sandy substrate, and anemonefish defend their host anemones on the outcrops. The two dumped aluminium boats reported by the original source are described as attracting their own resident marine life, though this detail has not been independently corroborated elsewhere.

## Dive Profile

Descend to the sandy bottom and navigate between coral outcrops, checking each formation for moray eels, nudibranchs, and anemonefish. Barrel sponges are found throughout. The maximum depth is approximately 22 meters, and the calm bay conditions allow for a relaxed, unhurried dive. Work gradually shallower for the safety stop.

## Tips and Recommendations

- A good site for newer divers -- calm conditions, moderate depth, and easy navigation
- Scan the sand near coral outcrops for mantis shrimp
- Macro lens recommended; the visibility (~10 meters) favours close-up work
- The sheltered bay means this site is diveable in most conditions
