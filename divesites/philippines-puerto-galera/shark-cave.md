# Shark Cave

East of Lighthouse Headland, a pair of overhangs at 27-30 meters that sources disagree on: some describe resident whitetip reef sharks as a current, reliable sighting; others describe the cave as having "used to" house them.

## Overview

Shark Cave lies east of Puerto Galera's Lighthouse Headland and consists of a gradual slope of ridges and rocky, sandy bottom leading to two overhangs at approximately 27-30 meters. Sources genuinely disagree on whether sharks are still a reliable sighting here: PADI's dive site directory and AsiaDiveSite describe whitetip reef sharks as regularly resting in the overhangs today, with divers able to approach closely with a light. Casalay's site guide, by contrast, describes the cave as having "used to house" white tip reef sharks, implying the sharks are now largely a historical association rather than a guaranteed sighting. An earlier version of this page stated flatly that "sharks are not typically seen here" -- that claim is not well supported and has been removed in favor of noting the actual disagreement between sources. The nearby mesa-like formation referred to locally as "The Atoll" is a separate, distinct site from Shark Cave itself.

## Site Information

- **Location**: East of Lighthouse Headland, Puerto Galera, Mindoro Island, Philippines
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 28 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 10-30 meters (varies by source)
- **Current**: Can be strong; sources describe currents here as treacherous at times
- **Water Temperature**: 28-29°C

## Marine Life

Large fan corals are a consistent feature around the overhangs. Grouper, batfish, sweetlips, snapper, octopus, nudibranchs, and blue-spotted stingrays are reported here across multiple sources, along with moray eels and lionfish. Whether whitetip reef sharks are a current or historical feature of the site is disputed between sources -- see Overview.

## Dive Profile

This site is typically visited at the start of a multilevel dive toward Pink Wall or as a waypoint on the way to The Canyons. Descend to the ledge around 25 meters, which tapers back into the cave/overhang feature at 27-30 meters. Approach the overhangs carefully and observe from the sandy bottom rather than crowding the entrance, particularly if sharks are present. Explore the surrounding reef and fan corals for the rest of the dive, watching for grouper, sweetlips, and stingrays.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Sources disagree on current shark presence here -- ask your guide about recent sightings rather than assuming either way
- If sharks are present, observe from a distance on the sandy bottom rather than crowding the overhang entrance
- Currents here can be strong -- this is an Advanced-rated site
- Check the large fan corals for octopus, moray eels, and nudibranchs
