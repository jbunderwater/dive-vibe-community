# Ernies Cave

Named after Ernie, a resident grouper who once lived in a rocky cavern here, this Advanced-rated site has two overhangs -- one around 21 meters, another near 27 meters -- and strong cross-currents.

## Overview

Ernies Cave, also referred to as Ernie's Point, takes its name from a grouper called Ernie who used to reside in a small cave at the site. Ernie has long since moved on, but the name stuck. The site actually has two cave-like features: a shallower cavern at roughly 21 meters set in a large rocky outcrop, and a second small cave deeper at around 27 meters. An earlier version of this page described a scuttled steel vessel as the site's main feature; that claim could not be corroborated during research (PADI, Casalay, Capt'n Gregg's, and Action Divers all describe this location as a cave/cavern site with no wreck) and has been removed. Strong cross-currents are a defining characteristic here, which is reflected in the Advanced rating from multiple operators.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Between Monkey Beach and Dungon Wall, Puerto Galera, Mindoro Island, Philippines
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Cave/cavern
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 26 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 15-20 meters
- **Current**: Strong cross-currents
- **Water Temperature**: 28-29°C

## Marine Life

Schools of surgeonfish, fusiliers, trevallies, mackerel, snappers, and unicornfish are commonly reported passing the site. Sea fans decorate the rocky outcrop around the shallower cavern, and the site is a known spot for large mantis shrimp, which build nests in tube-shaped holes near the cave mouths. Smaller groupers are occasionally seen around the caverns.

## Dive Profile

Descend to the shallower cavern at around 21 meters, set within a large rocky outcrop decorated with sea fans, and check the tube-shaped holes nearby for mantis shrimp. Continue to the second, deeper cave feature at approximately 27 meters. Strong cross-currents are common at this site, so plan accordingly and stay close to your guide. Watch the water column for passing schools of fusiliers, trevallies, and mackerel throughout the dive.

## Tips and Recommendations

- This is an Advanced-rated site -- strong cross-currents are the norm here
- Look for mantis shrimp in the tube-shaped holes near the cave mouths
- Watch the blue water for schooling trevallies, mackerel, and unicornfish
- A torch helps when checking the caverns for smaller groupers and sheltering fish
