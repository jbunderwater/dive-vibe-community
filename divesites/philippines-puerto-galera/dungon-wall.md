# Dungon Wall

A steep slope dropping into a sheer wall past 35 meters, with a scuttled sailing catamaran around 28 meters and gorgonians known to host pygmy seahorses.

## Overview

Dungon Wall begins as a steep slope before dropping into a sheer vertical wall that plunges past 35 meters. Around 28 meters, the wall includes the remains of a scuttled sailing catamaran, confirmed independently by more than one operator source, making this a multilevel dive combining wall, wreck-adjacent, and reef elements. Large gorgonian sea fans adorn the wall, known to harbour pygmy seahorses -- one of Puerto Galera's more reliable sites for finding them. Featherstars add colour along the wall's ledges, while lionfish and moray eels occupy holes and crevices in the rock face. Difficulty ratings vary by operator: most describe this as Intermediate, while Casalay rates it Advanced given the sub-30m wall section, so plan your depth and gas conservatively regardless of certification level.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Puerto Galera, Mindoro Island, Philippines
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 35 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 15-25 meters
- **Current**: Variable
- **Water Temperature**: 28-29°C

## Marine Life

Gorgonian sea fans on the wall face are the highlight, with pygmy seahorses reported among the branches. Featherstars perch along the wall's ledges and outcrops. Lionfish and moray eels occupy holes and crevices in the rock face, and nudibranchs are found throughout the site. The scuttled catamaran around 28 meters adds an additional habitat feature along the wall.

## Dive Profile

Descend along the slope to the wall's edge and follow it at depth, scanning gorgonian fans for pygmy seahorses. Around 28 meters, look for the remains of the scuttled sailing catamaran along the wall. The wall drops beyond 35 meters, so careful depth management is essential. Work along the wall face, then gradually ascend to the slope section above for the remainder of the dive, watching for lionfish and moray eels in crevices before your safety stop.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Scan the large gorgonians carefully -- pygmy seahorses blend in remarkably well
- Look for the scuttled catamaran remains around 28 meters
- Monitor depth closely, as the wall drops well beyond recreational limits
- Check crevices in the rock face for lionfish and moray eels
