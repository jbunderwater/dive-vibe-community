# Dungon Wall

A coral slope dropping into a vertical wall to about 30 meters, with a sunken catamaran along the way and large gorgonians known to host pygmy seahorses.

## Overview

Dungon Wall begins as a coral slope before dropping into a vertical wall. Sources are fairly consistent that the wall runs from roughly 10-12 meters down to about 25-30 meters, correcting an earlier version of this description that claimed depths "beyond 35 meters" based on a single source. Two independent sources -- PADI and Action Divers -- corroborate a sunken catamaran along the site, a feature not previously documented here. Large gorgonian sea fans adorn the wall, and Action Divers corroborates the original claim that these host pygmy seahorses. The blue water off the wall edge attracts passing barracuda.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Puerto Galera, Mindoro Island, Philippines
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 15-25 meters
- **Current**: Variable
- **Water Temperature**: 28-29°C

## Marine Life

The gorgonian sea fans growing on the wall face are a highlight, with pygmy seahorses reported among the branches. Chevron barracuda have been seen schooling in the water column off the wall. Moray eels (both white-eyed and clouded varieties) occupy holes and crevices in the rock face, and nudibranchs are found throughout the site. Lionfish, scorpionfish, porcupinefish, frogfish, and ringed pipefish have also been reported.

## Dive Profile

Descend along the slope to the wall's edge and follow it at depth, scanning gorgonian fans for pygmy seahorses and checking the sunken catamaran along the way. Work along the wall face, then gradually ascend to the slope section above for the remainder of the dive, watching the blue water for barracuda.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Scan the large gorgonians carefully -- pygmy seahorses blend in remarkably well
- Watch the blue water off the wall edge for barracuda
- Ask your guide to point out the sunken catamaran along the wall
- Monitor depth closely on the deeper sections of the wall
