# Las Gemelas

A pair of submerged rock formations near Los Arcos and the Playas Gemelas area of southern Banderas Bay — site identity and marine life are not yet independently confirmed beyond regional characteristics

## Overview

Las Gemelas (The Twins) is listed as a pair of closely spaced submerged rock formations near Los Arcos, south of Mismaloya, in the same general stretch of southern Banderas Bay as Playas Gemelas beach and the Princesa Vallarta wreck site. A re-audit of current sources found that "Las Gemelas" is consistently used online as the name of the beach and nearby coastline (Playas Gemelas) rather than as a description of a distinct twin-pinnacle dive site with its own name recognition among current dive operators — no source found during this pass independently confirms a "twin rock formation" dive site under this name, separate from the general Los Arcos diving area.

This entry is kept because the coordinates and depth profile are plausible for the area and no source actively contradicts the existence of such a site, but the description below has been scaled back to avoid asserting site-specific details that could not be verified. Divers and local operators with direct knowledge of this site are encouraged to help confirm or correct it.
