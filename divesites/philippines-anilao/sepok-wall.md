## Sepok Wall

Sepok Wall is a vertical rock wall dive near Anilao, its face roughly 80 percent covered in soft coral, descending from a 5-meter crest to about 27 meters.

## Overview

Sepok Wall is reached by a roughly 16-minute banca ride from Anilao-area resorts. The site is centred on a long vertical wall of solid rock dropping from around 5 meters to approximately 27 meters, with the wall face covered — per anilaodiving.com, about 80 percent — in multi-coloured soft coral. Currents typically run slight to moderate toward the southwest. Frogfish, leaf fish, twin-spot lionfish, and scorpionfish hide among the encrusted surfaces, while coral-covered bommies on the sandy floor offer complementary wide-angle compositions.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Anilao, Batangas, Philippines
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 27 meters

## Marine Life

Sepok Wall's wall face is densely covered in soft corals (approximately 80% coverage per anilaodiving.com), gorgonian fans, and barrel sponges. Specific species confirmed at this site include frogfish (small), leaf fish, twin-spot lionfish, scorpionfish, and anemonefish. Multiple nudibranch species are present throughout. The coral-covered bommies at the sandy base provide wide-angle composition opportunities. Note: "small reef sharks occasionally patrolling the bottom" was not confirmed in available sources for this specific site and has been removed. Pygmy seahorses in sea fans are reported for Anilao wall sites generally but were not site-specifically documented here.
