## Mainit Point

Mainit Point is a current-swept boulder slope off Anilao known for strong, sometimes "spicy" currents that draw giant trevally, whitetip reef sharks, and dog-toothed tuna — reclassified this pass from muck to drift after fresh sourcing showed it to be a pelagic-focused reef dive, not a critter/muck site.

## Overview

"Mainit" means "hot" in Filipino — the name refers to the current, not the water temperature. Dive-The-World describes it directly: "Mainit Point is typical of Anilao, consisting of a current-swept boulder-strewn slope. Mainit means hot, and the current rushing through the Maricaban Strait can get spicy, but it feeds a colourful and coral-heavy reef with myriad nudibranchs crawling all over it." A ScubaBoard thread independently groups Mainit Point with Beatrice Rock and Bahura as one of Anilao's strongest-current sites. **Correction from the previous QA pass**: this site was previously classified as a deep muck/critter dive (rhinopias, bobbit worms, hairy frogfish) despite its own cited sources not actually supporting that description. Fresh research across three independent sources (Dive-The-World, a ScubaBoard trip thread, and Philippine Dives) consistently describes Mainit Point as a current-swept reef/drift site with pelagics and a shallow cave, not a muck site. The muck-themed content has been removed. In calmer conditions the site shifts character — divers can explore a cave at roughly 7–8 meters that sometimes shelters resting whitetip reef sharks, and the reef itself is coral-heavy with abundant nudibranchs.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Anilao, Batangas, Philippines (Maricaban Strait)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Drift dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 5-15 meters
- **Current**: Strong and variable — described by Dive-The-World as able to get "spicy"; Advanced certification or solid drift experience recommended
- **Best Time**: October to May; best attempted early morning per trip reports

## Marine Life

In strong current, expect giant trevally, bluefin trevally, dog-toothed tuna, and great barracuda hunting in the flow — one trip report recorded up to four great barracuda hunting on a single dive. Whitetip reef sharks are reported at the site, particularly resting in the shallow (7–8m) cave during calmer conditions; occasional eagle rays also appear. When current is light, attention shifts to the coral-heavy reef itself, which Dive-The-World describes as having "myriad nudibranchs crawling all over it." Note: rhinopias, bobbit worms, hairy frogfish, and mandarin fish claims from the previous version of this page were not supported by any source found this pass and have been removed — those species are better documented at Anilao's dedicated muck sites (e.g., Secret Bay, Koala Reef), not at Mainit Point.

## Dive Profile

This is primarily a drift dive: descend onto the boulder slope and let the current carry you, watching for trevally, tuna, and barracuda working the flow. Larger boulders can offer shelter from strong current, useful for photographers. In lower-current conditions, divers can instead work the reef and check the 7–8 meter cave for resting whitetips. Follow your guide's briefing closely — current strength and direction determine which version of the dive you get.

## Entry and Exit

Access by banca from Anilao-area resorts. Live-boat drift pickup is standard practice given the current. Entry by giant stride; deploy SMB promptly so the boat can track your position, as strong current can carry divers some distance from the entry point.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Check current conditions with your guide before committing — Mainit Point can range from a relaxed reef dive to a demanding drift depending on tide
- Early morning dives are reported as most productive for pelagic sightings
- If conditions are calm, ask about the 7–8 meter cave for resting whitetip reef sharks
- This is a wide-angle dive when current is running — prioritize a wide-angle or fisheye setup over macro

## Safety Considerations

Strong, variable current is the primary hazard — Advanced certification or solid drift-diving experience is recommended, consistent with this site's Advanced rating. Always carry an SMB and deploy it early. Boat crew should be briefed on live-boat drift pickup before entry. Nearest hyperbaric facility is in Manila — several hours from Anilao.

## Photography

Mainit Point rewards wide-angle photography of trevally, barracuda, and tuna working the current, and a fisheye or ultra-wide lens is the better choice on a strong-current day. On calmer days, the coral-heavy reef and its nudibranchs support closer macro work, and the 7–8 meter cave is worth checking for resting whitetips.
