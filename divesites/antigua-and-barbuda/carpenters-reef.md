# Carpenter's Reef

Also known as Carpenter's Rock or Carpenter's Hole: a boulder site on Antigua's south coast, exposed to the Atlantic and dived as a drift in calm weather.

## Overview

DiveCarib describes Carpenter's Rock as a drift dive over large coral-covered boulders at 6 to 18 metres (20 to 60 feet), entirely exposed to the Atlantic and requiring calm weather. Xtreme Spots lists the site (as Carpenter's Hole) in the Windward Bay region on the south of Antigua with an average depth of about 13.7 metres and a maximum of about 24.4 metres, and describes ridges and valleys with soft corals and sponges. Depths therefore differ between sources; this page uses the larger Xtreme Spots maximum.

DiveCarib describes it as suited to certified divers used to boat diving and confident drift diving. Xtreme Spots reports visibility of 5 to 10 metres and a best season of mid-December to mid-May.

## Marine Life

DiveCarib names Caribbean reef sharks, spotted eagle rays, nurse sharks, green moray eels, Bermuda chub and Atlantic blue tang at this site, and notes invasive lionfish. Xtreme Spots mentions soft corals and sponges.

## Access and Conditions

This is a boat dive; DiveCarib notes it needs calm weather because of exposure to the Atlantic. Dive with a local operator and carry a surface marker for drift conditions.
