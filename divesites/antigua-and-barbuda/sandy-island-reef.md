# Sandy Island Reef

A small island at the entrance to St. John's Harbour, ringed by reefs and rock outcroppings, with named spots including The Ledge, Jewel Box and The Barge.

## Overview

California Diver describes Sandy Island at the entrance to St. John's Harbour as a small island ringed by reefs and rock outcroppings with brightly coloured corals, sea fans, gorgonians and many reef fish. The named dive spots listed are The Ledge, Jewel Box and The Barge. A 2006 ScubaBoard trip report describes a dive on the Sandy Island barge, which the diver estimated at about 50 by 25 feet and described as flat and fairly well preserved; this is a single anecdotal account.

Site-specific depth, difficulty and conditions were not found in the sources reviewed; the figures in this database are approximate.

## Marine Life

California Diver reports stingrays in the sandy patches nearby and garden eels that retreat into their sand tubes when disturbed, along with many reef fish. No other species are attributed to this site.

## Access and Conditions

This is a boat dive from St. John's or west-coast operators. Ask the operator which named spot is planned.
