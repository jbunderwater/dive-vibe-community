# Sunken Rock

Described by DiveCarib as the only known wall dive in Antigua: a large submerged rock on the south coast with a vertical drop and drift conditions.

## Overview

Sunken Rock is on Antigua's south coast. DiveCarib describes a wall dive with a vertical drop-off from about 2 metres (6 feet) to 25 metres (80 feet) or more, and calls it the only known wall dive in Antigua. Lemongrass Antigua describes a deep-water drift dive around a large underwater rock formation with towering vertical walls and deep crevices, to about 100 feet (30 metres), warns of unpredictable currents and rates it intermediate to advanced. Antigua and Barbuda's tourism site lists it as a ledge popular with more advanced divers.

DiveCarib describes it as suited to certified divers who are used to boat diving and confident drift diving.

## Marine Life

Species named at Sunken Rock: Caribbean reef sharks and ocean triggerfish (DiveCarib), with dolphins occasionally seen (DiveCarib); schooling jacks and barracuda (Lemongrass Antigua).

## Access and Conditions

This is a boat dive from south-coast operators. Because of the currents, a drift-capable surface marker and a guide familiar with the site are advisable.
