# Ariadne Shoal

An offshore shoal west of Antigua with named spots including Ming's Bank, The Cave, Grunts and the Zurich Train Station.

## Overview

California Diver places Ariadne Shoal about 7 miles off Antigua's west coast, roughly 50 minutes by boat from St. John's, and says it is rarely deeper than 50 feet (about 15 metres) and known for larger fish than most Antigua sites. A 2015 ScubaBoard post by an Antigua-based instructor gives a distance of about 12 miles west of Antigua and calls the site interesting and lively without giving depths. The distance therefore differs between sources; coordinates in this database reflect the 7-mile figure.

Named spots listed by California Diver are Ming's Bank, The Cave, Grunts and the Zurich Train Station. No source reviewed gives a difficulty rating; the Intermediate rating in this database is approximate.

## Marine Life

California Diver names turtles, nurse sharks, blacktip reef sharks and large schools of grunts and snappers at Ariadne Shoal.

## Access and Conditions

Boat dive only. California Diver gives the trip as about 50 minutes each way from St. John's, so check conditions and logistics with an operator.
