# Great Bird Island

A small cay off Antigua's northeast coast within the North East Marine Management Area, visited by snorkellers and divers and known for the Antiguan racer snake on land.

## Overview

Great Bird Island is in North Sound, about 2.5 to 3 kilometres northeast of Antigua (Wikipedia gives about 2.5 km; Yacht Warriors and International Parks say about three kilometres). It is part of the North East Marine Management Area. Yacht Warriors and International Parks say snorkellers and divers can see colourful fish, turtles and rays and highlight the water clarity. Wikipedia describes the islet as about 20 acres and the only place on Earth where the Antiguan racer can be seen in the wild; the snake lives on land and is not an underwater sight.

The sources reviewed do not give reef depths or dive-site details for Great Bird Island, so the depth range in this database is approximate. Yacht Warriors notes a warden has recently begun charging a fee for use of the island; check local regulations.

## Marine Life

Yacht Warriors and International Parks report turtles and rays often seen near the island, and colourful fish. No further species are attributed to the underwater site here.

## Access and Conditions

The island is reached by boat. Yacht Warriors describes the nearby anchorage as sheltered from swell with sandy bottoms.
