## Marianne Island

South Marianne is a challenging dive off the southern tip of Marianne Island, reached by a 30-minute boat journey from Praslin or La Digue, known for strong currents, granite pinnacles, and large aggregations of pelagic fish.

## Overview

The southern tip of Marianne Island — roughly a 30-minute boat ride from Praslin's Côte d'Or — is considered by Seyvillas to be "perhaps the best diving spot in the Seychelles' Inner Islands, with an overwhelming variety and quantity of fish." The topography features granite rock formations, caves, boulders, and pinnacles descending to around 25 metres. Current is a defining feature of the site: Seyvillas and Divebooker both describe it as occasionally strong, and diver-logged reports on ScubaDiving.place independently confirm current speeds around 2 knots, with average dive depths of 19–23 metres and visibility reported as excellent (around 25m in April). Grey reef sharks, eagle rays, turtles, barracuda, giant moray eels, and jackfish/yellowfin tuna are reported by Seyvillas and Divebooker as regular sightings, and the diversity and quantity of fish life is frequently highlighted by divers who visit the site. Two operators — White Tip Divers and Côte D'Or Octopus — run regular trips here. Seyvillas notes the conditions can be "challenging," with potentially rough seas on the boat ride out. This site is best suited to experienced divers comfortable with current and exposed open-water conditions.

## Site Information

- **Location**: South tip of Marianne Island, Seychelles Inner Islands
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Pinnacle/seamount
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 25 meters
