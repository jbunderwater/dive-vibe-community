## Marianne Island

South Marianne is a challenging dive off the southern tip of Marianne Island, reached by a 30-minute boat journey from Praslin or La Digue, known for strong currents, granite pinnacles, and large aggregations of pelagic fish.

## Overview

The southern tip of Marianne Island — accessible from Praslin or La Digue — is considered one of the finest dive sites in the Seychelles Inner Islands. The topography features granite rock formations, caves, boulders, and pinnacles descending to around 25 metres, with divers able to drift past boulders draped in soft coral and thread a few swim-throughs. Currents running past the island are described consistently across sources as occasionally strong, around 2 knots, which brings in schooling jacks, eagle rays, and grey reef sharks hunting in the current. Barracuda, giant moray eels, hawksbill turtles, guitarfish, whitetip reef sharks, and Napoleon wrasse are also reported here, alongside schooling snappers, octopus, stingrays, lionfish, scorpionfish, fusiliers, and lobsters. Seyvillas describes the conditions as "challenging," with potentially rough seas on the boat ride out. This site is best suited to experienced divers comfortable with current and exposed open-water conditions.

**Correction from prior version**: this page previously listed "yellowfin tuna" among the site's regular species. On re-audit, no source re-checked for South Marianne mentions tuna — Seyvillas' current listing attributes yellowfin tuna to a different, nearby site (Sister Reef), and it has been removed here as a likely misattribution.

- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
