## L'Ilot Island

L'Ilot (The Islet) is a tiny granite island at the northeast end of Beau Vallon Bay, about 500 metres from Mahé, with sheer granite drops forming ravines and arches covered in soft coral — a popular night dive site and training ground for local operators.

## Overview

L'Ilot sits roughly half a kilometre from Mahé's Beau Vallon coast, recognisable by the two coconut palms on its summit. Tribloo labels the site "Advanced – CMAS 2" and describes it as a drop-off and rocky canyon site with an arch formation, with sheer granite drops eroded into massive ravines bordered by large boulders draped in soft coral. Reported depth varies by source: Tribloo cites 30–40 metres at the deepest points of the drop-off, while Equinoxe Diving School and Divingseychelles.com both describe a shallower profile (average around 18m, maximum around 20–29m) for what appears to be the same site — this destination retains the shallower 20m figure pending a source that resolves the difference. The 10-minute boat ride from Beau Vallon makes this one of the most accessible offshore sites on Mahé, with light current and good visibility per Tribloo.

Species confirmed at L'Ilot by Tribloo (independently, in detail) include reef sharks, whale sharks, sea turtles, barracudas, moray eels, stingrays, groupers, scorpionfish, hogfish, and crayfish in the crevices — matching closely with Divingseychelles.com's shorter list (reef sharks, eagle rays, turtles, dolphins) and Equinoxe's note of a whale-shark viewing window in October–December. Seyvillas' Mahé guide also lists this site (as "l'Ilot") with rocky formations and soft coral but does not itself specify a whale-shark season. The site is described as well-suited for night dives when different species emerge, and it is used regularly by local dive schools for training dives due to its generally reliable conditions.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Northeast end of Beau Vallon Bay, ~500 m from Mahé, Seychelles
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
