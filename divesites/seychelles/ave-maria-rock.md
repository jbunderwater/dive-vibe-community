## Ave Maria Rock

Ave Maria Rock is a tiny rocky island in the channel between Praslin and the Sister Islands, ringed by granite boulders forming canyons and crevices that support abundant reef life and calm conditions suitable for beginner divers.

## Overview

Ave Maria sits in the channel between Praslin Island and the Sister Islands, accessible by short boat ride (under 30 minutes). Wannadive records the site at coordinates 4°19.043' S, 55°48.958' E with a maximum depth of 20 metres and average depth of 18 metres, rating it CMAS */Open Water difficulty. Seyvillas independently corroborates the 20m maximum depth and describes "deeper rocks with plentiful fish," rating the site "Moderate" difficulty.

The underwater terrain consists of granite boulders creating canyons, crevices, and overhangs. Both Wannadive and Seyvillas confirm reef sharks, stingrays, batfish, lobsters, octopus, barracuda, and sea turtles at this site. The current at Ave Maria is generally mild, making it a reliable all-conditions site and a popular pick for guided beginner dives from Praslin. Wannadive describes the site as "easy to find" by boat, with good visibility of 10–30 metres. (A previous version of this page also listed fusiliers and snappers schooling here; that detail could not be re-confirmed against either currently available source and has been removed pending a supporting citation.)
