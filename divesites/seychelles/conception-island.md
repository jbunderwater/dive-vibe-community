## Conception Island

Conception Island is a small, uninhabited granite island about 2 km west of Mahé, notable both as a current-swept dive site with two named sub-sites (Conception Rocks and Conception Arena) and as a documented aggregation site for endangered spotted eagle rays.

## Overview

Conception sits roughly 2 km off Mahé's north-western shore. Divernet describes the main dive as "a flat sand and coralline bottom at 25m" with "encrusted granite boulders" scattered about, while a separate site — Conception Rocks, at the island's southern point — features swim-throughs formed by granite boulders and deeply incised gullies; Conception Arena, on the western side, is a submerged granite reef forming a natural arena with a shelving sandy bottom, with reported depths varying between about 25m and 30m depending on source. Equinoxe Diving School confirms the strong-current conditions at Conception Arena and recommends it only for experienced divers.

Conception Island is a formally recognized Important Shark and Ray Area (ISRA) specifically for spotted eagle rays (*Aetobatus ocellatus*, an IUCN-listed Endangered species): researchers recorded 80 separate sightings between 2006–2013 and 2018–2022, including aggregations of up to 30 individuals at a mean depth of about 9 metres. Divernet's first-hand account also reports schools of bigeye jack and great barracuda, whitetip and grey reef sharks, several other ray species, and a notable encounter with bumphead parrotfish. Equinoxe and Divingseychelles.com additionally list batfish, stingrays, octopus, and groupers, along with occasional grey reef sharks and seasonal whale sharks (Divingseychelles.com notes whale sharks as seasonal without giving a specific month range for this site). Species mentioned in an earlier version of this page — scorpionfish, leaf fish, puffer fish, and fusiliers — could not be traced to any source specific to Conception Island and have been removed. The combination of current and exposed position makes this site best suited to experienced divers.

## Site Information

- **Location**: ~2 km west of Mahé, Seychelles Inner Islands
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 20 meters
