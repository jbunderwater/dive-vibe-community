## Biet Point

Also known as South Miniloc, this is El Nido's most-cited dive site — a reef/plateau in the channel between Miniloc and Simizu islands.

## Overview

Biet Point sits in a current-swept channel between Miniloc and Simizu islands. Dive shop listings (puertoparrot.com) describe the immediate site as a reef around 9-25 meters, centered on a lettuce/cabbage coral garden near the surface; broader regional guides (tabanka-divers.com, elnidoparadise.com) describe the surrounding area sloping down to 35-43 meters for divers who want to go deeper. One operator (philippinedives.com) characterizes it as a mixed "reef/wall" site, which likely reflects this shallow-reef-into-deeper-slope profile rather than a true vertical wall. Currents here can run stronger than at most other El Nido sites, particularly around tidal changes.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Bacuit Archipelago, El Nido, Philippines Palawan, Asia
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 25 meters

## Marine Life

Confirmed across multiple dive-shop listings: schools of yellow snappers and tuna, jacks, barracuda, squid, cuttlefish, octopus, nudibranchs, small crabs, hawksbill and green turtles, ribbon eels, ghost pipefish, Napoleon wrasse, grouper, and pufferfish (puertoparrot.com, philippinedives.com, tabanka-divers.com). Earlier drafts of this page mentioned whitetip reef sharks resting on the site; no source found in this research confirms shark sightings specifically at Biet Point, so that claim has been removed.
