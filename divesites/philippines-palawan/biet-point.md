## Biet Point

Biet Point, also known as South Miniloc, is El Nido's most popular dive site, positioned in the channel between Miniloc and Simizu islands.

## Overview

Biet Point sits in a nutrient-rich channel between Miniloc and Simizu islands, where currents feed an expansive cabbage (lettuce) coral garden that tops out around 12 meters. Multiple dive operators describe it as the most-dived site in El Nido, suited to a range of experience levels though currents can occasionally run strong. Reported depths vary by operator, from a shallow 5 meters on the coral garden itself down to 30-43 meters on the surrounding slope, so treat maximum depth as approximate. This is a reef and coral-garden site rather than a sheer wall — earlier data mislabeled it as a wall dive.

## Site Information

- **Location**: El Nido, Palawan, Philippines
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters

## Marine Life

Large schools of yellow snappers and juvenile barracuda are consistently reported over the cabbage coral garden, along with tuna, jacks, and Spanish mackerel in the current. Sea turtles (green and hawksbill), blue-spotted stingrays, moray eels, and frogfish are also reported by multiple operators, with occasional eagle ray and manta ray sightings. Ribbon eels and nudibranchs are noted in the coral itself.
