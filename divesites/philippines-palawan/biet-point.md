## Biet Point

Biet Point, also known as South Miniloc, is one of El Nido's most popular dive sites — a triangular reef plateau between Miniloc and Simizu islands famous for its cabbage-coral garden.

## Overview

Biet Point sits in the channel between Miniloc and Simizu islands, where current-driven nutrients feed a broad cabbage-coral (lettuce coral) garden starting around 9-12 meters (travel-palawan.com, aquanautelnido.com). Beyond the garden the reef opens onto a wider plateau, with sources reporting depths from roughly 25 meters up to 40 meters depending on how far the site is worked (tabanka-divers.com describes it as a "plateau dive, 5-40m"). Currents here can run stronger than at most other El Nido sites, which is part of what draws the large fish schools the site is known for.

**Correction note (2026-08-21 re-audit):** this site was previously classified as a wall dive. No source found in this audit describes a vertical wall or drop-off at Biet Point — every source consulted describes a reef plateau or garden, and it has been reclassified accordingly.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Between Miniloc and Simizu Islands, El Nido, Palawan, Philippines
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 25 meters

## Marine Life

Biet Point is known for large schools of yellow snapper and silver tuna hovering over the cabbage-coral garden (travel-palawan.com). Jacks, barracuda, squid, and cuttlefish are reported moving through the current-enriched water beyond the reef, and hawksbill and green turtles are confirmed here by tabanka-divers.com. Napoleon wrasse, ribbon eel, and bumphead parrotfish are reported by aquanautelnido.com. No source reviewed for this update confirmed sharks at this specific site, so shark sightings are not claimed here.

## Dive Profile

Most dives work the shallow cabbage-coral garden in the 9-15 meter range before checking deeper sections of the plateau. Currents can pick up quickly in the channel, so this is not treated as a pure beginner site despite its shallow garden depth.

## Entry and Exit

Boat dive from El Nido town operators; entry by giant stride or back roll, exit assisted by the boat crew.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Time your dive with slack or mild current where possible — this is one of the stronger-current sites in El Nido
- The cabbage-coral garden is fragile; maintain buoyancy control to avoid contact
- Watch the blue water beyond the reef edge for passing schools of snapper and tuna
