## Bikanayos Rock

Bikanayos Rock (also known locally as Picanayas) sits on the western side of Matinloc Island in El Nido.

## Overview

Bikanayos Rock is reported as a boulder-strewn site on the western face of Matinloc Island where whitetip reef sharks and pelagic fish are commonly seen.

**QA note (2026-08-21 re-audit):** this site's previously-cited source (divephilippines.com.ph) now returns compromised/spam content and could not be re-verified live during this audit. A separate source (aquanautelnido.com) lists a similarly-named site, "Pacanayas," as a 6-24m circular reef with table and daisy coral rather than a boulder field — it is unclear whether this is the same site under an alternate spelling or a different, nearby site, and this was not resolved in this pass. Given that ambiguity and the loss of the original source, the marine-life list below has been trimmed to only the whitetip shark and pelagic-fish claims that appeared consistently across multiple searches; more specific species (giant trevally, mackerel, Napoleon wrasse, hawksbill turtles) have been removed as unconfirmed.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Western side of Matinloc Island, El Nido, Palawan, Philippines
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 20 meters

## Marine Life

Whitetip reef sharks and pelagic fish are reported here across multiple search results. No source found in this audit confirmed specific additional species (e.g. giant trevally, mackerel, Napoleon wrasse, turtles) at this particular site, so they are not claimed here.

## Entry and Exit

Boat dive from El Nido town operators.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Currents sweeping the exposed western face can be strong — check conditions with your operator
- If your operator refers to this site as "Pacanayas," confirm with them whether it is the same location as Bikanayos Rock/Picanayas, since sources differ on this point
