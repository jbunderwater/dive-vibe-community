## Bikanayos Rock

Bikanayos Rock (also called Picanayas or Pacanayas) sits on the western face of Matinloc Island in El Nido and is one of the area's more reliable spots for whitetip reef shark sightings.

## Overview

Bikanayos Rock sits on the western side of Matinloc Island, where large boulders form natural corridors. It's known locally for whitetip reef sharks that use the boulder structure as a resting spot, along with regular pelagic fish activity as currents sweep past the exposed rock face. One dive operator (listing the site under the alternate spelling "Pacanayas") gives a depth range of roughly 6-24 meters.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Matinloc Island, El Nido, Palawan, Philippines
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 24 meters

## Marine Life

Two independent sources confirm whitetip reef sharks are commonly seen resting among the boulders here, along with regular pelagic fish activity. No source confirms grey reef sharks, Napoleon wrasse, hawksbill turtles, giant trevally, or mackerel specifically at this site — these species have been removed from the description as unsupported by the sources reviewed during this QA pass.

## Entry and Exit

Boat dive; entry alongside the boulder field on the current-exposed western face of Matinloc Island.

## Safety Considerations

Currents around the exposed rock can be strong. This is an advanced site — dive with a guide and maintain awareness of current direction relative to the boat.
