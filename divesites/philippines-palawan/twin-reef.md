## Twin Reef

Twin Reef is Port Barton's best-known shallow-water reef, popular with both snorkelers and beginner divers, roughly a 30-minute boat ride from the village.

## Overview

Twin Reef is an offshore reef around 100m across, with a reef flat that can be exposed at low tide (0-2m) and dropoffs beginning around 5m. It is widely described by local sources primarily as a snorkeling destination rather than a deep dive site, which fits its shallow profile. A 2025 coral-condition survey found the reef was significantly affected by the 2024 regional mass-bleaching event: only small patches — roughly 20-30m of the reef's best area — still carry healthy branching Acropora and foliose Montipora growing up to the waterline, with anchor damage from boats not using the available moorings adding further pressure. Visibility is around 10m, typical for this offshore location.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Port Barton, northern Palawan, Philippines
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 8 meters

## Marine Life

Confirmed sightings from local sources include butterflyfish, clownfish, and a range of colorful reef fish among the coral patches. Turtles are reported by at least one source but not described in detail. Broader regional Palawan reef fauna likely occurs here too, but only the species above are directly confirmed for this site.
