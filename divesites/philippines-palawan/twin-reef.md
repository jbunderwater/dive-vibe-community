## Twin Reef

Port Barton's headline reef, named for two parallel coral structures a short boat ride from the village.

## Overview

Twin Reef is Port Barton's best-known snorkel and dive site, named for its two parallel coral formations close to shore. A 2024-2025 coral-condition survey (rafsreefbrief.com) found the healthiest coral concentrated on a shallow reef flat at 0-2 meters, with drop-offs beginning around 5 meters — the reef itself is small, roughly 100 meters across. The same survey noted the 2024 regional mass-bleaching event left "healthy parts even smaller" than before, with visible dead coral patches, and recorded coral cover of hispidose and branching Acropora and foliose Montipora. Visibility runs around 10 meters. Older marketing copy describing this as suited to "beginners and experienced divers" (coffeehan.com, tikigo.com) is consistent with a shallow, easy site, though this research pass could not independently confirm a specific maximum depth beyond the shallow reef flat and drop-off.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Port Barton, northern Palawan, Philippines
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 20 meters

## Marine Life

Confirmed: turtles are regularly reported at the site (tikigo.com, rafsreefbrief.com survey notes). Coral cover includes hispidose and branching Acropora and foliose Montipora, though a substantial share was damaged or killed in the 2024 bleaching event. Earlier drafts named butterflyfish, angelfish, parrotfish, clownfish, and whitetip reef sharks specifically for this site; none of those species could be confirmed here in this research pass, so they have been removed. Palawan's regional reef fauna likely includes similar species, but no site-specific report was found.
