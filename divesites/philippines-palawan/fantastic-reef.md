## Fantastic Reef

A Port Barton reef known for its green coral formations near Double Island — and, per a recent survey, significant bleaching damage.

## Overview

Fantastic Reef sits near Double Island in Port Barton Bay and is best known among local operators for its green coral formations (tikigo.com, coffeehan.com). A 2024-2025 coral-condition survey (rafsreefbrief.com) found the reef flat at 2-3 meters dropping to roughly 10 meters, with visibility averaging only about 5 meters — typical for Port Barton Bay's inshore sites. That same survey found the drop-off "50% or more damaged or dead," with the reef flat over 90% dominated by macroalgae, both consistent with the 2024 regional coral-bleaching event. This is a meaningfully different picture than older dive-operator marketing describing a deeper 20-25 meter drop-off with "dramatic" formations — that deeper profile was not confirmed by the recent survey, and the two accounts are presented here as a genuine conflict rather than resolved in favor of one. Divers should ask their operator about current conditions before booking, since a site once marketed for vibrant coral may now show significant recent damage.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Near Double Island, Port Barton, northern Palawan, Philippines
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 25 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: ~5 meters per the 2024-2025 survey (older sources cite up to 10-25m)

## Marine Life

Confirmed: green coral formations (tikigo.com, coffeehan.com) and two green sea turtles logged during the 2024-2025 survey (rafsreefbrief.com). Earlier drafts of this page described "massive brain corals," schools of snapper, fusiliers, and barracuda, Napoleon wrasse, grouper, lionfish, and stingrays at this site — none of these were confirmed by any source found in this research pass, so they have been removed. Palawan's regional reef fauna likely includes species like these, but they should not be presented as specific to Fantastic Reef without a supporting source.

## Access

Port Barton dive and snorkel operators run boat trips to Fantastic Reef, typically as part of an island-hopping or day-trip itinerary; the crossing from Port Barton is short (coffeehan.com, tikigo.com).
