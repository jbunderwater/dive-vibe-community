## Fantastic Reef

Fantastic Reef is a Port Barton reef site known for deeper, more colorful coral than the nearby Twin Reef.

## Overview

Fantastic Reef sits deeper than Twin Reef and is consistently described by visitors as having healthier, more vibrant coral — purples, oranges, and greens across the coral heads, with fish moving quickly among the formations. It's a standard stop on Port Barton island-hopping tours alongside Twin Reef, typically visited by boat from the village.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Port Barton, northern Palawan, Philippines
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 25 meters

## Marine Life

Sources consistently describe "schools of colorful fish" moving through the coral formations, but no specific species have been confirmed for this site by any source found in this review. Previous versions of this page listed brain coral megafauna, Napoleon wrasse, grouper, lionfish, sea turtles, and stingrays as site-specific sightings — none of these could be traced to a source and have been removed. ## Entry and Exit

Port Barton dive and tour operators run boat trips to Fantastic Reef, typically combined with Twin Reef and other nearby stops on a single island-hopping day. No site-specific entry/exit procedure details were found in sources reviewed.
