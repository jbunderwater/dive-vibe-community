## Mac Point

Seagrass meadows and a quiet coral edge. Turtles are regular; dugong are reported here more than anywhere else in the Havelock set. Sensitive-species locations are coarsened on purpose.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Havelock Island (Swaraj Dweep), Andaman Islands. Surface approximation on a shallow seagrass/reef edge east of Havelock.
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 15 meters
- **Depth Range**: 10–15 m (typical ~12 m)
- **Recommended Level**: Open Water
- **Current**: Mild
- **Best Time**: October–May (peak December–April). Monsoon June–September.

## Safety Considerations

Coordinates are a surface approximation, not a mooring or exact entry point. Dive within your certification and training limits and carry an SMB.
