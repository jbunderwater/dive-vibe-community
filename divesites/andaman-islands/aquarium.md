## Aquarium

A sheltered circular reef that really does look like an aquarium: dense fish, multiple clownfish colonies and barrel sponges in 5–16 m. A staple for first fun dives after Nemo Reef.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Havelock Island (Swaraj Dweep), Andaman Islands. Surface approximation of the reef, not a mooring. Sources conflict on which side of Havelock it fringes (southeast per the Diver Atlas plate, southwest per some operator pages).
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 16 meters
- **Depth Range**: 5–16 m (typical ~10 m)
- **Recommended Level**: Open Water / Discover Scuba (supervised)
- **Current**: Mild
- **Best Time**: October–May (peak December–April). Monsoon June–September.

## Safety Considerations

Coordinates are a surface approximation, not a mooring or exact entry point. Dive within your certification and training limits and carry an SMB.
