## South Button

South Button is a shallow coral reef off South Button Island National Park, about 24 kilometres southwest of Havelock Island, visited as a day trip.

## Overview

South Button Island National Park is described by Wikipedia as India's smallest national park, about 24 km southwest of Havelock and roughly a two-hour boat trip (Tourist in India and Andaman Islands give 1.5-2 hours). All three sources describe shallow coral reefs, as shallow as about 1.8 metres, reachable by snorkellers and by beginner and experienced divers. None of the sources gives a maximum dive depth, so the 12 metres recorded for this site is an estimate for a shallow reef rather than a reported figure. Visibility is often more than 20 metres (Tourist in India). The island is uninhabited with no facilities, and Tourist in India and Andaman Islands report that the season runs December to April and that tour operators arrange forest-department permits.

## Site Information

- **Location**: South Button Island National Park, Andaman Islands (about 24 km from Havelock Island)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 12 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Often 20+ meters (Tourist in India)
- **Current**: Not reported by the sources consulted
- **Best Time**: December to April

## Marine Life

Species listed for South Button by at least two of the sources consulted include manta rays, devil rays, sea turtles, angelfish, nudibranchs and octopus. Andaman Islands and Wikipedia also list snappers, sweetlips, barracuda and lionfish, and Wikipedia and Andaman Islands list dugongs as park fauna; these are general park descriptions rather than individual dive reports.

## Dive Profile

The sources describe shallow coral reef rather than a wall or deep profile. Andaman Islands recommends early-morning dives for the clearest water, and notes that dives begin with a shallow check dive.

## Entry and Exit

Boat access only, about 1.5-2 hours from Havelock. Andaman Islands notes that visitors typically buy a forest pass at the check post, and Tourist in India says permits are usually arranged by tour operators; confirm current rules with your operator.

## Tips and Recommendations

- The park season runs December to April; May to October is monsoon
- Confirm permits through your operator before booking
- Plan for a long boat journey; the island has no facilities, so bring water, food and sun protection

## Safety Considerations

The site is remote with a long boat ride, so confirm your operator's safety equipment and communications. Dive within certification limits and carry an SMB.

## Photography

Good visibility and shallow reef make natural-light photography practical, according to the sources' descriptions.
