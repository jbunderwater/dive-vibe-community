## Pilot Reef

A broad, photogenic reef just off the channel, known for a resident leopard-shark family, marble rays and a wall of phantom bannerfish. High vis in season. A favourite between training sites and the offshore trinity.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Havelock Island (Swaraj Dweep), Andaman Islands. Surface approximation near the channel entrance north-west of Havelock.
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 25 meters
- **Depth Range**: 8–25 m (typical ~16 m)
- **Recommended Level**: Open Water
- **Current**: Mild
- **Best Time**: October–May (peak December–April). Monsoon June–September.

## Safety Considerations

Coordinates are a surface approximation, not a mooring or exact entry point. Dive within your certification and training limits and carry an SMB.
