## Nemo Reef

Beach No. 2's fringing reef (per Frogman) — where almost every Havelock diver starts. Dual reef separated by sand, clownfish, and the "Ghost Jeep", a Mahindra Commander jeep sunk as an artificial reef at 6–8 m (confirmed by ScubaGo/DIVEIndia and SSI MyDiveGuide). A common checkout and try-dive house reef — though ScubaLov states it has stopped diving Nemo Reef to reduce pressure on the site, while other operators continue to use it; that split is unresolved.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Havelock Island (Swaraj Dweep), Andaman Islands. Shore entry off Beach No. 2. Surface approximation of the inner reef.
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 16 meters
- **Depth Range**: 5–16 m (typical ~8 m)
- **Recommended Level**: Discover Scuba / Open Water
- **Current**: None
- **Best Time**: October–May (peak December–April). Monsoon June–September.

## Safety Considerations

Coordinates are a surface approximation, not a mooring or exact entry point. Dive within your certification and training limits and carry an SMB.
