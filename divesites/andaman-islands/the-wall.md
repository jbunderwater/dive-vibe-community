## The Wall

The Wall is a wall dive in the channel between Havelock (Swaraj Dweep) and Peel Island, dropping from a ridge at about 10 metres to a sandy channel floor at about 55 metres.

## Overview

The Wall sits in the channel between Havelock and Peel Island, roughly 5 km northwest of Havelock and 10-20 minutes by boat from the Havelock dive shops. A ridge at about 10-12 metres gives way on the western side to a wall that falls to a sandy channel bottom at about 55 metres (one operator gives 56 metres). The wall face is covered in purple soft coral, and fan corals are also reported. Operators describe small caves in the face at roughly 12-20 metres and note that the site can be done as a drift in the channel current. Operators differ on the length of the wall face: one reports about 80 metres, another more than 200 metres. Open Water divers can dive the ridge at 10-18 metres, while the deeper wall is for more experienced divers.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Channel between Havelock Island (Swaraj Dweep) and Peel Island, Andaman Islands
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 55 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Reported as 10-25 meters (operators give 15-20 m and 10-25 m)
- **Current**: Moderate to strong; often run as a drift
- **Best Time**: October to May (operators give October-May and December-April)

## Marine Life

Operators that describe this site report a resident Napoleon wrasse, giant groupers, and trevally. Barracuda, scorpionfish and octopus are named by two or more of the sources consulted, and Dive Andaman and Seahawks Scuba also list moray eels. Stonefish are reported by Barefoot Scuba, and one operator (Seahawks Scuba) reports whitetip reef sharks and dogtooth tuna. Seahawks Scuba reports that early-morning dives tend to see more marine life.

## Dive Profile

Dive operators describe three broad zones: the ridge at 10-18 metres, the wall face down to about 30 metres, and the deeper wall to 40 metres and beyond for suitably certified divers. The wall bottoms out on sand at about 55 metres. The wall is easy to descend past planned depth, so monitor depth and gas closely.

## Entry and Exit

Access is by boat from Havelock dive operators; Seahawks Scuba gives about 15-20 minutes by speedboat from Govind Nagar (Beach No. 3). Deploy an SMB on ascent for pickup.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Monitor depth closely, as the wall descends to 55 metres
- Look carefully before placing hands on the wall, as scorpionfish and stonefish are reported here
- Check current direction at the surface before descending
- Open Water divers should stay on the shallower ridge; deeper sections need appropriate certification and experience

## Safety Considerations

Currents are moderate to strong. Dive within your certification limits, carry an SMB, and keep contact with your buddy.

## Photography

The purple soft coral covering the wall face is a feature highlighted by operators for wide-angle work.
