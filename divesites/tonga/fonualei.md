## Fonualei

Fonualei is an uninhabited, active volcanic island roughly 70km northwest of Vava'u. It is a real, well-documented island geologically and geographically — but no source of any kind confirms it as an actively dived recreational site.

## Overview

**A note on this entry**: the previous version of this description contained a factual error and a large amount of unsourced content. It claimed the volcano's "most recent eruption" was "in the early 2000s" — Wikipedia, VolcanoDiscovery, and the Smithsonian Institution's Global Volcanism Program all agree the last confirmed eruption was in June 1957 (an earlier, larger eruption in 1846 spread ash across Vava'u and damaged crops there; unconfirmed gas/discoloration in the water was observed near the island in 2022, but this has not been characterized as an eruption). Separately, no dive shop, forum, database, or trip report found documents recreational diving at Fonualei at all — meaning the previous description's dive profile, current patterns, and marine-life claims (hammerhead sightings, manta visits, humpback whale encounters at the site) had no traceable source and have been removed rather than carried forward.

Fonualei itself measures about 2km in diameter with a maximum height of 188m, rising some 1,000m from the seafloor as part of the Tonga-Kermadec volcanic arc. It remains uninhabited today, though 19th-century islanders from the nearby island of Tokū once used it for gardens, and part of it was leased for agriculture in the 1980s.
