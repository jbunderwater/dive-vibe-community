## My Lady Lata II Wreck

A rusted wreck lying just off the main beach of Pangaimotu Island near Nuku'alofa, Tongatapu, visited mainly by snorkelers.

## Overview

**Chart note**: the OSM/LINZ chart record (hydrographic ID 3857299) lists this position as "Marmer" with the alternate name "Lady Lata".

A 2014 blog post (jouljet.blogspot.com) names the vessel My Lady Lata II, says it was caught in a cyclone about 12 years earlier (around 2002) and is half exposed above water, and describes a rusted but largely intact hull with visible stairways, ladders, railings, windows and doorways. A second ship sits beside it. Wikipedia describes an upturned ship about 50 m off the island's main beach without naming it; World Nomads says the wrecks lie off the beach, some about a five-minute swim from shore, and attributes them to Cyclone Gita (Category 4, February 2018). These accounts conflict on cause and date and may describe different vessels. The vessel's name, origin, size and sinking date are each supported by at most one fetched source, so none are asserted as fact. A fourth source (virtualoceania.net) previously cited was unreachable during this audit and is not relied on.

## Marine Life

World Nomads reports fish sheltering under a ledge of rotting wood and clams, starfish and urchins wedged in crevices beneath the wreck, within a marine reserve (Wikipedia says the Pangaimotu reef was declared a national marine reserve in 1989). The 2014 blog reports fish and other new life in and around the hulls and small itchy jellyfish-like organisms in the water. No other species are documented.

## Dive Profile

Mostly a snorkel site: the 2014 blog estimated a 30-45 minute swim to the wrecks and says a stronger swimmer can swim around the cabin.

## Entry and Exit

Boats run from Faua Wharf in Nuku'alofa, about 10 minutes (World Nomads, Wikipedia). World Nomads gives Monday-Saturday departures at 11am and returns at 4pm and notes schedules are unreliable, so confirm by phone.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Confirm the boat timetable before going
- Treat any single story of the wreck's origin with skepticism given the conflicting accounts

## Safety Considerations

Rusted metal and surge in shallow water are the obvious hazards; no source gives specific safety guidance. Entering the hull requires care and appropriate training.
