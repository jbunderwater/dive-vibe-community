## Anchor Point

Anchor Point (also listed by local operators as "Anchor Point Reef 1/2") is a reef dive site off the Kalpitiya peninsula on Sri Lanka's northwest coast, in the 14-17 meter depth range according to local dive operators.

## Overview

Two Kalpitiya-based dive operators independently list a site named "Anchor Point" (or "Anchor Point Reef"): one lists "Anchor Point Reef" at 15 meters as part of its shallow-reef tier (12-15m, between its beginner/DSD sites at 5-10m and its mid-depth reefs at 16-20m), and a second lists "Anchor Point Reef 1" at 14 meters and "Anchor Point Reef 2" at 17 meters. Both are boat dives reached from Kalpitiya. Beyond depth and the boat-access detail, the two operator listings do not provide marine life descriptions or a difficulty rating for this specific site, and no wreck-history, dive-forum, or encyclopedic source discussing this specific site was found during this audit — so the previous markdown's account of the anchors originating from "the historic spice trade and colonial shipping" could not be verified anywhere and has been removed as unsupported. It is worth noting Kalpitiya also has a separate, differently-named site ("No Anchor Reef" / "Broken Anchor Reef" depending on operator) that is explicitly marketed as a shallow beginner site — the similar naming across operators makes it easy to conflate site-specific facts between these different reefs, and this rewrite is careful not to import details from those other sites.

Because only two independent, site-specific sources were found (both dive-operator listings, not forums or encyclopedic sources), this entry does not meet the 3-source bar for full validation in this audit — see the report for details. The depth figures below (14-17m) come directly from those two operators and differ meaningfully from the previously listed 20m, which had no traceable source.

Sri Lanka's regional reef fauna (see destination overview) is expected here, but no site-specific marine life reports were found for Anchor Point. One operator's listing describes the site only in generic terms ("aquarium fishes") without naming species, and no independent source (forum, blog, or wreck/reef database) was found describing what divers actually encounter at this specific site. Claims in the previous version of this page — moray eels, lionfish, cuttlefish, parrotfish, grouper, sea turtles, barracuda, trevally, and whale sharks in a November-March season — could not be traced to any site-specific source and have been removed rather than repeated.
