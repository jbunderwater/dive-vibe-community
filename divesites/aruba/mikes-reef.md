## Mike's Reef

Mike's Reef is a coral reef known for large gorgonian clusters and a mix of brain, flower and star corals, recommended by several sources for coral photography.

## Overview

Visit Aruba describes Mike's Reef as having enormous clusters of gorgonians and brain and star coral, with purple and orange sponges, and lists a depth of 25 to 90 feet at Beginner level. Frommer's describes enormous gorgonian clusters with brain, flower and star coral. Aruba.com's Dutch-language guide lists a maximum of 27 metres, a shallow slope with many coral types, and larger soft corals in the deeper areas.

Sources place it differently: Visit Aruba says it is close to Mangel Halto, while Frommer's puts it off the central coast of De Palm Island.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Southern Aruba, near Mangel Halto per Visit Aruba
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 27 meters

## Marine Life

Visit Aruba and Frommer's both report barracuda. Frommer's also lists rainbow runners. Aruba.com mentions sea slugs, anemone shrimp and crabs among the corals.
