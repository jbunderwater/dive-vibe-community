## Kantil Reef

Kantil Reef is a coral reef on Aruba's south coast that slopes to roughly 30 metres, with some sources describing a drop-off.

## Overview

JADS Aruba describes Kantil Reef as a nice reef area with plenty of hard and soft coral, with the slope reaching about 100 feet (33 metres). Aruba.com's Dutch-language guide lists a maximum of 30 metres and describes a coral shelf with several coral types. Search-result summaries from DiveBuddy and Scuba Diving describe a drop-off to roughly 110 to 130 feet, and the site is classified here as a wall on the basis of the drop-off descriptions; JADS and Aruba.com describe a slope or shelf instead.

## Site Information

- **Location**: South coast, Aruba
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters

## Marine Life

Aruba.com lists eagle rays and manta rays around the site (a single source). No other source consulted gives site-specific marine life.
