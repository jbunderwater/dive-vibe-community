## Boca Catalina

Boca Catalina is a small sandy bay in the Malmok Beach area, popular for snorkeling and shore diving, with an easy entry from the beach.

## Overview

Aruba.com describes Boca Catalina as a small, secluded bay in the Malmok Beach area, reached by steps from a roadside parking area, with very clear water. Duikersgids lists it as a popular snorkel and dive location in a calm bay with white sand and a gradual slope to deeper water, entered from the sand, with limited current.

Depth reports differ. Duikersgids lists 15 metres, while Tropical Snorkeling gives 2 to 5 feet for the adjacent Catalina Cove snorkel area. Snorkel guides (Tropical Snorkeling, Enriching Pursuits) report an easy sandy entry at Boca Catalina Beach and say there is little to see directly in front of the beach, recommending a swim toward the rockier Catalina Cove, where booties help with the entry.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Boca Catalina Bay, Malmok area, northwest Aruba
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 15 meters

Duikersgids lists visibility of 10 to 30 metres (average 20 metres).

## Marine Life

Duikersgids names turtles and sergeant majors. Enriching Pursuits, writing about snorkeling the bay and cove, lists blue tang, sergeant majors, grunts, French angelfish, white-spotted filefish, parrotfish, surgeonfish, flying gurnard, spotted trunkfish, Bermuda chub and a zebra moray, with pillar and brain coral. Tropical Snorkeling reports a sandy bottom with seaweed patches off the beach itself and a wider list of fish and invertebrates at Catalina Cove.
