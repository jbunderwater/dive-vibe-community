## De Palm Slope

De Palm Slope is a coral slope off De Palm Island that is dived as a drift dive and also has the wreck of a Cessna 414 nearby.

## Overview

Visit Aruba describes De Palm Slope as magnificent coral formations sloping to great depths, excellent for drift diving and suited to intermediate and advanced divers, with a depth of 15 to 120 feet. Guide to Caribbean Vacations gives the same range and describes it as a drift dive accessible from De Palm Island or by boat. Duikersgids lists 20 metres as the site depth, and describes beautiful coral and the wreck of a Cessna 414 off Palm Island. Wikipedia's Palm Island article mentions a downed and submerged Cessna 414 at the island.

Duikersgids describes the site as a boat dive that can also be reached from shore once on Palm Island, with limited current; Zentacle lists shore entry via a short ferry from the mainland.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Off De Palm Island, southern Aruba
- **Entry Type**: Shore or boat entry
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 37 meters

## Marine Life

Visit Aruba reports great barracuda and frequent spotted eagle rays. Duikersgids names anemones, moray eels, nurse sharks, purple tube sponges and parrotfish.
