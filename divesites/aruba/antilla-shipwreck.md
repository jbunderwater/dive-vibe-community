## Antilla Shipwreck

This OpenStreetMap polygon entry represents the same SS Antilla wreck as the "SS Antilla Wreck" entry; the coordinates correspond to the polygon centroid of the wreck outline, and both entries refer to a single dive site.

## Overview

See the [SS Antilla Wreck](ss-antilla-wreck.md) page for the full history and site description. In brief: the Hamburg America Line freighter Antilla was scuttled by her crew on 10 May 1940 as Dutch marines moved to seize her, and lies mostly on her port side in Malmok Bay at depths of up to about 18 metres (Wikipedia). Reported lengths range from about 121 metres to about 144 metres depending on the source. The OpenStreetMap entry is tagged as a hull visible at low tide.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Malmok Bay, northwestern Aruba
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 18 meters

## Marine Life

Species reports for this wreck are listed on the SS Antilla Wreck page. Pure Diving Aruba names green turtles, moray eels, stingrays, octopus, margates and scorpionfish; Wikipedia adds lobsters, hawksbill turtles and blue tang.

## Penetration and Safety

Sources differ on whether the interior is entered, and Wikipedia notes that storm damage has led some divers to consider it unsafe. Overhead-environment diving requires appropriate training and equipment.
