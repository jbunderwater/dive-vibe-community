## Skalahein Reef

Skalahein is a south-coast boat dive reef with abundant hard coral and schooling fish, where deeper sections can have strong currents.

## Overview

JADS Aruba describes Skalahein as having a lot of hard corals and schooling fish, with a maximum depth of about 75 feet (25 metres). Aruba.com's Dutch-language guide lists a maximum of 36 metres, describes a reef full of corals and sponges, and warns of strong deep currents that make it challenging for advanced divers. Diveplannerpro also rates the site Advanced, citing depth and potential currents.

## Site Information

- **Location**: South coast, Aruba
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 25 meters

Depth reports vary between 25 and 36 metres across sources.

## Marine Life

Aruba.com reports large barracuda and horse mackerel at Skalahein. JADS Aruba describes schooling fish without naming species.
