## Rempart Serpent

Rempart Serpent is a reef dive site in Mauritius, Africa.

## Overview

Rempart Serpent (Snake Reef) is a dive site off the west coast of Mauritius, approximately 15 minutes by boat from Flic en Flac. The site is named after a winding snake-like rock formation at around 25–27 metres depth, draped in seaweed that sways in the current. The site is well-regarded for its concentration of camouflaged predators: stonefish, scorpionfish, lionfish, and moray eels are consistently reported by multiple local operators (diveseaurchin.com, mybasedivingmauritius.com). Mybasedivingmauritius.com's site-specific listing for Rempart Serpent additionally reports stingrays, turtles, trevallies, unspecified sharks, barracudas, sea slugs, nudibranchs, rhinopias, ghost pipefish, and stargazers, and recommends Nitrox certification for the dive. Strong currents are possible at this site, making it best suited to intermediate and experienced divers.

**Note on marine life:** "Sharks" (species unspecified) are now confirmed at this specific site by mybasedivingmauritius.com. However, the more specific claims of "grey reef sharks" and "manta rays" made in some general Mauritius diving articles remain unconfirmed for Rempart Serpent by name and are not repeated here.
