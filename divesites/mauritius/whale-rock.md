## Whale Rock

Whale Rock is a pinnacle dive site in Mauritius, Africa.

## Overview

Whale Rock is a submerged rock pinnacle off Mauritius' north coast, near Grand Baie / Pointe aux Canonniers, with depths ranging from around 12 metres at the shallowest to 35–38 metres at the base. The pinnacle features arches, swim-throughs, and a coral garden, and is known as a reliable location for well-camouflaged stonefish and scorpionfish (thediveglobe.com). At shallower depths, divers encounter clownfish, wrasses, parrotfish, triggerfish, butterflyfish, and small moray eels (divespiritmauritius.com). Deeper sections may yield leopard moray eels, large pufferfish, and occasional small sharks (thediveglobe.com). In summer months, tropicalwarehouse.co.uk reports occasional sightings of manta rays and swordfish at the site -- this is a single-source claim and not confirmed elsewhere.

**Note on location:** thediveglobe.com labels this site "South Coast," but that conflicts with the site's coordinates (Mauritius' northernmost point) and with three independent sources (tropicalwarehouse.co.uk, dododivers.com, zentacle.com) that all place it at Grand Baie on the north coast. This page follows the north-coast consensus; the "South Coast" label may be an error or refer to a different, similarly-named site.

**Note on depth:** Sources disagree considerably -- zentacle.com lists a shallow ~18m, divespiritmauritius.com and dododivers.com report up to 35–38m, and thediveglobe.com gives an average of 20m with a 35m maximum. The 32m figure recorded here sits within that range but should be treated as approximate.

## Site Information

- **Location**: North coast, Mauritius
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Pinnacle/seamount
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 32 meters
