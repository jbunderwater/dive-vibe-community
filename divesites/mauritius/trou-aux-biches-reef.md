## Trou aux Biches Reef

Trou aux Biches Reef is a reef dive site in Mauritius, Africa.

## Overview

Trou aux Biches on Mauritius' northern coast is one of the island's main diving hubs, with a barrier reef approximately one kilometre offshore providing access to numerous dive sites in short boat rides. The broader Trou aux Biches area is the base for the Stella Maru wreck dive, as well as deeper sites including the Silver Star and Waterlily/Emily wrecks. However, no local operator or dive database uses the exact name "Trou aux Biches Reef" for a specific site: the area's named reef dives are Anchor Reef (9–19m), The Tube (5–11m), Coral Garden (15–19m), Water Lily Reef, Holt's Rock, Turtle House Reef, 3 Anchors and Lost Anchor (bluewaterdivingcentre.com), and the shallow shore lagoon itself (snorkeling-report.com) runs only about 2 metres deep -- well short of the 12m recorded here. Common reef fish reported in the wider Trou aux Biches lagoon include Moorish idols, convict tangs, whitetail dascyllus, and sea turtles (snorkeling-report.com), but these cannot be tied to a specific 12m site under this name.
