## Hoi Siong No. 6 Wreck

Hoi Siong No. 6 Wreck is a wreck dive site in Mauritius, Africa.

## Overview

The Hoi Siong No. 6 is a former fishing vessel deliberately sunk in 2003 as an artificial reef off the southwest coast of Mauritius near Le Morne. The wreck sits at 28–29 metres depth and, having been down for two decades, has accumulated coral growth, providing habitat for barracuda, tuna, lionfish, butterflyfish, and schools of goatfish. The site is located in relatively sheltered waters near Île aux Bénitiers with good visibility, and is dived by all experience levels though the depth and open-water setting favour Intermediate-and-above certification.

**Note on organisations involved:** Some sources describe the sinking as a collaboration between the Mauritius Marine Conservation Society and the Mauritius Scuba Diving Association, with the Mauritius Oceanographic Institute mentioned in one additional source. These organisational details could not be independently re-confirmed across current dive-shop or database sources during this review, so they are reported with that caveat rather than stated as settled fact.

**Note on vessel identity — important caveat:** Vessel-tracking databases list detailed specifications (314 gross tons, built 1979, formerly named Fukuichi Maru No. 86) for a "Hoi Siong No. 5" — a sister vessel from the same fishing fleet, not this wreck. Those specifications are NOT used here, since attributing another hull's data to Hoi Siong No. 6 would be a fabrication. No verified length, tonnage, or build date for Hoi Siong No. 6 specifically was found in any source.

**Note on interior:** One source (mauritiusguidedtours.com) states the wheelhouse is intact and accessible, but this could not be verified against local operator sources. Wreck penetration always requires appropriate training and equipment and is never risk-free.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Le Morne / Île aux Bénitiers area, Mauritius
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 29 meters
