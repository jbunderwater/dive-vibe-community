## Cathedral Reef

Cathedral Reef is a cave dive site in Mauritius, Africa.

## Overview

La Cathédrale (the Cathedral) is widely regarded as Mauritius' most famous dive site, located five minutes by boat from Flic en Flac on the west coast. A drop-off combined with massive boulders has created a cave-like chamber where sunlight penetrates through rifts in the rock, producing cathedral-like shafts of light. The dive begins at around 12–18 metres and descends to around 28 metres, where the large cavern connects via arches, swim-throughs, and a chimney. Lobsters, moray eels, shrimps and stingrays shelter in the cracks and ledges, alongside general reef fish. Whitetip reef sharks have been reported at the site in winter months (mybasedivingmauritius.com).

**Note on depth:** Sources cite a maximum between 28 and 30 metres (mauritiusnow.com, mybasedivingmauritius.com), though some aggregate databases list up to 37 metres. The 28m figure is used here as it reflects the majority of local operator descriptions.

**Note on marine life:** Only lobsters, moray eels, shrimps, stingrays and winter whitetip reef sharks are confirmed for this specific site by local operators. Earlier drafts of this page named wrasses, sweetlips and groupers, but no source ties those species to Cathedral specifically -- they have been removed rather than left as an unverified regional list.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Flic en Flac, Mauritius
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Cave/cavern
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 28 meters
