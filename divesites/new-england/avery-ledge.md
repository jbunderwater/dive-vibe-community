# Avery Ledge

A submerged rocky ledge at the northern edge of the Dry Salvages group off Rockport, Massachusetts, described on navigational charts, though no dive-specific documentation was found.

## Overview

Avery Ledge lies immediately west of the Dry Salvages and California wreck site cluster off Rockport, Massachusetts — one of the most productive dive areas on the North Shore of Massachusetts. The ledge system sits in roughly 12–18 metres of water at the northern fringe of the Salvages shoal complex, where the rocky substrate gives way to the sandy approaches of Ipswich Bay. Like the neighboring California and Charles S. Haight sites, Avery Ledge benefits from the cold, nutrient-rich Gulf of Maine water that flows through this area, and the general cold-water character typical of the North Shore, though no site-specific marine-life survey was found. Avery Ledge lies close enough to the California wreck that the two are sometimes combined on a single charter, though no site-specific dive report confirming this was found.

## Dive Profile

Access via boat charter from Rockport or Gloucester. Descend to the rocky ledge at 10–18 metres and navigate the ledge edge from shallow to deeper sections. No site-specific marine-life data confirms which depth band is most productive. The ledge makes an excellent pairing with the California wreck, which lies only a short distance to the east. Plan slack tide windows carefully using Cape Ann tidal predictions. Ascend with adequate reserve and complete a 3-minute safety stop at 5 metres.

## Entry and Exit

Boat charter required from Cape Ann Divers (Gloucester) or other Rockport-area operators. Local knowledge of the Dry Salvages area is essential — submerged rocks create navigation hazards for boats throughout this zone. Anchor in sand adjacent to the ledge. Deploy SMB before ascending from depth.

## Safety Considerations

Submerged rocks in the Dry Salvages area create significant boat navigation hazards — a local captain is essential. Tidal current can be strong between the rock outcrops. Cold water year-round below 5 metres — 7mm wetsuit or drysuit recommended. Deploy SMB clearly before surfacing away from the boat.
