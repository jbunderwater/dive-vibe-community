# Martin Ledge

A rocky ledge off the Hull/Cohasset coast, part of the western South Shore ledge cluster southwest of Boston Harbor.

## Overview

Martin Ledge sits in the waters west and south of Boston Harbor in the Hull/Cohasset/Scituate triangle — a position that reflects the broad rocky ledge system that extends across Massachusetts Bay's southern approaches. The site coordinates place it among the westernmost members of the South Shore ledge cluster alongside Harding Ledge and Tewksbury Rock. These western ledges tend to be closer to shore and more accessible from Hull and Cohasset than the eastern ledges near Scituate. Martin Ledge at 18 metres maximum depth offers the same productive cold-water reef diving character as the rest of the ledge system — rocky substrate, dense invertebrate growth, and reliable cold-water fish populations.
