## Leštinka - Kaňon

A small, atmospheric granite quarry tucked into a forest canyon near Skuteč, part of the broader Leštinka diving area.

## Overview

Leštinka - Kaňon (also documented as the "Mikšov - Kaňon" quarry) is a small flooded granite quarry accessed from the village of Leštinka near Skuteč in the Pardubický region, part of a larger trapezoid-shaped cluster of over thirty flooded granite quarries between Miřetice, Hlinsko, Vrbatův Kostelec and Skuteč. The quarry lies at the end of a roughly 400-meter path through forest and meadow, past a transformer station and two cottages, then through a narrow canyon; a locked gate has blocked the approach road since 2017. Local municipal sources describe it as the most renowned and most visually striking of the Skuteč-area quarries, but also the most visited and most affected by littering/vandalism. It was historically connected by a narrow-gauge quarry railway to the neighboring Leštinka I - Zvěřinov quarry. Despite its modest depth, the quarry's atmospheric canyon setting and proximity to the larger Lom Leštinka diving complex make it a popular stop. The area is busy with campers in summer but quiet the rest of the year.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Leštinka, near Skuteč, Pardubický kraj
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 10 meters
- **Current**: None
- **Best Time**: Spring and autumn for fewer crowds

## Underwater Features

The quarry sits within the Železnohorský pluton geological complex; the surrounding rock is a fine-grained, light grey amphibole-biotite granodiorite. The quarry bottom holds remnants of mining/camp equipment and pipes, and one source (stranypotapecske.cz) additionally mentions a possible old diver-training device and an underwater grave marker, neither of which has been independently confirmed. Claims of a caisson and sunken museum exhibit at this specific quarry (as distinct from the main Lom Leštinka, which does have both) are based on a single source and have not been independently confirmed — do not expect to find them here. The canyon walls continue underwater, creating an enclosed, atmospheric diving environment.

## Marine Life

Regional freshwater fauna typical of small Bohemian granite quarries applies, but no site-specific fish or invertebrate sightings have been reported for this particular quarry in the sources consulted.

## Dive Profile

A straightforward shallow dive. Enter from shore and explore the quarry floor, following the walls for orientation. The maximum depth of 10 meters makes this suitable for training dives and checkout sessions. The confined space provides a good environment for practicing buoyancy skills.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Several dive centers in the broader Leštinka / Divepark area provide bottle filling, equipment rental, food, and accommodation
- This is a distinct quarry from the main Lom Leštinka — follow signs toward "Kaňon," and note the approach road has been gated since 2017
- A dry suit is recommended due to cold water temperatures
