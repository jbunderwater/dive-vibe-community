## Lom Bořená Hora

A flooded granite quarry near Prague featuring the wreck of a military Mil Mi-8S helicopter at 6-8 meters depth — the star attraction and one of the Czech Republic's most photogenic wrecks.

## Overview

Lom Bořená Hora is a flooded granite quarry in the village of Štětkovice, Příbram District, Central Bohemia, approximately 35 km from Prague near the Slapy Dam. The quarry operated from 1922 to 1970, extracting bluish Sedlčany granite used for monuments and paving stones, with a brief second period of extraction in the 1990s. Today its main attraction is the submerged wreck of a Mil Mi-8S military helicopter (serial number 0829). The aircraft crashed on 28 October 2001 near Okrouhlá Radouň village (not near Milevsko as sometimes reported) while carrying cosmonaut Vladimír Remek and astronaut Eugene Cernan, along with astronaut John Blaha and military personnel — 12 people in total. Both engines shut down during approach, leading to a hard landing; a Prague army technician was later tried on suspicion of failing to activate a fuel-pump switch, but the Písek district court acquitted him in September 2004 because the evidence did not conclusively establish the cause. All aboard survived, though four suffered injuries. Following legal proceedings over ownership of the wreck, a local diving center purchased it in 2007 and sank it at Bořená Hora in May 2009.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Štětkovice, Příbram District, Central Bohemia (Středočeský kraj)
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 22 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Up to 5 meters
- **Current**: None
- **Best Time**: Spring and autumn for best visibility

## Underwater Features

The Mil Mi-8S helicopter sits at approximately 6-8 meters depth (two independent sources), though one other report gives roughly 11 meters — fully accessible to recreational divers within the site's stated maximum depth either way. It can be entered with a torch and explored internally. Other sunken attractions include a Tatra truck, a Ford car, and a rocket model, all connected by guide ropes. The bluish granite walls provide an atmospheric backdrop.

## Marine Life

Freshwater species typical of Central Bohemian quarries. Fish congregate around the sunken vehicles and helicopter structure.

## Dive Profile

Enter from shore and navigate to the helicopter wreck at 6-8 meters — the quarry's centerpiece. Guide ropes connect the helicopter to the Tatra truck and Ford car at various depths. The maximum depth of 22 meters keeps the site well within recreational diving limits. Multiple objects spread across the quarry floor allow for extended exploration at comfortable depths.

## Tips and Recommendations

- The helicopter wreck is large enough to swim around and photograph from multiple angles
- Bring a camera — the helicopter, truck, and car make unique photo subjects
- Located near Slapy Dam, making it easy to combine with a reservoir dive for a full day
