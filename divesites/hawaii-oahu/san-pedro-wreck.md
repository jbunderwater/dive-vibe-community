## San Pedro Wreck

The San Pedro is an approachable intermediate wreck dive on Oahu's south shore—a Japanese-built fishing vessel intentionally sunk in 1996 a short distance from the YO-257, making dual-wreck dives on a single charter a popular option.

## Overview

The San Pedro sits on the south shore seabed with the main deck at 60–70 feet and the bottom at approximately 80–95 feet depending on source. The vessel is smaller and more navigable than the nearby YO-257, making it a good introduction to wreck diving for intermediate-level divers. The proximity to YO-257 (sources report roughly 70–100 feet away) allows dive operators to run both sites in sequence. Currents can be strong—a thorough dive briefing is essential. Atlantis Submarines operate a submersible tour route over this wreck, so divers may encounter the submarine during their dive.

## Historical Background

The San Pedro was a Japanese-built, Korean-owned longline fishing vessel that targeted tuna and swordfish in deep water and later served as an inter-island cargo ship. The vessel caught fire off the Big Island (near South Point) around 1975 and was towed to Keehi Lagoon, where it sat derelict/anchored for roughly two decades. The State of Hawaii then sold the hull to Atlantis Submarines Hawaii for $1 plus cleanup reimbursement; total preparation cost was approximately $360,000. Atlantis towed the vessel to its current position on the south shore near YO-257 and intentionally scuttled it there in 1996. The wreck's final resting place is on the south shore, not Keehi Lagoon — Keehi Lagoon was only where the hull sat derelict before the sinking.

Note on length: sources disagree. oahudiving.com and diveoahu.com both describe the vessel as approximately 90 feet long; rainbowscuba.com gives 111 feet. This discrepancy is unresolved in available sources.

Note on depth: oahudiving.com gives the bottom at approximately 80 feet; diveoahu.com gives 95 feet; rainbowscuba.com gives a range of 60–85 feet. Most sources cite 80–85 feet at the deepest point, with the main decks at 60–70 feet.

Note on distance to YO-257: sources vary between approximately 70 and 100 feet; the exact figure is not consistently reported.

## Site Information

- **Location**: South shore of Oahu, ~70–100 feet from YO-257 (sources vary)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Length**: Disputed — ~90 ft (oahudiving.com, diveoahu.com) vs 111 ft (rainbowscuba.com)
- **Main Deck Depth**: ~18–21 meters (60–70 feet)
- **Maximum Depth**: 26 meters
- **Special Note**: A short distance from YO-257—dual-wreck dive possible

## Marine Life

Sea turtles, eagle rays, and amberjacks are frequently reported at this site across multiple operator accounts. White-tip reef sharks have been observed in the forward hold. Overhead environments in the cargo holds should be entered only with appropriate wreck diving training.
