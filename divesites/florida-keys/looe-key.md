## Looe Key

Looe Key is a reef dive site in Florida Keys, North America.

## Overview

A spur-and-groove reef 6 miles offshore of Big Pine/Ramrod Key, entirely submerged, with depths from 7-30 feet. Named for HMS Looe, a British warship wrecked here in 1744; became a National Marine Sanctuary in 1981, later folded into the Florida Keys National Marine Sanctuary in 1990. Reported marine life includes reef sharks, spotted eagle rays, goliath grouper, and large barracuda.
