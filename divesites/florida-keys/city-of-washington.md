## City of Washington

City of Washington is a historic wreck dive in Florida Keys, North America.

## Overview

An official NOAA Shipwreck Trail wreck: an iron-hulled steamship launched in 1877, which was anchored near the USS Maine in Havana Harbor in 1898 when the Maine exploded. Later converted to a coal barge, it ran aground on Elbow Reef (Key Largo) on July 10, 1917 while under tow and became a total loss. The 325-foot debris field consists mainly of the lower hull and bilge; the stern's propeller shaft tunnel is identifiable. Depth is reported inconsistently — NOAA's summary cites 25 feet, while commercial dive sources report 20-32 feet.
