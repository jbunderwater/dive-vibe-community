## Benwood

Benwood is a historic wreck dive in Florida Keys, North America.

## Overview

An official NOAA Shipwreck Trail wreck: a 360-foot, 51-foot-beam Norwegian-owned freighter built in 1910, sunk after colliding with the steamship Robert C. Tuttle on April 9, 1942 — both ships were running dark due to U-boat concerns during WWII. Lies between French Reef and Dixie Shoals in 25-45 feet of water. The bow is the most intact section, rising about 25 feet off the bottom, and it's one of the most popular wreck dives in the sanctuary.
