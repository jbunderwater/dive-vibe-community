## Christ of the Abyss (Key Largo Dry Rocks)

Christ of the Abyss (Key Largo Dry Rocks) is a reef dive site in Florida Keys, North America.

## Overview

A shallow spur-and-groove reef in John Pennekamp Coral Reef State Park, seaward of White Banks about 6 miles off Key Largo. Home to the bronze "Christ of the Abyss" statue, standing in roughly 25 feet of water, with the surrounding reef ranging from about 10-30 feet. One of the most photographed and most-dived sites in the Keys, suitable for both snorkelers and divers.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Keys, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 9 meters
