## Grecian Rocks

Grecian Rocks is a reef dive site in Florida Keys, North America.

## Overview

A shallow, gently sloping Key Largo reef with depths from about 4-30 feet (the crest is exposed at low tide in places), within the Key Largo Dry Rocks-Grecian Rocks Sanctuary Preservation Area. Very popular for both beginner diving and snorkeling.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Keys, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 9 meters
