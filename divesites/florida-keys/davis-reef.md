## Davis Reef

Davis Reef is a reef dive site in Florida Keys, North America.

## Overview

An Islamorada reef and ledge (also called "Davis Ledge") with depths of roughly 15-30 feet, popular as an easy day and night dive; sources note frequent turtle sightings along the ledge after dark.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Keys, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 9 meters
