## Cayman Salvager

Cayman Salvager is a historic wreck dive in Florida Keys, North America.

## Overview

A 187-foot steel-hulled former Coast Guard buoy tender (built 1936), later used as a cable layer, mine planter, and freighter. Seized in 1980 during the Mariel boatlift for illegally transporting Cubans, it sank unexpectedly at dock in Key West and was refloated in 1985 for scuttling — but sank prematurely in about 90 feet of water, 7 miles SE of Key West, when a tow cable snapped, short of its intended 300-foot deep-reef target. Originally settled on its side; a later hurricane surge (reported as Hurricane Kate) flipped it upright. Known as a strong goliath grouper site.
