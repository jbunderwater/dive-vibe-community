## French Reef

French Reef is a reef dive site in Florida Keys, North America.

## Overview

A Key Largo reef known for numerous ledges, tunnels, and swim-throughs, including named features such as Hourglass Cave, Christmas Tree Cave, and Sand Bottom Cave — the largest of the swim-throughs, at around 25-30 feet. Depths on the main reef run from shallow down to about 45 feet, with a deeper southeast corner known as "French Deep" reaching roughly 55 feet.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Keys, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 17 meters
