## Eagle

Eagle is a historic wreck dive in Florida Keys, North America.

## Overview

An official NOAA Shipwreck Trail wreck: a 287-foot freighter (per NOAA's page; a few commercial sources say 269 feet — a minor discrepancy) launched in Holland in December 1962. Damaged by fire in October 1985 while operating as the Arron K.; purchased by the Florida Keys Artificial Reef Association and intentionally sunk December 20, 1985 about 3 miles northeast of Alligator Reef Light, after breaking free from its moorings the night before the scheduled sinking. Now lies on its starboard side in 110 feet of water; Hurricane Georges broke the hull in half in 1998.
