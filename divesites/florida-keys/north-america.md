## North America

North America is a historic wreck dive in Florida Keys, North America.

## Overview

An official NOAA Shipwreck Trail wreck: remains believed to be the three-masted vessel North America, built in Bath, Maine in 1833 and lost November 25, 1842 on Delta Shoals en route from New York to Mobile, AL. Rests in 14 feet of water in sand and grass flats north of Delta Shoals, east of Sombrero light. IMPORTANT: NOAA's own page states the identification "has not been definitively confirmed" — the coordinates given are for the general Delta Shoal area, not a confirmed precise wreck fix.
