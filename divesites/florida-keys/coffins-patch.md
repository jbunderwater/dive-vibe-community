## Coffins Patch

Coffins Patch is a reef dive site in Florida Keys, North America.

## Overview

A cluster of six patch reefs about 4 miles off Marathon/Key Colony Beach, part of a Sanctuary Preservation Area, with depths of roughly 5-25 feet. Known for boulder and brain coral formations, sea fans, and gorgonians; the Adelaide Baker wreck lies at its north end.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Keys, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 8 meters
