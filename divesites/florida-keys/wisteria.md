## Wisteria

Wisteria is a wreck dive site in the Florida Keys.

## Overview

Wisteria Island (also known locally as Christmas Tree Island) is a small uninhabited federal island created by US Navy dredging of Key West harbor in the late 1890s and early 1900s, located about 645 yards northwest of Key West. The island takes its name from the U.S. Public Health Service disinfecting steamer Wisteria — formerly a U.S. Lighthouse Service light tender — which sank while moored at the island in 1925 and burned to the waterline; the wreck was reportedly salvaged afterward. Historical photographs also show old ship remnants on the island's north side, but no dive-specific source confirms whether any in-water wreckage remains, its condition, depth profile, or marine life. No dive operator currently lists this as a site in their standard rotation; any visit should be coordinated directly with a Key West dive shop.
