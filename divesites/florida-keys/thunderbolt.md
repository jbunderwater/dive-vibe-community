## Thunderbolt

Thunderbolt is a historic wreck dive in Florida Keys, North America.

## Overview

An official NOAA Shipwreck Trail wreck: originally the Army minefield-tender Major General Wallace F. Randolph, built in 1942 by Marietta Manufacturing Co. Later used by Florida Power & Light for lightning-strike research and renamed Thunderbolt, then intentionally sunk March 6, 1986 as an artificial reef, 4-5 miles south of Marathon/Key Colony Beach. The 189-foot hull sits upright and intact on sand at 120 feet; a prominent cable-handling reel sits around 80 feet.
