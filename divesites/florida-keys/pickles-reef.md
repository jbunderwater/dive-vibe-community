## Pickles Reef

Pickles Reef is a reef dive site in Florida Keys, North America.

## Overview

A Key Largo reef roughly 5.5 nautical miles offshore, named for barrel-shaped hardened concrete/mortar cargo — mistaken for pickle barrels — scattered on the seafloor from a Civil War-era supply vessel that ran aground and broke up here. Main depths run 6-25 feet, with deeper ledges to about 40 feet on the seaward side.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Keys, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef

- **Maximum Depth**: 12 meters
