## Carysfort Reef

Carysfort Reef is a reef dive site in Florida Keys, North America.

## Overview

A four-mile-long reef system marked by the 112-foot Carysfort Lighthouse (built 1852, the oldest functioning lighthouse of its type in the US), about 6 miles off Key Largo. Thirteen mooring buoys mark dive spots along the reef, with a wall dropping to roughly 65-80 feet off the deeper section; depth varies noticeably by mooring, so treat it as a range rather than a single figure.

- **Site Type**: Reef
