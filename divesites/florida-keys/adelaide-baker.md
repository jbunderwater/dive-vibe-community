## Adelaide Baker

Adelaide Baker is a historic wreck dive in Florida Keys, North America.

## Overview

An official NOAA Shipwreck Trail wreck: originally the F.W. Carver, a 153-foot double-decked wooden sailing vessel built in 1863 in Bangor, Maine. Wrecked January 28, 1889 on Coffins Patch Reef while carrying sawn timber to Savannah, GA, after the captain misjudged his position; no loss of life. Remains scatter roughly 1,400 feet, including a 77-foot iron mast, at the north end of Coffins Patch, four miles south-southeast of Duck Key.
