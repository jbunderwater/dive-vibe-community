## Adolphus Busch Sr.

Adolphus Busch Sr. is a historic wreck dive in Florida Keys, North America.

## Overview

A 210-foot cargo freighter built in Scotland in 1951 (formerly named London, Topsail Star, Windsor Trader, and Ocean Alley), intentionally sunk December 5, 1998 near Looe Key, about 3 miles west of the reef. Sits upright on sand, with the wheelhouse at about 70 feet and the main deck around 90 feet. It was sunk without explosives — holes were cut and the hull flooded — to protect the surrounding sanctuary reef, funded largely by Adolphus Busch IV as a memorial to his grandfather.
