## San Pedro

San Pedro is a historic wreck dive in Florida Keys, North America.

## Overview

An official NOAA Shipwreck Trail wreck and State Underwater Archaeological Preserve: a 287-ton Dutch-built vessel from the 1733 Spanish treasure fleet, lost in a hurricane shortly after departing Havana on July 13, 1733. Rests in 18 feet of water about a mile south of Indian Key — the oldest wreck on the Shipwreck Trail.
