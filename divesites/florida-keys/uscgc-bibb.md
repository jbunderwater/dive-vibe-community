## USCGC Bibb

USCGC Bibb is a historic wreck dive in Florida Keys, North America.

## Overview

Sister ship to the already-listed USCGC Duane — a 327-foot Coast Guard cutter of the same class, intentionally sunk as an artificial reef on the same day in 1987, about half a mile from the Duane near Molasses Reef, Key Largo. Rests on its starboard side; the port gunnel rail sits around 95 feet, with the stern deeper at 130 feet.
