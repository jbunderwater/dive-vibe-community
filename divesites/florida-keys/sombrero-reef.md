## Sombrero Reef

Sombrero Reef is a reef dive site in Florida Keys, North America.

## Overview

A classic spur-and-groove reef off Marathon, marked by the 142-foot octagonal Sombrero Key Lighthouse (completed 1858, deactivated 2015). A designated Sanctuary Preservation Area accessed only by mooring buoy, with depths from a few feet near the lighthouse pilings to about 30 feet in the deeper channels.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Keys, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
