# El Islote

## Overview
El Islote ("The Islet") is a rocky island promontory at the southern end of Pulmo Bay, rising from a sandy seabed with coral growth, sea fans, and diverse reef fish colonising its flanks. The rock creates wall-like faces in places, descending to a sandy base at roughly 18 meters where boulder formations create sheltered pockets. Divers circle the formation starting from shallow water and work their way down to the sandy bottom among boulders. Sardine schools periodically swarm the site in bait-ball formations. An earlier version of this description mentioned a "golden grouper" at the site's deeper north face; no source consulted for this audit confirmed that species specifically at El Islote, so the claim has been removed. The site's relatively easy conditions and diverse scenery make it one of the best beginner-friendly sites in Cabo Pulmo, though intermediate divers will also find the marine variety rewarding.

## Site Information
- **Location**: Cabo Pulmo National Marine Park, Baja California Sur (southern end)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Pinnacle/seamount
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 18 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 10-25 meters
- **Current**: Generally mild
- **Best Time**: Year-round

## Marine Life
Sea fans, hard corals, multiple eel species (green, zebra, fine-spotted, jewel eels confirmed by local operators), moray eels, African pompano, groupers, snappers, sea turtles (including hawksbill turtles per Costa Coral Mexico), lobsters, crabs, sea spiders, nudibranchs, scorpionfish. Sardine schools periodically form bait balls around the rock. Regional marine life including bigeye trevally may transit the site; no site-specific jack tornado reports were found for El Islote, and no source confirmed a "golden grouper" specifically at this site.

## Dive Profile
Circle the seamount from shallow coral areas down to the sandy base at 18 meters. The boulder formations at the base are worth exploring for morays and sheltering fish. Conditions are generally calm, making this suitable for newer divers. The 45-minute park dive limit applies.

## Tips and Recommendations
- Good first-dive-of-the-trip site to get comfortable before deeper park sites
- Check the boulder formations at the base for moray eels and lobsters
- Sea fans on the upper slopes make excellent wide-angle subjects
- Circle the entire formation to explore the varied character of each face
