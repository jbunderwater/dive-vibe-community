# Los Morros

## Overview
Los Morros is a series of massive rock formations covered in sea fans and corals, located north of El Bajo and further offshore than the central reef sites. This is one of Cabo Pulmo's most iconic sites for the park's signature spectacle: the jack tornado. Massive schools of bigeye trevally form swirling columns so dense they can block out the sunlight overhead. The rocky formations feature tunnels, crevices, and sandy patches full of garden eels. Multiple current sources independently rate Los Morros as an advanced-level dive (rather than intermediate) due to swift, unpredictable currents, cooler water, and depths cited up to 18–24 meters depending on the source — this description has been updated accordingly following the 2026-07-22 re-audit. The high fish concentration is a direct result of the park's 30+ years of marine protection.

## Site Information
- **Location**: Cabo Pulmo National Marine Park, Baja California Sur
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 18 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 10-25 meters
- **Current**: Moderate to strong; unpredictable
- **Best Time**: Year-round; jack tornado most reliable October-May

## Marine Life
Bigeye trevally (jack tornado formations), large groupers including Gulf grouper and Goliath grouper, king angelfish, schooling snapper, southern stingrays, moray eels, green sea turtles, garden eels in sandy patches between formations, mobula rays (seasonal), whitetip reef sharks. The sheer biomass at this site is what makes it exceptional — quantity as much as variety.

## Dive Profile
Maximum depth generally cited around 18 meters along the broken reef, though at least one source describes deeper sections to 24 meters. Most of the action happens in mid-water as the jack schools form and reform around divers. Stay neutrally buoyant in the water column to enjoy the spectacle without disturbing the reef below. Current can be swift and requires strong buoyancy control — this is not a beginner site. The 45-minute park dive limit applies.

## Tips and Recommendations
- Wide-angle lens essential — this is the site for the classic jack tornado shot
- Stay still and let the school come to you rather than chasing
- Often combined with El Bajo on the same boat trip
- Be prepared for cooler water and stronger current than inshore sites — sources rate this dive as advanced-level
- Check the tunnel and crevice formations for resting groupers
