# Meros

## Overview
Meros — full local name El Bajo de los Meros ("Groupers' Shoal") — is Cabo Pulmo's northernmost reef, extending north from El Bajo along a similar north-south ridge. Two independent sources confirm the site's defining character: reef composition and fish life closely mirror El Bajo, but Meros "tends to see more turtles." Leopard grouper (with the rare golden color morph), yellowtail snapper, dog snapper, porkfish, and burrito grunt school in large numbers along the ridge's natural amphitheaters, and the site is best dived as a drift when current allows. Mobula rays are seen seasonally. During December through March, humpback whale songs are audible underwater across Cabo Pulmo generally — Meros is one of the sites where operators mention this — though no source confirmed a Meros-specific whale shark sighting as a regular occurrence. The 9–18 meter depth range and moderate conditions make Meros accessible to intermediate divers, though currents require care.

## Site Information
- **Location**: Cabo Pulmo National Marine Park, Baja California Sur (northern reef)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Pinnacle/seamount
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 18 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 10-25 meters
- **Current**: Mild to moderate
- **Best Time**: December-March for whale songs and mobula rays; year-round for reef life

## Marine Life
Leopard grouper (occasionally the rare golden color morph), yellowtail snapper, dog snapper, porkfish, burrito grunt, sea turtles (more numerous here than at neighboring El Bajo per source reports), mobula rays (seasonal). Humpback whale songs are audible underwater during December–March across the park, including at this site per operator reports. Whale sharks transit the broader Cabo Pulmo area but were not confirmed as a regular Meros-specific sighting in sources consulted.

## Dive Profile
Depths from 9 to 18 meters along the reef bar. Follow the meandering coral formations, checking overhangs for resting groupers. The reef connects to the El Bajo system to the south. The 45-minute park dive limit applies.

## Tips and Recommendations
- Dive in winter for the chance to hear humpback whale songs underwater
- Often combined with El Bajo on the same boat trip
- Look under overhangs and in crevices for the groupers the site is named after
- Keep an eye on the blue water — whale sharks and mobulas transit through without warning
