# Los Chopitos

## Overview
Los Chopitos (also known locally as Playa La Sirenita or "Mermaid Beach"/"Mermaids Beach") is a sheltered bay protected from wind and waves, used principally for training dives, resort courses, and refresher dives — access is by kayak, boat, or a walking trail from Los Arbolitos. The site is a gentle sandy-bottom entry that gradually descends from 2–3 meters to a maximum around 10–12 meters, making it the park's most accessible dive. The bay's protection from swell makes it a reliable alternative when outer sites have current. Sources agree the bay hosts a variety of small fish and marine invertebrates rewarding for macro-focused, patient divers, but a 2026-07-23 re-audit found no currently-verifiable source naming octopus specifically at this site (the earlier claim traced to a now-offline operator page) — that specific claim has been removed rather than repeated unconfirmed. The "Chopitos" name is commonly said to reference small squid-like cephalopods seen in the area, though no source independently confirms this etymology.

## Site Information
- **Location**: Cabo Pulmo National Marine Park, Baja California Sur
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 10 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 10-25 meters
- **Current**: Generally mild
- **Best Time**: Year-round

## Marine Life
Small reef fish and marine invertebrates in the shallow, sheltered bay (confirmed generically by Costa Coral Mexico and Cabo Pulmo Beach Resort). A macro diver's site rather than a pelagic site. Regional Cabo Pulmo marine life includes sea turtles, rays, and octopus, but no currently-verifiable source confirms these specifically at Los Chopitos — treat any species beyond "small reef fish and invertebrates" as unconfirmed for this site.

## Dive Profile
Shallow reef diving to 10 meters. The easy depth profile allows long bottom times within the 45-minute park limit. Navigate along the reef structure looking for small life in coral crevices and on rubble patches.

## Tips and Recommendations
- Macro lens useful — the sheltered bay rewards slow, patient observation
- Ideal site for certification open water training dives
- Calm conditions make this a reliable option when outer sites have strong current
- Can be linked to Las Casitas by swimming in the right conditions
