## המרכב (HaMerkav)

HaMerkav ("The Vessel") is an old shipwreck scattered across the shallow reefs near the Akhziv Islands, north of Nahariya — one of several named boat-accessible sites along the Rosh Hanikra–Akhziv coast.

## Overview

Local Israeli dive clubs describe HaMerkav as an old vessel that grounded on the reef here "decades ago," with its remains now scattered over a wide area of shallow reef near the Akhziv Islands. One dive club describes it as the wreckage of an old Lebanese-era merchant vessel; the Israeli Diving Federation's regional guide groups it with the nearby "Turkish Ship" (Wreck Turkish) as an older vessel that has sat on the reef for decades and attracts large lobsters and octopus, particularly at night. No source found gives a specific vessel name, build date, or exact sinking date/cause — treat the ship's identity as unconfirmed rather than assume it is Lebanese, Turkish, or otherwise.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Near the Akhziv Islands, Rosh Hanikra–Akhziv coast, northern Israel
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Maximum Depth**: 8 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 5–12 metres (northern Israeli Mediterranean, seasonal)
- **Current**: Moderate; site sits within the Rosh Hanikra–Akhziv marine reserve area
- **Special Requirements**: Boat access via a Nahariya/Shavei Zion area dive club; IDF Navy coordination may apply this close to the Lebanese border

## Marine Life

Regional dive sources report large lobsters and octopus around the scattered wreckage, especially on night dives. No further site-specific species list was found — beyond this, general northern-Israeli-Mediterranean reef/wreck fauna should be assumed rather than a confirmed list.

## Dive Profile

A shallow wreck scattered across reef terrain rather than a single intact hull. Given the shallow depth (8 m) and scattered debris field, this suits divers comfortable navigating broken structure over reef rather than a classic single-hull wreck penetration dive.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Book through a Nahariya/Shavei Zion dive club (e.g. Putsker, Indigo) familiar with current access arrangements
- Good candidate for a night dive given reported lobster and octopus activity
- Pair with nearby Akhziv Canyon or the Turkish Ship wreck, both accessed from the same area

## Safety Considerations

Shallow depth reduces decompression risk but scattered wreck metal can have sharp edges. Confirm current access/military-zone requirements with a local operator before diving this close to the Lebanese border.
