## עמוד 5 (Amud 5 / Pole 5)

A shore-entry reef dive in the northernmost part of the Rosh Hanikra Nature Reserve, named for a numbered electrical pole (#5) that marks the access point — a genuine, locally documented site rather than a placeholder name.

## Overview

Amud 5 is a real, named shore-dive site in the northernmost section of the Rosh Hanikra reserve, next to the reserve's internal access road at electrical pole marker #5. Divers enter from the shore and typically swim west and then south of the entry point, staying clear of a closed military zone to the north. Local dive club guidance restricts independent diving here to daylight hours only — night entry is explicitly prohibited at this site. Reported marine life includes sea hares (Aplysia dactylomela), several wrasse species (Symphodus spp.), gobies, and blennies among the rocky reef terrain.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Northernmost Rosh Hanikra Nature Reserve, near access-road pole marker #5, northern Israel
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Maximum Depth**: 10 meters
- **Special Requirements**: Daylight diving only; stay south/west of the entry point, away from the closed military zone to the north
- **Typical Visibility**: Typical northern Israeli Mediterranean 5–12 metres
- **Current**: Generally mild; some surge possible near cliffs

## Marine Life

Species specifically reported at this site: sea hares (Aplysia dactylomela), wrasse (Symphodus species), gobies, and blennies, among typical northern-Mediterranean rocky-reef invertebrates.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Diving is daylight-only at this site — no evening/night entry permitted
- Head west then south from the entry point; avoid the military zone to the north
- Coordinate with a Rosh Hanikra-area dive club for current access rules
