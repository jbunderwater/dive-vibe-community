## ספינת הדיג נץ (אלישבע) — Fishing Vessel Netz (formerly Elisheva)

A well-known wrecked fishing boat resting partly on the sand at Bonim Beach (Dor–Bonim Nature Reserve), north of Caesarea — built at Israel Shipyards in Haifa in the 1960s under the name Elisheva and driven ashore by a storm in 1970.

## Overview

According to local sources, the vessel was launched in the 1960s as the fishing boat Elisheva, built at Israel Shipyards in Haifa. On 17 March 1970 it left Ashdod port towing three motorboats bound for Haifa. A storm developed en route; a tow cable fouled the propeller, the engine stopped, and the boat — by then renamed Netz ("hawk") — was driven ashore on the sand strip north of the Dor–Bonim Nature Reserve, where its remains lie today.

The wreck sits in the surf zone rather than fully submerged offshore — multiple sources note that depending on sand movement and season, the hull is sometimes largely exposed on the beach and sometimes partly covered by the sea, a visible illustration of how much this stretch of coastline shifts over time. This makes it more of a shallow shore/surf dive and snorkel site than a deep wreck dive; treat any depth figure as approximate given how exposed the wreck can be.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Bonim Beach, north of the Dor–Bonim Nature Reserve, central Israeli Mediterranean coast (south of Haifa)
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Maximum Depth**: 10 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 5–10 metres (surf-zone conditions vary with sand movement)
- **Current**: Mild to moderate, surf-zone dependent

## Marine Life

No site-specific marine life reports were found for this wreck. The Dor–Bonim area generally supports typical Israeli-Mediterranean sandy/rocky-shore species, but no source confirms specific sightings at the wreck itself — treat any species list as regional background rather than site-specific.

## Dive Profile

A shallow, shore-accessible site suited to beginners and snorkelers as much as certified divers, given how exposed the wreck often is. Conditions and accessible depth vary significantly with recent storms and sand levels.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Check recent conditions locally — the wreck's exposure changes with the season and sand movement
- Good easy site for beginners, families, and snorkelers as well as divers
- Combine with a walk along Bonim/Dor beach, a popular coastal nature area

## Safety Considerations

Surf-zone entry can have surge; watch for sharp, corroded metal on the exposed hull. Suitable for Open Water-level divers in calm conditions.
