## Ermlund

Ermlund is a Norwegian freighter wreck resting approximately 1,000 yards north of Half Moon Caye at Lighthouse Reef Atoll, one of the few above-water visible wrecks in Belize.

## Overview

AmbergrisCaye.com's Lighthouse Reef diving page — the only site-specific source located for this wreck — states that the Ermlund lost power during a storm in 1971, was a total write-off, and was deposited on the reef by a large wave, coming to rest approximately 1,000 yards north of Half Moon Caye at Lighthouse Reef Atoll. That same source describes it as a Norwegian freighter of approximately 4,000 gross tons and 350-400 feet in length. This account is echoed by several travel and tour-operator pages, but on inspection those all appear to be repeating the same AmbergrisCaye.com description rather than independently corroborating it — no Wikipedia article, wrecksite/registry entry, Lloyd's Register record, or news archive for the Ermlund could be located. Per this destination's wreck-history standard (two independent sources required for vessel name, build date, length, tonnage, and cause), these specific figures — the 1971 date, the "Norwegian" nationality, the tonnage, and the exact length — should be treated as reported by a single source rather than independently confirmed.

What can be said with more confidence: a wreck locally known as the Ermlund sits visible above and below the waterline roughly 1,000 yards north of Half Moon Caye, and it has been colonized by reef life typical of Caribbean wreck sites (groupers, snappers, and sponge/coral encrustation are commonly reported at wrecks of this type in the area, though no source itemizes species specific to this wreck). Divers visiting Half Moon Caye often pair the Ermlund with the nearby Half Moon Wall in the same day.

Penetration diving into any wreck carries risk; interior exploration should only be undertaken by properly trained and equipped divers with appropriate certification.
