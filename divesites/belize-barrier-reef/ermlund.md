## Ermlund

Ermlund is a Norwegian freighter wreck resting approximately 1,000 yards north of Half Moon Caye at Lighthouse Reef Atoll, one of the few above-water visible wrecks in Belize.

## Overview

According to AmbergrisCaye.com's Lighthouse Reef diving page, the Ermlund lost power during a storm in 1971 and was driven onto the reef by a large wave, coming to rest approximately 1,000 yards north of Half Moon Caye at Lighthouse Reef Atoll — the largest of the wrecks in this part of the atoll. That same source describes it as a Norwegian freighter of roughly 4,000 gross tons and 350–400 feet in length. **This is currently a single-source claim**: no Wikipedia article, maritime registry, or second independent source has been located to cross-check the vessel's tonnage, exact length, or full name, so these figures should be treated as unconfirmed rather than established fact. The wreck has been colonized by reef life over more than five decades since the grounding. Divers visiting Half Moon Caye often pair the Ermlund with the nearby Half Moon Wall, combining wreck and wall diving at this remote atoll in a single day.

Penetration diving into any wreck carries risk; interior exploration should only be undertaken by properly trained and equipped divers with appropriate certification.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Lighthouse Reef Atoll, Belize (north of Half Moon Caye)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 25 meters
