## Half Moon Wall

Half Moon Wall is a dramatic drop-off on the southeast corner of Lighthouse Reef Atoll, widely cited by dive operators as one of the best wall dives in Belize.

## Overview

Half Moon Wall at Lighthouse Reef Atoll is widely regarded as one of the best wall dives in Belize. The reef crest is crowned with a thick forest of staghorn coral above a sandy shelf, PADI records a typical recreational maximum around 18 metres, while the wall face itself — draped in gorgonian sea fans and sponges — continues far deeper; broader Lighthouse Reef sources describe the site's full depth range as roughly 6–42 metres. Garden eels burrow in the sandy shelf, and PADI notes neck crabs camouflaged on the sea fans along with nudibranchs on the wall face.

Caribbean reef sharks, sea turtles, groupers, and spotted eagle rays are reported regularly by PADI's own site listing, and Scuba.com notes occasional white tip and bull sharks passing in deeper water off the wall. Manta rays are occasionally sighted at the sandy flats (per Scuba.com), though sources disagree on seasonality: a Dive The World guide to Half Moon Caye's seasonal marine life reports peak manta activity November through April at a location it calls "Manta Wall" within the Half Moon Caye area — it is not confirmed whether this refers to this exact site or a separate nearby wall, so treat the November–April season as regional rather than site-specific. Whale sharks are more reliably documented: the Belize Whale Shark Monitoring Program (supported by ECOMAR) and Dive The World both report whale sharks passing Lighthouse Reef during the April and May full-moon periods when snapper spawn draws them to the atoll.

## Marine Life

Caribbean reef sharks, sea turtles, groupers, and spotted eagle rays are the marine life most consistently reported at this site (PADI). Garden eels inhabit the sandy shelf at the top of the wall, and neck crabs and nudibranchs are found along the wall face. White tip and bull sharks are occasionally seen in deeper water off the wall (Scuba.com). Whale sharks pass through Lighthouse Reef in April and May during snapper spawn events (Dive The World, ECOMAR-supported monitoring program). Manta rays are occasionally sighted at the sandy flats, but their reported November–April peak season is documented for the broader Half Moon Caye area rather than confirmed for this specific site — confirm current manta activity with local operators before visiting specifically for that encounter.
