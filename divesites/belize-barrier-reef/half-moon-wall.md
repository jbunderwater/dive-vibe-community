## Half Moon Wall

Half Moon Wall is a wall dive site in Belize Barrier Reef, Caribbean.

## Overview

Half Moon Wall (also called Half Moon Caye Wall) at Lighthouse Reef Atoll is widely considered one of Belize's best wall dives. Divers typically begin on a sandy shelf and staghorn coral barrier around 9-15 meters before crossing gaps in the reef crest onto the open seaward wall, which drops sharply — sources describe the drop continuing to roughly 40-42 meters, with some divers nicknaming the site for its sense of a near-vertical abyss. PADI's own site listing records a shallower typical maximum of 18 meters, reflecting the depth most recreational, no-decompression dives on the site actually reach; the wall itself continues well past that. Currents are usually minimal to none, and visibility regularly exceeds 30 meters.

Hawksbill and green turtles graze sponges along the wall, Caribbean reef sharks patrol the drop-off, and spotted eagle rays and manta rays are reported by multiple operators, though none of the sources reviewed document a specific season for manta sightings at this site. Earlier drafts of this page stated that whale sharks pass Lighthouse Reef during April-May snapper-spawn aggregations; that claim has been removed — Belize's documented whale shark spawning aggregation is at Gladden Spit & Silk Cayes Marine Reserve near Placencia, roughly 150km south of Lighthouse Reef, and no source reviewed places whale sharks at Half Moon Wall.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Half Moon Caye, Lighthouse Reef Atoll, Belize
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters

## Marine Life

Hawksbill and green sea turtles graze sponges along the wall while Caribbean reef sharks patrol the outer edge, and spotted eagle rays and manta rays are regularly reported at the drop-off by multiple dive-site sources. Garden eels sway from the sandy areas at the wall's base, and grouper, angelfish, butterflyfish, and reef fish are common along the coral buttresses. No source reviewed documents whale sharks or a specific manta-ray season at this site — treat any seasonal claims with caution until confirmed with a local operator.
