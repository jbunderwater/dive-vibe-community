## M/V Transfer

M/V Transfer is a British cargo vessel that ran aground on Lighthouse Reef Atoll in 2005, now a developing artificial reef in Belize's most remote offshore atoll.

## Overview

The M/V Transfer was a 225-foot British cargo vessel that ran aground on Lighthouse Reef Atoll at around 11:30pm on January 6, 2005 — roughly 4.2 miles south of Sandbore Caye — after a faulty autopilot directed the ship into the reef at night. This account (grounding date, cause, and length) is corroborated independently by both 7 News Belize and AmbergrisCaye.com's photo record of the wreck. When Hurricane Emily subsequently passed through the Caribbean, storm surge pushed additional sections of the hull further onto the shallow reef structure — 7 News Belize reports that only about a third of the vessel's length was initially aground, growing to roughly two-thirds after the hurricane. The owner of the vessel was identified as the Ariane Shipping Company in 7 News Belize reporting; this ownership detail appears in a single source and has not been independently confirmed. Belize's Department of the Environment, Port Authority, and Coast Guard have at various points discussed salvage options, including cutting the hull apart for scrap. No source reviewed for this site describes the specific marine life colonizing the wreck, so no species claims are made here.

The remote location at Lighthouse Reef means fewer divers than mainland Belize sites, offering a quieter exploratory dive in waters with visibility regularly reaching 20–40 metres.

Penetration diving into any wreck carries risk; interior exploration should only be undertaken by properly trained and equipped divers.
