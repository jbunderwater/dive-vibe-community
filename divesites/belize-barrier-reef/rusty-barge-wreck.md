## Rusty Barge Wreck

Locally known as Changa's Wreck, this is the remains of an old barge lying just inside the reef near Mata Cut, a few miles north of Punta Arena Canyons, in less than 3 metres (10 feet) of water.

## Overview

AmbergrisCaye.com describes the remains of an old barge — sometimes called Changa's Wreck — lying just inside the reef at Mata Cut in less than 10 feet (about 3 metres) of water, which is shallower than this destination's prior recorded depth of 8 metres; the depth has been corrected to reflect the sourced figure. A variety of soft corals and encrusting sponges decorate the barge's frame, and the rusted-out hull is a hangout for a small school of snappers. The site is popular for combining fish and wreck photography, with the source noting especially dramatic scenes when midday sun sends shafts of light through holes in the hull. The wreck rests just behind the reef but within the cut, so even on calm days incoming ocean swells create mild currents and wave surge; conditions worsen when prevailing northeasterly winds are strong. No named vessel history, build date, or sinking cause has been confirmed for this structure — it is an unnamed, undated abandoned barge, and no source reviewed identifies specific fish species beyond the snappers already noted.
