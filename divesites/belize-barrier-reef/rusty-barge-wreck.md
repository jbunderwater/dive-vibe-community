## Overview

**Correction (2026-07-18 re-audit):** This site's coordinates (17.5003N, -88.0870W) place it roughly 12km east of Belize City, in the vicinity of the Drowned Cayes — not off Ambergris Caye/San Pedro. On inspection, the vivid description previously used here — soft corals, encrusting sponges, a resident school of snappers — matches those sources' description of a different, named wreck ("Changa's Wreck") near San Pedro, not this location.
