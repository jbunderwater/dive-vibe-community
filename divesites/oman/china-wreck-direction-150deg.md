## China Wreck (direction 150deg)

China Wreck is a wreck dive site in the Mirbat area of Oman's Dhofar region.

## Overview

The China Wreck is a wreck dive site lying along the Mirbat coastline in Oman's Dhofar region, accessible from Salalah operators. The directional label "150deg" in the site name is a navigational bearing from shore used to locate the wreck. The wreck lies at depths of up to 25 metres and is reached by boat, operated seasonally from October to May.

**Vessel identity — sources conflict, no Wikipedia article exists for this vessel under either spelling.** Two independent Omani news outlets, the Oman Observer (2021) and Muscat Daily (2021), report that Oman's Ministry of Heritage and Tourism completed a formal documentation survey of a "128-year-old" wreck off Mirbat, identified as the **Kiva** — a vessel that departed Bombay bound for Mecca in **1893** with approximately 979 pilgrims aboard, lost to a fire that broke out in the cargo hold. Dive operators in the region have subsequently linked this surveyed wreck to the China Wreck site.

Separately, several tour-booking listings (GetYourGuide, Expedia, and syndicated reprints) advertise a snorkeling excursion to a "**Kheva** Wreck" that sank in **1874** after a fire en route from Bombay to **Jeddah** — not Mecca — and rests in only **3–5 metres** of water, shallow enough for snorkellers without scuba gear.

"Kiva" and "Kheva" are widely treated online as the same vessel (a name-spelling variant), but the sources disagree on the sinking year (1893 vs. 1874) and destination (Mecca vs. Jeddah). More significantly, the **depth doesn't match**: this dive site is a 25-metre boat-accessible wreck, while the "Kheva Wreck" tourism product describes a 3–5 metre snorkel-depth wreck. This could mean two distinct wrecks have been conflated under similar names, or that a single debris field spans shallow-to-deep sections — the available sources do not resolve which. No primary maritime database (Lloyd's Register, Wrecksite.eu) entry was found for either name.

**Conflict note:** One Salalah operator describes the site only as "the remains of a former steam-powered vessel scattered over approximately 80 metres in a north-south direction" without naming the Kiva or Kheva. The name "China Wreck" may alternatively reflect porcelain or china cargo found among the debris. Given the unresolved year, destination, and depth conflicts, treat the vessel's precise identity, build date, tonnage, and exact sinking date as **unverified** — the only points corroborated across sources are that it was a fire-lost, Bombay-departure pilgrim vessel from the late 19th century.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Mirbat area, Dhofar region, Oman (approx. 70km from Salalah)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 25 meters
- **Season**: October to May (closed during khareef monsoon)
- **Vessel Identity**: Unverified — "Kiva" (1893, per 2021 Ministry survey) or "Kheva" (1874, per tour listings); sources conflict on year, destination, and depth. No Lloyd's Register or Wrecksite.eu record found.
