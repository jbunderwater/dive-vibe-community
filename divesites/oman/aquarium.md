## Aquarium

Aquarium is a reef dive site in the Mirbat area of Oman's Dhofar region.

## Overview

Aquarium is a coral reef site in the Mirbat area, approximately 70 kilometres from Salalah in Oman's Dhofar region, and one of three named shore/boat dive sites in the Mirbat cluster alongside Eagle Bay and China Wreck. The abundance of sunlight at this shallow depth favours coral growth, producing coral gardens that give the site its name.

Sources describing the Mirbat trio collectively report nudibranchs, flatworms, shrimp and other crustaceans, clownfish, octopus, morays, and stonefish among the coral, with rays, turtles, and barracudas also seen in the area. These observations are reported for the Mirbat site cluster as a whole rather than confirmed exclusively for Aquarium.

Aquarium is boat-accessible and operated seasonally from October to May.

**Note:** OSM originally classified this site as a wall dive. Multiple sources from Salalah operators describe it as a coral reef/garden site rather than a vertical wall. Site type has been corrected to reef.

**Correction (2026-08-16 re-audit):** An earlier version of this page described "hundreds of shrimp gobies" and "some of the largest moray eels in the region" at this site. That text was traced to a source describing a *different* dive site also named "Aquarium" in the Hallaniyat Islands — a remote location visited weekly by a single liveaboard, unrelated to this Mirbat shore site. The misattributed species list has been removed and replaced with cluster-level observations that are actually sourced to the Mirbat area (see above).

## Site Information

- **Location**: Mirbat area, Dhofar region, Oman (approx. 70km from Salalah)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 24 meters
- **Season**: October to May (closed during khareef monsoon)
