## Lighthouse Reef

Lighthouse Reef (also logged as Tanjung Menjangan or Lighthouse Bay) is a sheltered muck and coral-garden site in a small bay on the northern side of Sangeang Island, about 1.5 km west of the island's lighthouse.

## Overview

McFadyen's log describes a bay with a dry creek at its head, black sand bottom and coral reefs to the west and east. The dive started on sand at about 8 m, worked down to about 29 m, then ascended through the northern reef and shallow bommies; visibility was 30 m and water about 27 C. Starfish.ch describes a sheltered site with black sand and a colourful coral garden, good for muck diving.

Only two sources describe the site; ScubaBoard's database confirms name and GPS only. Not marked validated. The ScubaBoard database also lists a different "Lighthouse Reef" in the Komodo table.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Small bay, northern side of Sangeang Island, east Sumbawa
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Muck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 29 meters

## Marine Life

McFadyen logged Indian walking fish (frogfish), ornate ghost pipefish, dancing shrimp under anemones, pygmy seahorses on gorgonians and many nudibranch species. Starfish.ch lists nudibranchs, shrimps, crabs including boxer crabs, frogfish, pipefish and flame file shells.
