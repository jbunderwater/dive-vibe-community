## Lighthouse Reef

Sheltered muck and macro diving site in a bay on the northeast side of Sangeang Island, also referred to as Tanjung Menjangan (Lighthouse Bay) by some operators. Black volcanic sand slopes mixed with colorful coral garden on both the west and east sides of the bay. Premier critter diving with frogfish, nudibranchs, boxer crabs, pipefishes, pygmy seahorses, cuttlefish, and flame file shells with their signature electric-flash light displays. Calm conditions with minimal current make it suitable for less experienced divers compared to other Sangeang sites.

A first-hand dive log (Michael McFadyen) puts the entry on sand at around 8 m with a maximum depth of about 29 m and a shallow safety-stop area near 4 m, slightly deeper than the 25 m figure commonly quoted elsewhere — both are noted here since they fall within a plausible range for the same site. That account also confirms ornate ghost pipefish, dancing shrimp under anemones, pygmy seahorses on gorgonians, and multiple named nudibranch species (including Co's chromodoris and Phyllidia species), plus visibility around 30 m and water temperature near 27°C.

## Site Information

- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Muck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 25 meters
- **Visibility**: ~30 metres
- **Water Temperature**: ~27°C
