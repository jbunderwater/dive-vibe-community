## Bonto Reef

Bonto Reef is a macro dive site on Sangeang Island, near the settlement spelled Bonto, Bontoh or Bantoh.

## Overview

Liveaboard Indonesia describes Bonto Reef as a Sangeang macro site on the northeast side. Starfish.ch lists Bantoh as a fishing village on Sangeang's west side described as a good night dive site, and ScubaBoard's GPS database places Bonto Reef at roughly 8 12.6 S, 118 59.97 E. These sources differ on which side of the island is meant. No source found gives a depth, type or difficulty for the site.

Not marked validated: only one source describes the site.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Sangeang Island, east Sumbawa (side of island disputed: northeast per Liveaboard Indonesia; Bantoh village is on the west per starfish.ch)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 25 meters

## Marine Life

Liveaboard Indonesia reports ribbon eels, frogfish, ghost pipefish, mantis shrimp and several nudibranch species. No other source confirms these.
