## Rollercoaster

Rollercoaster (also known locally as "Tondok Rasa") is an advanced current dive at the northwest tip of Gili Banta (Banta Island), an uninhabited volcanic island situated between east Sumbawa and Komodo National Park. The site takes its name from the undulating underwater topography and the unpredictable push of vertical and horizontal currents.

## Overview

Rollercoaster sits at the most exposed, current-swept corner of Gili Banta. The site features undulating walls, steep slopes, and large rock formations with a deep canyon between them; reported maximum depth varies by source, from around 30 m on the upper slopes to as much as 45 m in the canyon. Both vertical and horizontal currents sweep the site, bringing plankton-rich water from the Sape Strait, and can shift intensity without warning mid-dive. The cold upwellings from Flores mean temperatures can drop noticeably compared to the rest of Sumbawa — an extra exposure layer is recommended. The canyon and rock formations attract a variety of sharks including grey reefs, whitetips, and blacktips, as well as schooling pelagics: giant trevally, big-eyed jacks, barracuda, and tuna. The strong, variable currents require Advanced certification, strong buoyancy control, and experience with negative entries. Lanyards for camera rigs are advisable. Day trips depart from Kalimaya Dive Resort (approximately 40 minutes by speedboat) or by liveaboard. Best season April to November.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Northwest tip of Gili Banta, Sumbawa / Komodo border
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters
- **Currents**: Strong — vertical and horizontal; cold upwellings possible
- **Best Season**: April–November

## Marine Life

Grey reef sharks, whitetip sharks, blacktip sharks, giant trevally, big-eyed jacks, yellowfin tuna, barracuda.

## Dive Profile

Negative entry is common given currents. Follow the undulating walls and slopes, using rock formations for shelter when needed. Depths to 30 m; pelagic action often in mid-water above the reef. Hold camera rigs securely in variable current.

## Safety Notes

Cold upwellings can surprise divers — plan for possible thermoclines and carry adequate exposure protection. Strong current requires current hook or reef hook readiness. Advanced divers only.
