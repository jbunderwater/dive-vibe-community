## Rollercoaster

Rollercoaster is an advanced drift dive at the northwest tip of Gili Banta, an uninhabited volcanic island between east Sumbawa and Komodo National Park.

## Overview

Diver's Paradise Komodo calls it an advanced drift dive with strong, variable currents that "can change intensity multiple times during a single dive" and can accelerate with little warning, requiring strong buoyancy control and current experience. Neptune Liveaboards calls it a "current-swept drift dive", giving 30-40 m on the northwest side. The ScubaBoard Gili Banta report describes the same area (as Tanduk Rusa, Roller Coaster or Highway to Heaven) as steep slopes to 40 m with large rock formations, deep canyons and fierce currents. This database previously recorded the site as a reef at 30 m; the type is now drift and the depth 40 m on the strength of those sources.

**Naming note**: ScubaBoard treats Roller Coaster and Tanduk Rusa as the same site, but this database keeps two entries about 7 km apart (the ScubaBoard GPS list gives Rollercoaster at 8 23.6 S, 119 16.5 E, which matches this entry). The overlap is unresolved.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Northwest tip of Gili Banta, Sumbawa / Komodo border
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Drift dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters
- **Currents**: Strong and variable

## Marine Life

Diver's Paradise Komodo: reef sharks, giant trevally, barracuda, tuna, fusiliers, and eagle and mobula rays. Neptune Liveaboards: coral trout, giant trevally, dogtooth tuna and occasional hammerheads on the northwest side; anthias, possible mantas, frogfish and nudibranchs on the northeast side. ScubaBoard (as Tanduk Rusa / Roller Coaster): grey reef, whitetip and blacktip sharks and two barracuda species.

## Safety Notes

Strong, unpredictable current; advanced divers with drift experience only. Secure camera rigs.
