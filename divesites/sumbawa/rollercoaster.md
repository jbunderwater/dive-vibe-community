## Rollercoaster

Rollercoaster is an advanced current-swept dive at the northern end of Gili Banta, an uninhabited volcanic island between east Sumbawa and Komodo National Park.

**Naming and duplicate flag**: Emperor Divers lists "Roller coaster a.k.a. Tanduk Rusa", ScubaBoard lists "Tanduk Rusa (Roller Coaster)", and Asia Dive Site's "Tanjung Rasa" (Highway to Heaven) matches the same description. This entry is probably a duplicate of the Tanduk Rusa entry (about 7 km apart in our data) and should be reconciled against GPS data.

## Overview

Diver's Paradise Komodo calls it strictly an advanced drift dive site, with strong and variable current that can change intensity several times in one dive. Emperor Divers and ScubaBoard describe undulating walls and steep slopes to 40 m, large rock formations and deep canyons, with both vertical and horizontal currents.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Northern end of Gili Banta, between Komodo National Park and Sumbawa
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters
- **Currents**: Strong and variable

## Marine Life

Grey reef, whitetip and blacktip sharks, giant trevally, big-eyed jacks and tuna are reported by Emperor Divers, with sharks also reported by ScubaBoard. Diver's Paradise Komodo reports reef sharks, giant trevally, barracuda, tuna, fusiliers and eagle and mobula rays. Emperor Divers reports blue ribbon eels on the sloping reef to the west.

## Safety Notes

Operators rate this as an advanced drift dive requiring strong buoyancy control and current experience.
