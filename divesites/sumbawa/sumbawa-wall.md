## Sumbawa Wall

Sumbawa Wall is a wall dive on the north coast of Sumbawa, near Satonda Island by recorded coordinates.

## Overview

Maleo Moyo Scuba Divers (scubadivemoyo.com) names "the stunning Sumbawa Wall" and describes a wall dive with a shallow plateau of healthy corals used for the safety stop. No depth was given. dive-the-world.com and Emperor Divers do not list the site. The 40 m depth in our data is unconfirmed.

## Site Information

- **Location**: North coast Sumbawa, near Satonda Island
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters
