## Tikno Reef

Tikno Reef is a macro dive site off a black sand and rocky beach on the northwest side of Sangeang Island.

## Overview

McFadyen's log describes a slope starting at about 10 m that falls to about 30 m, then a sharp drop-off to perhaps 40 m or more; the dive was dropped on sand at about 8 m and reached 31 m, with visibility of about 25 m and water of about 27 C. Asia Dive Site instead describes a macro site at about 18 m near Black Magic, breaking up over dark volcanic ash and sandy patches. ScubaBoard's database confirms name and GPS only.

**Depth conflict**: 18 m (Asia Dive Site) versus about 31 m with a deeper drop-off (McFadyen). The database uses 31 m and Advanced. The sub-30 m dives above the drop-off are not technical; the drop-off beyond 40 m is outside recreational limits. Not marked validated (two descriptive sources).

## Site Information

- **Location**: Northwest side of Sangeang Island, east Sumbawa
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Muck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 31 meters

## Marine Life

McFadyen logged orangutan crabs in anemones, hawkfish among gorgonians, leaf fish, firefish, ornate ghost pipefish, mantis shrimp, gobies on sea whips and several nudibranchs, with barrel sponges, black coral and sea whips on the reef. Asia Dive Site names no species for this site.
