## Satonda Reef

Satonda Reef refers to the diving around Satonda Island, a small volcanic crater island north of Sumbawa and west of Moyo Island. Wikipedia reports it was designated a Marine Nature Park (TWAL) in 1999 and that it holds a saltwater crater lake.

## Overview

Asia Dive Site describes a sheltered bay with a sandy bottom and sparse coral patches, suited to muck and macro diving, at depths of 5-25 m with weak currents. Dive The World and Emperor Divers also describe a sheltered sandy bay suited to muck, critter and night dives. Neptune Liveaboards notes night dives and highlights pygmy seahorses for macro divers.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Satonda Island, north of Sumbawa
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Muck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 25 meters
- **Currents**: Weak (Asia Dive Site)

## Marine Life

Clown frogfish (about an inch, bright yellow with dark red patches, often mistaken for a sponge), scorpion leaffish, ghost pipefish and boxer crab are reported by Asia Dive Site. Dive The World reports clown frogfish, crinoids, polyclad flatworms, arrow crabs and banded boxer shrimp. Neptune Liveaboards names pygmy seahorses, which no other source confirms; treat as single-source.
