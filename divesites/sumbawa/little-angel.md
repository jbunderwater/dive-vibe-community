## Little Angel

Little Angel is a reef site on the west coast of Moyo Island close to Angel Reef. Sources disagree on its shape, so its wall classification is unresolved.

## Overview

Three sources name Little Angel but describe it differently. Emperor Divers calls it a smaller reef slightly east of Angel Reef, a submerged plateau at about 5-15 m with coral bommies and sandy slopes. A Perth Scuba trip report from a Mermaid II liveaboard describes a "stunning coral wall" (no depth given). A ScubaBoard poster lists it only as a reef dive alongside Angel Reef and Angel Ridge. This database records a 35 m wall, which none of these sources confirm; it has not been changed because the sources conflict. Treat the type and depth as unresolved.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Moyo Island, west coast, Sumbawa
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 35 meters

## Marine Life

One trip report (Perth Scuba) logged clownfish guarding eggs, a whitetip reef shark, a turtle, longnose butterflyfish and a large school of rainbow runners on its Little Angel dive. Emperor Divers describes only "colorful marine life". These are single-dive observations, not a species list for the site.
