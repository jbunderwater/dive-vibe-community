## Tanduk Rusa

Tanduk Rusa ("Deer Antler") is an advanced dive at the northern tip of Gili Banta, an uninhabited volcanic island off northwest Sumbawa. A Dive Happy page states that Banta is a few miles from Komodo National Park and outside its boundaries.

**Naming and duplicate flag**: Emperor Divers and ScubaBoard use "Tanduk Rusa" and "Roller Coaster" for the same site (ScubaBoard also gives "Highway to Heaven"), and Asia Dive Site describes the same slopes-to-40 m, current-swept peninsula as "Tanjung Rasa". This database lists Tanduk Rusa and Rollercoaster as separate entries about 7 km apart; they are probably one site and should be reconciled against GPS data before further edits.

## Overview

ScubaBoard describes steep slopes to 40 m running southwest from the northernmost peninsula of Banta, with strong vertical and horizontal currents, large rock formations and deep canyons. Emperor Divers describes undulating walls and steep slopes to 40 m. Petites Bulles d'Ailleurs describes a small shallow promontory at about 12 m, sheltered from the surrounding current, used as a manta cleaning station; this is a single-source report and the guides there switch to a more protected site when conditions are poor.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Northern tip of Gili Banta, outside Komodo National Park per Dive Happy
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters
- **Currents**: Strong, vertical and horizontal

## Marine Life

Grey reef, whitetip and blacktip sharks are reported by ScubaBoard, Emperor Divers and Asia Dive Site, with barracuda (ScubaBoard, Asia Dive Site), giant trevally, big-eyed jacks, tuna and blue ribbon eels on the sloping reef (Emperor Divers). Manta rays and cleaner wrasses at the 12 m promontory are reported by Petites Bulles d'Ailleurs only.
