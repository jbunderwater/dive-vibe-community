## Tanduk Rusa

Tanduk Rusa ("Deer Antler") is an advanced current dive at the northern tip of Gili Banta, an uninhabited volcanic island off the northwest coast of Sumbawa, with a shallow promontory used by manta rays as a cleaning station.

## Overview

A Petites Bulles d'Ailleurs trip report describes a small underwater promontory at about 12 m, sheltered from the furious currents around it, where manta rays hover over coral spurs while cleaner wrasses remove parasites. A ScubaBoard trip report (Gili Banta, Part II) describes Tanduk Rusa, also called Roller Coaster and Highway to Heaven, as running southwest from Banta's northernmost peninsula, with steep slopes to 40 m swept by vertical and horizontal currents, large rock formations and deep canyons, and fierce currents.

**Naming and location conflict**: ScubaBoard treats Tanduk Rusa, Roller Coaster and Highway to Heaven as one site. Asia Dive Site describes the same description under "Tanjung Rasa (Highway to Heaven)". This database lists Tanduk Rusa and Rollercoaster as separate sites about 7 km apart, and its Tanduk Rusa coordinates (-8.46, 119.27) sit south of the K2 and Rollercoaster coordinates from the ScubaBoard GPS list, while the sources place Tanduk Rusa on the island's northernmost peninsula. The coordinates and the duplicate entry need a GPS reconciliation. Divehappy states Gili Banta is outside the Komodo National Park boundary.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Northern tip of Gili Banta (sources); outside the Komodo National Park boundary per Divehappy
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters
- **Currents**: Strong, vertical and horizontal

## Marine Life

Manta rays and cleaner wrasses at the promontory (Petites Bulles d'Ailleurs). The ScubaBoard report for this site (as Tanduk Rusa / Roller Coaster) names grey reef, whitetip and blacktip sharks and two barracuda species.
