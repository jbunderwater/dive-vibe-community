## K2

K2 is a submerged seamount on the northeast side of Gili Banta (Banta Island), close to the boundary of Komodo National Park. The seamount peaks at approximately 7 metres below the surface and descends to 25–40 metres, forming a coral garden that attracts large pelagics on the current-swept sides.

## Overview

K2 is a dive site roughly 1000 m south of Gili Banta's northwest tip, distinct from the exposed, current-raked conditions of nearby Rollercoaster. Sources describe the topography somewhat differently — this page previously described a seamount cresting at 7 m, while Dive Indonesia's dedicated page instead describes steep sandy slopes, scattered coral heads, and a deep wall running from about 5 to 30 m — both accounts agree on moderate-to-strong currents concentrating pelagic species: barracuda, dogtooth tuna, trevally, fusiliers, and reef sharks, plus occasional manta and eagle rays. The reef sections support a dense community of soft and hard corals, angelfish, butterflyfish, and parrotfish. Access by liveaboard or day trip from Kalimaya Dive Resort (~40 minutes) or other east Sumbawa operators. Best season April to November.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Northeast of Gili Banta, Sumbawa / Komodo border
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters
- **Currents**: Moderate to strong on current-facing sides
- **Best Season**: April–November

## Marine Life

Barracuda, dogtooth tuna, Spanish mackerel, sharks, angelfish, butterflyfish, parrotfish, fusiliers, soft and hard corals.

## Dive Profile

Ascend to the plateau at 7 m and explore the coral gardens before dropping to the flanks. Pelagics are concentrated on the upcurrent side. Max depth 25 m for intermediate; deeper possible on flanks.
