## K2

K2 is a dive site near Gili Banta. Sources conflict on where and what it is.

## Overview

ScubaBoard (Gili Banta Part II) places K2 inside a bay with a submerged ridge running north: a gentle slope with bommies and hard corals on the west side, and a mini wall with sea fans and black coral bushes on the northern point and east side. Scuba Hank NYC places K2 in the northern section of Komodo National Park near Gili Banta, at 10-30 m with moderate to strong, direction-changing current, recommended for intermediate to advanced divers.

**Conflict flagged**: the two sources differ on location and character, and may describe different sites or the same one. Neither Asia Dive Site nor Emperor Divers lists K2. Only two sources were found; not marked validated. The database depth is 25 m and the location is unconfirmed.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Near Gili Banta (details disputed, see above)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 25 meters
- **Currents**: Moderate to strong (Scuba Hank NYC)

## Marine Life

Scuba Hank NYC reports reef manta rays, white-tip reef sharks, Napoleon wrasse, giant trevally, red snapper, fusiliers, green turtles, moray eels, scorpionfish, nudibranchs, cleaner shrimp and reef crabs. ScubaBoard reports juvenile sweetlips, barramundi, midnight snapper, giant frogfish, leaf scorpionfish, ribbon eels and nudibranchs in the shallows. Because the two sources may describe different sites, these lists are not combined.
