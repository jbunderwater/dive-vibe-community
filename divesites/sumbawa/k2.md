## K2

K2 is a dive site on or near Gili Banta (Banta Island). Sources agree on little beyond a current-exposed coral area with a manta presence reported by one source.

## Overview

Sources disagree on both location and layout. Scuba Hank NYC places K2 near Gili Banta in "the northern section of Komodo National Park" and gives 10-30 m with moderate to strong, direction-changing currents; Divehappy states Gili Banta lies outside the park boundary. ScubaBoard's Gili Banta report places K2 inside the bay along a submerged ridge running north, with a gentle bommie-and-hard-coral slope on the west side and a mini wall of sea fans and black coral bushes on the north point and east side. ScubaBoard's Komodo Dancer logs describe the North Bay K2 with excellent visibility, coral slopes, fan-covered walls, a shallow coral garden and soft coral pinnacles. The earlier "vast coral garden" phrasing and the 50 m to 10 m visibility swing are not in the pages re-fetched; the "7 m summit" figure remains unconfirmed. Depth of 25 m in this database sits within the 10-30 m reported.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Gili Banta, North Bay (park membership disputed between sources)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 25 meters
- **Currents**: Moderate to strong, often changing direction (Scuba Hank NYC)

## Marine Life

Scuba Hank NYC: reef manta rays, white-tip reef sharks, Napoleon wrasse, giant trevally, red snapper schools, fusiliers, green turtles, moray eels, scorpionfish, nudibranchs, cleaner shrimp. ScubaBoard Gili Banta report: juvenile sweetlips, barramundi, midnight snapper, giant frogfish, leaf scorpionfish, ribbon eels, nudibranchs. ScubaBoard Komodo Dancer logs: sea cucumbers, nembrotha nudibranchs and big cuttlefish. Removed: barracuda, dogtooth tuna, Spanish mackerel, grey reef sharks, horse-eye jacks and crocodile needlefish (not found in the fetched pages for K2).
