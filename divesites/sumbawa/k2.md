## K2

K2 is a dive site on Gili Banta (Banta Island), just outside the boundary of Komodo National Park per most sources (though it's commonly marketed as part of the "Komodo diving" region). Sources disagree on its exact topography and depth: some describe it as a seamount/pinnacle with a shallow plateau, while ScubaBoard trip reports describe it more simply as a shallow, current-swept coral garden. A specific "7 m summit" figure could not be confirmed by any source found during this QA pass — depth ranges cited are closer to 10–30 m — so that number should be treated as unverified.

## Overview

K2 sits on Gili Banta and is reached by liveaboards and day-trip operators covering the island. A dedicated dive-site write-up describes a depth range of roughly 10–30 m with moderate to strong, direction-shifting currents, and calls out reef manta rays at cleaning stations, white-tip reef sharks, Napoleon wrasse, giant trevally, schooling red snapper and fusiliers, turtles, moray eels, and nudibranchs. Multiple ScubaBoard trip reports (from liveaboards transiting the area) independently describe K2 as "a vast coral garden in shallow water with lots of light and great visibility," though one notes visibility swinging from 50 m down to 10 m mid-dive, and grey/white-tip reef sharks, giant trevally, schooling horse-eye jacks and snapper, Napoleon wrasse, and crocodile needlefish. Barracuda, dogtooth tuna, Spanish mackerel, and general coral-garden fish (angelfish, butterflyfish, parrotfish) are consistent with these reports though not verbatim-confirmed by name in every source. Given the shallow-water, coral-garden character reported across sources, this is treated here as a reef site rather than a true offshore seamount/pinnacle — though the "seamount" description in some sources means a pinnacle classification is plausible and worth reconsidering in a future pass. Best season April to November.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Northeast of Gili Banta, Sumbawa / Komodo border
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 25 meters
- **Currents**: Moderate to strong, direction can shift with tide
- **Best Season**: April–November

## Marine Life

Confirmed for this site: reef manta rays at cleaning stations, white-tip reef sharks, grey reef sharks, giant trevally, Napoleon wrasse, schooling snapper (red snapper / horse-eye jacks per different reports), fusiliers, turtles, moray eels, nudibranchs, crocodile needlefish. Barracuda, dogtooth tuna, Spanish mackerel, angelfish, butterflyfish, and parrotfish are plausible for this coral-garden habitat but are carried over from a prior pass without a site-specific citation confirming them at K2 by name — treat with lower confidence than the list above.

## Dive Profile

Explore the shallow coral-garden plateau and drop toward the deeper flanks as conditions and certification allow; visibility has been reported to swing from as much as 50 m down to 10 m within a single dive. Pelagics tend to concentrate on the current-facing side.
