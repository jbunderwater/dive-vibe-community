## Senanus Island

A Saanich Inlet boat dive known specifically for large cloud sponge colonies in the 21-27 metre range, with a mooring buoy at the site.

## Overview

Senanus Island sits in Saanich Inlet, a fjord-like arm of water north of Victoria. Diver reports and the regional dive guide specifically describe Senanus Island as a cloud-sponge site: colonies at 21-27 metres (70-90 feet) large enough to "dwarf a buddy," with small rockfish or crabs visible peering out from openings in the sponge structure. A mooring buoy is reported at the site. Saanich Inlet is also known more broadly for periodic low-oxygen conditions at depth due to limited tidal exchange — divers should get current local guidance on safe depth limits from a Victoria-area shop before diving here.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Saanich Inlet, British Columbia, Canada
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 27 meters
- **Current**: Generally mild in the Inlet
- **Best Time**: Diving is described as good year-round, with especially clear visibility reported in winter

## Marine Life

The confirmed, site-specific feature at Senanus Island is its cloud sponge colonies at 21-27 metres — large enough that a sponge can visually dwarf a dive buddy, with small rockfish and crabs reported peeking out from openings in the sponge tissue. Beyond the cloud sponges and their resident small fish/crabs, no further site-specific marine life reports were found. Broader Saanich Inlet fauna (giant Pacific octopus, wolf eels, lingcod, nudibranchs, and winter six-gill shark sightings in the inlet's deep zones) is regionally documented but not confirmed specifically at Senanus Island in this research pass.

## Dive Profile

The site's signature feature — dense cloud sponge colonies — sits in the 21-27 metre range, so this is not a shallow dive despite Saanich Inlet's sheltered character. A mooring buoy is reported at the site for boat access. Given Saanich Inlet's known low-oxygen deep water in places, confirm current safe-depth guidance with a local operator before planning the dive.

## Entry and Exit

Boat access from Saanich Inlet launch points near Brentwood Bay. A mooring buoy is reported at the site.

## Tips and Recommendations

- The cloud sponge colonies at 21-27m are the primary reason to dive this site — bring a wide-angle lens
- Check current local guidance on safe depth limits in Saanich Inlet before the dive
- Winter is reported to offer particularly clear visibility for sponge photography

## Safety Considerations

Saanich Inlet is known for periodic low-oxygen conditions in its deeper water due to limited tidal exchange — get current guidance from a Victoria-area dive shop on safe depth limits for this specific site. Cold water (7-12°C typical for the inlet) requires drysuit protection. Boat traffic in the inlet includes ferries and private vessels; deploy an SMB before ascending.
