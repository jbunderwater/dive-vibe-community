# Caves

A network of well-illuminated caves and tunnels in Cape Greco National Park — exceptional visibility and swim-throughs up to 30 metres long.

## Overview

The Caves at Cape Greco National Park feature a network of natural underwater caves and tunnels carved into the lava rock cliffs. Caves reach lengths of up to 30 metres but remain well-illuminated with natural light entering through cracks and openings, making them accessible without specialist cave diving training. Maximum depth is approximately 15 metres. Visibility at this site is consistently among the best in Cyprus, typically 25–40 metres.

**Correction (2026-07-27 re-audit):** An earlier version of this entry named two specific features, "Horse Shoe Cave" and a "Blow Hole" exit. On re-checking all three cited sources (scubadivecyprus.com, underwater.journeys.cy, sirensdivecentre.com) directly, none of them use either name — scubadivecyprus.com describes the site simply as swim-through caves up to 30m with a 15m maximum depth, and underwater.journeys.cy names different features (The Cathedral, Love Bridge Cave, Blue Room) that belong to the neighbouring Cape Grecko Caves site, not this one. Those two names have been removed as unverified rather than repeated.

Shore entry requires a rocky giant stride from the cliff platforms. The site is part of the Cape Greco area that also includes the Cape Grecko Caves site a short distance away.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Cape Greco National Park, east Cyprus
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Cave/cavern
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 15 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 25–40 meters (exceptional)
- **Current**: Light
- **Best Time**: March to November

## Marine Life

Bream, damselfish, cornet fish, jacks, lionfish, groupers, octopus, moray eels, and sea turtles have been reported at the site. Bearded fire worms are present in the cave habitats.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Cave passages are naturally lit — torches useful for darker sections but not essential for the main passages
- Swim-through passages reach up to 30m long — plan air/gas accordingly
- Among the clearest water in Cyprus — 25–40m visibility typical
- Rocky shore entry — reef shoes and controlled giant stride recommended
