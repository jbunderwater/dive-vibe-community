# The Channel

A shore dive site on the Paphos west coast diving corridor — a rock wall and shallow gully reportedly used by amateur cave divers, though this has not been independently confirmed.

## Overview

The Channel is located on the Paphos west coast, within the Coral Bay dive site cluster between Sea Caves and the Amphitheatre area. A search-indexed extract of the Visit Pafos tourism site describes a large rock wall running parallel to the shore, with a natural gully or channel reaching 5–6 metres depth and two small caves offering easy access to amateur cave divers. This description could not be independently verified by directly fetching the source page (it returned no retrievable content), so it is reported here with that caveat rather than treated as confirmed. No other source consulted for the Paphos area (paphosguide.com, divingincyprus.net, Cyprus Diving Association) mentions this site by name.

Maximum depth is listed as 20 metres in OSM data, which is deeper than the 5–6m gully depth reported above — it's possible OSM's figure reflects a different or additional part of the site, or is simply a placeholder; this has not been resolved.
