## Uçak Batığı

Uçak Batığı (Airplane Wreck) is a wreck dive site near Bodrum, Turkey.

## Overview

Uçak Batığı ("Airplane Wreck") is a decommissioned Turkish Air Force Douglas C-47A Dakota, a twin-engine transport derived from the DC-3, sunk deliberately as an artificial reef off Karaada/Paçoz near Bodrum. Sualtı Gazetesi and Zentacle give the date as 1 July 2008 (sunk by BOSAD, the Bodrum underwater sports association); The Divers' Club gives 2007. Reported dimensions differ slightly: about 19 m long (Sualtı Gazetesi) or 19.57 m with a 28.96 m wingspan (Zentacle). No source consulted gives build date or tonnage, so none is stated here.

The wreck began in shallower water and later broke apart; Sualtı Gazetesi reports it split into two parts in 2010, and Zentacle attributes the breakup to winter storms and currents. Reported depths vary by source: Aquapro 17-32 m, The Divers' Club 17-34 m (engine and propeller ~17 m, cockpit ~23 m, fuselage ~34 m), Sualtı Gazetesi 16-28 m originally with the fuselage and tail now ~28-34 m, and Zentacle a maximum of about 25 m.

Sources disagree on difficulty and penetration. Aquapro calls the dive easy and states the body and tail are not penetrable except via a rear cabin door at 29 m; Zentacle rates it Intermediate and advises against any interior entry because the structure is unstable. Penetrating any wreck requires proper training and equipment; here, treat the interior as off-limits.

## Dive Profile

- **Entry**: Boat from Bodrum-area operators
- **Depth range**: roughly 16-34 m depending on section and source

## Marine Life

Aquapro reports groupers, tuna, lionfish, scorpionfish and many sea bream at this wreck. The other sources consulted name no species specific to this site.

## Safety

Lionfish and scorpionfish can sting if touched. The structure has continued to break down since 2010; dive with an operator who knows its current condition. Turkish rules cited by Zentacle require at least CMAS** / AOWD level for this depth.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Karaada/Paçoz Cove, Bodrum area, Muğla Province, Turkey (Aegean Sea)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 34 meters
