## Shark Sculpture

Shark Sculpture is a reef dive site at Güvercin Ada (Pigeon Island) near Kaş, Turkey.

## Overview

Güvercin Ada (Pigeon Island) is a boat-access site off Kaş whose shallows are described as full of life and calm enough for snorkelers and new or returning divers. A white marble shark sculpture, described as a remnant of past underwater art exhibitions that can startle first-time divers, gives the site its name. Deeper areas add an old, partly scattered wooden wreck at about 22 m and a sheet-metal wreck sunk as an artificial reef at about 28 m. A small reef at about 28 m carries many broken amphorae. Both operator pages note there is too much to cover in a single dive.

Sources differ slightly on depth: PADI lists a maximum depth of 30 m, while Nautilus Diving Kaş gives no overall maximum and cites features down to about 28 m. Neither page rates difficulty; shallow sections suit beginners, while the wrecks and 28 m reef call for deeper-certified divers. The sculptor, creation year and exact depth of the sculpture are not given by the sources consulted and are therefore not stated here.

## Marine Life

Reported at this site by the operator pages: rabbitfish and breams in the shallows, friendly striped groupers on the 28 m reef, and gobies with shrimp partners in the sand.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Güvercin Ada (Pigeon Island), near Kaş, Antalya Province, Turkey
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 28 meters
