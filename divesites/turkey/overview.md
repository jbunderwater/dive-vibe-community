## Turkey

Turkey's Mediterranean and Aegean coasts offer wall, reef and aircraft-wreck diving, with this database covering sites near Kaş and Bodrum.

## Description

This destination currently lists 4 dive sites: a reef and sculpture site at Güvercin Ada near Kaş, and three Bodrum-area sites (the Büyük Resif wall, the Aksona wall and the Uçak Batığı aircraft wreck). The only wreck listed, Uçak Batığı, is a retired Turkish Air Force C-47A Dakota scuttled by BOSAD as an artificial reef (1 July 2008 per most sources; one reports 2007), not a wartime loss. Regional water-temperature, season and cost figures were removed from this page because no source consulted in this pass supports them.

### Sites

- **Kaş**: Shark Sculpture (Güvercin Ada), shallow sculpture and reef with deeper wrecks, beginner-friendly in the shallows.
- **Bodrum**: Büyük Resif (wall, 34 m, currents), Aksona (wall, 30-35 m, ~50 min by boat), Uçak Batığı (C-47 wreck, sources report 17-34 m depending on section and year).

### Marine Life

Species are listed only on the individual site pages, where each is tied to a source reporting it at that site. No destination-wide species list is given.
