## Turkey

Turkey's Mediterranean and Aegean coasts offer world-class diving featuring an artificial-reef aircraft wreck, dramatic wall formations, vibrant reefs, and acclaimed dive destinations like Kaş and Bodrum.

## Description

Turkey's four sites in this database sit on two stretches of coast: Güvercin Ada (Pigeon Island) near Kaş on the Mediterranean, and the Bodrum area on the Aegean. They include a sculpture-and-wreck reef dive, two advanced walls (Büyük Resif and Aksona), and a deliberately scuttled C-47 Dakota aircraft wreck (Uçak Batığı). Regional fauna applies, but marine-life reports are given only on each site page where a source confirms sightings there.

### Dive Sites

- **Shark Sculpture** (Kaş): beginner-friendly shallows with a marble shark sculpture, a scattered wooden wreck at ~22 m and a sheet-metal artificial-reef wreck at ~28 m
- **Büyük Resif** (Bodrum): wall to 34 m, strong currents reported
- **Aksona** (Bodrum area): wall to 30-35 m, about 50 minutes by boat, archaeological finds reported
- **Uçak Batığı** (Bodrum): C-47A Dakota sunk in 2008 (one source: 2007); sections at roughly 16-34 m. Penetration requires proper training and equipment; sources advise against it.

No site-specific water temperature, visibility, season, or cost data were verified for this update; consult local operators.
