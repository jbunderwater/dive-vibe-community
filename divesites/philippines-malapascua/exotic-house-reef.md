## Exotic House Reef

Exotic House Reef is the protected house reef of Malapascua Exotic Island Dive Resort, a shallow muck-and-macro site built partly around sunken artificial structures.

## Overview

Exotic House Reef starts at around 5 meters and slopes gently to about 13 meters, within Exotic Resort's own protected area. Scattered coral and seagrass patches sit alongside deliberately placed artificial structures — coral-encrusted jeepneys, concrete blocks, and old wire frames — that have become a habitat for soft coral and macro life. The site received a Project AWARE Environmental Achievement Award in February 2008. Multiple sources recommend diving it both in daylight and at night, as different species are active at each.

## Site Information

- **Location**: In front of Malapascua Exotic Island Dive Resort, southern Malapascua Island, Philippines
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Muck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 13 meters

## Marine Life

Sources report frogfish, seahorses, trumpetfish, pipefish, starfish, nudibranchs, shrimp, crabs, octopus, and squid among the artificial structures and seagrass patches. Mandarin fish are reported at dusk in the rubble patches, similar to nearby Evo House Reef.
