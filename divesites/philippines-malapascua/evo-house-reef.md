## Evo House Reef

Evo House Reef is Evolution Diving Resort's house reef on Malapascua's southern coast, a short boat hop from the resort and one of the two site-specific locations on the island confirmed to host a resident mandarin fish population at dusk.

## Overview

The reef starts around 5 meters and slopes gently to about 15 meters. Evolution's own site describes divers regularly seeing pairs of crinoid squat lobsters and imperial shrimp, dozens of bobtail squid at dusk, coconut octopus and seahorses on nearly every dive, snake eels poking from the sand, stargazers, robust ghost pipefish, and several species of mantis shrimp. The house reef also hosts mandarin fish, which gather in patches of staghorn coral at around 6 meters for their dusk mating display.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Off Evolution Diving Resort, southern Malapascua, Philippines
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 15 meters

## Marine Life

Evolution Diving Resort's own site lists crinoid squat lobsters, imperial shrimp, bobtail squid (numerous at dusk), coconut octopus, seahorses, snake eels, stargazers, robust ghost pipefish, and multiple mantis shrimp species as regular sightings on their house reef, alongside the resident mandarin fish that display in staghorn coral patches around 6 meters at dusk. Species such as blue-ringed octopus that appeared in an earlier draft of this page were not confirmed by any source and have been removed.

## Dive Profile

An easy shallow dive well suited to a second or third dive of the day, or a dedicated dusk dive for the mandarin fish display — divers typically enter around 6pm as the sun sets, and are asked to keep torches off (or shielded) while watching the fish to avoid disturbing them.

## Entry and Exit

A short boat ride from Evolution Diving Resort; entry by giant stride.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Book the dusk mandarin fish dive specifically — timing around sunset is critical
- Keep torch light minimal and indirect during the mandarin fish display
- Daytime dives reward slow, careful searching for the resort's varied macro life

## Safety Considerations

Shallow depth (5-15m) keeps decompression risk low. Standard boat diving and night-diving precautions apply for the dusk dive.

## Photography

A strong macro site both day and night; the dusk mandarin fish display is the signature subject, requiring a fast lens and low-light technique.
