## Secret Wall

Secret Wall is a steep reef wall on Malapascua, described by one dive operator as plunging from around 12 meters to beyond 30 meters — a site for advanced and technical divers.

## Overview

Research for this page found only a single independent source describing Secret Wall specifically, so this description is intentionally brief. That source describes a wall dropping from roughly 12 meters to beyond 30 meters, with dramatic drop-offs and swirling currents that make conditions vary between dives.
