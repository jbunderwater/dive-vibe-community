## North Point

North Point is a coral-garden reef on Malapascua's exposed northern tip, where three independent dive-shop sources describe a macro-rich site rather than the deep advanced pinnacle this page previously claimed.

## Overview

This page previously classified North Point as an Advanced pinnacle dive to 35 meters with resident whitetip sharks, hammerhead sharks, and dogtooth tuna. That did not hold up under re-audit: three Malapascua dive-shop sources independently describe North Point as a reef or coral-garden site with canyons and small caverns, generally in the 6-27 meter range and rated Beginner to Intermediate. One broader third-party dive guide, by contrast, describes North Point as a more current-swept wall-and-slope site to 30 meters with schooling pelagics (barracuda, trevally, Napoleon wrasse, bumphead parrotfish, reef sharks, eagle rays). Rather than pick one version, both are noted here — it is plausible that North Point's character shifts significantly with current conditions, which several sources mention as often strong at this exposed point.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Northern tip of Malapascua Island, Philippines
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 27 meters
- **Current**: Often significant given the exposed northern position

## Marine Life

The consistent, source-backed list from local dive shops includes frogfish, scorpionfish, seahorses, nudibranchs, fire urchins, zebra crabs, whip corals, gobies, and juvenile boxfish. A separate third-party guide instead describes barracuda, trevally, Napoleon wrasse, bumphead parrotfish, reef sharks, and eagle rays — likely reflecting the site under stronger-current conditions. Earlier claims of resident hammerhead sharks, dogtooth tuna, and resident whitetip reef sharks are not supported by any of these sources and have been removed.
