## North Point

North Point, at the northern tip of Malapascua Island, is a current-exposed reef site whose reported depth range and difficulty vary significantly between sources.

## Overview

North Point sits at Malapascua's exposed northern tip. Sources genuinely disagree on its character: some describe a shallow (6-24m), beginner-friendly coral garden with soft corals and macro subjects; others describe a deeper (15-30m) wall-and-slope site with overhangs and small caverns, strong unpredictable currents, and pelagic activity, recommended for experienced divers only doing a negative entry. Rather than pick one account, both are noted here — actual conditions likely depend on which part of the point is dived and the day's current. The prior version of this page also attributed regular hammerhead shark sightings to North Point; no source found during this audit supports that claim. Hammerheads are reported by multiple sources only at Monad Shoal and Kimud Shoal, typically January to April.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Northern tip of Malapascua Island, Philippines
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 27 meters

## Marine Life

Shallower accounts report frogfish, fire urchins, zebra crabs, whip corals, gobies, juvenile boxfish, and nudibranchs. Deeper/current-exposed accounts report barracuda and trevally schools, Napoleon wrasse, bumphead parrotfish, reef sharks, and eagle rays. Divers should not expect all of these on a single dive.
