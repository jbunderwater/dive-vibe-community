## North Point

North Point is a sandy-and-coral site off Malapascua's northern tip, offering large coral heads and current-driven fish activity at a moderate 8-24 meter depth.

## Overview

North Point is described by multiple operators as a largely sandy and coral area undulating between about 8 and 24 meters, averaging around 22m. Current can change direction around the coral formations and ranges from light to strong depending on tide. It's rated suitable from Open Water level, with the caveat that current can pick up.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Northern tip of Malapascua Island, Philippines
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 24 meters
- **Current**: Variable, sometimes strong

## Marine Life

Large coral heads support nudibranchs and numerous crabs, with shrimp species more visible on night dives, and frogfish reported on the site (Kokay's Malapascua Diving). Evolution Diving Resort separately notes whip corals, gobies, and juvenile boxfish here. An earlier version of this page claimed hammerhead sharks at North Point — that is incorrect: Dive The World and other sources place hammerhead sightings at Kimud Shoal, a different site, and the claim has been removed.
