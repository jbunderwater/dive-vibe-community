## Lapus-Lapus

Lapus-Lapus is a reef and rock-formation site close to Malapascua, best known among local operators for its resident frogfish rather than schooling pelagics.

## Overview

Three independent Malapascua dive-shop sources describe Lapus-Lapus as a reef with rock formations, not a true pinnacle as this page previously claimed. It sits a short boat ride from Malapascua's resorts, in the channel area toward Gato Island, and is rated Beginner to Intermediate — accessible enough for less experienced divers while still offering worthwhile macro life.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Near Malapascua Island, Philippines (channel toward Gato Island)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 18 meters

## Marine Life

Sources consistently report giant and painted frogfish, mantis shrimp, cuttlefish, lionfish, scorpionfish, nudibranchs, cleaner shrimp, and glassfish. Earlier claims of schooling fusiliers, jacks, and barracuda, plus blue-spotted ribbontail rays, hawksbill turtles, and grey reef sharks, are not supported by any of these sources and have been removed.
