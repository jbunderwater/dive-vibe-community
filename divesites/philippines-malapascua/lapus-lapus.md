## Lapus-Lapus

Lapus-Lapus is a rock/coral pinnacle formation off northwest Malapascua Island, roughly a 10-15 minute boat ride from the island's dive resorts.

## Overview

Lapus-Lapus is described across multiple sources as rock formations and small canyons with a slopey pinnacle structure, in the 10-22 meter depth range. There is no fixed mooring line at the site, so it can turn into a light drift dive depending on current. Coral cover — both hard and soft — is reported as dense and in good condition.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Northwest of Malapascua Island, Philippines
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Pinnacle/seamount
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 18 meters

## Marine Life

Sources report frogfish (including giant and painted frogfish), nudibranchs, cuttlefish, cleaner shrimp, mantis shrimp, seahorses, flatworms, and juvenile sweetlips. An earlier version of this page also claimed blue-spotted ribbontail rays resting at the base of the pinnacle — no source found during this audit confirms that specific claim, and it has been removed.
