## Coral Garden Marine Protected Area

Coral Garden is a protected reef site known for strong currents that sustain a colorful soft-coral garden, with rock formations and small canyons offering shelter for macro life.

## Overview

Sources describe Coral Garden as a protected site where strong currents create favorable conditions for soft coral growth, with rock formations, small canyons, holes, and an overhang providing hiding spots. Divers are required to follow MPA rules explained in the dive briefing. Depth is one area of genuine source disagreement: general descriptions of the coral-garden feature cite a shallow slope to around 8-12 meters, while this OSM entry's original data describes a "deep perimeter" extending to 30 meters. Both figures are noted here rather than picking one, since no single source resolves the discrepancy.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Malapascua Island, Philippines
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 20 meters
- **Current**: Often moderate to strong — the defining feature of the site

## Marine Life

Sources describe huge sponges and sea fans as common, with pygmy seahorses reported "under perfect conditions," and rock formations/canyons sheltering nudibranchs, frogfish, flatworms, and other reef fish. Earlier claims of Napoleon wrasse, large grouper, resident sea turtles, and specific coral genera (table Acropora, brain coral, massive Porites) read as generic reef-conservation boilerplate with no site-specific source backing them, so they have been removed.
