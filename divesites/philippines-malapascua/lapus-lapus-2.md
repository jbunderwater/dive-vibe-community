## Lapus-Lapus 2

Lapus-Lapus 2 (also listed by dive shops as "Lapus II") sits beside the main Lapus-Lapus site off northwest Malapascua and transitions from a sloping reef into a small wall.

## Overview

Sources describe Lapus-Lapus 2 as beginning with a sloping reef at around 12 meters that turns into a small wall reaching roughly 20 meters, with a sandy bottom carrying black coral and sea fans sloping further to a maximum of about 30 meters. This is a structural correction from an earlier version of this page, which classified it as a pinnacle — no source uses that description for this specific site.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Northwest Malapascua Island, Philippines, adjacent to Lapus-Lapus
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters

## Marine Life

Sources report black coral and sea fans on the wall section, along with butterflyfish, damselfish, and anthias in the water column, and frogfish, nudibranchs, shrimp, and crabs among the coral growth.
