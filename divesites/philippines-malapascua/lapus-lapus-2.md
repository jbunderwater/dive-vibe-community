## Lapus-Lapus 2

Lapus-Lapus 2 (also called Lapus II) sits beside Lapus-Lapus off northwest Malapascua and is best described as a sloping reef that turns into a small wall, rather than a pinnacle.

## Overview

Multiple sources place Lapus-Lapus 2 about ten minutes by boat from Malapascua, right next to the main Lapus-Lapus site. It begins as a sloping reef around 12 meters, transitions into a small wall with a sandy base around 20 meters, and continues down to a maximum of about 30 meters where black corals and sea fans cover the sandy slope. This corrects an earlier version of this page that called it a pinnacle. Note there is also a separate, nearby "Lapus Pinnacle" site documented by some operators — a genuinely distinct pinnacle-shaped feature — which should not be confused with Lapus-Lapus 2.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Northwest of Malapascua Island, next to Lapus-Lapus, Philippines
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters

## Marine Life

Sources describe soft corals, sea fans, and sponges covering the site, with nudibranchs, frogfish, lionfish, needlefish, and pipefish reported throughout, plus anthias, butterflyfish, and damselfish in the shallower rock formations, and sea snakes occasionally encountered. Earlier claims of schooling barracuda and trevally, and resting sea turtles, are not supported by any of these sources and have been removed.
