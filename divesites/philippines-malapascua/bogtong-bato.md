## Bogtong Bato

Bogtong Bato — also known locally as Deep Rock — is a submerged pinnacle off the northwest coast of Malapascua Island, its flat top at around 14 meters dropping to soft-coral-covered walls near 30 meters.

## Overview

The name "Bogtong Bato" translates roughly as "single rock" in Cebuano, and the site matches that description: an isolated pinnacle rising from the seafloor between Malapascua and the Cebu mainland. Independent operator sources put the flat top at around 14 meters, with the surrounding walls dropping to a maximum of about 30 meters and soft coral covering the deeper sections. This depth range and the "Deep Rock" alternate name are consistent across multiple sources.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Northwest of Malapascua Island, Philippines
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Pinnacle/seamount
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters
- **Current**: Variable; can strengthen around the pinnacle
- **Best Time**: March to June (generally calmer conditions)

## Marine Life

Confirmed by multiple independent sources: schools of batfish, squid, nudibranchs, frogfish, pygmy seahorses, and sea fans on the deeper sections.

## Dive Profile

Descend to the pinnacle's base near 30 meters and spiral upward around the structure toward the flat top at 14 meters, which also serves as a good safety-stop location while continuing to observe reef life. Manage air carefully given the starting depth.

## Entry and Exit

Accessible by banca from Malapascua dive resorts. Entry by giant stride; return to the boat by ladder. Deploy SMB before ascending for boat crew visibility.

## Tips and Recommendations

- The pinnacle rewards slow, circular diving — cover all sides of the structure
- Look closely on the deeper sections for pygmy seahorses on the sea fans
- Bring a torch for the darker crevices lower on the pinnacle

## Safety Considerations

The 30-meter maximum depth requires careful no-decompression limit management. Currents can increase around the pinnacle. Deploy an SMB for the ascent.
