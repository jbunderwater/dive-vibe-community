## Bogtong Bato

Bogtong Bato — Cebuano for roughly "single rock," and listed by some operators under the English name "Deep Rock" — is an isolated pinnacle off northwest Malapascua Island.

## Overview

Bogtong Bato (also spelled Bugtong Bato) rises as a single isolated pinnacle from the seafloor, with its shallowest point reported around 14-16 meters and a base near 30 meters. It is consistently described across independent sources — under both its Cebuano and English ("Deep Rock") names — as a pinnacle dive rewarding a slow, circular approach around the structure.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Northwest of Malapascua Island, Philippines
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Pinnacle/seamount
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters

## Marine Life

Sources report batfish schools, squid and mackerel schools, black cowries, frogfish, pygmy seahorses, harlequin shrimp, scorpionfish, zebra crabs, and nudibranchs on and around the pinnacle.

## Dive Profile

Descend to the base of the pinnacle and work upward in a spiral, covering all sides of the structure as depth decreases — a pattern recommended by multiple local operators for this site.
