## Bogtong Bato

Bogtong Bato — also called Deep Rock — is a pinnacle northwest of Malapascua and a favorite among macro photographers for its resident frogfish and pygmy seahorses.

## Overview

The name "Bogtong Bato" translates roughly as "single rock" in Cebuano. Four independent Malapascua dive-shop sources confirm it as a pinnacle structure, some using the alternate name "Deep Rock," rising from around 12-16 meters at the top to a base near 30 meters. Difficulty is cited as Intermediate by most operators, with a couple describing it as Intermediate to Advanced given the depth and occasional current.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Northwest of Malapascua Island, Philippines
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Pinnacle/seamount
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters

## Marine Life

This is one of the better-corroborated sites in Malapascua: multiple operators independently and consistently report frogfish, nudibranchs, pygmy seahorses, ghost pipefish, and harlequin shrimp, plus schools of batfish, squid, mackerel, scorpionfish, lionfish, and anthias. Earlier claims of longnose hawkfish, filefish, sea snakes, and open-water sharks/trevally are not supported by these sources and have been removed.
