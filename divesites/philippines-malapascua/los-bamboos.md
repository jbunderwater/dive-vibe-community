## Los Bamboos

Los Bamboos, also listed as Los Bamboos Marine Sanctuary, is a quiet reef site on Malapascua tucked away from the island's busier dive routes.

## Overview

Los Bamboos sits away from Malapascua's most-visited sites and is frequently used by local operators as a training and skills-refinement dive as well as a relaxed macro/coral-garden dive for more experienced divers. Sources report a depth range of roughly 5 to 18 meters. An earlier version of this page attributed the site's name to "bamboo fish traps set by local fishermen" — no source supports this specific etymology, and it has been removed rather than repeated as fact.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Malapascua Island, Philippines
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 18 meters

## Marine Life

No site-specific marine life reports could be confirmed from the sources consulted beyond general references to "vibrant marine life and lush coral gardens" and macro subjects. Malapascua's regional reef fauna (nudibranchs, reef fish, occasional turtles) likely applies, but no site-specific sightings list is currently sourced for Los Bamboos.
