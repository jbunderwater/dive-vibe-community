## Los Bamboos

Los Bamboos — also known as Los Bamboos Marine Sanctuary — is a coral-garden training site used regularly by Evolution Diving Resort for courses, check dives, and periodic underwater debris cleanups.

## Overview

Los Bamboos is confirmed by multiple independent sources as a real, named site on Malapascua, referred to both as "Los Bamboos" and "Los Bamboos Marine Sanctuary." Depth is reported in the 5–18 meter range. Evolution Diving Resort uses it as a regular training location and has run periodic volunteer cleanup dives there through PADI AWARE's Dive Against Debris program. Beyond "coral garden" and general critter life, no source reviewed gave a confirmed, site-specific marine-life list — an earlier description naming garden eels and burrowing gobies at this site could not be verified and has been removed rather than repeated.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Malapascua Island, Philippines
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 18 meters

## Marine Life

Description based on regional diving characteristics for the shallow reef/coral-garden terrain reported by sources; no site-specific marine-life reports were found for Los Bamboos beyond general references to camouflaged critters in the coral. Divers should not expect a confirmed species list here — ask your guide what has been seen recently.

## Entry and Exit

Boat dive from Malapascua resorts, commonly used as a training or check-dive site. Entry by giant stride; return to the boat by ladder.

## Tips and Recommendations

- A good choice for skills practice, check dives, or a relaxed second dive given its Beginner rating and training-site use
- If you dive with an eco-conscious operator, ask whether a Dive Against Debris cleanup is scheduled here — the site has an established cleanup history

## Safety Considerations

Shallow depth and its use as a training site make this a low-risk dive. Standard boat-diving precautions apply.
