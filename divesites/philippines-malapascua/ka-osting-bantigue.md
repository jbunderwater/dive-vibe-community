## Ka-Osting / Bantigue

Ka-Osting and neighboring Bantigi form Malapascua's classic muck-diving pair — a shallow, mostly sand-and-rock area on the island's eastern side prized by critter hunters rather than coral divers.

## Overview

Ka-Osting (also spelled Ka Osting, and referred to by some operators as "East Garden") sits east of Malapascua Island and is a shallow, largely current-free site well suited to muck diving. It lies adjacent to Bantigi, and the two are frequently dived and described together, offering overlapping fauna on a mostly sand and rocky bottom rather than a coral reef slope. This entry corrects an earlier version of this page that described a coral reef with cleaning stations — no source supports that; both Ka Osting and Bantigi are consistently documented as muck sites.

## Site Information

- **Location**: East side of Malapascua Island, Philippines
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Muck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 18 meters

## Marine Life

Sources are consistent and specific here: resident hairy frogfish are the headline sighting at Ka Osting, alongside squat lobster, porcelain crabs, and numerous nudibranchs. Bantigi adds fire urchins, zebra crabs, dwarf lionfish, cuttlefish, seamoths, mimic octopus, and stargazers. Bobtail squid and decorator crabs are also reported, and the combined site is cited as one of Malapascua's better night dives for critter hunting.
