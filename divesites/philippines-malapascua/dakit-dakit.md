## Dakit-Dakit

Dakit-Dakit is a cluster of small, partly-submerged rock/island formations off Malapascua, with a shallow pinnacle top popular for photography, skills work, and relaxed second or third dives.

## Overview

Dakit-Dakit is described by local operators as "a scattering of small island formations, some below the surface and some above," with the pinnacle top reaching to roughly 5 meters and the surrounding structure stepping down to 12–14 meters. The site is prone to current, which keeps the soft coral garden on the formations in good condition. It is rated for Open Water divers by multiple operators, though currents can pick up unexpectedly.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Near Malapascua Island, Philippines
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Pinnacle/seamount
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 12 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 8-20 meters
- **Current**: Can be strong despite the shallow depth
- **Best Time**: Year-round

## Marine Life

Sources describe nudibranchs, flatworms, pipefish, and seahorses living within the soft coral growth on the formations, along with banded pipefish and cuttlefish. No thresher shark, reef shark, or other pelagic sightings are reported for this specific site in available sources — it is primarily a macro/critter site.

## Dive Profile

The pinnacle top sits around 5 meters with the surrounding structure stepping down to 12–14 meters, so the whole site can be covered in a single dive without deep-stop planning. Move slowly and check the coral surfaces closely for small, camouflaged subjects.

## Entry and Exit

Short banca ride from Malapascua resorts. Entry by giant stride or back roll; return to the banca by ladder.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Suits Open Water divers, but ask your guide about current conditions before entry
- A macro lens rewards patient searching of the soft coral growth
- The shallow depth allows for extended bottom time

## Safety Considerations

The shallow depth minimizes decompression risk, but current can build unexpectedly around the exposed rock formations. Deploy an SMB and stay close to your guide.

## Photography

Good natural light throughout the shallow depth range makes this a strong site for available-light macro and coral detail shots.
