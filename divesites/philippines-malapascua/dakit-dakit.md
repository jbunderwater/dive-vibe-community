## Dakit-Dakit

Dakit-Dakit is a shallow pinnacle off Malapascua Island, visible from shore near the dive centres, known among local operators as a nudibranch-rich soft-coral garden.

## Overview

Dakit-Dakit is a compact rock/coral pinnacle close enough to the island to be visible from the dive centres. Independent sources put its maximum depth at roughly 12–15 meters, with rock formations and a soft coral garden as the dominant terrain. The area is exposed to scattered small islands that can create locally strong currents, keeping the coral healthy but requiring attentive buoyancy control.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Near Malapascua Island, Philippines
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Pinnacle/seamount
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 15 meters
- **Current**: Can be locally strong around the surrounding islets

## Marine Life

Confirmed by multiple independent sources: nudibranchs (in enough quantity that one operator calls it "Nudibranch Heaven"), flatworms, pipefish, and seahorses among the soft coral. Given the shallow depth, this is a strong macro-photography site.

## Dive Profile

At 12–15 meters maximum, the site can be circled fully within a single dive. Work slowly around the pinnacle's soft coral growth, looking closely for nudibranchs and pipefish rather than covering ground quickly.

## Entry and Exit

Short banca ride from Malapascua resorts. Entry by giant stride or back roll; return to the boat by ladder.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Bring a macro lens and torch — this is a site for patient, close-focus photography
- Be ready for current around the pinnacle despite the shallow depth
- The shallow profile allows extended bottom time for thorough nudibranch and pipefish searches

## Safety Considerations

The shallow depth keeps decompression risk low, but currents around the surrounding islets can be locally strong — maintain good buoyancy and stay close to your guide. Deploy an SMB before ascending.
