## Dakit-Dakit

Dakit-Dakit is a shallow boulder-and-coral reef off Malapascua, a relaxed site suited to divers of all experience levels.

## Overview

Dakit-Dakit is a shallow reef structure south of Malapascua Island, with sources reporting depths in the 8-15 meter range. Coral cover is described as healthy soft coral over a boulder/reef formation. The shallow depth allows for extended bottom time and makes the site accessible to beginners as well as more experienced divers looking for an easy, relaxed dive.

## Site Information

- **Location**: South of Malapascua Island, Philippines
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 15 meters

## Marine Life

Sources report nudibranchs, flatworms, banded pipefish, and seahorses on the coral growth, along with soft coral cover throughout the site. No thresher shark or other pelagic sightings are reported at this site in any source consulted.
