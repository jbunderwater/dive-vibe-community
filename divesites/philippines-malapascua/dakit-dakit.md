## Dakit-Dakit

Dakit-Dakit is a shallow boulder-and-coral reef off Malapascua Island, reaching no more than about 15 meters — an easy, relaxed site favoured for its coral cover and reliable macro life.

## Overview

Dakit-Dakit is a shallow reef built around scattered coral-covered boulders, with the seabed reaching roughly 8-15 meters depending on the operator's route. Evolution Diving Resort describes it as a boulder/reef site; Malapascua Thresher Shark Divers and Diverbliss both list it as a reef dive rather than a freestanding pinnacle. The shallow depth and generally gentle conditions make it accessible to all certified divers and give long bottom times.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Near Malapascua Island, Philippines
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 15 meters
- **Best Time**: Year-round; morning for best light

## Marine Life

Sources describe soft coral cover with nudibranchs, banded pipefish, seahorses, and cuttlefish (Malapascua Thresher Shark Divers), and butterflyfish schools with orangutan crabs (Evolution Diving Resort). Diverbliss also lists flatworms and passing schools of jacks, sardines, and tuna. No source ties thresher shark or hammerhead sightings to this site specifically — those belong to Monad Shoal and Kimud Shoal.

## Entry and Exit

Short banca ride from Malapascua resorts. Entry by giant stride or back roll; the shallow water allows visual confirmation of the site before entry. Return to the banca by ladder.

## Tips and Recommendations

- The shallow depth makes this a good option for a second or third dive of the day, or a night dive
- Move slowly and check coral surfaces closely for the site's macro life
- Bring a macro lens for nudibranchs and pipefish

## Safety Considerations

The shallow depth minimises decompression risk. Standard boat diving precautions apply. Deploy an SMB before ascending.
