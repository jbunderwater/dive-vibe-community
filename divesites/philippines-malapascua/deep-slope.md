## Deep Slope

Deep Slope is a sand-dune-shaped reef slope off Malapascua, a well-regarded macro and muck-style site that also produces reliable garden eel and pygmy seahorse sightings.

## Overview

Deep Slope's topography has been described as a sand dune that gradually turns into a small wall, reaching a maximum of about 25 meters. The small wall section is covered in sponges, soft coral, and hard coral, with crevices sheltering scorpionfish and lionfish; the sandy base is a reliable spot for garden eels.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Near Malapascua Island, Philippines
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 25 meters

## Marine Life

Garden eels are reported on "every dive" per Diverbliss, with pygmy seahorses among the sea fans, plus green and hawksbill turtles, flamboyant cuttlefish, nudibranchs, pipefish, frogfish, and cowfish reported by Diverbliss and Scubadiving.place. No source ties thresher sharks to Deep Slope specifically — that sighting pattern is documented only at Monad Shoal and Kimud Shoal, both deep-water cleaning stations well offshore, and an earlier version of this page's thresher-shark claim for Deep Slope has been removed as unsupported.
