## Japanese Ship Wreck

## Overview

Two independently documented wrecks exist in this area, and this OSM entry appears to conflate elements of both:

1. **Lighthouse Wreck** — a Japanese WWII landing craft in roughly 3-7 meters of water, broken into two pieces, located about 5-10 minutes by boat from the Malapascua lighthouse — close to this entry's coordinates (within a few hundred meters). Sources disagree on the sinking story: one describes it as bombed just before reaching the beach while carrying a cargo of cement (visible today as "bags of cement" near the wreck), while another describes it as a vessel hiding from American forces that was spotted and bombed. Neither source gives a vessel name, build date, length, or tonnage.

2. **Tapilon Wreck** — an unidentified WWII Japanese cargo carrier (one source tentatively suggests it may be the Oakita Maru, but this is not confirmed by a second source) at roughly 22-32 meters depth, hit by torpedoes and now broken into three sections, located off Tapilon town — about 30 minutes by boat from Malapascua, i.e. considerably farther away than this entry's coordinates suggest.

This entry's depth (33m) is close to the Tapilon Wreck's range, but its coordinates sit right at the Lighthouse Wreck's location. No source resolves this contradiction, and no Wikipedia article was found for either vessel to cross-check against.
