## Hai Siang

Hai Siang is a 49-metre Taiwanese fishing vessel deliberately scuttled off Saint-Gilles as an artificial reef, now lying broken in two sections on white sand at around 50-54 metres — one of Réunion's deepest and most demanding wreck dives.

## Overview

The Hai Siang was a Taiwanese fishing boat — its name is reported to translate roughly as "wild boar" — that was abandoned by its owner and seized by the French Navy, reportedly after being caught fishing illegally in Réunion waters. It was deliberately sunk off Saint-Gilles to create an artificial reef and dive site.

**Sources disagree on when it was sunk.** A regional Réunion news outlet, linfo.re, and a local dive operator's account place the scuttling in early 1996. An earlier note attached to this listing (repeating older dive-shop marketing copy) placed it in "the 1980s." Both cannot be correct, and no source was found that resolves the discrepancy with primary documentation, so this listing does not assert a specific year.

The wreck rests on white sand at approximately 50-54 metres (sources converge on this depth; one low-quality aggregator listing gave an inconsistent ~20-metre reading and was discounted). A cyclonic swell subsequently broke the hull into two sections, separated by roughly ten metres, though its two masts remain visible on the sand. No dedicated Wikipedia article exists for this vessel, and no source gives a tonnage figure, so none is stated here.

## Marine Life

Operators report schools of snapper, lionfish, and stingrays around the wreck, with pelagic species sometimes seen in the water column during the ascent and safety stop.

## Dive Profile

This is a Level 3 (technical-adjacent) dive: the descent to the wreck takes roughly ten minutes and is typically done as a fast, direct drop to avoid drifting off the site in current, which can be strong at depth. Given the depth and current, it is not a beginner or intermediate site.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Off Saint-Gilles / Boucan Canot area, west Réunion
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 54 meters

## Safety

This is a deep wreck dive requiring appropriate certification (Level 3 / advanced deep or technical training) and careful gas planning. Currents at depth can be significant. Wreck penetration, where attempted, requires specific training and equipment and is never risk-free.
