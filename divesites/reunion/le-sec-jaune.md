## Le Sec Jaune

Le Sec Jaune ("the yellow shoal") is an isolated rock plateau near Saint-Leu, its shallow crown blanketed in vivid yellow coral — the source of its name — overlooking a drop-off toward the Tombant de la Pointe au Sel.

## Overview

Facing the protected Pointe au Sel reserve zone, Le Sec Jaune is a rock plateau rising close to the surface and covered in bright yellow coral. The plateau crown begins around 5 metres. Sources give slightly different maximum depths — PADI's site listing states a maximum of 20 metres, while another operator description puts the drop-off at 20-30 metres — so this listing keeps the wider range rather than picking one figure. It sits adjacent to the Tombant de la Pointe au Sel wall and is accessible to all certification levels.

## Marine Life

Sea turtles and eagle rays are regularly reported here, along with a good variety of reef fish and both hard and soft coral cover.

## Dive Profile

An easy dive across the coral-covered plateau, with the option to follow the drop-off toward deeper water for more experienced divers. Caves and arches are present among the coral formations.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Facing Pointe au Sel, south of Saint-Leu, Réunion
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters
