## La Passe des Trois-Bassins

A nutrient-rich channel through the reef crest at Trois-Bassins, facing Cap La Houssaye, known for strong currents and correspondingly abundant fish life.

## Overview

This pass cuts through the reef crest in front of Trois-Bassins village, between Saint-Gilles and Saint-Leu, facing Cap La Houssaye. The dive runs roughly 5-22 metres over coral drop-offs bordered by sandy patches with numerous crevices. Sources describe the pass as particularly rich in nutrients and plankton — which can reduce visibility at times — and specifically call out strong currents through the channel.

*Data correction (2026-08-27 QA): this site was previously classified as a reef rated Beginner. The pass/channel naming and the sourced description of strong currents match this project's rule that channels and passes default to drift; difficulty has been raised to Intermediate accordingly, though some operators still market the site toward less experienced divers given its otherwise approachable depth.*

## Marine Life

Sea turtles and large eagle rays are reported here, and a cave is noted as a feature of the site. The pass's nutrient flow supports a notably dense and varied fish population compared to nearby sites.

## Dive Profile

Divers move with the current through the pass, between the coral drop-offs and sandy crevice areas, watching for the plankton-driven visibility changes that come with the tidal exchange through the channel.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Trois-Bassins village, facing Cap La Houssaye, Réunion
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Drift dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 22 meters
