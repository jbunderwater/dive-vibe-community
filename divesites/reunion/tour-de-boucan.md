## Tour de Boucan

A rock pinnacle off Boucan Canot rising from a 22–25 metre seabed, its walls riddled with caves and canyons that shelter a dense population of reef fish.

## Overview

Tour de Boucan ("the tower of Boucan") is an isolated rock formation off Boucan Canot beach on Reunion's west coast, reclassified here from reef to pinnacle to reflect its actual structure — a tower rising from the seabed rather than a reef platform. Sources put the surrounding seabed depth at 22 metres (the majority figure) to as much as 25 metres, with the site typically dived across a 5–25 metre range. The tower's walls are cut with caves, canyons and drift-prone channels, and multiple dive shops run it as three separate dives depending on which face is worked. It is rated accessible to all levels, including beginners.

## Marine Life

Divers report clownfish and anemones, sea turtles, moray eels, groupers, lionfish, scorpionfish, lobsters and triggerfish, plus both hard and soft corals with yellow gorgonians on the tower's flanks. Barracuda and Napoleon wrasse are seen but described as less common.

## Dive Profile

A boat dive of roughly 15–30 minutes from the harbor, working the tower's faces between 5 and 25 metres depth. The richest life is reported near the top of the formation.

## Entry and Exit

Boat entry off Boucan Canot.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Off Boucan Canot beach, Saint-Paul area, Reunion, Africa
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Pinnacle/seamount
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 22 meters
