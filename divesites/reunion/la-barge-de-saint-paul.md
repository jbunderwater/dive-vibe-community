## La Barge de Saint-Paul

A small barge resting on black basaltic sand in Saint-Paul bay, riddled with openings and a favourite for night diving.

## Overview

La Barge de Saint-Paul (also called La Tonne) is a small wreck, reported at roughly 10 metres long, resting at 22 metres on black basaltic sand in Saint-Paul bay, facing the marine cemetery and about 100 metres from the Sea Venture wreck. Large anchor chains lie in the sand around it. Sources describe the hull as old and riddled with small openings, which — combined with its shallow depth relative to Reunion's other wrecks — makes it a popular and comfortable night dive.

## Marine Life

Sources consistently report stonefish, leaffish, frogfish (antennariidae), electric/torpedo rays and ghost pipefish sheltering in and around the wreck, along with schools of grunts, rubberfish and fusiliers. Dolphins and hunting tuna are occasionally seen passing nearby.

## Dive Profile

A shallow wreck dive at 22 metres, descending along the surface mooring chain to the barge on black sand. Weak current is typical. Often paired with Sea Venture on the same boat trip given the short distance between them.

## Entry and Exit

Boat entry via a surface mooring buoy in Saint-Paul bay.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Saint-Paul bay, facing the marine cemetery, Reunion, Africa
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 22 meters
