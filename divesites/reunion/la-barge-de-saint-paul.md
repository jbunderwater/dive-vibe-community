## La Barge de Saint-Paul

La Barge is a small 10-metre barge wreck resting on black basaltic sand at 22 metres in Saint-Paul Bay, a short distance from the larger Sea Venture wreck, and is one of Réunion's most popular night-diving sites.

## Overview

This small, ten-metre barge lies at 22 metres on black basaltic sand in Saint-Paul Bay, near the marine cemetery and roughly a hundred metres from the Sea Venture wreck. Its hull is riddled with small openings that divers can look (and, with training, swim) through, and large anchor chains lie nearby on the sand.

## Marine Life

Confirmed sightings at this site include frogfish (antennariids), stonefish, and torpedo/electric rays. Tuna and dolphins are occasionally seen hunting in the surrounding water. An earlier version of this listing's source notes referenced "leafy sea dragons" at this site; that claim has been removed — leafy sea dragons are found only in temperate southern Australian waters and do not occur in the Indian Ocean, and no independent source confirms the species here. This is flagged as a likely mistranslation or transcription error in an earlier source rather than a real sighting.

## Dive Profile

A shallow, approachable wreck dive at 10-22 metres on a sandy bay floor. Its structure and depth make it particularly well suited to night dives, when the frogfish, stonefish, and rays that shelter in and around the hull are more active.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Saint-Paul Bay, facing the marine cemetery, Réunion
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 22 meters
