## Le Jardin des Kiosques

Le Jardin des Kiosques sits on the outer slope of Saint-Leu's coral barrier, where a sand bank runs alongside a rich coral platform and, further north, fissures cut through stands of large tabular Acropora coral.

## Overview

This site covers the external slope of the coral barrier at Saint-Leu. A white sand bank runs beside the coral platform; heading north, divers reach a section of fissures ("coral tongues") with dense coral growth, including large tabular Acropora colonies. Reported depths vary somewhat by source — PADI lists 3-30 metres, while operator listings describe the site running from the surface to 32-35 metres — so this listing uses an approximate 30-35 metre range for the deeper limit. The site suits beginners through advanced divers.

## Marine Life

Confirmed site-specific sightings include eagle rays along the sand bank, stonefish, leaf scorpionfish, lizardfish, bluefin trevally, and stingrays. PADI's listing for this site also carries a generic "sharks, whales, dolphins" species tag that appears identically on multiple unrelated Réunion PADI site pages — this reads as boilerplate rather than a site-specific report, so it has not been repeated here.

## Dive Profile

Entry onto the outer reef slope, following the sand bank alongside the coral platform before working north into the fissured section with its tabular coral stands. Suitable for a wide range of experience levels given the shallow starting depth.
