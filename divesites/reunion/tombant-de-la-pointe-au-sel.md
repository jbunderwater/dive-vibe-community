## Tombant de la Pointe au Sel

Widely cited by local operators as Reunion's finest dive: a reef shelf off Saint-Leu that steps down before a vertigo-inducing drop-off, with seasonal hammerhead sharks and passing pelagics.

## Overview

The Tombant de la Pointe au Sel, off Saint-Leu, is repeatedly described by local operators as the most beautiful dive site on Reunion. The profile starts as a reef shelf that steps down from the shallows to around 25–27 metres before falling away in a near-vertical drop-off. Sources disagree on how deep that wall goes — figures of 60, 71 and 80 metres all appear across different operators — so this is best understood as a wall whose full extent is reported anywhere between 60 and 80 metres rather than a single confirmed number. Every source consulted describes it as a site for experienced divers, with Level 2 cited as the practical minimum; given the depths involved and current-dependent conditions, this entry is upgraded from Intermediate to Advanced.

## Marine Life

Scalloped hammerhead sharks are reported seasonally, with most sources placing the best window in October–November (one source specifies September–October); sightings are not guaranteed and depend on the season and current. Sources also report jacks, humphead parrotfish, tuna and barracuda in near-constant motion along the wall, with manta ray, whale shark and marlin described as possible but occasional passing encounters — not routine sightings. Eagle rays, moray eels, grouper and scorpionfish are also reported on the reef shelf.

## Dive Profile

A drift-influenced wall dive: descend the reef shelf to around 25–27 metres, then work the drop-off face. Pelagic activity is described by sources as dependent on the presence of current. Given the depth range, this is not a dive for anyone without advanced open-water experience.

## Entry and Exit

Boat entry roughly 10 minutes out from Saint-Leu.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Off Saint-Leu, Reunion, Africa
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters

## Safety

Depth and current make this a site for experienced divers only. Treat the base of the wall (60–80m reported) as outside recreational no-decompression limits for most divers — plan the dive to stay within your certification and gas limits rather than chasing depth.
