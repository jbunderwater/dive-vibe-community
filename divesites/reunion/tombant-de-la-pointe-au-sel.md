## Tombant de la Pointe au Sel

Widely rated by Réunion operators as the island's finest dive, this wall drops from around 27 metres to as deep as 71-80 metres off Saint-Leu — a serious, current-swept drop-off best reserved for well-qualified divers.

## Overview

South of Saint-Leu, the Tombant de la Pointe au Sel is a submarine cliff that multiple independent operators single out as the most beautiful dive site on Réunion. The wall begins around 25-27 metres and plunges to between 71 and 80 metres depending on the source; recreational divers on air typically turn the dive around 60 metres, well short of the true bottom. Level 2/3 certification is required.

*Data correction (2026-08-27 QA): this site's difficulty was previously listed as Intermediate. Given the confirmed depth range (drop-off starting at 25-27m and extending past 70-80m) and this project's standing rule that any site exceeding 30 metres is rated Advanced, difficulty has been corrected accordingly.*

## Marine Life

Operators consistently report reef fish, stingrays, and sea turtles on the wall face, along with jacks, humphead (Napoleon-type) parrotfish, tuna, and barracuda cruising in the blue. Occasional sightings of hammerhead sharks, whale sharks, and manta rays are advertised by local operators; these appear in marketing copy rather than being tied to specific verified encounters, so they are noted here as occasional/seasonal possibilities rather than routine sightings.

## Dive Profile

Descend along the wall from roughly 25-27 metres. Depending on certification and dive computer limits, divers typically work the wall face down to around 40-60 metres before turning, watching the blue water off the wall for passing pelagics.

## Site Information

- **Location**: South of Saint-Leu, Réunion
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 80 meters

## Safety

This is a deep wall dive with a drop-off well beyond recreational no-decompression limits. Careful depth and gas management is essential, and the site is not appropriate for beginner or newly-certified intermediate divers.
