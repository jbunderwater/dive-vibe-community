## Passe de l'Ermitage

Passe de l'Ermitage is a drift dive through a channel that a ravine has cut in the reef at Ermitage-les-Bains — a nutrient-rich exchange point between the lagoon and the open ocean where current can run strong.

## Overview

The pass was formed where a ravine carved through the coral barrier fronting Ermitage-les-Bains beach, creating a channel that exchanges water — and nutrients — between the sheltered lagoon and the open sea. Two independent operator write-ups (tribloo.com and le5element.com) explicitly describe it as a *plongée dérivante* (drift dive) and warn divers not to let the scenery distract them from the current. Depth is reported in the 6-25 metre range (one source narrows this to 10-22m), over a floor of sand, coral plateau, fissures, tunnels and small caves.

*Data correction (2026-08-27 QA): this site was previously classified as a reef rated Beginner. Given the explicit "drift dive" characterization and current warnings from two independent operators, it has been reclassified as a drift site and the difficulty raised to Intermediate. The pass/channel naming also matches this project's standing rule that channels and passes default to drift rather than reef.*

## Marine Life

Sea turtles and eagle rays are regularly reported, along with batfish (platax) that will often follow divers through the dive, and a variety of colourful reef fish drawn in by the nutrient flow through the pass.

## Dive Profile

Divers work through the channel with the current, moving between sandy stretches, coral plateau, and the pass's fissures and small tunnels. Visibility can be reduced by the plankton the pass carries between lagoon and ocean.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Ermitage-les-Bains, west Réunion
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Drift dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 25 meters
