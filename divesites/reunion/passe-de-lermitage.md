## Passe de l'Ermitage

One of Reunion's best-known dives: a channel cut through the reef crest at Ermitage-les-Bains where nutrient-rich water exchanges between lagoon and open ocean, drawing turtles and eagle rays to cleaning stations.

## Overview

Passe de l'Ermitage is a pass formed where a ravine has cut through the coral barrier fronting Ermitage-les-Bains beach, creating an exchange zone between the lagoon and open water that concentrates plankton and nutrients. PADI's own listing tags the site's dive type as "drift, reef," reflecting that the pass itself — not a static reef feature — is the primary characteristic, which is why this entry is reclassified here from reef to drift. Depth across the site runs roughly 6 to 25 metres over a mix of sandy bottom, coral plateau, and faults, tunnels and small caves. It is rated accessible to all levels, including baptism/discovery dives, though sources note that good visibility depends on conditions.

## Marine Life

Turtles and eagle rays are the signature sightings, both in open water and at cleaning stations where they stop to be groomed by cleaner fish. Sources also report stingrays, reef fish including surgeonfish, parrotfish and platax, plus groupers, barracuda, moray eels and lionfish.

## Dive Profile

A drift-influenced dive through the reef-crest channel, typically worked between 6 and 25 metres, with the cleaning stations as the focal point.

## Entry and Exit

Boat entry, roughly 15–30 minutes out from Saint-Gilles.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Ermitage-les-Bains, Saint-Gilles area, Reunion, Africa
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Drift dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 25 meters
