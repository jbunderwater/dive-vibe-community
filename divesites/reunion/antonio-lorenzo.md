## Antonio Lorenzo

A Chilean longliner seized for illegal fishing near Kerguelen and later scuttled off Saint-Leu as an artificial reef — now lying on its port side, largely intact, and rich in life.

## Overview

The Antonio Lorenzo was a Chilean longline fishing vessel intercepted by the French Navy patrol boat l'Albatros on 1 September 1998 while fishing illegally for Patagonian toothfish near the Kerguelen Islands, with more than 100 tonnes of toothfish found aboard. Seized and towed to Port des Galets on 14 September, the vessel was judged too deteriorated to remain in port; the regional FFESSM proposed sinking her as an artificial reef instead. After decommissioning and depollution, she was towed to Pointe des Chateaux near Saint-Leu and deliberately sunk on 16 December 1999. Sources agree her length was 53 metres; build date and tonnage are not documented anywhere found. She now lies on her port side, largely intact as a single piece with the rudder and propeller separated nearby, and divers can access the stern, bow, anchor chain and cargo holds.

Depth reporting is inconsistent across sources: figures of 38, 40 and 50 metres all appear. The majority of independently checked sources (Dodo Palme, Sous Les Etoiles 974) converge on roughly 40 metres, so that is used here; one source suggests the wreck may now rest deeper, closer to 50 metres, possibly reflecting settling over time, but this could not be independently confirmed. Certification requirements also vary by source — most cite Level 2 (PADI Advanced Open Water equivalent) as the minimum, one cites Level 3.

## Marine Life

Sources report spotted groupers, frogfish (antennariidae), rays, leopard moray eels, schools of snapper, spiny lobster, lionfish, nudibranchs, tropical turbot and pipefish, with occasional stingrays, manta rays and jacks passing through. One source notes coral colonization on the hull is still limited.

## Dive Profile

A wreck dive around 40 metres depth (see depth note above), exploring the stern, bow, anchor chain and cargo holds of a mostly intact hull lying on its port side.

## Entry and Exit

Boat entry off Pointe des Chateaux, near Saint-Leu.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Pointe des Chateaux, near Saint-Leu, Reunion, Africa
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters

## Safety

Level 2 minimum per most sources consulted (one source cites Level 3) — confirm requirements with your operator. Cargo-hold access should only be attempted with proper wreck-penetration training and equipment.
