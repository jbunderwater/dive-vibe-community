## Antonio Lorenzo

Antonio Lorenzo is a Chilean longline fishing vessel seized for illegal fishing near Kerguelen and deliberately sunk off Saint-Leu in December 1999 to create an artificial reef — now lying on its port side near the Tombant de la Pointe au Sel wall.

## Overview

The Antonio Lorenzo was a Chilean longliner caught by the French Navy patrol vessel *L'Albatros* fishing illegally for Patagonian toothfish (légine) in the Kerguelen/TAAF exclusive zone on 1 September 1998. It was towed to the port at Pointe des Galets on 14 September 1998. Rather than scrap the seized vessel, Réunion's regional FFESSM delegation proposed converting it into a deep artificial reef; after being depolluted and secured, it was towed to Pointe des Châteaux, Saint-Leu, and deliberately sunk on 16 December 1999 by opening holes in its hull. Follow-up studies between 1999 and 2003 found the wreck functioning successfully as an artificial reef without negative environmental impact.

## Dive Profile

The wreck lies on its port side near the base of the Tombant de la Pointe au Sel wall, making it accessible on the same boat trips as that site for suitably qualified divers. Level 3 / advanced certification is required given the depth.
