## Sea Venture

Reunion's only accidental wreck: a Panamanian timber freighter that capsized and sank while waiting for a berth in Saint-Paul bay in 1981, now resting upside down near La Barge.

## Overview

The Sea Venture was a Panamanian-flagged cargo ship, sources reporting her length as over 80 metres (one figure of 86m appears), carrying a cargo of timber logs. On 16 November 1981, while waiting in Saint-Paul bay for a berth to open at the Port des Galets, the ship — already stressed by heavy weather and destabilized by its cargo — took on water and sank. All 26 crew reached shore safely under their own power, saving personal effects, the ship's log and their papers. Unlike Reunion's other wrecks, which were deliberately scuttled to create artificial reefs, the Sea Venture is described by sources as the island's only accidental wreck. Her build date and tonnage are not documented in any source found.

She now lies inverted (hull up) in Saint-Paul bay, facing the marine cemetery and roughly 100 metres from La Barge de Saint-Paul. Reported depth ranges from about 40 metres (propellers/hull) to 49–50 metres depending on source. A fuel leak from the wreck caused a shoreline pollution incident on 21 September 1997, leading to a temporary diving ban; roughly 15 tonnes of diesel were removed from the wreck's tanks in a 1998 clean-up, after which diving access resumed.

## Marine Life

Sources report scorpionfish, tuna, sea bream, lionfish, emperor angelfish, nudibranchs, snapper, frogfish and electric rays around the wreck, along with numerous lobsters in the hull's cavities. Sharks are reported as occasional visitors to the area, though not a guaranteed sighting on every dive.

## Dive Profile

A deep wreck dive, descending a fixed line to the inverted hull at 40–49 metres. The two propellers, still well preserved, are a highlight. Given the depth, decompression stops are standard practice.

## Entry and Exit

Boat entry in Saint-Paul bay, near the marine cemetery and close to La Barge de Saint-Paul — the two wrecks are sometimes combined in a single outing.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Saint-Paul bay, facing the marine cemetery, Reunion, Africa
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 49 meters

## Safety

Level 3 certification required per every source consulted, with decompression obligations standard at this depth. The wreck was subject to a diving ban in 1997–98 following a fuel leak; confirm current access status with a local operator before diving.
