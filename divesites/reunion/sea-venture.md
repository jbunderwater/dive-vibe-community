## Sea Venture

Sea Venture is an 86-metre Panamanian cargo ship that capsized in Saint-Paul Bay in 1981 while waiting for a berth; multiple sources now describe the wreck as closed to diving due to structural instability, and that status is reflected here rather than presenting it as freely diveable.

## Overview

The Sea Venture was an 86-metre Panamanian cargo ship carrying a timber cargo, anchored in Saint-Paul Bay awaiting a free berth at the port of Pointe des Galets. On 16 November 1981, destabilized by its cargo and battered by bad weather, the ship took on water and capsized; all 26 crew members reached shore safely under their own power. It now lies upside-down facing Saint-Paul's marine cemetery, a few dozen metres from the smaller La Barge wreck, with its two propellers visible near 40 metres and the hull down to roughly 48-49 metres.

**Access status:** in 1997, corroded fuel tanks on the wreck leaked and polluted the Saint-Paul shoreline, prompting a diving closure. Access for dive clubs was reportedly reinstated around 2008. However, more recent listings (a Réunion dive-site directory and a diver forum thread) describe the wreck as **currently closed to diving due to instability of the structure**. This listing states that status explicitly rather than presenting the site as routinely accessible — divers should confirm current access with a local operator before planning a dive here. No tonnage figure was found in any source. No dedicated Wikipedia article covers this vessel (the only "Sea Venture" article on Wikipedia is the unrelated 1609 Bermuda-bound ship).

## Marine Life

Lobsters are reported using the structure for shelter. Pelagic species — grouper, tuna, mahi-mahi (dorado), and occasionally sharks — have been reported passing the site, per operator descriptions, though these are not confirmed as regular or guaranteed sightings.

## Dive Profile

When accessible, this is a deep, technically demanding dive on an inverted hull, with reported poor visibility at times. It requires Level 2 minimum per historical operator listings, though the site's current instability and closure status should be confirmed before diving.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Saint-Paul Bay, facing the marine cemetery, Réunion
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 49 meters

## Safety

Access to this wreck has reportedly been restricted at various times, most recently due to structural instability — confirm current status with a local dive operator before attempting this site. Wreck penetration, where permitted and attempted, always requires specific training and equipment and carries inherent risk.
