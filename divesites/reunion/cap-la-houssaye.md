## Cap La Houssaye

A shore-accessible reef at the southern point of Saint-Paul bay, known for a resident group of green turtles and an easy, all-levels profile.

## Overview

Cap La Houssaye marks the southern limit of the Baie de Saint-Paul on Reunion's west coast, inside the Reserve Naturelle Marine. It is one of the few sites on the island divable directly from shore. The terrain mixes coral reef, small caves and rocky outcrops with a seagrass bed to the east, and depths stay shallow throughout — most sources put the working range at 2 to 18 metres. A submerged cannon, cited by one operator as a relic feature of the site, sits at around 5 metres.

## Marine Life

Three resident green turtles are regularly reported in the seagrass bed at the north of the site, particularly around 10 metres. Divers also report reef fish, catfish, juvenile fish schools, nudibranchs and seahorses. Local authorities (Kelonia, the island's turtle observatory) have specifically flagged Cap La Houssaye as a site where divers must not touch, chase or otherwise disturb the turtles.

## Dive Profile

A gentle, shallow dive suited to all certification levels, following the reef and caves between roughly 2 and 18 metres, with the seagrass turtle habitat as a highlight to the north of the site.

## Entry and Exit

Shore entry — one of the only shore-accessible dive sites on Reunion's west coast.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Southern point of Baie de Saint-Paul, Reunion, Africa
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 18 meters
