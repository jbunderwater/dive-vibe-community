## Cap La Houssaye

Cap La Houssaye is a shore-accessible reef site off the cape between Saint-Paul and Saint-Gilles, known for its varied terrain — reef, small caves, arches, and a drop-off — and for consistently good sightings of camouflaged critters like scorpionfish and frogfish.

## Overview

Cap La Houssaye sits at the foot of the cape of the same name, on Réunion's sheltered west coast. It's rated for all levels, including complete beginners, thanks to a shallow, protected profile that ranges roughly 2-18 metres. The terrain is a mix of reef structure, small caves, and short drop-offs, with several swim-through arches that local guides know well and will route divers through.

## Marine Life

Dive operators report sea turtles, pipefish (syngnathids), and rays cruising near the surface as regular sightings. The site is also known among macro-oriented divers for camouflaged reef fish: scorpionfish (including a reported albino individual seen on the white pebble bottom), leaf scorpionfish, frogfish (antennariids), and nudibranchs, along with resident octopus.

## Dive Profile

Entry is from shore, descending onto the reef terrace before working through the site's arches and small caves in the 2-18 metre range. Guides typically look upward through open water periodically, since rays are often spotted near the surface rather than on the bottom.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Cap La Houssaye, between Saint-Paul and Saint-Gilles, Réunion
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 18 meters
