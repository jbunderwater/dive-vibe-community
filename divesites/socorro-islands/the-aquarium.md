# The Aquarium

A shallow volcanic reef off Socorro Island known for dense schools of Clarion angelfish and triggerfish

## Overview
The Aquarium is a shallow dive site off the east side of Socorro Island, named for its exceptional density and variety of reef fish — unusual for the Socorro archipelago, where most sites are defined by pelagic encounters rather than reef life. The volcanic substrate supports a concentrated community of tropical fish species in a relatively small area. Sources describe it as a macro-oriented dive that complements the archipelago's big-animal sites, though it receives far less detailed reporting from divers and operators than sites like The Boiler or Cabo Pearce.

## Site Information
- **Location**: Socorro Island (east side), Socorro Islands, Pacific
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 15 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 15-25 metres
- **Current**: Mild to moderate
- **Best Time**: November to June

## Marine Life
Two independent sources (AquaViews/Scuba.com and a broader roundup drawing on the same reporting) confirm large schools of endemic Clarion angelfish at this specific site, along with red-tailed and crosshatch triggerfish — the fish density that gives the site its name. The UW Photography Guide describes The Aquarium as "a macro dive" but its own diver did not enter the water at this site and reported no specific species beyond noting whales visible offshore during the surface interval — a detail this site inherited into its description. Beyond the confirmed angelfish and triggerfish schools, no source found documents specific additional species (moray eels, nudibranchs, resting sharks) at this particular site; the Revillagigedo's regional fauna — including occasional passing mantas, sharks, and dolphins — likely applies here as it does archipelago-wide, but no site-specific report of those sightings was found for The Aquarium.

## Dive Profile
A relaxed shallow dive, generally kept within recreational limits at this site. Work along the volcanic reef structures, watching for schools of Clarion angelfish and triggerfish. The shallow depth allows for extended bottom times — operators typically schedule it as a lighter dive rather than a headline stop, useful after deeper sites elsewhere in the archipelago.

## Entry and Exit
Zodiac tender from the liveaboard. Giant stride entry into relatively sheltered water compared to more exposed sites. Deploy an SMB during ascent. Conditions are generally calmer than the archipelago's more exposed pinnacle and wall sites.

## Tips and Recommendations
- Bring macro or close-focus wide-angle gear for the dense schools of Clarion angelfish and triggerfish
- A good site for extended bottom time after deeper dives elsewhere in the archipelago
- Keep an eye on the surface during intervals — whale sightings offshore have been reported here
- Gloves are prohibited throughout the marine park

## Safety Considerations
The shallow depth and typically milder conditions make this one of the more accessible sites in the archipelago, though the Advanced rating reflects the remote liveaboard-only location. Currents can still develop. The nearest hyperbaric chamber is approximately 30 hours away. Maintain standard safety practices.

## Photography
Endemic Clarion angelfish and dense triggerfish schools provide colourful, cooperative macro and close-focus wide-angle subjects in clear, shallow water with good natural light. The volcanic substrate provides interesting textures and backdrops.
