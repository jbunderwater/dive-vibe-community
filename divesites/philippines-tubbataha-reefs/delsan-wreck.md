## Delsan Wreck

Delsan Wreck is the only named shipwreck in Tubbataha, a small freighter that ran aground on the southeastern edge of South Atoll — divers call the dive their "Disneyland" not for the wreck itself, but for the shark-filled crevasse it marks the entrance to.

## Overview

The Delsan is a small freighter that ran aground on South Atoll, reportedly in the 1970s. **No source confirms the vessel's original name, builder, build date, or tonnage**, so none of those details are stated here. The wreck itself is shallow — a portion still breaks the surface at low tide, and it is too shallow in places for a full scuba visit — so divers use it mainly as a marker and entry point rather than the main event. The reef top starts around 5 meters and slopes gently to a wall at 23 meters, where a dramatic crevasse called "The Cut" (roughly 30 meters deep and 20 meters wide) plunges to about 40 meters and becomes a refuge from the current when it's running.

## Site Information

- **Location**: South Atoll (southeastern edge), Tubbataha Reefs Natural Park, Sulu Sea, Philippines
- **Entry Type**: liveaboard
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters

## Marine Life

When current is running, grey reef, silky, and whitetip sharks hunt schools of fish inside The Cut, and there have been recent tiger shark sightings. Dogtooth tuna, great barracuda, and giant trevally are regular in the current-fed water around the crevasse. The wreckage itself and surrounding reef are covered in coral and support a dense population of reef fish.
