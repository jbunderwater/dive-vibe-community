## Jesse Beazley Reef

Jesse Beazley Reef is an isolated, mushroom-shaped reef roughly 20 kilometers north of Tubbataha's main North and South Atolls — the northernmost, and least-visited, feature of the UNESCO World Heritage-listed park.

## Overview

Jesse Beazley's isolation from the two main atolls means it sees the fewest liveaboard visits in the park, and currents around it can be strong and unpredictable. The reef structure begins around 7 meters from the surface and drops steeply, past the working recreational range (to around 40 meters) toward depths beyond 50 meters. Not every itinerary includes it, since reaching it adds passage time from the main atolls.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Jesse Beazley Reef, ~20km north of the main atolls, Tubbataha Reefs Natural Park, Sulu Sea, Philippines
- **Entry Type**: liveaboard
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 20-40 meters
- **Current**: Often strong and unpredictable around the isolated structure
- **Best Time**: March to June

## Marine Life

Barracuda, Spanish mackerel, Napoleon wrasse, unicornfish, and surgeonfish move over the reef in schools, with grey reef sharks a consistent presence. Manta rays, eagle rays, and hammerhead, mako, and thresher sharks have been reported in the surrounding blue water, along with groupers on the reef itself. Turtles and occasional whale sharks are also reported.

## Dive Profile

The isolated structure allows diving either the outer reef face, where shark and pelagic activity concentrates, or shallower sections closer to the reef top. Given the distance from the main atolls, most itineraries schedule a single dedicated visit rather than multiple dives.

## Entry and Exit

Liveaboard access only; Jesse Beazley may not be included on every itinerary depending on weather and passage time from the main atolls. Entry from dinghy by giant stride; SMB for ascent. Park ranger briefings apply.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Confirm with your operator in advance whether Jesse Beazley is included — not every Tubbataha itinerary visits it
- Currents here can be strong and change direction; stay close to your guide
- The site rewards attention to open water as much as the reef itself, given the pelagic traffic

## Safety Considerations

The most remote site in the Tubbataha system, with unpredictable currents. Always carry an SMB and follow ranger guidance on entry timing. Medical evacuation from Jesse Beazley would take longer than from the main park areas.

## Photography

Wide-angle setups suit the reef's schooling fish and pelagic visitors. The isolated setting means less diver traffic and correspondingly less-disturbed animal behavior than more frequently dived sites in the main atolls.
