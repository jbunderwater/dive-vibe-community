## Shark Airport

Shark Airport takes its name from the sight of whitetip and grey reef sharks resting side by side along the reef, tails pointed up, resembling planes parked at an airport terminal.

## Overview

Located on the North Atoll in the Tubbataha Reefs Natural Park, Shark Airport is a broad plateau at around 15-18 meters dropping to a sandy shelf and ledges near 25-30 meters. It is one of the park's most reliable shark-observation sites: whitetip reef sharks (commonly 10-15 individuals) rest on the sand beneath overhangs, largely undisturbed by divers, while grey reef sharks and occasional nurse sharks pass through the same terrain. Note: earlier text on this page incorrectly attributed the name to the "frequency" of shark arrivals and departures - that account is not supported by any source and has been corrected here.

## Site Information

- **Location**: North Atoll, Tubbataha Reefs Natural Park, Sulu Sea, Philippines
- **Entry Type**: liveaboard
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters

## Marine Life

Whitetip reef sharks resting in numbers on the sandy plateau are the defining sighting, alongside grey reef sharks and, per some sources, nurse sharks. Hawksbill and green sea turtles are regularly seen in the shallows. African pompano, great barracuda, and red snapper are reported around the ledges, and hammerhead sharks are an occasional possibility in the blue rather than a guarantee.
