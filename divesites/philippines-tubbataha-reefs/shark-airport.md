## Shark Airport

Shark Airport is the largest shark cleaning station in the Tubbataha Reefs Natural Park, named for the way reef sharks rest side by side along the sand, tails raised like parked aircraft.

## Overview

Located at the north-eastern edge of North Atoll, Shark Airport is a broad sandy plateau at around 15 meters that steps down to a second shelf near 25 meters. Sharks use the sandy flats as a resting and cleaning area, arriving and departing steadily enough that early visitors gave the site its name. Strong currents sweep the plateau, which is why the site is recommended for advanced divers.

## Site Information

- **Location**: North Atoll, Tubbataha Reefs Natural Park, Sulu Sea, Philippines
- **Entry Type**: liveaboard
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 25 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 20-40 meters
- **Current**: Moderate to strong along the plateau
- **Best Time**: March to June

## Marine Life

Grey reef sharks are the dominant species resting and cruising the plateau, joined by silky sharks and guitar sharks, with whale sharks reported as occasional visitors. Whitetip reef sharks cruise the sloping drop-off beyond the plateau. Hawksbill and green sea turtles are regularly seen working the sunlit shallows. Shark Airport is also a productive night dive site — divers report fimbriated moray eels, blackspotted pufferfish, and giant star pufferfish after dark.

## Entry and Exit

Liveaboard access only. Dinghy entry by giant stride; SMB required for return to dinghy. Park ranger protocols apply to all Tubbataha diving. The site may be on a drift plan depending on current conditions.

## Safety Considerations

Currents across the open plateau can be strong and unpredictable; conservative depth and gas management are recommended given the remote location. Emergency evacuation from Tubbataha is a multi-hour operation. Always carry an SMB.
