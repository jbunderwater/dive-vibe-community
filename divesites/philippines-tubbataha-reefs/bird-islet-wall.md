## Bird Islet Wall

Bird Islet Wall drops off the northeastern corner of Tubbataha's North Atoll, beside the small, low-lying islet that gives the site its name and that many divers and operators rate among the best walls in the park.

## Overview

The wall begins around 5-8 meters and drops steeply; sources disagree on exactly how deep it runs, with one detailed trip account describing a shelf/lip near 40 meters that juts out before the wall continues, while another source describes the wall exceeding 60 meters. Given that disagreement, this entry keeps a conservative 40-meter working depth rather than asserting either figure as definitive. Bird Islet itself is a small sand-and-guano islet that has shrunk from about 1.5 hectares in 2004 to roughly 1.1 hectares in 2016 due to erosion, and it is an important seabird breeding site (home to around 100 recorded bird species) - a reminder that Tubbataha's significance extends above the waterline as well as below it.

## Site Information

- **Location**: North Atoll, Tubbataha Reefs Natural Park, Sulu Sea, Philippines
- **Entry Type**: liveaboard
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters

## Marine Life

The wall face is covered in soft coral, gorgonian growth, and barrel sponges. Reported sightings include leopard sharks, nurse sharks, and reef sharks, along with manta rays, eagle rays, turtles, and flounder. Manta rays are noted as frequent visitors to this stretch of wall.

## Safety Considerations

Currents at Bird Islet Wall are reported to be intense; this is not a beginner-friendly dive, consistent with the destination-wide Advanced minimum for Tubbataha.
