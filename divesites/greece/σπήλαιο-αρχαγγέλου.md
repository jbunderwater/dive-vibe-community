## Overview

Σπήλαιο Αρχαγγέλου — Greek for "Archangelos Cave" — is a sea cave on the eastern coast of Rhodes near the village of Archangelos and Stegna Bay, managed as a visitor attraction by the Grande Blue Hotel. It consists of a swimmable entrance cave with a pebble beach and a second, smaller cave reached further inside. It is reachable on foot from the hotel or by swimming into the bay under a natural rock arch, and is described in tourism sources as a snorkelling and swimming destination with clear water and dramatic light effects, not as a scuba dive site.

No dive operator, dive database, or forum documentation was found describing this location as a scuba cave dive. The previous version of this page described a technical cave dive requiring Advanced certification and cave-diver training (CMAS Cave Diver / PADI-TDI Cave Diver); that framing was not sourced and has been removed, since presenting an unverified overhead-environment dive as a documented technical site is a safety and accuracy risk. If this location is in fact dived by local operators as a cavern or cave site, that has not been confirmed by any source found during this research pass.

Regional marine life for sheltered eastern-Rhodes rocky coastline (grouper, moray eels, octopus, sea bream, wrasse) may be present in and around the cave mouth, but no source confirms specific sightings at this location, so no species are attributed to it here.
