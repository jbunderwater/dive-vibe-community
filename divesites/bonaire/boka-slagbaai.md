# Boka Slagbaai

*The main bay dive in Washington-Slagbaai National Park — a protected inlet with a healthy fringing reef accessible by 4WD, with ballast stones, old anchors, and cannons visible in the shallows. Currently closed to diving — see note below.*

## Overview

Boka Slagbaai is the bay dive at the historic Slagbaai plantation site in Washington-Slagbaai National Park. The bay offers good protection from the prevailing northeast trade wind, making it one of the more sheltered entries on the northwest coast. The fringing reef slopes from the bay's edge to 40 metres, though most marine life is concentrated in the shallower 10–12 metre band. Historical remnants — ballast stones, old anchors and anchor chains, and cannons in about 3 meters of water on the southern route — are a distinctive feature of the site. A STINAPA nature tag and park entry fee are required.

**Site closure**: STINAPA closed all Washington-Slagbaai National Park dive sites, including Boka Slagbaai, on May 1, 2023 to slow the spread of Stony Coral Tissue Loss Disease (SCTLD). InfoBonaire's live dive-site map still lists the site as closed as of this update, and a ScubaBoard discussion from November 2025 confirmed the park's numbered sites remained closed with no reopening expected before at least February 2026. Confirm current status with STINAPA before planning a dive here.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Washington-Slagbaai National Park (4WD + park entry + STINAPA tag required)
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 20–30 metres
- **Current**: Variable; the bay provides some shelter
- **Best Time**: Calm days within park operating hours

## Marine Life

Divers-Guide reports octopus, parrotfish, blenny, green moray eel, lionfish, barracuda, and eagle ray as typical sightings at this site. The low traffic load in the national park means fish behaviour is generally more natural than at the busy southern sites.

## Dive Profile

Shore entry from the bay beach. The reef slopes from the shallows to 40 metres. Most productive in the 10–25 metre range. Plan depth carefully given the 40-metre potential.

## Entry and Exit

4WD access to Slagbaai via the park road. Entry from the bay. Park closes at 17:00 — allow time for the drive out.

## Tips

- **This site is currently closed for diving (see closure note above) — check with STINAPA before planning a visit**
- The historic Slagbaai plantation buildings and salt works are worth exploring above water
- Flamingos frequent the nearby salinas

## Safety

Remote park location. Intermediate depth. Carry an SMB. Be self-sufficient.

## Photography

The bay setting with clear water is good for reef photography. The sheltered bay means calmer conditions than exposed northwest sites.

## Nearby Sites

Nukove is to the south on the main road (no 4WD needed). Wayaka 2 and Playa Funchi are other park sites in the area.
