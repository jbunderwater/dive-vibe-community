# Bisé Morto

*A remote far-north Bonaire reef inside Washington-Slagbaai National Park (BNMP #4) — a fringing reef and shallow coral plateau in the 6–30 metre range. Currently closed to diving — see note below.*

## Overview

Bisé Morto (BNMP #4) is a far-north Bonaire dive site at the edge of Washington-Slagbaai National Park on the northwest coast. The site name translates roughly as "dead deer" in Papiamentu. The reef includes stands of staghorn, elkhorn, brain, and finger coral, with a shallow plateau considered by some divers to be the best part of the site. Like the other far-north sites, it is more exposed than the central west-coast belt, and Divers-Guide notes that shore entry here has been reported as currently impractical — the site is best treated as a boat dive.

**Site closure**: STINAPA closed all Washington-Slagbaai National Park dive sites, including Bisé Morto, on May 1, 2023 to slow the spread of Stony Coral Tissue Loss Disease (SCTLD). InfoBonaire's live dive-site map still lists the site as closed as of this update, and a ScubaBoard discussion from November 2025 confirmed the park's numbered sites remained closed with no reopening expected before at least February 2026. Confirm current status with STINAPA before planning a dive here.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Far-north Bonaire, northwest coast, Washington-Slagbaai National Park — BNMP #4
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 15–30 metres
- **Current**: Medium to strong
- **Best Time**: Calm conditions; morning

## Marine Life

Divers-Guide reports regal angelfish, grouper, triggerfish, scorpionfish, and sea slugs (nudibranchs) at this site, along with common octopus. Both InfoBonaire and Divers-Guide separately note historical sightings of deep-water pelagics passing through — whale sharks, manta rays, and even migrating humpback whales — but these are occasional, not typical, encounters and should not be expected on a given dive.

## Dive Profile

A shallow coral plateau leads to a fringing reef that slopes to around 30 metres. Most reported activity is in the shallower 5–15 metre band on the plateau.

## Entry and Exit

Divers-Guide reports that shore entry at this site is currently impractical; it is normally dived as a boat dive from the far-north coast. Rocky shoreline typical of the northwest coast.

## Tips

- **This site is currently closed for diving (see closure note above) — check with STINAPA before planning a visit**
- Best combined with other north coast sites (Playa Frans, Karpata) on the same day when accessible

## Safety

Intermediate conditions due to exposed location. Check swell and wind before entry. Carry an SMB.

## Photography

Quieter reef with less dive traffic. Good conditions for unhurried reef photography.

## Nearby Sites

Boka Bartol, Boka Katuna, and Playa Frans are in the same far-north zone. Carel's Vision is further south.
