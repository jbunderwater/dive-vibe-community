## Turtle Rock

Turtle Rock is a boulder site at the south-west corner of the main bay of Similan Island 8, next to Donald Duck Bay (IDC Phuket), Similan Islands National Park, Phang Nga Province, Thailand. SSI lists several unrelated "Turtle Rock" sites on other islands, so check location before using other listings.

## Overview

IDC Phuket reports depths mostly 10-15 m, dropping to about 30 m swimming west, with swim-throughs and caverns mostly at 20-30 m, mild current usually running north to south, easy and suited to beginners and night dives. Similan Diving Tours describes a shallow sandy seabed suitable for all levels. The previous maximum of 25 m was not directly confirmed by these sources.

## Site Information

- **Location**: South-west corner of the main bay of Similan Island 8, next to Donald Duck Bay
- **Entry Type**: liveaboard
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 25 meters
- **Current**: Mild, north to south (IDC Phuket)

## Marine Life

IDC Phuket lists turtles, Kuhl's stingrays, gobies including red and purple fire gobies, garden eels, whitetip reef sharks (especially juveniles), leopard sharks, angelfish, butterflyfish, anemonefish, sweetlips, lionfish, scorpionfish, boxfish, octopus, trevally, giant morays, triggerfish and rare ornate ghost pipefish; night dives show banded boxer shrimp, Durban dancing shrimp, crabs and occasionally lobster. Similan Diving Tours shows spotted garden eels in a photo caption.
