## Boonsung Wreck

A former tin-mining dredger lying off the Khao Lak coast, Phang Nga Province, Thailand. It is outside the Similan Islands National Park proper and is dived as a day trip from Khao Lak or as an add-on to Similan trips.

## Overview

Khao Lak Scuba Adventures, IDC Phuket and Not Troubles Just Bubbles all describe the wreck as a former tin dredger. No Wikipedia article or maritime register entry for the vessel was found, so the history below rests on dive-operator accounts only, and those conflict:

- **Sinking date:** 1985 (Khao Lak Scuba Adventures); "between 1983 and 1985, exact date unknown" (IDC Phuket); "abandoned at sea in 1984" within a late-1970s to mid-1980s range (Not Troubles Just Bubbles).
- **Cause:** Not Troubles Just Bubbles says it was probably scuttled on purpose and notes a rumour that the Thai Navy damaged its top because it sat too close to the surface, and that some sources claim an accident. Khao Lak Scuba Adventures repeats a legend that it sank after a flushed toilet let water keep rushing in. IDC Phuket gives no cause. None of these is verified.
- **Size:** IDC Phuket gives about 40 m long, 12 m wide and 6 m high before the tsunami; Not Troubles Just Bubbles gives no dimensions and says it is no more than 5 m high. Other operator pages seen in search results put the length anywhere from about 30 to 60 m. Treat dimensions as unresolved.
- **Tsunami:** All three sources say the 26 December 2004 tsunami broke the wreck up. The number of large pieces is reported as four (Khao Lak Scuba Adventures) or five or six (IDC Phuket).
- **Build date and tonnage:** not found in any source; omitted.

The wreck sits on a flat sandy bottom at about 18-20 m (IDC Phuket, Not Troubles Just Bubbles); Khao Lak Scuba Adventures gives 12-20 m. Not Troubles Just Bubbles says the structure rises to within about 5 m of the surface and that Open Water divers limited to 18 m may find it challenging. Reported visibility ranges from near zero to 20 m+ (IDC Phuket) or 5-15 m (Khao Lak Scuba Adventures). IDC Phuket notes two mooring lines.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Off the Khao Lak coast, Phang Nga Province, Thailand (outside the national park)
- **Entry Type**: day trip
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 20 meters
- **Current**: Not stated in the sources checked

## Marine Life

Species reported at this wreck, by source:

- Khao Lak Scuba Adventures: snappers, fusiliers, batfish, trevally, crocodilefish, stonefish, scorpionfish, lionfish, ornate ghost pipefish, nudibranchs; whale sharks "occasionally" and sailfish also seen.
- IDC Phuket: trevally, barracuda, snappers, fusiliers, porcupinefish, leopard shark, blotched stingray, sweetlips, boxfish, anemonefish, lionfish, scorpionfish, stonefish, grouper, white-eyed, giant and honeycomb moray eels, nudibranchs, cuttlefish.
- Not Troubles Just Bubbles: honeycomb moray eels, porcupinefish and pufferfish, scorpionfish, lionfish, nudibranchs.

No source consulted gives a reliable season for whale shark visits.

## Safety Considerations

Khao Lak Scuba Adventures advises against penetrating the wreck because the separated sections are unstable, and Not Troubles Just Bubbles says divers cannot enter it. Any wreck penetration requires proper training and equipment.
