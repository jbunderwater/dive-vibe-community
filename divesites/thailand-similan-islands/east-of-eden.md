## East of Eden

East of Eden is on Koh Payu (called Island 7 by operators; Wikipedia's park numbering lists Ko Payu as island 6), Similan Islands National Park, Phang Nga Province, Thailand.

## Overview

**Access status conflict.** IDC Phuket reports the site closed to divers from the start of 2011 for coral recovery. Dive Happy's current Similan guide says East of Eden and Anita's Reef "remain closed to protect the corals" with no reopening date. Similan Diving Tours, however, says "it was closed for two years, it is open once more", and Sunrise Divers lists it without a closure. These cannot be reconciled from the pages available; confirm with your operator. The park is open roughly mid-October to mid-May and closed mid-May to mid-October ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Similan_Islands), [Dive Happy](https://divehappy.com/diving-similan-islands-quick-guide/)).

Sunrise Divers describes a gentle current and a slow drift dive.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Koh Payu (Island 7 per operators), Similan Islands National Park, Phang Nga Province, Thailand
- **Entry Type**: liveaboard
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters
- **Current**: Gentle (Sunrise Divers)

## Marine Life

Sunrise Divers reports turtles at this site, and sharks possible in the morning (not named). No other species could be traced to this site; the Similan Islands' regional fauna applies but no further site-specific reports were found.
