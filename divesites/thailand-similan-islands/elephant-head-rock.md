## Elephant Head Rock

Elephant Head Rock (Hin Pousar / Hin Pusar) is a granite boulder site in the Similan Islands National Park, Phang Nga Province, Thailand.

## Overview

Sunrise Divers reports depths over 40 m and says currents can be strong. Dive The World gives a maximum of 50 m, notes 30 m is "deep enough for most divers", and rates it among the more challenging Similan sites because of possible surge and current. Sources checked do not state a precise island location; the dataset coordinates are retained. The earlier claims of swim-through "labyrinth" details beyond these points were not re-confirmed and are omitted.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Hin Pousar, Similan Islands National Park, Phang Nga Province, Thailand
- **Entry Type**: liveaboard
- **Site Type**: Pinnacle/seamount
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters
- **Current**: Can be strong

## Marine Life

Dive The World lists whitetip reef sharks, leopard sharks, nurse sharks, giant trevally, titan triggerfish, Andaman sweetlips, purple fire gobies, McCosker's dwarf wrasse and Similan jawfish at this site. Similan Diving Tours shows a turtle in a photo caption. Cuttlefish, honeycomb morays and angelfish from the previous version were not confirmed and have been removed.
