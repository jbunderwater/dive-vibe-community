## Thailand Similan Islands

Granite-boulder and coral dive sites in the Similan Islands National Park and nearby Koh Bon and Koh Tachai, off Phang Nga Province, Andaman Sea.

## Description

The park is open roughly mid-October to mid-May and closed mid-May to mid-October ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Similan_Islands), [Dive Happy](https://divehappy.com/diving-similan-islands-quick-guide/)). The 2026-27 season reopened on 15 October 2026 according to news and travel pages (for example [Wego](https://blog.wego.com/similan-islands/)). Island numbering differs between sources: Wikipedia lists Ko Payu as island 6 and Ko Hin Pousar as 7, while dive operators number Koh Payu as Island 7 and Koh Similan as Island 8. Site-level closures are reported inconsistently (see East of Eden, Anita's Reef, Beacon Reef and Fantasea Reef), and Wikipedia reports Ko Tachai island has been closed to tourists since 15 October 2016. Always confirm site access with your operator.

### Access

- **Boat diving only**: sites are reached by liveaboard or day-trip boat from the Khao Lak / Phuket area.
- **Currency**: Thai Baht (THB). **Language**: Thai.

### Marine Life

Species are listed on each site page only where a source confirms them at that site. No destination-wide species list is made here.
