## Beacon Reef

Beacon Reef is on the eastern side of Similan Island 8 (Koh Similan), with Beacon Point at its southern end, in Similan Islands National Park, Phang Nga Province, Thailand.

## Overview

Similan Dive Center describes a coral plateau sloping from 5-12 m near the beach and dropping steeply to about 40 m; Similan Diving Tours gives a maximum of 30 m and says the reef is long enough for an hour-long drift dive in medium to strong current; IDC Phuket says the site starts around 5 m and drops to 30 m or deeper. Sunrise Divers reports boulders at 20-35 m. Similan Diving Tours calls it "not too difficult" and suitable for everyone; Similan Dive Center and IDC Phuket describe it as suitable for easy dives, training and all levels. Currents are described as rarely strong (Similan Diving Tours) or moderate (Similan Dive Center).

**Access status conflict:** IDC Phuket states that divers and snorkelers were barred from Beacon Reef (together with East of Eden) at the start of 2011 for coral recovery, and that it remained closed for restoration with no reopening date given; the page is undated. Similan Dive Center, Similan Diving Tours and Sunrise Divers describe the site as dived and mention no closure (Similan Diving Tours' page was last updated 29 March 2017). Confirm current access with your operator.

### Wreck: Rareung-Chon / Atlantis X

A wooden liveaboard named Rareung-Chon, also called Atlantis X (IDC Phuket, Similan Dive Center), lies on the reef. Sources agree it sank in 2002 with no casualties reported; they disagree on the details:

- **Cause:** bad weather (IDC Phuket, which dates it August 2002); a bilge pump malfunction (Similan Dive Center); the captain beaching the bow toward shore and striking the reef (Similan Diving Tours).
- **Depth:** bow about 15-16 m and stern about 30 m (Similan Dive Center, Similan Diving Tours, Sunrise Divers 15-30 m); IDC Phuket instead says it rests at about 28 m on the sloping reef.
- **Condition:** "almost disintegrated" (IDC Phuket) versus "relatively intact" (Similan Diving Tours).
- Build date, length and tonnage were not found in any source and are omitted. No Wikipedia entry was found.

Similan Dive Center advises against penetration because of narrow corridors; any wreck penetration requires proper training and equipment.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Eastern side of Similan Island 8 (Koh Similan), Similan Islands National Park, Phang Nga Province, Thailand
- **Entry Type**: liveaboard
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters
- **Current**: Rarely strong to moderate

## Marine Life

Similan Diving Tours lists green and hawksbill turtles, banded sea krait, batfish, trumpetfish, lionfish, clown and titan triggerfish, moray eels, blue-spotted stingray, nudibranchs, shrimps, crabs and cuttlefish. Similan Dive Center lists angelfish, pufferfish, porcupinefish, butterflyfish, triggerfish, moray eels, bluefin trevally, fusiliers, long-nosed emperors, goatfish, green and hawksbill turtles, banded sea krait, shrimps, crabs and nudibranchs. IDC Phuket mentions batfish above the wreck, oriental sweetlips, soldierfish, butterflyfish and porcelain crabs. Manta rays, eagle rays and leopard sharks listed in a previous version were not confirmed by these sources and have been removed.
