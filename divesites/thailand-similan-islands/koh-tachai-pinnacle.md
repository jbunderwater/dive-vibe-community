## Koh Tachai Pinnacle

Koh Tachai Pinnacle lies about 500 m south of Koh Tachai island (Wikipedia's park numbering: island 11), Similan Islands National Park, Phang Nga Province, Thailand.

**Data-quality note:** The dataset lists "Twin Peaks", "The Dome" and "Koh Tachai Pinnacle" as three entries with coordinates a few hundred meters apart. Sunrise Divers describes one site with a dome-topped main pinnacle plus a smaller second pinnacle; Hobo-ya Similan calls the same formation "Twin Peaks" (a dome-shaped rock ringed by smaller reefs); Divezone lists "Ko Tachai (also called Twin Peaks)" and a separate "Koh Tachai The Dome" entry without saying whether they are the same spot. The three entries very likely overlap and are flagged for maintainer review (possible merge), not deleted here. Wikipedia reports Ko Tachai island has been closed to tourists since 15 October 2016; operators still list the pinnacle dive, so confirm current access with your operator.

## Overview

Sunrise Divers: the main pinnacle top is about 12-14 m with a sandy base from about 30 m; a smaller second pinnacle is reached across sand at about 25 m; "best dived as a deep dive"; currents can be strong, with descent on a line and shelter among boulders. Hobo-ya Similan gives a maximum of 35 m and average 15 m, with the strongest current of the sites on its cruises. Divezone gives a normal diving depth of 10-25 m with medium current. The dataset's 45 m maximum could not be confirmed.

## Site Information

- **Location**: About 500 m south of Koh Tachai, Phang Nga Province, Thailand
- **Entry Type**: liveaboard
- **Site Type**: Pinnacle/seamount
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 45 meters
- **Current**: Strong, variable

## Marine Life

Sunrise Divers: manta rays, leopard sharks on deeper sand, batfish near the pinnacle top, schooling fish, sea fans. Hobo-ya Similan: whale shark, manta ray, leopard shark, whitetip reef shark, giant and bluefin trevally, great barracuda, Napoleon fish, lunar fusilier, longfin batfish, Andaman sweetlips, emperor angelfish, fairy basslets, fire gobies, giant anglerfish. Divezone: manta rays, leopard sharks, turtles, whale sharks "if you're lucky", barracuda, giant trevally, lionfish, pipefish, seahorses, whitetip sharks. Banded sea snakes, mantis shrimp, bigeye trevally and lobsters from the previous version were not confirmed and have been removed.
