## Christmas Point

Christmas Point is a granite boulder dive at the north-west corner of Similan Island 9 (Koh Bangu), Similan Islands National Park, Phang Nga Province, Thailand.

## Overview

Boulders drop to about 35-40 m (Sunrise Divers, IDC Phuket). The signature feature is an arch swim-through at about 24 m (Similan Diving Tours, Sunrise Divers); IDC Phuket also lists a small arch at about 18 m, a triangular tunnel at about 20 m and a large arch at 24-25 m, with a sandy bottom at 30 m and deeper. Similan Diving Tours reports medium current that is strong at certain times, and IDC Phuket says current "can be very strong", usually running south-west to north-east or the reverse, with no mooring line (free descent). Operators differ on suitability: Similan Diving Tours says conditions on the day decide and some operators accept any certified diver; IDC Phuket recommends Advanced Open Water and Deep Diver training. Dive Happy lists Christmas Point as an active site (no closure).

## Site Information

- **Location**: North-west corner of Similan Island 9, Similan Islands National Park, Phang Nga Province, Thailand
- **Entry Type**: liveaboard
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters
- **Current**: Medium to very strong, south-west/north-east, varies with tide

## Marine Life

Species confirmed at Christmas Point by the sources checked:

- Similan Diving Tours: clown anemonefish, Durban shrimp, leopard shark, whitetip and blacktip reef sharks, guitar shark, ribbon eel, chevron barracuda, Napoleon wrasse, oriental sweetlips, bigeye trevally, red fire goby; manta rays and whale sharks "occasional".
- IDC Phuket: whitetip, blacktip and leopard sharks, guitar sharks, rare bull sharks, manta rays, Kuhl's stingrays, whale sharks, Napoleon wrasse, groupers, giant morays, ribbon eels, garden eels, barracuda, trevally, fusiliers, nudibranchs, among others.
- Sunrise Divers: whitetip sharks, nudibranchs, ribbon eels.

Batfish, triggerfish, surgeonfish and unicornfish listed in the previous version were not confirmed by these sources and have been removed.
