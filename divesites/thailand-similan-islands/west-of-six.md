## West of Six

"West of Six" is the name Dive The World uses; Similan Diving Tours, IDC Phuket and Aussie Divers Phuket describe a site called West of Eden on the western side of the northern end of Koh Payu (Island 7 per operators). These appear to be the same site; the match is inferred from location and shared features, not stated by any source.

## Overview

Giant granite boulders, canyons, soft corals and giant sea fans (IDC Phuket). Depth is roughly 10-30 m, with boulders continuing to about 40 m and beyond (Aussie Divers Phuket); IDC Phuket gives 12-35 m. Current is reported to flow south to north almost always (Aussie Divers Phuket). Aussie Divers Phuket and IDC Phuket call it a great dive for all levels; Dive The World notes shallow boulder tops suit less experienced divers. Aussie Divers Phuket reports no closure; Similan Diving Tours calls it good for drift diving. The park is open roughly mid-October to mid-May and closed mid-May to mid-October ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Similan_Islands), [Dive Happy](https://divehappy.com/diving-similan-islands-quick-guide/)).

## Site Information

- **Location**: West side of the northern end of Koh Payu, Similan Islands National Park, Phang Nga Province, Thailand
- **Entry Type**: liveaboard
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters
- **Current**: South to north (Aussie Divers Phuket)

## Marine Life

Dive The World lists frogfish, ribbon eels, giant morays, blotched fantail ray, ornate ghost pipefish, tiger cowries, turtles and great barracuda. Aussie Divers Phuket additionally lists, among others, sweetlips, batfish, frogfish, garden eels, ribbon eels, ghost pipefish, spiny lobsters and turtles; IDC Phuket lists lobsters, red fire gobies, cowries and stonefish.
