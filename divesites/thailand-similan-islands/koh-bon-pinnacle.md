## Koh Bon Pinnacle

Koh Bon Pinnacle is a submerged pinnacle at Koh Bon (island 10 in Wikipedia's park numbering), north of the main Similan group, Similan Islands National Park, Phang Nga Province, Thailand.

## Overview

Aussie Divers Phuket describes Koh Bon as a cleaning station for manta rays with depths of 5-40 m and notes that, during the 2011-2012 liveaboard season, it was the standout place to see mantas in the region (the page does not describe it as a pinnacle). Dive Happy describes yellow soft corals covering craggy limestone and manta rays sometimes appearing, and says Koh Bon is usually dived on liveaboard itineraries. Search-result summaries of other operator pages give the pinnacle top at about 18 m and the base between 40 and 50 m, and describe a yellow-soft-coral west wall; those pages could not be fetched, so depth is reported as a range. Wikipedia notes Ko Bon and Ko Tachai were added to the national park in 1998. The park is open roughly mid-October to mid-May and closed mid-May to mid-October ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Similan_Islands), [Dive Happy](https://divehappy.com/diving-similan-islands-quick-guide/)).

## Site Information

- **Location**: Koh Bon, north of the main Similan Islands, Similan Islands National Park, Phang Nga Province, Thailand
- **Entry Type**: liveaboard
- **Site Type**: Pinnacle/seamount
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters
- **Current**: Reported strong in some sources; not re-confirmed

## Marine Life

Aussie Divers Phuket reports manta rays at a cleaning station, plus octopus, cuttlefish, barracuda and numerous shrimp at Koh Bon. Dive Happy reports manta rays. Eagle rays and blacktip reef sharks from the previous version were not confirmed and have been removed.
