## Deep Six

Deep Six is a boulder site off the north point of Koh Payu (Island 7 per operators), Similan Islands National Park, Phang Nga Province, Thailand.

## Overview

Dive The World says the rock starts about 5 m below the surface and drops beyond 40 m, rated intermediate to advanced because currents can be strong. Similan Diving Tours places it north of Island 7 and suited to experienced divers. Aussie Divers Phuket's West of Eden page says West of Eden ends where Deep Six begins. Wikipedia's park numbering lists Ko Payu as island 6, which may explain the "Six" in the name; operators number it 7. The "Living Room" feature in the previous version was not re-confirmed and is omitted.

## Site Information

- **Location**: North point of Koh Payu, Similan Islands National Park, Phang Nga Province, Thailand
- **Entry Type**: liveaboard
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters
- **Current**: Can be strong (Dive The World)

## Marine Life

Dive The World lists boxfish, lyretail grouper, powderblue surgeonfish, emperor angelfish, moray eels, blue-spotted stingrays, whitetip reef sharks, leopard sharks, dogtooth tuna and giant trevally. Similan Diving Tours shows a blue-spotted stingray in a photo caption. Rainbow runners and fusilier schools from the previous version were not confirmed.
