## Anita's Reef

Anita's Reef is a gently sloping reef and boulder site in the Similan Islands National Park, Phang Nga Province, Thailand.

## Overview

Sunrise Divers gives a range from 5 m to nearly 30 m; Dive The World calls it easy with gentle currents, a good check-out dive; Similan Diving Tours describes mild currents and a very popular site. **Access status conflict:** Dive Happy's guide says Anita's Reef remains closed to protect the corals (no reopening date), while Similan Diving Tours and Sunrise Divers list it without any closure. Confirm with your operator. Sunrise Divers also mentions a fishing-boat wreck at 28-40 m here; this appears in only one source and is omitted under the two-source rule. The earlier statement placing the site on the east coast of Island 5 was not confirmed by the sources checked.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Similan Islands National Park, Phang Nga Province, Thailand (island not confirmed)
- **Entry Type**: liveaboard
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 28 meters
- **Current**: Mild

## Marine Life

Dive The World lists lionfish, oriental sweetlips, clown triggerfish, titan triggerfish, longnose hawkfish, bluefin and giant trevally, spotted garden eels, Kuhl's stingrays and fantail stingray at Anita's Reef.
