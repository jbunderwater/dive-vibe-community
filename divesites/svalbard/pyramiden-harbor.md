## Pyramiden Harbor

Pyramiden Harbor lies at Pyramiden, an abandoned Soviet coal-mining settlement on Spitsbergen.

## Overview

Pyramiden was founded by Sweden in 1910 and sold to the Soviet Union in 1927. As much as nine million tonnes of coal were extracted between 1955 and 1998; mining stopped on 31 March 1998 and the last permanent resident left on 10 October that year. The hotel was renovated and reopened in 2013. Wikipedia reports a small summer population, mostly tourism workers.
