## Isfjorden Kelp Forest

Isfjorden Kelp Forest is a boat-dive entry in Svalbard's second-longest fjord.

## Overview

Isfjorden is the second-longest fjord in Svalbard and the largest in the archipelago, on Spitsbergen's west coast, with Alkhornet guarding its northern entrance and Barentsburg, Longyearbyen and Pyramiden among the settlements around it. Wikipedia records a first temporary whaling station in 1612, with the English whaling there until at least the late 1650s.

The fjord's Wikipedia article says nothing about kelp or diving. Detailed kelp research found in this pass concerns Kongsfjorden, not Isfjorden, so no kelp-forest description is given here and the "kelp forest" label in the site name is unconfirmed by any source found. The Arctic fauna of the region applies in general, but no site-specific marine-life reports were found for this site, so no species are named.
