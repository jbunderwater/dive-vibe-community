## транспортный рефриджератор "Петрозаводск"

The Petrozavodsk is a refrigerated cargo ship that ran aground at the southern tip of Bear Island (Bjørnøya) on 11 May 2009 and remains there, broken in two.

## Overview

MS Petrozavodsk was a Russian refrigerated cargo ship (reefer) acting as a freezer ship for a fleet of fishing vessels in the Barents Sea. She ran aground on 11 May 2009 on rocks at the foot of high cliffs near the southern tip of Bear Island, a reef just under the cliffs, and all 12 crew were rescued by helicopter. Two sources report the cause as a grounding with the first officer on watch: a Norwegian court sentenced the captain to 15 days and the first officer to 40 days in prison; both were accused of being under the influence of alcohol, and the first officer was also accused of falling asleep on duty and of entering a protected zone (closed to vessels over 40 feet between 15 May and 31 August, to protect seabird colonies).

Removal was judged dangerous because of frequent rockfalls from the cliffs. By March 2010 the bow had broken away from the rest of the ship, and Wikipedia reports the wreck had split in two by 2011. A June 2013 expedition-cruise log describes the wreck, seen from a Zodiac, as showing advanced rust and decay. Spill reporting differs: Wikipedia states the diesel (about 50 tons on board) spread over roughly 3 square kilometres but evaporated with little damage, gCaptain reported only a small sheen that dissipated, and Spitsbergen-Svalbard.com noted about 53 tons of fuel on board and that the ship seemed to be losing fuel, probably heavy oil.

We found no dive report, depth survey or operator listing for this wreck. The 25 m maximum depth in our data is unverified (the OSM record also tags the wreck as visible at both high and low tide, so it is at least partly above water). Any dive would involve an unstable, deteriorating structure beneath rockfall-prone cliffs, inside a seabird protection zone, and should not be attempted without checking current rules with the Governor of Svalbard. No claim is made that penetrating the wreck is safe.

## Wreck History

- **Name history**: Nordlandia (1980) -> Zapolyarye (2003) -> Petrozavodsk (2004), per Wikipedia (single source for the renaming).
- **Built**: launched 4 September 1978 by Skala Skipasmidja, Faroe Islands; in service 2 February 1980 (Wikipedia only; not confirmed by a second source).
- **Dimensions**: 67 m length and 12 m beam (Wikipedia only; no second source found), 1,130 GT (Wikipedia only). Deadweight conflicts: Wikipedia gives 1,372 DWT while gCaptain and MarineLink give 1,287 DWT.
- **Wrecked**: 11 May 2009, southern tip of Bear Island (Wikipedia, Spitsbergen-Svalbard.com, Barents Observer).
- **Cause**: grounding; sentences and intoxication/sleeping allegations as above.
- **Current state**: bow separated by March 2010; split into two parts by 2011 per Wikipedia; decaying in place.
