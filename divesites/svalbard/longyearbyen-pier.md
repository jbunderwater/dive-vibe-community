## Longyearbyen Pier

Longyearbyen Pier is a harbour dive entry at Svalbard's main settlement.

## Overview

Longyearbyen lies on the south-western shore of Adventfjorden, a bay about 7 km long and 4 km wide on the southern side of Isfjorden (Wikipedia). The town has the Longyearbyen Diving Club (LDK), a small non-commercial club run by local enthusiasts that organises diving; its website links to a "Dive Sites" page, but no pier, harbour or specific site is named in the content found.
