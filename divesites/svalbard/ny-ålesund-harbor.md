## Ny-Ålesund Harbor

Ny-Ålesund Harbor is an Arctic reef dive site in Kongsfjorden, Svalbard.

## Overview

Ny-Ålesund is a research settlement on the shore of Kongsfjorden, Svalbard, at about 78°55'N. The Kings Bay Marine Laboratory there lists a dive locker and a compression chamber; Kings Bay AS describes it as the only decompression chamber on Svalbard, and says use must be coordinated with laboratory staff and relevant diving groups. The laboratory is located right on a beach with easy access to boats. Scientific diving in Kongsfjorden (BlueARC project) has documented kelp forests of large oarweed, winged kelp and sugar kelp from the surface to roughly 15-25 metres, with red algae persisting to at least 40 metres.

No source found describes the harbour dive site itself (depth profile, bottom, temperature, visibility, access rules for recreational divers). Earlier text on those points, on a minimum of 30 drysuit dives, on the absence of commercial dive operations, and a species list of starfish, nudibranchs and shrimp, could not be traced to any source and has been removed. Fjord-level wildlife reports are on the [Kongsfjorden](kongsfjorden.md) page; no site-specific marine-life report was found for the harbour. Any diving here needs coordination with Kings Bay AS and polar-safety planning.
