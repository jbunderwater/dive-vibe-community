## Ny-Ålesund Harbor

Ny-Ålesund Harbor is a research-station harbour on Kongsfjorden, Svalbard, used as a base for scientific diving.

## Overview

Ny-Ålesund, at about 78°55'N, is described by Wikipedia as the northernmost functional civilian settlement in the world. It is a research town owned and operated by Kings Bay, with institutions from many countries. The Kings Bay Marine Laboratory sits on the beach near the harbour with easy access to boats, and has a dive locker (tank filling panel for 200 and 300 bar) and a Norwegian Underwater Technology twin-lock decompression chamber, which Kings Bay describes as the only one on Svalbard. Kings Bay does not supply compressor or tanks; visiting dive groups bring their own, and diving access must be coordinated in advance with Kings Bay Laboratory staff, with the dive group responsible for its own dive safety and planning.

The surrounding Kongsfjorden has been studied by scientific divers; Framforum reports kelp forests of large oarweed, winged kelp and sugar kelp extending from the surface to 15-25 m, with red algae persisting to at least 40 m, and a 7-8 fold increase in shallow kelp biomass at the Hansneset study site since the 1996/98 surveys. Those surveys were made at Hansneset and other fjord sites, not specifically in the harbour. We found no harbour-specific dive report, so the 18 m maximum depth, site type and boat entry in our data are unverified. Wikipedia notes that airport tickets to Ny-Ålesund are only available with Kings Bay's permission, so independent recreational visits are not straightforward.

## Marine Life

No harbour-specific marine-life reports were found. Reports of fauna for the wider fjord are on the [Kongsfjorden](kongsfjorden.md) page.

## Requirements

Diving here is organised through the research infrastructure: coordination with Kings Bay Laboratory is required. We found no published recreational-diver requirements, so none are stated here.
