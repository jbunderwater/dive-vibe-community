## Ny-Ålesund Harbor

Ny-Ålesund Harbor is a research-station harbour entry on Kongsfjorden, Svalbard.

## Overview

Ny-Ålesund sits on the northwest coast of Kongsfjorden. The Kings Bay Marine Laboratory there is "located right on a beach with easy access to boats" and lists a dive locker and a compression chamber among its facilities (Ny-Ålesund Research Station). The BlueARC scientific diving campaigns (May 2024 and September 2025) were run from this base and revisited fjord transects such as Hansneset; those surveys were not specifically in the harbour.

No source found describes the harbour's own depth, bottom, visibility or water temperature, so those values are not given; the 18 m depth below is from the existing unverified dataset. The Kongsfjorden kelp findings are described on the Kongsfjorden page and are not attributed to the harbour here. The Arctic fauna of the region applies in general, but no site-specific marine-life reports were found for this site, so no species are named.

## Access and Requirements

No commercial dive operation at Ny-Ålesund was identified. Oceanwide Expeditions requires divers on its Spitsbergen trips to be advanced and experienced in cold-water diving with at least 30 drysuit dives; this is that operator's requirement, not a harbour-specific rule. Diving at the research station itself appears to be organised for scientific projects; contact Kings Bay AS before planning any dive.
