## Coconut Tree Reef

Coconut Tree Reef (also called Coconut Tree Rocks) is a reef off St. Kitts that begins on a flat and slopes to a wall reported to reach about 31 metres.

## Overview

Pro Divers calls Coconut Tree Rocks a typical reef dive for the area with an abundance of small fish, at 40-80 ft with sand grottos. Active Caribbean Travel says the reef reaches about 40 ft (12 m) along with a wall that plunges to 100 ft (31 m). Dive The World says the dive begins on the flat and slopes to greater depths. Depths differ by source (12-24 m versus up to 31 m); the deeper figure is used.

## Site Information

- **Location**: St. Kitts
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 31 meters

## Marine Life

Small groupers, grunts and snapper are reported by Dive The World, Pro Divers and Active Caribbean Travel. Pro Divers also lists barracuda, spotted moray eels and lobsters; Active Caribbean Travel adds eels, turtles and nurse sharks.
