## Monkey Shoals

Monkey Shoals is a coral atoll of about one square mile in the Narrows between St. Kitts and Nevis, with several dive sites ranging from shallow shelves to deeper overhangs.

## Overview

Pro Divers describes Monkey Shoals as an atoll of coral about 1 mile square, located directly between St. Kitts and Nevis and about three miles offshore, with excellent visibility and plentiful fish life. My St Kitts Dive Buddy describes a number of sites from shallow fish nurseries to deep overhangs, with choppy conditions and occasional currents; Pro Divers notes currents and wind can sometimes prevent diving. Reported depths vary by source: 40-70 ft (12-21 m) per My St Kitts Dive Buddy and Pro Divers, and 12-31 m per Active Caribbean Travel, so the deeper figure is used here. Dive Training Magazine calls it a shallow limestone reef.

## Site Information

- **Location**: The Narrows, between St. Kitts and Nevis
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 31 meters

## Marine Life

Nurse sharks, moray eels and large spiny lobsters are reported at the deeper overhangs (My St Kitts Dive Buddy). Scorpionfish, lobster, nurse sharks and flying gurnards are listed by Active Caribbean Travel, and Dive Training Magazine reports rays, occasional nurse sharks and flying gurnards.
