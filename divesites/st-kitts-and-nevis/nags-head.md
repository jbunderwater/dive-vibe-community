## Nag's Head

Nag's Head is a battered rock formation at the southern tip of St. Kitts, described as the island's only drift dive and recommended for experienced divers.

## Overview

Pro Divers locates Nag's Head on the southernmost tip of St. Kitts, a battered rock formation at 30-60 ft with volcanic boulders covered in fire coral and sponges. Dive Training Magazine calls it St. Kitts' only drift dive, for experienced divers, with strong currents where the Caribbean clashes with the Atlantic. Active Caribbean Travel gives 6-24 m. Dive Nevis separately describes Nag's Head North (15-60 ft): a gentle sandy slope on one side and coral-covered boulders on the other. Earlier versions called this a wall; no source checked in this pass describes a wall, so the site type is now drift.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Southern tip of St. Kitts
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Drift dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 24 meters

## Marine Life

Pro Divers reports black sea urchins and occasional eagle rays; Active Caribbean Travel lists corals, sponges, grasses, sea urchins and rays. At Nag's Head North, Dive Nevis reports southern stingrays buried in sand, tarpon and schools of brown chromis.
