## Secca del Semaforo

Deep gorgonian-covered pinnacle on sand, about half a mile off Capo Enfola, dived as a technical dive in the absence of current.

## Overview

Diving in Elba places the Secca del Semaforo about half a mile off Capo Enfola. It describes a large "panettone" on the sand that rises from 52 m to about 40 m, covered with red gorgonians (Paramuricea clavata), yellow gorgonians (Eunicella cavolini) and many sponges. A diver's account (Underwater Tales) puts the site inside the Gulf of Viticcio, with the top at 38 m and white sand at 55 m, and found the granite block's crevices holding congers, morays, scorpionfish, European lobsters and large spiny lobsters; it also notes bryozoans on the gorgonians. Bolle d'Azoto gives 34-55 m (rock rising from over 50 m to about 35 m) and a minimum of Rec2/Advanced. Blu Elba describes the top at 40 m and says the anchor is normally dropped on it, with red and yellow gorgonians, small and large groupers, octopus, scorpionfish and lobsters.

Catshark eggs are found on the gorgonians, and small-spotted catsharks (Scyliorhinus canicola) may stay in the crevices after laying (Diving in Elba, Bolle d'Azoto, Underwater Tales). A large St. Peter's fish (Zeus faber) is reported by Diving in Elba and Bolle d'Azoto. Diving in Elba lists the dive as 42-52 m, "very difficult", possible only without current, and reserved for divers with technical certification; the descent is a jump into the blue beyond 40 m along the anchor line or a buoy. The account from Underwater Tales describes a long decompression with two decompression cylinders. A smaller neighbouring pinnacle, the Semaforino, is described separately by Diving in Elba and is not covered here.

Elba CED uses the name "Secca del Semaforo" for a secca in the Gulf of Viticcio about 300 m from the coast with a top at 28 m, which matches what Blu Elba lists as a separate site, "Secca del Viticcio" (top at 28 m, base 40 m on sand and Posidonia, advanced). These two are probably different rocks, so the depth figures for "Semaforo" above (top 34-40 m, base 52-55 m) follow the majority of sources.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Elba, Europe (about half a mile off Capo Enfola, Gulf of Enfola/Viticcio, Portoferraio)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Pinnacle/seamount
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 52 meters
