## Punta Morcone

Wall dive in front of the point that shelters Morcone beach on the west side of the Calamita promontory, descending from a rocky cap at about 3 m to about 40 m.

## Overview

The dive is made in front of Punta Morcone, from which it takes its name. Infoelba describes a start on a rocky cap at about 3 m and a descent along a wall covered with Parazoanthus to about 40 m. Around 27 m there are dens of octopus, conger eels and moray eels; further down come yellow gorgonians and a few groupers. On the way up through the posidonia meadow gobies, small torpedo rays, wrasse and rainbow wrasse are found, with an occasional cuttlefish and damselfish accompanying the return to the boat. Marina di Campo Diving also describes a spot well populated by octopus, with morays, congers and groupers in the crevices and torpedo rays, wrasse, gobies and rainbow wrasse in the posidonia.

Scubago places the site off the Punta Morcone promontory on the southern coast of Elba, with the wall dropping from about 11 m to 40 m, and lists congers, morays, scorpionfish and octopus in the holes of the wall and barracuda often seen in the deep zone. Aquanautic Elba, whose base is on Morcone beach, lists a depth range of 7-38 m, easy navigation and possible currents. None of the sources gives an explicit difficulty rating.

It is not the same as the shallow, walk-in dive in Morcone bay (2-10 m) listed separately on Scubago. The park's rules page states that the waters of Elba are not included in the perimeter of the Park.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Elba, Europe (off Punta Morcone, Morcone bay, Capoliveri)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters
