## Secca di Capo Vita

Offshore boulder shoal north of Capo Vita, in the Piombino channel off Cavo, with its top at about 6 m and tunnels and swim-throughs between stacked rocks.

## Overview

Elba CED describes an oval shoal of about 300 x 200 m in the middle of the channel between Elba and Piombino, north of Capo Vita and about half an hour by boat from Cavo, whose top starts at 6 m and reaches 20 m on a sandy bottom. Bolle d'Azoto (Cavo) places it about one mile from the tip of Capo Vita, with a plateau at about 5 m and a base at about 20 m. Cavo Diving quotes 7-22 m on the north-west side and 7-24 m on the south-east side, and says the shoal is marked on nautical charts.

The shoal is a pile of large boulders forming caves, tunnels and passages, with sponges and parazoanthus in many colours and light filtering through the cracks (Elba CED, Infoelba). Infoelba describes a first tunnel of about 6-7 m that narrows until divers would have to swim backwards, near a second, wider tunnel. Cavo Diving describes a small sponge-covered cave with two large moray eels and a tunnel about 30 m long ending in a small room open to the sky, and advises looking at a second, blind tunnel only from the outside. These are overhead environments and should only be entered with suitable training, equipment and local guidance. Infoelba mentions an old, encrusted anchor with its chain at about 13 m; Cavo Diving mentions a large anchor abandoned long ago by a dredger, used to position the mooring on the top of the shoal. The sources do not say whether these are the same anchor.

Reported marine life includes conger eels, moray eels and octopus in the crevices (Elba CED, Cavo Diving), scorpionfish and corvina (Infoelba, Cavo Diving), groupers, some lobsters, clouds of white seabream (Cavo Diving) and fan mussels (Pinna nobilis) on the sand beyond the tunnels (Infoelba). Cavo Diving says the shoal is popular with freedivers and spearfishers, so the fauna is wary. Cavo Diving states that the current normally runs from north-west to south-east; Bolle d'Azoto says currents dominate the site; Elba CED calls the dive easy but warns of passing vessels. Bolle d'Azoto lists 6-20 m with a minimum Open Water certification.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Elba, Europe (offshore north of Capo Vita, off Cavo, north-east Elba)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Pinnacle/seamount
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 24 meters
