## Gemini East

Eastern point of the Gemini islets, a pair of small rocky islets off the west coast of the Capo Calamita promontory in Capoliveri.

## Overview

Aquanautic Elba's dive-site map lists this point as "Gemini Ost" (Gemini East), with a depth range of 0-20 m, easy navigation, no current, and octopus and snails as the featured animals. It is one of three separate Gemini points on that map (West, East and Rock); on the supplied coordinates East is 130 m from West.

For the Gemini rocks as a whole, VirtualElba and Wikipedia describe a rocky bottom with small caves and natural arches at 15-20 m, yellow Parazoanthus axinellae with damselfish, and on the north-western slope gorgonians, lobsters and Anthias anthias; nudibranchs such as Flabellina and Hypselodoris valenciennesi are also mentioned. These are group-level descriptions and are not attributed to this point specifically.

No site-specific description of the topography, entry method or conditions of the east point was found beyond the operator's map entry. ElbaWorld notes heavy boat and snorkeller traffic around the Gemini in the tourist season.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Elba, Europe (eastern Gemini islet, off Capo Calamita, Capoliveri)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 20 meters
