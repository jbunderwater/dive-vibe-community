## Secca di Santa Lucia

Small three-rock shoal rising to 6-8 meters, a few hundred meters off Elba's north coast opposite Capo Bianco.

## Overview

The Secca di Santa Lucia lies almost opposite Capo Bianco, a few hundred meters from the north coast, and is passed when leaving the roadstead of Portoferraio toward Capo Enfola. It is a classic cone with a main rock and two smaller rocks slightly apart. Operators disagree on the depths: Elba CED and Bolle d'Azoto give the top at 6 m and the maximum at 20 m, while Diving in Elba and Blu Elba give the top at 8 m and a range of 8-22 m, with the rock dropping to sand at 22 m. Blu Elba puts the tops of the two smaller rocks, to the south-east, at about 18 m; Bolle d'Azoto says they are detached about ten meters from the main rock.

The boat anchors on the top and divers descend the eastern slope, the steepest and most scenic, covered with Parazoanthus (Diving in Elba). The dive then goes south along the fissures, around the two small rocks, and back to the main rock for two laps at different depths, or, in Elba CED's version, a spiral path. Named for this site are large scorpionfish, octopus, conger and moray eels (Elba CED notes a long-standing shared lair of large congers and morays on the small rocks), some shy groupers, corvina and dentex hunting over the Posidonia, and clouds of damselfish and saddled sea bream. Barracuda are reported: Elba CED describes a resident school for several years, while Blu Elba says groups are only sometimes seen. Diving in Elba rates the site easy with absent to light current, and Blu Elba says it suits all levels.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Elba, Europe (off Capo Bianco, on the Portoferraio harbour approach)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Pinnacle/seamount
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 22 meters
