## Picchi di Pablo

Two rocky pinnacles that rise to within about 5 m of the surface above a vertical wall falling to about 38 m, on the Capoliveri coast near Punta Bianca.

## Overview

The site is named after two pinnacles that stand above a vertical wall ending on a sandy bottom at about 38 m (Stefano Sub, Isola d'Elba App); other sources give 35 m (Infoelba, Puntala Diving Center), 36 m (Biodivers) or about 40 m (Rio Diving), so the base is best described as 35-40 m. Elba CED gives a depth range of 5 to 38 m and describes a rocky channel between the wall and the coast where groups of sea breams and salps gather. From about 8 m the outer wall has deep crevices with moray eels, octopus, scorpionfish and forkbeards, and the pinnacle between 8 and 12 m is covered in Parazoanthus (sea daisies). At the foot of the wall, a group of boulders carries red gorgonians and the burrows of large conger eels; Elbainsel, Stefano Sub and Infoelba also report yellow gorgonians (Eunicella). Scubaportal and Elba CED place a small cave with shrimp on its roof at about 32 m; Elbainsel and Marelino Sub say 35 m and Rio Diving says 40 m. The shrimp is given as Plesionika narval by Scubaportal and Elbainsel and as Parapandalus by Isola d'Elba App and Stefano Sub.

Elba CED, Isola d'Elba App, Infoelba and Scubaportal describe the dive as suitable for beginners and experienced divers alike because the upper part of the pinnacles can be dived at shallow depth; Stefano Sub rates it easy and Biodivers medium-easy, and both list the current as light or slight. Daily Nautica quotes a range from 3 m to 39 m. Access is by boat from Porto Azzurro; sources give the trip as about 40 minutes (Isola d'Elba App) or about 15 minutes (Scubaportal), and the mooring buoy is at 9 m according to Infoelba or 5-6 m on rock according to a 2011 report. Scubaportal also mentions seahorses in the nearby bay at about 5 m. Night dives are offered by at least one operator.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Elba, Europe (near Punta Bianca, Capoliveri coast, south of Capo Calvo)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 38 meters
