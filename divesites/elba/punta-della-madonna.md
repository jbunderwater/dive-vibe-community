## Punta della Madonna

Boat wall dive on the promontory just west of Marciana Marina, with a fractured wall, a canyon and two statues.

## Overview

Punta della Madonna is the second point after the port of Marciana Marina and lies a few minutes by boat from the port. The promontory extends about a hundred meters out to sea. Underwater there is a second promontory, a submerged "panettone" slightly detached from the coast, where the boat moors. Diving in Elba describes descending the inner side of the wall from terrace to terrace, reaching a rock plateau at about 30 m, after which the wall continues well beyond 40 m toward open water. The profile it describes then turns into a longitudinal canyon broken by horizontal fissures, ending at a plateau between two rocks that holds a statue of Christ, now colonized by marine growth. Elba CED describes a vertical canyon rising from 35 to 18 m and a weathered statue of the Madonna to the west; DuecentoBar places a small Madonna statue at about 14 m.

Sources give different figures for the wall: about 42 m (Elba CED), beyond 44 m (Visit Tuscany), a 0-45 m range (Diving in Elba) and 12 m to over 40 m (DuecentoBar). Named for this site are lobsters, conger and moray eels, sea bream, octopus, cardinalfish (Apogon imberbis), Stenopus spinosus, Galathea, small slipper lobsters, cowries, hermit crabs, nudibranchs and Parazoanthus; Elba CED also mentions an occasional resident European lobster. Diving in Elba rates the dive easy with medium current, and Elba CED says it suits all certification levels, from beginners to experienced divers.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Elba, Europe (Punta della Madonna, west of Marciana Marina)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 42 meters
