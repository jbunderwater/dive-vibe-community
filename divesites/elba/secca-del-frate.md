## Secca del Frate

Shoal with two rocky caps just north of the islet of Palmaiola in the Piombino channel, exposed to current and visited by eagle rays and other pelagic fish.

## Overview

Elba CED places the shoal in the channel between Elba and Piombino, north of Palmaiola and about 100 m from the rock, with two caps about 30 m apart (one north-east, one south-west); the boat anchors on the southern cap at about 7 m. The most interesting area is reached keeping the two caps on the left: a vertical wall down to 18 m, then a gentle slope onto sand at 25 m. Infoelba gives a top at 3 m and a maximum of 30 m, and Bolle d'Azoto describes a rocky cone rising from 30 m to about 5 m below the surface, with a sandy bottom and one side falling into the blue while the other slopes gently. Riodiving describes two seccas about 250 m north-east of Palmaiola named after the nearby rock, il Frate.

Currents are the defining feature. Elba CED reports medium to strong currents all year; Infoelba says the current is often very strong; Diving in Elba reserves the dive for experienced divers despite the modest depths. Riodiving says a westerly current can make conditions very difficult but gives very clear water, while an easterly (Levante) current makes the water much more turbid. Bolle d'Azoto lists an Open Water minimum and warns of boat traffic overhead at the surface.

Reported marine life: eagle rays in the blue (Elba CED, Diving in Elba, Riodiving, Bolle d'Azoto); tuna and large amberjack (Diving in Elba); dentex hunting damselfish on the second shoal (Riodiving); occasional sunfish in season and dolphins in summer (Bolle d'Azoto); damselfish, rainbow wrasse, octopus, moray and conger eels in holes (Riodiving); groupers, scorpionfish, yellow sea fans, sea spirographs and nudibranchs along the wall (Infoelba), with lobsters in the cracks (Bolle d'Azoto); and rocks coated in parazoanthus (Diving in Elba, Riodiving).

## Site Information

- **Location**: Elba, Europe (Piombino channel, north of Palmaiola islet, off Cavo and Rio Marina)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Pinnacle/seamount
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters
