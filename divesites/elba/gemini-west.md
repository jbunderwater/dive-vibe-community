## Gemini West

Western point of the Gemini islets, a pair of small rocky islets off the west coast of the Capo Calamita promontory near Innamorata beach in Capoliveri.

## Overview

The Isole Gemini are a group of small rocky islets very close to the western coast of the Capo Calamita promontory, near Innamorata beach in the municipality of Capoliveri. Scubago describes "Gemini West" as a shallow dive site, ideal for beginners, where you follow a scree edge towards the open sea, from the surface to a maximum depth of 14 m. It lists snails, scorpionfish, moray eels, rays, octopus, cuttlefish, barracuda and sand eels. Aquanautic Elba's dive-site map lists the same point with a depth range of 0-12 m, very easy navigation, no current, and snails and crabs as its featured animals.

General descriptions of the Gemini rocks (VirtualElba, Wikipedia) mention a rocky bottom with small caves and natural arches at 15-20 m, yellow Parazoanthus axinellae with damselfish, and on the north-western slope gorgonians, lobsters and Anthias anthias, plus nudibranchs such as Flabellina and Hypselodoris valenciennesi. Because this point is reported at 12-14 m, those deeper features probably belong to other parts of the group. ElbaWorld notes that the area is busy with snorkellers and boats in the tourist season, so care is needed around motorised craft.

The sources read do not state the entry method for this point; the operator map corresponds to boat dives from Morcone. Aquanautic lists Gemini West, Gemini East and Gemini Rock as separate points, while other sources treat the Gemini rocks as a single dive area.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Elba, Europe (western Gemini islet, off Capo Calamita, Capoliveri)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 14 meters
