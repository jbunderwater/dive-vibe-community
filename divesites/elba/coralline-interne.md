## Coralline Interne

Coralline Interne is a coral-rich rock ridge off Capo Fonza on Elba's south coast, listed by dive centres as a dive separate from the neighbouring Secca di Fonza.

## Overview

Dive Center Blu lists the Coralline interne as a short distance from the Secca di Fonza: a submerged tongue of rock rich in coral, described as an excellent dive for any level, even the most advanced, where a very large lobster nicknamed "il mostro" can sometimes be seen at depth. Aquanautic Elba's dive-site map marks it with red coral and a chimney ("Kamin"), a depth of 3-42 m, navigation from easy to demanding and possible current.

Other centres describe a Coralline dive at Capo Fonza with matching features; that they are the same dive is an inference, not stated by the pages. Elba CED says emerging rocks lie about 80 m from shore beyond Capo di Fonza, where three dives are possible. The first is an east-facing wall down to about 30 m, from where divers can ascend along a siphon (chimney) to about 15 m, with red coral in the crevices, white and yellow gorgonians, and the wall resting on sand at about 42 m. Marina di Campo Diving gives a top at 4 m dropping to 45 m and describes channels descending from the top; in one, at about 30 m, is the entrance to a cave whose vault is covered with coral branches (some cut in the past), with a second siphon leading up to about 15 m and the entrance as the only exit; it warns divers not to raise sediment. Overhead spaces like these need appropriate training and equipment and are not suitable for untrained divers. Species named for the Coralline dive include moray eels, conger eels, groupers, nudibranchs and crustaceans. The deeper variants called Coralline Esterne, with caps at about 28 m and 40 m, need experience and suitable certification (Elba CED).

The name overlaps with others: Diving in Elba calls the Secca di Fonza "Corallina", and Elbaworld's "Le Coralline" (a 37 m limestone mass, maximum depth 40 m, marked by a yellow buoy) may refer to either. Entry is not stated by any page; the dive is offered by boat-based dive centres.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Elba, Europe (off Capo Fonza, between the Gulfs of Marina di Campo and Lacona)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 42 meters
