## La Miniera

A dive site on the Capoliveri coast of south-east Elba that a local dive centre lists for its black sand and spider crabs; little else is documented.

## Overview

The only site-specific source found is the public dive-site map of Aquanautic Elba, a Capoliveri dive centre, which marks "La Miniera" at these coordinates and lists its special features as black sand and spider crabs, a depth of 6-40 m, easy navigation and a rarely present current. The name was supplied as "La Mineria"; the map spells it "La Miniera". The name presumably refers to the nearby iron mines of Monte Calamita, but no source confirms this.

No source reviewed describes the underwater topography, the entry type, the presence of any mining remains or the marine life beyond the spider crabs. The site type and entry type shown here are placeholders: the map only says the bottom is black sand, so "muck" is the closest available category, and the boat entry is assumed from the dive centre's boat-based operation, not stated. This entry should be treated as unverified until a second source is found.
