## Junker 52

Remains of a German Junkers Ju 52 transport aircraft, mainly the tail section, lying on sand and silt at 35-37 m in front of the Portoferraio lighthouse.

## Overview

Local operators place the wreck in front of the Portoferraio lighthouse on a sandy seabed at 35 m (Blu Elba Diving, whose page also says 30-35 m). A diver's report on Taucher.Net gives 37 m for the wreck itself, about 300 m off the shore below the lighthouse. A hotel guide page gives 40-45 m, a figure not supported by the dive-operator sources. Blu Elba Diving says only the tail remains, which keeps the dive short given the depth; the Taucher.Net report describes the tail as completely preserved; VisitItaly says the basic structure is intact although some parts have been removed.

The Taucher.Net report notes that the Ju 52 was built of aluminium, whose structures already show clear signs of weakness, and that apart from the wreck there is only sand and silt, so the site suits photographers rather than souvenir hunters. Silt and seagrass blades are easily stirred up and reduce visibility; VisitItaly also mentions poor visibility and describes the current as mild. The dive is described as a decompression dive for experienced divers (Taucher.Net; Blu Elba Diving: advanced divers only; Hotel Anna: advanced or deep certification with a dive centre and support boat).

The wreck lies close to the ferry lane, and Taucher.Net asks divers to set an anchor buoy with a dive flag, to anchor only in the best weather, and to lower the anchor slowly because an anchor strike can damage the wreck. The same report says the wreck is hard to find without correct GPS data and that positions published in one guidebook were deliberately wrong. No marine life is documented in the sources read, and none describe entering the fuselage; any wreck penetration requires specific training and equipment. Date and circumstances of the aircraft's loss could not be confirmed in the sources read.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Elba, Europe (off Portoferraio, in front of the Portoferraio lighthouse, north coast)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 37 meters
