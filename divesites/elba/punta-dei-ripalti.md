## Punta dei Ripalti

A rocky point on the southern tip of Elba, off the Capoliveri coast, with boulders close to shore and a shoal that drops to about 45 m.

## Overview

Elba CED places Punta dei Ripalti about 4.5 miles south of Porto Azzurro and calls it the southernmost point of Elba; Infoelba also describes the point as the southernmost on the island. The bottom is rocky near the coast with many large boulders, and in front of them a shoal about 100 m long (Stefano Sub says about 150 m) drops to roughly 45 m, with walls on both sides. Boulders detached from the coast are covered in red gorgonians. Stefano Sub describes mooring in shallow water at about 9 m, small and medium groupers sheltering among the boulders, the shoal starting at 13 m, and a second, shorter parallel shoal to the north-east where barracuda and dentex are seen in the blue; it adds that ocean sunfish (Mola mola) can be seen in early summer. Infoelba lists octopus and moray eels on the steep wall down to 45 m and amberjacks, bonito and snappers passing in the blue.

Sources disagree on depth: Omnisub describes the cape as running from 5 to 25 m with a large rock down to 35 m, while Elba CED, Infoelba and Stefano Sub give about 45 m (Stefano Sub lists 50 m as the maximum). Elba CED and Stefano Sub describe the site as suitable for divers of all levels because of the shallow boulders. Infoelba warns that currents are very strong and can make the dive much more demanding; Stefano Sub lists the current as "possible". A Capoliveri mooring buoy named "Ripalti Grottoni" appears in a 2011 report (10-12 m over rock, about 25 m from the coast), and other operators list "Grottoni" or "Ripalti / Grottone" as a neighbouring or related dive, so the exact extent of this site is not clear from the sources.

## Site Information
