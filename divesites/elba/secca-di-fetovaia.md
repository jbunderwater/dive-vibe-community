## Secca di Fetovaia

Secca di Fetovaia is a shoal off Fetovaia bay on Elba's south-west coast, with its top at 12-15 m and a wall carrying red gorgonians from about 30 m, reaching 40-50 m.

## Overview

Diving in Elba places the secca in front of Fetovaia beach, roughly at the entrance of the bay. The dive starts on the top at about 12-15 m, divided by rocky ridges, then goes out over a wall that begins to be covered with red gorgonians from 30 m; moray eels and lobsters look out of their holes. Marina di Campo Diving gives a top at 13 m sloping gently down, dense red gorgonian branches at about 35 m on the south side, and dentex, passing tuna and very dense shoals of occhiate at 20-25 m on the north side. It says large shoals of barracuda are almost resident for at least 8-9 months a year, and that the crevices hold lobsters, other crustaceans and large conger and moray eels. Elba CED puts the top at about 15 m with the secca reaching 50 m on the outer side, and says beginners can see many conger eels, moray eels and groupers at shallow depth.

Fetovaia.it rates the dive for experts, with a depth of 30-40 m, possible current and excellent visibility; it lists octopus, moray eels and dentex, possible passing amberjack, barracuda shoals and tuna, and warns of the remains of an old fishing net at about 20 m. The distance offshore is given as about 400 m (Fetovaia.it) or half a mile (Marina di Campo Diving). The top is thus described as interesting for less experienced divers, and the deeper gorgonian wall for more experienced ones.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Elba, Europe (off Fetovaia bay, Campo nell'Elba)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Pinnacle/seamount
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters
