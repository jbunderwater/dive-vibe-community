## Capo Calvo

A boat dive from a sheltered bay south-east of Porto Azzurro, with a sand and posidonia bottom leading to boulders and a large field of red gorgonians at 30-45 m.

## Overview

Elba CED and Stefano Sub describe a small bay just outside the Porto Azzurro gulf, heading south-east, that is sheltered from the scirocco; the bottom alternates sand and posidonia and boats moor at about 5-7 m (Elba CED) or 9 m (Stefano Sub, Isola d'Elba App). Stefano Sub gives the trip from Porto Azzurro as about 30 minutes by boat. Elba CED describes two routes. The first runs at 10-25 m along a rocky slope of fallen blocks (franata) parallel to the coast, with two "twin walls" covered in Parazoanthus, schools of barracuda and small amberjacks. The second, deeper and more demanding, crosses a sandy plateau by swimming perpendicular to the coast for at least 7-8 minutes to reach a garden of red gorgonians between 30 and 45 m, where lobsters, a few European lobsters and large pelagic fish are often seen. Elba CED and Infoelba both warn that currents push toward open sea to the east and make the return difficult.

Stefano Sub and Isola d'Elba App describe the first boulders at about 25 m, yellow gorgonians (Eunicella cavolini) appearing deeper, and a very large colony of red gorgonians (Paramuricea clavata) that can be followed to 45 m or along the 35 m contour. Branches of false black coral (Gerardia savaglia) are reported at 33 m and 40 m (Elba CED, Infoelba) or at 41 m (Stefano Sub). On the way back up, the operators describe two small walls covered in sea daisies (Parazoanthus axinellae), a few small groupers and large schools of barracuda and dentex, ending on a boulder slope at about 15 m; Isola d'Elba App adds bonito, sea breams and salps there and says a seahorse may be found in the posidonia (look but do not touch). Maximum depths quoted are 50 m (Stefano Sub, Isola d'Elba App, Infoelba) and 52 m (Biodivers). Operators call the dive simple to medium-easy and suitable for all levels when the shallow route or the reversed direction is used, but the deep route goes beyond typical recreational limits and needs suitable training, equipment and gas planning.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Elba, Europe (bay of Capo Calvo, south-east of Porto Azzurro, Capoliveri coast)
