## Scoglio del Remaiolo

A rock rising in the middle of the Remaiolo bay below the Costa dei Gabbiani, before the Calamita mines, with an easy circuit around the rock and walls and a ridge that fall beyond 40 m.

## Overview

Elba CED places the rock halfway between Porto Azzurro and Golfo Stella, below the Costa dei Gabbiani promontory, past Capo Turco and before the Punta Calamita mines, and calls it one of the best-known dives on Elba. Stefano Sub describes an emergent rock and a ridge running more than 150 m south-east from it toward open water. The south-east side falls almost vertically and is covered in Parazoanthus, sponges and yellow gorgonians, while the south-west side descends more gradually with red gorgonians. Elba CED describes a west route moored on rock, following a wall that becomes steadily more vertical, with red gorgonians and caves, to about 45 m; and a south route starting on a coralligenous plateau at 12-14 m, then a ridge with sides that fall vertically beyond 40 m, leading to a shoal covered in dense Paramuricea gorgonians and yellow sponges. Stefano Sub notes catshark egg cases, lobsters and European lobsters on that shoal. Virtualelba and Infoelba describe a rock at the end of the ridge from about 40-45 m that continues to more than 50-55 m.

A shallower option exists: Stefano Sub, Infoelba, Puntala Diving Center and PADI describe a circuit around the rock that stays at 20 m or less, with groupers, conger eels, moray eels and nudibranchs among the crevices. Reported fish in the open water around the rock include dentex, barracuda and amberjack; several sources also mention sunfish, John Dory and torpedo rays. Depths of 45 m and more exceed typical recreational limits and need suitable training, equipment and gas planning. Sources give maximum depths of 45 m (Elba CED, Daily Nautica), 50 m (Aquanautic Elba map), 55 m (Biodivers, Infoelba, Virtualelba) and 60 m (Stefano Sub). Biodivers rates the dive as difficult, while Stefano Sub rates it easy because of the shallow circuit; both list the current as possible. The rock is a boat dive; Elba CED and PADI describe it as such, and Infoelba notes that the neighbouring Remaiolo beach can only be reached by sea.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Elba, Europe (Remaiolo bay, below the Costa dei Gabbiani, Capoliveri, south-east Elba)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 45 meters
