## L'Ancorone

Deep rocky wall and boulders north of Capo Vita, known for a very large admiralty-type anchor reported at about 42 m.

## Overview

Local sources place the site a few hundred metres from the Secca di Capo Vita: about 200 m according to Elba CED and Infoelba, about 300 m according to Riodiving. Infoelba adds that it lies about one mile from the Isola dei Topi. Elba CED describes a rocky wall and large boulders from 27 m to 40 m; Bolle d'Azoto lists 27-45 m and gives 45 m as the base of the shoal; Infoelba describes a vertical wall plunging from a sand platform at about 25 m. The dive is described as very long, so bottom time must be planned carefully (Elba CED, Infoelba), and as exposed to currents and wind, needing a calm-sea day (Elba CED). Ferries and large vessels pass nearby, and Riodiving says the difficulty lies in the descent and ascent in open sea and in the anchor's depth. Bolle d'Azoto lists the site under both recreational and technical diving and says recreational divers get very few minutes at the bottom.

The main feature is a large anchor. Elba CED calls it a medieval admiralty-type anchor, more than 5 m high, wedged under big boulders, seen when starting the dive from the south; Infoelba gives about 5 m and a depth of about 42 m on sand between rocks; Riodiving says it is 6 m long, taller than the boulder under which one fluke is wedged, and lies at 42 m. All these are dive-operator descriptions; no archaeological source dating the anchor was found, so its age should be treated as unverified.

Reported marine life includes lobsters and crayfish in the cracks, scorpionfish, gurnards, torpedo rays on the sand and sea breams (Elba CED, Infoelba), moray eels and crabs (Infoelba), and dentex (Riodiving), with parazoanthus on the rocks around the anchor (Infoelba). Infoelba and Riodiving mention very clear water and, on the ascent, pelagic fish in the blue.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Elba, Europe (offshore north of Capo Vita, near the Secca di Capo Vita, off Cavo)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 45 meters
