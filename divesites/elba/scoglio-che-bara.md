## Scoglio che Bara

Submerged rock in front of a large boulder near Capo Stella, with a cap at about 7 m, a through-crack and a Parazoanthus-covered wall, dropping to about 35 m.

## Overview

Elba CED describes reaching Golfo Stella by rounding Golfo Lacona along the coast, where a large vertical boulder overhangs the shore; about 60 m in front of it lies a submerged rock with its cap at about 7 m. On the west-facing wall a through-crack leads to the other side of the rock; the wall is covered entirely with Parazoanthus, and groupers, sea breams, saddled seabream and tight groups of damselfish shelter in the crack. At about 35 m the crack is encrusted with yellow sponges, and Elba CED notes that the exposure to favourable currents makes a good habitat for nudibranchs. Sub Now says the boulder seems to fall, which gives the site its name (in Elban dialect, it says, "barare" means to fall), and describes the cap at about 7 m some 50 m from it, the Parazoanthus wall and the split, then a mushroom-shaped ledge covered with large yellow sponges and inhabited by lobsters and nudibranchs; groupers, moray eels, octopus, sea breams, corvina, damselfish and rainbow wrasse are listed. Sub Now gives a depth of 35 m, 40 minutes, difficulty medium to high and AWD-Deep certification.

Infoelba describes the rock as cone-shaped and just off the Capo Stella promontory, reaching 35 m quickly, with the wreck of a small wooden motor boat on the bottom and a spiral ascent around the cone to a Parazoanthus-covered tip at about 15 m, and calls it breathtaking at night. Aquanautic Elba's dive-site map lists "Scoglio che Bara" with barracuda and a small wooden wreck, 6-40 m, easy navigation and rare currents. Nothing about the history of the wreck was found.

The approach described is by boat. The park's rules page states that the waters of Elba are not included in the perimeter of the Park.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Elba, Europe (off Capo Stella, Golfo Stella side, Capoliveri)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Pinnacle/seamount
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 35 meters
