## Punta Galera

A tongue of rock just past the old Ginevro mine on the Capoliveri coast of south-east Elba, with a shallow ridge and a second ridge that drops to 50 m and deeper.

## Overview

Elba CED and Infoelba place Punta Galera just after the Ginevro mine when arriving from Porto Azzurro; Elba Report says it lies just below the Monte Calamita mines. It is a line of rock running south-east from the coast with a cap at 7-10 m and both sides falling to about 25 m. Elba CED describes two routes. The first circles the ridge from the north side, past crevices lined with sponges and sea daisies (margherite di mare) where small groupers hide, to the southern end at about 20 m where crevices shelter small lobsters and conger eels, then back up to a wall a few metres below the surface covered in yellow Parazoanthus and large white spirographs; Elba CED presents this circuit as suitable for beginners. The second route, for experienced divers, crosses a sand strip from the north side to another ridge that descends beyond 50 m, forming a small wall with large red gorgonians (Paramuricea clavata) mixed with yellow Eunicella cavolinii.

Stefano Sub describes a boat dive moored at a buoy over the shoal that runs about 100 m south-east from the coast, then crossing to a second shoal at 19 m where large dentex and barracuda are often seen, a crack at 27 m covered in yellow and red gorgonians with groupers, forkbeards and conger eels, and a continuation south along the 35 m contour before returning by the same route. Infoelba describes a wall covered in yellow gorgonians that are replaced by red ones with depth, and lists sea breams, damselfish and saddled bream over the 19 m rocks. Elba Report also mentions pelagic fish and eagle rays.

Sources give different maximum depths: 35 m (Biodivers), beyond 50 m (Elba CED) and 60 m (Stefano Sub). Depths beyond about 40 m exceed typical recreational limits and need suitable training, equipment and gas planning. Elba CED, Infoelba and Stefano Sub all warn that currents are often strong here; Elba CED notes that divers who move far from the boat find the return tiring. Operators rate the site anywhere from easy (Stefano Sub) to medium (Biodivers).

## Site Information

- **Location**: Elba, Europe (just past the Ginevro mine, below Monte Calamita, Capoliveri)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 50 meters
