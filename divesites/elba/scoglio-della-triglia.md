## Scoglio della Triglia

Scoglio della Triglia is a rock outcrop just outside the Gulf of Marina di Campo, a few minutes by boat from the coast, long known for an underwater Madonna statue.

## Overview

Elba CED and Marina di Campo Diving describe a large emerging rock that continues underwater to about 20 m. Its shape gives shelter to groupers and passing dentex, and the parts not exposed to light are covered with parazoanthus and colourful encrusting growth; small rainbow wrasse (donzelle) and damselfish (castagnole) often surround the statue. Marina di Campo Diving adds that, past the statue, divers head south keeping the wall on the left to a group of rocks around 30 m that shelters many resident fish, then circle back round the rock. Aquanautic Elba's map gives a depth of 0-30 m, easy navigation and possible current, and lists a wall as well as the statue. Marina di Campo Diving, Dive Center Blu and Infoelba all call the dive suitable for all levels.

The Madonna statue was placed on the seabed by fishermen around 1970, at about 15 m (Infoelba, Marina di Campo Diving) or 18 m (Dive Center Blu). An Elbareport article of 17 August 2022, written by a Marina di Campo dive centre, says a plastic statue was placed on 15 August 1969 at 18 m on the west side after an earlier statue on top of the rock was swept away by storms, and that this one was destroyed in an unknown way in November 2021; the Coast Guard and a dive centre recovered the remains but an attempt to rebuild it failed. Several dive-centre pages still describe the statue, so their text appears out of date.

The islet belongs to the land territory of the Parco Nazionale Arcipelago Toscano, but the Park states that Elba's waters are not included in its perimeter (islepark.it).

## Site Information

- **Location**: Elba, Europe (just outside the Gulf of Marina di Campo)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters
