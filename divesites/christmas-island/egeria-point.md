## Egeria Point

Egeria Point is a wall and pinnacle dive at the southwestern corner of Christmas Island, consistently cited as one of the island's best sites for shark encounters.

## Overview

Located at the southwestern tip of Christmas Island, Egeria Point sits adjacent to Pig Rock and offers a combination of fringing reef slopes and a pinnacle structure teeming with life. Extra Divers Christmas Island's own site listing and SSI both confirm a 10-22 metre depth range. Wave erosion has carved caves into the limestone cliffs — confirmed independently by Just Gotta Dive's Christmas Island trip report — and these formations provide shelter for resting whitetip reef sharks, described across sources as a reliable, near-guaranteed sighting at this site. Barracuda are also regularly seen. Grey reef sharks patrol the open water at the site, and the site attracts attention from large open-ocean species. SSI and Extra Divers both describe the site as suited to intermediate-to-advanced divers due to its exposed nature and depth. No confirmation of schooling scalloped hammerheads at this specific site was found in available sources.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Christmas Island, Oceania
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 22 meters
