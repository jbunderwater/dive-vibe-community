## Cream Garden

Cream Garden (also referred to as Cream Gardens) is a kelp-fringed reef and wall site where the anchorage sits in 15-30m, with the wall dropping much further at the point itself. Two independent dive-shop and marine-science sources report bronze whaler sharks and groups of large kingfish at this site.

## Overview

Cream Garden combines a kelp-forest anchorage area (15-30m) with a wall that drops steeply at the point -- one dive-shop account describes it continuing to roughly 60m, well beyond recreational limits, while a marine-science survey of the same area records a decompression zone at 6-8m and wall diving around 30m. Recreational dives here stay within the 22m maximum depth recorded for this entry; the deeper wall exists but is a technical-diving extension rather than the standard profile. Bronze whaler sharks and large kingfish are reported here by two independent sources, along with pink maomao schools.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Poor Knights Islands, Northland, New Zealand
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 22 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 15-30 meters (good for reef work)
- **Water Temperature**: 14-17°C (winter/May-Sept), 19-25°C (summer/Nov-april)
- **Current**: Mild to variable
- **Best Time**: May to September for visibility; year-round diving possible

## Marine Life

The following are specifically reported for Cream Garden across independent sources:
- Bronze whaler sharks (reported by a dive-shop operator and a marine-science survey independently)
- Large kingfish, seen in groups
- Pink maomao schools
- Kelp forest cover, with moving seaweed noted as a feature of the site

The previous list here (hard/soft corals, sponges, snapper, giant trevally, blue maomao, stingrays, moray eels) matched the generic list used across most other Poor Knights entries and could not be confirmed specifically for Cream Garden, so it has been removed. General Poor Knights reef life is likely present but unconfirmed at this site.

## Dive Profile

Descend to the reef structure and explore at a leisurely pace. The moderate depth of 22 meters allows extended bottom times. Work the reef methodically, examining both the larger reef structure and the small organisms colonizing it. The cream-coloured sponges and diverse encrusting life reward close inspection. Fish life can be active—look for schools in the blue water off the reef while also examining the reef crevices and overhangs. Allow ample time for photography or simple observation—this is not a site requiring rushed diving.

## Entry and Exit

Boat entry via giant stride directly to the reef. Your guide will indicate interesting reef features and depth profile. Exit is via ascent and boat pickup at the surface.

## Tips and Recommendations

- **Excellent intermediate reef dive**: A solid choice for divers building Poor Knights experience
- **Photographers welcome**: The diverse reef life rewards both wide-angle and macro work
- **Leisurely pace recommended**: Take time to observe reef details and fish behaviour
- **Good learning site**: The moderate depth and manageable conditions make this ideal for learning Poor Knights diving
- **Early or late dive option**: Consider this as a second dive when you're developing your dive profile for the day
- **Snorkel approach**: Even snorkelers can enjoy the shallow reef portions

## Safety Considerations

- Excellent intermediate-level reef dive
- Manageable depth allows extended exploration
- Current is generally mild; maintain buddy contact
- Good buoyancy control essential for reef protection
- Suitable for intermediate divers and those building Poor Knights experience
- Always dive with a buddy

## Photography

Cream Garden offers excellent photographic diversity:
- **Macro**: Detailed shots of sponges, anemones, nudibranchs, and small fish
- **Reef composition**: Wider shots showing reef structure and marine life relationships
- **Fish behaviour**: Schooling patterns and predator-prey interactions
- **Sponge detail**: The cream-coloured sponges are particularly photogenic
- **Wide-angle**: Fish schools in the blue water beyond the reef
- The moderate depth and calm conditions allow relaxed, thoughtful photography approach
