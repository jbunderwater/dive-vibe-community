## Kleine Knip

Kleine Knip (Playa Kenepa Chiki) is a shore dive at an undeveloped beach on Curaçao's northwest coast — a 10–15 minute surface swim across sand leads to a healthy wall with confirmed sightings of turtles, moray eels, groupers, and squid.

## Overview

Kleine Knip sits below limestone cliffs on the northwest coast, a small, quieter neighbour to Grote Knip nearby. The beach has limited facilities (toilets and shade huts are available) and sees fewer divers than the more developed west-coast sites, making it a good choice for a calmer experience.

The dive requires a surface swim of approximately 10–15 minutes across sandy bottom before reaching the drop-off. Divers-Guide.com describes the coral here as "beautiful and healthy" between 10 and 25 metres, with the bottom turning mainly sandy at greater depth. One diver reviewing the site on ShoreDiving.com rated the coral formations along the wall above any others seen in 22 dives on Curaçao, while cautioning that overall fish diversity was modest compared to some other Curaçao sites.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Northwest coast, near Landhuis Knip
- **Entry Type**: Shore
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 25 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 25 metres
- **Current**: Usually limited

## Marine Life

Confirmed at this site: Mediterranean/Roman moray eels, grouper, sea turtles, lionfish, and squid (Divers-Guide.com, Zentacle diver reviews). ScubaBoard trip reports from this stretch of the west coast also note goldentail eels, red-lipped blennies, and occasional snapper and barracuda. An earlier version of this page attributed regular eagle-ray sightings to Kleine Knip specifically; no independent source found in this review confirms eagle rays at this site (as opposed to neighbouring Grote Knip, where a diver log does report one), so that claim has been removed here.

## Dive Profile

Enter from the sandy public beach. Fin across a sandy bottom for 10–15 minutes (slow pace) until the wall appears. The wall is beautiful from 10–20 metres with healthy coral coverage and sponge growth. Below 20 metres the coral density decreases and the bottom becomes mainly sandy. Divers typically work along the wall before returning across the sand to exit.

## Entry and Exit

Straightforward sandy beach entry. The beach has some coral rubble near the water's edge — reef shoes recommended. Wade out until you can float, then surface swim to the reef. Exit by the same route.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Watch for turtles and moray eels along the wall face, and squid in open water on the approach
- Go early (before 8 AM) for calmer conditions and less boat traffic
- Combine with Grote Knip (adjacent bay) for a full day of diving
- Bring everything you need — facilities are basic

## Safety Considerations

The long sand swim means you are at some distance from shore when you reach the wall. Carry a surface marker buoy. Fishing from shore occasionally occurs — be aware of lines in the water. A surface marker buoy is mandatory.
