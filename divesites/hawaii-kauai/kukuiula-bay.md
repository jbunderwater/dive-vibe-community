## Kukuiula Bay

Kukuiula Harbor's sheltered, breakwater-protected bay is best documented as the launch point for Kauai's south shore and Ni'ihau boat charters; it offers calm, beginner-friendly water for informal shore diving and snorkeling, though it is not listed as a dedicated guided shore-dive product by local operators.

## Overview

The harbor's protective breakwater keeps large swells out of the bay, creating some of the most consistently calm nearshore water on Kauai's south coast. Maximum depth reaches approximately 50 feet (15m) in the deeper sections of the bay, based on a prior curated estimate that could not be independently re-confirmed against a dive-specific source in this pass.

**Correction (2026-08-07 re-audit):** Earlier versions of this page stated that "Fathom Five Divers operates guided shore dives from this site." This is not supported by current source material — Fathom Five's own certified-shore-dive listings cover only Koloa Landing and Tunnels Beach, and its Kukuiula Harbor page describes the location strictly as a beach/launch-point amenity ("calm waters ideal for a leisurely swim"), not a dive site. That claim has been removed. What is well documented: the harbor serves as the departure point for south shore boat charters and Ni'ihau day trips (May–September), and the beach itself has restrooms, showers, and picnic facilities.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Poipu area, 12 miles southwest of Lihue Airport, South Shore, Kauai (between Spouting Horn and Po'ipu Beach)
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 15 meters
- **Water Temperature**: 25–28°C (77–82°F)
- **Facilities**: Restrooms, showers, picnic tables; no lifeguard
