## Seraya Kecil Dive Site

Seraya Kecil ("small Seraya") is a small island roughly 9km north of Labuan Bajo, home to the Sudamala Resort's house reef and a straightforward, beginner-friendly introduction to Komodo diving rather than the muck/critter destination it was previously described as.

## Overview

Seraya Kecil sits just off the north coast near Labuan Bajo, reachable in about 45 minutes by traditional boat or 15 minutes by speedboat. Aqura Dive, the PADI dive center based at the Sudamala Resort on the island, runs shore and jetty dives directly on the surrounding house reef. Divers exploring the reef's dropoff from the resort jetty report a coral garden with jackfish and snappers schooling beneath the jetty, sea turtles, mackerel, and various anemonefish around the reef — a gentle, accessible reef dive well suited to beginners, Discovery/try-dive divers, and night diving. The independent Komodo dive guide starfish.ch separately notes Seraya Kecil primarily as a popular snorkelling spot close to Labuan Bajo, consistent with a shallow, sheltered reef character rather than a current-swept or muck-diving site.

A previous version of this entry described Seraya Kecil as a black-sand muck-diving destination with mimic octopus, wonderpus octopus, flamboyant cuttlefish, and blue-ringed octopus. That content could not be traced to any source describing this Komodo location and closely mirrors descriptions of Bali's unrelated "Seraya Secrets" muck site near Tulamben, roughly 400km away — an apparent site-name mix-up. It has been removed.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Seraya Kecil island, ~9km north of Labuan Bajo, Komodo National Park, East Nusa Tenggara, Indonesia
- **Entry Type**: Shore or boat entry
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 20 meters
- **Current**: Light; sheltered house reef

## Marine Life

Sources confirm sightings at this specific site: jackfish and snappers schooling beneath the resort jetty, sea turtles, mackerel, and anemonefish among the anemones on the reef. Beyond these confirmed species, this destination's regional Komodo reef fauna generally applies, but no further site-specific reports were found — additional species are not named here to avoid overstating what has actually been documented at this exact location.

## Entry and Exit

Shore dives are run directly from the beach at Sudamala Resort; boat/jetty dives are also offered onto the same house reef. Conditions are calm and sheltered, making entry and exit straightforward.

## Tips for Divers

Seraya Kecil's house reef is used for Discovery/try dives to around 10 meters, making it a good option for less experienced divers or as a relaxed second dive. It is also noted as a good night-dive site. Depth beyond the shallow dropoff explored on guided beginner dives is not specified in available sources.
