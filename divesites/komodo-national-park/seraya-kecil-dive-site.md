## Seraya Kecil Dive Site

Seraya Kecil dive site sits near Labuan Bajo, at the eastern edge of Komodo National Park, East Nusa Tenggara, Indonesia.

## Overview

Seraya Kecil Island, near the village of Seraya on Flores just outside the eastern park boundary, is home to Sudamala Resort Seraya and its partner dive operation, Aqura Dive, which offers guided and unguided shore dives on the resort's house reef. Important distinction: the well-known muck-diving site "Seraya Secrets," often associated with the name "Seraya," is located in Tulamben, Bali — roughly 400km away — and is an entirely different location. It should not be confused with this Komodo-area site.

A 2026 research pass found only general resort/house-reef context for this location and could not confirm whether it is genuinely muck terrain (black sand/rubble slope) or primarily a house reef. The site_type was speculatively changed from "reef" to "muck" in an earlier QA pass based on Komodo's general reputation for muck diving near Labuan Bajo, but this remains unconfirmed one way or the other — three independent, site-specific sources have not been found.
