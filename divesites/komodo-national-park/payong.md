## Payong

Payong (likely the same site as "Payung Island," a spelling variant, near Padar in central Komodo National Park) has one credible source describing an unusual rocky, canyon-cut landscape rather than the open coral-garden reef previously described here — short of this project's 3-source bar, so the site remains unvalidated pending further confirmation.

## Overview

Payong is listed among the dive sites near Padar Island in central Komodo National Park. The Komodo National Park official site (komodonationalpark.org) — under the name "Payung Island" — describes "a very interesting underwater landscape with huge rocks that give the illusion of a wreck dive," with numerous canyons, best dived at slack tide (preferably low-tide slack), water temperatures in the low-to-mid 20s°C, and relatively low visibility of 5–10 meters. Other search results repeat this same phrasing, suggesting they are syndicated from the same official source rather than independent confirmations — so only one genuinely independent source was found, short of the 3-source validation bar used across this destination.

This single-sourced description is notably different in character from the open, sloping coral-garden reef previously described here (which had no site-specific sourcing at all). Given the conflict and the lack of independent corroboration, site_type is left as-is pending further research rather than being changed on a single source.
