## Yellow Wall

A dramatic wall along the east coast of Nusa Kode — also referred to as "Yellow Wall of Texas" by at least one operator — named for the profusion of bright yellow soft corals (Tubastrea) and robust sea cucumbers. Crevices harbour nudibranchs, cleaner shrimp, and decorator crabs, with clown triggerfish, fork-tailed fairy basslets, whitetip reef sharks, and green turtles observed along the face. Best dived in the afternoon for optimal light. Medium to strong currents.

Note on difficulty: sources conflict. dive-the-world.com and divekomodo.com both rate this site "Intermediate," while divehappy.com and divezone.net (via the prior QA) support an Advanced classification. This entry keeps Advanced given the destination's remote-liveaboard character and the deeper 40m maximum recorded here (2026-08-09 QA).

## Site Information

- **Location**: East coast of Nusa Kode, southern Komodo National Park
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters
- **Typical Depth Range**: 5–40 meters (richest life at 15–25 meters)
- **Current**: Medium to strong
- **Best Time**: Afternoon for optimal sunlight on the wall face
