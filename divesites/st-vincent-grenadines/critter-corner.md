## Critter Corner

Critter Corner is a muck dive site in St. Vincent and the Grenadines, Caribbean.

## Overview

Critter Corner is a shallow macro site off St. Vincent. DiveSSI's feature on St. Vincent describes it as a photographer's site in only about 8 meters of water, where bottom time is limited more by camera capacity than by air, and Bluewater Dive Travel lists it as a macro dive site. PADI's site listing gives coordinates matching this entry but no further description.

## Site Information

- **Location**: St. Vincent and the Grenadines, Caribbean
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Muck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 8 meters

## Marine Life

Reported at this site: flying gurnards (Bluewater Dive Travel, DiveSSI), frogfish, goldspotted eel, arrow shrimp and blennies/gobies (Bluewater Dive Travel), and seahorses, torpedo rays, pistol shrimp and cardinalfish (DiveSSI).
