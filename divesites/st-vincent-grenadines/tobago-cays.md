## Tobago Cays

Tobago Cays Marine Park protects Horseshoe Reef and five uninhabited cays in the southern Grenadines.

## Overview

The park surrounds five uninhabited cays (Petit Bateau, Petit Rameau, Baradal, Jamesby and Petit Tabac), the inhabited island of Mayreau and three islets. Horseshoe Reef stretches about 4 km, encloses four of the cays, and Petit Tabac lies just outside it to the east. DiscoverSVG lists the main dive sites as Horseshoe Reef, Petit Tabac Reef, Number 9 and World's End Reef. Depth for the entry (12 m in our data) was not re-confirmed.

## Site Information

- **Location**: St. Vincent and the Grenadines, Caribbean
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 12 meters

## Marine Life

Tobago Cays Marine Park reports that sea turtles nest and feed in the park; DiscoverSVG says green turtles, comfortable around people, can be swum with along Horseshoe Reef, and that World's End Reef has stingrays. Earlier claims of hawksbill and leatherback turtles, nurse sharks and eagle rays at the Cays were not re-confirmed and were removed.
