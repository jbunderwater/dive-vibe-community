## Anchor Reef

Anchor Reef is a wall dive site in St. Vincent and the Grenadines, Caribbean.

## Overview

Anchor Reef is a wall dive on St. Vincent's leeward coast with swim-throughs and coral formations. It is named for an old ship's anchor lodged in the reef (Dive Training Magazine), and SVG Tourism states it was voted one of the top 100 dive sites in the world. Depth sources differ: theDiveGlobe lists a maximum of 30 meters (average 18 meters) and rates the site beginner, while the data file records 36 meters; treat the wall as a 30-36 meter dive. Difficulty is kept at Intermediate because of the wall depth. theDiveGlobe describes it as a macro-life hotspot.

## Site Information

- **Location**: St. Vincent and the Grenadines, Caribbean
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 36 meters

## Marine Life

Site-specific reports describe several frogfish species and seahorses (Dive Training Magazine, theDiveGlobe, Bluewater Dive Travel), ghost pipefish (theDiveGlobe), and several types of eel and squid among the gorgonians (Bluewater Dive Travel).
