## New Guinea Reef

New Guinea Reef (Questelles) is a shore-entry dive on St. Vincent's leeward coast known for black coral.

## Overview

Zentacle's Questelles/New Guinea Reef entry says the water is about 30 feet from the parking spot, entered from the beach at the end of the downhill road. Current can reach about 2 knots, is usually strong at the point, and rarely strong before the Dive St. Vincent mooring; the reviewer considers the point for advanced divers. Bluewater Dive Travel notes an astounding variety of black coral. Conflict: Zentacle lists the maximum depth as unknown, so the 46 m in our data (from an earlier audit) is unconfirmed. Earlier claims of the bay being calm 95% of the time and a police-station landmark were removed as unverified.

## Site Information

- **Location**: St. Vincent and the Grenadines, Caribbean (Questelles, leeward coast)
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 46 meters

## Marine Life

Lots of black coral is reported by Zentacle and Bluewater Dive Travel. Zentacle's reviewer notes heavy fishing and very few large fish, with small fish and pristine coral. Earlier claims of frequent turtle and large-school sightings were removed as contradicted by this report.
