## New Guinea Reef

New Guinea Reef, at Questelles on St. Vincent's leeward coast, is a shore-entry dive known for its black coral.

## Overview

New Guinea Reef (also known as Questelles Reef) is reached by shore entry off the Leeward Highway near the Questelles police station, with parking about 30 feet from the water (Zentacle). The bay is reported calm about 95% of the time, maximum current is about 2 knots, and the current is usually strong at the point but rarely strong before the Dive St. Vincent mooring. The reviewer rates the dive "for advanced divers only" beyond the point. Maximum depth is not stated in current sources; the 46-meter value in the data file is an unconfirmed estimate and should be verified with a local operator.

## Site Information

- **Location**: St. Vincent and the Grenadines, Caribbean (Questelles, leeward coast)
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 46 meters

## Marine Life

Sources describe pristine coral and an unusually rich variety of black coral species (Zentacle, Bluewater Dive Travel). Zentacle notes that heavy fishing means few large fish, with small fish and coral the main attraction. No individual fish species are confirmed for this site.
