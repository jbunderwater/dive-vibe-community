## Mayreau Gardens

Mayreau Gardens is a drift dive off Mayreau island, within Tobago Cays Marine Park.

## Overview

SVG Tourism describes Mayreau Gardens as an expansive coral reef formation and a good drift dive. Mayreau island lies within the Tobago Cays Marine Park boundary. No source reviewed states the depth; the data file lists 24 meters as an unconfirmed estimate. Bluewater Dive Travel lists a separate wreck site off Mayreau; it is not part of Mayreau Gardens and no wreck details are given here.

## Site Information

- **Location**: St. Vincent and the Grenadines, Caribbean (Mayreau, Tobago Cays Marine Park)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 24 meters

## Marine Life

Crinoids, garden eels and flamingo tongues are reported here (Bluewater Dive Travel, SVG Tourism). No other species are confirmed for this site.
