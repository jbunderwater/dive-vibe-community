## Rocher de la Perle

Rocher de la Perle is a wall dive site in Martinique, Caribbean.

## Overview

**Possible duplicate notice**: this entry and "Site de Plongée La Perle" elsewhere on this site both describe what appears to be the same real-world islet - Ilet de la Perle, off Anse Céron near Le Prêcheur - recorded with different coordinates from two separate data-gathering passes. They have been re-audited in parallel but not merged in this pass, to avoid unreviewed data loss; flagged for a future de-duplication review.

Rocher de la Perle is a volcanic islet off Anse Céron, within the marine reserve off Le Prêcheur (referred to by martinique-tour.com as the Réserve Marine and, by some other sources, associated with Albert Falco), on Martinique's wild northwest coast. Sources describe walls and drop-offs here in the 20-60 metre range - martinique-tour.com gives 5-60m with the most dramatic drop-offs between 20-60m, while quebecyachting.ca separately describes "more than 40 metres." Currents can be strong, and Martinique Tour's write-up describes the site as best suited to experienced divers.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Martinique, Caribbean
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters

## Marine Life

Confirmed across sources: coral fish, sea turtles, barracudas, jacks (carangues), groupers, black gorgonians, and barrel sponges. This re-audit removed the previous version's more specific claims of "hundreds" of horse-eye jacks and consistently dense reserve fish populations, which could not be traced to a source in those exact terms.
