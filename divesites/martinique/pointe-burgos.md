## Pointe Burgos

Pointe Burgos is a basaltic dome and drop-off between Grande Anse and Anse du Bourg in Les Anses d'Arlet, southwest Martinique — considered one of the area's best dives, with a small beginner plateau alongside fissures that drop past 55 metres.

## Overview

The site is formed by a basaltic lava dome that separates Grande Anse beach from the village of Anses d'Arlet. A drop-off descends gently from the surface to more than 50 metres, riddled with rockslides that begin around 12m and fissures that in places reach 60m. A small plateau between 3 and 12 metres gives less experienced divers a shallower option, but the site as most commonly dived is an advanced drop-off/wall dive. This entry was previously miscategorised as a 12m beginner reef dive — three independent dive-shop sources confirm the site is primarily a deep drop-off.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Les Anses d'Arlet, southwest Martinique
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 60 meters

## Marine Life

Sources describe morays, lobsters, turtles, gorgonians, sponges, and platax (batfish) throughout the site's rocks and fissures, with "sometimes large specimens" in the crevices. Large schools of trevally, sizeable snappers, and barracudas are reported passing the drop-off. No source names specific stingray or eagle-ray sightings here, so those claims from the previous description have been removed.

## Dive Profile

Descend to the plateau (3-12m) for beginner sections, or continue down the rockslides and fissures for the full drop-off dive to 50-60m depending on experience and air supply.

## Safety Considerations

The deep sections of Pointe Burgos require comfort with drop-off/wall diving and NDL management at depth — this is not a beginner-depth dive despite the shallow plateau near the surface.
