## Citadelle

La Citadelle, off Le Prêcheur on Martinique's northwest coast, combines a coral plateau from 6-30 metres with a dramatic drop-off reaching 60 metres — accessed by boat, not shore as previously recorded.

## Overview

The site lies between Anse Belleville and Les Abymes, facing the town of Le Prêcheur. A coral plateau descends in stages from about 6m to 30m, populated by dozens of coral colonies, before giving way to a drop-off ("gouffre spectaculaire") that continues to around 60m, its walls covered in sponges and gorgonians. Sources are consistent that minimum certification is Niveau 1/Open Water, since divers can stay on the shallower plateau. This entry was previously recorded as a 16m shore dive; three independent sources instead describe a boat-accessed plateau-and-wall site down to 60m, so site type, depth, and entry type have all been corrected.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Off Le Prêcheur, northwest Martinique
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 60 meters

## Marine Life

Sources report parrotfish, "sorbs" (a local reef fish), king mackerel (thazards/kingfish), spiny lobsters, and sea turtles on the plateau, with sponges and sea fans (gorgonians) covering the drop-off walls. Volcanic bomb formations on black sand are also noted near the site. No source found supports the previous description's claims about slipper lobsters or an especially "relaxed" turtle population, so those have been removed.

## Dive Profile

Descend to the coral plateau (6-30m) for the shallower, beginner-accessible portion of the dive, or continue to the drop-off for depths approaching 60m — a decision made with the dive guide based on certification and experience.

## Entry and Exit

Boat dive; recommended operators include Corail Club Caraïbes (Trois-Îlets) and Espace Plongée Martinique (Pointe du Bout marina), among others running trips to the northern coast.
