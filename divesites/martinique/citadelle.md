## Citadelle

"The Citadel" is a well-known coral plateau and drop-off dive in the Le Prêcheur area of northwest Martinique - and, per a 2026-08-13 re-audit, a considerably deeper and more advanced site than this page previously described.

## Overview

**Major correction**: this page previously described Citadelle as a 16-metre beginner shore dive. Two independent dive-shop sources instead describe it as a coral plateau that descends in stages to a sheer drop-off of 50-60 metres - Tribloo gives "a sheer drop of 60 metres" between Anse Belleville and Les Abymes, and Espace Plongée Martinique lists "La Citadelle" at 0-50m with a "dramatic volcanic tombant." Québec Yachting separately confirms La Citadelle as a real site in the same northern cluster as Rocher de la Perle and Les Jardins du Prêcheur. Depth has been corrected from 16m to 50m and difficulty from Beginner to Advanced. **Entry type changed from shore to boat as an editorial judgment call, not a confirmed fact**: none of the three sources consulted state access method directly, but a drop-off to 50-60m is not consistent with a shore-only dive profile. Flagged for a human editor with local knowledge to confirm.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Le Prêcheur area, northwest Martinique (between Anse Belleville and Les Abymes per Tribloo)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 50 meters
- **Best Time**: November to May

## Marine Life

No source consulted for this re-audit named specific species for this site. The previous version of this page's claims about turtles, parrotfish, moray eels, and lobster diversity here could not be traced to a source and have been removed rather than restated as fact. Martinique's general reef and drop-off fauna likely applies, but nothing here is confirmed as specific to this site.

## Dive Profile

Sources describe a coral plateau that descends in stages before reaching the main drop-off at 50-60m - a profile suited to experienced divers managing depth and no-decompression limits carefully, not the shallow, unhurried profile previously described on this page.

## Entry and Exit

Listed here as a boat dive based on the depth profile, but this is an editorial judgment call, not a source-confirmed fact - divers should confirm access with a local operator before planning this dive.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Advanced certification and comfort with deep, drop-off diving is required here — this is not a beginner or casual dive
- The northern coast is less dived than the south, per Espace Plongée and Tribloo
- Combine with nearby St. Pierre wreck sites for a full northern Martinique dive day, subject to depth/NDL planning between dives

## Safety Considerations

The drop-off depths (50-60m) place large parts of this dive beyond recreational no-decompression limits; bottom time must be planned and managed carefully, and this is not a site for less experienced divers. The northern coast can be more exposed to swell than the south — check conditions with a local operator.

## Photography

Wide-angle shots of the volcanic drop-off are likely the strongest subject here, though this re-audit found no site-specific photography guidance in the sources consulted; the previous version's specific claims about macro subjects (nudibranchs, cleaning shrimp) have been removed as unconfirmed.
