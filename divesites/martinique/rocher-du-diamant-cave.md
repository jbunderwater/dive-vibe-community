## Rocher du Diamant Cave

This is the fault/tunnel that dive shops call the "cathedral" — a fissure that runs completely through the Diamond Rock islet, entered from roughly 16-25 metres.

## Overview

Multiple Diamond Rock dive-shop pages describe an underwater cathedral formed by a fault that pierces the islet from side to side, with light beams filtering through the opening. It is one of a dozen distinct circuits offered around the same rock (see the separate Diamond Rock entry for the wall/pinnacle circuits). No source gives this specific tunnel circuit a separate depth figure beyond the general 16-25m range at which the fissure is typically entered.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Martinique, Caribbean
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Cave/cavern
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 25 meters

## Marine Life

No source gives species specifically for the tunnel/cathedral passage itself. General Diamond Rock reports (which may reflect the surrounding rock rather than the tunnel specifically) mention hawksbill turtles, moray eels, and crustaceans sheltering in crevices, and sea fans, soft corals, and sponges on the rock faces. Regional Caribbean reef fauna applies more broadly, but no site-specific report names fish species inside the tunnel itself.
