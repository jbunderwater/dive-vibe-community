## Site de Plongée La Perle

La Perle (Îlet/Rocher de la Perle) is a volcanic islet 2,800 metres off Le Prêcheur, on Martinique's northwest coast, within the Réserve Marine du Prêcheur — one of the island's least-visited and most current-exposed dive sites.

## Overview

The islet — administratively part of Le Prêcheur, opposite Anse Couleuvre — rises 38m above the water and sits within the Albert Falco Réserve Marine du Prêcheur. Dives range from about 5m to 60m depending on the circuit chosen. Because of frequent current and swell, sources recommend a minimum of Level 2 / Advanced Open Water certification. This entry was previously duplicated in our data under the separate curated name "Rocher de la Perle" with different (less precise) coordinates roughly 8km away; both names refer to the same landmark, so the duplicate entry has been removed and this OSM-sourced record — which carries a Wikidata identifier — is kept as canonical.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Le Prêcheur, northwest Martinique (Réserve Marine du Prêcheur)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 60 meters

## Marine Life

Sources report moray and conger eels, stonefish, squirrelfish, parrotfish, big-eye jacks, grouper, snappers, and barracudas, plus occasional pelagic species and blue triggerfish. Hawksbill turtles and large schools of horse-eye jacks are also reported by dive-shop sources. The very clear water at this offshore site is repeatedly noted as allowing good visibility of these species.

## Safety Notes

Strong, unpredictable currents in this channel require experienced dive planning; only divers with Advanced/Level 2 certification and drift-diving experience should attempt this site, and always with a local operator familiar with the current patterns.
