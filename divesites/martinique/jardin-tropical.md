## Jardin Tropical

Jardin Tropical is recorded as a shallow, garden-like reef in the Les Anses d'Arlet area of southwest Martinique.

## Overview

**Unresolved identity note (2026-08-12 QA):** A well-documented dive site called "Jardins de Salomon" exists off Cap Salomon in the same Anses d'Arlet area, matching this entry's "garden" theme and shallow, beginner-friendly coral character — coral formations and canyons down to about 25m, with a shallow 0-12m zone. However, Jardins de Salomon is boat-accessed (about 6 minutes from shore by dive-centre boat), while this entry is recorded as a shore dive, and no source could confirm with certainty that the two are the same site rather than a separate, undocumented location. Rather than guess, entry type and depth have been left as previously recorded, and unconfirmed marine-life specifics (seahorses) have been removed.

## Site Information

- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Reef
