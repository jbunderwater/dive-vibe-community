## Angelina B.

Angelina B. is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

One of the more compact tugboats deployed as an artificial reef in Okaloosa County, measuring just 65 ft (20m) in length but offering up to 32 ft (10m) of relief. Originally owned by Brown Marine Services (a local barge and tug company, since closed), the county deployed the tug as an artificial reef in 1999 at a depth of 135 ft (41m). Divers may spot a resident goliath grouper, along with jacks, barracuda, and even the occasional bull shark.
