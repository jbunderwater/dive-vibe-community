## Crab Trap Destin Reef

Crab Trap Destin Reef is a reef dive site in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

Destin, Okaloosa County. One of four shore-accessible reefs deployed by Okaloosa County in spring 2019. Features 68 four-disc modules placed to create a pattern of four wavy lines running parallel to shore. Shallowest modules ~15 ft (4.5m), deepest ~23 ft (7m). Damselfish, blennies, sheepshead, snapper, triggerfish, grouper, occasional octopus/sea turtle; stingrays and flounder commonly seen on the sand.
