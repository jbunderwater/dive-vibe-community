## Sherman Tug

Sherman Tug is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

Sherman Tug was an 80-ft-long (24.5 m) tugboat deployed as an artificial reef in May 1996. She sits at a depth of 80 ft (24.5 m).

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 24.5 meters
