## Sherman Tug

Sherman Tug is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

An 80-ft (24.5m) tugboat, put down as a reef in May 1996; she now sits at 80 ft (24.5m).

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 24.5 meters
