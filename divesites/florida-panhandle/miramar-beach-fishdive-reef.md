## Miramar Beach Fish/Dive Reef

Miramar Beach Fish/Dive Reef is a reef dive site in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

Initially deployed in 2017, features 98 tons of concrete in the form of 30 modules of varied design. Provides 18 ft (5.5m) of relief off a sandy seabed that bottoms out at a depth of 62 ft (19m).

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 19 meters
