## St. Joe Community Foundation Reef 2

St. Joe Community Foundation Reef 2 is a reef dive site in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

This artificial reef features 219 tons of reef modules deployed in July 2020 with a depth of approximately 80 ft (24.5 m).

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 24.5 meters
