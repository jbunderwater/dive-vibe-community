## Hathaway Bridge Span #14

Hathaway Bridge Span #14 is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

Span 14, another piece of the old Hathaway Bridge, belongs to the shallower inshore cluster near the pass — about 73 ft (22.5m) — and has largely come apart under the currents Hurricane Michael generated in 2018.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 22.5 meters
