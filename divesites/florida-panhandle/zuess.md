## Zuess

Zuess is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

A towboat larger than the popular Miss Louise — sources report her length as either 98 or 105 ft. Built in Nashville, TN by the Nashville Bridge Company in 1943 for Clark Super Gas Co. and named Emory T. after the company's founder; an independent shipbuilding record lists her later name as Zeus rather than Zuess. She was sent to the bottom as an artificial reef June 29, 1992; the guidebook's claim that she was sold to a Canadian company beforehand could not be independently confirmed. No superstructure remains: the wheelhouse and main cabins are gone, leaving a large hole in the main deck and two upright push pads - the tallest point of the wreck, reaching 92 ft (28m). Hull largely buried in sand; seabed at 105 ft (32m). Added to the Florida Panhandle Shipwreck Trail in 2020. Almaco jacks, tomtates, vermilion snapper, mangrove and lane snapper cruise the site, with scamp grouper hiding in the space beneath the deck.
