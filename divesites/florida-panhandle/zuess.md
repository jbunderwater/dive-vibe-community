## Zuess

Zuess is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

A 105-ft towboat, larger than the popular Miss Louise. Built in Nashville, TN by the Nashville Bridge Company in 1943 for Clark Super Gas Co. and named Emory T. after the company's founder. Sold to a Canadian company in 1992, sent to the bottom as an artificial reef June 29, 1992. No superstructure remains: the wheelhouse and main cabins are gone, leaving a large hole in the main deck and two upright push pads - the tallest point of the wreck, reaching 92 ft (28m). Hull largely buried in sand; seabed at 105 ft (32m). Added to the Florida Panhandle Shipwreck Trail in 2020. Almaco jacks, tomtates, vermilion snapper, mangrove and lane snapper cruise the site, with scamp grouper hiding in the space beneath the deck.
