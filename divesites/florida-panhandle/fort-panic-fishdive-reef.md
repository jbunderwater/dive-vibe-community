## Fort Panic Fish/Dive Reef

Fort Panic Fish/Dive Reef is a reef dive site in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

The county deployed 124 tons of reef modules here in 2017. The 32 modules sit at an average depth of 61 ft (18.5m) and provide a maximum relief of 18 ft (5.5m).

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 18.5 meters
