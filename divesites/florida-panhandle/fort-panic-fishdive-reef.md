## Fort Panic Fish/Dive Reef

Fort Panic Fish/Dive Reef is a reef dive site in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

The county deployed 124 tons of reef modules here in 2017 — 32 units — averaging 61 ft (18.5m) with up to 18 ft (5.5m) of relief.
