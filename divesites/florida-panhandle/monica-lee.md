## Monica Lee

Monica Lee is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

A small, 49-ft tugboat, built 1955, that started her career on Florida's east coast with McCulley Marine Services (Stuart, FL) - a company that does a lot of artificial reef work in the southeast Florida region. Sold to Turn Key Marine Service in 2006 (a boating company near Destin) and eventually acquired by Okaloosa County as an artificial reef. Anchors the center of an X-shaped artificial reef area known as Conch Reef, with pyramid-shaped modules extending outward in lines hundreds of feet away (not usually visible from the wreck itself). Sits upright on a sandy seafloor with a slight list to port; her depth is what makes her accessible to experienced divers only - top of the wreck reaches 100 ft (30.5m), seabed at 117 ft (35.5m). The forward wall of the wheelhouse has been torn away, with jagged metal from the window casings making that area difficult to access for all but the most experienced wreck divers; access points to the tug's small interior remain at the first level of the superstructure. Schooling tomtates and large red snapper are common, along with barracuda, yellow jacks, and greater amberjacks; whitespotted soapfish, blue angelfish, and several damselfish species also frequent the site.
