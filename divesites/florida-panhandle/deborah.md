## Deborah

Deborah is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

Originally a 135-ft-long tugboat, Deborah flipped over as she was being deployed as an artificial reef in 1999, ultimately settling upside down at a depth of 135 ft (41m). The small space beneath her overturned hull shelters plenty of grouper, snapper, and grunts, while stingrays are common in the nearby sand.
