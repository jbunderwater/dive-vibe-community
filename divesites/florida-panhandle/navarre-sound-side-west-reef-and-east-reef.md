## Navarre Sound-Side West Reef and East Reef

Navarre Sound-Side West Reef and East Reef is a reef dive site in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

Two separate reefs, both deployed in May 2012, each built from posts topped with three limestone-concrete discs. The West Reef (77 modules) averages 15 ft (4.5m) and sits about 300 ft (91m) offshore; the East Reef (28 modules, coordinates 30.38528, -86.85638) averages 12 ft (3.5m) roughly 150 ft (45.5m) out, in Santa Rosa Sound — expect lower visibility than the Gulf-side reef but calmer current. Juvenile sheepshead, grouper, mangrove snapper, damselfish, and blennies are typical here.
