## Navarre Sound-Side West Reef and East Reef

Navarre Sound-Side West Reef and East Reef is a reef dive site in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

Navarre Beach, Santa Rosa County. Two reefs deployed May 2012, each with central posts supporting three limestone concrete discs. West Reef: 77 modules at an average depth of 15ft(4.5m), about 300ft(91m) from shore. East Reef: 28 modules at an average depth of 12ft(3.5m), about 150ft(45.5m) from shore, in Santa Rosa Sound (lower visibility than the Gulf-side reef, but milder currents). Juvenile sheepshead, grouper, mangrove snapper, damselfish, and blennies commonly reported.
