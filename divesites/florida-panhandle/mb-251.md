## MB-251

MB-251 is a reef dive site in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

MB-251 is an artificial reef that features 118 tons of reef modules. The site was deployed in May 2019 and has a depth of approximately 77 ft (23.5 m).

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 23.5 meters
