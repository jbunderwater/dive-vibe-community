## FH-16

FH-16 is a reef dive site in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

395 tons of concrete material, including Jersey barriers and manhole covers, donated by Eglin Air Force Base and deployed by Okaloosa County in 2017. Sits at a depth of 66 ft (20m), offering 10 ft (3m) of relief from the sandy bottom.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 20 meters
