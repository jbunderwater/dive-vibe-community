## FH-16

FH-16 is a reef dive site in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

Eglin Air Force Base donated 395 tons of concrete debris — Jersey barriers, manhole covers — that the county deployed here in 2017. The material rises about 10 ft (3m) off the bottom at a depth of 66 ft (20m).

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 20 meters
