## Lane Gilchrest Reef

Lane Gilchrest Reef is a reef dive site in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

This artificial reef consists of 8,000 tons of concrete rubble from the Gulf Breeze fishing bridge spread across 60 acres of seafloor. It has a maximum depth of 50ft(15m).

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 15 meters
