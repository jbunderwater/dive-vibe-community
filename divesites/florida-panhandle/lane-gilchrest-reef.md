## Lane Gilchrest Reef

Lane Gilchrest Reef is a reef dive site in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

The Gulf Breeze fishing bridge's old rubble — 8,000 tons of it — was spread across 60 acres of seafloor to build this reef, which tops out at a maximum depth of 50 ft (15m).

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 15 meters
