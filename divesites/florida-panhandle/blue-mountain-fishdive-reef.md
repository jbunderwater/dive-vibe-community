## Blue Mountain Fish/Dive Reef

Blue Mountain Fish/Dive Reef is a reef dive site in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

Deployed in 2017 at a depth of 55 ft (17m). Features 30 artificial reef modules totaling 121 tons.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 17 meters
