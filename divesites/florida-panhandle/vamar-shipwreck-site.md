## Vamar Shipwreck Site

Vamar Shipwreck Site is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

Sank March 21, 1942 roughly 3.7 miles offshore Mexico Beach and became Florida's ninth Underwater Archaeological Preserve in 2004. Resting in only about 7.6 m (25 ft) of water, it's an accessible, shallow historic wreck at the eastern edge of the Panama City dive circuit.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 7.6 meters
