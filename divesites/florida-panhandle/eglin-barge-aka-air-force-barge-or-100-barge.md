## Eglin Barge (aka Air Force Barge or 100' Barge)

Eglin Barge (aka Air Force Barge or 100' Barge) is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

Deployed in 1977 - officially the seventh deployment of the Okaloosa County artificial reef program. Goes by various names, including 100' Barge, Eglin Barge, and Air Force Barge, all descriptive of the origin and size of the original vessel. Offers just 4 ft (1m) of relief from the seabed at a depth of 68 ft (20.5m). A goliath grouper is reported to inhabit the bow area.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 20.5 meters
