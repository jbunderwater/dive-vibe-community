## Eglin Barge (aka Air Force Barge or 100' Barge)

Eglin Barge (aka Air Force Barge or 100' Barge) is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

Deployed in 1977 as the seventh addition to Okaloosa County's reef program, this barge goes by several nicknames — 100' Barge, Eglin Barge, Air Force Barge — all pointing back to her origin and size. She offers only about 4 ft (1m) of relief at a depth of 68 ft (20.5m), and a goliath grouper has reportedly taken up residence near the bow.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 20.5 meters
