## 18-Mile Bridge (Hathaway Span #11)

18-Mile Bridge (Hathaway Span #11) is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

A span from the old Hathaway Bridge that once connected Panama City to Panama City Beach, also known as 18-Mile Bridge. Span 11 was deployed in May 1988 and sits on the seabed at a depth of 107 ft (32.5 m). Despite the depth, the span has deteriorated somewhat after several decades underwater.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 32.5 meters
