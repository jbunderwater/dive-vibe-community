## 18-Mile Bridge (Hathaway Span #11)

18-Mile Bridge (Hathaway Span #11) is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

Span 11 is one piece of the old Hathaway Bridge, demolished when Panama City and Panama City Beach got a replacement crossing; it went down as an artificial reef in May 1988, the seventh deployment under the county's reef program. Locals also call the site 18-Mile Bridge. It offers modest relief — about 4 ft (1m) — off a seabed at 68 ft (20.5m), and a goliath grouper has been reported holding near the bow.
