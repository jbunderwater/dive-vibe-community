## MB-248

MB-248 is a reef dive site in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

130 tons of reef modules make up this site, deployed in April 2018 in a north-south line stretching 760 ft (230m), at an average depth of 78 ft (24m).

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 24 meters
