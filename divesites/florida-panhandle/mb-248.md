## MB-248

MB-248 is a reef dive site in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

MB-248 is an artificial reef consisting of 130 tons of reef modules deployed in April 2018. The modules form a north-south line that stretches 760 ft (230 m) in length at an average depth of 78 ft (24 m).

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 24 meters
