## Main Stack

Main Stack is a reef dive site in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

Also called Destin Bridge Rubble, one of the most popular sites in the area. Features sections of roadway and other concrete rubble from the old Destin Bridge at a depth of 65 ft (20m). The debris stretches across three connected sites called Main Stack, East Stack, and North Stack. Divers explore the many overhangs and small ledges formed by the debris, often spotting sea bass, angelfish, grunts, and snapper.
