## Hathaway Bridge Spans #5 and 6

Hathaway Bridge Spans #5 and 6 is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

Spans 5 and 6, salvaged from the old Hathaway Bridge, sit in shallower water than most of their counterparts — around 73 ft (22.5m) — and have likewise been battered by the currents Hurricane Michael stirred up in 2018. Note: these two spans' coordinates place them well away from the other Hathaway clusters logged in this database, which fits the pattern of bridge material being scattered across multiple separate permitted reef sites rather than kept together.
