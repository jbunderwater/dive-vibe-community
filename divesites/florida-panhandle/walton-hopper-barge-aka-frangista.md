## Walton Hopper Barge (aka Frangista)

Walton Hopper Barge (aka Frangista) is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

A steel hopper barge measuring 195 ft in length and 35 ft in width. The county deployed it in 1999 along with 100 tons of steel ballast at a depth of 77 ft (23.5m).

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 23.5 meters
