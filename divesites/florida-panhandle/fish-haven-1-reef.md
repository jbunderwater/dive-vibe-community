## Fish Haven #1 Reef

Fish Haven #1 Reef is a reef dive site in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

Deployed in 2017, features 32 modules weighing 124 tons and offering 18 ft (5.5m) of relief off the sandy seafloor at a depth of 75 ft (23m).

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 23 meters
