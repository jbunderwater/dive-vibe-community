## FH-15

FH-15 is a reef dive site in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

This site holds 370 tons of concrete culverts, manhole covers, slabs, and bridge beams, all donated by Eglin Air Force Base and put down in 2017. Maximum depth is 71 ft (21.5m), with up to 11 ft (3.5m) of relief off the sandy bottom.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 21.5 meters
