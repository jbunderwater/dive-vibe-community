## Chickasaw

Chickasaw is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

Built in 1908 as a steam tugboat in Pensacola, FL — independent sources confirm the build year and location but not the guidebook's claim that she served the Army Corps of Engineers. Her length is also reported inconsistently: 102 ft per the guidebook, 107 ft per other listings. **Sourcing conflict**: sources disagree sharply on when she became a reef — the guidebook and some operators describe her sinking at the dock sometime in the 1970s, while other independent listings date her deployment to 1993, a 23-year gap I could not resolve. She now rests at about 70 ft (21.5m) a few miles outside the pass.
