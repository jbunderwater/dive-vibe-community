## Chickasaw

Chickasaw is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

Chickasaw was originally a 102-ft-long (31 m) steam tugboat built in 1908 for the Army Corps of Engineers. She was scuttled to form an artificial reef in 1970, just a few miles outside of the pass at a depth of around 70 ft (21.5 m).

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 21.5 meters
