## Hathaway Bridge Spans #3A and 4

Hathaway Bridge Spans #3A and 4 is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

These bridge spans are from the old Hathaway Bridge that once connected Panama City to Panama City Beach. Spans 3A and 4 sit at a depth of around 100 ft (30.5 m). Their depth has helped keep them in decent condition compared to the shallower Hathaway spans.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 30.5 meters
