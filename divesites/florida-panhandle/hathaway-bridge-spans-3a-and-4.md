## Hathaway Bridge Spans #3A and 4

Hathaway Bridge Spans #3A and 4 is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

Like the other numbered spans in this database, these two pieces came from the old Hathaway Bridge that once linked Panama City to Panama City Beach. Spans 3A and 4 rest at roughly 100 ft (30.5m) — deep enough that they've held up noticeably better than the shallower spans in the group.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 30.5 meters
