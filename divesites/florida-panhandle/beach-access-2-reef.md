## Beach Access #2 Reef

Beach Access #2 Reef is a reef dive site in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

One of four shore-accessible reefs Okaloosa County put in during spring 2021, this site arranges 97 four-disc modules into a swirl pattern, flanked by three-module 'star' clusters on both the inshore and seaward sides. Depths run roughly 15-22 ft (4.5-6.5m), with the shallowest points still at least 6 ft (3m) under the surface — none of the modules break 10 ft from the top. Damselfish, blennies, snapper, triggerfish, and grouper are common, and divers occasionally spot an octopus or sea turtle. A diver-down flag is legally required here.
