## Tully

Tully is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

A deep site featuring a 100-ft push tug on the seabed at approximately 135 ft (41m). Provides shelter for plenty of reef fish that hang out in and around the structure.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 41 meters
