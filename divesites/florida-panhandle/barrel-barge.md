## Barrel Barge

Barrel Barge is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

A popular site with local divers, deployed by Okaloosa County in 1992 along with large fuel tanks and the bed of a dump truck welded to the top of the barge. Hurricanes have since scattered the additions around the site, but it still offers 16 ft (5m) of relief above the sand at a maximum depth of 72 ft (22m). Divers report seeing plenty of jacks, spadefish, snapper, and flounder.
