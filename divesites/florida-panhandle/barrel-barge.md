## Barrel Barge

Barrel Barge is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

Local divers favor this 1992 deployment, which originally included large fuel tanks and a dump-truck bed welded on top of the barge — hurricanes have since scattered those extras around the site. Relief still reaches 16 ft (5m) off the sand, topping out at a maximum depth of 72 ft (22m). Jacks, spadefish, snapper, and flounder are all regularly reported here.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 22 meters
