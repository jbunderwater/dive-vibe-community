## Chepanoc

Chepanoc is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

Built for the US Navy at Gulfport Boiler & Welding Works in Port Arthur, TX, as an Allaquippa-class harbor tug, designated YT-381 and later YTB-381/YTM-381. The guidebook dates her build to 1944, redesignation to YTM-381 to 1957 (serving out of the 1st Naval District and Naval Station Newport, RI), and her strike from the naval record to 1986 — those vessel-specific dates weren't independently confirmed, though the class and designation history check out. Acquired by Okaloosa County and deployed as an artificial reef in the 'Starfish reef complex' on December 1, 2005. She took just over an hour to sink, settling upright with a slight list to port, bow pointing roughly south; wheelhouse top at 83 ft (25.5m), seabed 106-108 ft (32.5-33m). Her funnel was removed prior to scuttling; a large hole behind the wheelhouse permits limited penetration, with some wall corrosion, particularly on the starboard side. Jacks (almaco, greater amberjack) swarm the wreck; Atlantic spadefish and vermilion snapper schools sometimes obscure it; angelfish and butterflyfish cruise the hull; a resident goliath grouper is often found in the wheelhouse; large barracuda patrol the water column above; bull sharks occasionally seen on the outskirts.
