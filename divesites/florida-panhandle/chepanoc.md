## Chepanoc

Chepanoc is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

Built in 1944 in Port Arthur, TX (Gulfport Boiling and Welding Works) for the US Navy as a large harbor tug, designated YTB-381 ('yard tug big'). Served the Pacific Fleet, later transferred to the Pacific Reserve Fleet, reactivated in 1957 as YTM-381 (district harbor tug medium) operating out of the 1st Naval District and Naval Station Newport, RI. Struck from the naval record in 1986; acquired by Okaloosa County and deployed as an artificial reef in the 'Starfish reef complex' on December 1, 2005. She took just over an hour to sink, settling upright with a slight list to port, bow pointing roughly south; wheelhouse top at 83 ft (25.5m), seabed 106-108 ft (32.5-33m). Her funnel was removed prior to scuttling; a large hole behind the wheelhouse permits limited penetration, with some wall corrosion, particularly on the starboard side. Jacks (almaco, greater amberjack) swarm the wreck; Atlantic spadefish and vermilion snapper schools sometimes obscure it; angelfish and butterflyfish cruise the hull; a resident goliath grouper is often found in the wheelhouse; large barracuda patrol the water column above; bull sharks occasionally seen on the outskirts.
