## Phoenix Barge (aka Lost Barge)

Phoenix Barge (aka Lost Barge) is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

This 80-ft (24.5m) steel barge, sometimes called Lost Barge, sits at 75 ft (22.5m) with roughly 8 ft (2.5m) of relief above the sand — a favorite among local spearfishers.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 22.5 meters
