## Phoenix Barge (aka Lost Barge)

Phoenix Barge (aka Lost Barge) is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

An 80-ft steel barge sitting at a depth of 75 ft (22.5m), offering only 8 ft (2.5m) of relief above the sandy seabed. A popular site with spearfishers.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 22.5 meters
