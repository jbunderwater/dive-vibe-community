## Chevron Oil Rig

Chevron Oil Rig is a reef dive site in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

Two former oil platform jackets donated and deployed in 1993 under Florida's "Rigs to Reefs" program, rising 50-80 feet off the bottom in depths of roughly 24-42 m (80-137 ft). The structure attracts dense marine growth and is popular with lobster hunters.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 42 meters
