## DAN Safety Barge

DAN Safety Barge is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

A small, low-profile barge sits just south of the pass here, in about 60 ft (18m) of water — an easy, accessible spot that still provides decent habitat for reef life.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 18 meters
