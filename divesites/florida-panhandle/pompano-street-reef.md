## Pompano Street Reef

Pompano Street Reef is a reef dive site in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

Destin, Okaloosa County. One of four shore-accessible reefs deployed by Okaloosa County in spring 2021. Features 90 four-disc modules in the shape of a star surrounded by a circle, with a cluster of six reef modules at each corner. Shallowest modules ~16 ft (5m), deepest ~22 ft (6.5m). Damselfish, blennies, snapper, sea bass, triggerfish, sheepshead, grouper, occasional octopus/sea turtle/stingray; flounder common in the sand.
