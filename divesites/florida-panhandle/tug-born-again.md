## Tug Born Again

Tug Born Again is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

This 65-ft (20m) tugboat, her hold packed with metal pipes, was scuttled as a reef in 1991. Decades underwater have left her showing some wear, but she's still worth the visit at 95 ft (29m).

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 29 meters
