## Baskins Barge

Baskins Barge is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

An 80-ft lighter barge deployed as an artificial reef in 1996 at a depth of 135 ft (41m). Originally used to deploy range towers east of Apalachicola. Tops out at a minimum depth of 92 ft (28m), offering over 40 ft (12m) of relief. Amberjacks, grouper, snapper, and spadefish reported.
