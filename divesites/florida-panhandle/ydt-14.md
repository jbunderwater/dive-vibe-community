## YDT-14

YDT-14 is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

A 132.5-foot Navy dive tender, one of two sister ships intentionally sunk as artificial reefs in April 2000. The upper structure sits around 20 m (65 ft) with the wreck resting near 29-30 m (95-100 ft).

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters
