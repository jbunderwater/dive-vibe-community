## Brown Barge (aka Fort Walton Barge)

Brown Barge (aka Fort Walton Barge) is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

One of the longest barges deployed as an artificial reef in Okaloosa County, the 200-ft barge has since broken into three sections. Sits at a depth of 78 ft (24m), providing 10-12 ft (3-3.5m) of relief above the seabed.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 24 meters
