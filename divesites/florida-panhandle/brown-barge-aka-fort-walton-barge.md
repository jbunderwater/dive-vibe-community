## Brown Barge (aka Fort Walton Barge)

Brown Barge (aka Fort Walton Barge) is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

At 200 ft (61m), this is one of the longer barges Okaloosa County has sunk as a reef, though she's since broken into three sections. She rests at 78 ft (24m), with 10-12 ft (3-3.5m) of relief above the seabed.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 24 meters
