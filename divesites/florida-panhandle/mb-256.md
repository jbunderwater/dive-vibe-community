## MB-256

MB-256 is a reef dive site in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

MB-256 is an artificial reef consisting of more than 50 tons of reef modules deployed in May 2020 at a depth of 78 ft (24 m).

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 24 meters
