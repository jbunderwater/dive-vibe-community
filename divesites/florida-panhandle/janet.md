## Janet

Janet is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

An 85-ft tugboat deployed as an artificial reef October 1, 1997; little is known about her history before scuttling. Two-tiered superstructure rises 17 ft (5m) above the sand; top of the wreck at 80 ft (24.5m), seabed at 97 ft (29.5m). The open top of the main structure allows easier exploration for divers with wreck experience; slight corrosion in the aft port section. Schooling tomtates and vermilion snapper are the norm, along with blue angelfish, cobia, and grouper swimming around the wreck; stingrays and toadfish are often found out in the sand that surrounds the wreck, while barracuda tend to hang out above the wheelhouse.
