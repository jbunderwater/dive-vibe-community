## USS Massachusetts (BB-2)

USS Massachusetts (BB-2) is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

A pre-dreadnought battleship (BB-2) scuttled in shallow water roughly 1.5 nautical miles SSW of Pensacola Pass, now a Florida Underwater Archaeological Preserve. Exposed gun turrets, ammunition tubes, and anchor chain remain visible in only 8-9 m (26-30 ft) of water. Despite the shallow depth, currents can be strong and the site is not recommended for novice divers. Marine life documented at this specific site includes schools of fish, sponges, corals, sea turtles, and as many as seven Goliath grouper spotted at once.
