## Underwater Museum of Art

Underwater Museum of Art is a reef dive site in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

About a mile off Grayton Beach State Park — boat access only — sits the first permanent underwater sculpture park in the US. The Cultural Arts Alliance of Walton County and the South Walton Artificial Reef Association are the confirmed partners behind the project (the guidebook also credits Visit South Walton, which independent sources list more as a tourism-board host of UMA content than a founding partner). The museum opened with seven sculptures on June 25, 2018, and ten more were added in 2019, with further additions planned. The sculptures rest on a sandy bottom at 59 ft (18m), spaced 15-20 ft apart for easy navigation between them; standout pieces include Swara Skull, Propeller in Motion, JYC's Dream, Depth of Decision, and Anamorphous Octopus. Grunts, snapper, sea turtles, and octopuses are regular residents, and sharks are known to pass through.
