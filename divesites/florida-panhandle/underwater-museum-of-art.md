## Underwater Museum of Art

Underwater Museum of Art is a reef dive site in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

Santa Rosa Beach, Walton County. The first permanent underwater sculpture park in the US, roughly a mile offshore of Grayton Beach State Park (boat access only). An initial 7 statues were deployed in June 2018, with 10 more added in 2019, a partnership of Visit South Walton, the South Walton Artificial Reef Association, and the Cultural Arts Alliance of Walton County. Sandy seafloor at 59 ft (18m); statues spaced 15-20 ft apart for easy navigation. Notable pieces include Swara Skull, Propeller in Motion, JYC's Dream, Depth of Decision, and Anamorphous Octopus. Grunts, snapper, sea turtles, and octopuses reported; sharks known to cruise through.
