## Eglin LCM-1

Eglin LCM-1 is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

The second of two LCMs (landing craft) deployed in Okaloosa County, a 74-ft craft donated by Eglin Air Force Base in 1991. Sits at a depth of 103 ft (31.5m) and has deteriorated more than her sister site LCM-8, located to the northwest.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 31.5 meters
