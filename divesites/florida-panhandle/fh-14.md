## FH-14

FH-14 is a reef dive site in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

Smaller than the more popular Mac's Reef to the east, FH14 features 590 tons of concrete targets from Eglin Air Force Base. Deployed in 2015 at a depth of 72 ft (22m), the debris sits in a north-south pile that acts as the center for reef clusters to the west, north, and east, connected by more than 500 ft (150m) of debris trails to aid navigation.
