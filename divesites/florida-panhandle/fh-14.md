## FH-14

FH-14 is a reef dive site in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

Smaller than the better-known Mac's Reef to the east, this site is built from 590 tons of concrete targets donated by Eglin Air Force Base and deployed in 2015. The debris forms a north-south pile at 72 ft (22m) that serves as the hub for several reef clusters to the west, north, and east, all connected by more than 500 ft (150m) of navigable debris trails.
