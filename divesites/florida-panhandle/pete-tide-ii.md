## Pete Tide II

Pete Tide II is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

A 180-foot former oil-field supply boat sunk as an artificial reef in 1993, about 12 nautical miles south of Pensacola Pass. The wreck sits upright with an intact superstructure and visible pilot house, with depths from roughly 18 m (60 ft) at the top of the structure to 30-32 m (100-105 ft) at the seafloor.
