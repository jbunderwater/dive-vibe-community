## Pete Tide II

Pete Tide II is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

Anchor-handling tug supply (AHTS) vessel, 165ft(50.5m) long, built 1973 by Halter Marine in Lockport, LA. Serviced Gulf of Mexico oil fields for two decades before being deployed as an artificial reef in May 1993; one of the original members of the Florida Panhandle Shipwreck Trail (established 2012). Sits upright on sand at 102ft(31m), with the top of her wheelhouse reaching 62ft(19m). Offers three deck levels and three large holds that sit open and accessible in the main deck area toward the stern; further penetration opportunities exist for divers with overhead-environment experience, while the relatively open wheelhouse is accessible to divers with good buoyancy control. Atlantic spadefish swarm the wreck; large red and lane snapper, barracuda, and king mackerel patrol the waters above; blue angelfish, cocoa damselfish, sharpnose puffers, and spotfin butterflyfish common on the structure itself. Divers should watch for entanglement hazards from hanging wires and monofilament fishing line.
