## Tennessee Chuck McKibbonville Reef

Tennessee Chuck McKibbonville Reef is a reef dive site in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

More than 75 tons of limestone and concrete reef modules went down here in May 2020, settling at a depth of 65 ft (20m).

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 20 meters
