## Tennessee Chuck McKibbonville Reef

Tennessee Chuck McKibbonville Reef is a reef dive site in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

Deployed in May 2020, this reef features more than 75 tons of limestone and concrete reef modules at a depth of 65 ft (20 m).

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 20 meters
