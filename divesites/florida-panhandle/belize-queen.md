## Belize Queen

Belize Queen is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

An 85-ft tugboat deployed as an artificial reef by Okaloosa County in May 2001; officially known as the Bob Reay Reef, named for an Okaloosa County resident who died in 2000 shortly before the ship was scuttled. Added to the Florida Panhandle Shipwreck Trail in 2020. Her stern is almost entirely buried in sand while the bow rests about 12 ft (3.5m) above the seafloor, giving the wreck a pronounced upward tilt; wheelhouse top at 96 ft (29m), seabed at 112 ft (34m). Wheelhouse and part of the main cabin remain upright, though the entire rear section has been lost, leaving a large open space facing the stern. Jacks (greater amberjack, almaco jack) patrol the water column, porgies and blue angelfish are common, rock hind frequently spotted, invasive lionfish also reported here, slipper lobsters hide in shadowy recesses.
