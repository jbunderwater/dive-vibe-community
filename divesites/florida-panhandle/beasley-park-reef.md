## Beasley Park Reef

Beasley Park Reef is a reef dive site in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

Okaloosa County put down 69 four-disc concrete modules here in spring 2019, one of four shore-accessible reefs the county added around that time. The modules form a cross inside a square, with three-module clusters anchoring each inner corner. Depth runs from about 15 ft (4.5m) at the shallow end to roughly 23 ft (7m) at the far modules, with everything sitting at least 6 ft (2m) under the surface. Snapper, triggerfish, and grouper are the regulars here. State law requires a diver-down flag whenever anyone's in the water at this site.
