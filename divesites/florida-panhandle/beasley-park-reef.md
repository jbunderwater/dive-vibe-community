## Beasley Park Reef

Beasley Park Reef is a reef dive site in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

This is one of four shore-accessible reefs deployed by Okaloosa County in the spring of 2019. The Beasley Park Reef features a total of 69 four-disc reef modules in the shape of a cross surrounded by a square, with four sets of three modules each located at the inside corners of the cross. The shallowest modules are embedded in the sandy seabed at a depth of 15ft(4.5m), while the deeper ones bottom out at closer to 23ft(7m). All modules are at least 6ft(2m) below the surface of the water while the tops of the deepest modules sit closer to 16ft(5m) deep. Divers and snorkelers are likely to see snapper, triggerfish and grouper here. A diver down flag is required by law when divers and snorkelers enter the water at this site.
