## Navarre Gulf-Side Reef

Navarre Gulf-Side Reef is a reef dive site in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

Navarre Beach, Santa Rosa County. First phase deployed September 2012 (three rows of ten modules, extending 275ft/84m into the Gulf); expanded in 2017 with 48 more modules in three rows on either side of the original. Shallowest original modules are often buried by a sandbar; uncovered modules bottom out between 11 and 12.5ft(3.5 and 4m).

## Site Information
