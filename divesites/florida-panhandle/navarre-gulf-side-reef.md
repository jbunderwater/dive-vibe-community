## Navarre Gulf-Side Reef

Navarre Gulf-Side Reef is a reef dive site in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

The county's first phase here went in during September 2012 — three rows of ten modules extending 275 ft (84m) into the Gulf — followed by a 2017 expansion that added 48 more modules flanking the original rows. A sandbar frequently buries the shallowest of the original modules; the ones still exposed sit at 11-12.5 ft (3.5-4m).
