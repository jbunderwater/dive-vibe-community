## Antares

Antares is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

A 387-ft(118m) cargo freighter, built 1968 in Bilbao, Spain (Euskalduna de Construcción Naval) and christened Mirenchu. Sold in 1972 to Marítima Del Norte and renamed Sierra Jara; resold 1980 to Panama-based Ferilpis Shipping as Acmi; ran aground in the Berbice River, Guyana due to tidal-schedule confusion, refloated and towed to the Dominican Republic, and deemed a total loss — but was instead repaired and resold to Altair Maritime as Altair I (1983), then Galazio Kyma (1991), and finally Antares (1992) before being decommissioned in 1993. Acquired by the Escambia County artificial reef program and deployed September 27, 1995 — just one week before Hurricane Opal made landfall near Pensacola as a Category 2 storm, breaking the newly deployed wreck's hull into large, scattered pieces (the superstructure held its own against the currents). Highest point (starboard bow corner) at 94ft(28.5m); sandy seafloor around 120ft(36.5m), reaching 130ft(39.5m) in the deeper middle section near the superstructure. Bull sharks commonly seen patrolling; large snapper, amberjacks, spadefish, and a resident goliath grouper often spotted in the bridge section. Sources explicitly flag the wreck as dangerous to fully explore in one dive given its size and depth.
