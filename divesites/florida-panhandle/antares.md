## Antares

Antares is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

A 387-foot freighter intentionally sunk September 27, 1995, roughly 21-24 nautical miles ESE of Pensacola Pass. Originally one of the largest intact artificial reefs in Florida, repeated hurricanes have since broken the wreck apart; only the stern remains largely intact today, spanning depths of about 27-40 m (90-130 ft). Sources explicitly flag the wreck as dangerous to penetrate.
