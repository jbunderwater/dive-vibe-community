## Miramar Frangista Fish/Dive Reef

Miramar Frangista Fish/Dive Reef is a reef dive site in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

The county's original 2017 deployment featured 121 tons of concrete in the form of 31 reef modules at a depth of 68 ft (20.5m). An additional 63 modules totaling 233 tons were deployed in 2019 in groups adjacent to the original site.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 20.5 meters
