## Henderson Park East Reef

Henderson Park East Reef is a reef dive site in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

This is one of four shore-accessible reefs deployed by Okaloosa County in the spring of 2019. Features 40 four-disc reef modules in the shape of a diamond, with two rows of modules along its sides plus four at the center. The shallowest modules are embedded in the sandy seabed at a depth of 15ft(4.5m), while the deeper ones bottom out at closer to 17ft(5m). All modules are at least 6ft(2m) below the surface. A diver down flag is required by law when divers and snorkelers enter the water at this site.
