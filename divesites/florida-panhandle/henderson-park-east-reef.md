## Henderson Park East Reef

Henderson Park East Reef is a reef dive site in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

Part of a four-reef series Okaloosa County put down in spring 2019, this site uses 40 four-disc modules arranged in a diamond — two rows along each side plus a cluster of four at the center. The shallow modules sit around 15 ft (4.5m); the deepest bottom out near 17 ft (5m), with everything kept at least 6 ft (2m) below the surface. A diver-down flag is legally required at this site.
