## Inlet Beach Fish/Dive Reef

Inlet Beach Fish/Dive Reef is a reef dive site in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

This 2017 deployment consists of 31 modules — 124 tons of concrete habitat — resting at about 58 ft (17.5m).

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 17.5 meters
