## Dylan

Dylan is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

A 61-ft steel-hulled sailboat seized by US Customs in 2019 after agents found 400 lbs of cocaine on board with a street value of $7.5 million. The county acquired the vessel and sank her as an artificial reef a year later. Sits at a depth of 119 ft (36.5m) with her shallowest point at 107 ft (32.6m); the first element of a cluster of nearby wrecks known as the 'boatyard site'.
