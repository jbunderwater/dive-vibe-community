## Hathaway Bridge Spans #7, 8, 9 and 10

Hathaway Bridge Spans #7, 8, 9 and 10 is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

These bridge spans are from the old Hathaway Bridge that once connected Panama City to Panama City Beach. Spans 10, 9, 8, and 7 sit at a depth of around 113 ft (34.5 m), which has helped protect these spans and kept them in decent condition compared to the other Hathaway spans.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 34.5 meters
