## Hathaway Bridge Spans #7, 8, 9 and 10

Hathaway Bridge Spans #7, 8, 9 and 10 is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

Spans 7 through 10, all from the demolished Hathaway Bridge, rest at around 113 ft (34.5m) — depth that has worked in their favor, leaving them in noticeably better shape than the county's shallower Hathaway spans.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 34.5 meters
