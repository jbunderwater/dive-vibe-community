## BJ Putnam

BJ Putnam is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

Before becoming an artificial reef, this 180-ft (55m) vessel reportedly worked as an oilfield supply boat and later a fish processor. She was renamed for a local charter captain, businessman, and community figure before being sunk in November 1993; she now sits at 106 ft (32.5m). **Single-source flag**: this vessel's working history and deployment date could not be independently corroborated — multiple charter listings confirm a wreck named BJ Putnam exists off Panama City, but none verify the specifics above. Treat this history as unverified until a second source is found.
