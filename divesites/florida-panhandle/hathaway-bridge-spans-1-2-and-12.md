## Hathaway Bridge Spans #1, 2 and 12

Hathaway Bridge Spans #1, 2 and 12 is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

These bridge spans are from the old Hathaway Bridge that once connected Panama City to Panama City Beach. Spans 1, 2, and 12 are clustered within the group of inshore artificial reefs that sit close to the pass in relatively shallow water (around 73 ft/22.5 m). These spans have largely collapsed after the strong currents generated by Hurricane Michael in 2018.
