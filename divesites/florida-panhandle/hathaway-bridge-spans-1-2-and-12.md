## Hathaway Bridge Spans #1, 2 and 12

Hathaway Bridge Spans #1, 2 and 12 is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

More material from the old Hathaway Bridge: spans 1, 2, and 12 sit together among the county's inshore reef cluster near the pass, in comparatively shallow water — about 73 ft (22.5m). Hurricane Michael's currents largely collapsed these three in 2018.

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
