## Mac's Reef

Mac's Reef is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

This Navy work barge, designated 2154, reportedly once supported salvage-diver training out of the local base before the county sank her as a reef in 2007, at a depth of 97 ft (30m). The site carries the name of Richard 'Mac' McCullen, a local diver said to have passed away a few years ahead of the deployment. **Single-source flag**: despite checking Bay County's artificial reef association, UF/IFAS reef records, and multiple dive-shop and news sources, no independent record of this specific site could be found — note this is a different site from Okaloosa County's Charles H. McClenahan Memorial Reef, which is also nicknamed 'Mac's Reef.' Treat the details here as unverified until a second source is found.
