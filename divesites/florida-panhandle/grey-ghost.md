## Grey Ghost

Grey Ghost is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

A former 105-110-foot Navy tugboat intentionally sunk on July 12, 1978, roughly 22 nautical miles offshore of Panama City Beach. The wreck rests on its port side along a natural limestone reef, in depths of about 32-34 m (105-110 ft).

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 33 meters
