## USS Oriskany

USS Oriskany is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

A 911-foot Essex-class aircraft carrier deliberately sunk on May 17, 2006 approximately 22.5 nautical miles southeast of Pensacola Pass, widely cited as the world's largest artificial reef — nicknamed "The Great Carrier Reef." The Oriskany served in the Korean and Vietnam Wars before being reefed. The flight deck sits at roughly 44 m (145 ft) with the shallowest superstructure around 24 m (80 ft) and the seafloor near 65 m (212 ft). Florida Fish and Wildlife explicitly advises against entering the interior due to structural uncertainty — this is not a wreck where penetration should be treated as safe.
