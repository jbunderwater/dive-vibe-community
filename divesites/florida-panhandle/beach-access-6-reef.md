## Beach Access #6 Reef

Beach Access #6 Reef is a reef dive site in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

This is one of four shore-accessible reefs deployed by Okaloosa County in the spring of 2021. Features 90 four-disc modules forming concentric circles surrounded by a square, with three star clusters of six modules each at the seaward edge. The shallowest modules are embedded in the sandy seabed at a depth of 16ft(5m), while the deeper ones bottom out at closer to 22ft(6.5m). A diver down flag is required by law when divers and snorkelers enter the water at this site.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 6.5 meters
