## Beach Access #6 Reef

Beach Access #6 Reef is a reef dive site in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

This is one of four shore-accessible reefs Okaloosa County deployed in spring 2021: 90 four-disc modules forming concentric circles inside a square, bordered by three six-module star clusters on the seaward edge. Depths span roughly 16-22 ft (5-6.5m). As with the county's other shore reefs, a diver-down flag is legally required while in the water.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 6.5 meters
