## Thomas Hayward (SS Thomas Heyward)

Thomas Hayward (SS Thomas Heyward) is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

A 360-foot steel Liberty Ship built in 1942, which served in WWII and the Korean War before being sunk as an artificial reef in 1977 about four miles south of Okaloosa Island Pier. The wreck rises up to 25 feet off the seafloor in roughly 27 m (90 ft) of water. Note: the ship's historic name is spelled "Thomas Heyward," but local dive-site naming commonly uses "Thomas Hayward" — a spelling discrepancy worth flagging.
