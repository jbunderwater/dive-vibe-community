## Sea Barb

Sea Barb is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

Also called Odyssey, an 85-ft steel-hulled paddlewheeler reportedly once used on the Mississippi River, deployed as an artificial reef in 2002. Rests on a sandy seabed at a depth of 110 ft (33.5m).

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 33.5 meters
