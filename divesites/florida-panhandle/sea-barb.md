## Sea Barb

Sea Barb is a historic wreck dive in Florida Panhandle, North America.

## Overview

Also known as Odyssey, this 85-ft (26m) steel-hulled paddlewheeler — said to have once worked the Mississippi River — became an artificial reef in 2002. She now rests on sand at 110 ft (33.5m).

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Panhandle, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 33.5 meters
