# Santa Iria

A protected bay on São Miguel's north coast — volcanic lava reef with a no-fishing designation.

## Overview

Santa Iria is a bay (Porto de Santa Iria) on the north coast of São Miguel near Rabo de Peixe, designated as a no-fishing area. The bay features a sheltered lagoon with a narrow inlet from the sea, with one side comprising a lava flow and the other reinforced with large boulders.
