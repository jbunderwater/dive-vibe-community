# Gruta dos Enxaréus

Narrow cave corridors on Flores Island where groups of six or more large stingrays regularly shelter — two 30-metre passages barely over a metre wide.

## Overview

Gruta dos Enxaréus on Flores Island is a dramatic coastal cave system situated at the base of sea cliffs on the headland of Ponta da Caveira, between Santa Cruz and Caveira. The site features two corridors, each approximately 30 metres long and in places barely over 1 metre wide, with an average depth of 15 metres and a maximum of 23 metres — confirmed verbatim, identically, by both dive.visitazores.com and turismo.azores.gov.pt. Access is by boat, 10 minutes from Santa Cruz Quay. Coordinates were tightened on 2026-07-15 to match turismo.azores.gov.pt's precise figure (39.4265, -31.1409); the previous coordinates were an approximation roughly 2.5 km off. Note for future research: a similarly-named but distinct site, "Gruta dos Enchareús," exists on Santa Maria Island — the two are easy to confuse, but this entry is confirmed to be the Flores site.

The defining feature is common stingrays (*Dasyatis pastinaca*) that regularly use the cave as shelter; divers frequently encounter groups of six or more large stingrays at the wide entrance or inside the corridors. Despite modest depths, the narrow passage exploration restricts the site to Advanced Open Water certified divers.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Ponta da Caveira, Flores Island, Azores
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Cave/cavern
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 23 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 15–25 meters
- **Current**: Weak
- **Best Time**: June to September

## Marine Life

Common stingrays (*Dasyatis pastinaca*) in groups of six or more inside the cave corridors — the defining feature, confirmed identically by dive.visitazores.com and turismo.azores.gov.pt. Outside in the sheltered bay: grey triggerfish and white trevallies (*Pseudocaranx dentex*), confirmed by both sources. The two sources disagree on the third species: dive.visitazores.com names Almaco jacks (*Seriola rivoliana*), while turismo.azores.gov.pt names greater amberjack (*Seriola dumerili*) — both are jacks in the same genus, but the exact species is unresolved between sources rather than picked arbitrarily.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Groups of 6+ large stingrays regularly shelter inside the narrow corridors
- Two 30 m-long corridors barely over 1 m wide — requires controlled buoyancy and a slender profile
- Advanced certification required; the cave environment is the primary challenge, not depth
- 10 minutes by boat from Santa Cruz, Flores
- Flores is one of the most remote Azores islands — plan logistics carefully (ferry or small aircraft from Faial)
