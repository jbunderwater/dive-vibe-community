# Rostro de Cao

A small volcanic islet off São Miguel's south coast near São Roque — named for its dog-face rock profile above the waterline.

## Overview

Rostro de Cão (Dog's Face) is a coastal volcanic islet and rock formation (Ilhéu de Rosto de Cão) on the south coast of São Miguel, located near São Roque between Ponta Delgada and Lagoa. The islet is named for a natural rock formation resembling a dog's face, visible from the coastline.
