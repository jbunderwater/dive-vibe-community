## Giants Castle

Giants Castle is a drift wall dive off Tofo, where a crenellated reef ridge drops to 33–35 metres — best experienced as a natural drift along the castle-like ramparts. Sources disagree on exactly how far the wall runs (roughly 350 metres per one operator, up to two kilometres per another).

## Overview

The wall at Giants Castle starts at around 26 metres and drops to a seabed variously reported between 30 and 35 metres, with the eastern side of the reef presenting a series of ledges, overhangs, and crevices. Sources disagree on the wall's total length: Accommodation Mozambique describes it running "more than 350 metres" north to south, while Tofo Scuba's current site listing describes the ridge running for "two kilometres." Both figures are reported here rather than one being picked arbitrarily. The site complements the prevailing north-to-south current, making it an ideal drift dive when conditions allow. Distance from Tofo is also reported inconsistently — Tofo Scuba and Accommodation Mozambique both cite roughly 6 km / 10 minutes by boat, while Liquid Dive Adventures cites 3 km / 5–10 minutes. Operators at Tofo recommend negative entries and proficiency with drift procedures; bottom time on air runs roughly 21 minutes at these depths.

Confirmed marine life at Giants Castle includes large potato bass and stingrays, multiple ray species (eagle rays, devil rays, blotched fantail rays, Kuhl's stingrays), schooling bannerfish, great barracuda, kingfish, and moray eels. Manta rays have been documented at cleaning stations along the wall. Bowmouth guitar sharks have also been recorded here. Sailfish and blue marlin are occasional open-water visitors.

The existing description attributing Zambezi sharks and hammerheads to this site specifically is not confirmed in operator records for Giants Castle — those species are more reliably associated with the Pinnacles sites at Ponta do Ouro to the south. Divers should expect large reef fish and ray encounters; shark sightings are possible but not a primary feature of this site.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Tofo, Inhambane Province, Mozambique
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 35 meters
