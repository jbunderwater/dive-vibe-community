# Green Pearl

*Green Pearl, near Gibson Bight, combines a wall with sand channels and cleaning stations.*

## Overview

Green Pearl, near Gibson Bight, combines a wall with sand channels and cleaning stations. Octopus Dive School describes an 'underwater garden' plateau area that is also useful for safety stops.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Roatán, Bay Islands, Honduras
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 18 meters

## Marine Life

Jawfish, flounders, green sea turtles and southern stingrays are reported by Octopus Dive School.
