# Bear's Den

*Bear's Den is a swim-through/cavern site for experienced Open Water and Advanced divers.*

## Overview

Bear's Den is a swim-through/cavern site for experienced Open Water and Advanced divers. Octopus Dive School describes a spacious cavern reached through a narrow entrance, opening into a chamber with glassy sweepers circling under overhead light effects.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Roatán, Bay Islands, Honduras
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Cave/cavern
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 20 meters

## Marine Life

Glassy sweepers, juvenile spotted drums, lettuce sea slugs, trumpetfish and neck crabs are reported by Octopus Dive School.
