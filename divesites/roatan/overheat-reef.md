# Overheat Reef

*Overheat Reef, off Sandy Bay roughly 15-20 minutes from West End docks, is a protected shallow plateau (about 5-18 meters per Octopus Dive School) used for courses and buoyancy training, with a nearby wall for more experienced divers and a reputation for good night diving.*

## Overview

Overheat Reef, off Sandy Bay roughly 15-20 minutes from West End docks, is a protected shallow plateau (about 5-18 meters per Octopus Dive School) used for courses and buoyancy training, with a nearby wall for more experienced divers and a reputation for good night diving.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Roatán, Bay Islands, Honduras
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 15 meters

## Marine Life

Green sea turtles, eagle rays, creole wrasses, blue chromis, toadfish, frogfish and sergeant majors are reported by Octopus Dive School.
