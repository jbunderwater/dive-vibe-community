## Overview

Odyssey Stern is a mooring point giving access to the stern section of the Odyssey, Roatán's largest shipwreck — a 91-meter (300-foot) freighter intentionally sunk in 2002 after a fire during a rebuild (see "Odyssey Wreck" for full vessel history). The stern structure rises to roughly 18 meters (60 feet) below the surface per the Roatan Tourism Bureau, with the sand bottom deeper. Cut access holes allow entry to the interior; conditions inside change over time and should be confirmed with a local operator before penetration.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Roatán, Bay Islands, Honduras
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters

## Marine Life

Large groupers, barracuda and moray eels are reported on the stern structure by Splash Inn and DreamWrecks.
