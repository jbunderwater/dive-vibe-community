# Texas

## Overview

Texas (also called "The Point") is a condition-dependent drift dive at Roatán's western tip, reaching a plateau around 27 meters (90 feet) with massive barrel sponges. Multiple operators rate it among the island's best dives, best suited to Advanced divers given the current and depth; Octopus Dive School and Roatan Divers recommend Nitrox for deeper exploration.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Roatán, Bay Islands, Honduras
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 18 meters

## Marine Life

Sargassum triggerfish — reported by multiple operators as found at no other Roatán site — plus angelfish, hogfish, barracuda and Atlantic spadefish are seen here per Octopus Dive School.
