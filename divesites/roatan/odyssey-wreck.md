# Odyssey Wreck

*The Odyssey is a 91-meter (300-foot), 15-meter (50-foot) wide freighter, formerly owned by Hybur Limited, that suffered a catastrophic fire during a rebuild and was donated for scuttling.*

## Overview

The Odyssey is a 91-meter (300-foot), 15-meter (50-foot) wide freighter, formerly owned by Hybur Limited, that suffered a catastrophic fire during a rebuild and was donated for scuttling. Roughly 50 truckloads of debris were removed and steel hatch covers welded shut before the vessel was towed from French Harbour and intentionally sunk off Roatán's north coast on November 15, 2002, as an artificial reef and dive site. It rests at approximately 35 meters (115 feet) overall, with the stern structure rising to around 18 meters (60 feet) and the bow to around 22 meters (73 feet). It is Roatán's largest shipwreck. No source consulted gives the vessel's build date or tonnage.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Roatán, Bay Islands, Honduras
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 33 meters

## Marine Life

Schoolmaster snappers, black groupers, barracuda, tuna, Caribbean reef sharks and moray eels are reported at the wreck by the Roatan Tourism Bureau. Splash Inn and DreamWrecks add red snappers and glassy sweepers on the interior structure.
