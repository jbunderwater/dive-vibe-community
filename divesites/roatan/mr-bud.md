# Mr Bud

*Mr. Bud is a small wreck resting on sand adjacent to French Cay, near a drop-off that divers also explore.*

## Overview

Mr. Bud is a small wreck resting on sand adjacent to French Cay, near a drop-off that divers also explore. Reef Smart Guides describes it as a fishing boat sunk as an artificial reef in 1994; other regional accounts describe it instead as a cargo/shrimp boat scuttled around 1994-1995 — sources disagree on the exact vessel type and sinking year, and that discrepancy is noted here rather than resolved by guessing. CoCo View Resort, whose house reef system includes the wreck, lists it at 45-60 feet (14-18 meters), consistent with the depth already on file. No source consulted gives the vessel's length, tonnage or original build date.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Roatán, Bay Islands, Honduras
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 18 meters

## Marine Life

Seahorses, yellowhead jawfish, garden eels, nudibranchs and stingrays are reported on the wreck by Reef Smart Guides and CoCo View Resort. The Roatan Aggressor's dive log independently reports seahorses, jawfish, nudibranchs, sergeant majors guarding nests, and octopus during a night dive on the site.
