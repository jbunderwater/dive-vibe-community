# Pirata's Dream

*Pirata's Dream is Roatan Marine Park's first donor-named dive site, commemorated with a plaque.*

## Overview

Pirata's Dream is Roatan Marine Park's first donor-named dive site, commemorated with a plaque. Octopus Dive School describes a swim-through connecting a shallow reef to a wall, with a canyon and platform around 12 meters (40 feet) and depths ranging 9-21 meters (30-70 feet).

## Site Information

- **Location**: Roatán, Bay Islands, Honduras
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 21 meters

## Marine Life

Eagle rays, sea turtles, trumpetfish, jawfish, neck crabs and decorator crabs are reported by Octopus Dive School.
