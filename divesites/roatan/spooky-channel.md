# Spooky Channel

*Spooky Channel is a canyon/swim-through system with narrow tunnels, overhangs and wall sections, described by Octopus Dive School as 'one of Roatán's finest dives.' Splash Inn lists a depth around 29 meters; Roatan-Diving.com describes an eerie atmosphere from the crevasse's dim light.*

## Overview

Spooky Channel is a canyon/swim-through system with narrow tunnels, overhangs and wall sections, described by Octopus Dive School as 'one of Roatán's finest dives.' Splash Inn lists a depth around 29 meters; Roatan-Diving.com describes an eerie atmosphere from the crevasse's dim light.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Roatán, Bay Islands, Honduras
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Cave/cavern
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 27 meters

## Marine Life

Toadfish, scorpionfish, lettuce sea slugs, glassy sweepers and moray eels are reported by Octopus Dive School.
