# Butcher Bank

*Butcher's Bank is a sandy plateau/macro site popular with beginner divers.*

## Overview

Butcher's Bank is a sandy plateau/macro site popular with beginner divers. Sources disagree substantially on maximum depth: DiveBuddy reports 111-120 feet (34-37 meters), PADI's site page lists 30 feet (9 meters), and Sun Divers Roatan describes typical dives in the 40-60 foot (12-18 meter) range. Roatan Divers describes both a shallow and a deeper option at this site, which may explain the spread — divers should confirm depth plans with their operator rather than relying on any single figure here.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Roatán, Bay Islands, Honduras
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 37 meters

## Marine Life

Pipefish, flounder, soles and turtles are reported by Sun Divers Roatan; the site is known among local operators primarily for macro/critter life on sand rather than reef structure.
