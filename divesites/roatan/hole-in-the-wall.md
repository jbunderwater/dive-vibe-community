# Hole In The Wall

## Overview

Hole in the Wall is a signature Advanced-level chute/swim-through: a long tunnel with unusual "Swiss cheese" rock formations that begins around 12 meters and exits through the reef wall into open blue water at roughly 30 meters, per Octopus Dive School. Roatan Clearwater Adventures describes the wider area as a network of canyons, swim-throughs and a cave formed by ancient freshwater erosion through the reef wall.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Roatán, Bay Islands, Honduras
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Cave/cavern
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters
