# Deep Seaquest

*Sea Quest Deep is described by Sun Divers Roatan and Roatan Divers as sandy plateaus and patch reef at Open Water diver level, part of the Sea Quest site complex.*

## Overview

Sea Quest Deep is described by Sun Divers Roatan and Roatan Divers as sandy plateaus and patch reef at Open Water diver level, part of the Sea Quest site complex.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Roatán, Bay Islands, Honduras
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 24 meters

## Marine Life

Turtles and stingrays are reported at the Sea Quest complex by Roatan Divers; no source specifically distinguishes sightings at the deep section from the shallow one.
