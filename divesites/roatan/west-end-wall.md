## Overview

West End Wall, at Roatán's western tip, is one of the island's most popular dives — a shallow reef edge that drops dramatically into a wall reaching roughly 27-30 meters (90-100 feet), per PADI and Octopus Dive School. Currents from multiple directions create good conditions for varied fish species; the site is often the starting point for a drift toward Texas.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Roatán, Bay Islands, Honduras
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters

## Marine Life

Barracuda, wahoo, eagle rays, permits and adult crevalle jacks are reported by Roatan-Diving.com and PADI; hammerhead sharks and sailfish are noted as occasional, not guaranteed, sightings.
