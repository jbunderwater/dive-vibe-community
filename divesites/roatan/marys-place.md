# Mary's Place

*Mary's Place is one of Roatán's most celebrated wall dives, featuring a deep, extended crack in the wall formed by volcanic activity, plus dramatic overhangs.*

## Overview

Mary's Place is one of Roatán's most celebrated wall dives, featuring a deep, extended crack in the wall formed by volcanic activity, plus dramatic overhangs. CoCo View Resort notes it won Scuba Diving Magazine's 2018 Readers' Choice Award and lists a plateau spanning roughly 9-18 meters that extends to 36 meters at the crack.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Roatán, Bay Islands, Honduras
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 36 meters
