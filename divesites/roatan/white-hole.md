## White Hole

White Hole is a wall dive site in Roatán, Caribbean.

## Overview

White Hole takes its name from a distinctive circular patch of bright white sand at 35 feet, surrounded by coral heads. A plateau extends to around 60 feet before the wall drops away beyond 120 feet. Shrimp, anemones, lobster, and encrusting sponges are reported around the coral patches, with large schools of fish emerging from the deep blue on the wall section. White Hole is also noted as a popular night dive site. Rated intermediate to advanced.

*Note: An earlier version of this page described "domesticated" large groupers approaching divers at this site. That claim could not be confirmed against the source cited for White Hole and has been removed.*
