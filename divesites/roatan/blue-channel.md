# Blue Channel

## Overview

Blue Channel, off the West End shore, is a shallow channel with swim-throughs and small caves, gradually sloping from about 3 meters to 13-14 meters per Octopus Dive School and Splash Inn. It transitions into a shallow sandy lagoon area good for macro photography.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Roatán, Bay Islands, Honduras
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Drift dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 14 meters

## Marine Life

Upside-down jellyfish, southern stingrays, eagle rays, hermit crabs, flounders, spotted drumfish and moray eels are reported across Sun Divers Roatan and Roatan Clearwater Adventures.
