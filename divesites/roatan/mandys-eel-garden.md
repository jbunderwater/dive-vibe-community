# Mandys Eel Garden

*Mandy's Eel Garden, off West Bay Beach, is named for the garden eels that appear to grow out of the sand near the base of the wall.*

## Overview

Mandy's Eel Garden, off West Bay Beach, is named for the garden eels that appear to grow out of the sand near the base of the wall. The reef begins around 7 meters and the wall drops to roughly 20 meters per Octopus Dive School; large sand channels provide habitat for burrowing species. It's a popular site for early-morning macro photography and training dives.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Roatán, Bay Islands, Honduras
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 20 meters

## Marine Life

Garden eels, nudibranchs, flounders, seahorses, spotted eagle rays and jawfish are reported by Octopus Dive School and Roatan Clearwater Adventures. Stingrays and hogfish are also noted by Roatan Clearwater Adventures.
