## Coral Gardens

Coral Gardens is a shallow, sheltered reef in Batali Bay within the Soufriere-Scotts Head Marine Reserve, notable for its dense finger and pencil coral formations and an exceptional variety of macro critters.

## Overview

Nestled in Batali Bay inshore from Danglebens Pinnacles — Dive Dominica lists this as "Coral Gardens North" — Coral Gardens is one of the most sheltered dive sites in the southern marine reserve. The reef starts at 20–30 feet with large areas of finger coral and pencil coral over a sandy bottom, the sand harboring red heart sea urchins and small clusters of garden eels along with occasional upside-down jellyfish. The site is well-suited to beginners and snorkelers, and conditions are generally calm.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Dominica, Caribbean
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner

## Marine Life

Dive Dominica documents turtles, horse-eye jacks, sennet schooling where currents sweep the reef, and yellow-headed jawfish in the sandy areas at this site. A prior version of this description also listed frogfish (in several color variants) and occasional nurse sharks; neither claim could be traced to a source confirming sightings specifically at Coral Gardens, so both have been removed rather than left as unverified padding.
