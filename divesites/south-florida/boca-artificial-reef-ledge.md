## Boca Artificial Reef Ledge

Boca Artificial Reef Ledge is a natural ledge off Boca Raton with a small group of concrete Erojacks, dived as a reef drift.

## Overview

Boca Artificial is part of the outer reef system, running parallel to shore. The main ledge faces west, holds the highest concentration of marine life and coral growth, and has an undercut with the inside structure reaching more than 10 feet in height, its top edge heavily covered in soft corals, sponges, algae and other invertebrates (Aqua Life Divers). The site includes a small artificial reef of roughly 20 Erojacks, originally stacked about 6 feet high in a sandy depression, with several displaced by Hurricane David and later storms (Aqua Life Divers). Florida Scuba Diving says the Erojacks were added in the late 1970s in collaboration with Florida Atlantic University. Palm Beach County's reef list shows "Boca Raton Reef 1" as 19 Erojacks and "Boca Ledge" as a ledge next to an artificial reef (Florida Go Fishing), so the Erojack count differs by one between sources. Depth reports vary widely: 40-70 feet (Florida Scuba Diving), 55-82 feet south of Boca Inlet (a Western Oregon University diver's list from 2006) and "0-30 feet" (Aqua Life Divers, which is inconsistent with the other sources).

## Site Information

- **Location**: South Florida, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 25 meters

## Marine Life

Aqua Life Divers reports angelfish, Bermuda chub, barracuda, lobster and a variety of reef fish. Florida Scuba Diving adds large predators including goliath groupers and hammerhead sharks, nurse sharks, stingrays, moray eels and sea turtles.

## Dive Profile

Reef/drift dive for Open Water divers (Aqua Life Divers; Florida Scuba Diving). Florida Scuba Diving reports visibility around 50-55 feet and light to moderate current.

## Entry and Exit

Boat dive.

## Safety Considerations

- Drift diving in the Gulf Stream calls for an SMB.
- Overhangs and undercuts are not an invitation to enter caves or tight spaces without training.
