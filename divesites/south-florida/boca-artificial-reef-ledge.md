## Boca Artificial Reef Ledge

Boca Artificial Reef Ledge is a reef dive site in South Florida, North America.

## Overview

An outer natural ledge off Boca Raton running parallel to shore, with the highest coral and sponge cover on its west-facing main ledge (10+ feet of relief). Includes a supplemental artificial component of 20 concrete "Erojacks," some displaced by hurricanes. Typically dived as a drift dive; angelfish, chub, barracuda, lobster, and occasional goliath grouper and hammerhead sightings reported.
