## Fowey Rocks

Fowey Rocks is a reef dive site in South Florida, North America.

## Overview

A shallow reef area (also called Fowey Reef/Fowey Light) surrounding the 1876 Fowey Rocks Lighthouse at the northern edge of Biscayne National Park, Miami-Dade County — north of the Florida Keys proper. Includes scattered remains near the wreck of the Arratoon Apcar in 10-20 feet of water. Supports groupers, angelfish, yellowtail, and coral growth; suitable for both diving and snorkeling.
