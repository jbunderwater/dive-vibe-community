## MV Castor

A 258-foot Dutch freighter seized with more than 10,000 pounds of cocaine aboard, now one of Boynton Beach's most reliable year-round Goliath grouper sites.

## Overview

The MV Castor is a 258-foot Dutch freighter (built 1970) that was stopped by the U.S. Coast Guard and seized by U.S. Customs in 1999 after agents found roughly 10,000-10,127 pounds of cocaine hidden aboard. The vessel was later acquired for Palm Beach County's Artificial Reef Program and sunk on December 14, 2001 south of Boynton Inlet. It has since become a dependable year-round Goliath grouper site.

## Site Information

- **Location**: South Florida, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 34 meters

## Vessel History

Built in 1970, the Castor was a 258-foot Dutch freighter intercepted by the U.S. Coast Guard in 1999 carrying an estimated 10,000-10,127 pounds of cocaine hidden in bags of brown sugar. After forfeiture, Palm Beach County acquired the vessel for its Artificial Reef Program and scuttled her off Boynton Beach on December 14, 2001.

## Marine Life

The Castor is known as a year-round Goliath grouper site, with 20-30 fish commonly present and gatherings exceeding 100 individuals during the August-October spawning aggregation. Large groupers (300-600 lbs) are frequently reported holding around the hull and cargo holds, and baitfish schools work the structure.

## Dive Profile

The wreck lies at approximately 34 meters (about 110 feet) maximum depth off Boynton Beach. Divers typically work the hull and open cargo areas where Goliath grouper congregate, with the spawning aggregation season (Aug-Oct) drawing the largest numbers of fish and divers alike.

## Entry and Exit

Boat dive only, run by Boynton Beach and Palm Beach County-area charters. Depth requires Advanced Open Water certification.

## Tips and Recommendations

- The Goliath grouper spawning aggregation (Aug-Oct) is the highlight dive of the year here — expect crowded charters during that window.
- Deep-diver training or experience is recommended given the depth.

## Safety Considerations

- Depth (~34m/110ft) puts this near recreational no-decompression limits; monitor bottom time, gas, and ascent rate closely.
- Currents can be present at this offshore site; carry an SMB and stay with your group.
- Give Goliath grouper space, especially during spawning aggregations when large numbers of fish congregate in close quarters.
