## MV Castor

The MV Castor is a Dutch-built freighter sunk off Boynton Beach on December 14, 2001 as part of Palm Beach County's artificial reef program, known for its goliath grouper.

## Overview

The Castor lies in roughly 80-118 feet of water off Boynton Beach, oriented north-south with the bow facing south (South Florida Diving Headquarters; Scuba Diver Life). Depth reports differ: South Florida Diving Headquarters gives 80-110 feet with the bow at about 80 feet and deck at 95-100; Scuba Diver Life gives a maximum of about 118 feet, structure starting at about 85 feet and deck at about 95; the Palm Beach County Diving Association says 110 feet.

## Site Information

- **Location**: South Florida, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 34 meters

## Marine Life

- **Goliath grouper:** All three dive sources report a goliath grouper community. South Florida Diving Headquarters says about 100 gather during spawning in late July, Scuba Diver Life says "20 or 30 goliaths on the wreck any time of year" and well over 100 in late September, and the Palm Beach County Diving Association says the wreck becomes a spawning aggregation site by mid-August. The spawning-month disagreement is unresolved.
- **Baitfish and predators:** Scad schools, jacks and barracuda (South Florida Diving Headquarters; Palm Beach County Diving Association); baitfish swirls near the bow (Scuba Diver Life).
- **Growth:** Orange cup corals and sponges (South Florida Diving Headquarters; Palm Beach County Diving Association).

## Vessel History

- **Build:** Built in 1970 in the Netherlands as the *Dorothee Bos*; South Florida Diving Headquarters gives the Bodewes yard at Martenshoek, and both it and Scuba Diver Life say the ship passed through several names, including *Mer Star*, before becoming *Castor*.
- **Length:** 258 feet by 37 feet (South Florida Diving Headquarters; Scuba Diver Life; Florida Go Fishing's Palm Beach County reef list gives 258 x 37 x 45). The Palm Beach County Diving Association gives 238 feet, so length is disputed.
- **Seizure:** U.S. Customs seized the vessel in 1999 after cocaine was found aboard; South Florida Diving Headquarters says 10,127 pounds hidden in sugar bags, and Scuba Diver Life says over 10,000 pounds.
- **Sinking:** Deliberately sunk December 14, 2001 as part of the Palm Beach County Artificial Reef Program (South Florida Diving Headquarters; Scuba Diver Life; Palm Beach County Diving Association). The Palm Beach County Diving Association says the midship section where the holds were has since collapsed and lies mostly flat.

## Dive Profile

Boat dive, advanced depth range. The Palm Beach County Diving Association says two more wrecks, the Budweiser Bar and the Captain Tony, lie a short distance from the Castor and can be dived together in the right conditions.

## Entry and Exit

Boat dive only. Wreck interiors require proper training and equipment; the midship has partly collapsed, so entanglement and silt-out hazards are real.

## Safety Considerations

- Depth of 100+ feet limits bottom time and increases narcosis risk.
- Current and boat traffic: use an SMB.
