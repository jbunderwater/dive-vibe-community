## MV Castor

MV Castor is a historic wreck dive in South Florida, North America.

## Overview

A 258-foot Dutch freighter seized by U.S. Customs in 1999 while carrying over 10,000 lbs of cocaine, later sunk December 14, 2001 as part of Palm Beach County's Artificial Reef Program off Boynton Beach. A year-round Goliath grouper aggregation site, with 20-30 goliaths commonly present and 100+ during the August-October spawning season.
