## Tracey

Tracey is a historic wreck dive in South Florida, North America.

## Overview

A 131-foot vessel seized by U.S. Customs for drug trafficking, deployed as an artificial reef off Fort Lauderdale on March 2, 1999. Sits upright, bow pointing north, near the Jay Scutti and Merci Jesus wrecks as part of the "Fort Lauderdale Wreck Trek." Carries a memorial plaque to local dive instructor Ken Vitale.
