## Tracey

The Tracey is a 131-foot former offshore supply vessel deployed as an artificial reef off Fort Lauderdale in 1999 and carries a memorial to dive instructor Ken Vitale.

## Overview

An upright wreck in about 55-72 feet of water off Fort Lauderdale (South Florida Diving Headquarters; Duikersgids and PADI give 21 m / 70 feet). It is part of the Fort Lauderdale wreck trek: the Tracey is connected by chain to the Jay Scutti about 200 feet to the south, with the Merci Jesus to the north (South Florida Diving Headquarters).

## Site Information

- **Location**: South Florida, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 22 meters

## Marine Life

Duikersgids reports barracuda and snapper at the site. South Florida Diving Headquarters mentions "all the marine life" without naming species. PADI's list of sharks, dolphins and whales is a generic regional list and is not used. No other species are attributed to this wreck here.

## Vessel History

- **Type and size:** A supply vessel that serviced offshore oil rigs (Duikersgids; PADI); 131 feet (about 40 m) long with a 32-foot beam (South Florida Diving Headquarters; Duikersgids). Original name and build year were not found in the sources consulted.
- **Seizure and sinking:** Seized by U.S. Customs for drug trafficking, towed to the Miami River where it sat derelict, then sunk as an artificial reef (South Florida Diving Headquarters; Duikersgids). South Florida Diving Headquarters gives March 2, 1999; Duikersgids says March 1999; Sea Experience's list says sunk in 1998, so the year is disputed.
- **Name:** The wreck is also called the Ken Vitale, with a plaque honoring the local dive instructor mounted on the ship (South Florida Diving Headquarters; Duikersgids; PADI).

## Dive Profile

Upright on a sand bottom, bow pointing north (South Florida Diving Headquarters). Sea Experience's list notes easy penetration and use for wreck classes; training is still required for any overhead entry.

## Entry and Exit

Boat dive, about 30 minutes' travel per Duikersgids. Recreational boat traffic passes through the area (Duikersgids).
