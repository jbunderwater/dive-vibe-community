## Hollywood Beach Mermaid Artificial Reef

A 20-module artificial reef about a mile off Hollywood Beach featuring mermaid and diver sculptures as part of the "1000 Mermaids" eco-art reef project.

## Overview

Deployed by the Ocean Rescue Alliance in partnership with the Hollywood Community Redevelopment Agency, this artificial reef was the organization's Hollywood Beach installation of its broader "1000 Mermaids" reef project (launched in 2019). Of the 20 modules originally deployed, 5 are sculptural pieces — mermaid figures, a swim-up "mermaid tail," and an industrial diver-suit sculpture — with the rest built as standard reef structures intended to attract marine growth. A March 2025 survey by the International SeaKeepers Society and University of Florida researchers documented 25 structures at the site; sources do not agree on whether this reflects added modules since the original deployment or a difference in how structures were counted.

## Site Information

- **Location**: South Florida, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 12 meters

## Marine Life

A 2025 biodiversity survey assessed marine growth and colonization across the structures, but detailed species-level findings from that survey have not been published in accessible sources. General reef-fish colonization is expected as the modules mature; no site-specific species list beyond this could be confirmed.

## Dive Profile

The reef sits about a mile offshore in roughly 35-40 feet of water. Divers typically circulate among the 20-25 modules, viewing the sculptural centerpieces (mermaid figures, the mermaid-tail photo feature, and the diver-suit sculpture) alongside the plainer reef-ball style structures.

## Entry and Exit

Boat dive only, run by Hollywood Beach and Fort Lauderdale-area charters.

## Tips and Recommendations

- This is a newer artificial reef, so coral and sponge growth is still developing on the structures — expect a still-maturing habitat rather than dense established growth.
- The sculptural modules make for good underwater photography subjects.

## Safety Considerations

- Depth (~12m/40ft) is well within recreational limits.
- As with any artificial reef with sculptural features, avoid touching or standing on the structures to protect developing coral growth.
