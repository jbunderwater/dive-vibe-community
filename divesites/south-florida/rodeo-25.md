## Rodeo 25

Rodeo 25 is a historic wreck dive in South Florida, North America.

## Overview

A 215-foot Dutch freighter intentionally sunk May 12, 1990 off Pompano Beach to mark the 25th anniversary of the Pompano Beach Fishing Rodeo. Twin masts rise 68 feet from the wheelhouse and are a favored photography subject; the forward mast has since fallen to starboard.

## Site Information

- **Location**: South Florida, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
