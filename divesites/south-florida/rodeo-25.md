## Rodeo 25

Rodeo 25 is a 215-foot Dutch-built freighter sunk off Pompano Beach on May 12, 1990 to mark the 25th Pompano Beach Fishing Rodeo.

## Overview

A deep wreck in roughly 80-126 feet of water (South Florida Diving Headquarters gives 80-126 feet, WreckWiki 122 feet maximum with 32 feet of relief, Aqua Life Divers 90-140 feet). Twin masts rose 68 feet above the wheelhouse; the forward mast has since laid over to starboard in the sand (South Florida Diving Headquarters). WreckWiki says the wreck lists significantly to starboard with signs of collapse in the forward hull, and Aqua Life Divers says Hurricane Irma in September 2017 pushed the wreck onto its starboard side and broke up the main deck, which now rests at about 113 feet over a seafloor of about 125 feet.

## Site Information

- **Location**: South Florida, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 38 meters

## Marine Life

South Florida Diving Headquarters describes bait balls gathering around the mast with jacks and other predators swooping in. WreckWiki says the wreck is decorated with orange cup coral, which it describes as invasive. No other species are named in the sources consulted for this QA pass.

## Vessel History

- **Build:** Launched June 19, 1956 as the Dutch coaster *Nore* (WreckWiki; South Florida Diving Headquarters says built 1956 as *Nore*). Aqua Life Divers also says 1956 Dutch freighter.
- **Later names:** The sources list *Booker Trojan* (1969), a Caricom name, *Cari Gulf Venture* / *Carigulf Venture*, and *Windward Trader* (1986). Spellings and dates differ slightly between sources.
- **Length:** 215 feet (South Florida Diving Headquarters; WreckWiki; Aqua Life Divers); 33-foot beam (South Florida Diving Headquarters).
- **Sinking:** Became a derelict on the Miami River and was sunk as an artificial reef on May 12, 1990 (South Florida Diving Headquarters; WreckWiki; Aqua Life Divers says May 1990), commemorating the Pompano Beach Fishing Rodeo's 25th anniversary. Aqua Life Divers adds that she was loaded with drums of ether seized in drug-lab raids, which the county bomb squad detonated; that detail is single-sourced. Spectator numbers differ (about 100,000 per South Florida Diving Headquarters, tens of thousands per Aqua Life Divers).

## Dive Profile

Anchor dive requiring Advanced Open Water or equivalent experience (South Florida Diving Headquarters; Aqua Life Divers).

## Entry and Exit

Boat dive. The ship was prepped for penetration before sinking (Aqua Life Divers), but after storm damage the structure is shifted and partly collapsed; interior access needs wreck training, redundant gas and lights and should not be treated as routine.

## Safety Considerations

- Depth beyond 100 feet makes this a narcosis and no-decompression-time concern.
- The wreck has been moved and further damaged by storms; do not assume interior spaces are stable.
