## Juno Ledge

A roughly two-mile limestone ledge off Juno Beach best known for its winter lemon shark aggregations and the scenic "Horseshoe" formation.

## Overview

Juno Ledge is a limestone ledge system running for about two miles off Juno Beach in 65-90 feet of water, with 15-20 foot drop-offs and mini-canyons cut into the reef. A coliseum-like formation locally called "the Horseshoe" is considered one of the most scenic sections. The nearby Shark Canyon area sits in 70-85 feet. ## Marine Life

Juno Ledge is best known for large lemon shark aggregations from December through March, with groups of 50 or more reported. Caribbean reef sharks, Goliath grouper, loggerhead turtles, and spotted eagle rays are also regularly reported, and occasional hammerhead and tiger shark sightings have been logged at the site.

## Dive Profile

The ledge runs roughly north-south with 15-20 foot drop-offs, small canyons, and undercuts that shelter sharks and reef fish. The Horseshoe, a coliseum-shaped section of the ledge, is a favored spot to observe the lemon shark aggregation from a respectful distance. Depths across the site range from about 65 to 90 feet, with the adjacent Shark Canyon area running 70-85 feet.

## Entry and Exit

Boat dive only, run by Jupiter and Juno Beach-area charters as a drift or live-boat dive along the ledge, with divers surfacing on an SMB for pickup.

## Tips and Recommendations

- December through March is peak lemon shark season — book in advance, as this is one of the area's highest-demand dives.
- Carry a surface marker buoy; drift conditions are common along the ledge.
- Maintain distance from shark aggregations and avoid chasing or crowding animals.

## Safety Considerations

- This is an advanced dive: depth (up to 90 feet), possible current, and the presence of large shark aggregations all require solid buoyancy control and situational awareness.
- Currents along this stretch of coast can be strong; stay with your buddy and group.
- Surface with an SMB deployed — boat traffic in the area is significant.
