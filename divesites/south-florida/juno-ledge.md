## Juno Ledge

Juno Ledge is a reef dive site in South Florida, North America.

## Overview

A roughly two-mile-long limestone ledge system off Juno Beach with 15-20 foot drop-offs, mini-canyons, and a formation locally called "the Horseshoe," in 65-90 feet of water. Known for large lemon shark aggregations (December-March, groups of 50+), plus spotted eagle rays, moray eels, turtles, and occasional hammerhead and tiger shark sightings.
