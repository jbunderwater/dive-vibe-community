## Juno Ledge

Juno Ledge is a multi-mile limestone ledge off Juno Beach and Jupiter, reached by boat and known for shark encounters.

## Overview

Underwater Journal describes a large reef track spanning several miles between Jupiter Inlet and Juno Beach, with profiles from about 65 feet (20 m) on top down to about 93 feet (25 m) and a double-shelf structure; it gives the ledge height as averaging 6-15 feet. Scuba.com says the ledge begins at about 68 feet and extends about 20 feet down to the sand. A ScubaBoard diver reports the ledge reaches about 130 feet and "continues for miles". Height and depth therefore vary by source. Earlier text calling it a "two-mile-long" ledge with mini-canyons and a "Horseshoe" formation could not be traced to any source and has been removed. The destination bounding box in destinations.json extends to 27.0 N, so the earlier note that this site is outside the bounds was incorrect.

## Site Information

- **Location**: South Florida, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 28 meters

## Marine Life

- **Lemon sharks:** Scuba.com says that from December to March groups of 50 or more are not uncommon along Florida's east coast; Underwater Journal says lemon sharks are among the more common species off Jupiter year-round.
- **Other sharks:** A ScubaBoard thread reports numerous bull sharks, sandbar and dusky sharks and a hammerhead on one dive, plus Caribbean reef and nurse sharks. Underwater Journal and Scuba.com also list bull, tiger and hammerhead sharks regionally.
- **Other:** Goliath grouper and loggerhead turtles (Scuba.com; Underwater Journal also lists green and hawksbill turtles), spadefish, moray eels and soft corals (Scuba.com).
- Earlier text listing spotted eagle rays could not be traced to a source and has been removed.

## Dive Profile

Boat dive; a ScubaBoard poster recommends asking operators for two drops on Juno Ledge and rates it well above the nearby Shark Canyon. Scuba.com also mentions a nearby ledge, Lee's Ledge (60-85 ft). A ScubaBoard report cites visibility of about 100 feet and water around 78 F on one day.

## Entry and Exit

Boat dive only.

## Safety Considerations

- Depths of 65-93 feet or more put this at advanced level; carry an SMB.
- A ScubaBoard poster notes bull sharks became more active around safety stops and ascents; keep calm and stay with the group.
