## Radio Tower Pyramids

Radio Tower Pyramids is a reef dive site in South Florida, North America.

## Overview

Nineteen steel-and-concrete pyramid structures built from decommissioned Radio Mambi broadcast tower sections, deployed off Miami Beach in 1988 by the Miami-Dade Department of Environmental Resources Management. Each pyramid rises about 20 feet from the seabed; frequently combined with the nearby Miss Patricia/Miss Karline wrecks and Rock Pile artificial reef on charter itineraries.

- **Location**: South Florida, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
