## Radio Tower Pyramids

The Radio Tower Pyramids are artificial reef structures built from Radio Mambi broadcast tower sections and sunk off Miami Beach in 1988.

## Overview

DiveSpots says nineteen steel-and-concrete pyramids made from sections of Radio Mambi radio towers were sunk in 1988, designed by Ben Mostkoff, then director of Miami-Dade's artificial reef program at DERM; the site is also called Ben's Antenna Reef. The pyramids rest in about 50 feet of water and each rises about 20 feet from the bottom (DiveSpots). Diver's Paradise also counts nineteen pyramids at 50 feet. The county reef list on Florida Go Fishing has two related entries: "Bens Antenna Reef: 15 Radio Tower Pyramids" and "Radio Towers: 19 20' Radio Tower sections fabricated in Pyramid configuration", so the count is 15 or 19 depending on the entry. Charters often combine it with the Miss Patricia/Miss Karline wrecks and the Rock Pile reef as a wreck trek (DiveSpots).

## Site Information

- **Location**: South Florida, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 15 meters

## Marine Life

Diver's Paradise says the site provides habitat for juvenile fish and occasional barracuda and attracts large schools of fish all day and night; DiveSpots says numerous fish inhabit the pyramids day and night. Neither names other species.

## Dive Profile

Diver's Paradise describes it as not a great photography spot but an excellent site for navigation training, and notes that compass readings can be affected near steel structures.

## Entry and Exit

Boat dive.
