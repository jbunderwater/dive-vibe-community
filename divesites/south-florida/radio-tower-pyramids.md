## Radio Tower Pyramids

Nineteen steel-and-concrete pyramids built from a decommissioned Miami radio station's broadcast towers, doubling as a fish nursery and a compass-navigation training site.

## Overview

Radio Tower Pyramids — also known as "Ben's Antenna Reef" after DERM Artificial Reef Program director Ben Mastkoff, who designed it — consists of 19 steel-and-concrete pyramid structures built from decommissioned Radio Mambi broadcast tower sections and deployed off Miami Beach in 1988. Each pyramid rises roughly 20 feet from a seabed at about 45-50 feet of water. The site is often combined with the nearby Miss Patricia/Miss Karline wrecks and Rock Pile artificial reef on charter itineraries.

## Site Information

- **Location**: South Florida, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 15 meters

## Marine Life

The pyramids function as an important nursery habitat for juvenile fish, and great barracuda are commonly reported hovering near the tops of the structures alongside numerous tropical reef fish, both by day and at night.

## Dive Profile

Depth across the site runs roughly 45-50 feet, with each of the 19 pyramids rising about 20 feet from the bottom. An interesting quirk: the antenna material embedded in the structures can interfere with dive compasses, which makes the site a popular spot for navigation training among local instructors.

## Entry and Exit

Boat dive only, typically run by Miami Beach-area charters, often combined with the nearby Miss Patricia/Miss Karline wrecks and Rock Pile reef as part of a Wreck Trek-style itinerary.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Be aware that compass readings can be affected near the structures — rely on natural navigation or your dive computer's other features as a backup.
- Ask your operator about pairing this site with the nearby Miss Patricia/Miss Karline wrecks for a two-tank trip.

## Safety Considerations

- Depth (up to 15m/50ft) is well within recreational limits for certified divers.
- The steel pyramid structures can have sharp edges where corroded; avoid contact.
