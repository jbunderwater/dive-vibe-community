## Mercedes I

Mercedes I is a historic wreck dive in South Florida, North America.

## Overview

A 194-foot freighter built in Hamburg in 1952 (originally named Jacob Rusch). On November 23, 1984 she broke her mooring during a storm and grounded on a Palm Beach seawall — the well-known "Mollie Wilmot" incident; Broward County purchased and sank her as an artificial reef off Fort Lauderdale in March 1985. Now twisted into two pieces with large openings; penetration carries entanglement risk and requires appropriate training. Notable for black grouper, hogfish, glassy sweepers, and large baitfish schools.
