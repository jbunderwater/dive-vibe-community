## Sea Emperor (Aqua Zoo)

Sea Emperor (Aqua Zoo) is a historic wreck dive in South Florida, North America.

## Overview

An overturned hopper barge off Boca Raton carrying roughly 1,600 tons of drainage culverts, deployed as part of a legal settlement after a dredging company damaged local reef habitat. The stacked culvert pipes create swim-through structure nicknamed the "Aqua Zoo."

## Site Information

- **Location**: South Florida, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 21 meters
