## Jupiter Wreck Trek (Zion Train / Miss Jenny / Esso Bonaire)

Jupiter Wreck Trek (Zion Train / Miss Jenny / Esso Bonaire) is a historic wreck dive in South Florida, North America.

## Overview

A three-wreck drift site off Jupiter comprising the Zion Train (164 ft, scuttled 2003, since broken up by hurricanes), the inverted barge Miss Jenny, and the 146-foot freighter Esso Bonaire — the oldest and largest of the three, sitting upright with open cargo holds. The site is a major Goliath grouper spawning aggregation site (August-September, 40-60+ individuals) and hosts lemon sharks, particularly January-February. The Esso Bonaire's cabin area can be entered but requires proper wreck-diving training; divers should not descend into her lower compartments.
