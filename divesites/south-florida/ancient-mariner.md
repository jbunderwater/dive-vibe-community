## Ancient Mariner

The Ancient Mariner is the former Coast Guard cutter Nemesis, sunk off Deerfield Beach on June 9, 1991 as an artificial reef.

## Overview

A 165-foot cutter sitting upright with a slight list to port in about 70 feet of water, with the bow pointing southeast and the aft deck collapsed (South Florida Diving Headquarters; Aqua Life Divers; WreckWiki gives 70 feet maximum). The wheelhouse is a prominent feature. The Berry Patch Tug lies a short swim off the bow.

## Site Information

- **Location**: South Florida, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 21 meters

## Marine Life

South Florida Diving Headquarters reports that a large goliath grouper guards the wreck and is usually found in the wheelhouse, and that the wreck is heavily colonized with fish moving between it and the nearby reef. Aqua Life Divers also says the wreck is covered with sea life. No other species are named by the sources consulted.

## Vessel History

- **Cutter:** U.S. Coast Guard cutter *Nemesis* (WPC-111), a 165-foot "B" class cutter, launched July 7, 1934 by Marietta Manufacturing Co., Point Pleasant, West Virginia (South Florida Diving Headquarters; WreckWiki). Commissioned in October 1934 (WreckWiki). A Florida Go Fishing reef list gives the build year as 1933, so build year differs by source.
- **Service:** Coast Guard rescue and anti-submarine/convoy escort duty in World War II (South Florida Diving Headquarters; Aqua Life Divers; WreckWiki). Decommissioned 1964 (South Florida Diving Headquarters; Aqua Life Divers).
- **Restaurant:** Converted to a floating restaurant. WreckWiki says it operated on the Fort Lauderdale river from 1979 until a 1986 hepatitis A outbreak closed it, while Aqua Life Divers says the conversion was in 1981; the dates differ.
- **Sinking:** Donated to Broward County's artificial reef program and sunk June 1991 (South Florida Diving Headquarters), June 9, 1991 (Aqua Life Divers; WreckWiki).

## Dive Profile

The aft deck has collapsed and the best access is forward (South Florida Diving Headquarters; Aqua Life Divers). Aqua Life Divers describes the swim off the bow along 150 degrees for about 150 feet to the Berry Patch Tug.

## Entry and Exit

Boat dive. WreckWiki lists the entry skill level as basic Scuba Diver; Aqua Life Divers lists Open Water. Any penetration requires wreck training and equipment regardless of how open the structure appears.
