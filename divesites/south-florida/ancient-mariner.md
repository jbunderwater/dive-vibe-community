## Ancient Mariner

Ancient Mariner is a historic wreck dive in South Florida, North America.

## Overview

A former U.S. Coast Guard cutter (commissioned 1934, served WWII rescue and anti-submarine duty, later operated as a restaurant vessel) sunk as an artificial reef off Deerfield Beach on June 9, 1991. Sits upright with a slight list to port; a resident large Goliath grouper is commonly found in the wheelhouse. Located near the Berry Patch Tug.
