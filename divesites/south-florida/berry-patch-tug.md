## Berry Patch Tug

Berry Patch Tug is a historic wreck dive in South Florida, North America.

## Overview

A 62-foot tug (built 1940 as the A.L. Spencer, used to tow sand barges from the Bahamas) sunk August 15, 1987 as an artificial reef. Sits upright, settled into the sand with its bow facing south, reachable on the same dive as the nearby Ancient Mariner in the right current.

- **Location**: South Florida, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
