## Madison Blue Spring State Park

Madison Blue Spring State Park is a cave dive site in Florida Springs, North America.

## Overview

A state park spring on the Withlacoochee River with a basin (roughly 82 ft wide, 25 ft deep) feeding into a large, well-documented cave system popular with both cavern-level training divers and full-cave-certified divers. The shallow/entrance zone runs about 60-70 feet, while the larger, deeper portion of the system drops below 100 feet and reaches roughly 145 feet in its farthest surveyed sections — nearly 8 miles of surveyed passage in total per one source.
