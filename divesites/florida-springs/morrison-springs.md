## Morrison Springs

Morrison Springs is a cave dive site in Florida Springs, North America.

## Overview

Morrison Springs sits in Morrison Springs County Park in Ponce de Leon, Walton County, in the Florida Panhandle. The open basin bottoms out at around 20 feet (6 meters) and holds a platform anchored near the bottom that divers commonly use for skills practice. A funnel-shaped opening in the middle of the basin gives access to an underwater cave that bottoms out between 45 and 50 feet (13.5 and 15 meters); it is not nearly as extensive as the system at nearby Vortex Spring, and only divers with cave diving experience should enter it. Many divers also work the shallow edges of the basin, where fish and other creatures shelter among the roots of the surrounding cypress trees — spotted and largemouth bass, bluegill, catfish, carp, mullet and sunfish are all present.

Visibility is greatly influenced by the nearby Choctawhatchee River: when the river is running high (above 5 feet or 1.5 meters), visibility can drop quickly.

## Access

The county park has restroom facilities, covered picnic tables, and boardwalks leading to the spring. The main diver access is the sandy beach immediately adjacent to the parking lot; divers can enter there or walk the boardwalk and stride jump off the floating dock that extends farther out into the water. A boat ramp just to the south is used by fishing boats, which have the right to fish in the area — divers should fly diver-down flags and surface with caution. There is ample parking at the park, at 874 Morrison Springs Road, Ponce De Leon, Florida 32455. Approximate drive times: 84 minutes from Pensacola, 67 minutes from Destin, 67 minutes from Panama City.
