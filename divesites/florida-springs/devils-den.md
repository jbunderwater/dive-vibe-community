## Devil's Den

Devil's Den is a cave dive site in Florida Springs, North America.

## Overview

A collapsed sinkhole (karst window) near Williston with a small surface opening leading into a large, partly water-filled limestone cavern described as an "inverted mushroom" shape. Operated as a private diving and training facility since the early 1990s; entry is via stairs descending into the sinkhole rather than a typical beach or dock entry. The site was the location of a significant 1960 excavation (96 dives) that recovered Pleistocene-era fossils of at least 47 mammal species — including mastodon, ground sloth, dire wolf, and saber-toothed cat — plus human remains and artifacts from a chamber roughly 70 feet down; the fossils are now held at the University of Florida's Natural History Museum. Water is a constant 72°F (22°C).
