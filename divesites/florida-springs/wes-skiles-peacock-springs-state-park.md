## Wes Skiles Peacock Springs State Park

Wes Skiles Peacock Springs State Park is a cave dive site in Florida Springs, North America.

## Overview

A state park (renamed around 2010 for diver/filmmaker/explorer Wes Skiles) protecting an interconnected system of karst windows — Peacock I, II, III, and Orange Grove Sink — that together form one of the longest surveyed underwater cave systems in the continental United States. Peacock I is the most accessible entrance; Peacock III is a mostly low, silty syphon cave except for a notably deep chamber; Orange Grove Sink has a large open terminal basin with the cavern zone beginning on its downstream side. Total mapped passage length is cited inconsistently across sources (roughly 33,000 feet per one source vs. over 38,000 feet per Wikipedia). Full cave penetration is restricted to full-cave-certified divers; Florida State Parks manages permitted access in cooperation with cave-diving organizations.
