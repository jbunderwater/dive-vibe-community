## Vortex Spring

Vortex Spring is a cave dive site in Florida Springs, North America.

## Overview

A large spring basin in Holmes County (Panhandle, outside the core north-central Florida springs belt) with training platforms in shallow water (~20 ft) and a cavern/cave entrance descending to a barred security gate at about 115 feet — the cave is physically continuous to roughly 310 feet but barricaded there for diver safety. Water is a constant 68°F (20°C) with no thermocline. The site has a documented history of diving fatalities in its cave system since the early 1990s, reinforcing that only full-cave-certified divers with appropriate training should attempt any penetration beyond the cavern zone.
