## Troy Spring State Park

Troy Spring State Park is a cave dive site in Florida Springs, North America.

## Overview

A first-magnitude spring near Branford maintaining a constant ~72°F, home to the wreck of the Confederate sidewheel steamboat Madison, scuttled in September 1863 by owner/commander Capt. James Felix Tucker to prevent Union capture. IMPORTANT ACCESS NOTE: unlike most sites in this region, Florida State Parks permits open-water scuba diving ONLY here — cave entry and dive lights are explicitly prohibited, and solo diving is not allowed. Diving is restricted to the open-water portion of the spring, down to about 70 feet.
