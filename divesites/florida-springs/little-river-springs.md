## Little River Springs

Little River Springs is a cave dive site in Florida Springs, North America.

## Overview

A Suwannee County park spring near O'Brien with an underwater cave system extending more than 1,200 feet, popular with certified cave divers internationally. The cavern zone near the entrance sits in shallower water, while the connected cave passages drop through a distinctive steep "chimney" formation to around 100 feet. Full cave penetration requires cave certification and appropriate technical equipment.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Springs, North America
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Cave/cavern
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 20 meters
