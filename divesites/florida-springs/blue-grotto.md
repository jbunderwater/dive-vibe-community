## Blue Grotto

Blue Grotto is a cave dive site in Florida Springs, North America.

## Overview

A spring-fed sinkhole near Williston with a wide, open surface basin leading to a large underwater cavern chamber. Visibility commonly runs near 100 feet (30 m) and water stays a constant ~72°F (22°C). Its accessibility and clarity make it widely used for open-water and cavern training dives.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Springs, North America
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Cave/cavern
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters
