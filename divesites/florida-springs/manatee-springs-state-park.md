## Manatee Springs State Park

Manatee Springs State Park is a cave dive site in Florida Springs, North America.

## Overview

Florida's first state park (designated January 1968), a first-magnitude spring feeding the Suwannee River via the longest spring run on that river. The site has a long history of documented Indigenous (Timucuan) use and was visited and named by naturalist William Bartram in 1774. Multiple connected karst cave openings exist here — Headspring, Catfish Hotel, Sue Sink, and Friedman's Sink — along a fracture zone; cave diving is permitted for certified cave divers with depth and lighting restrictions, developed in cooperation with the Florida Skin Divers Association and the National Association for Cave Diving. The main spring run is closed to swimmers in winter to protect manatees that shelter there (manatees are seasonally present, not guaranteed on any given dive).
