## Ginnie Springs (Devil's Spring System)

Ginnie Springs (Devil's Spring System) is a cave dive site in Florida Springs, North America.

## Overview

A first-magnitude spring complex on the Santa Fe River near High Springs, privately operated as a day-use recreation site since 1976 (Wray family ownership since 1971). It is one of the most-dived cavern and cave systems in the United States, anchored by the connected Devil's Eye and Devil's Ear vents, which access an extensive tunnel network (roughly 30,000 feet of mapped conduits); the nearby Little Devil vent is a narrow, deep fissure. An iron grate has been installed over a hazardous section of the "Ginnie Ballroom" cavern following prior diving fatalities. The cavern zone is accessible to cavern-certified divers; the connected cave system beyond the daylight zone requires full cave certification. Water stays a constant ~72°F (22°C) with very high visibility.
