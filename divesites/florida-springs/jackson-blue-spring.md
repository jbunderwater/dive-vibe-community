## Jackson Blue Spring

Jackson Blue Spring is a cave dive site in Florida Springs, North America.

## Overview

A spring in Jackson County (Panhandle, outside the core north-central Florida springs belt) that feeds Merritt's Mill Pond, a 202-acre, roughly 4.5-mile-long reservoir near Marianna. Full cave certification is required before diving the connected cave system here; the spring/pond is closed to divers during summer months to allow recreational swimmer access. The vent opening itself is comparatively shallow (~16.5 ft).

## Site Information

- **Location**: Florida Springs, North America
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Cave/cavern
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 5 meters
