## Pleasant Island Reef

A sand and gravel bar in Icy Strait near Pleasant Island — a tidal/navigational feature, not a documented dive site.

## Overview

Pleasant Island Reef is documented in the Alaska gazetteer (GNIS-derived) as a "sand and gravel bar," located 0.6 miles off the south coast of Pleasant Island in Icy Strait, about 45 miles west of Juneau. This is a navigational/tidal feature rather than a described dive destination. It does not appear on DiveAlaska's local dive site listing (which covers only Prince William Sound and Resurrection Bay-area sites) or the Southeast Alaska scuba directory maintained by akseatrails.org (which lists Ann Coleman Wall, Sunshine Cove, Magic Island, and Mountain Point — not this site).
