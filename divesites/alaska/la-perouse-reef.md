## La Perouse Reef

A reef named in the tradition of French-expedition place names common on this coast, near the southwest edge of the Langara Island area.

## Overview

La Perouse Reef sits at the southwest edge of the Langara Island cluster. The name is consistent with other French-expedition place names in the immediate region (e.g. Mount La Pérouse on Graham Island), which commemorate the 1786 survey by Jean-François de Galaup, comte de Lapérouse. No source independently confirms or describes this specific reef, and none reference diving here.

**Geographic note:** This site's coordinates place it near Langara Island and northwest Graham Island, part of Haida Gwaii, British Columbia — not the Ketchikan area of Southeast Alaska as earlier text on this page stated. The Langara Island area is internationally known for salmon and halibut sport fishing (Langara Fishing Adventures, West Coast Fishing Club), not scuba diving.
