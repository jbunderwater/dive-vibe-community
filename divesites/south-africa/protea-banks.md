# Protea Banks

An offshore fossilised sandbank south of Durban, dived as an advanced drift dive and known for shark encounters.

## Overview

DAN South Africa places Protea Banks about 7.5 km out to sea from Shelly Beach on KwaZulu-Natal's south coast, with the reef lying at 27 to 40 m, about 6 km long and 800 m wide. Dive Worldwide gives a similar picture (about 7 km offshore, 30 to 40 m). DAN SA describes it as a fossilised sandbank rising from about 60 m. Sources agree that dives are generally drift dives in strong current, and that divers should be qualified as advanced.

Mozambique Experience divides the site into Northern Pinnacles (caves, overhangs and ledges) and Southern Pinnacles (high-energy drift), and describes a surf launch from the beach. DAN SA reports visibility ranging from 5 to 25 m within a single week.

## Site Information

- **Location**: South Africa, Africa
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Pinnacle/seamount
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters

## Marine Life

Sharks reported at Protea Banks by DAN SA are scalloped and great hammerheads, ragged-tooth (sand tiger), tiger, Zambezi (bull), oceanic blacktip and dusky sharks, plus giant guitarfish. Dive Worldwide and Mozambique Experience list overlapping species. Seasons conflict: DAN SA says schooling hammerheads visit October to March, while Mozambique Experience gives November to May for hammerheads and tigers. Both sources put ragged-tooth shark aggregations in the winter months (about June to November). No other species are named here because no site-specific source confirmed them.

## Safety

Because all dives are in the deep range with strong current, sources recommend advanced certification, a surface marker buoy and reef hook. Water temperature of 19 to 26 C is reported by Mozambique Experience only.
