## Caves Sodwana

Caves and Overhangs is a reef site on Two Mile Reef at Sodwana Bay with ledges and swim-throughs.

## Overview

X-Ray Mag lists Caves and Overhangs among the named sites on Two Mile Reef. Dive Planner Pro gives a maximum depth of 15 m and describes substantial coral and rock overhangs, with table and brain corals on the surrounding reef, and rates it beginner-friendly. Because the structure is overhangs and swim-throughs on a reef, it is classified as a reef rather than a cave dive; it is not an overhead-environment cave dive.

## Site Information

- **Location**: South Africa, Africa
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 15 meters

## Marine Life

Dive Planner Pro reports lobsters, pineapplefish, soldierfish, squirrelfish and moray eels at the overhangs. Earlier mentions of knifejaws and coelacanth were removed; X-Ray Mag places the coelacanth in Jesser Canyon at about 104 m, not at this site.
