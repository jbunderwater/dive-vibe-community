## Caves Sodwana

This entry corresponds to "Caves & Overhangs," a well-known swim-through site on Two Mile Reef at Sodwana Bay.

## Overview

Caves & Overhangs sits on Two Mile Reef within iSimangaliso Wetland Park, a UNESCO World Heritage Site on South Africa's subtropical KwaZulu-Natal coast. Operator listings describe a cave large enough to swim through, surrounded by overhangs and ledges. Reported depth varies by source — Coral Divers cites 10-16m for the "Caves & Overhangs" area, while other Sodwana operators list similarly-named overhang features up to about 22m — so treat 10-22m as the realistic working range rather than a single fixed figure. Sandy patches near the caves are noted by local guides for turning up shark's teeth.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Sodwana Bay, KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa (Two Mile Reef)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Cave/cavern
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 18 meters

## Marine Life

Coral Divers reports rays and turtles in the Caves & Overhangs area of Two Mile Reef, and colourful coral-associated fish typical of plate and staghorn coral habitat. Regional Sodwana species such as coelacanth are sometimes mentioned by operators for deeper adjacent canyons well beyond recreational cave depths — no source confirms a coelacanth sighting at this specific cave, so that claim has been removed rather than repeated as fact.
