## Raggie Cave Aliwal

Raggie Cave is a cave and overhang dive on Aliwal Shoal off Umkomaas, KwaZulu-Natal, known for ragged-tooth sharks in winter.

## Overview

Depth figures vary by source: PADI lists 16 m, Argophilia 14 to 18 m, Dive Planner Pro 18 m, and one operator page up to 19 m. Sources describe caves, gullies, swim-throughs and overhangs with hard coral. Argophilia says ragged-tooth sharks arrive by early July and leave by the end of September; Dive Planner Pro gives June to November. PADI lists Raggies Cave among the shoal's named sites.

## Site Information

- **Location**: South Africa, Africa
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Cave/cavern
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 18 meters

## Marine Life

Ragged-tooth sharks (Carcharias taurus) are reported at this site by all sources checked, in winter. Dive Planner Pro also lists pyjama sharks, and an operator page lists false stonefish and scorpionfish at the site. No further species are attributed here.

## Notes

Sources stress a hands-off approach: observe sharks and corals without touching. This page does not state any rule about entering the cave while sharks are present, because no source checked confirmed one.
