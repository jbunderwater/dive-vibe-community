## Overview

This entry's coordinates fall within the Two Mile Reef area of Sodwana Bay, KwaZulu-Natal — a real and well-documented reef system. However, no independent Sodwana Bay dive-site guide checked for this QA pass (Amoray Diving, Pisces Diving, Coral Divers, Sodwana Bay Lodge Scuba Centre, ScubaRank, Divearoo, Divernet) lists a site named "Cathedral" at Sodwana. A site named "Cathedral" is well documented elsewhere in South Africa — a 27m amphitheatre-shaped ragged-tooth shark site at **Aliwal Shoal**, roughly 500 km south of Sodwana Bay (confirmed by PADI, Wikivoyage, and Aliwal Dive Centre; see this destination's [Raggie Cave Aliwal](/divesites/south-africa/raggie-cave-aliwal) entry for that area).

The previous version of this page described "towering coral spires and archways" at Sodwana that could not be traced to any source. Given the naming collision with the genuine Aliwal Shoal site and the total absence of "Cathedral" from every Sodwana-specific source checked, this entry is most likely a data error from an earlier curated gap-fill pass rather than a real, separately-named Sodwana site. It has not been re-validated and no site-specific claims are made below.
