## MV Produce Wreck

The MV Produce is a Norwegian bulk carrier that struck Aliwal Shoal in 1974 and now lies broken into three sections on the reef's north-eastern pinnacle.

## Overview

The MV Produce was a Norwegian bulk carrier laden with a full cargo of molasses when she struck the north-eastern pinnacle of Aliwal Shoal on 11 August 1974. Accounts describe the collision happening in rough conditions; the crew had roughly 30 minutes to abandon ship and were rescued, with no loss of life. Sources disagree on the vessel's length — Wikivoyage's dive guide states 176 metres, while other diving-guide sources put it closer to 168 metres — so her length is reported here as approximately 168-176 metres pending a more authoritative shipping record. Her build date and tonnage could not be confirmed from the sources checked for this QA pass and are omitted rather than guessed; a previously-published figure of 119 metres for her length could not be traced to any source and has been removed as unverified.

She came to rest on her starboard side, broken into three sections — the bow and stern are largely intact, but the midships section has collapsed — at a depth of 30-32 metres. The wreck lies within the Aliwal Shoal Marine Protected Area, and operators do not permit penetration dives.

## Site Information

- **Location**: North-eastern pinnacle, Aliwal Shoal, off Umkomaas, KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 32 meters
- **Length**: Sources report 168-176 meters (unresolved conflict — see Overview)
- **Penetration**: Not permitted by local operators

## Marine Life

Endemic harlequin goldies (Pseudanthias connelli) are reported schooling through the wreckage by Wikivoyage. Tropical reef fish are reported generally around the structure; broader Aliwal Shoal species such as ragged-tooth sharks, oceanic blacktips, and manta rays are known from the shoal as a whole (see the Aliwal Shoal entry) rather than confirmed specifically on the Produce itself in the sources checked.
