## Rocktail Bay

Rocktail Bay is a coral reef diving area in the Maputaland Marine Reserve, part of iSimangaliso Wetland Park, KwaZulu-Natal.

## Overview

JSE Magazine describes the reefs off Rocktail as the only coral reefs in the country within the Maputaland Marine Reserve, and Natural Habitat Adventures says Rocktail's divers have sole access to these sites. X-Ray Mag reports the sites as mostly shallow, generally not deeper than 18 m, with Gogo's Reef the showpiece, featuring collapsed features, gullies, overhangs, crevices and swim-throughs. It describes a RIB launch from a shallow reef forming a natural harbour near the beach. Only X-Ray Mag gave depth and topography, so those details are single-sourced.

## Marine Life

X-Ray Mag reports potato groupers, honeycomb moray eels and hawksbill turtles among the reef's residents, along with several shark and ray species. Its season notes mention loggerhead and leatherback turtle nesting in summer and humpback whale passage in winter, which are area observations rather than reef-site sightings. No other species are named here.
