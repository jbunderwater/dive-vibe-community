# Two Mile Reef Sodwana

The largest reef system at Sodwana Bay in iSimangaliso Wetland Park, KwaZulu-Natal.

## Overview

X-Ray Mag describes Two Mile Reef as Sodwana's largest system, about 1.8 km long and 900 m wide, with depths from 8 to 35 m, gullies, overhangs and swim-throughs, and suitable for Open Water divers. Wikipedia calls it the most popular area, with depths from 9 m to over 30 m at the outer edge. Scubaversity gives a typical depth of 10 to 18 m, so depth reports conflict depending on which part of the reef is meant. Named sites within it include Wayne's World, Coral Gardens, Simon's Cave, Pinnacles, Anton's and Bikini (see the separate Pinnacles and Caves pages).

The sources describe boats launching from the beach through the surf from Jesser Point.

## Marine Life

The destination's regional fauna applies, but no site-specific sighting reports were found, so no species are named here. The Sodwana area as a whole is reported to have about 1,200 fish species (X-Ray Mag), which is a regional figure, not a Two Mile Reef count.
