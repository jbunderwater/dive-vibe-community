## Manta's City

Manta's City (Mantas City) is a shallow patch-reef site at Providencia, adorned with large coral heads, sponge fields, and abundant reef fish.

## Overview

Two local dive-shop directories independently place this site much shallower than earlier assumed: Felipe Diving Shop lists it at around 9 metres (30 feet) as a coral-head-and-sponge-field site suitable for snorkeling, and The Dive Shop Old Providence gives a 5–12 metre range over circular sandy-bottomed patch reefs, rating it Beginner. The site sits within the UNESCO Seaflower Biosphere Reserve. Whether it reliably offers manta ray sightings — as its name suggests — is not confirmed by any source consulted; published descriptions focus on the reef fish, coral heads, and sponges, without confirmation of regular manta ray encounters at this specific site. Anda Di Wata independently confirms this as an active dive site they visit.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Providencia Island, Caribbean
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 12 meters

## Marine Life

The Dive Shop Old Providence reports star and soft corals, sponges, large stingrays, schooling snappers, surgeonfish, sergeant majors, angelfish, parrotfish, and starfish at the site; Felipe Diving Shop separately confirms large coral heads and sponge growth. Anda Di Wata has documented peacock flounder at Manta's City specifically — an unusually precise species attribution for a bottom-dwelling species confirmed at this individual site. No source confirms manta ray sightings here despite the name.
