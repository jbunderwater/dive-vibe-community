## Manta's City

Manta's City is a shallow circular patch reef at Providencia — named for the large stingrays commonly mistaken for manta rays here, not for confirmed manta sightings.

## Overview

Manta's City (also referenced as "Manta's Place" by Scuba Diving Magazine and some operators) is a shallow site within the UNESCO Seaflower Biosphere Reserve, made up of circular patch reefs over sandy bottoms. The Dive Shop Old Providence, a local operator, explains the site's name directly: large stingrays here are frequently mistaken for manta rays, which is where the name comes from — no source confirms actual manta ray sightings at this site. That same operator gives a shallower depth range of 5-12 metres than earlier estimates; the previous 20-metre figure used for this entry could not be traced to any source and has been corrected. Anda Di Wata confirms this as an active dive site they visit.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Providencia Island, Caribbean
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 12 meters

## Marine Life

Large stony coral formations shelter schools of snapper, sergeant majors, butterflyfish, goatfish, surgeonfish, angelfish, and parrotfish, with sea turtles and large stingrays reported at the site. Anda Di Wata has documented peacock flounder at Manta's City specifically via a dated photo caption — a single-source but site-specific sighting, not a regional species list.
