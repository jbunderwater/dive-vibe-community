## El Planchon

El Planchon is Providencia's documented WWII-era wreck, its inverted, coral-encrusted hull resting in shallow water and serving as an artificial reef for reef sharks, groupers, and schooling fish.

## Overview

El Planchon lies within the coral reef system surrounding Providencia. Multiple independent sources -- a local dive shop, an English-language dive travel article, and Spanish-language sources -- consistently describe it as a WWII-era vessel that resupplied German U-boats during the war, though one source characterizes it simply as a "German tanker" rather than a Colombian ship in German service; this discrepancy in the vessel's origin is unresolved. According to one account, the crew scuttled the ship themselves as the end of the war approached, but this detail comes from a single, weakly-sourced account and should be treated as unconfirmed rather than fact.

The wreck's hull lies inverted and is now densely colonised by coral, with its side forming an artificial cliff face that is easy to explore from the outside. Divers can see into the interior through openings in the hull, but entering the wreck is not permitted for safety reasons -- this is not a penetration dive.

**Depth conflict:** Sources report the wreck's depth differently -- Just Gotta Dive and a Spanish-language dive guide both give approximately 15 metres, while The Dive Shop Old Providence gives a deeper range of 18-24 metres. No vessel length or tonnage is stated here: a "El Planchon" listed on PADI with specific dimensions (30m/12t) was checked and found to describe an entirely different wreck at Fuerte Island, Cordoba, roughly 500km away on Colombia's mainland coast -- those figures are not applied to this site to avoid conflating two different vessels that happen to share a common Spanish name ("planchon" is a generic term for a flat-bottomed barge or tanker).

**Note on naming:** This entry was previously listed under the name "Blue Diamond." Re-research found that name refers exclusively to a different, well-documented wreck at San Andres island, roughly 90km from Providencia -- confirmed by PADI, Zentacle, TripAdvisor, and several other independent sources, none of which place it at Providencia. No source could be found describing a wreck named "Blue Diamond" at Providencia itself. El Planchon is the only WWII wreck documented at Providencia across every source consulted, so the entry has been renamed and re-validated accordingly.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Providencia Island, Caribbean
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 15 meters

## Marine Life

Parrotfish, groupers, moray eels, lobsters, and gray reef sharks are reported living in and around the wreck by a local dive operator. Triggerfish and reef sharks are also reported using the wreck as shelter. Basket stars are noted on the soft corals that have colonised the hull. No source-confirmed marine life beyond these reports should be assumed present.
