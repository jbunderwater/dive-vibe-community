## Convento Reef

Convento Reef (El Convento) is a multi-level coral platform dive within the UNESCO Seaflower Biosphere Reserve, listed by two Providencia dive operators as an Intermediate-rated site.

## Overview

Now independently confirmed by two local dive-shop site directories found in a 2026-08-25 research pass, well beyond the single Facebook confirmation from the prior validation. Felipe Diving Shop describes it as a "mound coral formation" reaching 60–140 feet (18–43m), rated multi-level; The Dive Shop Old Providence gives a 16–40 metre range over "a platform with sandy areas and a western wall," rating it Intermediate. The depth and difficulty below have been corrected accordingly from an earlier 15m/Beginner placeholder that had no source backing. The site sits within the protected barrier reef system of the Seaflower Biosphere Reserve. Its existence as a named dive site is also confirmed by a Felipe Diving Facebook video showing diving at "El Convento."

## Site Information

- **Location**: Providencia Island, Caribbean
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 24 meters

## Marine Life

The Dive Shop Old Providence reports lobsters, crabs, pufferfish, angelfish, trumpetfish, jacks, moray eels, reef sharks, and stingrays at the site.
