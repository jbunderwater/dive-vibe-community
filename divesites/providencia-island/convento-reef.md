## Convento Reef

Convento (El Convento) is a platform-to-wall dive within the UNESCO Seaflower Biosphere Reserve, reaching considerably deeper than previously recorded — corrected here from a shallow 15-metre reef to a 40-metre wall dive following a 2026-08-23 re-audit.

## Overview

Convento was originally logged with only a single weak source: a Felipe Diving Facebook video confirming a parrotfish sighting at "El Convento." A 2026-08-23 re-audit located two established Providencia dive-shop site directories that both independently describe the site as a platform-to-wall profile far deeper than the earlier 15-metre estimate. The Dive Shop Old Providence lists Convento at 16–40 metres ("platform with wall," extensive sand flats). Felipe Diving Center — the same operator whose Facebook post originally confirmed the site — lists it in its own site directory at 60–140 feet (18–43 metres), describing "coral mound growth" at a multi-level rating. Both sources agree the site is considerably deeper than a shallow reef and involves a wall/platform structure rather than a simple coral garden, so site type has been corrected from reef to wall and difficulty from Beginner to Advanced to match. The site sits within the protected barrier reef system of the Seaflower Biosphere Reserve.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Providencia Island, Caribbean
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters

## Marine Life

The Dive Shop Old Providence reports lobsters, crabs, gray reef sharks, and stingrays along the platform and wall. A parrotfish was photographed at the site per the original Felipe Diving social post. Coral cover is described as mounded/hard-coral growth by Felipe Diving Center rather than the open reef structure previously assumed.
