## The Pinnacle

The Pinnacle (called "The Peak" by the one source that describes it) is an advanced dive on dramatic submerged pinnacle formations within the UNESCO Seaflower Biosphere Reserve — a site this destination's other researched dive-shop and forum sources do not independently corroborate.

## Overview

SeaCrush describes "The Peak (Providencia)" as known for "dramatic underwater pinnacles, large pelagic species like manta rays, and colorful corals, offering more advanced diving experiences," without giving a specific depth. On re-audit (2026-08-23), the two other sources previously cited for this site were re-checked and do not actually support it: Pelecanus Colombia's full Providencia site list covers only Nick's Place, Felipe's Place, and Stairway to Heaven, and Joe's Scuba Shack's current Providencia list (Blue Hole, Table Rock, Felipe's Place, Stairway to Heaven, NX, Turtle Rock, Tete's Place, Planchon, Snapper Shoal) does not include it either. Neither of the two established Providencia dive-shop site directories checked (Felipe Diving Center, The Dive Shop Old Providence) lists a "Pinnacle" or "Peak" site by any name. With only one confirming source found, this site is left unvalidated rather than marked validated on a single citation. The 35-metre maximum depth in the database is unchanged from the prior estimate but is not independently confirmed.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Providencia Island, Caribbean
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Pinnacle/seamount
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 35 meters

## Marine Life

SeaCrush cites manta rays and large pelagic species as the site's signature encounters, with colorful corals on the pinnacle structure. No independent source corroborates this, and no additional site-specific marine life reports were found.
