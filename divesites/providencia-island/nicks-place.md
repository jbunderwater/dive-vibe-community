## Nick's Place

Nick's Place is a wall dive dropping to 40 metres or more, grouped with nearby sites Spiral and Sponge Valley on the same section of the outer barrier reef — though one source describes a shallower, lower-difficulty reef of the same name.

## Overview

Nick's Place begins at approximately 18 metres before a wall, penetrated by a large crack, drops to 40 metres or more, with stair-like ledges eroded into the rock along the descent. DiveAdvisor lists this site under the combined name "Spiral/Sponge Valley/Nick's Place," treating them as adjoining formations on the same stretch of reef, and Just Gotta Dive (calling it "NX") describes the same crack-and-ledge wall with a cave around 42 metres. The Dive Shop Old Providence independently confirms a platform-with-cliff-wall site reaching 40 metres or deeper, rated Intermediate to Advanced. The site is within the UNESCO Seaflower Biosphere Reserve.

**Source conflict:** Pelecanus Colombia describes a site it calls "Nick's Place" quite differently — a shallow reef of 15-30 metres, low difficulty, with only "sponges and many reef fish" mentioned. This is a substantially different character than the deep wall described by the other two sources. Rather than silently pick one version, both are noted here: the wall/40m/Advanced characterization is used for this entry because it is corroborated by two independent, more detailed sources, but divers should be aware that at least one operator describes a shallower experience under the same name.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Providencia Island, Caribbean
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters

## Marine Life

Large schools of Creole wrasse and parrotfish are reported along the wall drop by Just Gotta Dive. The Dive Shop Old Providence separately reports crabs, shrimp, lobsters, nudibranchs, snappers, groupers, and occasional gray reef sharks at this site. Moray eels occupy the ledges in the shallower sections.
