## Tete's Place

Tete's Place is a very shallow coral shoal known as Providencia's "fishiest" dive site, with dense pillar coral formations and large schools of reef fish at just 7–9 metres depth.

## Overview

Tete's Place is a coralline shoal close to Providencia's shoreline at 6 to 9 metres, featuring a step-like descent over a sandy and rocky bottom. Felipe Diving Shop and The Dive Shop Old Providence both independently confirm the shallow pillar-coral-reef profile and rate it Beginner/"initial dives." The site is notable for its living stands of pillar coral — a species now considered functionally extinct in Florida but thriving here. Waterpixels describes the site as a sheltered spot used for second dives after a morning on the outer reef. The shallow, calm conditions make it equally suitable for snorkelers and entry-level divers. It sits within the UNESCO Seaflower Biosphere Reserve.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Providencia Island, Caribbean
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 9 meters

## Marine Life

Waterpixels reports mixed schools of bluestriped and French grunts, plus snappers, sweetlips, juvenile fish, and puffer fish sheltering here, with nurse sharks also present. The Dive Shop Old Providence separately confirms moray eels, stingrays, rockfish, jacks, puffer fish, angelfish, and pillar corals, and notes occasional bottlenose dolphin sightings. Earlier notes citing squirrelfish, peacock flounder, and yellow-spotted stingray at this specific site could not be re-confirmed against a fetchable source and have been removed. The site is described by local operators as among the "fishiest" dives on Providencia for sheer fish density.
