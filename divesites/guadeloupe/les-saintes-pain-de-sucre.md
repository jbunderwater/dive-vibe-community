## Les Saintes - Pain de Sucre

Pain de Sucre (Sugarloaf) is a reef dive in the sheltered bay beneath the famous sugarloaf-shaped hill at Terre-de-Haut, one of the signature snorkel and dive sites of Les Saintes.

## Overview

The dive takes place beneath the 53-metre volcanic Pain de Sucre hill (basalt columns) on the south side of Terre-de-Haut. The sloping reef descends to around 20 metres, with regional sources describing dives here ranging from roughly 10 to 40 metres depending on where the boat drops. The bay's sheltered position produces calm conditions suitable for intermediate divers. Night dives are offered at this site by Les Saintes operators.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Pain de Sucre Bay, Terre-de-Haut, Îles des Saintes, Guadeloupe
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 20 meters

## Marine Life

**Correction (2026-08-05 re-audit):** the previous version of this page stated that nurse sharks "rest under coral overhangs" and that sea turtles are "abundant and relaxed around divers," both attributed to Down to Scuba and other island sources. A full re-audit this pass — including direct and search-based re-checks of downtoscuba.com, padi.com and several other Pain de Sucre-specific pages — could not re-confirm either claim against a source that ties it to this specific site. Both statements have been **removed** per the marine-life-accuracy and anti-hallucination policy rather than carried forward unverified.

What re-confirmed sources do support: a coral reef with gorgonians, sponges and "hundreds of fish and crustaceans" close to the bay edge (fr.guadeloupe-tourisme.com, en.wikipedia.org "Pain-de-Sucre"), and that sea turtles and reef fish are present in Les Saintes' seagrass beds and snorkeling areas generally (regional sources). No source-confirmed, site-specific species list currently exists for Pain de Sucre beyond this general reef character — regional Saintes fauna (turtles, reef fish, occasional nurse sharks elsewhere in the archipelago) may apply, but should not be presented as confirmed sightings at this exact site until a site-specific source is found.

## Entry and Exit

Boat access from Terre-de-Haut. Les Saintes-based operators serve Pain de Sucre. Ferry from Basse-Terre or Trois-Rivières (30 minutes) to Terre-de-Haut. SMB required on ascent.
