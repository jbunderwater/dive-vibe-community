## Stingray city

Stingray City is a sheltered lagoon dive off Paje and Jambiani known for blue-spotted stingrays.

## Overview

Buccaneer Diving describes the dive as around the main channel of the lagoon, where the chances are high of seeing dozens of blue-spotted stingrays, continuing to the eastern reef and its large coral formations. Scuba Diver Life describes it as a sheltered site suited to beginners. Buccaneer Diving gives a 6-22 m range for its lagoon sites overall.

## Site Information

- **Location**: South East Zanzibar (Paje/Jambiani area), Tanzania
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 10 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Not confirmed for this specific site
- **Current**: Sheltered lagoon (Scuba Diver Life)

## Marine Life

Blue-spotted stingrays are reported by both sources, often by the dozens (Scuba Diver Life). Scuba Diver Life also notes a good chance of seeing seahorses.

## Dive Profile

Main lagoon channel first, then on to the eastern reef (Buccaneer Diving).

## Entry and Exit

Boat dive, within the reef about 15 minutes from Buccaneer Diving's Paje base.

## Safety Considerations

Blue-spotted stingrays have a venomous tail spine; avoid touching them or settling on the sand beside them.
