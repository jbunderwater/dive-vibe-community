## Pawoni Hill

Pawoni Hill is one of three outer-reef Powoni sites off Paje and Jambiani, known for table corals.

## Overview

Buccaneer Diving lists Powoni North, Hill and South together at 12-30 m (9-30 m on its second page) as outer-reef drift dives with table corals. Scuba Diver Life also mentions the Powoni area.

## Site Information

- **Location**: South East Zanzibar (Paje/Jambiani area), Tanzania
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Not confirmed for this specific site
- **Current**: Dived as a drift (Buccaneer Diving)

## Marine Life

Buccaneer Diving reports sweetlips and unicornfish at Powoni Hill, with occasional pelagics in the blue.

## Dive Profile

Outer-reef drift over table corals (Buccaneer Diving).

## Entry and Exit

Boat dive via the barrier-reef gap, 5-20 minutes by boat per Buccaneer Diving.

## Safety Considerations

Drift dive on the outer reef; deploy a surface marker buoy before ascending.
