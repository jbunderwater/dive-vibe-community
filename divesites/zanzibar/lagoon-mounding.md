## Lagoon Mounding

Lagoon Mounding is an inner-lagoon coral reef dive off Paje and Jambiani, known for small critters.

## Overview

Buccaneer Diving describes Lagoon Mounding as a beautiful inner reef with a stunning coral landscape. The operator gives a 6-22 m range for its lagoon sites overall and no figure for this site alone.

## Site Information

- **Location**: South East Zanzibar (Paje/Jambiani area), Tanzania
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 16 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Not confirmed for this specific site
- **Current**: Sheltered inner-reef position; not otherwise confirmed

## Marine Life

Buccaneer Diving lists nudibranchs, leaf fish, snake eels, pipefish, batfish, scorpionfish, puffers and trumpetfish for this site.

## Dive Profile

Not confirmed by available sources beyond an inner-reef coral dive.

## Entry and Exit

Boat dive, within the reef about 15 minutes from Buccaneer Diving's Paje base.

## Safety Considerations

No site-specific hazards were found in available sources.
