## Lagoon Mounding

Lagoon Mounding is an inner-reef site in the protected lagoon behind the barrier reef of South East Zanzibar (the Paje/Jambiani lagoon system), used regularly for night dives.

## Overview

The one confirmed source, Paje-based Buccaneer Diving, lists Lagoon Mounding as an inner reef site in the 6-22 meter range with a varied landscape, commonly used for night dives. The depth has been corrected upward from the previous 12m figure to sit within that reported range. Specific claims from the earlier write-up about titan triggerfish nesting behavior and cuttlefish hunting patterns at this site were not found in the source and have been removed rather than carried forward as fact.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Lagoon, South East Zanzibar (Paje/Jambiani area), Tanzania
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 16 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Not confirmed for this specific site
- **Current**: Very light — protected lagoon position
- **Best Time**: October to April (northeast monsoon, South East Zanzibar)

## Marine Life

Per Buccaneer Diving, divers can expect nudibranchs, leaf fish, snake eels, pipefish, batfish, scorpionfish, puffers, and trumpetfish. This list comes from the one confirmed source and should be treated as representative rather than a guarantee of any single dive's sightings.

## Dive Profile

Not described in detail by available sources beyond "varied landscape." The site is regularly run as a night dive by the source operator.

## Entry and Exit

Boat access from Paje/Jambiani-area operators.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Ask about scheduling a night dive here specifically — it is the one activity the confirmed source calls out for this site
- Look for the macro subjects the source lists (nudibranchs, pipefish, leaf fish) rather than expecting large pelagic encounters

## Safety Considerations

No site-specific hazards beyond standard reef diving precautions were found in available sources. Deploy an SMB for ascent as a general practice.

## Photography

Not confirmed by available sources. Given the macro-oriented species list (nudibranchs, pipefish, leaf fish), a macro lens is a reasonable choice.
