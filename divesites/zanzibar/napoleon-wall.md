## Napoleon Wall

Napoleon Wall is an outer-reef wall off Paje and Jambiani named for a resident Napoleon wrasse.

## Overview

Buccaneer Diving lists Napoleon Wall at 12-35 m as a drift dive with a spectacular drop-off, named for the resident Napoleon wrasse. Its second page describes coral formations with smaller reef life in cracks and overhangs. A DiveSSI listing for a Napoleon Wall at 28-38 m was found, but its location was not confirmed.

## Site Information

- **Location**: South East Zanzibar (Paje/Jambiani area), Tanzania
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 35 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Not confirmed for this specific site
- **Current**: Dived as a drift (Buccaneer Diving)

## Marine Life

Buccaneer Diving reports the resident Napoleon wrasse and various moray eels, with rays occasionally seen.

## Dive Profile

Drop-off with soft and hard corals (Buccaneer Diving).

## Entry and Exit

Boat dive via the barrier-reef gap, 5-20 minutes by boat per Buccaneer Diving.

## Safety Considerations

At up to 35 m, agree a turnaround depth, monitor no-decompression limits and deploy a surface marker buoy.
