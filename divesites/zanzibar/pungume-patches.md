## Pungume Patches

Pungume Patches is a reef dive near Pungume Island in the Kizimkazi/Menai Bay area southwest of Zanzibar Island, dived as a drift site by experienced divers.

## Overview

The one confirmed dive-specific source, Dive Horizons Zanzibar, lists "Pungume Reef" (the closest name match found to this OSM entry) as a mix of coral reefs and open sand patches in the 10-30 meter range, recommended for experienced divers as a drift dive, with rays and seasonal whale sharks reported. Pungume Island itself is well documented by general tourism sources (snorkeling trips, sandbank excursions) as a real, existing location southwest of Zanzibar near Kizimkazi, which supports the site's general location, though those sources are not diving-specific and do not confirm details like depth or fish life. Difficulty has been raised from Intermediate to Advanced to match the "experienced divers, drift diving" characterization in the one dive-specific source found.
