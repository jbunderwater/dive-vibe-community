## Little Wall

Little Wall is a mapped wall site on the reef beside Mnemba Island, off Zanzibar's northeast coast.

## Overview

This site's coordinates place it near Big Wall and the Memba 1 entry on the Mnemba Island reef. Scubafish Zanzibar describes a "Small Wall" at Mnemba, a steep wall at 3-40 m suited to all levels with variable current, but no source used the exact name "Little Wall", so the two may or may not be the same site.
