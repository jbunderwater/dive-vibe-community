## Sau Inn Garden

Sau Inn Garden is a coral garden dive on Zanzibar's southeastern outer reef, named after the nearby Sau Inn beach hotel in the Jambiani area.

## Overview

The one confirmed source, Buccaneer Diving, describes Sau Inn Garden as "a stunningly beautiful garden of assorted corals with amazing colors and a plentitude of micro life and fish," in the 6-20 meter range, with a small deep ledge, and reports Napoleon wrasse, giant kingfish, and occasional barracuda there. This differs from the previous write-up, which named emperor and regal angelfish and titan triggerfish nesting behavior as defining features of this site — those specific claims were not found in the source and have been removed rather than carried forward.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Outer reef, South East Zanzibar (Paje/Jambiani area), Tanzania
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 20 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Not confirmed for this specific site
- **Current**: Buccaneer Diving describes the current here as generally allowing "a leisurely dive over the gardens and alongside the walls"
- **Best Time**: October to April (northeast monsoon, South East Zanzibar)

## Marine Life

Per Buccaneer Diving: assorted colorful corals and abundant micro life, plus Napoleon wrasse, giant kingfish, and occasional barracuda. No other species are confirmed for this site by available sources.

## Dive Profile

The site is described as suitable for a leisurely dive across the coral garden and alongside its walls, with a small deep ledge as a notable feature. It is also used for open water training dives per the source, consistent with a moderate, accessible profile.

## Entry and Exit

Boat access from Paje/Jambiani-area operators, typically 5-20 minutes from the "mlango" reef gap depending on which operator's base is used.

## Tips and Recommendations

- This site doubles as a training-dive location for some operators, which suggests generally forgiving conditions
- Ask about the small deep ledge mentioned by the source if you want to see beyond the main garden

## Safety Considerations

No site-specific hazards beyond standard reef-diving precautions were found in available sources.

## Photography

Not confirmed by available sources. The coral-garden setting suits wide-angle reef photography; the "plentitude of micro life" suggests macro subjects are also available.
