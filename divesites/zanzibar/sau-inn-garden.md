## Sau Inn Garden

Sau Inn Garden is an outer-reef coral garden off Paje and Jambiani with a deeper ledge.

## Overview

Buccaneer Diving lists Sau Inn Garden at 6-20 m (6-25 m on its second dive-site page) as an outer-reef drift dive over a garden of assorted corals with plentiful small life, with a deeper ledge.

## Site Information

- **Location**: South East Zanzibar (Paje/Jambiani area), Tanzania
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 20 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Not confirmed for this specific site
- **Current**: Dived as a drift (Buccaneer Diving)

## Marine Life

Buccaneer Diving reports leaf fish and ribbon eels, and Napoleon wrasse, giant kingfish and barracuda on the ledge.

## Dive Profile

Coral garden with a deeper ledge (Buccaneer Diving).

## Entry and Exit

Boat dive via the barrier-reef gap, 5-20 minutes by boat per Buccaneer Diving.

## Safety Considerations

Drift dive on the outer reef; deploy a surface marker buoy before ascending.
