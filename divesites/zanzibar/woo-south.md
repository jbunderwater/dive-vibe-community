## Woo south

Woo South is an outer-reef drop-off dive off Paje and Jambiani with a wide reported depth range.

## Overview

Buccaneer Diving lists Woo South at 12-75 m, a drop-off whose depth varies by group, with impressive coral formations. The 30 m figure recorded here is a recreational-range reference.

## Site Information

- **Location**: South East Zanzibar (Paje/Jambiani area), Tanzania
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Not confirmed for this specific site
- **Current**: Not confirmed for this specific site

## Marine Life

Buccaneer Diving reports barracuda and giant kingfish at this site.

## Dive Profile

Coral formations on top, then a drop-off whose depth is set by the group (Buccaneer Diving).

## Entry and Exit

Boat dive via the barrier-reef gap.

## Safety Considerations

The reported range extends to 75 m, well beyond recreational limits; agree a turnaround depth with the guide.
