## Cross Over

Cross Over is a drift/reef site between Kri and Koh islands in the Dampier Strait, rated Intermediate to Advanced by local operators for its unpredictable currents.

## Overview

Cross Over sits in the channel between Kri and Koh islands, where currents flowing through the Dampier Strait bring nutrients that support rich coral and fish life. Sources describe a steep slope with strong, unpredictable currents. Water temperature averages 27–29°C.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Dampier Strait, Raja Ampat, West Papua, Indonesia (between Kri and Koh islands)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef with drift characteristics
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced (sources report Intermediate-to-Advanced; currents are unpredictable)
- **Maximum Depth**: Sources report 5–30 meters
- **Current**: Strong and unpredictable
- **Best Time**: October to April

## Marine Life

Sources confirmed at this specific site (sunrise-divers.com, rajaampatbiodiversity.com, papua-diving.com): bumphead parrotfish feeding on the corals, blacktip reef sharks, and — per rajaampatbiodiversity.com — jacks and tuna passing through in the current. No further site-specific species detail was found; regional Dampier Strait fauna (fusiliers, anthias, reef fish) is expected but not independently confirmed for this exact site.

## Entry and Exit

Access is by dive boat, entering upcurrent and drifting with the flow between the two islands. Currents are described as unpredictable, so dive with a guide experienced in this channel's tidal timing. Deploy an SMB before ascending.

## Safety Considerations

Strong, unpredictable currents require drift-diving experience and careful buddy/guide contact. Carry an SMB and be prepared for the boat to pick up some distance from the entry point.
