## Mioskun

Mioskun (also spelled "Mios Kon") is a relaxed, easy-going reef in the eastern Dampier Strait — sources describe it as a "lazy dive" suited to all levels, known for wobbegong sharks and pygmy seahorses rather than pelagic action.

## Overview

Unlike the current-swept pinnacles elsewhere in the eastern Dampier Strait, Mioskun is consistently described as a gentle, accessible site. Water temperature averages 27–29°C.

**MAJOR CORRECTION (2026-08-27 initial validation):** the previous version of this page described Mioskun as an "exposed pinnacle" with a "resident population of grey reef sharks that school above the pinnacle summit... in groups of 15–30 individuals" as its "defining encounter," rated Advanced. **No source found on re-research supports any of this.** Three independent sources (Papua Diving, Raja Ampat Biodiversity Eco Resort, and La Galigo Liveaboard) instead describe Mioskun/Mios Kon as an easy, All-Levels-to-Beginner "lazy dive," with wobbegong sharks, snappers, and pygmy seahorses — not schooling grey reef sharks. The grey-reef-shark content appears to have been fabricated or confused with another site and has been entirely removed. Site type has been corrected from pinnacle to reef, and difficulty from Advanced to Beginner, to match all three sources.

*Note: this database also carries a separate entry, "Myoskon" (different spelling), which has not been independently re-researched — do not assume it describes the same site.*

## Site Information

- **Location**: Eastern Dampier Strait, Raja Ampat, West Papua, Indonesia
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 25 meters
- **Current**: Generally mild
- **Best Time**: October to April

## Marine Life

Sources confirmed at this specific site (papua-diving.com, rajaampatbiodiversity.com): wobbegong sharks, striped snapper, pygmy seahorses, and mantis shrimp.

## Entry and Exit

Boat access from eastern Dampier Strait liveaboards or day-trip operators. Entry via giant stride; conditions are typically manageable for less experienced divers.

## Safety Considerations

Standard reef-diving protocols apply. This is one of the gentler sites in the eastern Dampier Strait per available sources — no source documents strong current or shark-schooling hazards here.
