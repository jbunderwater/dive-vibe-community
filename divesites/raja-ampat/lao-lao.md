## Lao lao

Lao lao (also written Lau Lau) is a current-swept pinnacle/seamount in the Dampier Strait, described by sources as sharing character with Blue Magic — swirling barracuda, jack schools, and a signature aggregation of ribbon sweetlips at the base.

## Overview

Lao lao's slope is covered in clusters of pinnacles and coral bommies that offer some shelter from the current. Sources describe strong currents, swirling barracuda, crowds of jacks, and numerous batfish, with a huge school of brightly coloured ribbon sweetlips holding position against the current at the base of the reef. Manta rays have been documented here in the Dampier Strait. Water temperature averages 27–29°C.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Dampier Strait, Raja Ampat, West Papua, Indonesia
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Pinnacle/seamount
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced — strong currents
- **Maximum Depth**: Sources report a range from 8–25 meters (Raja Ampat Biodiversity Eco Resort) up to 10–36 meters (remoteandafloat.com)
- **Current**: Strong
- **Best Time**: October to April; mantas noted "in season" per one source

## Marine Life

Sources confirmed at this specific site (rajaampatbiodiversity.com, papua-diving.com, remoteandafloat.com): swirling barracuda, jack schools, numerous batfish, oceanic manta rays, snapper, and a signature large school of ribbon sweetlips at the reef base; moray eels (rajaampatbiodiversity.com).

## Entry and Exit

Boat access only. Currents are strong, so entry timing and direction should be set by an experienced guide. Carry an SMB and deploy before ascending.

## Safety Considerations

Rated Advanced due to strong currents typical of Dampier Strait pinnacles. Divers should have current-diving experience. Carry an SMB and, where operators provide them, a reef hook for holding position without disturbing coral.
