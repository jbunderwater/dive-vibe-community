## Tank Rock

Tank Rock is a pinnacle site near Fiabacet in Misool, southern Raja Ampat — a submerged ridge formed by three eastern pinnacles rather than a single reef structure, as this update corrects from the prior "reef" classification.

## Overview

The site consists of three pinnacles rising from deeper water, capped with pristine hard coral growth. Fish density here can be high enough to obscure the coral below on active days. The formation sits within the Fiabacet dive area, one of Misool's better-known reef clusters.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Fiabacet area, Misool, Raja Ampat, West Papua, Indonesia
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Pinnacle/seamount
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters

## Marine Life

Sources confirmed at this specific site (sunrise-divers.com, lagaligoliveaboard.com): large grouper, dense schools of fish, grey reef sharks, Napoleon wrasse, snappers, barracuda, and turtles, over a pristine hard coral base.

## Entry and Exit

Boat access from Misool liveaboards or Misool Eco Resort. Deploy an SMB on ascent; assess current before descending given the pinnacle's exposed position.

## Safety Considerations

Difficulty is classified Advanced in line with Raja Ampat's remote, liveaboard-access diving character and the site's depth and exposed pinnacle position.
