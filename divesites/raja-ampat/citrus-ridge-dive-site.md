## Citrus Ridge Dive Site

Citrus Ridge is a drift dive off the coast of Gam Island in central Raja Ampat, named for the explosion of yellow and orange soft corals that blanket its underwater ridge.

## Overview

The site's terrain is a mix of coral gardens, gentle slopes, and reef outcrops spread along a ridge running roughly 10–27 metres deep. Currents here run moderate to strong along the reef, producing a genuine drift dive rather than a static coral-garden swim. The soft coral coverage — the site's defining feature — is dense enough to give the ridge its citrus-toned color even at a distance.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Off Gam Island, Raja Ampat, West Papua, Indonesia
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Drift dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 27 meters (sources report a 10–27m range)
- **Current**: Moderate to strong; well suited for intermediate-to-advanced drift divers
- **Best Time**: October to April

## Marine Life

Sources confirmed at this specific site (lagaligoliveaboard.com, divernet.com, meridianadventuredive.com) describe dense soft coral, sponge, and reef-outcrop cover typical of a current-swept ridge, but none of the three sources consulted for this update named specific fish or shark species sighted at Citrus Ridge. An earlier version of this page listed sweetlips, whitetip reef sharks, hawksbill turtles, and eagle rays here — those claims could not be traced to a site-specific source and have been removed. Expect Raja Ampat's typical current-fed reef fish community (fusiliers, anthias, surgeonfish) along the ridge, consistent with the site's topography, though this is a general inference rather than a confirmed sighting list.

## Entry and Exit

Boat access from liveaboards or day-trip operators. Divers enter upcurrent of the ridge and drift its length; the guide sets entry timing based on current direction and strength. Deploy an SMB on ascent for boat pickup.

## Safety Considerations

Difficulty is classified Advanced, both for the moderate-to-strong current here and in line with Raja Ampat's remote, liveaboard-access diving character generally. Never surface without an SMB in a current zone; stay close to the guide's briefed drift line.
