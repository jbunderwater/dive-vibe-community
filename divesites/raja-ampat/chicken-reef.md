## Chicken Reef

Chicken Reef sits between Kri and Mansuar islands in the Dampier Strait, its offbeat name commemorating Raja Ampat dive pioneer Max Ammer's dive buddy, who reportedly drew his knife in fright at the sight of the reef's resident sharks.

## Overview

The reef is built around large coral bommies and gorgonian fans on a submerged structure whose top sits around 5 metres below the surface, sloping down to roughly 30 metres. Current runs mild to strong depending on tidal state, and the reef's productivity draws a consistent shark presence. The name's origin story is corroborated independently by two sources describing the same encounter with Max Ammer during the site's exploration.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Between Kri and Mansuar Islands, Dampier Strait, Raja Ampat, West Papua, Indonesia
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters
- **Current**: Mild to strong
- **Best Time**: October to April

## Marine Life

Sources confirmed at this specific site (rajaampatbiodiversity.com, lagaligoliveaboard.com, divernet.com, meridianadventuredive.com): whitetip and blacktip reef sharks patrolling the reef; wobbegong sharks camouflaged against the bommies; barracuda and giant trevally hunting the reef's smaller fish; fusiliers; pipefish; pygmy seahorses; nudibranchs; flatworms; crabs and shrimp; and crocodilefish.

## Entry and Exit

Boat access from Dampier Strait liveaboards or day operators based near Mansuar or Arborek. Entry via giant stride. Deploy an SMB on ascent — the Dampier Strait carries significant boat traffic.

## Safety Considerations

Difficulty is classified Advanced given the reef's mild-to-strong tidal current and Raja Ampat's remote, liveaboard-access diving character generally. Carry an SMB and monitor current conditions with your guide before descending.
