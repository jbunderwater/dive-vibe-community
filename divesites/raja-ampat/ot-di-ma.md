## Ot Di Ma

Ot Di Ma is a current-swept reef site in the central Dampier Strait between Gam and Kri islands. Sources refer to this area interchangeably as "Otdima" or "Ot Di Ma," describing a dynamic, multi-directional current site with dense fish aggregations.

## Overview

This site is described by sources as sitting mid-strait, where tidal currents can arrive from several directions in a single dive, concentrating large schools of fusiliers, snappers, and sweetlips around the reef, with wobbegong sharks sheltering in crevices. Water temperature averages 27–29°C.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Central Dampier Strait, Raja Ampat, West Papua, Indonesia (between Gam and Kri)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced — sources recommend prior current-diving experience
- **Maximum Depth**: Sources report 5–25 meters
- **Current**: Strong, multi-directional and unpredictable
- **Best Time**: October to April

## Marine Life

Sources confirmed at this location (scubaboard.com, rajaampatbiodiversity.com, papua-diving.com): large schools of fusiliers, snappers, and sweetlips; a specific sweetlips aggregation surrounded by pygmy sweeper fish (papua-diving.com); wobbegong sharks in the crevices; turtles.

## Entry and Exit

Boat access from Dampier Strait operators or liveaboards. Currents here are unpredictable in direction, so entry should be timed and directed by an experienced local guide. Deploy an SMB before ascending.

## Safety Considerations

Not a beginner site. Currents can shift direction within a single dive. Prior experience with current diving is recommended. Carry an SMB and stay close to the guide.
