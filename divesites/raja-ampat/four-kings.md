## Four Kings

Four Kings is a cluster of underwater pinnacles halfway between Jef Pele and Wayilbatan islands in Misool, rising from around 40 metres to within 5–7 metres of the surface — one of southern Raja Ampat's signature big-fish and macro combination dives.

## Overview

The pinnacles are blanketed in soft coral, sea fans, sponges, and tunicates, providing shelter for a wide range of critters as well as a magnet for schooling pelagics drawn by the nutrient-rich currents that sweep through the site. **Correction (2026-08-27 re-audit):** earlier versions of this page listed maximum depth as 30m; wallacea-divecruise.com and other operator sources confirm the pinnacles' bases reach approximately 40m, consistent with the site description already given in the text. Depth has been corrected to 40m.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Misool area, Raja Ampat, West Papua, Indonesia
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Pinnacle/seamount
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters (shallowest pinnacle top approximately 5–7 meters)
- **Current**: Can be strong; contributes to the site's productivity

## Marine Life

Sources confirmed at this specific site (wallacea-divecruise.com, birdsheadseascape.com): schools of barracuda numbering in the hundreds, schooling trevally and jacks, scorpionfish, and Napoleon wrasse, alongside dense soft coral, sponge, and sea fan cover on the pinnacle structures.
