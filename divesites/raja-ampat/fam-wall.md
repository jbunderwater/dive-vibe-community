## Fam Wall

Fam Wall — also listed by operators as Keruo Channel or "Dinding Warna Banyak" ("many-colored wall") — is a steep-banked wall/channel dive running between Keruo and Keruwo islands in the Fam group of southern Raja Ampat.

## Overview

Fam Wall drops from a shallow reef crest into a north–south channel roughly 300 metres long between the two Keruo islands. Sources report the maximum depth as between 25 and 30 metres (thescubadirectory.com gives 25m; dive-the-world.com and La Galigo give 30m for the channel/wall). The wall and channel walls are covered in gorgonian sea fans — a well-documented spot to search for pygmy seahorses — along with soft coral that starts yellow and orange in the shallows and darkens with depth. Currents through the channel can run strong, and operators recommend intermediate-to-experienced divers. Water temperature in the region averages 27–29°C.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Fam Islands, Raja Ampat, West Papua, Indonesia
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive (also referred to as Keruo Channel)
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: Sources report 25–30 meters
- **Current**: Can run strong through the channel; intermediate-to-experienced divers recommended
- **Best Time**: October to April

## Marine Life

Sources confirmed at this specific site (lagaligoliveaboard.com, dive-the-world.com): pygmy seahorses on the gorgonian fans, barracuda, blacktip reef sharks, midnight snappers, angelfish, spadefish, hawkfish, jackfish, soldierfish, manta rays, giant trevally, octopus, green turtles, moray eels, wrasse, and schooling yellow-fin fusiliers. thescubadirectory.com additionally lists it as a wall dive to 25m without further species detail.

## Dive Profile

Begin at the reef crest and follow the wall/channel, working the gorgonian fans for pygmy seahorses. The channel runs for roughly 300 metres between the two islands, giving a natural drift line to follow. Currents can be strong, so this is best dived with an experienced guide who knows the tidal timing.

## Entry and Exit

Access by liveaboard tender — the Fam Islands are several hours from Sorong and beyond the range of most day-trip operations. Entry by giant stride or back roll in calm conditions. Exit to the tender with an agreed surface signal, particularly if the current is running.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Ask the guide to point out gorgonian fans known to host pygmy seahorses — they are easy to miss without local knowledge
- Current can be strong through the channel; be current-competent before booking this site
- The Fam Islands receive relatively little dive pressure, so take extra care with buoyancy around the fans

## Safety Considerations

The channel's current and the wall's drop-off both require active depth and position management. In a remote location like the Fam Islands, emergencies require significant evacuation time; plan conservative dive profiles and dive with a guide familiar with the site's tidal behavior.
