## Otdima Reef

Otdima (also written Ot Di Ma) is a current-swept reef/atoll formation in the central Dampier Strait, between the islands of Gam and Kri, known for strong, unpredictable multi-directional currents and dense fish aggregations.

## Overview

Otdima sits mid-strait where currents can arrive from several directions at once depending on tidal state — divers and guides describe it as a genuinely dynamic, current-driven site rather than a calm reef dive. Multiple independent sources (a ScubaBoard trip report, Raja Ampat Biodiversity Eco Resort, and Papua Diving) describe massive schools of fusiliers, snappers, and sweetlips surrounding the reef structure, with wobbegong sharks hiding in the crevices. Water temperature averages 27–29°C.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Central Dampier Strait, Raja Ampat, West Papua, Indonesia (between Gam and Kri)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 25 meters
- **Current**: Strong, multi-directional and unpredictable
- **Best Time**: October to April

## Marine Life

Sources confirmed at this specific site (scubaboard.com, rajaampatbiodiversity.com, papua-diving.com): large schools of fusiliers, snappers, and sweetlips (including a specific group of sweetlips that congregates under a rock, surrounded by pygmy sweeper fish, per papua-diving.com); wobbegong sharks in the crevices; turtles (per rajaampatbiodiversity.com).

## Entry and Exit

Boat access from Dampier Strait operators or liveaboards. Given the site's unpredictable currents, entry timing and direction should be set by an experienced local guide. Deploy an SMB before ascending.

## Safety Considerations

This is not a beginner site. Currents can come from multiple directions within a single dive and shift without warning. Divers should have significant experience with current diving before attempting Otdima. Carry an SMB and stay close to the guide.
