## Mayhem Dive Site

Mayhem is a large submerged pinnacle on the western edge of the Dampier Strait, just offshore from the island of Gam — a current-driven site known for dense schooling fish, wobbegong sharks, pygmy seahorses, and an occasional manta cleaning station.

## Overview

Mayhem delivers its best action when a moderate current is running, bringing large schools of fusiliers, snappers, jacks, and batfish together at the reef edge. The pinnacle's many large bommies lead up toward the shallows, where a cleaning station occasionally attracts manta rays. Water temperature averages 27–29°C.

**Correction (2026-08-27 initial validation):** the previous version of this page described "chevron barracuda stacking in columns" and "grey reef sharks" patrolling the flanks — neither claim could be confirmed by any source found and both have been removed. Confirmed shark sightings here are whitetip reef sharks, not grey reef sharks. Maximum depth has been corrected from 25m to 30m to match sourced range (7–30m).

## Site Information

- **Location**: Western Dampier Strait, off Gam Island, Raja Ampat, West Papua, Indonesia
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Pinnacle/seamount with bommies
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced — best dived with moderate current running
- **Maximum Depth**: Sources report 7–30 meters
- **Current**: Variable; site is most productive with a little current running
- **Best Time**: October to April

## Marine Life

Sources confirmed at this specific site (dive-the-world.com, rajaampatbiodiversity.com, emperordivers.com): large schools of fusiliers, snappers, jacks, and batfish; wobbegong sharks and pygmy seahorses on the bottom; whitetip reef sharks and large grouper; a shallow cleaning station occasionally visited by manta rays; glassfish (rajaampatbiodiversity.com).

## Dive Profile

Drop in along the current side of the pinnacle and watch the schools of fish parade past. As bottom time runs down, work along the large bommies toward the shallows, checking the cleaning station for mantas and looking for wobbegong sharks and large grouper along the way.

## Entry and Exit

Boat access only. If current is running, a direct descent to the pinnacle is important to avoid drifting past it. Carry an SMB and deploy before ascending.

## Safety Considerations

Advanced-rated due to current exposure typical of Dampier Strait pinnacles. Divers should be comfortable managing current and have a reef hook available. Never surface in open water without an SMB deployed.
