## Yatouche

Yatouche is listed in OSM as a wreck dive site at the northern end of the Gulf of Aqaba.

## Overview

Yatouche (also spelled Yatush) appears in OSM data as a wreck dive at approximately 20 metres depth near the northern end of the Gulf of Aqaba. The OSM coordinates (lat 29.504°N, lon 34.920°E) place this site in the vicinity where Jordan, Israel, and Egypt converge at the top of the Gulf — an area where the Eilat (Israel) and Aqaba (Jordan) coastlines are very close together. The wreck known as "Yatush" (Hebrew: יתוש, meaning "mosquito") is documented by Eilat dive operators north of Coral Beach, Eilat, Israel, at a stern depth of roughly 24–26 metres and a bow depth of 30–33 metres — deeper than the 20m figure recorded here. Two independent sources describe the vessel not as an Israeli-built gunboat but as a former U.S. Navy patrol vessel that served off Vietnam and was transferred to Israel after the American withdrawal; it then served with the Israeli Navy before being sunk as an artificial reef (sources disagree on whether this was the mid-1980s or 1994).

No independent dive operator source in Jordan attributes this site to Jordanian waters, and it does not appear in any Jordanian dive site listing reviewed during this or the prior QA pass. The site is most likely in Israeli waters and may have been included in Jordan's OSM data in error due to proximity to the Israel-Jordan maritime boundary. Divers intending to visit this site should confirm its jurisdiction and access arrangements with a local dive operator in the Aqaba/Eilat area before diving.
