## Gorgon 1

Gorgon 1 is named for a gorgonian sea fan (sources disagree on whether there is one or two) that watches over a trio of coral pinnacles — one of Aqaba's most reliable turtle-spotting sites.

## Overview

The site's namesake is a large gorgonian fan coral at around 16 metres near the three pinnacles. Sources genuinely disagree on the count: Arab Divers and an independent diver blog (Freediving Jordan) both describe "the" gorgonian fan coral in the singular, while Jordan Travel describes "two large Gorgonia corals" as the origin of the name. Rather than pick a number, both accounts are noted here. The three pinnacles rise from around 10–18 metres up to the surface, each supporting its own community of marine life and cleaning stations. The central pinnacle has a particularly active cleaning station. Beyond the third pinnacle, the reef flattens to a shelf with a house-sized cabbage coral sheltering numerous reef fish including a resident large yellowmouth moray.

Documented marine life at this specific site includes hawksbill turtles (described as Aqaba's best chance for a reliable turtle encounter), fusiliers, schooling banner fish, big-eye emperors, sergeant majors, glassfish, lionfish, stonefish, moray eels, grouper, and cleaner shrimp. The site is reachable both from shore — through a narrow zig-zag channel (about 3m wide, 4m deep) cut through the fringing reef — and by boat; no source describes boat-only access. Its shallow depth and calm conditions make it suitable for beginners.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Jordan - Aqaba, Middle East
- **Entry Type**: Shore or boat entry
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 18 meters
