## Barracuda Point

This entry's coordinates (-6.2729, 39.5532) place it off Zanzibar's southeast coast, near Paje/Jambiani — not "northern Zanzibar Island" or Nungwi as the previous version of this page claimed. Two independent dive operators (one based in Paje, one based in Matemwe but servicing the whole island) describe a "Barracuda Point" in exactly this area, accessed via the "mlango" (barrier-reef gap) about 15 minutes by boat from the Paje dive base.

## Overview

Both sources describe Barracuda Point as a coral garden with mounds and overhangs sheltering shellfish and reef fish, entered with a drop onto a bed formation and a slope down to about 20 meters — not the 25-meter figure currently on file. Notably, neither source that specifically discusses this site describes schooling-barracuda behavior in detail; the name implies barracuda encounters, but the "spiraling tornado of hundreds of barracuda" narrative in the previous version of this page is not supported by either source and appears to be embellishment. Only two qualifying sources were found, and their site descriptions use near-identical wording, which is a caveat worth flagging rather than treating as fully independent confirmation — a third distinct source was not found despite searching ScubaBoard, Zentacle, and several regional dive guides.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Southeast Zanzibar, near Paje/Jambiani, accessed via an outer-reef "mlango" — correcting the previous "northern Zanzibar Island"/Nungwi framing
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Drift dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 20 meters
- **Current**: Present given the drift-dive framing; not further quantified by sources

## Marine Life

Confirmed by sources: a coral garden with mounds and overhangs sheltering "a variety of shellfish and some of the shyer reef fish." No source names specific fish species or confirms the schooling great-barracuda encounters implied by the site's name, so those claims from the previous version of this page have been removed rather than repeated.

## Dive Profile

Per the two sources found, the dive begins with a drop onto a bed formation on the outer reef and follows a slope to approximately 20 meters, taking in the coral mounds and overhangs along the way.

## Entry and Exit

Boat access from the Paje dive base, roughly 15 minutes to the mlango (reef gap), then out to the site — not from Nungwi as the previous version of this page described.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Confirm with your operator whether the name "Barracuda Point" reflects reliable barracuda sightings or is simply a traditional site name — this was not confirmed either way in this research
- As a drift site accessed through a reef gap, timing with tide/current is likely relevant; confirm with the boat crew

## Safety Considerations

As a drift dive through a reef gap, current awareness and buddy contact are the primary general concerns; no site-specific hazard beyond this was described by sources.

## Photography

Not specifically documented by sources; the coral mounds and overhangs suggest reasonable macro/wide-angle reef photography, but no source describes specific photographic subjects at this site.
