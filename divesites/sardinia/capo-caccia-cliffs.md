## Capo Caccia Cliffs

Capo Caccia Cliffs form the outer underwater wall of the Capo Caccia-Isola Piana Marine Protected Area, the same headland that contains the Nereo Cave system.

## Overview

The underwater cliffs of Capo Caccia drop vertically to around 30 metres in northwestern Sardinia, riddled with caves, cracks, and overhangs carpeted in red coral and encrusting sponges. This is the same headland that harbors the Nereo Cave system, and the exterior walls share its biological richness -- barracuda patrol in open water while moray eels and large groupers occupy the deeper crevices. The Capo Caccia-Isola Piana MPA was established by ministerial decree on 20 September 2002, and the wall's biological richness reflects over two decades of reduced fishing pressure.
