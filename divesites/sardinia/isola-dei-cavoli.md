## Isola dei Cavoli

Isola dei Cavoli is a small granite island in the Capo Carbonara Marine Protected Area, home to the Madonna del Naufrago statue and the natural rock formation known as "La Nave."

## Overview

Isola dei Cavoli is a small granite island within the Capo Carbonara Marine Protected Area, established in 1999, near Villasimius in southeastern Sardinia. Massive granite boulders create fissures, cavities, and eroded formations descending to about 40 metres. The site's best-known feature is the Madonna del Naufrago, a 3.2-metre, roughly 5-tonne pink trachyte statue by sculptor Pinuccio Sciola, placed at 11 metres depth on July 8, 1979 and dedicated to the Madonna di Bonaria, patroness of sailors -- it remains the focus of an annual July commemoration for those lost at sea. "La Nave" is a natural granite rock resembling the bow of a ship (not an actual wreck; no source supports the "Roman" name previously used for it here), with dive routes running from shallow water down to about 40 metres. PADI documents salps, mullet, damselfish, wrasse, groupers, sea bream, and barracuda around La Nave specifically. A red gorgonian colony is also reported at the nearby Sandbank of Mezzo, though that detail could not be independently re-confirmed this pass.
