## Nereo Cave

Nereo Cave is a limestone cave system at Capo Caccia, about 200 metres from Neptune's Grotto, whose reported scale varies dramatically between sources.

## Overview

Nereo Cave is carved into the limestone cliffs of Capo Caccia, roughly 200 metres from Neptune's Grotto, in the far northwest of Sardinia. Sources disagree sharply on its size: Italian-language sources (Italian Wikipedia, SardegnaTurismo) describe a 300-metre main gallery with two to three entrances, extending to about 500 metres of connected tunnels in total. The English-language Wikipedia article, by contrast, cites a considerably larger system of roughly 4 kilometres with around 10 entrances. This listing does not resolve that discrepancy -- divers should expect the smaller, better-documented figures (a few hundred metres of passage) to describe what a typical dive actually covers. Depths inside the system range from the surface to about 33 metres. The walls are draped in dense red coral colonies and yellow Leptopsammia pruvoti cup corals, and several chambers contain air pockets. Lobsters, conger eels, moray eels, and groupers shelter inside. The cave lies within the Capo Caccia-Isola Piana Marine Protected Area, established by ministerial decree on 20 September 2002.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Capo Caccia, Sardinia, Europe
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Cave/cavern
