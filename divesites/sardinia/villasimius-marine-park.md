## Villasimius Marine Park

Villasimius Marine Park is the Capo Carbonara headland and its surrounding waters, protected since 1999 across three regulated zones.

## Overview

The Villasimius Marine Park encompasses the Capo Carbonara headland and surrounding waters in southeastern Sardinia, protected since 1999 and managed across three zones: Zone A is a full reserve with no access, Zone B allows regulated activity, and Zone C is a partial reserve with lighter restrictions. Granite boulders and pinnacles create crevasses and depressions colored by yellow sea daisies (Parazoanthus) and red gorgonians. Dentex and corvina (meagre) are reported moving through submerged "forests" of gorgonians and sponges, large dusky groupers (Epinephelus marginatus) and gilthead sea bream are commonly seen at the Cala Caterina shoal, and barracuda schools are a regular sight at the Variglioni dei Cavoli. The park's shallow inner reef areas suit beginners and snorkelers, while the deeper granite formations and the nearby Isola dei Cavoli attract more experienced divers.
