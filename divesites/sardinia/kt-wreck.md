## KT Wreck

KT Wreck (KT12) is a World War II German military transport wreck lying in two separated sections off Orosei, Sardinia, whose precise dimensions and the exact distance between its two sections are disputed across sources.

## Overview

The KT12 was a KriegsTransporter (armed military cargo vessel) built at the OTO shipyards in Livorno, Italy, and delivered to the German Kriegsmarine on May 19, 1943. Length is most commonly cited as approximately 68 metres with an 11-metre beam (one English-language dive-shop source instead gives 60 metres/225 feet). Displacement is also disputed: dedicated Italian wreck-history sources put it around 700 tonnes, while several dive-shop sources cite 834 tonnes; the attacking submarine's own action report separately estimated the target at roughly 1,200 tonnes. On June 10, 1943, on her maiden voyage carrying fuel drums and vehicles bound for North Africa, she was struck near the bow by a torpedo from the British submarine HMS Safari. The bow separated and sank almost immediately; the burning stern section remained afloat for about 41 minutes before going down, with the action logged at position 40.21N, 09.45E.

Here too sources conflict: dive-shop pages (Protec Sardinia, Four Jandals) describe the two sections lying roughly 300 metres apart, while specialist Italian wreck-history sources (SUB Magazine, Scubaportal) put the separation at 700-800 metres. Both sections now rest on sand at depths of roughly 24 to 35 metres. The stern section sits upright with its deck gun, propeller, and rudder still intact; cargo cranes lie nearby on the seabed, along with truck and generator-cart remains and empty fuel drums scattered along the debris field.

Marine life reported at the wreck by multiple independent site descriptions includes groupers, moray eels, conger eels, scorpionfish, and lobsters (spiny and slipper), with sea bream and occasional amberjack also noted.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Gulf of Orosei, eastern Sardinia
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 34 meters
