## MuMart (museo sommerso)

MuMart (museo sommerso) is Sardinia's underwater art museum at Golfo Aranci, now reachable by snorkel from shore as well as by boat.

## Overview

MuMart (Maritime Museum of Art) is an underwater sculpture gallery at Golfo Aranci, inaugurated on July 26, 2013 with 14 contemporary works by international artists (Angi, Boero, Bricalli, Cardillo, Carloni, Chapalain, Ciccobelli, Cogorno, Kosice, Mustica, Nussbaum, Patterson, Polesello, Sturla) on marine themes. In 2023 the municipality relocated the sculptures to shallower water closer to Terza Spiaggia beach specifically to widen access: divers and snorkelers alike can now reach the works, whereas the original installation was mainly a dive/submarine-tour experience. The pieces remain anchored between roughly 4 and 7 metres depth. Access options today are guided scuba dive, snorkel directly from the beach, or the seasonal Mizar I glass-bottom boat departing Golfo Aranci harbour.
