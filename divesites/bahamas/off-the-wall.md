## Off The Wall

Off The Wall is a wall dive off Cape Eleuthera associated with the Cape Eleuthera Institute's offshore research infrastructure, situated where the Exuma Sound begins its dramatic drop.

## Overview

Off The Wall is independently confirmed (via Zentacle's own listing) to be an alternate name for the "CEI Offshore Cage" — better known as the **Cobia Cage**, a Cape Eleuthera Institute aquaculture research structure. The cage — described as spaceship-shaped, roughly 3,000 m², with shark-resistant netting — floats in about 24 meters (80 feet) of water, anchored to a sandy slope approximately 75 yards from where the wall drops off. Divers descend past the cage and can then explore the wall face itself, which continues well beyond recreational limits. Recorded dive logs for this site show shallower maximum depths (around 15 meters) as well, suggesting many dives here don't push to the cage's full depth.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Cape Eleuthera, southern Eleuthera, Bahamas
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 20-30 meters
- **Current**: Moderate; Cape Eleuthera is known for current-influenced conditions
- **Best Time**: November to May

## Marine Life

Caribbean reef sharks are a defining sighting here, reported zig-zagging across the sand and along the wall edge near the cage. A loggerhead sea turtle has been reported at the site, along with amberjack near the structure. Macro life on the sand includes lettuce sea slugs and arrow crabs. One source mentions a hammerhead shark occasionally frequenting the sandy area, but the author who reported it noted this was unconfirmed — we've left it out as a named species for that reason.

## Dive Profile

Descend toward the cage, which sits at roughly 24 meters anchored to the sandy slope near the wall edge, then follow the wall edge if continuing deeper. Ascend along the wall, complete a safety stop at 5 meters, and surface for pickup. The wall face drops away steeply beyond the cage — maintain awareness of your depth.

## Entry and Exit

Boat dive only. The site is within range of The Dive Shop at Cape Eleuthera Resort, approximately 7 minutes by boat from the dock.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Ask your dive operator about current conditions before entry — Cape Eleuthera can have significant current
- The cage structure makes a good photo subject alongside the ambient reef
- Bring a torch for examining crevices in the wall face

## Safety Considerations

Depths exceed 30 meters just off the recreational limit at this site. A dive computer is essential. The open Exuma Sound exposure can create surge at the wall face. Complete a safety stop at 5 meters and carry an SMB for open-water ascent.
