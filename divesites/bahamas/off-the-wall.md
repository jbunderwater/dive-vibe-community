## Off The Wall

Off The Wall is a wall dive off Cape Eleuthera associated with the Cape Eleuthera Institute's offshore research infrastructure, situated where the Exuma Sound begins its dramatic drop.

## Overview

Also referenced as the CEI Offshore Cage site, Off The Wall is located at the edge of the wall system running along Cape Eleuthera's southwestern coast. The site marks the point where the reef top drops away into the deep Exuma Sound. The cage itself is a decommissioned aquaculture structure — a joint Cape Eleuthera Institute / University of Miami "SeaStation" project originally used to farm cobia — and today's re-check confirms it floats in about 80 feet (24m) of water, anchored roughly 75 yards from where the wall drops off (allstarliveaboards.com), which lines up with the previously-stated 24-25m figure. Divers can descend to the cage level and then follow the wall down to around 95 feet (29m), where amberjack, turtles, and coral are found (allstarliveaboards.com). **Depth/difficulty conflict:** zentacle.com's own structured listing for this site gives a max depth of only 49.2 feet (15m) and rates it Beginner difficulty — both figures are inconsistent with the cage-and-wall depths (80-95ft/24-29m) described in that same source ecosystem and with the 30m/Intermediate figures in our data. This looks like a database default rather than a considered rating; the fuller descriptive sources support a deeper, more Intermediate-to-Advanced profile, but the conflict is flagged here rather than silently resolved.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Cape Eleuthera, southern Eleuthera, Bahamas
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 20-30 meters
- **Current**: Moderate; Cape Eleuthera is known for current-influenced conditions
- **Best Time**: November to May

## Marine Life

Cape Eleuthera wall sites consistently produce reef shark encounters (capeeleuthera.com). A loggerhead turtle has been specifically observed swimming past the cage, and "various sharks" are reported using it (allstarliveaboards.com). Amberjack are specifically confirmed off the wall near the cage at ~95ft (allstarliveaboards.com) — this claim holds up under re-audit. The cage's netting itself hosts confirmed macro life: lettuce sea slugs, arrow crabs, shrimp, and neck crabs (allstarliveaboards.com). A hammerhead shark is described by that same source as "reportedly" frequenting the sandy area near the cage — note the source's own hedging language, so this is presented as an anecdotal rather than confirmed sighting. Grouper and generic "smaller reef fish species" mentioned in the prior draft are regionally typical for Cape Eleuthera but were not independently confirmed at this specific site in today's sources.

## Dive Profile

Descend to the reef flat (approximately 15 meters), then follow the wall edge down to your planned depth limit. The cage structure at around 25 meters is a natural reference point. Ascend along the wall, complete a safety stop at 5 meters, and surface for pickup. The wall face drops away steeply — maintain awareness of your depth.

## Entry and Exit

Boat dive only. The site is within range of The Dive Shop at Cape Eleuthera Resort, approximately 7 minutes by boat from the dock.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Ask your dive operator about current conditions before entry — Cape Eleuthera can have significant current
- The cage structure makes a good photo subject alongside the ambient reef
- Bring a torch for examining crevices in the wall face

## Safety Considerations

Depths exceed 30 meters just off the recreational limit at this site. A dive computer is essential. The open Exuma Sound exposure can create surge at the wall face. Complete a safety stop at 5 meters and carry an SMB for open-water ascent.
