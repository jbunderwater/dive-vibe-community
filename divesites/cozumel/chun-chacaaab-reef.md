# Chun Chacaaab Reef

A southern Cozumel reef positioned between Maracaibo and the main reef line, with a coral structure distinct from the rest of the island's dive sites and reduced boat traffic due to its remote location.

## Overview

Chun Chacaaab (also spelled Chun Chacaab) is a Mayan place name for a reef positioned between Maracaibo and the rest of the island, often dived in combination with Maracaibo as a southern-circuit pairing (reefsofcozumel.com). The site's remoteness from the main tourist concentration means it receives far fewer dive boats than the central corridor sites, and can involve current running in multiple directions along with surge (deepstop.wordpress.com). Its structure and vegetation differ noticeably from other Cozumel reefs — extensive fields of sea plumes, sea rods, and sea fans, running in oblique, parallel coral benches along the coast, covered in sponges, gorgonians, black coral, and algae (reefsofcozumel.com, prodiveinternational.com, aquamarinas.com). Sources report depths from about 60 to 100 feet (18–30m), with most of the dive spent around 70 feet (21m) and shallower ridgetops near 50 feet (15m); our listed 21m reflects the commonly-cited typical depth, though deeper valleys down to ~30m are reported by one source. The site is rated Advanced due to unpredictable currents and large sea swells, and is recommended for Nitrox 32 per reefsofcozumel.com.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Southern Cozumel, between Maracaibo and the main reef line, Quintana Roo, Mexico
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 21 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Not separately confirmed for this site by current sources
- **Current**: Sometimes unpredictable; rounded ridges run parallel to water movement
- **Best Time**: Typically dived alongside Maracaibo on a southern-circuit trip when conditions allow safe access to the far south

## Marine Life

Confirmed sightings, cross-referenced across reefsofcozumel.com, prodiveinternational.com, aquamarinas.com, and a first-person trip report (deepstop.wordpress.com): hawksbill turtles (both adult and juvenile turtles reported feeding), reef sharks, nurse sharks, spotted eagle rays, black groupers, green morays, octopus, and spotted drum, alongside common reef fish — damselfish, barracuda, groupers, creole wrasse, grunts, snappers, bar jacks, parrotfish, chubs (including Bermuda chubs), schoolmasters, French grunts, blue chromis, and red seastars. Lobsters and crabs shelter under overhangs (aquamarinas.com, deepstop.wordpress.com), and lionfish have been reported in crevices and overhangs (prodiveinternational.com). Southern stingrays are also documented. No source confirms splendid toadfish, Nassau grouper, tiger grouper, or angelfish specifically at this site — those claims have been removed as unverified for Chun Chacaaab despite being present elsewhere in Cozumel.

## Dive Profile

The dive runs mostly around 21m (70ft) along rounded, parallel coral ridges that run with the current, with ridgetops closer to 15m (50ft) and the deepest valleys near 24–30m (80–100ft) depending on the source. Spend time investigating the sea plume, sea rod, and sea fan fields and the crevices/overhangs sheltering French grunts, schoolmasters, and lobsters. Current direction and strength are the main variables in dive planning here. Ascend with an SMB before surfacing.

## Entry and Exit

One of the longer boat rides in the Cozumel circuit — typically combined with Maracaibo as a southern-circuit pairing given the shared boat transit to the far south. Full drift protocol with SMB deployment applies.

## Tips

- Nitrox 32 is recommended for this site (reefsofcozumel.com)
- The reduced boat traffic means wildlife is significantly less habituated — move slowly and deliberately
- A southern day combining Chun Chacaaab with Maracaibo makes efficient use of the long boat transit
- Carry an extra tank's worth of planning conservatism — the distance from recompression facilities warrants it

## Safety

Remote southern position extends emergency response time significantly. All standard drift diving precautions apply with particular emphasis on SMB deployment. Currents here can be unpredictable and boat assistance is limited given the remote location. Verify dive insurance and the nearest recompression chamber location (San Miguel) before departing.

## Photography

The less-trafficked reefs of the far south offer relatively undisturbed wildlife and good visibility for wide-angle work capturing the sea plume, sea rod, and sea fan fields that distinguish this reef from Cozumel's other sites. No site-specific photography guidance beyond general reef-photography practice was found in sources.
