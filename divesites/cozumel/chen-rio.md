# Chen Rio

An east-coast Cozumel reef offering a dramatically different character from the calm leeward sites — exposed to open Caribbean surge with untamed reef formations.

## Overview

Chen Rio sits on Cozumel's windward eastern coast, which faces directly into the open Caribbean Sea without the shelter of the island's mass. Wikipedia and multiple travel sources confirm the core geography: a natural rocky rim separates a small, calmer pool from the open sea, which is why Chen Rio is popular as a beach despite the otherwise unprotected, often-hazardous eastern shoreline. Coleman Concierge's Cozumel dive guide describes it as a beach dive with "a small bay sheltered from the surf and current on the inside and a drop off to the abyss on the outside" — 60ft (18m) maximum depth, Intermediate difficulty. The Maya place name is commonly given as meaning "stone mouth" or derived from a river system in the island's interior (sources vary on the precise translation).

## Dive Profile

Diving Chen Rio requires careful current and swell assessment from shore before entering. The natural bay formed by the rocky rim provides a sheltered area to a maximum of about 18m (60ft), with a drop-off to deeper, more current-exposed water on the outside of the rim (colemanconcierge.com). Beyond this, no source provides a detailed dive profile — bottom time decisions should be conservative and deferring to conditions. This is not a site to explore solo or on a first east-coast dive.

## Entry and Exit

Chen Rio is a shore dive accessed from the coastal road on Cozumel's east side. A natural rocky rim creates a sheltered pool; entry is into this protected bay area. Exit through the same entry point. Conditions must be assessed on arrival — if surf is breaking significantly over the rocky rim or undertow is present, the dive should not be attempted. Restaurants at the beach make this a convenient stop on an east-coast day trip.

## Tips

- Assess conditions from shore before gearing up — if swell is breaking heavily into the bay, postpone
- Calmer, settled-weather days are safer regardless of season; this is not a site to gamble on
- Combining Chen Rio with a stop at the Chen Rio beach restaurant makes for a full east-coast day
- Intermediate certification and shore-dive experience in variable conditions are genuine prerequisites here
- The dive may be less than 30 minutes if conditions deteriorate — manage expectations and enjoy what is offered

## Safety

This is a weather-dependent shore site: surge creates risk of contact with the reef, and the eastern coast is generally unprotected outside the rocky-rim bay (en.wikipedia.org, colemanconcierge.com). Never attempt this site without local knowledge of the daily sea state. Emergency response from the east coast takes longer than at leeward sites given the distance from San Miguel, though no source quantifies this specifically for Chen Rio — plan accordingly.
