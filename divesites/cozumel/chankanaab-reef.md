# Chankanaab Reef

A beginner-friendly shore dive within Chankanaab National Park, combining easy coral reef access with a certified-diver cave entrance — the only shore-accessible cave dive in Cozumel.

## Overview

Chankanaab Reef fronts the protected national park of the same name on Cozumel's southern coast, offering easy shore entry. Depth figures vary modestly by source — 30–50 feet (9–16 m) per one dive database, 35–40 feet (10.7–12 m) per another — consistent with this entry's 12-meter maximum. Current is generally described as mild, running roughly north–south, and one source notes it "sometimes" carries cooler fresh water in from the adjacent lagoon. For certified divers, the submerged cave La Quebrada is confirmed by a source as "the only cave you can dive through from shore in Cozumel"; cave entry is restricted to certified divers, and cave dives may be cancelled on days with strong current. The freshwater influx into the area is consistent with a halocline effect where fresh and salt water meet, though no source directly describes experiencing a halocline layer inside the cave itself, so that detail should be treated as plausible rather than confirmed. Underwater statues (a Christ figure, the Mayan rain god Chaac, and a Virgin Mary) are also part of the dive.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Chankanaab National Park, southern coast, Cozumel, Quintana Roo, Mexico
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 12 meters
- **Current**: Mild, generally north–south

## Marine Life

Long-snout seahorses, spotted drum, parrotfish, angelfish, grunts, snappers, grouper, trumpetfish, wrasse, and amberjacks are reported at the shallow reef, and turtles are seen regularly (sources did not specify hawksbill vs. green, so that species-level detail has been removed). Additional confirmed residents include king crab, spiny lobster, moray eels, octopus (on night dives), and Caribbean reef squid. The splendid toadfish — endemic to Cozumel — is commonly reported elsewhere on the island, but no source checked for this audit confirmed it specifically at Chankanaab, so that claim has been removed rather than repeated. Similarly, "glassy sweepers" inside La Quebrada cave could not be confirmed by any source and has been dropped; the cave's specific resident fauna is not well documented in the sources available.
