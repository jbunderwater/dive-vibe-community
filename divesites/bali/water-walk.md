## Overview

Research strongly indicates this entry is mistagged rather than a real scuba dive site. The OSM node carries only minimal tags (`name=Water Walk`, `sport=scuba_diving`) with no further detail. Its coordinates (-8.7065673, 115.2631469) sit in the Semawang/Mertasari Beach area of south Sanur — a match, within roughly 500 m by geocoding, for the published location of "Seawalker," a commercial underwater-walking attraction that operates from Mertasari Beach in front of the Puri Santrian Hotel. Seawalker guests wear a sealed, air-fed helmet and are led along the seabed to a maximum depth of about 4.5 m (15 ft); it is explicitly marketed to non-swimmers and non-divers and does not require certification. It is not a scuba dive site. An extensive search of dive operator listings, PADI's site database, ScubaBoard, and general web search for "Water Walk" as a named scuba dive site near Tulamben, Sanur, or Nusa Penida returned no matches — every reference found was to the Seawalker/helmet-diving activity. The coordinates also place this entry roughly 80 km by road from the Tulamben cluster this record was grouped with, which is itself inconsistent with it being a Tulamben-area dive site. The previous reef/cuttlefish description had no site-specific source and has been removed rather than repeated; see Marine Life below.
