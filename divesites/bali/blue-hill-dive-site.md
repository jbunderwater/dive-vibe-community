## Overview

This entry sits in the Tulamben corridor between the USAT Liberty wreck and the Drop-off/Wall, and its name suggests a connection to Bluehill Dive Resort Tulamben (bluehilltulamben.com). However, the resort's own site lists its house reef under the name "Coral Garden" — a shallow shore-entry reef roughly 8–12m deep — not "Blue Hill." No dive shop, ScubaBoard thread, or dive database consulted names a site called "Blue Hill Dive Site." It's possible this OSM node is a duplicate or mislabeling of a nearby reef (potentially the "Coral Garden Tulamben" entry already tracked separately in this database) rather than an independently named site. Because no site-specific source could be confirmed, this page does not describe a dive profile or attribute specific marine life to this location — an earlier version of this page did so, but none of those claims traced to a verifiable source, so they have been removed.
