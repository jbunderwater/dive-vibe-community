## Great White Shark Cage Dive Site

Generic surface cage dive for great white sharks at the Neptune Islands.

## Overview

This entry represents the surface cage dive offered at the Neptune Islands. Surface cages sit at about 2 m and need no certification ([Wikivoyage - Shark cage diving at the Neptune Islands](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Diving_in_South_Australia/Shark_cage_diving_at_the_Neptune_Islands)); [Bluewater Dive Travel - South Australia 2025 Trip Recap](https://www.bluewaterdivetravel.com/article/south-australia-trip-recap-2025) describes a hookah-supplied surface cage alongside a separate seafloor cage for certified divers. It likely duplicates the Neptune Islands North entry, and the exact bay or anchorage is not given by any reviewed source.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Neptune Islands Group, South Australia
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Depth**: about 2 m ([Wikivoyage - Shark cage diving at the Neptune Islands](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Diving_in_South_Australia/Shark_cage_diving_at_the_Neptune_Islands))

## Marine Life

Great white sharks are the target species at Neptune Islands cage dives ([Bluewater Dive Travel - South Australia 2025 Trip Recap](https://www.bluewaterdivetravel.com/article/south-australia-trip-recap-2025), [Wikivoyage - Shark cage diving at the Neptune Islands](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Diving_in_South_Australia/Shark_cage_diving_at_the_Neptune_Islands), [Marine Parks SA - Neptune Islands Group Marine Park](https://www.marineparks.sa.gov.au/find-a-park/eyre-peninsula/neptune-islands)). No further site-specific species reports were found, so none are listed.

## Notes

Guests are not allowed to exit the cages ([Wikivoyage - Shark cage diving at the Neptune Islands](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Diving_in_South_Australia/Shark_cage_diving_at_the_Neptune_Islands)). No source-verified temperature, visibility, or current data for this location were found.
