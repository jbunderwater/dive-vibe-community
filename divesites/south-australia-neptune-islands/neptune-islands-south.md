## Neptune Islands South

Seabed cage dive for great white sharks, for certified divers.

## Overview

The Neptune Islands Group includes the South Neptune Islands (northern island 35 m high, southern/Lighthouse Island 37 m high), where water reaches 80 m within 1.5-2 km of the group ([Wikipedia - Neptune Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neptune_Islands)). Cage diving in the park uses a cage lowered to the kelp-covered seabed; [Bluewater Dive Travel - South Australia 2025 Trip Recap](https://www.bluewaterdivetravel.com/article/south-australia-trip-recap-2025) says Rodney Fox is the only operator in the world with a seafloor cage. Seabed dives require PADI Open Water or equivalent ([Wikivoyage - Shark cage diving at the Neptune Islands](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Diving_in_South_Australia/Shark_cage_diving_at_the_Neptune_Islands)).

No reviewed source states that the seabed cage is stationed at South Neptune specifically; it is documented for the Neptune Islands as a whole.

## Site Information

- **Location**: South Neptune Islands group, off Port Lincoln, South Australia
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Depth**: sources conflict: 18 m ([Wikivoyage - Shark cage diving at the Neptune Islands](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Diving_in_South_Australia/Shark_cage_diving_at_the_Neptune_Islands)), 60-80 ft / about 18-24 m ([Bluewater Dive Travel - South Australia 2025 Trip Recap](https://www.bluewaterdivetravel.com/article/south-australia-trip-recap-2025)), about 70 ft / 21.3 m ([DAN Alert Diver - South Australia's Great White Shark Adventure](https://world.dan.org/?p=26855)). Recorded as 20 m.

## Marine Life

Great white sharks are the species reported from the cage ([Bluewater Dive Travel - South Australia 2025 Trip Recap](https://www.bluewaterdivetravel.com/article/south-australia-trip-recap-2025), [DAN Alert Diver - South Australia's Great White Shark Adventure](https://world.dan.org/?p=26855)). No source reviewed confirms other species from the seabed cage at this location, so none are listed.

## Notes

Guests are not allowed to exit the cages ([Wikivoyage - Shark cage diving at the Neptune Islands](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Diving_in_South_Australia/Shark_cage_diving_at_the_Neptune_Islands)). No source-verified temperature, visibility, or current data were found. Verify depth, certification and conditions with the operator.
