## Jalousie Beach Reef

Jalousie Beach Reef sits at the base of Gros Piton in the dramatic bay between Gros and Petit Piton — a UNESCO World Heritage Site — continuing the volcanic wall diving character of the Piton coastline in shallow, shore-accessible form.

## Overview

Scuba St. Lucia's dive-site listing places Jalousie at the base of Gros Piton, describing schooling fish including creole wrasse and bar jack among the site's corals and sponges. The reef slope holds a variety of corals, sponges, and sea fans at depths to about 15 metres. The volcanic backdrop and the iconic Piton silhouette above the surface make this one of the more visually striking shore dives in the area.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Jalousie Bay, St. Lucia, Caribbean
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 15 meters

## Marine Life

Creole wrasse, bar jack, and the occasional southern sennet are directly confirmed by Scuba St. Lucia's dive-site page, re-fetched live today. A source named stolentime.com lists a site called "Gros Piton Reef" whose description and species list ("schooling fish, particularly creole-wrasse, bar jack and the occasional southern-sennet" plus "moray eels, reef crabs, lobsters") closely matches this site's location and character — likely the same site under a different name used by that source. Moray eels, reef crabs, and lobsters are added on that basis. Seahorses and trumpetfish were reported in prior research citing SMMA and PADI, but those pages again returned no usable content on this pass; they remain carried-forward claims unconfirmed for a second audit cycle.
