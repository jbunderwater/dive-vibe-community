## Jalousie Beach Reef

Jalousie is a reef dive at the base of Gros Piton on St. Lucia's southwest coast.

## Overview

Scuba St. Lucia lists Jalousie among its boat dives and describes it as situated at the base of Gros Piton, with a great range of corals and sponges. Scubaverse describes a mix of wall and reef between the Pitons. Only two independent sources were found for this site; no source consulted gives a depth or difficulty rating, so the 15 m maximum depth and Beginner rating below are unconfirmed.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Base of Gros Piton, Jalousie, St. Lucia
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 15 meters


## Marine Life

Scuba St. Lucia reports schooling fish including creole wrasse, bar jack, and occasionally southern sennet.
