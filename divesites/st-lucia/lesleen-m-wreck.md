## Lesleen M Wreck

The Lesleen M is a roughly 165-foot freighter deliberately sunk as an artificial reef in Anse Cochon Bay and is one of St. Lucia's most accessible wreck dives.

## Overview

**Identity and history.** Sources consistently describe a 165-foot (about 50 m) freighter ([Scuba St. Lucia](https://scubastlucia.com/diving/), [PADI Blog](https://blog.padi.com/5-must-dive-sites-st-lucia/), [Islander Keys](https://www.islanderkeys.com/lesleen-m-wreck-st-lucia/), [Stolen Time](https://stolentime.com/dive-sites-in-saint-lucia)); [Saint Lucia Tourism (stlucia.org)](https://stlucia.org/en/things-to-do/diving/) lists the length as both 165 ft and 167 ft. Most sources give 1986 as the sinking year ([Scuba St. Lucia](https://scubastlucia.com/diving/), [PADI Blog](https://blog.padi.com/5-must-dive-sites-st-lucia/), [Islander Keys](https://www.islanderkeys.com/lesleen-m-wreck-st-lucia/), [Caribbean-Diving.com](https://caribbean-diving.com/dive-sites/lesleen-m-freighter-wreck-st-lucia), [Saint Lucia Tourism (stlucia.org)](https://stlucia.org/en/things-to-do/diving/)); [Stolen Time](https://stolentime.com/dive-sites-in-saint-lucia) specifies October 1986, and other web listings give 1985, so the year is not fully settled. [Caribbean-Diving.com](https://caribbean-diving.com/dive-sites/lesleen-m-freighter-wreck-st-lucia) and [Stolen Time](https://stolentime.com/dive-sites-in-saint-lucia) say it was sunk by the Department of Fisheries; [Islander Keys](https://www.islanderkeys.com/lesleen-m-wreck-st-lucia/) adds that the vessel was stripped before sinking. No source consulted gives the ship's original name, build date, builder, tonnage, or previous service, and no Wikipedia or wreck-registry entry was found, so none of these are stated here.

**Location and depth.** [PADI Blog](https://blog.padi.com/5-must-dive-sites-st-lucia/), [Islander Keys](https://www.islanderkeys.com/lesleen-m-wreck-st-lucia/), and [Saint Lucia Tourism (stlucia.org)](https://stlucia.org/en/things-to-do/diving/) place it in Anse Cochon Bay; [Stolen Time](https://stolentime.com/dive-sites-in-saint-lucia) labels it as at Anse La Raye, and [Scuba St. Lucia](https://scubastlucia.com/diving/) says it is about 15 minutes by boat north of its dive center. Depth figures vary: [Scuba St. Lucia](https://scubastlucia.com/diving/) and [Stolen Time](https://stolentime.com/dive-sites-in-saint-lucia) give 30 ft to the deck and 65 ft at the deepest point; [Islander Keys](https://www.islanderkeys.com/lesleen-m-wreck-st-lucia/) gives 60 ft; [PADI Blog](https://blog.padi.com/5-must-dive-sites-st-lucia/) gives 40-65 ft (12-20 m); [Saint Lucia Tourism (stlucia.org)](https://stlucia.org/en/things-to-do/diving/) gives 40-60 ft; [Caribbean-Diving.com](https://caribbean-diving.com/dive-sites/lesleen-m-freighter-wreck-st-lucia) gives about 22 m (60 ft) with a listed maximum of 20-30 m. The database figure of 18 m sits inside this spread.

**Condition.** The wreck sits upright on sand ([Scuba St. Lucia](https://scubastlucia.com/diving/), [Islander Keys](https://www.islanderkeys.com/lesleen-m-wreck-st-lucia/), [Caribbean-Diving.com](https://caribbean-diving.com/dive-sites/lesleen-m-freighter-wreck-st-lucia)), is encrusted with soft corals, sponges, and hydroids ([Stolen Time](https://stolentime.com/dive-sites-in-saint-lucia)), and has an accessible hold and engine room ([PADI Blog](https://blog.padi.com/5-must-dive-sites-st-lucia/), [Stolen Time](https://stolentime.com/dive-sites-in-saint-lucia)). Penetration of the hold or engine room requires proper training and equipment and is never risk-free, even on a shallow wreck that operators recommend to newly certified divers.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Anse Cochon, St. Lucia
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 18 meters

## Marine Life

Moray eels are reported by [Scuba St. Lucia](https://scubastlucia.com/diving/), [PADI Blog](https://blog.padi.com/5-must-dive-sites-st-lucia/), [Islander Keys](https://www.islanderkeys.com/lesleen-m-wreck-st-lucia/), [Caribbean-Diving.com](https://caribbean-diving.com/dive-sites/lesleen-m-freighter-wreck-st-lucia), and [Saint Lucia Tourism (stlucia.org)](https://stlucia.org/en/things-to-do/diving/); lobsters by [Scuba St. Lucia](https://scubastlucia.com/diving/), [Islander Keys](https://www.islanderkeys.com/lesleen-m-wreck-st-lucia/), and [Saint Lucia Tourism (stlucia.org)](https://stlucia.org/en/things-to-do/diving/); frogfish by [Scuba St. Lucia](https://scubastlucia.com/diving/). French angelfish are reported by [PADI Blog](https://blog.padi.com/5-must-dive-sites-st-lucia/), [Stolen Time](https://stolentime.com/dive-sites-in-saint-lucia), and [Saint Lucia Tourism (stlucia.org)](https://stlucia.org/en/things-to-do/diving/), and [Stolen Time](https://stolentime.com/dive-sites-in-saint-lucia) adds juvenile Nassau grouper and queen angelfish. [Islander Keys](https://www.islanderkeys.com/lesleen-m-wreck-st-lucia/) also lists turtles, spider crabs, shrimp, red snapper, barracuda, and small tuna; [Caribbean-Diving.com](https://caribbean-diving.com/dive-sites/lesleen-m-freighter-wreck-st-lucia) lists scorpionfish and passing pelagics.
