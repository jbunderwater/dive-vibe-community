## Daini Koyomaru Wreck

The Daini Koyomaru is a Japanese dredger that was deliberately sunk in 1996 as an artificial reef off St. Lucia's west coast, and is rated as an Advanced dive.

## Overview

St. Lucia Tourism, DeeperBlue, and ScubaWeather agree the vessel is a Japanese dredger sunk on purpose, with St. Lucia Tourism and DeeperBlue giving 1996. ScubaWeather describes it as a 244-foot vessel bought by the St. Lucian government in the early 1990s and sunk after its dredging work ended; St. Lucia Tourism also gives 244 feet. The build date and tonnage are not reported in any source consulted, so neither is stated here. DeeperBlue also mentions a separate, larger dredger of over 350 feet near Vieux Fort described as a sister ship, so published length figures for these dredgers are not consistent.

**Sources disagree on orientation and depth.** St. Lucia Tourism describes the wreck lying on its side at about 108 ft (33 m). DeeperBlue reports that a trapped air pocket turned the vessel upside down during sinking, with a maximum depth of about 100 ft and the intact hull at 55 ft. Scubaverse gives about 30 m (100 ft). DeeperBlue notes that penetration is possible along its length but that collapsed decks make navigation confusing, with powerful lights and planned decompression stops advised. Wreck penetration requires proper training and equipment. St. Lucia Tourism recommends Nitrox. Sources place the wreck off Anse Cochon; the stored coordinates in this database are further north and are under review.

## Site Information

- **Location**: West coast, near Anse Cochon, St. Lucia
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 33 meters


## Marine Life

St. Lucia Tourism reports large French angelfish, jacks, barracuda, and a large pufferfish. DeeperBlue reports large schools of jacks circling the wreck and regularly seen turtles. Scubaverse also lists jacks and barracuda.
