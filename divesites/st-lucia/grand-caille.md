## Grand Caille

Grand Caille is a wall dive off Soufrière on St. Lucia's west coast, known for deep-water gorgonians and sea whips.

## Overview

Scuba St. Lucia describes Grand Caille as "a very dramatic dive site with deep water gorgonians and sea whips," with huge brain corals and barrel sponges in pristine condition. Stolen Time describes large boulders in the shallows with a sheer wall below. St. Lucia Tourism gives a starting depth of about 40 ft with the wall lip around 50 ft; no source gives a firm maximum depth, so the 30 m figure below is unconfirmed.

**The name's translation differs by source:** Scuba St. Lucia gives the Patois as "Large Reef," Stolen Time gives "Great House," and The Moorings lists the alias "The Big House."

## Site Information

- **Location**: Soufrière, St. Lucia
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters


## Marine Life

Stolen Time reports that the site has long been known for big fish, with occasional large barracuda, though big fish are now much rarer because of fishing. No other site-specific marine-life reports were found; St. Lucia's regional reef fauna applies.
