## Coral Gardens Soufrière

Coral Gardens is a reef and slope dive at the base of Gros Piton in the Soufrière Marine Management Area.

## Overview

Scuba St. Lucia lists Coral Gardens among its boat dives under Gros Piton and describes dense forests of sea plumes in the shallows that shelter juvenile reef fish. St. Lucia Tourism gives a depth range of 15-90 feet (about 5-27 m) with five-finger corals. Stolen Time describes a steep slope with a variety of coral species and large barrel sponges that continues for hundreds of feet, advising divers to watch their depth. Difficulty is reported inconsistently: Bluewater Dive Travel aims it at more experienced divers, while Scubaverse describes it as beginner-friendly.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Base of Gros Piton, Soufrière, St. Lucia
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 27 meters


## Marine Life

Scuba St. Lucia reports sargassum triggerfish in the deeper areas with the odd barracuda, and juvenile reef fish among the sea plumes in the shallows. Stolen Time also reports sargassum triggerfish and occasional schools of barracuda.
