## Maria Island Reef

Maria Island Reef lies off the Maria Islands Nature Reserve near Vieux Fort in southern St. Lucia and is documented in OpenStreetMap as a reef.

## Overview

The Maria Islands are a nature reserve off the south coast of St. Lucia near Vieux Fort. St. Lucia Tourism states that independent visits to the islands are not permitted and that access is through organised tours, and AFAR reports a seasonal closure during bird nesting. Snorkelling is the main reef activity described. No dive operator or dive guide consulted on 2026-10-23 lists a scuba site at Maria Island, so depth, conditions, and marine life are unconfirmed (the 15 m figure below is a database estimate). Contact the St. Lucia National Trust or a licensed local operator before planning a dive here.
