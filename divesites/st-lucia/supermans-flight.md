## Superman's Flight

Superman's Flight is a drift dive along a sloping wall at the base of Petit Piton on St. Lucia's southwest coast, within the Soufrière Marine Management Area.

## Overview

Scuba St. Lucia describes a drift dive "in the shadow of the Petit Piton" along a steep slope covered in soft corals with abundant fish life. PADI describes a sloping wall with a moderate current that makes it a favourite for drift divers, with soft corals and massive sponges. The Moorings and Stolen Time both attribute the name to the cliff's appearance in Superman II; the Moorings also describes boulders and canyons along the wall.

Depth and difficulty are reported inconsistently. Two sources list a maximum depth of 1,500 ft, which is clearly an error, and others give no figure, so the 50 m maximum below is an unconfirmed estimate. Difficulty is described variously as suitable for all levels with a mild drift (St. Lucia Tourism), intermediate to advanced with possible strong currents (The Moorings), and a moderate current (PADI). Check conditions with a local operator.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Base of Petit Piton, St. Lucia
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 50 meters


## Marine Life

PADI reports soft corals, massive sponges, and schools of grunts and parrotfish. The Moorings reports schools of reef fish, squid, and frogfish.
