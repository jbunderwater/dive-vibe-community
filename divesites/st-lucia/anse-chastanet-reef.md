## Anse Chastanet Reef

Anse Chastanet Reef is a shore dive starting a few steps from the beach at Anse Chastanet, within the Soufrière Marine Management Area.

## Overview

Scuba St. Lucia describes a shallow plateau at roughly 5-25 feet (1.5-7.6 m) that drops to about 140 feet (about 43 m) at its outer edge, and notes the site is good for macro photography. St. Lucia Tourism gives 15-60 feet, and the Anse Chastanet resort lists 20-140 feet for the area, so reported ranges vary with where on the reef they are measured. PADI calls the close shore access and shallow depths a good fit for shore diving, and the Anse Chastanet resort reports more than 150 tropical fish species across the reef (operator claim, not independently confirmed). Bluewater Dive Travel describes it as beginner-friendly.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Anse Chastanet, Soufrière, St. Lucia
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 20 meters


## Marine Life

Scuba St. Lucia reports moray eels, parrotfish, blennies, crabs, and schools of needlefish. St. Lucia Tourism reports large trumpetfish and turtles, and Scubaverse reports juvenile fish, trumpetfish, and seahorses.
