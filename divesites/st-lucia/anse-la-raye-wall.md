## Anse La Raye Wall

Anse La Raye Wall, off the northwest-coast village whose name means "Bay of Rays," mixes steep sloping reef and vertical wall.

## Overview

[PADI Blog](https://blog.padi.com/5-must-dive-sites-st-lucia/) describes a dive that starts over a shallow wall of fire coral, drops onto huge boulders, then a coral-covered slope and vertical wall, with the main wall starting at about 18 m (60 ft) and descending to 34 m (110 ft); it recommends Advanced Open Water certification and drift-diving experience, and notes gorgonian fans, sea whips, and huge barrel sponges. [Saint Lucia Tourism (stlucia.org)](https://stlucia.org/en/things-to-do/diving/) gives the same 60-110 ft range, and [Stolen Time](https://stolentime.com/dive-sites-in-saint-lucia) describes a shallow wall with a slope of huge boulders below, fire coral in the shallows, and purple vase and barrel sponges deeper. [Scuba St. Lucia](https://scubastlucia.com/diving/) lists it among its further-away Piton-side boat dives, with a wall falling from the surface and sea whips, gorgonians, soft coral, and large feather duster worms. The maximum depth has been corrected from 40 m to 34 m to match the sources.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Anse La Raye, St. Lucia, Caribbean
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 34 meters

## Marine Life

[PADI Blog](https://blog.padi.com/5-must-dive-sites-st-lucia/) and [Saint Lucia Tourism (stlucia.org)](https://stlucia.org/en/things-to-do/diving/) report stingrays and eagle rays, and [PADI Blog](https://blog.padi.com/5-must-dive-sites-st-lucia/) ties the site name to them. [Stolen Time](https://stolentime.com/dive-sites-in-saint-lucia) lists jacks, Bermuda chub, and spotted drums, and calls the site "probably the best place for spotting turtles"; this is a single-source comparative claim and is attributed rather than asserted.
