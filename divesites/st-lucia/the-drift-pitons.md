## The Drift (Pitons)

This entry represents drift diving along the Piton coast of St. Lucia. It is not a separately named dive site in any source consulted.

## Overview

No operator or dive guide consulted on 2026-10-23 lists a site named "The Drift (Pitons)." Drift diving along the Piton coast is documented under named sites such as Superman's Flight (a drift dive at the base of Petit Piton, per Scuba St. Lucia and PADI) and Fairyland (drift diving along a wall, per Scuba St. Lucia and PADI); see those pages for sourced detail. The depth and difficulty below are curated estimates and have not been confirmed. This entry is a candidate for merging with Superman's Flight.
