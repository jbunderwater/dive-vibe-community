## Coral Gardens Grace Bay

Coral Gardens Grace Bay is a reef dive site in Turks and Caicos, Caribbean.

## Overview

Coral Gardens at Grace Bay, also known as Bight Reef, is a shallow reef in Princess Alexandra National Park, reached from the beach (Visit TCI lists beach accesses off Penn's Road and Stubb's Road). It is a single main coral ridge extending roughly 350ft (107m) from the beach, with depths of about 15-20ft (4.5-6m) at the outer reaches. Popular for snorkelling and shallow diving.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Turks and Caicos, Caribbean
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 6 meters

## Marine Life

Visit TCI reports green turtles (fairly common), southern stingrays, occasional spotted eagle rays, and nurse sharks; fish including stoplight and blue parrotfish, bar jacks, Nassau grouper, queen angelfish, barracuda, yellowtail snapper, and queen triggerfish; and Caribbean spiny lobster and spotted moray eels in ledges and crevices.
