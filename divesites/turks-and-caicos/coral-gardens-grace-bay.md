## Coral Gardens Grace Bay

Coral Gardens Grace Bay is a reef dive site in Turks and Caicos, Caribbean.

## Overview

Coral Gardens at Grace Bay — also known as Bight Reef — is the most popular snorkelling and shallow-diving site in the Turks and Caicos, set within the Princess Alexandra National Park just steps from the beach. The reef consists of a single main coral ridge extending roughly 350ft (107m) out from the beach, with water depth ranging from about 3-16ft (1-5m) near shore to 15-20ft (4.5-6m) at the outer reaches.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Turks and Caicos, Caribbean
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 6 meters

## Marine Life

Green and hawksbill turtles are among the site's most reliable sightings, often seen resting or grazing in the seagrass and unusually approachable given the regular presence of snorkellers and divers. Stingrays, eagle rays, lobsters, and crabs are also reported on the reef, alongside general colorful reef fish.
