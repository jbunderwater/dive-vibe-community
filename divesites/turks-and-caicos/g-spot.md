## G-Spot

G-Spot is a boat-accessed wall dive west of French Cay, reportedly named for a bend in the wall shaped like the letter G.

## Overview

G-Spot is the easternmost of three mooring buoys west of French Cay, about a 45-minute boat ride from Providenciales in good weather. The mooring sits in about 50ft (15m) of water, but the wall drops steeply and divers pushed off the buoy by wind or current can end up over 100ft (30m). Wannadive reports a maximum of 30.5m, medium current (1-2 knots) and lists it for CMAS*/Open Water divers, and Zentacle lists it as Beginner with a 60ft maximum; this database rates it Intermediate given the boat transit and deep wall. Dive Provo describes soft corals on a plateau leading to a V-shaped sheer drop; Aqua TCI describes a small niche in the wall that creates eddies and a field of black coral. Zentacle and Wannadive both draw on the same 2012 guide account, so they are not independent of each other.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Turks and Caicos, Caribbean
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters

## Marine Life

Reef sharks along the wall and nurse sharks resting on coral beds around 50ft (Zentacle, Wannadive, from the same 2012 account); barracuda (Aqua TCI, Dive the World); yellowtail snappers and turtles (Dive the World). Large red or orange elephant ear sponges are reported by Zentacle, Wannadive and Dive the World. Barrel sponges named in an earlier version of this page were not found in any source and were removed.
