## Black Forest

Black Forest is a wall dive site in Turks and Caicos, Caribbean.

## Overview

Black Forest, also listed by operators as Black Coral Forest, is a Northwest Point wall dive. Xtreme Spots reports the wall starts at about 45ft (13.7m) and drops to a ledge near 200ft (61m) to the south, with an average depth of about 65ft (19.8m) and a recreational maximum near 110ft (33.5m). Visibility is reported at 10-30m with mild current, and the site is recommended for intermediate and experienced divers.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Turks and Caicos, Caribbean
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 24 meters

## Marine Life

Xtreme Spots reports black corals, parrotfish, Caribbean sharpnose puffer, and queen triggerfish. Times of the Islands lists the site among Northwest Point's well-known dives but gives no species.
