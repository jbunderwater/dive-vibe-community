## Southwind Wreck

Southwind Wreck is a freighter wreck near Providenciales that sources describe as reduced to loose debris; little else is documented.

## Overview

Visit TCI's wreck-diving page refers to the 'Southwind freighter' wreck near the W.E. freighter off Turtle Cove and says it 'today consists only of some loose debris'. That page gives no depth, build date, length, tonnage or sinking cause, and Visit TCI's separate shipwrecks gallery of eight wrecks does not list it. A 2009 DiveBuddy listing, seen only as a search-result excerpt that could not be opened, reportedly places a debris field in 60-80ft (18-24m) of water leading to the edge of the wall; this conflicts with the 15m depth, shore entry and Beginner rating recorded in this database, none of which could be confirmed. No vessel history is stated because no second independent source was found. For context, Visit TCI says the nearby W.E. freighter was found abandoned and sunk on purpose in 1988 and lies at 90-165ft (30-50m), too deep for recreational dive operators. Confirm with a local operator before planning.
