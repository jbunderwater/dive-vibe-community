## Turks and Caicos

Pristine wall dives, reef sharks, and crystal-clear warm Caribbean waters.

## Description

Turks and Caicos is a notable diving destination in the Caribbean region with depths ranging from 5 to 30 meters. Water temperatures range from about 24°C (75°F) in late January to about 30°C (86°F) in July and August (Visit TCI), with visibility of roughly 10-35 meters (The Dive Globe). Diving is possible year-round; The Dive Globe lists November to May as the best period.

### Diving Opportunities

- **Wall Diving**: Walls off Northwest Point, West Caicos, French Cay and Grand Turk are the main draw, with boat access
- **Night Diving**: After-dark diving reveals nocturnal marine species and different reef behaviors

### Accessibility

- **Getting There**: Turks and Caicos is accessible via international and regional flights to nearby airports. Check with airlines for current routes and connections.
- **Dive Operators**: Professional dive operators offer equipment rental, guided dives, certification courses, and boat trips to offshore sites.
- **Accommodation**: Options range from dedicated dive resorts to budget-friendly guesthouses, with many properties located near popular dive sites.
- **Transportation**: Local transportation and dive operator transfers are the primary means of reaching dive sites.
- **Facilities**: Dive sites vary in available amenities; operator-run sites typically provide comprehensive facilities while remote sites may have limited infrastructure.

### Marine Life & Environment

- **Water Conditions**: Water temperatures range from 24-30°C (75-86°F) with visibility of 10-35 meters (33-115 feet). Current varies by site.
- **Marine Biodiversity**: Visit TCI reports Caribbean reef sharks and lemon sharks as the most frequently seen sharks, southern stingrays and spotted eagle rays as common, and humpback whales passing through January to April. Site-specific sightings are on each site page.
- **Conservation**: Marine protected areas help preserve the reef ecosystems and regulate diving activities.

## Additional Information

- **Best Time to Visit**: November to May per The Dive Globe. Diving is possible year-round.
- **Currency**: US Dollar
- **Language**: English
- **Safety**: Always dive within certification limits. Be aware of boat traffic, fire coral, sea urchins. Verify the location of the nearest hyperbaric chamber before diving.
