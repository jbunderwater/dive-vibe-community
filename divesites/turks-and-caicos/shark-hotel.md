## Shark Hotel

Shark Hotel is a wall dive site in Turks and Caicos, Caribbean.

## Overview

Shark Hotel is a Northwest Point wall dive. Dive Provo describes the wall as starting at about 45ft (14m) at the mooring and dropping to 80-100ft (24-30m), where it ends in a plateau forming a shelf, with a large stand of pillar coral near the mooring. Aqua TCI describes a deep swim-through along a sheer wall.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Turks and Caicos, Caribbean
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 24 meters

## Marine Life

Dive Provo reports schools of grunts, snappers, and goatfish and small reef sharks. Aqua TCI reports blue chromis and hunting mackerels, and states that nurse sharks breed here, which gave the site its name. Sources therefore differ on which sharks are involved (small reef sharks versus nurse sharks).
