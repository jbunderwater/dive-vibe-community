## The Hole in the Wall

The Hole in the Wall is a narrow vertical crack at Northwest Point that leads divers from the top of the wall to its face.

## Overview

At Northwest Point Marine National Park, a hole on top of the wall reef drops from about 55ft (17m) and exits on the wall face at roughly 90-95ft (27-29m). Xtreme Spots and Dive Provo both warn that only one diver fits inside at a time, and Dive Provo notes extensive sheet coral formations. Xtreme Spots rates it for intermediate and advanced divers, boat access, with visibility of 10-30m. Confined passages carry risk and call for appropriate training. Coordinates were corrected 2026-07-07 after an earlier entry placed the site about 70km east.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Turks and Caicos, Caribbean
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Cave/cavern
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 27 meters

## Marine Life

Dive the World reports brittle corals, lobsters and schools of small reef fish in the crack. Xtreme Spots reports impressive coral formations along the wall and schools of yellowtail and mahogany fish. No source consulted confirms larger animals (sharks, rays, turtles) at this specific crack.
