## Chief Ministers

Chief Ministers is a Grand Turk wall dive off Governor's Beach, known for garden eels.

## Overview

Chief Ministers is a site on the Grand Turk Wall, near Governor's Beach and just offshore from the governor's mansion at Waterloo (Frommer's). Atlas & Boots describes the Grand Turk wall as about a five-minute boat ride from Governor's Beach, with a small ledge giving way to open deep water. scubadiving.place classes the site as reef and wall, gives an average depth of 26m (reviewer logs 12-27m), about 1 knot of current and visibility up to 25m, and lists Grand Turk Diving Co and Oasis Divers as operators. A 2017 Deepblu dive log records a boat entry and a 16.8m maximum depth. This is not a shipwreck; an earlier version of this entry was mislabelled as a wreck.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Turks and Caicos, Caribbean
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 26 meters

## Marine Life

Garden eels are the site's signature feature (Frommer's and other guides in search results) and scubadiving.place reports barracuda. Atlas & Boots lists triggerfish, razorfish, batfish, yellowtails and Nassau groupers on its Chief Ministers dive, but the article does not clearly tie each species to this mooring, so they are not claimed here. Earlier mentions of stingrays and moray eels could not be confirmed and were removed.
