## Chief Ministers

Chief Ministers is a Grand Turk wall-diving site near Governor's Beach, known for its eel garden approach to the famous Grand Turk Wall.

## Overview

**Corrected 2026-07-07.** This site was previously listed as "Chief Minister Wreck," a shore-entry wreck south of Providenciales. That was incorrect on every count: no source consulted names a shipwreck by this name anywhere in the Turks and Caicos, and the site's own coordinates place it off Grand Turk, not Providenciales. It has been renamed to match its real-world name, "Chief Ministers," and reclassified as a wall/reef site.

Chief Ministers sits near Governor's Beach, just offshore from the governor's mansion at Waterloo on Grand Turk, and is used as an access point to get "a proper glimpse of the famous [Grand Turk] wall." Divers typically cross a sandy bottom around 40ft before reaching the wall, where the reef drops off into the depths and divers continue along a ledge around 70ft. The site is notable for a field of garden eels, and dive reports describe roughly 75ft visibility and very light current.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Turks and Caicos, Caribbean
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 26 meters

## Marine Life

The eel garden is the site's signature feature. Barracuda and stingrays are reported here along with moray eels and shoaling fish. Re-audit note: some general write-ups about Governor's Beach diving mention manta rays and Nassau grouper, but those references were not clearly specific to this exact mooring, so they are noted here as regionally plausible rather than confirmed for this site.
