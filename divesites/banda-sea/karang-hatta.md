## Karang Hatta

Karang Hatta (also called Sekaru — meaning "shallow area" in local usage) is a submerged reef system located a few kilometres off the south coast of Pulau Hatta. It sits closest to the Banda Trench (sources report depths of approximately 5,900–6,000 m) of any commonly dived site in the archipelago, which concentrates an exceptional range of pelagic species over its sloping reefs.

## Overview

The reef slopes gradually from around 5 m down to beyond 40 m. Multiple sources place the productive action zone at around 25 m, where dense neon fusilier schools — yellow and blue, running so thick at times that they block the view — sweep down the reef slope. Large dogtooth tuna, eagle rays, giant barracuda, and schooling trevally follow the fusiliers. Blacktip and whitetip reef sharks, grey reef sharks, and green turtles are reported at this site. Hammerhead sharks are also reported here by multiple sources, most often when upwelling currents are active; their presence is unpredictable rather than routine.

The deeper wall sections feature large sea fans and elephant ear sponges, per multiple sources.

## Dive Profile

Gently sloping reef from 5 m to 25–30 m sweet spot, then dropping more steeply. Typical dive depth 15–35 m. Current variable — upwelling pushes concentrate fish on the reef edge. Advanced divers can follow the deeper wall sections; rated Advanced overall, consistent with the destination's remote, liveaboard-only character.

## Marine Life

- Schooling fusiliers (yellow and blue; extremely dense aggregations)
- Dogtooth tuna, giant barracuda, schooling trevally
- Eagle rays, green turtles
- Blacktip and whitetip reef sharks, grey reef sharks; hammerhead sharks (current-dependent, unpredictable, reported by multiple sources)
- Groupers, triggerfish, butterflyfish, snappers
- Moray eels and octopuses
- Large elephant ear sponges and sea fans on deeper sections

## Entry / Exit

Boat dive from liveaboard. Hatta Island is approximately 25 km from Banda Neira. Calmer conditions are typical but current can pick up along the outer reef.

## Tips

- Best combined with Goa Hatta for a full day on Hatta Island
- Best season for pelagics: September–November and March–April when currents are stronger
- Check current direction with your guide before entry

## Photography

Outstanding wide-angle destination. The fusilier schools combined with the deep blue background make for dramatic open-water compositions. Bring a large dome port for reef-and-blue shots.
