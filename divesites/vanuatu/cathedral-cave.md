## Cathedral Cave

Known as "The Cathedral", this is a limestone cavern off the Pango Peninsula, south of Port Vila on Efate, where sunbeams enter through an open roof.

## Overview

Big Blue Vanuatu describes entry in about 22 m of water through a rock pool into a wide, open limestone cavern lit by sunbeams. Divers then follow an outside wall back to the boat. Vanuatu Tourism describes a tall, narrow cavern stretching down to 28 m, with a "chimney" that rises to a pool at the surface inside the reef. Other listings give maximum depths of about 25-27 m (DiveBuddy), so figures vary by source.

Big Blue Vanuatu notes the dive is weather dependent: swell and current can make the cavern tricky.

## Practical Information

- **Depth Range**: entry around 22 m; reported maximum 25-28 m
- **Currents**: Swell and current can affect the cavern; weather dependent
- **Entry**: Boat dive
- **Best For**: Calm-weather days; divers interested in light effects in a cavern

## Marine Life Highlights

Big Blue Vanuatu lists shrimp, nudibranchs and leaf scorpionfish at the Cathedral.
