## Ollies Lolly

Ollie's Lolly is a shallow reef off Efate near Port Vila, just past Blacksands Reef and close to Hideaway Island.

## Overview

Vanuatu Tourism describes a reef about 9 m deep with many soft corals and large red anemones, with clownfish living among them and very clear water. Dive Adventures describes the reef as starting 9 m below the surface and known for a rare red anemone full of clownfish. Zentacle lists it as a boat dive rated Beginner, suitable for all levels, and notes a listed maximum of about 12 m. Depth reports therefore range from about 9 to 12 m.

## Practical Information

- **Depth Range**: about 9-12 meters
- **Entry**: Boat dive
- **Best For**: Beginners and photographers

## Marine Life Highlights

Clownfish living in a rare red anemone, plus a wide variety of reef fish, according to Vanuatu Tourism and Zentacle.
