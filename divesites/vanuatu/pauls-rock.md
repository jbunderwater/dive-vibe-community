## Paul's Rock

Paul's Rock is a long-extinct volcanic plug in Port Havannah, northern Efate, rising from deep water to within a few metres of the surface, between Hat Island and the mainland.

## Overview

McFadyen describes a single bommie with a shear wall, a canyon, a gully and a low tunnel on the northern side. Depth reports: the rock rises from over 35 m to under 2 m, the southern wall drops from about 10 m to over 35 m, the gully is about 20 m and the top of the bommie about 5 m. Visibility is usually 20 m or more. Trips run from Havannah Harbour, for example on the trimaran Golden Wing (McFadyen) or with Tranquility Island Dive.

The Havannah describes a rough oval reef with several swim-throughs and a drop-off wall at one end, a boat dive to a maximum of 35 m. It describes the site as a "taboo reef" under custom ownership, with fish feeding part of the dive.

## Practical Information

- **Depth Range**: top at under 2-5 meters; wall to over 35 meters
- **Visibility**: usually 20 m or more (McFadyen)
- **Entry**: Boat dive
- **Best For**: Divers comfortable with a wall to about 35 m; the shallow top is also used by snorkellers

## Marine Life Highlights

McFadyen reports trevallies, red cod, large gorgonians, a huge moray eel, anemones with clownfish and a long-finned bannerfish. Travel-news-photos-stories describes a semi-tame flowery cod nicknamed Baxter that escorts divers. The Havannah names coral trout, trevally and surgeonfish and says a turtle or two usually visit.
