## Paul's Rock

Paul's Rock is a long-extinct volcanic plug rising from the floor of Havannah Harbour on Efate, its summit reaching to within about 4 metres of the surface — a seamount dive best known for its resident semi-tame grouper "Baxter" and its status as a taboo reef under local custom.

## Correction note

Earlier data for this site incorrectly placed it near Espiritu Santo and classified it as a shallow reef. Research confirms Paul's Rock is actually located in Havannah Harbour on Efate, in the west, and is a volcanic seamount/pinnacle, not a reef — both the location and site type have been corrected below.

## Overview

Paul's Rock is reached on day trips out of Havannah Harbour, typically combined with a visit to nearby Hat Island — Sailaway Cruises has run trips here aboard the sailing trimaran *Golden Wing* since 1981. The rock itself is roughly oval, rising as a seamount from the harbour floor to about 4 metres below the surface, with cavernous swim-throughs and a drop-off wall on one side that descends to roughly 35 metres.

Under local custom, Paul's Rock is a "taboo reef" — a protected site under indigenous ownership — which has left its fish population notably tame and abundant. Fish feeding is a regular part of the dive here, and the reef's best-known resident is a semi-tame flowery cod nicknamed "Baxter," who is known to escort divers around the site.

## What to Expect

Divers can expect close encounters with Coral Trout, Trevally, and Surgeonfish, along with regular turtle sightings. The swim-throughs and the drop-off wall give the dive some structural variety beyond the seamount's summit. Because the site's shallow top (around 4m) is accessible to snorkelers while its wall drops to around 35 metres, it suits a wide range of experience — but the deeper wall sections push this into advanced-diver territory.

## Practical Information

- **Depth Range**: Summit around 4 meters; wall drops to approximately 35 meters
- **Entry**: Boat/day-sail from Havannah Harbour (Sailaway Cruises and other Efate operators)
- **Best For**: Advanced divers wanting to work the full depth of the wall; the shallow summit also suits snorkelers on the same trip

## Marine Life Highlights

Coral Trout, Trevally, Surgeonfish, turtles, and the resident flowery cod known locally as "Baxter." Fish here are notably tame due to the site's taboo/protected status and regular fish feeding during dives.
