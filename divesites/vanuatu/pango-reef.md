## Overview

Pango Reef lies on the outskirts of Pango Point, south of Port Vila, reached by boat in under 30 minutes (Wannadive). Zentacle and Wannadive (which list the site as Pango Wall) describe a reef plain sloping gently into open blue water with hard and soft coral gardens, where the dive often ends near a "cathedral" rock formation on the peninsula.

Wannadive gives an average depth of 12 m and a maximum of 23 m, low current (under 1 knot) and visibility of 10-30 m; Zentacle lists about 12 m and rates it Beginner.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Near Pango Point, Efate, Vanuatu
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 23 meters

## Marine Life

Zentacle and Wannadive report hawksbill turtles, anemones, nudibranchs, mushroom corals and gorgonian fans.
