# Honeybone Drift

A drift dive over Honeybone Reef outside Port Vila Harbour on the southern side of Mele Bay, one of several reef sites regularly run by Efate dive operators alongside nearby Twin Bommies.

## Overview

Honeybone Reef sits a short boat ride (around 15 minutes) from Port Vila-area dive operators, and is regularly listed alongside Twin Bommies and Bob's Alley as one of the area's standard reef dives. Depths across the site are reported in the roughly 9–26 metre range, with soft and hard coral covering the reef.

Note on sourcing: only two independent sources were found describing this site in detail (Michael McFadyen's dive report and a secondary Vanuatu diving review blog), short of the three-source bar used elsewhere in this destination. The description below is kept conservative as a result, and this site has not been marked fully validated.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Outside Port Vila Harbour, Mele Bay, Efate, Vanuatu
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Drift dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 25 meters

## Marine Life

Soft and hard corals are reported across the site. Turtles and moray eels have been mentioned in secondary sources for this reef, but as these were not independently confirmed across enough sources to meet our validation bar, treat them as reported-but-unconfirmed rather than guaranteed sightings.

## Entry and Exit

Access is by dive boat from Port Vila-area operators, roughly 15 minutes from the dive base.

## Safety Considerations

Maintain good buddy contact and carry an SMB for signaling a pickup boat, standard practice for any drift dive in this area.
