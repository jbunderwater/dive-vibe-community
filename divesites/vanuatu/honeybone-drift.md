## Overview

Honeybone Reef is a boat dive about 15 minutes from the Port Vila dive base (Zentacle). Zentacle describes soft and hard corals in all colours and rates it Beginner with depths of 9-26 m; DiveBuddy lists a maximum of 25-27 m. Diving in Vanuatu lists it among the reef sites alongside Twin Bommies and Bobs Alley. No source found describes it as a drift dive.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Port Vila area, Efate, Vanuatu
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 25 meters

## Marine Life

Zentacle reports turtles and moray eels, plus soft and hard corals.
