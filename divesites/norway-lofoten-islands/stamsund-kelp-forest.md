## Stamsund Kelp Forest

Stamsund Kelp Forest is a shallow cold-water shore dive in Lofoten's kelp habitat near the fishing village of Stamsund on Vestvågøya.

## Overview

Stamsund is a Hurtigruten port on the south coast of Vestvågøya, roughly midway along the Lofoten chain. The shoreline here features the dense Laminaria hyperborea kelp forests that characterise much of Lofoten's shallow coastline, growing from just below the surface to the light limit at around 15–20 metres.

Diving within Lofoten's kelp forests is a documented regional activity: X-Ray Mag describes kelp macro diving outside Ballstad harbour — a separate village roughly 25km from Stamsund — as one of the highlights of the region, with kelp stems there providing habitat for nudibranchs (including Facelina auriculata and Polycera quadrilineata), amphipods, hermit crabs, and blade shrimp. Earlier drafts of this page attributed that Ballstad-specific species list to Stamsund; on re-audit we found no source connecting those observations to Stamsund itself, so the list has been removed from this entry. The kelp forest habitat type (Laminaria hyperborea, shading the seabed in a cathedral-like canopy) is a genuine regional description, but the specific critters are not confirmed here.

The maximum depth of this entry is approximately 14 metres. The site is rated Intermediate due to cold-water conditions: water temperature is 2–10°C depending on season, requiring drysuit competency year-round. Shore entry on rocky Norwegian coastline requires appropriate footwear and cold-water diving experience. No dive operator or published source has been found that names a specific site as "Stamsund Kelp Forest"; this remains a curated entry reflecting the general diving character of the Stamsund shoreline rather than a confirmed named dive site.
