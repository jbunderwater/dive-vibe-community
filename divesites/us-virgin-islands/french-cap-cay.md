## French Cap Cay

French Cap is an uninhabited cay about six miles south of St. Thomas, known for its rock pinnacle spires and exposed, rarely dived conditions.

## Overview

St. Thomas Scuba Diving describes rock spires shooting up from a 95-foot (29 meter) bottom to about 40 feet, and notes dive boats rarely visit and only run in perfect weather; Visit USVI describes cathedral-like rock spires to swim through, unprotected seas, rough conditions, and strong currents, and rates it advanced only. Dive Champ lists an advanced-level pinnacle dive with a maximum depth of 23 meters while its text says depths nearing 100 feet; that page itself states it is unverified. Sources therefore report maximum depth between about 23 and 30 meters. Difficulty also differs: JJ Divers recommends it for intermediate divers while Visit USVI and Dive Champ say advanced; this listing keeps Advanced because of the exposure and current.

## Site Information

- **Location**: US Virgin Islands, Caribbean
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Pinnacle/seamount
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters

## Marine Life

Green moray eels appear in all three sources. Visit USVI lists black triggerfish, sharks, sea turtles, and southern sennet; Dive Champ lists sharks, queen angelfish, barracuda, and lionfish; JJ Divers lists jacks, barracuda, reef fish, and octopus.
