## The Pinnacle (St. Croix)

The Pinnacle is the local name for the rock spires of Salt River Canyon's West Wall, rising toward the surface from the canyon's deep interior.

## Overview

This formation — also called "The Pinnacles" by operators — sits on the western edge of Salt River Canyon and is believed to have been shaped by an ancient, above-ground waterfall before the surrounding land submerged. The maze of rock spires is riddled with swim-throughs, cuts, ledges, overhangs, and recesses, offering a style of topography that is genuinely unusual among Caribbean dive sites. A mooring near the top of the formation sits in shallow water, with the spires themselves dropping toward the canyon's much deeper walls, which continue well beyond recreational limits.

## Site Information

- **Location**: US Virgin Islands, Caribbean
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Pinnacle/seamount
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 29 meters

## Marine Life

Rays are regularly seen gliding around the pinnacles, with schools of reef fish sheltering under ledges and creole wrasse, black durgon, and squirrelfish common along the formation. Deeper water brings jacks, large grouper, permit, and barracuda, along with occasional larger pelagics such as sharks.
