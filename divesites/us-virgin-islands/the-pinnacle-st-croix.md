## The Pinnacle (St. Croix)

The Pinnacle (St. Croix) is a listing whose identity is unclear in the sources: it is either a separate seamount or the same feature as Salt River's West Wall, also called The Pinnacles.

## Overview

Villa Margarita lists 'The Pinnacle' as a separate sea mount boat dive for advanced divers only, with a summit at about 90 feet (27 meters). GoToStCroix, in contrast, uses 'The Pinnacles' as another name for the Salt River Canyon West Wall and describes no separate seamount. The previous version of this page described the West Wall's swim-through topography under this name; that has been removed as it cannot be tied to this listing. Because only one source describes a separate Pinnacle and the maximum depth is not stated by any source, this listing is not marked validated. Check with a local St. Croix operator before relying on it.

## Site Information

- **Location**: US Virgin Islands, Caribbean
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Pinnacle/seamount
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 29 meters

## Marine Life

Villa Margarita reports swimming reef sharks, big jacks, and black coral at the separate Pinnacle seamount. No other source consulted confirms these at this specific site.
