## Rhone

**Data-placement note**: This wreck lies off Salt Island in the British Virgin Islands, not within US Virgin Islands waters. It is included in this destination's dataset because it is commonly paired with USVI charters, but it is geographically a BVI site and this record has been flagged for placement review rather than silently corrected, since removing it is outside the scope of this QA pass.

The RMS Rhone is a Royal Mail ship lost in the 1867 hurricane off Salt Island in the British Virgin Islands.

## Overview

Wikipedia and Divernet agree she was built by Millwall Iron Works in London and launched on 11 February 1865, with a length of about 310 feet (94-95 meters), a 40-foot beam, and 2,738 gross tons. Stormy Pirates agrees on 310 feet and describes an iron-hulled sail-steamer. Wikipedia and Divernet both date her loss to 29 October 1867, when a hurricane struck the Virgin Islands and wrecked her off Salt Island. Casualty counts are inconsistent: Wikipedia and Divernet both say 123 died, but Wikipedia also gives about 145 aboard with 25 surviving (about 120 lost) and Divernet says 147 aboard, so the exact figures are unresolved. The wreck lies in two sections. Reported depths differ: Wikipedia gives the bow at about 24 meters (80 feet), the stern at about 9 meters, and a maximum of 26 meters (85 feet); Divernet gives 27 meters (90 feet) at the bow and 6 meters at the stern; Stormy Pirates gives about 80 feet for the bow and 35 feet for the stern. Wikipedia and Divernet both note the wreck was used in the 1977 film The Deep. Wreck penetration requires proper training and equipment.

## Marine Life

Divernet lists snapper, squirrelfish, grouper, white-spotted filefish, coral banded shrimp, orange cup corals, hydroids, encrusting corals, small sea fans, and plumes at the wreck. No other source consulted named species at this site.
