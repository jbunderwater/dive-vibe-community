## Pantar Strait

Pantar Strait is the channel between Alor and Pantar islands that contains many of the area's named dive sites rather than a single dive site itself.

## Overview

Starfish.ch notes strong currents running through the strait, affecting sites such as Bama. The Occasional Traveller describes the waters around the strait as deep and cold. Named sites within the strait (for example Bama, Munaseli/Current Alley and the Cathedral) are the actual dive stops; no operator reviewed lists Pantar Strait itself as a dive site.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Pantar Strait, between Alor and Pantar islands, East Nusa Tenggara, Indonesia
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Drift dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters

## Marine Life

The Occasional Traveller reports pilot whales, melon-headed whales and spinner dolphins seen from the boat in mid-May, and says the June to November migration season may bring other whales. These are boat sightings, not dive observations.

## Dive Profile

No dive profile exists for the strait as a whole; see the individual sites.

## Safety Considerations

Strong currents and cold water. Always dive with a guide and carry a surface marker buoy; currents in the Alor area are described as strong at several sites.
