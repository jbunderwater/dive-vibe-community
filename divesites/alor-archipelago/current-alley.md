## Current Alley

Current Alley is a strong-current ridge and wall corner in the Pantar Strait, known for hammerhead and other large-animal encounters.

## Overview

Alor Tanapi lists Current Alley as another name for Yellow Corner and reserves it for advanced divers because of strong current; The Occasional Traveller describes Munaseli / Yellow Corner as about 20 to 30 meters depending on position on the wall, with a reef hook recommended and a drift after unhooking. Nautika Dive Alor describes Munaseli as a deep dive with a plunging wall in medium to strong current. Dive-the-World describes Current Alley as a channel and seamount in the strongest tidal flow of the strait. Emperor Divers calls it a drift dive with powerful currents. The 40 meter maximum depth is not confirmed by these sources.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Pantar Strait, Alor Archipelago, East Nusa Tenggara, Indonesia (also known as Yellow Corner or Munaseli)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Drift dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters

## Marine Life

Alor Tanapi reports barracuda, blacktip sharks, trevally and large Napoleon wrasse, with hammerheads possible at certain times and eagle rays occasional. The Occasional Traveller reports hammerhead sharks, mobula rays, blacktip sharks, giant barracuda and eagle rays. Dive-the-World gives a hammerhead peak window of September to November. Nautika lists grey reef and silvertip sharks for Munaseli.

## Dive Profile

Reef hooks are recommended by The Occasional Traveller. The dive becomes a drift once unhooked.

## Safety Considerations

Strong to ripping currents are reported by several sources; advanced divers only. Always dive with a guide and carry a surface marker buoy; currents in the Alor area are described as strong at several sites.
