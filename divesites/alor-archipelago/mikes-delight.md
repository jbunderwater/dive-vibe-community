## Mike's Delight

Mike's Delight is a coral slope with boulders, dived as a slow drift and known for sea snakes.

## Overview

Emperor Divers describes a slow drift over a rugged slope with boulders reaching 25 meters. Malaysia Asia calls it a healthy reef with mild to strong currents, and Girls That Scuba says the dive is about the corals.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Alor Archipelago, East Nusa Tenggara, Indonesia
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 25 meters

## Marine Life

Malaysia Asia reports black and white sea snakes, which it describes as harmless if not disturbed. Emperor Divers lists sea snakes, lobsters and lionfish, and Girls That Scuba mentions sea snakes, lobsters and boxfish.

## Dive Profile

A drift dive along the slope; no source reviewed gives a detailed route.

## Safety Considerations

Currents range from mild to strong. Do not touch sea snakes. Always dive with a guide and carry a surface marker buoy; currents in the Alor area are described as strong at several sites.
