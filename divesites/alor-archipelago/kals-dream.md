## Kal's Dream

Kal's Dream is a current-swept seamount with two pinnacles lying between Kepa and Pura islands.

## Overview

Starfish.ch (which calls the site Batu Kepa) places two underwater pinnacles northwest of Kepa Island with tops at 7 meters and 2 meters and low-lying corals, and warns that currents from the north hit the rocks head on, so conditions are often too wild to dive. Other operators call the site Batu Mandi; sources use both names. Dive-the-World describes it as a seamount between Kepa and Pura islands and Emperor Divers as a vibrant seamount known for colorful corals and swarming anthias. Maximum depth is not stated in the sources reviewed; the 35 meter figure is approximate.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Between Kepa and Pura islands, Alor Archipelago, East Nusa Tenggara, Indonesia
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Pinnacle/seamount
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 35 meters

## Marine Life

Starfish.ch reports barracudas, a whitetip shark, a marble ray, dogtooth tuna, three large Napoleon wrasses, trevallies, sweetlips, coral crouchers and yellow-spotted scorpionfish. Emperor Divers mentions barracudas, sharks and rays, and Dive-the-World lists anthias, fusiliers, surgeonfish, snappers, grey reef sharks and barracudas. Hammerheads are not confirmed here by these sources.

## Dive Profile

Plan the dive around the pinnacle tops and the prevailing current. Starfish.ch advises diving here only when conditions are right.

## Safety Considerations

Strong and variable currents can pick up mid-dive. Always dive with a guide and carry a surface marker buoy; currents in the Alor area are described as strong at several sites.
