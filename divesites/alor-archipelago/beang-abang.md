## Beang Abang

Beang Abang (Beangabang) is a black sand muck bay with volcanic hot springs on southern Pantar.

## Overview

Alor Tanapi describes a black sand bay on southern Pantar with volcanic hot springs. Starfish.ch lists three sites in Beangabang Bay (Hot Property, Ribbon Eel Run and The Lava Flow) and notes the water there is generally colder. DAN Alert Diver describes 90-minute dives on a black-sand slope off the beach. Depth is not stated in the sources reviewed; the 20 meter figure is approximate.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Southern Pantar Island, Alor Archipelago, East Nusa Tenggara, Indonesia (also known as Beangabang)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Muck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 20 meters

## Marine Life

Alor Tanapi reports ribbon eels, ghost pipefish, octopus, nudibranchs and hairy frogfish. DAN Alert Diver reports coconut octopus, mating bubble shells, juvenile lionfish and hellfire anemones with harlequin crabs. Emperor Divers also lists wonderpus, ghost pipefish and nudibranchs at Beang Abang.

## Dive Profile

Slow muck-style dives over the black sand slope; see the sources for site-by-site detail.

## Safety Considerations

Colder water than the rest of the archipelago is reported. Always dive with a guide and carry a surface marker buoy; currents in the Alor area are described as strong at several sites.
