## The Cathedral

The Cathedral (Motolang) is a coral-covered rock wall with jumbled boulders and a swim-through, named for its cathedral-like underwater rock formations.

## Overview

Starfish.ch describes huge jumbled rocks and boulders and a steep wall covered with hard and soft corals and sponges, with a swim-through cave at about 25 meters. Alor Tanapi reports a swim-through at around 30 meters and highlights pristine, dense coral cover; it recommends a 5 mm wetsuit for the cooler southern water. Emperor Divers describes walls, caves and a swim-through, and calls it a good spot for pelagics without naming species. Dive-the-World describes an impressive submerged cavern or overhang. Sources therefore report the swim-through depth as between roughly 25 and 30 meters. No source reviewed states a verified maximum depth for the site; the 35 meter figure is approximate.

## Site Information

- **Location**: South Alor / Pantar Strait area, East Nusa Tenggara, Indonesia (also known as Motolang)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 35 meters

## Marine Life

Starfish.ch reports Bargibant's pygmy seahorses, a Pontohi seahorse in Halimeda algae, a bluering octopus, two frogfish species, and a large tawny nurseshark (*Nebrius ferrugineus*) in the cave. No other source reviewed confirms species at this site.

## Dive Profile

Descend along the wall toward the swim-through. A torch is useful for the cave passage. Exact route and timings were not found in the sources reviewed.

## Safety Considerations

Depths in the 25 to 35 meter range require advanced training and careful gas planning. Only enter the swim-through if the exit is visible. Always dive with a guide and carry a surface marker buoy; currents in the Alor area are described as strong at several sites.
