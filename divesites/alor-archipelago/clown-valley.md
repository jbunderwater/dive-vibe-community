## Clown Valley

Clown Valley (Apuri) is an anemone-covered reef off Pura Island, reported by divers as having an exceptionally dense concentration of sea anemones.

## Overview

Starfish.ch describes the site as astonishing because "every surface is covered in sea anemones", and notes bubbles rising from the ground, which the author links to Pura's volcanic nature. Malaysia Asia places Clown Valley on the southeast side of Pura Island and calls it the largest concentration of sea anemones in the world, a claim that is also repeated by Emperor Divers and Girls That Scuba as a reputation rather than a measured fact. Dive-the-World describes it as a sandy slope with an extensive garden of pink-tipped anemones. Depth figures are not given by most operators; one charter listing gives a range of 3 to 25 meters, so the 20 meter maximum here is approximate.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Pura Island, Alor Archipelago, East Nusa Tenggara, Indonesia (also known as Apuri or Anemone Valley)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 20 meters

## Marine Life

Starfish.ch and Malaysia Asia both report Clark's anemonefish (*Amphiprion clarkii*) and tomato anemonefish (*Amphiprion frenatus*) living in the anemones. Starfish.ch also mentions two yellow *Miamira miamira* nudibranchs in the shallows and sea apples; Malaysia Asia also photographed a sea apple. Other species named by single sources are not repeated here.

## Dive Profile

Operators describe a shallow dive among the anemones; no source reviewed gives a specific route. Take care with buoyancy, as the seabed is largely covered in living anemones.

## Safety Considerations

Currents in the surrounding strait can be strong. Always dive with a guide and carry a surface marker buoy; currents in the Alor area are described as strong at several sites.
