## Mucky Mosque

Mucky Mosque is a muck dive on a black sand and rubble slope in Kalabahi Bay, regarded by several operators as one of Alor's best macro sites.

## Overview

Dive-the-World describes a steep black sand and rubble slope to 27 meters with some man-made debris. Starfish.ch notes long ropes hung with hydroids and small corals hanging from the shore, and says the site suits night dives. Nautika Dive Alor lists it as Masjid Ampera, good for muck and night dives. DAN Alert Diver describes a steep sand, mud and rubble slope in front of a mosque.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Kalabahi Bay, Alor, East Nusa Tenggara, Indonesia (also known as Masjid Ampera)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Muck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 27 meters

## Marine Life

Starfish.ch reports Rhinopias scorpionfishes, leaffishes, ghostpipefishes, frogfishes including a giant frogfish, seahorses, dragonets, crabs, shrimps and many nudibranchs. Underwater Journal reports, from a night dive, lacy and paddle-flap rhinopias (*Rhinopias aphanes*, *R. eschmeyeri*), spiny devilfish, painted frogfish, a stargazer, a crocodile snake eel and a Spanish dancer. DAN Alert Diver mentions blue-ringed and wonderpus octopuses and iridescent bobtail squid, and Dive-the-World lists bobtail and pygmy squid.

## Dive Profile

A slope dive with typical muck-style slow searching; night dives are popular according to the sources above.

## Safety Considerations

Silty bottom: poor buoyancy and finning reduce visibility. Handle no animals. Entry details were not given in the sources reviewed.
