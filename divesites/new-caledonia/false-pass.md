# False Pass

A drift dive through a break in the barrier reef near Poum, 410 km north of Noumea. Pristine, rarely-dived reef with numerous caves, tunnels, holes, and bays, a forest of very large colourful gorgonian sea fans, and turtle sightings.

## Overview

False Pass is located near Poum at the far northern tip of Grande Terre, approximately 410 kilometres north of Noumea, reached by boat from Malabou Beach Resort. This remote location -- well off the tourist circuit -- means the reef is in pristine condition and rarely dived.

The site is a break in the barrier reef, dived as a drift, with "numerous caves, tunnels, holes and bays" per Michael McFadyen's firsthand dive log -- the source of most site-specific detail available for this remote location. The reef drops from the surface to approximately 30 meters. The reef is decorated with a forest of large gorgonian sea fans in vivid colours. Turtles are regularly photographed here.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Near Poum, northern tip of Grande Terre, New Caledonia (410 km N of Noumea)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 20+ meters
- **Water Temperature**: 22-27 degrees C
- **Current**: Moderate (drift dive)
- **Best Time**: No site-specific seasonal data found; October conditions reported by McFadyen were 24C with 20m+ visibility

## Marine Life

Turtles are confirmed at this site via McFadyen's photo log. The gorgonian sea fan forest is a defining visual feature. No other species could be confirmed as specific to False Pass during this QA pass -- the broader Poum reef system (visited by the local operator, Pacific Plongee) is known for marlin and up to 11 shark species, but these are not documented as occurring at False Pass specifically, so they are not listed here as site sightings.

## Dive Profile

Enter upstream of the pass and drift with the current through the reef's caves, tunnels, and holes. The site drops from the surface to approximately 30 meters. Admire the gorgonian sea fan forest and watch for turtles, before ascending to the reef top for the safety stop.

## Entry and Exit

Access requires a dive boat from Poum or a liveaboard -- this is an extremely remote dive site. Entry by giant stride. Exit by SMB pickup after the drift.

## Tips and Recommendations

- The caves, tunnels, and holes are the centrepiece of the dive -- bring a wide-angle lens with strobes for the gorgonian forest
- This site requires significant travel from Noumea -- typically accessed on multi-day dive trips to the north via Malabou Beach Resort
- The remote location means the reef condition is outstanding -- among the best in New Caledonia
- The gorgonian sea fans are very large and colourful -- some of the finest specimens in the country

## Safety Considerations

The reef's caves, tunnels, and holes create overhead-environment sections -- maintain visual contact with exit points throughout. The 30-meter maximum depth requires careful gas management. Current through the pass is moderate but consistent -- stay with the group. The remote location means medical facilities are far away; dive conservatively.
