## Ambon

Ambon Bay in Maluku Province is a muck diving destination built around black sandy slopes and jetty sites, with wall, cave, arch and wreck dives on the south coast and in the bay.

## Description

Neptune Liveaboards describes Laha as Ambon's most famous muck diving spot, and Dive the World calls it the best macro dive site in Ambon. Most bay sites are sandy slopes with rubble, stones, sponges and scattered coral, often beside working jetties. Starfish and Bottles Up Diving group several of them (Laha, Twilight Zone, Rhino City) along the same north-bay stretch of coast, so site names and boundaries differ between operators. The psychedelic frogfish (Histiophryne psychedelica) was described in 2009 from this area according to Wikipedia, but a ScubaBoard post dated 10 November 2026 reports no verified sightings since 2018.

Outside the muck sites, the database includes the Duke of Sparta wreck (the SS Aquila, bombed in 1958), Hukurila Cave and Pintu Kota on the south coast, Pantai Nama (listed as Nama Point) and Amahusu on the bay slopes.

### Diving Opportunities

- **Muck Diving**: Laha, Rhino City, Twilight Zone, Air Manis and the Psychedelic Frogfish Site label (a historic location within the Laha complex)
- **Wreck Diving**: Duke of Sparta Wreck, with the stern at about 12-15 m and the bow at about 35 m or deeper depending on the source
- **Cave and Arch Diving**: Hukurila Cave (chimney entrance and swim-throughs) and Pintu Kota (large arch at about 15-17 m), both on the south coast reached by boat
- **Wall and Slope Diving**: Nama Point (Pantai Nama wall and slope) and Amahusu Reef

### Accessibility

- **Dive Operators**: Operators named in the sources reviewed include Maluku Divers/Spice Island Divers at Laha (Wikipedia, ScubaBoard) and Dive Into Ambon (search results); confirm current operations directly.
- **South Coast Sites**: Indo Pacific Images describes Pintu Kota and Hukurila as day boat trips, roughly an hour along the south coast from the harbour entrance.
- **Not verified**: Flights, hyperbaric facilities, water temperatures, best-season and seasonal sighting claims were not confirmed against the sources reviewed and are not stated here; check current local information.

### Marine Life

Marine life is documented per site on each site page. No destination-wide species list is given here because the sources reviewed report species at specific sites.

## Additional Information

- **Currency**: Indonesian Rupiah (IDR)
- **Language**: Indonesian (Bahasa Indonesia) and Ambonese Malay
