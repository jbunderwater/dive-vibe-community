## Psychedelic Frogfish Site

Psychedelic Frogfish Site is a descriptive label for the Laha I area of Ambon Bay, where the psychedelic frogfish (Histiophryne psychedelica) was photographed in 2008 and its holotype collected; recent sightings are not verified.

## Overview

"Psychedelic Frogfish Site" is not a name used for a distinct dive site in the dive-operator pages reviewed (Starfish, Bottles Up Diving, Dive the World, Neptune Liveaboards). It marks the historic location of the species' rediscovery within the Laha complex, which is described in the Laha entry.

Per Wikipedia, the species was first collected in June 1992 in a shipment of fishes from Bali to the Dallas Aquarium, and rediscovered in the wild in January 2008, when guides Buck Randolph, Fitrie Randolph and Toby Fadirsyair of Maluku Divers photographed one at Ambon. The holotype was collected on 2 April 2008 at the Laha I dive site at Maluku Divers, and the species was described by Theodore Pietsch, R. J. Arnold and D. J. Hall in Copeia in 2009. Wikipedia reports the fish at 5 to 7 m depth, about 20 m from shore, on coral rubble at a "muck" site with few corals, and a length of up to about 15 cm. Wikipedia describes a "remarkable pigment pattern of white swirling stripes". Starfish states it was found at Twilight Zone, which sits in the same stretch of coast.

Sighting status: a ScubaBoard thread has a February 2019 post reporting one tiny individual on coral (no site named). In a post dated 10 November 2026, a diver at Spice Island Divers reports that the dive manager is "not aware of ANY verified sightings since 2018" and that a recent operator photo was a re-post of a 2018 image. One poster in the thread says the species spends most of its time deeper and only comes up to recreational depths during spawning; this is a single forum claim. Treat any encounter as exceptional, and note that posters in the thread discourage publicising exact locations.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Treat a sighting as an unlikely bonus rather than an expectation.
- Photograph without touching or stressing the animal if one is found.

## Safety Considerations

The depth reported by Wikipedia is shallow (5 to 7 m). Use a surface marker and watch for boat traffic in Ambon Bay.
