## Pintu Kota

Pintu Kota ("Gate of the City") is a south-coast dive built around a very large underwater arch at about 15-17 m, with sea fans and gorgonians on the surrounding wall and reef.

## Overview

Starfish translates the name as "Gate of the City", notes a large rock forming an arch above water, and describes a dive mostly on the arch, with a steep slope on the other side. It gives the arch at about 15-17 m and says the dive finishes at the arch's highest point at around 7 m. Bottles Up Diving describes a large archway at 17 m, reef, strong surface currents and visibility that "can be an amazing 30+ metres". Indo Pacific Images describes archways in a small headland with a healthy reef and clear water, reached by boat roughly an hour along the south coast from the harbour entrance. Dive the World describes an outer-coast wall with rock formations, cracks and overhangs that "drops to over 30 metres and is encrusted with a vibrant array of sea fans". Divernet calls it a very large arch.

Sources characterise the site differently: arch (Starfish, Bottles Up Diving, Indo Pacific Images, Divernet), wall (Dive the World, Neptune Liveaboards), and muck diving with large arches (Bluewater Dive Travel). The database lists it as cave/cavern on the strength of the arch descriptions. The 30 m listed below reflects the Dive the World wall figure; the arch itself is at about 15-17 m.

## Site Information

- **Location**: South coast of Ambon Island, about an hour by boat from the harbour entrance
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Cave/cavern
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 30+ meters reported (Bottles Up Diving)
- **Current**: Strong surface currents reported (Bottles Up Diving)

## Marine Life

- **Starfish**: sea fans and gorgonians; small critters as well as wide-angle subjects.
- **Bottles Up Diving**: Napoleon wrasse and larger fish, sometimes manta rays.
- **Dive the World**: sea fans, gorgonians, black corals, occasional tuna and barracuda, moray eels, lobsters and nudibranchs.
- **Divernet**: boxer crabs and tiny nudibranchs found by guides.
- **Indo Pacific Images**: a superb fan beneath the archway (photo caption).

## Dive Profile

Starfish describes descending to the arch at 15-17 m and finishing at its highest point at around 7 m. Dive the World describes the wall continuing beyond 30 m, so watch depth carefully.

## Entry and Exit

Reached by boat on the south coast. Confirm sea conditions and the mooring or drop-off plan with your operator.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Starfish and Bottles Up Diving both recommend it for wide-angle photography and note clear water.
- Often visited together with Hukurila Cave on south-coast day trips (Indo Pacific Images).

## Safety Considerations

Bottles Up Diving reports strong surface currents, and the slope and wall continue deeper than the arch. Use a surface marker and monitor depth throughout.
