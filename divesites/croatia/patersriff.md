## Patersriff

Patersriff ("Father's Reef" in German) lies off the east coast of Prvić Island in the Kvarner, just north of Kap Samonjin. German-language dive guides list it as a deeper reef known chiefly for its red gorgonian fans, recommended for experienced divers because of its depth.

## Overview

According to reiseinfo-kroatien.com, Patersriff drops steeply to around 40 metres off the east coast of Prvić. The site's main draw is a dense stand of red gorgonian sea fans (Paramuricea clavata) alongside red horn coral ("Keulen-Seescheiden" — club sea squirts) on the deeper sections. Current is usually light and visibility is described as excellent, which is why the site is popular with underwater photographers. Because of the depth, the source recommends the dive only for experienced divers.

## Site Information

- **Location**: East coast of Prvić Island, Kvarner, Croatia
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 25 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Reported as excellent
- **Current**: Usually light
- **Best Time**: May to October

## Marine Life

Red gorgonian sea fans (Paramuricea clavata) and red horn coral / club sea squirts are specifically reported at this site by reiseinfo-kroatien.com. No other site-specific species reports were found; beyond the gorgonians, regional Adriatic/Mediterranean fauna applies.

## Dive Profile

The dive descends steadily to the 40-metre maximum off the east coast of Prvić, with the gorgonian and horn coral growth concentrated on the deeper sections. Given the depth, plan the profile conservatively with adequate gas reserves and a computer-managed ascent. Ascend slowly and complete a safety stop at 5 metres.

## Entry and Exit

Dive boats from Baška (Krk) or other Kvarner operators serving Prvić Island access this site. Entry by giant stride. Deploy an SMB on ascent for boat pickup.

## Tips and Recommendations

- This is an advanced-level dive due to its 40-metre depth — verify certification and gas planning before booking
- The red gorgonian fans and horn coral are the highlight — a torch brings out their true colour
- Light current and excellent visibility make this a favourite with underwater photographers
- The site name is a reminder of the rich German-language diving tradition in this part of Croatia

## Safety Considerations

This is a deep dive to 40 metres and is recommended only for experienced, advanced-certified divers. Plan gas and no-decompression limits carefully. Carry an SMB and dive with a buddy.

## Photography

The red gorgonian fans and horn coral are the signature macro/close-up subjects at this site, and the reported excellent visibility and light current make it a favoured spot for underwater photographers.
