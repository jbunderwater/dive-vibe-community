## Kap Tanki

Kap Tanki, also listed by local operators as "Sika Tanki," is a wall and canyon dive on Sv. Grgur island in the Kvarner Gulf near Rab — not in the Dugi Otok/Zadar Archipelago as an earlier version of this page claimed. That claim placed the site over 200 km from its actual coordinates and has been removed. Kron Diving, a Rab-based operator, places it just beyond the neighbouring Canyon dive site on the same island.

## Overview

Kron Diving describes this as a wall dive with canyon features and a small tunnel, known for current and a colourful wall, with depths from about 5 to 40 metres; divers anchor in 8-10 metres and follow a figure-8 route along the wall. A separate ScubaGo listing for "Sika Tanki" corroborates an average working depth around 25 metres with a maximum of 40 metres. Marine life reported by Kron Diving includes sea slugs (nudibranchs), scorpionfish, octopus, anemones (sometimes hosting spider crabs or harlequin shrimp), and sponges and gorgonians on the outer wall.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Sv. Grgur island, Kvarner Gulf, near Rab, Croatia (beyond the Canyon dive site)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Not confirmed by any source
- **Current**: The site is specifically noted for current — check conditions with the operator
- **Best Time**: May to October (general Kvarner season)

## Marine Life

Kron Diving reports nudibranchs (sea slugs), scorpionfish, octopus, anemones (occasionally hosting spider crabs or harlequin shrimp), sponges, and gorgonians on the outer wall. These are the only site-specific marine life reports found.

## Dive Profile

Anchor at 8-10 metres and descend along the anchor line. The shallow section (10-15m) includes a small tunnel; the outer wall then drops to a maximum of around 40 metres. The typical route follows a figure-8 pattern around the canyon and wall features.

## Entry and Exit

Reached by boat from Rab to Sv. Grgur island, just past the Canyon site. Entry by giant stride or back roll, descending on the anchor line given the current at this site.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Current is a defining feature of this site — follow the anchor line and stay with your guide
- Check anemones closely for spider crabs or harlequin shrimp
- Combine with the neighbouring Canyon dive on the same island for a two-site day from Rab

## Safety Considerations

This site is specifically noted for current by the operator that dives it — confirm conditions before entering and follow the anchor line on descent and ascent. Always carry an SMB and dive with a buddy.

## Photography

The colourful outer wall (sponges and gorgonians) and the small tunnel section offer wide-angle subjects; nudibranchs, scorpionfish, and anemone-dwelling crustaceans are reported macro subjects.
