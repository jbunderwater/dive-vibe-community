## Zabodaski

Zabodaski is a small islet in the Lošinj archipelago, Kvarner Gulf, dived from Mali Lošinj-based operators. It combines two shallow reefs near a small lighthouse anchorage with a dramatic vertical wall on its western side that drops almost from the surface to over 40 metres.

## Overview

Near the small lighthouse, an anchorage sits over a flat bottom at about 5 metres, with two nearby reefs: a shallower one at roughly 4–11 metres (including a small cave) and a second, about 50 metres away, ranging 14–22 metres — both reported as good for beginners and those developing navigation skills. Separately, the wall on the island's west side descends steeply and near-vertically from the surface to around 42 metres, which is the site's defining, more advanced feature. Divers report finding fragments of ancient pottery on the sandy bottom near the sheltered anchorage, reflecting the site's long use as a safe anchorage.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Zabodaski islet, Lošinj archipelago, Kvarner Gulf, Croatia
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 42 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Good, with minimal current reported
- **Current**: Generally minimal
- **Best Time**: May to October

## Marine Life

Local dive-site descriptions report moray eels, lobster, red mullet (Mullus surmuletus), and dusky grouper (Epinephelus marginatus) along the wall, with small lobster noted at depths from about 25 metres. Fragments of ancient pottery are reported on the sandy bottom near the anchorage. These are site-specific reports rather than a generic regional list.

## Dive Profile

For the shallow programme, explore the two reefs near the lighthouse anchorage (4–11m with a small cave, then 14–22m). For the wall dive, descend along the near-vertical west-side wall, watching for moray, grouper, and lobster in crevices; deeper lobster sightings are reported from ~25m down. Ascend along the wall/reef with a safety stop.

## Entry and Exit

Dive boats from Mali Lošinj-based operators serve this site. Entry by giant stride or back roll near the lighthouse anchorage or along the wall. Deploy an SMB on ascent, particularly for the open-water wall dive.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Choose the shallow twin-reef profile for less experienced divers, or the west wall for an advanced deep dive
- Watch crevices along the wall for moray eel, dusky grouper, and lobster
- Scan the sandy bottom near the anchorage for ancient pottery fragments (observe only, do not remove — cultural heritage protections apply)
- The small cave on the shallow reef is a nice add-on for the beginner-level dive

## Safety Considerations

This site has two distinct profiles: a beginner-friendly shallow reef pair (4–22m) and a much more demanding vertical wall to ~42m. Confirm with your operator which profile is planned, and treat the wall dive as an advanced/deep dive requiring appropriate training, gas planning, and an SMB.

## Photography

The near-vertical wall with gorgonian and coral growth is a strong wide-angle subject; the shallow reefs and small cave suit close-focus wide-angle and macro work, with grouper and moray as reliable subjects along the wall.
