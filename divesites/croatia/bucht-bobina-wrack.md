## Bucht Bobina (Wrack)

Bucht Bobina ("Bay of Bobina") is a bay on the southeastern side of the Istrian peninsula near Brestova — not Krk Island. Unterwasserweb.de's Kvarner-coast dive catalogue lists it as "Segelschiff in der Bucht Bobina" (sailing ship in Bobina Bay), an unidentified wooden sailing vessel wreck. **No vessel name, build date, tonnage, or sinking narrative could be confirmed by any source, and only one source describing the wreck was found — this history should be treated as unconfirmed.**

## Overview

According to unterwasserweb.de, the wreck is the collapsed remains of a wooden sailing ship, with its hull disintegrated and equipment parts scattered across the seabed nearby. From the boat, divers descend into the bay to around 20 metres, with a reported maximum depth of 30 metres. Visibility is described as good, current as generally light, and the site is considered suitable for all categories of trained scuba divers — not restricted to advanced-only. No details on the vessel's identity, age, or cause of sinking were available; do not treat the wreck's history as established fact.

## Dive Profile

Descend from the boat into the bay to around 20 metres before reaching the wreck remains, with a reported maximum depth of 30 metres. Explore the scattered, decayed hull and equipment debris. The wreck is described as being in a collapsed/disintegrated state rather than an intact structure. Ascend slowly and complete a safety stop at 5 metres.

## Entry and Exit

Dive boats from operators serving the Brestova/southeastern Istria coast access this site (corrected from the previous "Krk-area operators," which reflected the site's incorrect prior location). Entry by giant stride. Ascend via the anchor line and deploy an SMB for open-water portions of the ascent.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Treat any claims about the wreck's identity or history with caution — no vessel name, date, or cause of sinking has been confirmed by independent sources
- A torch is useful given the collapsed, low-lying nature of the wreckage
- Good reported visibility makes this a pleasant, relaxed wreck dive suitable for a range of certification levels
- A 5–7 mm wetsuit is recommended in spring and autumn

## Safety Considerations

Depth is within standard recreational limits (reported max 30 m). The wreck is collapsed rather than intact, so there is no significant penetration hazard, but divers should still avoid disturbing the fragile, decayed structure. Always carry an SMB and dive within your certification level.
