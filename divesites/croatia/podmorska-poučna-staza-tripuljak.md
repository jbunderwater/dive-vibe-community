## podmorska poučna staza Tripuljak

"Podmorska poučna staza" translates from Croatian as "underwater educational trail." This one is located in **Tripuljak cove, inside Telašćica Nature Park on the island of Dugi Otok, Zadar County** — this corrects an earlier version of this page, which incorrectly placed the site in the Šibenik area; three independent sources (a Zadar regional news outlet, the official Zadar tourism portal, and a nature/heritage magazine) consistently place it in Telašćica park on Dugi Otok. The trail was opened on 23 June 2017. At approximately 200 metres long and a maximum depth of just a couple of metres, this shallow, marked route is designed for snorkelling, beginner diving experiences, and marine education, offering an accessible introduction to the Adriatic's underwater world within one of Croatia's best-known protected marine landscapes.

## Overview

The Tripuljak underwater trail is a marked, roughly 200-metre shallow-water route, typically completed in about 30 minutes, through a nearshore habitat inside Telašćica Nature Park. Multiple-language audio guides and educational signage along the trail identify key species and habitats; equipment rental is available at the trailhead for visitors without their own mask and snorkel. Sources describe the trail as passing over habitat supporting fish, sea urchins, algae, sea sponges, crustaceans, and mollusks, and note that archaeological artefacts can also be seen along the route. The trail was developed with partial funding from the SEA-Med project ("Development of Sustainable Economic Activities in Marine Protected Areas"), involving Udruga Sunce and WWF. The site is suitable for all ages and abilities who are comfortable swimming with a mask and snorkel.

Sources describe general habitat categories present along the trail — fish, sea urchins, algae, sea sponges, crustaceans, and mollusks — without naming specific species observed at this location, so none are asserted here beyond those general categories.

## Dive Profile

The entire trail is conducted at very shallow depth (a couple of metres or less). Snorkellers and beginning scuba divers can follow the trail markers through the educational route at their own pace, typically completing it in around 30 minutes with the aid of an audio guide. The extreme shallowness makes this a no-pressure experience with essentially unlimited time. No safety stop is required.

## Entry and Exit

Entry is from shore at Tripuljak cove within Telašćica Nature Park. Entry is via wade-in or shallow water entry. The calm, shallow conditions require no special entry technique. Equipment rental is available at the trailhead.

## Tips and Recommendations

- An excellent introduction to Mediterranean marine life for families, children, and first-time snorkellers
- Use the multi-language audio guide and read the trail markers as you go — the educational value is a key part of the experience
- An underwater camera or housing is ideal here — the shallow depth and calm conditions make for easy photography
- Combine with a visit to the rest of Telašćica Nature Park, including its saltwater lake and cliffs
- Rent a mask and snorkel at the trailhead if you don't have your own gear

## Safety Considerations

The shallow maximum depth makes this one of Croatia's safest sites. No decompression considerations apply. Standard snorkelling safety applies — stay aware of boat traffic in the area and use appropriate surface signals.

## Photography

The bright, naturally lit shallow water makes this an outstanding photography site. Macro and close-focus shots of the trail's marine life and educational markers are the highlight, with clear ambient light throughout.
