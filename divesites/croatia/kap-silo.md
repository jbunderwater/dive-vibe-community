## Kap Silo

Kap Silo (Kap Šilo) is the southern tip of Prvić Island in the Kvarner — not to be confused with the village of Silo on Krk. German-language dive guides describe it as a pyramid-shaped reef pinnacle that steps down into very deep water, making it a destination for divers with depth experience.

## Overview

According to reiseinfo-kroatien.com, the dive begins around 12 metres at the southern point of Prvić, where a pyramid-shaped reef falls away in stages to as much as 104 metres at its deepest — well beyond recreational limits (Croatia's national recreational dive limit is 40 metres). Divers report abundant scorpionfish and two-banded seabream, along with striking red gorgonian fans that contrast with the turquoise water. The source explicitly recommends the site only for experienced divers, citing both the depth and often stronger current at this exposed point.

## Marine Life

Reiseinfo-kroatien.com specifically reports scorpionfish and two-banded seabream in abundance, plus red gorgonian fans on the deeper reef. No other site-specific species reports were found.

## Dive Profile

The dive begins at around 12 metres at the southern tip of Prvić and follows the pyramid-shaped reef down in stages. Recreational divers should plan to turn around well within Croatia's 40-metre recreational limit — the formation itself continues to depths suited only to technical divers. This is not a beginner profile; careful gas planning and depth discipline are essential.

## Entry and Exit

Dive boats from Baška (Krk) or other Kvarner operators serving Prvić Island access this site. Entry by giant stride or back roll. Deploy an SMB for ascent and boat pickup, especially given the current at this exposed cape.

## Tips and Recommendations

- This is an advanced, depth-focused dive — not suitable for divers without deep-diving experience
- Confirm current conditions with the boat operator before descending; the cape is often exposed to stronger current
- The red gorgonian fans on the deeper reef are the visual highlight
- Strict depth and gas discipline is required given how far the formation continues below recreational limits

## Safety Considerations

This site's defining hazard is depth: the reef steps down well past recreational limits, and current is often stronger here than at sheltered sites. Only experienced, advanced-certified divers should dive this site, with a dive computer, adequate gas reserves, and a clear turnaround plan. Carry an SMB and dive with a buddy.

## Photography

The red gorgonian fans against the turquoise water are the standout subject reported for this site, best suited to divers comfortable working at depth.
