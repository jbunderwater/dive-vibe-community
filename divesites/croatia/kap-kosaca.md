## Kap Kosaca

Kap Kosaca (Rt Kosača) is a wall dive at the northern end of Sv. Grgur, a small former-prison island in the Kvarner Gulf near Rab — not in the Dugi Otok/Zadar Archipelago as an earlier version of this page claimed. That claim placed the site over 200 km from its actual coordinates and has been removed. Two independent Rab-based sources — Kron Diving and the ScubaGo/MyDiveGuide listing "Rt Kosača" — both describe the same site: a short boat ride from Rab to the start of Grgur island, beginning on a shallow plateau and dropping via a gently sloping wall.

## Overview

Kron Diving describes Kosaca as a wall dive on multiple terraces, letting divers choose their working depth between roughly 5 and 30 metres. The ScubaGo/MyDiveGuide listing independently describes the same layout: a shallow plateau at about 5 metres that descends south-west to a wall gently dropping to around 30 metres, and both sources call it a good site for less experienced divers. Marine life reported by Kron Diving includes nudibranchs, octopus, lobster, sea bream, salema, and grouper, with octopus often found near resident grouper.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Northern end of Sv. Grgur island, Kvarner Gulf, near Rab, Croatia
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Not confirmed by any source
- **Current**: Not specifically documented for this site
- **Best Time**: May to October (general Kvarner season)

## Marine Life

Kron Diving reports nudibranchs, octopus, lobster, sea bream, salema, and grouper at this site, with octopus commonly seen near grouper. These are the only site-specific marine life reports found; do not assume other species without further confirmation.

## Dive Profile

Enter and descend to the shallow plateau at roughly 5 metres, then follow the terraced wall south-west and down to a maximum of about 30 metres, choosing a working depth to suit experience level. Ascend back up the terraces and complete a safety stop in the shallows.

## Entry and Exit

Reached by boat from Rab, roughly a short crossing to the start of Sv. Grgur island. Entry by giant stride or back roll from the dive boat.

## Tips and Recommendations

- The terraced wall structure lets divers of different experience levels choose an appropriate depth on the same dive
- Watch for octopus near the resident grouper
- Combine with the nearby Canyon or Sika Tanki sites on Sv. Grgur for a varied day from Rab

## Safety Considerations

Standard recreational wall-diving precautions apply: monitor depth against the terraced profile, carry an SMB, and dive with a buddy. Current strength at this specific site is not documented by available sources — confirm conditions with the dive operator before entry.

## Photography

No site-specific photography notes were found beyond the marine life listed above (nudibranchs, octopus, sea bream, grouper), which suggests macro and fish-portrait opportunities on the terraced wall.
