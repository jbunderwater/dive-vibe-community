## Krivica

Uvala Krivica is a sheltered bay on the southern coast of Lošinj island in the Kvarner Gulf, used both as a natural anchorage and as a dive site by operators in the Lošinj area.

## Overview

The bay itself is shallow (roughly 3–10m, well protected from the Bura and eastern winds), while the associated dive site descends further over mixed rocky terrain. Sources disagree somewhat on the details: one dive-site database lists a maximum depth around 30 metres with 10–25 metre visibility rated for intermediate divers, while another lists an average depth around 35 metres with lower (5–10 metre) visibility. Given this conflict, treat depth as up to roughly 30 metres and visibility as variable rather than reliably excellent.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Southern coast, Lošinj Island, Kvarner Gulf, Croatia
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Reported as variable; sources disagree (5–10m vs. 10–25m)
- **Current**: Not clearly confirmed; the bay itself is well sheltered
- **Best Time**: May to September

## Marine Life

No site-specific marine life reports were found for Krivica. Regional Kvarner/Adriatic fauna applies, but no species are named here since sources did not confirm specific sightings at this site.

## Dive Profile

Descend from the sheltered bay area out toward the rocky slope, working down to a maximum of roughly 30 metres. Given the conflicting visibility reports, plan for the possibility of reduced visibility (5–10m) rather than assuming the clearest Adriatic conditions. Ascend slowly with a safety stop.

## Entry and Exit

Dive boats from Lošinj-area operators serve this site. Entry by giant stride or back roll. Deploy an SMB on ascent for boat pickup.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Confirm current visibility conditions with your operator before the dive — reports vary between sources
- The sheltered bay makes this a good choice when open-water sites are affected by wind/swell
- Combine with another Lošinj-area site for a full day programme

## Safety Considerations

Standard intermediate parameters, but confirm actual depth and visibility with your operator on the day given the conflicting data found in research. Carry an SMB and dive with a buddy.

## Photography

Photographic conditions depend on which visibility report proves accurate on the day — plan for both wide-angle (if clear) and closer-range macro (if visibility is reduced) subjects.
