## Wreck Tihany

A wreck lies at the foot of the rock Školjić at the entrance to Unije port (Lošinj area of the Kvarner), resting at approximately 33–35 metres depth. **Sources disagree, materially, on the vessel's identity**, and this has not been resolved: the dive is listed as "Wreck Tihany" by Underwater Photography and ScubaSpace (which give a length of ~60 metres and a sinking date of 12 February 1917 or, per one aggregated account, 1912), but Adriadiving.com states the wreck at this exact location (Školjić, Unije) is actually the *Etnea* (also ~60 m, sunk 1917), and explicitly says *Tihany* is a separate "twin/sister ship" of the Etnea lying in Montenegrin waters, "often mixed up" with it. A third, less reliable aggregated account describes a "Tihany" built in Trieste in 1908, only 45 metres long, sunk 12 February 1917 while under tow — a length figure that conflicts with the ~60 m figure given by the other sources. Given this unresolved three-way identity conflict, no vessel name, build history, exact length, or exact sinking date is asserted here as fact; only the physical location and depth are treated as reasonably confirmed. One detail is corroborated across sources regardless of which name is correct: ancient amphora fragments have been found in the immediate vicinity of the stern, indicating this stretch of water has been a shipping hazard for millennia.

## Overview

The Tihany rests on the seabed at approximately 35 metres, providing a substantial wreck dive at the upper end of recreational limits. At 60 metres length, the wreck can reward multiple dives exploring different sections. The presence of amphora fragments nearby adds archaeological interest. Visibility in Lošinj area waters typically reaches 10–30 metres. The site is recommended for advanced divers.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Near Školjić island, off Unije island, Kvarner/Lošinj area, Croatia
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 35 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 10–30 meters
- **Current**: Generally mild
- **Best Time**: May to October

## Marine Life

No site-specific marine life reports verified against independent sources for this wreck. Regional Adriatic/Kvarner fauna applies at this depth range.

## Dive Profile

Descend to the highest point of the wreck — typically the superstructure — then work down to 35 metres along the hull. Complete a circuit of the accessible sections with a torch. Ascend via the shot line and complete a minimum 3-minute safety stop at 5 metres. Gas management is important at this depth.

## Entry and Exit

Dive boats from Mali Lošinj or Unije operators access this site. Entry by giant stride or back roll. Ascend via shot line or deploy SMB for free ascent.

## Tips and Recommendations

- A torch is essential for inspecting the wreck interior
- Good buoyancy control will prevent silt disturbance
- The amphora fragments near the stern are historically significant — do not disturb them
- This should be the deepest dive of the day given the 35m depth

## Safety Considerations

The 35-metre maximum depth is at the upper recreational limit. Gas management and careful ascent are important. Never penetrate enclosed spaces without proper training and equipment. Carry an SMB and dive with a buddy.

## Photography

The wreck structure at 35m provides wide-angle subjects in the clear Kvarner water. The amphora fragments near the stern are historically significant macro subjects. A torch and strobe combination reveals the encrusting organisms on the hull.
