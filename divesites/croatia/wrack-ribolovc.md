## Wrack Ribolovc

Ribolovac ("Fisherman") is a sunken fishing vessel lying off Cape Pelegrin near Stomorska on the island of Šolta, a short boat ride from Split. It is one of the best-known and most popular wreck dives in the Split area.

## Overview

Ribolovac is a wooden-hulled fishing boat resting close to shore (roughly 50 metres out) at Cape Pelegrin, Šolta. Sources vary on her exact length — most dive operators (Opensea, Venus Diving) cite approximately 28 metres, while PADI's listing gives a smaller figure of around 15 metres by 5 metres; the site description here follows the ~28m figure reported by multiple independent local operators. Depth to the wreck is reported as roughly 22–24 metres, with the shallowest structure around 13 metres and dives sometimes starting from a shot line at 3 metres. Fishing nets and equipment remain visible on the structure. Its proximity to shore and modest depth make it accessible to less experienced divers, while the intact nets and superstructure still interest more experienced wreck divers.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Cape Pelegrin, near Stomorska, Šolta island, Split area, Croatia
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 24 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 10–20 meters
- **Current**: Generally mild
- **Best Time**: May to October

## Marine Life

Opensea.hr reports groupers, damselfish, moray eels, and octopuses specifically at this wreck. (Note: a separate PADI listing for this site also mentions sharks, whales, dolphins, and turtles, but these read as generic regional filler rather than site-specific reports and are not repeated here.)

## Dive Profile

Descend to the wreck, which sits between roughly 13 and 24 metres, and make a full circuit of the hull and remaining fishing nets. Peer into accessible openings with a torch. The dive is relaxed and unhurried — allow time for close-up exploration of the nets and encrusting life. Finish with a gradual ascent and a 3-minute safety stop at 5 metres.

## Entry and Exit

Dive boats from Split-area and Šolta-area operators serve this site. Entry by giant stride or back roll. Deploy an SMB on ascent.

## Tips and Recommendations

- A torch is essential for inspecting cavities and bringing out the colours of encrusting organisms
- This is an excellent second dive of the day after a deeper site
- Good buoyancy control is important to avoid disturbing the silty bottom around the hull
- The site can be combined with a reef or wall dive nearby for a full day programme

## Safety Considerations

Depth and conditions are well within recreational limits. The main hazard is silt disturbance in enclosed sections. Always dive with a buddy and carry an SMB for boat pickup. Dive within your certification level.

## Photography

The compact wreck offers intimate macro photography opportunities — nudibranchs, octopus, and encrusting invertebrates all reward close inspection. Wide-angle shots of the full hull structure can be captured in good visibility conditions. The sandy bottom provides a clean, bright background.
