## Black Hills

Black Hills is a sea mound (pinnacle) dive with current and 130+ ft (40+ m) of relief.

## Overview

[We Dive Utila](https://www.wediveutila.com/utila-dive-sites) describes Black Hills as a sea mound whose top is at about 35 ft (10 m), sloping to 130+ ft (40+ m) at the base, with current often present; circling it takes about 15 minutes at 60 ft (18 m). [Scuba Diver Life](https://scubadiverlife.com/the-best-dive-sites-in-utila/) lists it as a seamount on the south side. Boat dive; conditions can be demanding, so it is rated Intermediate here.

## Marine Life

Reported by We Dive Utila at this site: barracuda, Atlantic spadefish, horse-eyed jacks, king mackerel, creole wrasse, free-swimming green moray eels, hawksbill turtles and large schools of fish. This is a single-source species list.

## Dive Profile

- **Top**: ~35 ft (10 m)
- **Base**: 130+ ft (40+ m)
- **Circuit at 60 ft**: ~15 minutes
- **Current**: often present (We Dive Utila)

## Notes

Earlier claims about north side location, morning-only access and other details were removed as unconfirmed; the location is given as south side by Scuba Diver Life.
