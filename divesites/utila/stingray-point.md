## Stingray Point

Stingray Point is a shallow reef with a large sand channel, rated Beginner here.

## Overview

[We Dive Utila](https://www.wediveutila.com/utila-dive-sites) describes a reef top at 5-25 ft (1.5-8 m) and a sand channel sloping to about 130 ft (40 m), with the coral to the west described as among the healthiest on the island. [Zentacle](https://www.zentacle.com/Beach/4619/sting-ray-point) lists a "very pretty reef", boat access, Beginner level and a maximum depth of 49.9 ft (15 m). Sources therefore report different maximum depths (about 15 m versus about 40 m), likely because one counts only the reef and the other the sand channel.

## Marine Life

Reported by We Dive Utila: seahorses, garden eels, pipefish, flounder, spotted eagle rays and stingrays.

## Dive Profile

- **Reef top**: 5-25 ft (1.5-8 m)
- **Sand channel**: to ~130 ft (40 m) per We Dive Utila

## Notes

Earlier claims about drift access, current and south/west exposure, plus snapper, grouper, parrotfish, angelfish and wrasse, were removed as unconfirmed.
