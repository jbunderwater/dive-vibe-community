## CJ's Drop Off

CJ's Drop Off is a north side wall dive rated Advanced for its depth.

## Overview

[We Dive Utila](https://www.wediveutila.com/utila-dive-sites) describes a reef top at 20-45 ft (6-14 m) with sand channels, and a wall dropping to 140+ ft (43+ m). A diver in a [ScubaBoard thread](https://scubaboard.com/community/threads/best-dive-sites-in-utila.384423/) places the wall start at about 30 ft and compared it to "diving in the grand canyon"; the same thread reports barrel sponges below 130 ft spawning in April. [Scuba Diver Life](https://scubadiverlife.com/the-best-dive-sites-in-utila/) calls it a sheer wall on the north side. Boat dive.

## Marine Life

Reported by We Dive Utila: spotted eagle rays, barracuda, moray eels and sea turtles. Barrel sponges (not fish) are noted by ScubaBoard.

## Dive Profile

- **Reef top**: 20-45 ft (6-14 m)
- **Wall**: to 140+ ft (43+ m)

## Safety

Monitor depth closely on a deep wall and stay within your training and no-decompression limits.

## Notes

Previously stated details on Turtle Harbour, tide/current behaviour, afternoon conditions and wrasse/damselfish were removed as unconfirmed.
