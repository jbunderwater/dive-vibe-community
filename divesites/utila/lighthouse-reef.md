## Lighthouse Reef

Lighthouse Reef is a reef wall about five minutes by boat from Utila town.

## Overview

[We Dive Utila](https://www.wediveutila.com/utila-dive-sites) (listed as "Light House Reef") describes a reef top at about 10 ft (3 m) and a wall dropping to about 80 ft (24 m), with sand patches, about 5 minutes from Utila town.

## Marine Life

Reported by We Dive Utila: snapper, moray eels, porcupinefish, toadfish, hawksbill turtles and spotted eagle rays. Single-source list.

## Dive Profile

- **Reef top**: ~10 ft (3 m)
- **Wall**: to ~80 ft (24 m)

## Notes

Only one fetched source documents this site. Earlier claims (lighthouse height, rope sponges, grunts, groupers, needlefish, goatfish, wrasses, night octopus/lobster, visibility range) were removed as unconfirmed.
