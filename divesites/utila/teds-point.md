## Ted's Point

Ted's Point is a reef wall with sand channels and a small wreck on the sand.

## Overview

[We Dive Utila](https://www.wediveutila.com/utila-dive-sites) describes a reef top at 10-15 ft (3-5 m), a wall to about 40 ft (12 m) and a small sunken sailboat at about 60 ft (18 m). [UnderwaterPhotography.com](https://www.underwaterphotography.com/Dive-Sites/Dive-Site.aspx?ID=4854) calls it a wall dive with a sloping wall to about 70 ft and "a small wreck on the sandy bottom". [The Scuba Directory](https://thescubadirectory.com/divesiteprofile/Ted's-Point-Utila-Honduras-Dive-Site) gives about 18 m (60 ft) and tags it as a wreck. Boat dive.

## Wreck

[Geoff Schultz's log](https://geoffschultz.org/Log_Page.php?subjectid=Utila:_Dive_Sites) describes the wreck as a ferro-cement sailboat, stripped of its contents and mast, at 65-75 ft, with an 18-inch hole near the chain locker; the cause of sinking is stated as unknown. Other sources put it at about 60 ft. Vessel name, build date, length, tonnage and sinking cause are not documented in any source found. An earlier claim that it was deliberately sunk as an artificial reef was removed because no source supports it.

## Marine Life

- We Dive Utila: groupers, blue tang, green moray eels, spotted eagle rays.
- UnderwaterPhotography.com: schools of black margate near the buoy, moray eels, stingrays, spotted eagle rays.
- Geoff Schultz: garden eels near the wreck and a soapfish nearby.

A coral- and sponge-encrusted barbecue grill is reported as a photo subject (UnderwaterPhotography.com).

## Dive Profile

- **Reef top**: 10-15 ft (3-5 m)
- **Wall**: to ~40 ft (12 m), sloping to ~70 ft per one source
- **Wreck**: 60-75 ft (18-23 m), sources vary
