## Ron's Wreck

Ron's Wreck is a small sunken boat at about 60 ft (18 m) near Moon Hole.

## Overview

[We Dive Utila](https://www.wediveutila.com/utila-dive-sites) lists Ron's Wreck as part of the Moon Hole site: a wreck within a U-shaped reef wall on a gently sloping sandy bottom at about 60 ft (18 m), described only as a small fishing boat. A web search snippet describes it as an old shrimp fishing boat. A previous version of this page also described a tugboat deliberately sunk as an artificial reef; no source found supports that, so it was removed. Vessel name, build date, length, tonnage and sinking cause are not documented in any source found, and no Wikipedia entry exists. Boat dive.

## Marine Life

No site-specific marine-life reports were found in the sources consulted; Utila's regional Caribbean reef fauna applies, but no species are attributed to this site here. (We Dive Utila mentions crustaceans and octopus around Moon Hole generally, not at the wreck.)
