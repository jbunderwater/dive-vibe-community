## Pretty Bush

Pretty Bush (also logged as Black Coral Wall/Pretty Bush) is a south side reef wall known as a macro site.

## Overview

[Scuba Diver Life](https://scubadiverlife.com/the-best-dive-sites-in-utila/) lists Pretty Bush on the south side as a macro photography site. [Geoff Schultz's sailing log](https://geoffschultz.org/Log_Page.php?subjectid=Utila:_Dive_Sites) logs "Black Coral Wall/Pretty Bush" with the mooring at about 30 ft on the reef edge, a wall dropping at roughly 60 degrees to 100 ft or more, and mostly mountainous star coral; the author noted a storm appeared to have damaged coral in the past. Boat dive.

## Marine Life

No site-specific marine-life reports were found in the sources consulted; Utila's regional Caribbean reef fauna applies, but no species are attributed to this site here. The Geoff Schultz log notes few fish seen on that dive; Scuba Diver Life mentions macro photography without naming species.

## Dive Profile

- **Mooring / reef edge**: ~30 ft (9 m)
- **Wall**: to 100+ ft (30+ m)

## Notes

Given the wall to 100+ ft, the Beginner rating in the data is not supported by the sources and should be reviewed. Previously listed 13 ft start depth, nudibranchs, crustaceans and reef fish were removed as unconfirmed.
