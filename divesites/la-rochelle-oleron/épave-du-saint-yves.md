## Épave du Saint-Yves

A French steam tanker sunk by a leftover wartime mine in 1947, lying in the shipping channel between Ré and Oléron — a rarely visited wreck.

## Overview

The *Saint-Yves*, a 71-metre steam tanker built in 1939 at the Ateliers et Chantiers de France in Dunkerque, struck a forgotten WWII mine on 25 May 1947 while carrying 4,500 tons of gasoline toward La Pallice. The explosion ruptured the hull and flooded the engine room, and she capsized to starboard. The wreck lies at about 35 m at 46°07.179'N, 001°28.575'W, roughly 4 nautical miles from La Pallice harbour.

Because it sits in the busy commercial approach channel to the port of La Pallice, the wreck is seldom dived and remains little documented — no marine-life reports are available. Any dive here requires careful planning with a local operator around both shipping traffic and the strong tidal streams of the Pertuis.

Only one site-specific source currently documents this wreck, so details should be treated as provisional.
