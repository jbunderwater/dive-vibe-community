## Plateau de Chaucre

A rocky plateau off the northwest tip of Île d'Oléron — the island's main shore-diving area, worked by the local club from Chaucre beach.

## Overview

The rocky plateau off the Pointe de Chaucre (Saint-Georges-d'Oléron) drops away in small step-like ledges in roughly 12–18 m of water (the position recorded here is approximate, just off the point). The natural cove at Chaucre beach makes for an easy entry, and the local dive school runs training, introductory and exploration dives here from shore and by boat, exploring the rocky bottom and small cave-like overhangs of the surrounding sites.

Visibility on this stretch of Atlantic coast is modest and strongly tide-dependent, and dives are scheduled around slack water. No detailed site-specific marine life records were found for the plateau itself; the wider Chaucre sector is described by operators as rocky terrain with drop-offs, fissures and boulder fields.
