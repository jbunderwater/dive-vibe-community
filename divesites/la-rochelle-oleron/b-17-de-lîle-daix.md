## B-17 de l'île d'Aix

The wreck of a Boeing B-17 Flying Fortress bomber lying in shallow water in the sheltered inner Pertuis, near Île d'Aix.

## Overview

Local operators dive the remains of a B-17 Flying Fortress off Île d'Aix, known as the "Wilde Cargo". It is generally identified with B-17F 42-30064 *Wild Cargo*, reported missing in action near La Rochelle on 16 September 1943; aviation records place that crash off Île Madame, a few miles further south, so the identification and exact position remain uncertain (the coordinates recorded here are approximate, placed off Île d'Aix per the operators who dive it).

Sources also differ on depth — the operator lists 13 m maximum while other dive guides report 18 m. Either way this is one of the area's gentler dives, in the more sheltered waters of the inner Pertuis d'Antioche. Seahorses are reported on the surrounding bottom, a local specialty.
