## Épave du Nautilus

A modern Dutch coaster wrecked off Ars-en-Ré in 1988, now broken into three sections at easy depth — the most accessible of the area's larger wrecks.

## Overview

The *Nautilus*, an 82-metre Dutch coaster carrying barium sulphate, was wrecked on 3 February 1988 about 1.2 nautical miles south-southwest of the Pointe de Grignon at Ars-en-Ré (the position recorded here is approximate, derived from that bearing). The hull broke into three distinct sections and now lies on its port side in 18–20 m of water.

The stern section is the highlight, with the propeller and rudder usually surrounded by a large school of pollack; the bow section carries a spare propeller and a fine anchor. Conger eels, pouting, nudibranchs and anemones colonise the wreckage. Local clubs typically dive it by boat from Ars-en-Ré, timing dives to slack water.
