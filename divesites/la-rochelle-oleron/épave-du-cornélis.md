## Épave du Cornélis

A compact tug wreck lost to a mine in 1942, sitting whole on clean sand west of Île de Ré.

## Overview

The *Cornélis* was a riveted-steel tug about 27 m long with a 6.2 m beam. On 15 April 1942 she sortied from La Rochelle to sweep for drifting mines in the access channel used by U-boats heading for the La Pallice submarine base, and was sunk by a mine — most likely one she had snagged. The wreck was relocated decades later during a seabed-mapping campaign at 46°08.229'N, 001°41.958'W.

She lies on a bottom of light sand at 31–33 m. Because the wreck is small and the seabed pale, the whole tug typically comes into view seven or eight metres before the bottom — a rare treat in the often turbid Pertuis Charentais. Like the other offshore wrecks here it is dived by boat at slack water, in cold Atlantic water (roughly 12–16 °C in summer).
