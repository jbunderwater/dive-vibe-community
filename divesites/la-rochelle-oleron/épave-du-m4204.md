## Épave du M4204

A small German auxiliary minesweeper lying near the Sauerland, often dived on the same outing.

## Overview

The *M4204* was a trawler built in 1918 and pressed into German service as an auxiliary minesweeper; local dive operators report her sunk in 1940. The wreck lies at about 36 m roughly 600 m from the Sauerland (the position recorded here is approximate, derived from that distance), and her winch and elements of the rigging are still recognisable on the seabed.

Only one site-specific source (the La Rochelle operator Sam Diving) currently documents this wreck, so details of her history and layout should be treated as provisional. Conditions match the neighbouring Sauerland: an exposed offshore site with strong tidal currents, dived by boat at slack water.
