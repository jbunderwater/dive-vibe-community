## Elba Reef, South Plateau

The south plateau of Elba Reef is the section of the reef where the Levanzo Wreck lies.

## Overview

Seawolf Safari describes the southern plateau as sitting on the edge of a deep chasm, and dive3d.eu places the Levanzo stern on the southern plateau at about 27 m; Red Sea Diving mentions north and south plateaus without further detail. Sources reviewed do not give a separate depth range for the plateau itself. For the wider reef and the wreck, see the Elba Reef and Levanzo Wreck pages.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Elba Reef, Egypt-Sudan border
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Depth Reference**: Levanzo stern reported at 22-27 m

## Marine Life

No site-specific marine life reports were found for this site. The regional Sudan Red Sea fauna applies, but no species are attributed here. Species reported around the Levanzo Wreck are listed on that page.

## Safety Considerations

Dive within your certification limits, use a surface marker buoy, and confirm current conditions and official travel advisories with your liveaboard operator before booking.
