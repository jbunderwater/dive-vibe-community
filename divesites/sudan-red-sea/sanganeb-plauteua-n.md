## Sanganeb Plauteua N

The north plateau of Sanganeb, an atoll-like reef off Port Sudan that is part of a UNESCO World Heritage serial site.

## Overview

Dive the World describes Sanganeb as rising from about 800 m, and a World Heritage listing source places it roughly 24-30 km northeast of Port Sudan (sources differ). It was inscribed by UNESCO in 2016 as part of Sanganeb Marine National Park and Dungonab Bay - Mukkawar Island Marine National Park. Blue Force Fleet describes a plateau with a steep drop-off on the northern part, exposed to current and best dived in calm seas, early in the morning. The lighthouse: sources disagree on its age, with one lighthouse directory giving 1955, another source giving construction starting in 1950 on a site with a structure since at least 1907, and a third giving 1958-1964. No site-specific depth figure was found for the north plateau.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Sanganeb, northeast of Port Sudan
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 20 meters

## Marine Life

Reef-level reports for Sanganeb exist (see Dive the World and Blue Force Fleet), but sources reviewed do not attribute species to the north plateau specifically, so none are named here.

## Safety Considerations

Dive within your certification limits, use a surface marker buoy, and confirm current conditions and official travel advisories with your liveaboard operator before booking.
