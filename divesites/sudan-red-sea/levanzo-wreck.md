## Levanzo Wreck

Levanzo Wreck (SS Levanzo, also listed as SS Isola di Levanzo) is an Italian cargo steamer wrecked on Elba Reef, near the Egypt-Sudan border.

## Overview

Sources describe the SS Levanzo as an Italian steel-hulled cargo steamer lost on Elba Reef. Loss details (date, route, cargo) appear in only one source checked and are not stated here until a second source confirms them. dive3d.eu reports the stern on the southern plateau at about 27 m, keel up, with the bow sloping to about 70 m on sand. Sadko Safari Fleet gives a depth of 20-40 m, so depth figures differ by source. Builder, build year, length, tonnage and the precise sinking sequence were not confirmed by any source checked, and are not stated. Wreck penetration requires proper training and equipment and is never "safe".

## Site Information

- **Location**: Elba Reef, Egypt-Sudan border (Halaib Triangle)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Depth**: sources report 20-40 m (Sadko) to about 70 m at the bow (dive3d.eu)

## Marine Life

Sadko Safari Fleet reports schools of jackfish and barracuda around the wreck, with corals and sponges on its surfaces. No other site-specific species reports were verified.

## Access

Reached by liveaboard. Confirm depths and conditions with the dive guide; deep sections need appropriate technical training.
