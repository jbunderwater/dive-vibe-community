## Abu Seif

Abu Seif (also spelled Abu Sheif) is described by Scuba Diving magazine as notable for shallow water in a list of otherwise deep Sudan sites. Only one source was found, so this entry is not marked validated.

## Overview

A Scuba Diving magazine feature (read via search summary because the page blocked direct fetching) describes hours of exploration among cracks and crevices sprawling just below the surface, with sunbeams through the water. Dive the World, Seawolf Safari and Royal Evolution pages reviewed do not list the site. No depth figure was found in any source; the dataset value of 12 m is unconfirmed.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Sudan Red Sea (liveaboard access)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 12 meters

## Marine Life

The single source describes sea goldies near the surface and anemones with clownfish a little deeper. No other species are attributed.

## Safety Considerations

Dive within your certification limits, use a surface marker buoy, and confirm current conditions and official travel advisories with your liveaboard operator before booking.
