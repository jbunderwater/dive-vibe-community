## Angarosh

Angarosh is a deep offshore reef in the Dungonab Bay - Mukkawar Island area, known for hammerhead and other sharks. Its name is reported to mean 'Mother of Sharks'.

## Overview

Dive the World reports the reef lies about 12 km east of Mukawwar Island and 5 km southwest of Abington Reef, with deep plateaus and fissured walls rising to about 10 m. Plateau depths differ by source: Dive the World gives steps at 40, 30 and 18 m on the northern plateau and 20 and 45 m on the southern plateau, while Blue Force Fleet gives two eastern plateaus at about 25 m and 40 m. A DeeperBlue trip report describes hammerhead encounters at 35 m and deeper.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Near Mukawwar Island, Dungonab Bay area
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 45 meters

## Marine Life

Hammerhead sharks are named by all sources; Dive the World also lists tiger sharks, silvertips, whitetip reef sharks, barracuda, tuna and trevally, and Blue Force Fleet lists grey reef sharks, jackfish and bumphead parrotfish in the shallower areas.

## Safety Considerations

Dive within your certification limits, use a surface marker buoy, and confirm current conditions and official travel advisories with your liveaboard operator before booking.
