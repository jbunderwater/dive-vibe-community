## Angarosh

Angarosh is a steep-walled reef with plateaus and a small sandy island, whose name is reported to mean "mother of sharks".

## Overview

Dive The World describes Angarosh as a deep dive site with plateaus and fissured walls about 12 km east of Mukawwar Island, with steps at 40, 30 and 18 m on the northern plateau and at 20 and 45 m on the southern, walls rising to about 10 m, visibility often over 25 m and caverns in the shallower areas. Blue Force Fleet describes a steep-walled reef with a small sandy island, two plateaus on the eastern side at about 25 m and 40 m, a name derived from an Arabic phrase meaning "mother of sharks", and says it was first dived by Hans Hass. The sources disagree on plateau depths (25/40 m versus 20/45 m). Royal Evolution lists it as a full-day reef stop.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Dungonab Bay - Mukkawar Island National Park, ~12km east of Mukawwar Island, ~5km southwest of Abington Reef
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters

## Marine Life

Dive The World lists hammerheads, tiger sharks, silvertips, whitetip reef sharks, barracuda, bluefin tuna and trevally. Blue Force Fleet lists hammerheads, grey reef sharks, barracuda, jackfish and, in the shallows, bumphead parrotfish. An earlier claim of hammerhead schools of 50+ could not be confirmed and was removed.
