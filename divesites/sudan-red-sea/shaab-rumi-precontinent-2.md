## Shaab Rumi, Precontinent 2

Precontinent II (Conshelf II) was Jacques Cousteau's 1963 experimental undersea habitat at Sha'ab Rumi, and its remains are a shallow historic dive.

## Overview

Wikipedia reports that Conshelf II was set up in 1963 at Sha'ab Rumi: six oceanauts lived about 10 m down in the Starfish House for 30 days, and two lived at 30 m in the deep cabin for a week. Royal Evolution instead says five divers lived there for a month, so the head count is disputed between sources. Wikipedia states that two structures were dismantled and removed and that the rest became destinations for recreational divers, without saying which. Dive The World describes a shallow site suitable for day or night dives with remains of the habitats on the seabed, including a submersible hangar, shark cages and a tool shed, and warns that only experienced wreck divers should enter the confined structure. Blue Force Fleet places the remains just outside the lagoon entrance at Sha'ab Rumi.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Sha'ab Rumi lagoon, northern Sudan Red Sea (~35km from Port Sudan)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters

No site-specific marine-life reports were found for this site. Sudan's regional Red Sea fauna applies in general, but no species are attributed to this location.
