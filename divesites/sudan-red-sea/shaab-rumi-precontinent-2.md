## Shaab Rumi, Precontinent 2

Precontinent II (Conshelf II) was Jacques Cousteau's 1963 underwater habitat experiment at Sha'ab Rumi; remains of the project can be visited just outside the reef's lagoon entrance.

## Overview

Wikipedia records that Conshelf II was set at Sha'ab Rumi in 1963. The Starfish House sat at about 10 m and housed six oceanauts for 30 days; a deep cabin at about 30 m housed two for one week; a submarine hangar sheltered a two-person submersible nicknamed the 'diving saucer'. Other accounts give different headcounts (five or seven) and deep cabin depths (about 26 m), so these figures vary by source. Wikipedia states that two structures were dismantled and that the rest became recreational dive attractions. Blue Force Fleet places the remains just outside the lagoon entrance, and a DeeperBlue trip report describes a night dive on the submarine house, from about 5 m down to about 14 m.
