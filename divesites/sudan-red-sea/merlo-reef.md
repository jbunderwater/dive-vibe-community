## Merlo Reef

Merlo Reef (Sha'ab Merlo) is a frequently visited reef about 2 km southwest of Angarosh, described by some sources as a large coral pinnacle.

## Overview

Dive The World describes Merlo as an elongated reef about 2 km southwest of Angarosh, with vertical crevices to 15 m on the eastern wall, a northern plateau with levels including one at 25-30 m, cracks, caverns and a soft coral bommie; its deepest level is beyond recreational limits. Blue Force Fleet describes it as a reef with a huge coral pinnacle that drops into deep water, covered in soft corals. The "pinnacle" versus "elongated reef" description therefore differs between sources. Dive The World also notes that the remains of the liveaboard Freedom, grounded in 1999, lie on the western wall; this is a liveaboard hull, not a historic wreck. Royal Evolution lists Merlo as a full-day reef stop with a night dive.

## Site Information

- **Location**: ~1 mile southwest of Angarosh, offshore northern Sudan Red Sea
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Pinnacle/seamount
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 20 meters

## Marine Life

Dive The World lists giant moray eels, anemonefish, goatfish, snappers, unicornfish, whitetip reef sharks, Napoleon wrasse, grey reef sharks, butterflyfish, and hammerheads in deeper channels. Blue Force Fleet adds that oceanic mantas are sometimes seen.
