## Merlo Reef

Merlo (Sha'ab Merlo) is a reef with a large soft-coral-covered pinnacle and walls on the northern Sudan route.

## Overview

Blue Force Fleet describes a reef with a large coral pinnacle that sinks into deep water, covered in soft corals. Dive the World describes walls rising from about 450 m, crevices on the eastern wall down to about 15 m, and a northern plateau with an intermediate level at 25-30 m and a deeper level beyond recreational limits where hammerheads may pass through a channel. Egyptian Cruising Company describes a steep-walled reef. Dive the World also mentions the remains of a Sudanese liveaboard, Freedom, that ran aground in 1999; one other source suggests it lies on a neighboring reef, so the location is not stated here.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Northern Sudan Red Sea, near Angarosh
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Pinnacle/seamount
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters

## Marine Life

Dive the World lists giant moray eels, anemonefish, goatfish, snappers, unicornfish, whitetip reef sharks, Napoleon wrasse, grey reef sharks, butterflyfish and passing hammerheads. Blue Force Fleet reports oceanic mantas sometimes seen.

## Safety Considerations

Dive within your certification limits, use a surface marker buoy, and confirm current conditions and official travel advisories with your liveaboard operator before booking.
