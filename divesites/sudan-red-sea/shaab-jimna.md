## Shaab Jimna

Shaab Jimna (also Jumna or Jibna) is an offshore pelagic reef southeast of Port Sudan known for hammerhead sharks.

## Overview

Dive The World describes Shaab Jibna (Jimna) as a pelagic offshore reef more than 50 km southeast of Port Sudan, known for large schools of curious hammerheads that often approach divers. Blue Force Fleet describes it as a reef more than 5 miles long north of Suakin, with openings into an interior lagoon and a large cavity on the outer wall at about 30 m. DivePlannerPro lists it as a reef with wall topography requiring advanced experience. Maximum depth is not stated by these sources; the depth in the dataset is unconfirmed.

## Site Information

- **Location**: 50+ km offshore, southeast of Port Sudan
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 28 meters

## Marine Life

Dive The World reports large schools of hammerheads. Blue Force Fleet reports hammerhead, grey reef and whitetip sharks with occasional silvertips, plus resident sharks, tuna and carangids at the outer wall.
