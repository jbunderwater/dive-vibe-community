## Elba Reef East (Southplateau)

Elba Reef East (Southplateau) is part of the Elba Reef complex in the Halaib Triangle on the Egypt-Sudan border.

## Overview

Elba Reef lies in the Halaib Triangle on the Egypt-Sudan border. Maldives Magazine describes three reefs forming a triangle, with north and south plateaus that slope from about 20 m to drop-offs at 45 m and deeper; the north plateau edge is noted for enormous coral fans. It gives no plateau detail for the east and west sides. Royal Evolution, a Hurghada-based operator, lists the Levanzo wreck at Elba as an Egyptian site, so this entry's classification under Sudan is uncertain. The separate North/South/East/West "plateau" subdivisions used for this dataset's GPS points are not confirmed by these sources, and only two sources could be re-confirmed on 2026-10-09, so the site is not marked validated. No source describes an east plateau specifically.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Elba Reef southeast, Egypt-Sudan border (Halaib Triangle)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 16 meters

## Marine Life

Operator sources list many species for the Elba complex, but they are marketing-level lists for the whole reef (and some are tied to neighbouring sites such as Daedalus), so no species are attributed to this specific point. Regional Red Sea fauna applies in general.
