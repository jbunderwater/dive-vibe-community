## Elba Reef, North Plateau

The north plateau of Elba Reef. Only one source reviewed describes it in detail, so this entry is not marked validated.

## Overview

Seawolf Safari describes a long northern plateau starting at about 20 m and dropping to about 45 m. Red Sea Diving mentions north and south plateaus without depth or detail. Other operators give Elba Reef as a whole a range of 10-40 m. Because only one source describes the plateau itself, the depth should be treated as approximate.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Elba Reef, Egypt-Sudan border
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 45 meters
