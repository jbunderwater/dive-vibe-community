## Elba Reef

Elba Reef is a remote, little-visited reef complex on the Egypt-Sudan border, known for drop-offs, untouched coral and the Levanzo Wreck.

## Overview

Sadko Safari Fleet describes Elba as one of the southernmost reefs of Egypt, with part of it extending into Sudanese waters, and Seawolf Safari places it on the Sudan-Egypt border. Reported depth range is 10-40 m (Sadko Safari Fleet, Red Sea Diving). Seawolf Safari describes a triangular reef with long north and south plateaus, the northern one starting at about 20 m and dropping to about 45 m and the southern one sitting on the edge of a deep chasm. Red Sea Diving notes that past 40 m dives become technical. The main attraction is the Levanzo Wreck on the south plateau (see its own page).

## Site Information

- **Location**: Egypt-Sudan border
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters

## Marine Life

Grey reef sharks, hammerheads and barracuda are named by all three sources reviewed; Sadko Safari Fleet also reports large schools of pelagic fish.

## Safety Considerations

Dive within your certification limits, use a surface marker buoy, and confirm current conditions and official travel advisories with your liveaboard operator before booking.
