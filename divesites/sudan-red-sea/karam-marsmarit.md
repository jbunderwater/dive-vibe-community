## Karam Marsmarit

Karam Marsmarit (Karam Masamirit) is a reef in the far south of Sudan's Red Sea, described by one German dive report as an intact coral garden. Only one source was found, so this entry is not marked validated.

## Overview

A taucher.net dive report places the reef about 1.5 nautical miles from Masmarit (Masamirit) Island, whose lighthouse is visible from afar. The reef is elongated and bends slightly north, with steep walls and a narrow, colorful plateau with a coral garden at about 20 m. The listing gives a maximum depth of 30 m, an advanced rating and boat or zodiac entry. Earlier text on this page about strong surface current and sharks and Napoleon wrasse on the southeast walls could not be traced to any source and was removed.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Near Masmarit Island, Suakin area, Deep South
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters

## Marine Life

The single source reports blue-spotted stingrays resting under table corals, moray eels in the reef wall and banded shrimp cleaning them, plus mackerel and bannerfish, with large fish staying in the blue water. No sharks were reported in that source.

## Safety Considerations

Dive within your certification limits, use a surface marker buoy, and confirm current conditions and official travel advisories with your liveaboard operator before booking.
