## Gota Mesharifa

Gota Mesharifa (Mesharifa) is a small sand island in a lagoon near the Sudanese shore, reported as a seasonal manta ray site.

## Overview

Dive The World describes Mesharifa as a small sand island in a large lagoon near the Sudanese shore where visibility tends to be limited by plankton and dozens of manta rays gather from August to November where current brings food. Royal Evolution schedules it as an afternoon dive and overnight stay only in October, tied to the manta season. Only these two sources were re-confirmed on 2026-10-09, so the site is not marked validated (three independent sources are required). Depth is not stated in either source. Difficulty is tagged Advanced on the basis of liveaboard-only access, not site conditions.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Northern Sudan Red Sea, sheltered lagoon close to shore
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 15 meters

## Marine Life

Dive The World and Royal Evolution report manta rays during the manta season (August to November, or October).
