## Shab Rumi, Plateau S

The southern area of Sha'ab Rumi, a large barrier reef near Port Sudan, where current is stronger than elsewhere on the reef.

## Overview

Blue Force Fleet describes Sha'ab Rumi as a large barrier reef with two lagoon entrances on its west side, and notes that the southern area has stronger currents that bring nutrients from deep water. Dive the World describes an expansive reef plateau at 20-30 m. Operators report hammerheads at Sha'ab Rumi from November to April (Dive the World). Sources reviewed do not separate the plateau areas of the reef when describing wildlife.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Sha'ab Rumi, central Sudan Red Sea
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters

## Marine Life

Reef-level reports for Sha'ab Rumi exist, but none of the sources reviewed attribute species to the southern plateau specifically, so none are named here.
