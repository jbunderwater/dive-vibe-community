## Inchcape 1

Inchcape 1 is a historic wreck dive in UAE - Fujairah, Middle East.

## Overview

Inchcape 1 was originally named Gray Swift 2, a US-built vessel shipped to the UAE in the early 1970s for Inchcape Shipping Services. Nemo Diving Center and PADI report that she was decommissioned and deliberately sunk off Al Aqah as an artificial reef; Nemo gives 12 December 2001, and Khaleej Times gives 2001. Sources report the wreck at 30 metres (Nemo, PADI/dive.site) to about 32 metres (Khaleej Times), upright on sand. Nemo reports pink and purple Dendronephthya soft corals on the bow, yellow snappers, trevally, cardinalfish, barracuda, moray eels, scorpionfish and rays near the stern, with seahorses and nudibranchs on the stern in good conditions. Nemo reports Cyclone Gonu (2007) tore off the wheelhouse. Builder, length and tonnage are not consistently documented across independent sources and are omitted.

## Site Information

- **Location**: UAE - Fujairah, Middle East
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 32 meters

## Dive Notes

Nemo Diving Center and Khaleej Times recommend the site for experienced or certified advanced divers with good buoyancy control; visibility is reported at about 10 metres. Any wreck penetration requires proper training and equipment.
