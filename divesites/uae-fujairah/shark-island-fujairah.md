## Shark Island Fujairah

Shark Island, officially Sira Khorfakkan, is a small rocky island on the southeastern edge of Khor Fakkan Bay.

## Overview

Shark Island offers two dive options: a bay side where hard coral covers an area of roughly 200 m, and a wall of large granite boulders that PADI describes as a 250 m swim. Following the rock outward reveals table corals spanning at least 2 m (PADI). Nemo Diving Center describes the island as sitting in a protected bay, with conditions manageable for most of the year; no source consulted describes it as a formal marine protected area. Boats leave from Khorfakkan beach (Gulf News).

Marine life reported at this site: blacktip reef sharks, most often on the eastern side from November to April (Nemo Diving Center, Gulf News; sightings are not guaranteed), large turtles and honeycomb moray eels (Nemo Diving Center, PADI), and shoals of cornetfish, trevally, mackerel, fusiliers, sergeant majors and damselfish (Nemo Diving Center, PADI).

## Site Information

- **Location**: UAE - Fujairah, Middle East
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 14 meters

## Dive Notes

Depth is reported as 5 to 14 m (PADI) and up to 15 m (Nemo Diving Center); a listing on another dive-booking site reports far deeper water on the south side, which this research could not confirm. Visibility is given as 5 to 15 m by Nemo Diving Center. Nemo advises staying on the bay side if currents pick up along the wall, and keeping fins off the shallow, fragile coral.
