## The Car Cemetery

The Car Cemetery is a historic wreck dive in UAE - Fujairah, Middle East.

## Overview

The Car Cemetery is an artificial reef created in 1988, when about 200 cars and trucks were deliberately sunk off Fujairah. Nemo Diving Center reports that Cyclone Gonu reduced the number to around 50 vehicles. The remains sit at roughly 16-18 metres with visibility around 10 metres, and guide ropes connect the wrecks. Nemo reports nudibranchs (including chromodorids and Gymnodoris), seahorses, blennies, honeycomb moray eels, occasional stingrays and Omani clingfish.

## Site Information

- **Location**: UAE - Fujairah, Middle East
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 18 meters

## Dive Notes

Nemo advises against entering the enclosed car bodies, which are unstable, and warns of fishing nets and entanglement risk on some vehicles. It describes the site as suitable for Open Water divers and above with good buoyancy control.
