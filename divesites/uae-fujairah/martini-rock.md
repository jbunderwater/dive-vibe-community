## Martini Rock

Martini Rock is a pinnacle dive site in UAE - Fujairah, Middle East.

## Overview

Martini Rock, near Khor Fakkan, is described by Visit Sharjah as about 160 metres long and 80 metres across, with average visibility of 5-15 metres. PADI lists the same area as Martini Wall, describing large granite boulders with many cracks, a sandy section, and hard corals and anemone fields on the bay rock, with a 50-minute swim end to end. No site-specific marine life reports were found in the sources reviewed for this site; the destination's regional fauna applies.

## Site Information

- **Location**: UAE - Fujairah, Middle East
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Pinnacle/seamount
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 19 meters

## Dive Notes

Depth figures differ: PADI's description gives 12-19 metres while its listing header shows 12 metres. The cracks and deeper sections suit divers with some experience.
