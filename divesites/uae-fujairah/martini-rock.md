## Martini Rock

Martini Rock is a pinnacle dive site in UAE - Fujairah, Middle East.

## Overview

Martini Rock (also listed as "Martini Wall" by some operators) is a pair of underwater pinnacles roughly 160 metres long and 80 metres wide, close to Khor Fakkan on the UAE's east coast. Large granite boulders with cracks and overhangs form the structure, draped in soft corals — including Dendronephthya in orange, purple, and yellow — and green-blue whip corals, alongside hard coral and anemone fields in the shallower bay-facing sections. Shoaling Arabian mackerel, trevally, fusiliers, sergeant majors, and damselfish circle the pinnacles, and reported sightings include cuttlefish, lionfish, broomtail wrasse, blacktip reef sharks, seahorses, and turtles.

## Site Information

- **Location**: UAE - Fujairah, Middle East
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Pinnacle/seamount
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 20 meters

## Dive Notes

Sources report depths ranging from around 12 metres in the shallower bay area up to roughly 20-22 metres at the base of the pinnacles, with the more complex features — cracks, overhangs, and deeper sections — best suited to divers with some experience. A full circuit of the site can take up to 50 minutes.
