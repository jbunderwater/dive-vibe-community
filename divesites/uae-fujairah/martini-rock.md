## Martini Rock

Martini Rock (also listed as Martini Wall) is a rock formation in Khor Fakkan Bay on the UAE east coast.

## Overview

PADI describes Martini Rock as about 160 m long and 80 m wide at its widest point, made up of two pinnacles with rock and some soft corals; Visit Sharjah gives the same dimensions and describes a reef. DivePlannerPro describes a single large pinnacle with walls, slopes and overhangs, so sources differ on the exact structure. PADI lists the site as a 25-minute boat trip south from Al Aqah, the operator's furthest site. The wreck Inchcape 2 lies opposite it.

Marine life reported at this site (PADI): Arabian mackerel, trevally, sergeant majors, fusiliers, domino damselfish, porcupine puffer, moray eels, jawfish, barracuda, green and hawksbill turtles, nudibranchs, shrimp and crabs. DivePlannerPro additionally describes snapper, chromis and anthias.

## Site Information

- **Location**: UAE - Fujairah, Middle East
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Pinnacle/seamount
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 19 meters

## Dive Notes

PADI gives a depth range of 12 to 19 m. Visit Sharjah reports visibility averaging 5 to 15 m and up to about 20 m on clear days; DivePlannerPro reports 5 to 15 m. Reports of current differ between visitors (DivePlannerPro). Sources differ on difficulty: DivePlannerPro calls it beginner-friendly, while the deeper areas near the base of the rock suit divers with more experience.
