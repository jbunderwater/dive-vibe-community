## Dibba Rock

Dibba Rock is a wall dive site in UAE - Fujairah, Middle East.

## Overview

Dibba Rock is a small rocky island just off the coast of Dibba Al-Fujairah, ringed by coral. Freestyle Divers describe the deeper sections on the east side and shallower water to the north, south and west; Nemo Diving Center describes long sloping sides covered in soft and boulder corals, with green and purple whip corals on the seaward side. Freestyle Divers report it as the oldest marine protected area in the UAE and a no-fishing zone for more than 15 years. Operators report green turtles (common on the open-sea side), trumpetfish, moray eels, parrotfish, clownfish and jawfish, with cuttlefish, rays, squid and blacktip reef sharks in deeper water. Whale sharks have rarely been seen in the area.

## Site Information

- **Location**: UAE - Fujairah, Middle East
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 16 meters

## Dive Notes

Sources differ on maximum depth: Freestyle Divers gives 16 metres (coral sections at 2-14 metres), while Nemo Diving Center lists figures of 12 and 30 metres on the same page. Both operators describe the site as suitable for beginners and a good night dive, and both note strong currents at tidal changes and possible thermoclines. At low tide Nemo recommends the north (sea-facing) side; the south side is shallow and better for snorkelling.
