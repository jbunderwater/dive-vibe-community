## Inchcape 2

Inchcape 2 is a historic wreck dive in UAE - Fujairah, Middle East.

## Overview

Inchcape 2 is the second vessel donated by Inchcape Shipping Services for artificial-reef use. Nemo Diving Center and Khaleej Times report she was decommissioned and sunk in 2002 (Nemo: April 2002, opposite Martini Wall; Khaleej Times: off Khor Fakkan). Sources report depth between about 17 and 23 metres (Nemo) and about 22 metres (Khaleej Times). Nemo reports that Cyclone Gonu ripped off the wheelhouse in 2007. Divers report fusiliers, large pufferfish, small boxfish, chromis, scorpionfish, moray eels, crabs and rays (Nemo, Khaleej Times), and Nemo reports numerous nudibranchs. Original name, builder, length and tonnage are not consistently documented across independent sources and are omitted.

## Site Information

- **Location**: UAE - Fujairah, Middle East
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 23 meters

## Dive Notes

Nemo Diving Center recommends the site for certified advanced divers; visibility is reported at about 8-10 metres. Any wreck penetration requires proper training and equipment.
