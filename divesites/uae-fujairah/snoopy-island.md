## Snoopy Island

Snoopy Island is a small rocky island just off Al Aqah beach, named for its resemblance to the cartoon character Snoopy lying on his back.

## Overview

Snoopy Island sits just off the shore of the Sandy Beach Hotel and Resort in Al Aqah. Sources describe a fringing reef with rocky walls, coral gardens, sand and boulder fields, with the richest coral and fish life reported on the southern and eastern sides (DivePlannerPro). Shore access is reported by Shark Divers Club and DivePlannerPro, which says no boat is needed. Cyclone Gonu damaged coral here in 2007, though a Gulf News report said the damage was less severe than at Dibba Rock because rocks shelter much of the coral.

Marine life reported at this site: green and hawksbill turtles (Sandy Beach Dive Centre, DivePlannerPro), blacktip reef sharks (occasional, in deeper areas per Nemo Diving Center and DivePlannerPro), moray eels, cuttlefish and rays (Nemo Diving Center, Shark Divers Club, Sandy Beach Dive Centre), and parrotfish, angelfish and butterflyfish (Nemo Diving Center, Sandy Beach Dive Centre).

## Site Information

- **Location**: UAE - Fujairah, Middle East
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 15 meters

## Dive Notes

Reported depths differ: Sandy Beach Dive Centre gives 5 to 8 m for scuba diving, while Nemo Diving Center says most dives around the island are 5 to 15 m; the maximum depth shown here is the upper reported value. Visibility is reported as 8 to 15 m by Sandy Beach Dive Centre and 10 to 20 m by Nemo Diving Center, with June to September plankton blooms mentioned by Nemo. Divers Down lists a separate, deeper "Snoopy Deep Reef" site nearby.
