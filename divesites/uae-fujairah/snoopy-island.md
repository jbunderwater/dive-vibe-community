## Snoopy Island

Snoopy Island is a reef dive site in UAE - Fujairah, Middle East.

## Overview

Snoopy Island is a small rocky island just off Al Aqah, named for its resemblance to the cartoon character Snoopy lying on his back. Shark Divers Club describes easy shore access, shallow depths and warm, calm water. Nemo Diving Center reports most dives at roughly 5-15 metres with visibility typically 10-20 metres. Reported sightings include turtles, moray eels, cuttlefish, rays, angelfish, parrotfish and butterflyfish; reef sharks are reported mostly in deeper areas, and Shark Divers Club describes blacktip reef sharks as occasional.

## Site Information

- **Location**: UAE - Fujairah, Middle East
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 15 meters

## Dive Notes

Nemo Diving Center gives October to May as the best period (water around 22C in winter to 30C in summer); June to September is diveable but warmer water and plankton blooms can reduce visibility.
