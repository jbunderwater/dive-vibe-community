# Sunrise Reef

A real, named Manta Ray Bay Resort dive site featuring hundreds of acres of gradually sloping hard coral and large coral pinnacles — corrected on 2026-08-13 re-audit after a prior QA pass mistakenly marked it unconfirmed

## QA Note (2026-08-13)
A previous QA pass (2026-06-16) incorrectly concluded that "Sunrise Reef" was not a confirmed named site. It IS listed with its own page at mantaray.com/sites/sunrise-reef/ (the earlier pass only checked the general /sites/ index page, which lists the name without a description, and missed the dedicated page) and is independently described by visityap.com's dive site database. The description below has been rewritten from these two sources. Despite additional targeted searches this pass (ScubaBoard, PADI, Wannadive), a third independent source could not be found, so this site is **not yet marked validated** under this project's three-source rule — but the data below is corrected and site-specific rather than generic.

## Overview
Sunrise Reef is a gradually sloping reef extending across "hundreds of acres" according to Manta Ray Bay Resort, with prime coral development concentrated between 12-21 meters (40-70 feet). Numerous large coral pinnacles rise from the reef floor. Visit Yap's dive site database independently describes it as "hard coral heaven with an exceptional variety of hard corals in pristine condition," claiming more coral species here than in the entire Caribbean. Current is light, making this an accessible, unhurried dive.

## Site Information
- **Location**: Yap, Federated States of Micronesia (northern boundary of the resort's regularly-dived area, per mantaray.com)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 18-30+ meters (60-100+ feet)
- **Current**: Light, 0-5 km/h (0-2 mph)
- **Best Time**: Year-round

## Marine Life
Confirmed by mantaray.com: abundant reef fish populations, sea turtles, stingrays, and sharks, with giant clams nestled in coral crevices. No further species detail is available from the two sources found this pass. Generic reef species (anemonefish, surgeonfish, parrotfish, nudibranchs, etc.) that appeared in the site's earlier, unvalidated description have been removed — they were not specifically attributed to Sunrise Reef by any source and should not be presented as site-specific until a source confirms them.

## Dive Profile
A reef dive with the best coral development between 12-21 meters. The gradual slope and light current allow unhurried exploration among the large coral pinnacles rising from the reef floor. No further site-specific dive-plan detail (turnaround points, typical route) is available from the sources found; divers should follow their guide's briefing.

## Entry and Exit
Boat access, consistent with other Yap outer-reef sites. No site-specific entry/exit detail beyond "boat dive" is available from the two sources found this pass.

## Tips and Recommendations
- Take time over the coral pinnacles rather than swimming past them — the site's reputation rests on hard coral variety and density rather than big-animal encounters
- Look for giant clams tucked into crevices among the pinnacles
- Pairs well as a lower-intensity reef dive alongside Yap's manta and shark sites

## Safety Considerations
Conditions are described as light-current and moderate-depth by both sources found. Standard reef diving practices apply: buoyancy control near coral, SMB use, and buddy awareness. No site-specific hazards are documented.

## Photography
No site-specific photography guidance was found in the sources checked this pass. The hard coral pinnacle topography and light current suggest wide-angle reef compositions would suit the site, but this is inference rather than a sourced claim.
