## MSC Baltics III

MSC Baltics III is a historic wreck dive in Newfoundland, North America.

## Overview

The MSC Baltic III is a large container vessel that grounded on the northern coast of Newfoundland in February 2025 and, as of mid-2026, remains under an active salvage operation with a safety exclusion zone in place. The wreck is not currently accessible for recreational diving. Divers interested in this site should monitor official Canadian Coast Guard and Transport Canada advisories for updates before planning any visit.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Newfoundland, North America
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 25 meters

## Current Status

The MSC Baltic III grounded on February 15, 2025, after losing power in severe winter weather near Wild Cove in the Bay of Islands, Newfoundland. All crew members aboard were rescued by helicopter. Resolve Marine, contracted by Mediterranean Shipping Company, has been carrying out cargo removal and salvage since spring 2026. As of a July 2026 update, the operation had cleared all but four of the roughly 462 containers originally aboard, and crews had moved into the hull-splitting phase of the operation — installing chains and pullers to divide the vessel at its cracked section, with the plan to pull the forward section ashore for dismantling and metal recycling while the stern section is expected to be dealt with into 2027. Crews are also monitoring hydrogen sulfide levels from waterlogged containers and running a contaminated-water treatment system to manage runoff, and residual fuel remains in the tanks pending further cleanup. **The site is not accessible for recreational diving; an active exclusion zone remains in place under Transport Canada and Canadian Coast Guard direction.**

## Safety Considerations

This site is off-limits to recreational divers as of 2026. An active exclusion zone enforced by the Canadian Coast Guard prohibits approach. Monitor Transport Canada and Canadian Coast Guard advisories for any updates on exclusion zone status before considering any visit.
