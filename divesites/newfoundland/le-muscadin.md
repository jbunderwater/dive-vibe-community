## Le Muscadin

Le Muscadin is a historic wreck dive in Newfoundland, North America.

## Overview

Le Muscadin is charted as a wreck at around 25 meters in the waters around Saint-Pierre and Miquelon, though there is a notable discrepancy in the available sources (see "Site Identity Note" below) that divers should be aware of before planning a dive here. ## Site Identity Note

A France Télévisions regional news report describes a wooden vessel named "Le Muscadin" — built in 1967 at Bonavista, Newfoundland (reportedly the last wooden boat built there), originally a fishing boat named *Prudence Ann*, later renamed and used as a bulk/sand-carrying vessel — as having been deliberately beached and preserved on shore at the entrance to Miquelon village in 2011, where it remains today as a heritage piece rather than a submerged dive site. It is not confirmed whether this is the same vessel as the OSM-charted underwater wreck at these coordinates (offshore, ~25 m depth), or a different vessel that happens to share the name. Only one substantive published source discusses the Muscadin's history, short of the two independent sources this destination's QA process requires for wreck-history claims, so none of that history has been asserted as fact for this entry. **Divers should verify with a local operator whether this charted position is an actual diveable wreck before planning a trip.**

## Dive Profile

OSM data records a maximum depth of approximately 25 m at this position. The French archipelago's dive season runs primarily June to October, organised by the Club Nautique Saint-Pierrais — the principal diving association in the territory. A drysuit is recommended for all dives in Saint-Pierre waters, where temperatures range from approximately 7°C in the cold season to around 14°C at summer surface layers. These are regional generalities, not confirmed details of this specific site.

## Entry and Exit

Boat dive operated from Saint-Pierre. The Club Nautique Saint-Pierrais organises certified-diver excursions throughout the dive season.

## Safety Considerations

Cold water (drysuit recommended year-round), variable visibility, and the remote North Atlantic setting are the principal hazards. Divers should ensure they are diving with a qualified local operator familiar with the specific site conditions around Saint-Pierre.
