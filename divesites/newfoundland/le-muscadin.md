## Le Muscadin

Le Muscadin is a beached wooden hulk on land at Miquelon village — NOT an underwater dive site.

## Overview

**Correction (2026-08-15 re-audit):** previous versions of this entry described Le Muscadin as a French fishing vessel that "sank" and rests at 25 meters depth with nudibranchs, sea stars, and soft coral on the hull. Research for this update found no support for any of that — it appears to have been fabricated. According to la1ere.franceinfo.fr (France Télévisions' Saint-Pierre-et-Miquelon regional outlet), Le Muscadin is a wooden fishing boat built in 1967 (the last wooden boat built at the Bonavista, Newfoundland workshops), originally named Prudence Ann and used to carry herring. Fisherman Joseph Poirier later bought her and renamed her Muscadin, a family nickname. In 2011 she was **deliberately run aground on land** at the entrance to Miquelon village, on the road to Langlade, with plans (never fully realised) for a museum space; the nearby Maison de la Nature et de l'Environnement opened in 2017 as a separate facility. The hulk remains at the village entrance, slowly deteriorating from weather and time. She is not underwater and is not a scuba dive site.

**Only one independent source was found confirming this correction** — below this destination's 3-source validation bar — so this entry is marked `validated: false` pending further research. It is flagged for maintainer review: Le Muscadin does not appear to belong in a dive-site database at all, since it is not diveable.

## History

Built 1967, Bonavista, Newfoundland (last wooden vessel built at those workshops); originally named Prudence Ann, used for herring fishing; later purchased and renamed Muscadin by Joseph Poirier. Deliberately grounded on land at the Miquelon village entrance in 2011.

## Marine Life

Not applicable. The vessel is not underwater; no marine life claims apply.

## Safety Considerations

This is not a dive site. If visiting as a shore landmark, ordinary land-based caution around a deteriorating wooden structure applies (do not climb on or enter the hulk).
