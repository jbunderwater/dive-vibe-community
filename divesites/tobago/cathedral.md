## Cathedral

Cathedral (also referenced locally as Flying Manta) is a reef dive in Tobago's Speyside area — dramatic rocky overhangs and ledges give the site its name, and the site is within the northeast corridor known for manta ray encounters.

## Overview

Located in the Speyside area on Tobago's northeast coast, Cathedral takes its name from the dramatic rocky overhangs and ledges that create a cathedral-like underwater atmosphere. The site is part of the larger Speyside dive zone that benefits from the Guiana Current and upwelling from the South American continental shelf. Depths extend to approximately 37 metres on the deeper portions of the site. The site is also sometimes referred to as Flying Manta in local usage, (the identity of the two names rests on nearby listed coordinates and is not confirmed by a single source). Visibility in the Speyside area is typically Tobago's best, reaching 25–35 metres. Water temperature is 26–28°C, with cooler upwelling possible.
