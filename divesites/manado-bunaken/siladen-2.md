## Siladen 2

Siladen's walls drop steeply to around 35 metres with strong coral growth — as with Siladen 1, sources describe the island's walls collectively rather than distinguishing a numbered second section.

## Overview

As with Siladen 1, none of the sources reviewed for this pass separately document a "Siladen 2" as distinct from "Siladen 1" — Siladen's own dive map instead names points like Siladen Jetty and Siladen Utara. This entry, like its neighbour, is described using the general character of Siladen's walls confirmed by Starfish.ch: steep, dropping to about 35 metres, with very good coral growth and abundant nudibranchs.

## What to Expect

Coral growth on the wall is reported as very good, with abundant nudibranchs. Murex's "Siladen Wall" entry additionally reports cuttlefish, turtles and schooling fusiliers. Previously invented claims that this site has "larger gorgonian fans," "more grey reef sharks," or an "east-facing" orientation distinct from Siladen 1 are not supported by any source and have been removed.

## Conditions

- **Depth range:** 5-35 m
- **Visibility:** 15-30 m
- **Currents:** Moderate to strong; direction and strength variable
- **Water temperature:** 26-30°C
- **Best season:** May-October

## Tips

Ask your Siladen-based operator which specific wall section they mean by "Siladen 2" — naming isn't standardised across sources.
