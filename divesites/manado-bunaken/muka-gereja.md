## Muka Gereja

Muka Gereja — "in front of the church" in Indonesian — is a wall dive with very little current, often used as a first dive in the park.

## Overview

The site is described as a steep wall with caverns and overhanging cliffs, large barrel sponges, and rich coral growth. Visibility can reach up to around 30 metres in good conditions. The calm conditions and shallow, snorkel-friendly water above the wall make it accessible to non-divers on the surface as well.

## What to Expect

On the wall, giant frogfish, flatworms, orangutan crabs, and nudibranchs have all been reported. In open water, the usual pelagic mix includes whitetip and blacktip reef sharks (often resting rather than actively hunting) and eagle rays. Angelfish, butterflyfish, parrotfish, sweetlips, turtles, and sea snakes round out the reef community.

## Conditions

- **Depth range:** Up to 25-30 m depending on source
- **Currents:** Mild to very light — one of the calmer sites in the park
- **Best for:** First/orientation dives, snorkelling companions

## Tips

The barrel sponges and overhanging cliffs are worth a slow, deliberate look — the frogfish and orangutan crabs reported here tend to be well camouflaged.
