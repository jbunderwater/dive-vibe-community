## Cela Cela

Cela Cela (Celah Celah) is a wall cut with cracks and fissures on Bunaken's south coast; sources disagree on how strong its current runs.

## Overview

Dive The World describes Cela Cela as a wall with drift potential and "strong current running parallel to the reef," while Divezone lists it primarily as a convenient night-dive site close to the Bunaken beach, implying calmer conditions. Given this conflict, this listing is graded Intermediate rather than Beginner pending clarification from a dive operator.

## What to Expect

Dive The World reports pygmy seahorses, ghost pipefish, giant morays, sea snakes, dogtooth tuna and occasional eagle rays and blacktip sharks. Tasik Ria adds whitetip and blacktip reef sharks, eagle rays, and green and hawksbill turtles. The site's defining feature — cracks and fissures running through the wall — is confirmed by both sources.

## Conditions

- **Depth range:** 3-30 m
- **Visibility:** 15-25 m
- **Currents:** Sources disagree: one reports strong current parallel to the reef, another describes it as a calm near-shore night-dive site
- **Water temperature:** 27-30°C
- **Best season:** May-October

## Tips

Bring a torch for the cracks and fissures — that's where the small critters concentrate. Ask locally about current strength before committing, since sources disagree on how exposed this site is.
