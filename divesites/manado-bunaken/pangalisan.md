## Pangalisan

Pangalisan is a vertical wall on Bunaken Island that descends in large steps, cut through with cracks and small caves.

## Overview

Visibility around the site is typically about 20-25 metres, and currents can run strong enough to make this a drift-style dive. Average recorded depth on one dive tracking database is around 27 metres, with current in the 1-2 knot range.

## What to Expect

Lionfish, turtles, sharks, nudibranchs, sea urchins, feather stars, and anemones are all reported here, along with crabs, lobsters, and tuna passing in open water. One operator's description of a nearby "Pangalingan" site — south of Manado Tua, a drift dive with schooling snappers and fusiliers fed by strong current — is a plausible match for this site given the name similarity, but it could not be confirmed with certainty to be the same location, so is noted here rather than presented as fact.

## Conditions

- **Depth range:** ~5-35 m (average ~27 m)
- **Currents:** Strong; suited to drift diving
- **Visibility:** ~20-25 m

## Tips

Treat this as a current-dependent dive — confirm tidal timing with your operator, since the step topography can make navigation trickier if the current picks up mid-dive.
