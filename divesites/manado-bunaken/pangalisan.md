## Pangalisan

Pangalisan (Pangalisang) is a stepped wall on Bunaken's east side where a shallow terrace of coral gardens gives way to a 35-metre wall trimmed with lace coral.

## Overview

Siladen Resort's site guide and DiveJourney both describe Pangalisan's reef flat sloping to roughly 10 metres before dropping into a near-vertical wall to 35 metres. Currents are generally a gentle-to-moderate drift, with occasional eddies along the wall.

## What to Expect

Sources report thousands of reef fish on the reef top, large unicornfish and needlefish in the shallows, and schooling long-jawed mackerel and fusiliers. On the wall, pygmy seahorses, halimeda ghost pipefish, ornate ghost pipefish and squat lobsters are reported, along with turtles and pyramid butterflyfish. Dugongs are mentioned as an occasional, not reliable, sighting by one source.

## Conditions

- **Depth range:** 3-35 m
- **Visibility:** 15-25 m
- **Currents:** Light to moderate, gentle drift with occasional eddies
- **Water temperature:** 27-30°C
- **Best season:** May-October

## Tips

The eddies along the wall are a good place to slow down and search for the site's ghost pipefish and squat lobsters rather than drifting straight through.
