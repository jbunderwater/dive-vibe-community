## Sachiko's

Sachiko's Point is a sloping wall dive on Bunaken Island reported to be a good spot for concentrations of larger fish.

## Overview

Sources describe a steep slope/sloping terrain topography with wire corals present, and caves at depth where sharks rest. Current is typically none to light, though some divers report "nice current" on certain visits, and the site is generally recommended for advanced divers, though it can suit beginners in calm conditions.

## What to Expect

Big fish are the highlight: large tuna, emperors, angelfish, banner fish, and sweetlips, plus turtles. Whitetip sharks are reported resting in caves at depth, while blacktip reef sharks are seen actively hunting. Reef fish present include damselfish, butterflyfish, parrotfish, fusiliers, and wrasse. One source also notes very large schools of red-tooth triggerfish. Visibility is frequently excellent, with 30 m+ reported.

## Conditions

- **Depth range:** ~10-25 m sloping terrain, extending deeper
- **Currents:** Typically none to light, occasional stronger current
- **Visibility:** Often 30 m+
- **Diving level:** Advanced

## Tips

Check the caves at depth carefully for resting whitetip sharks, and watch the open water for the larger tuna and emperor activity this site is known for.
