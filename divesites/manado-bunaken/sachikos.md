## Sachiko's

Sachiko's Point is a wall dive on Bunaken's north-east coast running from the shallows to beyond 40 metres, known for strong currents and large trevally schools.

## Overview

Murex Resorts and Dive The World both describe Sachiko's Point as a wall dive with strong current, shallow to 40-plus metres. No source consulted for this review confirms an origin story about the site being named after a specific Japanese diver who "championed" it — that detail has been removed as unsourced folklore.

## What to Expect

Murex reports barracuda, blacktip reef sharks, Napoleon wrasse, batfish, moray eels and bumphead parrotfish. Dive The World adds schooling blue-fin trevally, giant trevally, green turtles and blue-spotted stingrays.

## Conditions

- **Depth range:** Shallow to 40+ m
- **Visibility:** 15-30 m
- **Currents:** Strong
- **Water temperature:** 27-29°C
- **Best season:** April-October (calmer on this coast)

## Tips

Currents here are consistently strong — this is not a first-Bunaken-dive site. A negative entry direct to depth is commonly used given surface conditions.
