## Muka kampung

Muka kampung, nicknamed "Turtle City," sits directly in front of Bunaken Island's main village and is one of the most reliable turtle-viewing wall dives in the national park.

## Overview

Muka means "in front of" and kampung means "village" in Bahasa Indonesia — the site takes its name from its position facing Bunaken's main village. PADI and Two Fish Divers (whose resort adopted this site under the PADI AWARE Dive Against Debris programme) both describe it as a wall dive with a large resident population of green and hawksbill turtles, average depth around 22 metres, and a permanent mooring line near a sandy sloping bay.

## What to Expect

Turtles are the headline attraction — sources describe a resident population numbering in the dozens across the site. Reported sightings include trumpetfish, angelfish, triggerfish, butterflyfish, clownfish, pelagic fish, reef sharks, eagle rays and moray eels, alongside soft and hard coral cover on the wall face.

## Conditions

- **Depth range:** 5-22 m
- **Visibility:** 15-25 m
- **Currents:** 1-3 knots
- **Water temperature:** 25-30°C
- **Best season:** May-October

## Tips

Two Fish Divers has run reef-clean-up (Dive Against Debris) events at this site — ask about current conservation initiatives when you book. Morning dives tend to catch turtles more active and feeding.
