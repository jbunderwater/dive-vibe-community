## Muka kampung

Muka kampung, nicknamed "Turtle City" by operators, is a steep sloping wall on Bunaken Island reaching a maximum depth of around 30 metres, accessed via a permanent boat mooring line.

## Overview

The site's nickname comes directly from its reputation for turtle sightings — PADI's dive site listing describes it as home to green and hawksbill turtles as a headline feature. The topography is a steep sloping wall with some sandy areas, including a sandy sloping bay near the mooring line. Current is typically mild to medium, with occasional mild up-and-down currents.

## What to Expect

Large schools of pyramid butterflyfish and red-tooth triggerfish are reported here, along with pelagic visitors such as Spanish mackerel, tuna, eagle rays, and small reef sharks. On the bottom, divers can look for shrimp, lionfish, leaf scorpionfish, moray eels, and octopus, plus the usual mix of reef fish, nudibranchs, flatworms, and crabs among the coral and sponges.

## Conditions

- **Depth range:** Up to 30 m
- **Currents:** Mild to medium, with occasional mild vertical currents
- **Entry:** Boat, via permanent mooring line

## Tips

Current can shift direction slightly during the dive given the up/down currents reported here, so stay aware of your position relative to the wall and the mooring line.
