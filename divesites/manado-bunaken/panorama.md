## Panorama

Panorama is associated with Panorama Dive Resort's house reef on Bunaken, described by the resort as a shallow coral garden giving way to a drop-off with turtles.

## Overview

Unlike most sites in this database, "Panorama" does not appear as an independently named dive site in the broader Bunaken dive-shop and forum literature reviewed for this pass — it is documented primarily through Panorama Dive Resort's own description of its house reef: a shallow seagrass and coral-nursery area giving way to a drop-off, with turtles a consistent sighting and occasional sharks and mantas. Other results returned for "Panorama" were resort reviews (TripAdvisor, Wanderlog) rather than dive-site-specific sources, so this description leans more heavily on a single operator source than other entries in this database.

## What to Expect

The resort's own materials describe turtles as a near-certainty and mention occasional sharks and manta rays, without further species detail. Because this is a single-source description, treat the marine-life claims here as less independently verified than other sites in this destination.

## Conditions

- **Depth range:** 4-25 m (resort house-reef description)
- **Visibility:** 15-25 m (regional estimate, not independently confirmed)
- **Currents:** Described as generally calm, especially compared to Bunaken's east coast
- **Water temperature:** 27-30°C
- **Best season:** May-October

## Tips

If diving with Panorama Dive Resort, ask staff directly which offshore sites they mean by "Panorama" versus the house reef — the name isn't standardised in the wider literature.
