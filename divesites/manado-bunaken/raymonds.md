## Raymond's

Raymond's Point is a vertical wall on Bunaken Island's western shores, usually carrying strong current and often dived as a drift.

## Overview

The site is a steep sloping wall with an unusual abundance of wire corals, many hosting wire coral shrimp and xenia crabs. A large overhanging cave is a notable feature, along with a spectacular reef top covered in hard and soft corals.

## What to Expect

The overhanging cave is home to large lobsters and crabs. Current is most of the time mild to moderate but can turn strong depending on the tide, so this is classed as an advanced-level site. Reported depth range is 15-40 metres with visibility of 10-30 metres.

## Conditions

- **Depth range:** 15-40 m
- **Currents:** Mild-moderate typically, can be strong
- **Visibility:** 10-30 m
- **Diving level:** Advanced

## Tips

Check the overhanging cave carefully with a torch for lobsters and crabs, but be mindful of current strength near the cave mouth, which can pick up unpredictably with the tide.
