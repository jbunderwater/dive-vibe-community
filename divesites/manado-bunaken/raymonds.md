## Raymond's

Raymond's Point is a vertical wall on Bunaken's north-west coast with usually strong current, a single large cave, and good photography conditions on the shallower reef top.

## Overview

Divezone describes Raymond's Point as a vertical wall with usually strong current that makes it a good drift-dive site, plus one large cave worth exploring. Tasik Ria lists the site's depth range as roughly 5-35 metres with sponge and coral cover on the wall.

## What to Expect

Confirmed features are the wall's hard and soft coral cover, the large cave, and blue-spotted stingrays reported on the site. Photography conditions on the reef top are noted as good. No source consulted for this review documents the site's exact naming origin, so any "named after a local guide" story should be treated as unconfirmed folklore rather than fact.

## Conditions

- **Depth range:** 5-35 m
- **Visibility:** 15-30 m
- **Currents:** Usually strong
- **Water temperature:** 26-30°C
- **Best season:** May-October

## Tips

Expect to dive this one as a drift — plan for a downcurrent pickup rather than trying to swim back to a fixed point. The large cave is worth a look if your guide has time to explore it.
