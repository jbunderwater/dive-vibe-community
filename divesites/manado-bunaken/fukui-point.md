## Fukui Point

Fukui Point is described by PADI as "the only sloping reef in Bunaken — all other dive sites are walls," making it a distinctive change of pace within the park.

## Overview

**Note on a source conflict:** Most operator sources (PADI, Divezone) describe Fukui as a gently sloping reef, and PADI is explicit that it is the park's one exception to the wall-diving norm. One independent source (starfish.ch) instead describes it as "a wall dive," with giant tridacna (clam) shells and coral damage noted around them. Given that two sources agree on the sloping-reef character and it is PADI's most specific, emphatic claim, `site_type: reef` is kept here, but readers should be aware of the conflicting description.

The site features coral gardens, an artificial reef structure, a cleaning station, and a pinnacle near the corner. Current is usually zero to mild but can strengthen toward medium-strong, especially near the corner.

## What to Expect

Giant clams, sea turtles, barrel sponges, garden eels, blue-spotted rays, and octopuses are reported on the reef itself. Near the pinnacle and corner, larger pelagics — eagle rays, giant barracuda, giant trevally, and tuna — are seen more often. Other species reported include lionfish, trumpetfish, butterflyfish, angelfish, scorpionfish, stingrays, and moray eels.

## Conditions

- **Depth range:** Up to 30 m
- **Currents:** Zero to mild typically, medium-strong at the corner
- **Diving level:** Beginner-friendly on the reef flat, more current-aware diving needed at the corner

## Tips

Spend most of your bottom time on the coral garden and cleaning station rather than pushing to the corner unless conditions are calm — the corner's current can turn a relaxed reef dive into a demanding one quickly.
