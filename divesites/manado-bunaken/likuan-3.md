## Likuan 3

Likuan 3 (Lekuan III) is the northern section of Bunaken's Lekuan wall system, generally regarded as the calmer of the three numbered sites.

## Overview

As with Likuan 1 and 2, sources describe the Lekuan wall as one continuous system rather than sharply distinct numbered sites. Murex rates the sites Beginner-to-Advanced depending on section and current; the wall drops well past recreational depth at its base.

## What to Expect

Reported sightings across the Lekuan system include pyramid butterflyfish, green sea turtles, reef sharks, eagle rays, pygmy seahorses, orangutan crabs and nudibranchs. Cela Cela and Lekuan III are also both cited as good night-dive locations by Starfish.ch's guide.

## Conditions

- **Depth range:** 3-40+ m
- **Visibility:** 15-25 m
- **Currents:** Light to moderate; generally the calmest of the Lekuan sections
- **Water temperature:** 27-30°C
- **Best season:** May-October

## Tips

Operators often recommend Likuan 3 as an introduction to the Lekuan wall system before attempting the deeper, more current-exposed sections.
