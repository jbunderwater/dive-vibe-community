## Pangulingan

Pangulingan is a wall dive at the northern end of Bunaken Island that opens onto a steep, deep wall before transitioning through a coral slope and a shallower boulder field.

## Overview

The dive starts with a steep and deep wall where big pelagic species pass in the blue — tuna, schools of jacks, whitetip and blacktip sharks, barracuda, and turtles have all been reported. The wall then gives way to a coral slope at roughly 20-25 metres, rich with reef fish, soft and hard corals, and groups of batfish.

## What to Expect

Continuing up from the coral slope, divers reach a boulder area in the 5-10 metre range, where nudibranchs, ribbon eels, moray eels, mantis shrimp, and crabs are commonly found. Sources note the site is best in medium to strong current — not recommended for beginners in those conditions.

## Conditions

- **Depth range:** ~5-35 m across the wall, slope, and boulder sections
- **Currents:** Can be medium to strong
- **Diving level:** Advanced

## Tips

The site rewards a full-profile dive — don't rush past the boulder field at the end, as the macro life there (ribbon eels, mantis shrimp) is a highlight distinct from the wall's big-fish action.
