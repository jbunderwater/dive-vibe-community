## Pangulingan

Pangulingan is a steep, deep wall northeast of Manado Tua where strong currents draw big pelagics before the dive settles onto a sandy boulder slope rich in macro life.

## Overview

Starfish.ch's Zubi guide places Pangulingan (also spelled Pandalingan) northeast of Manado Tua, describing strong currents and large schools of mackerel, barracuda, whitetip reef sharks, Napoleon wrasse and tuna. Operator descriptions consistently frame the dive in two acts: a steep wall in the blue, followed by a coral-boulder slope around 20-25 metres.

## What to Expect

In open water along the wall: tuna, schools of jacks, whitetip and blacktip sharks, barracuda and large turtles are reported. On the coral-boulder slope at 20-25 metres, expect batfish and reef fish; further up in the 5-10 metre boulder field, look for nudibranchs, ribbon eels, moray eels, mantis shrimp and crabs.

## Conditions

- **Depth range:** 5-35 m
- **Visibility:** 15-30 m
- **Currents:** Strong and can be unpredictable
- **Water temperature:** 26-30°C
- **Best season:** May-October

## Tips

Current reading before entry matters here — this is a site where the boat crew's experience makes a real difference. Save air for the shallower boulder field, where the macro life rewards a slow final stretch of the dive.
