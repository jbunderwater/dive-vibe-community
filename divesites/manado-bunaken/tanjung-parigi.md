## Tanjung Parigi

Tanjung Parigi ("Well Corner") is a vertical wall on Bunaken's north-east side with a snaking, upright profile and a shore-accessible sandy-slope entry inside the bay.

## Overview

PADI's site page places Tanjung Parigi in the north bay of Bunaken, taking its name from a small village nearby. It describes a "snaky wall standing upright" with an exposed reef top, black coral, sponges and gorgonians, entered via a large sandy slope with patches of reef inside the bay, and notes the site is diveable year-round.

## What to Expect

Confirmed features: black coral, sponges and gorgonians on the wall; on the sandy slope, squat lobsters, scorpionfish and jawfish are reported as interesting critter finds. Pelagic fish are described as an occasional, lucky sighting rather than a guaranteed one — sources do not name specific pelagic species at this site.

## Conditions

- **Depth range:** 3-35 m
- **Visibility:** 15-25 m
- **Currents:** Variable; sources do not report a specific rating
- **Water temperature:** 27-29°C
- **Best season:** Year-round, weather permitting

## Tips

Search the sandy slope inside the bay carefully for squat lobsters and jawfish before heading out to the wall proper.
