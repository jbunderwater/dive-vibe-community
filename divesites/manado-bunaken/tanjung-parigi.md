## Tanjung Parigi

Tanjung Parigi — "Well Corner" in Indonesian, named for a small village in the northern bay of Bunaken — is a wall dive that begins inside a bay on a large sandy slope with patches of reef before meeting the wall at a corner.

## Overview

PADI's site listing notes a stronger current specifically on the corner, between the Lekuan 2 and Lekuan 3 sections of wall. The dive combines a reef/wall transition with hard and soft corals.

## What to Expect

Larger species reported include eagle rays, bumphead parrotfish, tuna, rainbow runner, sharks, Napoleon fish, and whitetip/blacktip reef sharks. Schools of red-tooth triggerfish and pyramid butterflyfish are common, alongside angelfish, batfish, and general reef fish. Macro life includes leaf scorpionfish, nudibranchs, decorator crabs, pygmy seahorses, lionfish, and shrimp. This is a boat-access-only site.

## Conditions

- **Depth range:** Not explicitly stated by the source, but part of the deeper Bunaken wall system
- **Currents:** Stronger specifically at the corner
- **Entry:** Boat only

## Tips

The bay's sandy slope at the start of the dive is a good spot to look for macro life before the current picks up at the corner.
