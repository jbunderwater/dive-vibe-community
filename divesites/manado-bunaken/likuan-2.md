## Likuan 2

Likuan 2 (Lekuan II) sits at the centre of Bunaken Island's Lekuan wall series, a vertical wall descending in deep water with numerous small caves and sandy slopes.

## Overview

The wall drops from a shallow crest, with divers able to finish dives in shallow coral gardens around 3 metres. Currents here are typically medium, and visibility can exceed 30 metres in good conditions. The site sits immediately adjacent to Likuan I within Bunaken National Marine Park.

## What to Expect

Sources describe splendid marine biodiversity: black-tip and white-tip reef sharks (reported resting in caves), sea turtles on ledges, barracuda, and — occasionally — manta rays. Napoleon wrasse, emperor angelfish, groupers, lionfish, and scorpionfish are also reported, along with schools of fusiliers and dense macro life among the sponges.

## Conditions

- **Depth range:** ~2-40+ m
- **Currents:** Medium
- **Visibility:** Can exceed 30 m
- **Diving level:** Advanced

## Tips

The macro life here is genuinely rich — budget time near the smaller caves and sponge formations rather than only scanning the blue for pelagics.
