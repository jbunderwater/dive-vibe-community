## Likuan 2

Likuan 2 (Lekuan II) is the central section of Bunaken's Lekuan wall system, with a wall that Dive The World reports extending beyond 60 metres.

## Overview

Divezone describes Likuan II as "a very nice dive site with a wall descending in deep water." Dive The World's overview of the Lekuan sites as a group notes the wall runs well beyond recreational limits, and multiple sources treat Lekuan II as the busiest and most photographed of the three sections.

## What to Expect

Reported sightings across the Lekuan sites include green sea turtles, whitetip reef sharks, pyramid butterflyfish, eagle rays, pygmy seahorses and nudibranchs. Barrel sponges and gorgonian sea fans are consistently mentioned as wall features.

## Conditions

- **Depth range:** 2-40+ m
- **Visibility:** 15-30 m
- **Currents:** Moderate to strong; upwellings possible
- **Water temperature:** 27-30°C
- **Best season:** May-October

## Tips

This is often the busiest of the three Lekuan sites — an early morning slot gives the best light and fewer other groups on the wall.
