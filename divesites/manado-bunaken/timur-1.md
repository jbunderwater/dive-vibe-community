## Timur 1

Timur 1 is the southern section of Bunaken's east-coast wall, a steep face with good reef life that sees less traffic than the south-coast sites.

## Overview

Sources describe Bunaken Timur collectively as a steep wall on the island's east side, roughly 35-40 metres deep, with caves and cracks and good macro photography opportunities. As with Timur 2, individual numbered sections aren't separately documented by the sources reviewed, so this description covers the wall system as a whole.

## What to Expect

Green and hawksbill turtles are consistently reported, along with occasional eagle rays and an array of macro critters. The wall carries fire coral in places alongside its soft and hard coral cover.

## Conditions

- **Depth range:** 4-40 m
- **Visibility:** 15-25 m
- **Currents:** Can be present; normally reserved for intermediate divers
- **Water temperature:** 27-30°C
- **Best season:** May-October; reduce east-coast visits in north-east swell season

## Tips

Timur 1 and 2 pair well as a single-day excursion when east-coast conditions are favourable.
