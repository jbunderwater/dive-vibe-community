## Gorango

Gorango — "shark" in the local Manadonese dialect — is a wall dive in the northern Mantehage-area waters of Bunaken National Marine Park, not on Bangka Island as a previous QA pass incorrectly recorded.

## Overview

Raja Laut Dive Resort and Coral View Dive Resort both translate Gorango as "shark" in Manadonese and describe wall diving with colourful hard and soft coral. Notably, Raja Laut's own description adds that the site "does not have more sharks than any other spot" — directly contradicting a separate, less clearly sourced claim (found via aggregated search, not independently verified) that grey reef and hammerhead sharks are regularly seen here. Given this conflict, shark sightings at Gorango should be treated as unconfirmed despite the site's name. Coordinates place the site near Mantehage island, consistent with an older page title referencing "Mantehage Island Gorango," not Bangka Island.

## What to Expect

Confirmed: a wall with colourful hard and soft coral. One less-reliable aggregated source additionally describes cracks and vertical canyons with parrotfish, surgeonfish, triggerfish, snappers and groupers, and mentions occasional bumphead parrotfish and Napoleon wrasse schools — these are noted here as reported but not independently re-verified. As above, shark sightings are actively disputed by one of the two confirmed sources, so they are not listed as a confirmed feature.

## Conditions

- **Depth range:** Field lists 30 m; not independently re-confirmed this pass
- **Visibility:** 15-25 m (regional estimate)
- **Currents:** Moderate to strong per one source; not independently confirmed by the second
- **Water temperature:** 27-30°C
- **Best season:** May-October

## Tips

Don't dive Gorango expecting guaranteed shark sightings — despite the name, one of the two operator sources located for this review explicitly says the site has no more sharks than anywhere else in the park.
