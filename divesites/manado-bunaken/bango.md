## Bango

Bango is a sandy-slope wall dive named after a nearby village in the northern waters of Bunaken National Marine Park, not on Bangka Island as a previous QA pass incorrectly recorded.

## Overview

Two independent operator guides (Raja Laut Dive Resort and Coral View Dive Resort) describe Bango consistently: named after the village, a sandy slope with outstanding soft coral and many critters. Neither source places it on Bangka Island — Bango's coordinates (1.693 N, 124.780 E) sit within the Mantehage/northern park area, roughly 3 km from the neighbouring Gorango site, and nowhere near Bangka Island's real location further north-east (around 1.85 N, 125.15 E).

## What to Expect

Confirmed features are a sandy slope with strong soft coral cover and a high density of small critters, with a wall section nearby featuring sea fans and large schools of fish. Neither source consulted names specific fish or invertebrate species at Bango, so treat any detailed species list as unconfirmed until a site-specific report is found.
