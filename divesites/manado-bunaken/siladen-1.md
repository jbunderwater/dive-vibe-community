## Siladen 1

Siladen's walls drop steeply to around 35 metres with strong coral growth — this numbered section is one of several dive points around the small island east of Bunaken.

## Overview

Starfish.ch describes the Siladen wall as "steep," dropping to about 35 metres with very good coral growth and abundant nudibranchs. None of the sources checked for this review distinguish a numbered "Siladen 1" from "Siladen 2" as separate named sites — Siladen's dive map instead lists points like Siladen Jetty and Siladen Utara. This description covers the general character of Siladen's walls rather than a verified distinction between the two numbered entries in our database.

## What to Expect

Coral growth on the wall is reported as very good, with abundant nudibranchs. Murex Resorts' "Siladen Wall" entry additionally reports cuttlefish, turtles and ribbons of schooling fusiliers. Beyond this, treat other species claims as general Coral Triangle fauna rather than confirmed for this specific numbered site.

## Conditions

- **Depth range:** 4-35 m
- **Visibility:** 15-30 m
- **Currents:** Light to moderate
- **Water temperature:** 27-30°C
- **Best season:** May-October

## Tips

Ask your Siladen-based operator which specific wall section they mean by "Siladen 1" — naming isn't standardised across sources.
