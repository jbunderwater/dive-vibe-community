## Timur 2

Timur 2 is part of the Bunaken Timur ("East Bunaken") wall system, sharing the same steep-wall, good-reef-life character as Timur 1 along Bunaken Island's east coast.

## Overview

Sources describe the Bunaken Timur wall as normal diving depth (10-25 m, wall dropping further), an excellent macro photography location thanks to numerous small caves and cracks, and one of the park's most popular sites overall, generally with no current.

## What to Expect

Whitetip reef sharks and turtles are reported "with some luck," alongside butterflyfish, parrotfish, surgeonfish, wrasse, and stingray. Banded sea kraits are also commonly reported moving along this stretch of wall.

## Conditions

- **Depth range:** ~10-40 m
- **Currents:** Typically none to light
- **Diving level:** Advanced recommended by some operators, though listed here as Intermediate

## Tips

Because Timur 1 and Timur 2 share the same wall system, operators often combine them in a single-day excursion on days with favourable east-coast conditions.
