## Timur 2

Timur 2 is the northern section of Bunaken's east-side wall, a steep coral face known for macro life packed into its caves and cracks.

## Overview

Bunaken's east wall (Timur, meaning "East") is broken into sections by local operators; Divezone and Dive The World describe a wall to roughly 35-40 metres with good reef life, small caves and cracks, and note the site is well suited to macro photography.

## What to Expect

Confirmed sightings include green and hawksbill turtles, occasional eagle rays, and dense nudibranch diversity — Tasik Ria specifically notes Chromodoris and Nembrotha species and the occasional zebra butterflyfish alongside the more common pyramid butterflyfish. Fire coral is present on parts of the wall.

## Conditions

- **Depth range:** 4-40 m
- **Visibility:** 15-25 m
- **Currents:** Can be present; normally reserved for intermediate divers
- **Water temperature:** 27-30°C
- **Best season:** May-October; reduce east-coast visits in north-east swell season

## Tips

Bring a torch for the caves and cracks — the nudibranch diversity here is one of the better reasons to slow down rather than cruise the wall.
