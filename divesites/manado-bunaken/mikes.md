## Mike's

Mike's Point is an advanced wall off Bunaken's north-west corner, shaped like a natural amphitheatre and known for silvertip shark sightings on its deeper reaches.

## Overview

Divezone.net describes Mike's Point as "a very beautiful wall with a shape of an amphitheatre... covered with massive corals," with silvertip sharks reported here specifically. Tasik Ria lists it as a coral wall running 15-40 metres.

## What to Expect

Confirmed sightings include silvertip sharks along the deeper wall, plus bumphead parrotfish, Napoleon wrasse, mackerel and tuna in the water column. The wall face carries dense sponge growth. Beyond these species, treat other marine life as part of the Coral Triangle's general fauna rather than confirmed for this specific site.

## Conditions

- **Depth range:** 15-40 m
- **Visibility:** 15-30 m
- **Currents:** Moderate to strong; can be unpredictable
- **Water temperature:** 26-30°C
- **Best season:** May-October

## Tips

This is not a site for uncertain buoyancy — currents can shift direction. Dive with a guide who knows the site, and keep an SMB ready given the open-water return to the boat.
