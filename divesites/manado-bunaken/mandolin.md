## Mandolin

Mandolin is a wall dive in the strait between Bunaken Island and Manado Tua, where the wall drops away into very deep offshore water.

## Overview

Current here is often strong, which makes the site suited to drift diving. The wall stretches along the western side of Bunaken Island for several kilometres as part of the broader wall system.

## What to Expect

Napoleon wrasse and bumphead parrotfish are reported, along with dugong sightings noted by at least one source. A separate source describes the reef with small outcrops starting around 45 metres, and lists sharks and tuna as species seen from the site.

## Conditions

- **Depth range:** ~10-45 m depending on how deep the dive is taken
- **Currents:** Often strong
- **Diving level:** Intermediate to advanced given current strength

## Tips

Given the strong-current reputation and depth, confirm entry timing with your operator and be prepared for a drift-style dive rather than a static wall inspection.
