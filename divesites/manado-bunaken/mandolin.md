## Mandolin

Mandolin is named for the reef's mandolin-shaped outline from above, dropping in steps to 20-45 metres with overhangs and ledges.

## Overview

PADI's site page confirms the mandolin-instrument naming origin and describes a steep wall descending in steps with overhangs and ledges, usually dived as a drift due to mild-to-moderate current. Dive The World lists a deeper range of 20-45 metres and rates the site Advanced; Divezone independently describes a wall "going deep down with large steps" and numerous small caves. Sources vary on difficulty — this listing follows Dive The World's Advanced rating given the depth range documented, while PADI's description reads more moderately.

## What to Expect

Dive The World reports whip corals with commensal shrimp and whip gobies, Napoleon (Maori) wrasse, bumphead parrotfish, mappa pufferfish and whitetip reef sharks, plus porcelain crabs on bubble coral and candy crabs on soft coral.

## Conditions

- **Depth range:** 20-45 m
- **Visibility:** 15-25 m
- **Currents:** Usually mild to moderate; can run stronger at depth
- **Water temperature:** 27-30°C
- **Best season:** May-October

## Tips

This is a drift site by design — let the current carry you along the steps rather than fighting it, and budget gas carefully given the deeper range some sources report.
