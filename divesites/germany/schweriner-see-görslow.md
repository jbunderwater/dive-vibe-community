## Schweriner See - Görslow

A shore or boat dive on the east shore of the Schweriner Innensee with a shallow weedy plateau, old boat wrecks and the remains of a former dive parcours.

## Overview

Görslow lies on the east shore of the Schweriner Innensee. The local dive operator lists it as a site reached either by car (limited parking) or by a roughly 15-minute boat ride from Raben Steinfeld, with a maximum depth of about 25 m. A shallow plateau at about 3 m is densely vegetated; deeper down lie two old boat wrecks, a motorboat wreck with its steering wheel still in place, and the remains of an abandoned dive parcours whose objects are now heavily overgrown. The OSM entry tags the shore entry as steep.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Görslow, east shore of the Schweriner Innensee, Mecklenburg-Vorpommern
- **Entry Type**: Shore or boat entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 25 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Not documented
- **Current**: None reported (lake)
- **Best Time**: Not documented

## Underwater Features

Two old boat wrecks, a motorboat wreck with steering wheel, and an abandoned parcours with a bicycle, engine block, traffic light, benches, a toilet seat and an artificial sea mine - all heavily encrusted, according to the operator.

## Entry and Exit

Reached by car with limited parking, or by boat from the Raben Steinfeld base (dive-schwerin.de). There are no toilets on site. The OSM entry describes a steep shore entry.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Parking is limited, so arriving by boat from Raben Steinfeld may be easier
- The shallow 3 m plateau is worth time at the end of the dive

## Safety Considerations

Mind boat traffic on the Innensee when ascending. Cold water at depth calls for appropriate thermal protection.
