## Walchensee - Hackl

A relaxed shore dive on Walchensee's southern toll road, known for a sunken VW Beetle among tree trunks and rocks.

## Overview

Hackl lies on the toll road from Einsiedl towards Jachenau, about 2 km along at a larger parking area. Tauchclub Bamberg calls it a comfortable but interesting dive and well suited to training and night dives (especially the right-hand part). Walchensee is an alpine lake at about 800 m altitude in the Bavarian Prealps (Landkreis Bad Tölz-Wolfratshausen), with a maximum depth reported between about 192 m (Wikipedia) and 196-198 m (local dive sources). Diving is permitted here under a legal ordinance of the Landratsamt Bad Tölz-Wolfratshausen, but only at the released sites and times; several sites are closed from 15 October to 1 February. The lake feeds the Walchensee hydroelectric plant, so the water level is drawn down in winter (dive sources cite 4-5 m; Wikipedia gives a range between 795 m and 802 m), which changes entries noticeably.

## Site Information

- **Location**: South shore of Walchensee on the toll road from Einsiedl towards Jachenau
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 25 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Not documented; ColdWater Films notes it can at times be quite poor
- **Current**: None documented
- **Best Time**: 1 February to 14 October only - closed from 15 October to 1 February (ColdWater Films, Tauchclub Bamberg, Rabediver)

## Underwater Features

A VW Beetle wreck lies at about 15 m (ColdWater Films, DiveVision) or about 18 m (besser-tauchen.de, Rabediver), along with tree trunks and rocks; Rabediver highlights an underwater forest.

## Marine Life

Tauchclub Bamberg notes that fish are encountered rather rarely here. For the lake as a whole, local dive bases list lake trout, char (Saibling), pike, whitefish (Renke), perch, burbot (Aalrutte) and bullhead (Koppe).

## Dive Profile

Tauchclub Bamberg describes entering on the left side of the headland, descending to about 10 m and continuing left; the Beetle is reached after about 10 minutes and is easy to miss in poor visibility.

## Entry and Exit

Shore entry from the parking area via rock steps (Rabediver). The access road is a toll road.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Use a compass heading from the headland - the car is easy to miss in poor visibility
- Good site for training dives outside the closure period

## Safety Considerations

Tauchclub Bamberg rates it ideal for beginners, but the altitude, cold water and variable visibility still call for appropriate experience. Walchensee lies at roughly 800 m altitude, so altitude (Bergsee) decompression procedures apply. Local sources describe the water as very cold (about 4-8 °C) and recommend a dry suit and two independent cold-water regulators on a twin-outlet valve; lights are also listed as mandatory kit by one local source. Night diving is only allowed from one hour before sunrise until one hour after dark.
