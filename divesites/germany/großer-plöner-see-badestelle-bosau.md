## Großer Plöner See - Badestelle Bosau

A beach entry at the Bosau bathing area on the south-eastern shore: a long shallow section, then a silty slope into deeper water.

## Overview

The Großer Plöner See is the largest and deepest lake in Schleswig-Holstein. The tourism board gives a maximum depth of 58 m in the eastern Plön basin and 30 m in the shallower western Ascheberg basin, both formed by glaciers. It is a busy lake with scheduled passenger ships, sailing boats and canoes. For the Bosau area, the tourism board names the traditional divers' entry at the bathing area, about 250 m from the car park, with diving depths of up to 30 m. Taucher.Net reviewers describe the site as beginner-friendly but unspectacular.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Bosau, Kreis Ostholstein, Schleswig-Holstein
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: About 2-3 m (Taucher.Net); worsens immediately if the silt is disturbed
- **Current**: None
- **Best Time**: Not documented

## Underwater Features

Taucher.Net reviewers describe a long shallow section at 1-2 m followed by a slope. The bottom is thickly covered in silt, with broken branches, submerged wood and sparse water plants.

## Marine Life

Taucher.Net reviewers saw little fish activity but found freshwater crayfish.

## Entry and Exit

Entry is directly from the bathing beach, about 250 m from the car park (tourism board; Taucher.Net). Older Taucher.Net information restricts diving here to members of the VDST Schleswig-Holstein federation and requires registration with the local fisherman. The regional tourism board requires divers to leave their dive certification clearly visible in their car and to inform the holder of the fishing rights before diving; questions go to the state coastal agency (LKN). Swimming and diving are prohibited within 100 m of the passenger-ship landings while the boats are running (Wednesday-Sunday 11:00-18:00, plus Friday 19:00-21:00). Divers must follow the rules of the Schleswig-Holstein diving federation (TLV-SH), must not take any WWII munitions they find, and should report them. The diving club TUK e.V. (page last updated 2022) names the Plöner See as a lake that requires a personal TLV-SH authorisation card, issued only to TLV-SH members. The tourism board does not mention this, so check the current access rules before diving.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Excellent buoyancy control keeps the visibility intact over the silty bottom
- Plan your route around the hourly passenger-ship traffic (Taucher.Net)

## Safety Considerations

Heavy boat traffic in summer makes a surface marker buoy essential. WWII munitions have been dumped in Schleswig-Holstein lakes - never touch suspicious objects. The thermocline brings a sharp temperature drop, so dress for cold water.
