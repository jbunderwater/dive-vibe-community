## Schweriner See - Raben Steinfeld

The main shore entry on the south shore of the Schweriner See, with a dive base on a campground and a set of marked parcours, platforms and sunken boats in 5-20 m.

## Overview

The Raben Steinfeld entry lies at the southern end of the Schweriner Innensee, south-east of Schwerin. A dive base operates on the campground, and several marked routes start from its jetty. The house parcours in the shallows (to about 7 m) include an upturned telephone booth, a training platform at 5 m, a VW Derby car and a first boat wreck; a deeper parcours reaches about 16 m with small boat wrecks and a buoyancy tunnel at 7-10 m. Two wreck tours run to 19-20 m past a series of deliberately sunk pleasure boats and small vessels. Taucher.net reports a first platform at 6 m and a steep slope to 11 m. The lake itself reaches about 52 m (Wikipedia; 200bar gives ~60 m), but the dive routes here stay above 20 m.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Raben Steinfeld, south shore of the Schweriner See, Mecklenburg-Vorpommern
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 20 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Variable - reported as typically 2-3 m (taucher.net), with 10-15 m reported in July 2018 (200bar) and very clear water reported in October
- **Current**: None reported (lake)
- **Best Time**: Year-round according to 200bar; the campground hosting the dive base is seasonal (spring to autumn)

## Underwater Features

All reachable from the same jetty: telephone booth (about 2 m), training platforms (5 m and 12 m on the apnoea parcours), a mirror at 7 m, a VW Derby car, a buoyancy park with free-hanging frames, pallets used by pike, and a buoyancy tunnel at 7-10 m. The wreck tours pass a cabin cruiser (about 16 m), a sailing dinghy (about 17 m), a motor cruiser, a rescue cutter (about 17 m), a sailing yacht (about 20 m) and a motor ship (about 17 m) - all deliberately placed attractions rather than historic wrecks. The operator notes that its wreck tours require deep-diving training.

## Marine Life

Taucher.net reviews report perch, carp, pike and dense schools of small fish, especially under the jetty; 200bar mentions large pike at the first platform. The operator describes submerged trees with freshwater sponges in the Raben Steinfeld bay. Wikipedia lists perch, pike, pikeperch, eel and whitefish species (bream, rudd, roach, bleak) for the Schweriner See.

## Dive Profile

The shallow house parcours (to about 7 m, roughly 25 minutes) suits check-out and night dives; the deeper parcours goes to about 16 m, and the two wreck tours to 19-20 m take 50-70 minutes according to the operator.

## Entry and Exit

Entry through shallow water to the jetty; divers can kit up at the jetty or jump from it, and there is a sandy exit to the right of the jetty (taucher.net). Taucher.net and 200bar both mention a small entry fee payable at the base; fills are available on site. The base also runs boat shuttles to other sites on the lake.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Descend outside the boat jetties, as the operator advises
- The shallow parcours is suitable for night dives according to the operator
- Choose the deeper wreck tours only with deep-diving training

## Safety Considerations

The Schweriner See carries a lot of leisure boat traffic; 200bar advises watching for ferries and pleasure boats. Cold water below the thermocline calls for appropriate thermal protection.
