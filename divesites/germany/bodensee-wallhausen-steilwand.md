## Bodensee - Wallhausen Steilwand

A demanding wall dive at Konstanz-Wallhausen where the wall starts in hip-deep water and plunges past 80 m.

## Overview

The Wallhausen Steilwand lies on the Bodanrück shore of the Überlinger See at Konstanz-Wallhausen. Tauchteam Bodensee describes a steep wall beginning in hip-deep water and falling to 80 m and more (lake depth here around 90 m), with a rock formation known as the 'Klinker' at about 43 m that can be passed through at around 50 m. Both Tauchteam Bodensee and 200bar.de state that the site is only for experienced wall divers. 200bar.de notes that access has become heavily regulated by local conditions and restrictions.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Konstanz-Wallhausen (Uferstraße), Überlinger See, Baden-Württemberg
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 50 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 2-5 m reported, good to 5 m and worse below (200bar.de, Mar 2018)
- **Current**: Not documented
- **Best Time**: Not documented

## Underwater Features

Tauchteam Bodensee lists a small 'fir forest' at about 12 m, a surfboard at about 23 m and the Klinker rock formation at about 43 m.

## Entry and Exit

Tauchteam Bodensee describes entry via the slip ramp next to the harbour; a second forest entry was unavailable at the time of writing. Divers must keep at least 100 m from Wallhausen harbour and stay out of the diving-ban zone around navigation mark 22 (Teufelstisch), which 200bar.de says has been extended considerably towards Wallhausen. Tauchteam Bodensee mentions only a few parking spaces on Uferstraße.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Perfect buoyancy control is essential on this wall
- Know exactly where the Teufelstisch ban zone begins before entering
- Plan a depth limit in advance; the wall offers no natural bottom

## Safety Considerations

The wall drops to depths far beyond recreational limits with no bottom to stop a descent. 200bar.de notes that even in summer the Bodensee is only about 4-6 °C below roughly 10 m, so regulators can free-flow; cold-water regulators and cylinders with two separate outlets and two first stages are recommended, and first Bodensee dives should be made with a lake-experienced buddy. Under the Bodensee-Schifffahrts-Ordnung, shore divers must display a code flag 'A', and diving is prohibited within 100 m of harbour entrances and ferry landing areas; vessels always have right of way.
