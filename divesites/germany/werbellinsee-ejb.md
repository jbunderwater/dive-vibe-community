## Werbellinsee - EJB

A shore entry from the dive base on the grounds of the EJB Werbellinsee, with three training platforms and old wrecks to 14 m, and boat trips to the barge wrecks at Kap Horn.

## Overview

The dive base on the extensive shore grounds of the Europäische Jugenderholungs- und Begegnungsstätte (EJB) Werbellinsee, on the east shore about 2 km beyond Altenhof, is the only place on the private grounds where diving is allowed. Taucher.net gives a maximum depth of 14 m for the house site, with three underwater platforms suited to training and historic wrecks including a brick-carrying barge (Ziegelwrack). The base also runs boats to Kap Horn, where five historic barge wrecks lie at about 12-16 m.

## Site Information

- **Location**: EJB Werbellinsee, east shore south of Joachimsthal, Brandenburg
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 14 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Variable - 200bar reported 0.5-5 m for the lake in August 2018
- **Current**: None reported (lake)
- **Best Time**: 200bar lists a dive-base season of May-September at weekends, otherwise by prior arrangement

## Underwater Features

Three platforms for training and a brick-carrying barge wreck (taucher.net); taucher.net also mentions remains described as a church tower, which no other source confirms. By boat from the base: Kap Horn, with five historic wrecks, two of them damaged by anchoring boats and one in excellent condition (taucher.net Kap Horn page).

## Marine Life

Wikipedia lists roach, perch, pike, tench, burbot, eel, spined loach, three-spined stickleback and spiny-cheek crayfish for the Werbellinsee, with zebra mussels covering hard surfaces.

## Entry and Exit

Shore entry at the base on the EJB grounds, reached via the Joachimsthaler Straße on the east side of the lake. Diving is only permitted from the base, because the grounds are private (taucher.net); check before travelling that the base is staffed. Fills and rental are available on site. Kap Horn is most easily reached by boat from the base.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Check that the base is open before you travel
- The platforms make this a good site for training and check-out dives
- Book the boat to Kap Horn for the barge wrecks

## Safety Considerations

Taucher.net notes heavy boat traffic around Kap Horn and recommends a surface marker buoy. Emergency calls should give rescue point WRP 1-16 for the base (OSM) or WRP 1-17 for Kap Horn (taucher.net). Cold water at depth calls for appropriate thermal protection.
