## Bodensee - Überlingen Liebesinsel

A deep wall dive on the Überlinger See where the slope steps down to a near-vertical wall from about 28 m to beyond 45 m.

## Overview

Überlingen - Liebesinsel is one of several shore entries along the Überlingen lakefront on the Überlinger See, the north-western arm of Lake Constance. The bottom descends in stages from 6-7 m to an impressive wall starting at around 28 m, which drops almost vertically beyond 45 m; Tauchteam Bodensee describes undercut rock formations around 50 m and a monotonous mud slope below that, with total depth over 60 m. 200bar.de describes a shallow zone with sloping sand and remains of pile dwellings, with the wall starting from about 25 m, and gives 50 m as the site depth. Tauchteam Bodensee rates it for experienced and technical divers.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Überlingen (Seubertweg / In den Mühlen), Überlinger See, Baden-Württemberg
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 50 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 2-5 m reported (200bar.de, Feb 2018); weather-dependent
- **Current**: Not documented
- **Best Time**: Not documented

## Underwater Features

A submerged pipeline marks the main descent route. To the east a large fallen tree trunk reaches down to about 45 m; to the west the fractured wall has several cave-like niches. Remains of pile dwellings are reported in the shallows (200bar.de).

## Marine Life

200bar.de reports small perch in the rock crevices.

## Dive Profile

The drop-off starts quickly after entry, so there is little shallow water to cross. One-way dives to the neighbouring Überlingen entries (Post and Tennisplätze) are possible according to Tauchteam Bodensee.

## Entry and Exit

Entry is via stone steps at the promenade. Parking is at a paid car park above the railway crossing, followed by a short walk to the promenade; neighbouring business car parks are not for divers. 200bar.de asks divers to kit up at the car rather than leaving boxes on the shore, noting that this provokes diving bans.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Plan the dive around the wall depth; the site suits divers experienced in deep cold-water diving
- Carry adequate gas for decompression if going beyond recreational limits
- Use the pipeline as a navigation reference on the way down and back

## Safety Considerations

Depths beyond 40 m are reached quickly, with the associated narcosis and decompression risks. 200bar.de notes that even in summer the Bodensee is only about 4-6 °C below roughly 10 m, so regulators can free-flow; cold-water regulators and cylinders with two separate outlets and two first stages are recommended, and first Bodensee dives should be made with a lake-experienced buddy. Under the Bodensee-Schifffahrts-Ordnung, shore divers must display a code flag 'A', and diving is prohibited within 100 m of harbour entrances and ferry landing areas; vessels always have right of way.
