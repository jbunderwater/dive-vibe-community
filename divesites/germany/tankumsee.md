## Tankumsee

A shallow former gravel pit near Isenbüttel (Gifhorn) used mainly for dive training, with a training platform and a few placed objects.

## Overview

The Tankumsee was created around 1970 as a gravel pit during construction of the Elbe-Seitenkanal and opened as a bathing lake on 26 June 1976. It measures roughly 800 x 1,300 m (about 57 ha) and is a busy recreation area with a sand beach more than 1 km long. Wikipedia gives a maximum depth of 16.7 m; diver sources give 13 m (200bar.de) and 11 m (Tauchclub Wolfsburg). The local dive club TC Hibaru e.V. has its clubhouse on the north shore. Divers describe the lake as better suited to training than to sightseeing because of the limited visibility.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Isenbüttel, Landkreis Gifhorn, Lower Saxony
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 13 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Poor - about 3-4 m on average from May to September, below 1 m in winter, 0.5-1.5 m reported in October
- **Current**: None
- **Best Time**: May to September

## Underwater Features

200bar.de lists a bathtub, a stripped vehicle and a training platform. Taucher.Net reviewers describe a good training platform and a mostly sandy bottom.

## Marine Life

200bar.de reports many zander and eels, and pike over 1 m long. A Taucher.Net reviewer saw an eel; another found only sandy bottom with no visible life.

## Entry and Exit

According to 200bar.de, diving is free of charge; summer parking costs about EUR 5, and divers should notify the DLRG lifeguard station before diving. Registration with the local club is not required but welcome. Tauchclub Wolfsburg names the entries at the lakeside hotel and at the ramp by the sailing-boat jetties at the southern end as the best, and asks divers to keep diving inside the bathing zone to a minimum from May to September.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Use the lake for skills training rather than sightseeing
- Keep good buoyancy - visibility drops quickly once the bottom is stirred up
- Avoid the busy bathing area in summer

## Safety Considerations

Visibility is often around 1 m, so stay close to your buddy and use a compass or line. The lake is a busy bathing and water-sports area in summer; use a surface marker. Cold water outside summer requires suitable thermal protection.
