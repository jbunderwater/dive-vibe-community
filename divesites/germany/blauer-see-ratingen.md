## Blauer See Ratingen

A small former limestone quarry lake in a leisure park in Ratingen, with steep walls and stair entry, dived at weekends through an on-site dive school.

## Overview

The Blauer See formed in the mid-1930s when groundwater filled an abandoned limestone quarry; its steep walls show limestone turning into dolomite. It is part of a leisure park with boat hire and other attractions. Dive sources give the maximum depth as 15 m, while Wikipedia states the lake is up to 10 m deep and that the often-quoted 35 m refers to the quarry, not the water. The lake's own website lists diving, including try-dives and courses, run by a dive school on Saturdays and Sundays.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Blauer See, Ratingen (Mettmann district), near Düsseldorf
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 15 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 0.5-6 m (200bar, 2015); poor from the entry down to about 8 m, better but much colder below. Taucher.net reviews range from very clear to very poor below 6 m
- **Current**: Not documented
- **Best Time**: Not documented

## Underwater Features

Taucher.net reviewers describe steep quarry walls and placed objects: a platform, mine carts and rails, a small boat and a treasure chest. One review mentions a straight cave passage of about 100 m requiring advanced training; this is not confirmed by other sources, and overhead environments need proper cave training and equipment.

## Marine Life

200bar lists pike, perch, carp, eels and catfish. A taucher.net reviewer reported a red-eared slider turtle.

## Entry and Exit

Entry and exit are from the shore via a long staircase, which reviewers note requires some fitness. Older sources (2013-2015) report that divers must register in advance and pay a day fee, with free parking for divers. The lake's website currently lists diving through an on-site dive school on Saturdays and Sundays from 10:00 to 16:00. There is no fill station on site according to 200bar and taucher.net.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Contact the on-site dive school before visiting; access conditions have changed over the years
- Bring enough filled tanks
- Descend below the murky upper layer for better visibility

## Safety Considerations

Visibility near the entry is often poor. Do not enter any cave or overhang without cave training. The staircase exit is strenuous in full gear. Cold-water thermal protection is needed below the thermocline.
