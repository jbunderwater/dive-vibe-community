## Straussee

A natural lake in Strausberg north-east of Berlin with a managed shore entry at the Kulturpark and a collection of sunken objects in the shallows.

## Overview

The Straussee lies in the town of Strausberg, north-east of Berlin, and is described by Das Tauchen Magazin as the only Berlin-area dive lake with a direct S-Bahn connection. Diving is organised from the Kulturpark in Strausberg, where a dive base and the local diving club are located. The lake reaches about 26 m, although one source notes it is debated whether that figure includes the soft silt in the central basin. The base itself describes the shallower zone down to about 10 m as the most interesting part of the lake.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Kulturpark / Wriezener Straße, Strausberg, Brandenburg
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 26 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Variable; a 200bar.de report from August 2018 gave 1-3 m with green, milky water, and divers describe a 'green twilight' at depth
- **Current**: None documented
- **Best Time**: Not documented; the base operates daily in the main season and on weekends in winter

## Underwater Features

Sources list three training platforms (about 4 m, 6.5 m and 7 m), sunken fishing boats in various states of decay, two drop tanks from MiG fighter jets, a steel diving bell at around 15 m, a so-called "Hechtbaum" (pike tree) and, in deeper water, a sailboat wreck. The Strausberg diving club maintains a white-marked platform at about 4 m.

## Marine Life

Pike, perch and roach are named by several sources. Das Tauchen Magazin also lists carp, bream, tench, eel, zander and burbot (mostly at night); the base mentions occasional zander. Divers repeatedly report pike standing among submerged trees and tolerating cameras.

## Entry and Exit

Entry is over a shallow sandy bathing spot under large trees near the base, about 100-150 m from the base building. Diving requires registration and a briefing by the dive base or the local club; a permit is required under Brandenburg water law according to 200bar.de. A daily fee is charged per diver (sources cite EUR 7-8). Parking is available at the base. The entry is shared with bathers.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Plan to spend most of the dive in the 4-10 m zone where the objects and fish are concentrated
- Visibility near the entry drops quickly on busy weekends; one diver reported arriving after 60 other divers had already been in the water
- Bring a light for the darker water below the shallows

## Safety Considerations

Motorised fishing boats are permitted on the lake, so surface carefully and use a surface marker. Visibility can be poor, especially near the busy entry. Cold water below the thermocline requires appropriate thermal protection.
