## Chiemsee - Strandbad Urfahrn

The documented shore entry of the Chiemsee at the Urfahrn bathing beach near Breitbrunn, with an 'underwater park' of sunk objects.

## Overview

The Strandbad Urfahrn near Breitbrunn, on the west shore of the Chiemsee between Rimsting and Gstadt, is the documented shore entry of Bavaria's largest lake. Taucher.Net describes a shallow shore entry, a maximum depth of about 20 m and an "underwater park" of sunk objects down to about 18 m. justDiving.de says the lake bed here slopes to about 24 m, that vegetation (probably stonewort) reaches only 5-6 m, and that sunk objects lie at 6-18 m to the left of the bathing jetty, towards the Wasserwacht.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Strandbad Urfahrn, Breitbrunn am Chiemsee, west shore of the Chiemsee
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 20 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 1-10 m; one Taucher.Net diver notes that more than 3 m is probably the exception, while justDiving.de calls it generally quite good and dependent on season and depth
- **Current**: Not documented
- **Best Time**: Not documented

## Underwater Features

- Old rowboat at about 11 m (Taucher.Net)
- A sailboat reported in the area, a door frame and a thick pipe (a water intake) (Taucher.Net)
- Various sunk objects at 6-18 m to the left of the bathing jetty, towards the Wasserwacht (justDiving.de)

## Marine Life

On a late-September dive justDiving.de saw schools of juvenile perch and whitefish (Renken) and two sizeable pike lurking above the weed, and reports that noble crayfish should be present (only a dead one was seen).

## Entry and Exit

Enter from the Strandbad. Taucher.Net reports a large car park, toilets and a snack bar, and justDiving.de notes that you can kit up at the car and be in the water after a few steps. Stay away from bird sanctuaries and shipping areas, where diving is prohibited and dangerous (justDiving.de).

**Permit status:** The Bavarian Palace Administration (Schlösserverwaltung) lists no general diving order (Allgemeinverfügung) for the Chiemsee and says to contact the responsible Landratsamt about a diving permit. An older justDiving.de article (2009) describes diving here as tolerated. Clarify the current rules with the Landratsamt before diving.

## Tips and Recommendations

- The Taucher.Net report mentions water around 9°C at depth and 7 mm suits
- Head left from the jetty to find the sunk objects

## Safety Considerations

Visibility can be poor, so stay close to your buddy and use a light. The lake has passenger shipping - keep clear of shipping routes and surface with an SMB.
