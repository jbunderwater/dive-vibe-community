## Wassersportsee Zülpich

A former lignite pit lake at Zülpich where diving is limited to a marked zone managed by the local dive club, with depths to about 40 m.

## Overview

The Wassersportsee Zülpich (Zülpicher See) is the flooded residual pit of the Zülpich brown coal opencast mine, filled after mining ended in 1969; it covers about 85 hectares. Diving is allowed only in a marked area, with the entry between the lake stage and the surf school. Access is managed by the Tauchsportclub Zülpich: members dive with a personal permit, and trained guest divers can dive with a club member by appointment. The club quotes 40 m depth; a taucher.net review gives 38 m, with thermoclines at 8-10 m and around 15 m below which the water drops to 6-8 °C.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Wassersportsee Zülpich, Zülpich-Lövenich (Euskirchen district)
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: From about 1 m after snowmelt to about 10 m in summer (taucher.net)
- **Current**: Not documented
- **Best Time**: Not documented

## Underwater Features

Taucher.net reviews from 2004-2011 describe a sunken toy excavator, a telephone booth at about 8 m, traffic signs, a platform, artificial reefs with mussel colonies and Christmas trees. These reports are older and may no longer be current.

## Marine Life

Taucher.net reviewers report crayfish, eels, perch, pike, carp and roach.

## Entry and Exit

Diving is permitted only within the marked zone. Members need a personal, non-transferable diving permit carried at all times; the club's rules require a minimum of VDST DTSA Silver (Rescue-equivalent) to dive without supervision, while Bronze/Open Water divers must dive with an instructor or Gold/Divemaster diver. A valid dive medical is required. Guest divers need prior approval from the club board and must belong to a CMAS-affiliated club or hold individual insurance. Divers must keep about 20 m from the shoreline during training dives and avoid disturbing resting fish and spawning areas. Violations lead to immediate withdrawal of the permit.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Arrange a guest dive with the club well in advance; there is no walk-up access
- Bring a dive medical certificate and proof of insurance
- Plan for the cold layer below the thermoclines

## Safety Considerations

Water below 15 m is cold (6-8 °C reported) and dark after snowmelt, and depths beyond 30 m are available. Solo diving is not allowed. Respect other lake users, as the lake is used for water sports.
