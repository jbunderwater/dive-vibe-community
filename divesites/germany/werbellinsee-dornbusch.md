## Werbellinsee - Dornbusch

A deep shore dive to a wooden cargo barge (Kaffenkahn) lying at about 29-38 m, with a technical-diving platform and a yacht wreck beyond - for experienced divers only.

## Overview

The Werbellinsee in the Schorfheide is about 10 km long and reaches 55-58 m. It is known for the wrecks of wooden cargo barges (Kaffenkähne) that carried building materials towards Berlin; 200bar dates them to the 18th century and describes them as about 30 m long, while BerlinTaucher.de dates them to the 18th-19th century. At Dornbusch a relatively well-preserved barge lies with its bow at about 29 m and its rudder and stern at about 36-38 m, with the bow, stern, anchor winch and rudder still recognisable (200bar). BerlinTaucher.de describes a second Kaffenkahn at the site, a technical-diving platform at about 40 m and the aluminium yacht 'Nautilus' at 44 m. The site can be dived independently without a dive base.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Dornbusch, north-east shore of the Werbellinsee near Joachimsthal, Brandenburg
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 38 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Variable - 200bar reported 0.5-5 m in August 2018 and recommends offshore wind; dark and easily silted at depth
- **Current**: None reported (lake)
- **Best Time**: Weekdays, especially in summer, to avoid crowds (BerlinTaucher.de); diveable year-round

## Underwater Features

A Kaffenkahn wreck with intact bow, stern, anchor winch and rudder; a second, less complete barge; a technical platform at about 40 m and the aluminium yacht 'Nautilus' at 44 m (BerlinTaucher.de, 200bar). Wikipedia notes that the Werbellinsee wrecks are colonised by zebra mussels.

## Marine Life

BerlinTaucher.de reports particularly large perch and pike plus crucian carp, tench, bream, roach and rudd, with catfish, eels and burbot also in the lake. Wikipedia notes that the wrecks are used by burbot and ruffe.

## Entry and Exit

Shore entry from the grassy bank; there are several car parks near the beach (BerlinTaucher.de), and taucher.net mentions two shallow entry points. No dive base or registration is required (BerlinTaucher.de, 200bar).

## Tips and Recommendations

- Plan the dive for the depth: BerlinTaucher.de recommends twin tanks and stage cylinders with suitable gases
- Use a closed surface marker buoy for the ascent
- Visit on weekdays to avoid crowds and boat traffic

## Safety Considerations

This is a deep, dark, cold dive: 200bar notes that water at depth stays around 5 °C and rates Dornbusch for advanced divers only. Silt can reduce visibility to zero. Avoid free ascents in open water because of heavy boat traffic. Do not enter the wrecks; wreck penetration requires proper training and equipment.
