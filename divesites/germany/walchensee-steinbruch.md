## Walchensee - Steinbruch

A rocky scree-slope dive on the toll road along Walchensee's east shore, with a small wall from about 30 m and a sunken maypole.

## Overview

The Steinbruch (quarry) lies on the toll road from the village of Walchensee towards Jachenau. besser-tauchen.de and Tauchclub Bamberg describe a large parking area in front of a rock face, with benches and tables on the shore. Walchensee is an alpine lake at about 800 m altitude in the Bavarian Prealps (Landkreis Bad Tölz-Wolfratshausen), with a maximum depth reported between about 192 m (Wikipedia) and 196-198 m (local dive sources). Diving is permitted here under a legal ordinance of the Landratsamt Bad Tölz-Wolfratshausen, but only at the released sites and times; several sites are closed from 15 October to 1 February. The lake feeds the Walchensee hydroelectric plant, so the water level is drawn down in winter (dive sources cite 4-5 m; Wikipedia gives a range between 795 m and 802 m), which changes entries noticeably.

## Site Information

- **Location**: East shore of Walchensee on the toll road towards Jachenau
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Not documented for this site; local dive bases describe 10-15 m as normal for the lake, varying with season and weather
- **Current**: None documented
- **Best Time**: Year-round (ColdWater Films, Rabediver, Tauchclub Bamberg)

## Underwater Features

A rocky scree field with larger boulders descends in steps, with fissured rock faces full of small cracks and niches; from about 30 m a smaller wall with overhangs and crevices begins (Tauchclub Bamberg). besser-tauchen.de describes a large mirror at a little over 30 m to the right and a maypole at a little over 15 m to the left of the entry; Rabediver also lists the maypole and an underwater course of pyramids and paths.

## Marine Life

Tauchclub Bamberg reports large burbot (Aalrutte) resting in the rubble, many toads in the shallows during mating season (late April to mid May) and large fish shoals especially on autumn afternoons. besser-tauchen.de reports many caddisfly larvae and snails.

## Entry and Exit

Entry is next to a wooden bench via a small staircase and rocks; besser-tauchen.de notes it becomes more difficult with snow and low water, and Rabediver describes it as steep, rocky terrain. The access road is a private toll road.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Budget for the toll road
- Check the maypole (~15 m) and mirror (~30 m) as navigation references
- Visit in late April to mid May for the toad migration in the shallows

## Safety Considerations

Rabediver rates the site for experienced divers; the steep rocky entry requires care, especially in winter. Walchensee lies at roughly 800 m altitude, so altitude (Bergsee) decompression procedures apply. Local sources describe the water as very cold (about 4-8 °C) and recommend a dry suit and two independent cold-water regulators on a twin-outlet valve; lights are also listed as mandatory kit by one local source. Night diving is only allowed from one hour before sunrise until one hour after dark.
