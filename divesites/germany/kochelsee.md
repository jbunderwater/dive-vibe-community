## Kochelsee

A deep prealpine lake at the foot of the Kesselberg where diving is permitted, but which is known for poor visibility and has several no-dive zones around the power plant.

## Overview

Kochelsee lies at about 600 m at the edge of the Bavarian Prealps and reaches a maximum depth of 66 m. Diving with breathing apparatus is permitted under a legal ordinance of the Landratsamt Bad Tölz-Wolfratshausen. The Walchensee hydroelectric plant discharges cold water from Walchensee into the lake, which keeps Kochelsee cool. Dive-specific documentation is sparse: 200bar.de notes an entry on the south shore, and a diver's report on deep-down-under describes a stony bottom from 0-2 m, vegetation from about 2-6 m and then only fine sediment, with visibility getting worse as depth increases.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Kochelsee, near Kochel am See and Schlehdorf, Landkreis Bad Tölz-Wolfratshausen, Bavaria
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 66 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Poor - notorious for low visibility; a user report gave about 3-4 m (October 2013), and visibility worsens with depth over fine sediment
- **Current**: None documented; turbulence near the Walchensee power plant outlet (no-dive zone)
- **Best Time**: Not documented

## Marine Life

Seen.de lists whitefish (Renke), pike, brown, lake and rainbow trout, zander, perch, tench, carp, eel and cyprinids for the lake (in an angling context, not from dive reports).

## Entry and Exit

200bar.de reports a south-shore entry and no registration requirement. The general ordinance prohibits diving in the outlet canal of the Walchensee power plant and within at least 100 m of the power plant installations including the outlet canal, in the power plant canal and the artificial lake outlet including the lock at the north end (Loisach outlet), near the public boat routes and landing stages, and near and in the reed belt on the north shore. Diving is allowed only from one hour before sunrise until one hour after sunset, and never between 22:00 and 05:00. The local tourist office recommends consulting the local dive schools before diving. There is no filling station on site.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Check the current general ordinance of the Landratsamt for the no-dive zones before choosing an entry
- Keep well away from the power plant outlet
- Expect low visibility and bring good lights

## Safety Considerations

Strong turbulence and fluctuating flow can occur near the power plant installations, which is why the ordinance keeps divers at least 100 m away. Low visibility over silty bottom makes navigation and buddy contact difficult. The water is cold because of the inflow from Walchensee.
