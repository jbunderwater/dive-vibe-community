## Walchensee - Einsiedlbucht

A gently sloping training bay at Einsiedl, the southernmost point of Walchensee, with sunken boats and other exhibits.

## Overview

The bay lies at Einsiedl at the southern tip of the lake, where local sources describe a dive base on the grounds of the lakeside hotel (one source reports it closed in 2013). besser-tauchen.de describes a very comfortable entry from a jetty and a gradually sloping bottom where depths beyond 20 m are only reached with effort. Walchensee is an alpine lake at about 800 m altitude in the Bavarian Prealps (Landkreis Bad Tölz-Wolfratshausen), with a maximum depth reported between about 192 m (Wikipedia) and 196-198 m (local dive sources). Diving is permitted here under a legal ordinance of the Landratsamt Bad Tölz-Wolfratshausen, but only at the released sites and times; several sites are closed from 15 October to 1 February. The lake feeds the Walchensee hydroelectric plant, so the water level is drawn down in winter (dive sources cite 4-5 m; Wikipedia gives a range between 795 m and 802 m), which changes entries noticeably.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Einsiedl, southern tip of Walchensee
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 20 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Not documented as a figure; besser-tauchen.de reports that visibility is often poor for long periods after snowmelt, when meltwater pushes into the bay, and was best from the February season start until April
- **Current**: None documented
- **Best Time**: 1 February to 14 October only - Rabediver lists this period and besser-tauchen.de confirms the winter closure applies here

## Underwater Features

besser-tauchen.de lists sunken boats, letterboxes, a bell frame and a complete computer workstation; Rabediver describes an underwater course (parcours) and seagrass meadows.

## Marine Life

Rabediver reports pike hunting in the seagrass meadows. For the lake as a whole, local dive bases list lake trout, char (Saibling), pike, whitefish (Renke), perch, burbot (Aalrutte) and bullhead (Koppe).

## Entry and Exit

Comfortable shore entry directly from a jetty (besser-tauchen.de) or flat entry over the lawn (Rabediver).

## Tips and Recommendations

- Best visibility is reported early in the season, February to April
- Suitable for training dives thanks to the gentle slope

## Safety Considerations

Poor visibility after snowmelt can make navigation difficult. Walchensee lies at roughly 800 m altitude, so altitude (Bergsee) decompression procedures apply. Local sources describe the water as very cold (about 4-8 °C) and recommend a dry suit and two independent cold-water regulators on a twin-outlet valve; lights are also listed as mandatory kit by one local source. Night diving is only allowed from one hour before sunrise until one hour after dark.
