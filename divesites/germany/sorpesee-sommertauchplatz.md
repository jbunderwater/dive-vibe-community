## Sorpesee - Sommertauchplatz

The summer dive site of the Sorpe reservoir in the Sauerland, a sheltered buoyed bay with a gently sloping bottom.

## Overview

The Sorpesee has two designated dive sites run by a local dive school, a summer site and a winter site. The Sommertauchplatz is a protected bay with buoys in place and a gradually sloping bottom, which MyDiveGuide describes as beginner-friendly. Maximum depth is 26 m at full reservoir level. A local club lists the summer site as open from April to November; outside that period diving moves to the winter site.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Sorpesee, Sundern (Hochsauerlandkreis)
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 26 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Described as fairly good by taucher.net reviewers; no figures documented
- **Current**: Not documented
- **Best Time**: April to November (summer site season per a local club)

## Underwater Features

Ichthyo describes following the left-hand wall and a bank thickly encrusted with mussels. Reviewers on taucher.net note there is nothing spectacular in the water, and a local club mentions hidden "gimmicks" placed in the lake.

## Marine Life

A local club lists pike, perch, eels and crayfish for the Sorpesee.

## Entry and Exit

Parking is by the road, and from there it is a walk down to the water - about 120 m according to taucher.net and about 200 m downhill according to Ichthyo. Taucher.net reviewers warn that the road is busy. Prior registration is mandatory, and taucher.net reports a day pass bought from the dive school. Benches, tables and a toilet are reported at the water. Tank filling and rental are only available at the dive school's base in Sundern, not at the site.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Register before you arrive; registration is handled for both Sorpesee sites together
- Bring filled tanks - there is no filling at the site
- Plan for the walk back uphill in full gear

## Safety Considerations

Take care crossing and parking along the busy road. Depth varies with reservoir level. Cold-water thermal protection is needed.
