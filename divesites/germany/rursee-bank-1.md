## Rursee - Bank 1

The deeper of the Rursee's two designated dive sites - a slate plateau followed by a near-vertical rock wall, open only to experienced buddy teams.

## Overview

Bank 1 is one of two official dive sites in the Eschauel bay of the Rursee (Schwammenauel reservoir) in the Eifel. According to the Dürener Unterwasserclub, a slate plateau at about 15 m leads to a near-vertical rock face dropping to about 48 m; the Aachener Unterwasserclub notes 40 m is reached quickly. Clubs describe the underwater scenery as rock and slate formations, and the narrow valley makes it comparatively dark and cold.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Eschauel bay, Rursee (Schwammenauel reservoir), Nideggen-Schmidt
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 48 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Clubs describe clear water with generally good visibility; taucher.net notes attractive light and shadow in the first 10 m
- **Current**: Not documented
- **Best Time**: April to October (only open season)

## Underwater Features

- Slate plateau at about 15 m and a near-vertical rock wall below it
- A sunken fishing boat known locally as "the Bounty", reachable from Bank 1 for experienced divers
- A garden gnome placed at decompression depth (DUC Düren)

## Marine Life

Taucher.net lists pike, perch and eels; the Aachener Unterwasserclub mentions large pike in some seasons. Clubs describe flora and fauna as relatively sparse.

## Dive Profile

After the plateau the wall descends directly into deep water, so depth has to be planned before the dive and managed on the wall.

## Entry and Exit

Entry is via concrete steps with ropes down the steep bank; usability depends on the reservoir level. Diving at the Rursee is permitted only at Bank 1 and Bank 2 in the Eschauel bay of the Schwammenauel reservoir. The TSV NRW meeting point is the large car park on the Eschaueler Weg in Nideggen(-Schmidt); on weekends local clubs run a small shuttle van from the car park to the banks. The season runs from the first Saturday in April to the last Sunday in October, with regular operation from 9:30 to 17:00 on weekends and holidays; opening is signalled by the Alpha flag at Bank 1. Advance online registration with the TSV NRW is required, a supervisor is present, and divers need a valid dive medical and diving accident insurance, shown on site. A daily fee (or annual pass) applies, and parking is charged at weekends. Commercial training is not allowed. Full thermal protection (hood and gloves), two regulators and an underwater light are mandatory. There is no fill station, dive shop, showers or toilets. Under the TSV NRW group rules, Bank 1 is closed to beginners, juveniles and CMAS*/CMAS** divers unless accompanied by an instructor; a buddy pair must both be at least CMAS***/Divemaster or equivalent.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Register online with the TSV NRW before the day
- Bring filled tanks and all mandatory equipment; nothing is available on site
- Check your pair meets the Bank 1 qualification rule

## Safety Considerations

The wall gives immediate access to depths well beyond 40 m in dark, cold water, and the steep entry and exit are strenuous. The wreck should only be approached with proper training and equipment and never penetrated without them. Hood, gloves, redundant regulators and a light are mandatory.
