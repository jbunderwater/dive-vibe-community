## Kulkwitzer See - Einstieg E1 Göhrenz

The southern entry point of Kulkwitzer See at Göhrenz, a gently sloping sandy-beach entry into the shallower part of this former lignite pit.

## Overview

Kulkwitzer See lies on the western edge of Leipzig near Markranstädt. It formed from flooded lignite opencast mines: coal was mined here from 1864, underground at first and by opencast methods from 1937; the remaining pits were flooded from 1963 and the lake opened in 1973 (Wikipedia). The lake is one of the best-known dive lakes in eastern Germany and has three designated dive entries. E1 at Göhrenz, on the south shore next to a dive school, is described on Taucher.Net as a gently sloping sandy beach reached about 150 m from the car park, with the terrain staying shallow at first and deeper sections at 8-12 m. Taucher.Net gives about 14 m as the maximum depth for this area; the lake itself reaches 32-36 m depending on the source.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Göhrenz (Markranstädt), south shore of Kulkwitzer See, west of Leipzig, Saxony
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 14 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Reported as clear; no figures documented for this entry
- **Current**: None
- **Best Time**: Not documented

## Underwater Features

- Submerged tree stumps and logs, stone alignments and aquatic vegetation (Taucher.Net)
- A training platform at about 10 m (Taucher.Net); Ichthyo also mentions an underwater platform and a small sunken boat nicknamed "Titanic" at about 13 m for the Göhrenz entry

## Marine Life

Taucher.Net reports roach, tench, carp, pike, crayfish and schools of juvenile fish at the Göhrenz entry, and a large wels catfish that is said to be present but rarely seen.

## Entry and Exit

Diving at Kulkwitzer See is only permitted from the three designated entry points (E1 Göhrenz in the south, E2 and E3 on the east shore). A 2019 club report notes that diving in the shallow shore zone (under about 5 m) outside the entry points is not allowed to protect flora and fauna, and that divers snorkel out roughly 50 m before descending. According to Seen.de, neither dive base on the lake charges a diving fee; parking at the lake is paid. Taucher.Net reports the entry about 150 m from the car park; Ichthyo gives about 200 m. Taucher.Net notes angling zones on the lake, particularly on the western shore.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Use this entry for shallow, relaxed dives among the vegetation and tree remains; the deeper attractions of the lake are reached from the east-shore entries
- Respect the angling areas and stay within the designated entry corridor

## Safety Considerations

The water is cold below the thermocline: a club report measured 7°C at 23 m in July. Plan thermal protection accordingly and keep clear of boats and swimmers in summer.
