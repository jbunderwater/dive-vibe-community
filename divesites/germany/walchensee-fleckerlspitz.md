## Walchensee - Fleckerlspitz

A remote wall dive east of the Pioniertafel on Walchensee's north shore, with an easy entry from a lawn but a long walk in.

## Overview

Fleckerlspitz lies further along the path past the Pioniertafel and cannot be reached by car. ColdWater Films calls it their favourite Walchensee site. There is a large lawn with benches at the site. Walchensee is an alpine lake at about 800 m altitude in the Bavarian Prealps (Landkreis Bad Tölz-Wolfratshausen), with a maximum depth reported between about 192 m (Wikipedia) and 196-198 m (local dive sources). Diving is permitted here under a legal ordinance of the Landratsamt Bad Tölz-Wolfratshausen, but only at the released sites and times; several sites are closed from 15 October to 1 February. The lake feeds the Walchensee hydroelectric plant, so the water level is drawn down in winter (dive sources cite 4-5 m; Wikipedia gives a range between 795 m and 802 m), which changes entries noticeably.

## Site Information

- **Location**: North shore of Walchensee, east of Urfeld beyond the Pioniertafel
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Not documented for this site; local dive bases describe 10-15 m as normal for the lake, varying with season and weather
- **Current**: None documented
- **Best Time**: Year-round (ColdWater Films, Rabediver)

## Underwater Features

Underwater the route leads over large boulders to the right towards a steep wall (besser-tauchen.de). Rabediver highlights varied rock formations and sunken trees.

## Marine Life

Rabediver reports that burbot (Aalrutte) can be seen here with some luck; besser-tauchen.de reports frog and toad spawn in the shallows in spring, followed by tadpoles. For the lake as a whole, local dive bases list lake trout, char (Saibling), pike, whitefish (Renke), perch, burbot (Aalrutte) and bullhead (Koppe).

## Dive Profile

Head right over the boulders to the wall; besser-tauchen.de suggests using the shallows to the left and right of the exit for the safety stop.

## Entry and Exit

Reach the site from Urfeld (limited parking) on foot with a handcart or by boat; Rabediver gives about a 35-minute walk. ColdWater Films notes an access permit is needed for the road. The entry itself is flat and comfortable.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Allow time and energy for the long walk in with equipment
- Use the shallows near the exit to finish the dive

## Safety Considerations

The remote location means help is far away; plan conservatively and carry a means to call for help. Walchensee lies at roughly 800 m altitude, so altitude (Bergsee) decompression procedures apply. Local sources describe the water as very cold (about 4-8 °C) and recommend a dry suit and two independent cold-water regulators on a twin-outlet valve; lights are also listed as mandatory kit by one local source. Night diving is only allowed from one hour before sunrise until one hour after dark.
