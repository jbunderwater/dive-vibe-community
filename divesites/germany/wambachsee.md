## Wambachsee

A small, 12 m deep former gravel pit in Duisburg's Sechs-Seen-Platte, and the only lake of the group where diving is allowed.

## Overview

The Wambachsee is an artificial lake of about 11 ha in the south of Duisburg (Wedau/Buchholz), part of the Sechs-Seen-Platte recreation area, formed like its neighbours by sand and gravel extraction. German Wikipedia states it is the only lake of the Sechs-Seen-Platte where diving is permitted, that it is a training lake of the German divers' association VDST, and that a diver entry is on the east shore. justDiving (2009) says the city of Duisburg released the lake for diving and describes training platforms and sunken boats. The lake is shared with anglers, canoeists and pedal and rowing boats.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Sechs-Seen-Platte, Duisburg-Wedau/Buchholz, North Rhine-Westphalia
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 12 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Poor. 200bar reported 1-3 m (April 2016); Taucher.Net reviewers describe under 1-2 m at 5 m and near zero below about 7-10 m (2005, 2011, 2015)
- **Current**: None reported
- **Best Time**: Not documented

## Underwater Features

- Training platforms under buoys (VDST training lake). A February 2015 Taucher.Net review says the platforms under the three buoys had rusted through and were unusable
- Sunken boats; justDiving says one of them lies between the entry and the platform and can be reached by following the red line on the right (seen from the shore)

## Marine Life

justDiving lists large pike, carp, eel, roach, perch and crayfish, plus pumpkinseed sunfish and koi carp it has seen itself. A 2005 Taucher.Net review reports many perch and pike near the shore.

## Entry and Exit

The entry is a staircase into the water directly at Kalkweg, car park 1, on the right; the Masurensee on the left is reserved for sailors and surfers (justDiving). On weekdays or early in the day you can park right by the entry sign. 200bar lists no registration requirement and no fill station at the lake, and Taucher.Net reviewers mention free access and good parking.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Stay shallow if you want to see anything: reviewers found the best conditions in the top 5 m
- Bring a strong light and a compass; below about 7 m reviewers report near-zero visibility
- Expect other users on the water (boats, canoes, anglers on the opposite shore)

## Safety Considerations

Visibility can drop to near zero below the thermocline, so stay close to your buddy and consider a line or reel. A 2005 review measured 22°C at the surface and 9°C at 8 m in July, so plan thermal protection for depth. Do not rely on the old platforms, which were reported as rusted in 2015.
