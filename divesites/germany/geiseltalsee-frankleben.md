## Geiseltalsee - Frankleben

The east-shore dive area at Frankleben, with a beginner-friendly beach entry and boat access to forests, power poles and a long mining break-off edge.

## Overview

The Frankleben dive base sits on the east shore of the Geiseltalsee, a few metres from the beach. One dive site is reachable directly from the beach and is suitable for beginners and children, while other sites scattered around the lake are reached by boat. The Geiseltalsee.com description lists underwater forests, power poles and a 3 km long break-off edge with the remains of roads and railway tracks from the mining era. Depths in this area range from 3 m to 38 m.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Oststrand, Naundorfer Straße, Braunsbedra-Frankleben, Saxony-Anhalt
- **Entry Type**: Shore or boat entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 25 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 10-25 m depending on weather and wind, usually 10-15 m
- **Current**: None documented
- **Best Time**: Not documented

## Underwater Features

Drowned forests, the remains of the railway lines and mining relics mentioned by the base, power poles and the long break-off edge with roads and rails.

## Entry and Exit

Entry is over the beach, less than 100 m from the base, where there are tables and benches for kitting up and parking on site. Diving is allowed only via the resident dive bases under the lake's general order; registration is required. A surface marker buoy is mandatory on the lake.

## Tips and Recommendations

- The shore site is a good choice for training and first lake dives
- Book a boat dive to reach the break-off edge and forest sites

## Safety Considerations

Boat traffic and wind can affect conditions; always carry a surface marker. Cold water at depth requires appropriate thermal protection.
