## Murner See - Turm

The north-shore tower entry of the Murner See, with a submerged forest, walls to 20 m and a guide rope into the lake's deepest part.

## Overview

The Turm (tower) entry is the second designated diving area of the Murner See, at the observation tower on the Schwarzenfeld side of the north shore (VG Wackersdorf). spielis-lab describes a submerged forest with a mystical atmosphere, steep walls reaching about 20 m, statues placed around the lake and a guide rope running through the centre of the lake to the maximum depth of about 48 m.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Observation tower (Aussichtsturm), north shore of the Murner See (Schwarzenfeld side), Landkreis Schwandorf, Bavaria
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 48 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Very clear water is reported (spielis-lab; attributed to the acidic pH); Taucher.Net gives about 10 m for the lake
- **Current**: Not documented
- **Best Time**: Not documented

## Underwater Features

- Submerged forest (spielis-lab)
- Steep walls to about 20 m on the left-hand side (spielis-lab)
- Statues placed around the lake and an old slide (spielis-lab)
- Guide rope through the centre of the lake down to the maximum depth (spielis-lab)

## Marine Life

Pike and perch are reported for the lake (Taucher.Net), and Wiesenberger Diving also lists wels catfish.

## Entry and Exit

spielis-lab describes two entries from the car park: one about 50 m away through a small wood, with a steep slope and stairs, and a flat, gradual entry about 30 m further left that is easier with heavy equipment. A portable toilet is on site. Diving is regulated by the diving ordinance of the Landkreis Schwandorf (Tauchverordnung), which defines permitted and prohibited zones; the VG Wackersdorf names two designated diving areas, the "Rutschn" (Wackersdorf side) and the observation-tower area (Schwarzenfeld side, north shore).

## Tips and Recommendations

- Use the flat entry if you carry heavy or twin-tank equipment
- The guide rope helps with navigation but leads straight to the deepest part of the lake - decide your turn depth before the dive

## Safety Considerations

The rope leads to about 48 m, beyond typical recreational limits; deep dives here require appropriate training and gas planning. Cold water at depth (Taucher.Net reports about 8°C). The nearest decompression chamber listed by Taucher.Net is in Regensburg.
