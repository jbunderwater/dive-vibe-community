## Edersee - Tauchzone Berich

Dive zone 1 of the Edersee, in the bay where the flooded village of Berich lies, with house foundations, streets and a monastery wall.

## Overview

The Edersee is a large reservoir in northern Hesse. The local dive base explains that, as a federal waterway, it is managed under a dam ordinance that designates only two dive zones. The zones are marked with yellow buoys and closed to boat traffic. Diving is allowed at any time of day or night without registration. Diving outside the zones is an administrative offence that the water police can enforce. The water level varies a lot over the year, so every depth given for the lake refers to the full reservoir level of 245 m above sea level: the current depth of an object is the current water level minus the object's elevation (Tauchbasis Edersee). Dive zone 1 lies in the Bericher Bucht, over the remains of the village of Berich, which was flooded by the reservoir. According to the dive base, the village was demolished and only the foundations remain. The zone was extended in 2005 to include the so-called "Fischbucht". Visibility here is usually somewhat worse than in the Sitte zone, but there is more to see (TWC Delphin).

## Site Information

- **Location**: Bericher Bucht, Edersee, Waldeck-Frankenberg, Hesse
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 25 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Often poor; 200bar reported 0.5-1 m in July 2018. Edersee-Tauchen gives May to mid-July as the best period
- **Current**: None
- **Best Time**: May to mid-July (visibility)

## Underwater Features

- The plateau of the former village at 10-15 m (at full level), with old streets, foundations, troughs and inscribed stone plates (Tauchbasis Edersee)
- The monastery wall ("Klostermauer"), about 8-10 m long, at about 14 m, and the rubble of the monastery church nearby
- A small rocky wall around the point of the bay that turns into a slope at about 20 m
- The old Berich bridge (roadway about 28 m, riverbed about 33 m at full level). 200bar and TWC Delphin place it outside the zone, and the dive base's own route description warns that you are outside the zone with boat traffic overhead there, although the base also lists it in its table for zone 1

## Marine Life

The dive base reports schools of roach and small pike near the shore, and medium-sized pike and zander on the plateau. Edersee-Tauchen notes large zander guarding their young on submerged vegetation from late May.

## Dive Profile

The dive base describes routes from both entries. From the "Denkmal" entry, you descend onto the plateau, swim south to the drop-off and follow it west at about 12 m for about 20 minutes to reach the monastery wall. From the "Eiche" entry, you swim south-east at 12 m without sight of the bottom to the drop-off, with depths of up to 25 m at full level along the way. The base rates both as dives of medium difficulty.

## Entry and Exit

Two entries, each with a small car park: at the Berich memorial ("Denkmal"), via a footpath below a small stone tower, and at the oak ("Eiche"). Dive only inside the buoyed Tauchzone; no registration or permit is required. The lake is in a landscape protection area, so running compressors requires an official permit. Divers have the right to ask anglers to stop fishing during dive operations (Tauchbasis Edersee). At low water in late summer the water can be turbid, and getting in and out may involve some scrambling (TWC Delphin).

## Tips and Recommendations

- Check the current water level before planning; depths and even the entry conditions change a lot over the year
- Orientation is difficult for first-time visitors, so get a briefing and use a compass
- TWC Delphin often calls this zone "Eiche"

## Safety Considerations

Don't follow the old road to the bridge: it lies outside the zone, involves deep diving with decompression, and surfacing there exposes you to passenger-boat traffic (Tauchbasis Edersee). Parts of the approach from the Eiche entry have no bottom in sight.
