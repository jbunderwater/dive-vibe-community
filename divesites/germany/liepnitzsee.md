## Liepnitzsee

A clear, small lake near Berlin designated for diving, entered from a small bathing spot by the ferry at Ützdorf, with fish-rich weed beds in the shallows.

## Overview

The Liepnitzsee north of Berlin near Wandlitz is designated as a diving water (seen.de). The dive entry is a small bathing spot near Ützdorf, to the right of the ferry to the island of Großer Werder, reached on foot from the car park at the ferry (a walk of 250-300 m). The official bathing-water profile gives a maximum depth of 17.5 m (Wikipedia), while 200bar cites about 20 m. The vegetated shallows hold most of the life; 200bar describes the deeper parts as rather bleak and muddy. Wikipedia records that a US B-17 bomber crashed into the lake on 12 September 1944 and was later stripped by hobby divers, with only small fragments said to remain in the mud; a German fighter is also said to lie in the bay off Ützdorf, but its type differs between reports and taucher.net calls the aircraft-wreck stories unverified rumours.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Ützdorf, Wandlitz, Brandenburg
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 17 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: About 3-5 m in the shallows (200bar, August 2018; taucher.net); Wikipedia cites up to 5 m; about 1 m in deeper areas (taucher.net)
- **Current**: None reported (lake)
- **Best Time**: Not documented; 200bar notes the lake is ice-free

## Marine Life

200bar and taucher.net report plenty of fish in the vegetated shallows, including pike, and crustaceans. Wikipedia lists eels, pike, rudd, whitefish (Maränen) and crayfish for the lake.

## Entry and Exit

Park at the paid car park by the ferry and walk about 250-300 m down to the small bathing spot to the right of the ferry; vehicles cannot reach the shore (taucher.net, 200bar). No registration is required (200bar). There is no filling station at the lake - the nearest is at the Wandlitzsee (seen.de). Do not park in the forest, especially in periods of fire risk (200bar).

## Tips and Recommendations

- Bring filled cylinders - there are no fills at the lake
- Turn right after entering to stay clear of the ferry route (200bar)
- Spend the dive in the weedy shallows where the fish are

## Safety Considerations

An hourly ferry crosses to the island campground near the entry, so keep away from its route and use a surface marker. Motorboats are otherwise banned on the lake (Wikipedia). The walk to the water with full kit is steep.
