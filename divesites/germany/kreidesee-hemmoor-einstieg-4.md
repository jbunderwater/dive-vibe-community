## Kreidesee Hemmoor - Einstieg 4

The sandy southern beach entry to the wreck plateaus with boats, caravans and cars.

## Overview

The Kreidesee in Hemmoor (Lower Saxony, on the B73 between Cuxhaven and Hamburg) is a flooded chalk quarry. Chalk was extracted here from 1862 to 1976; the pit, which had reached a depth of almost 120 m, filled with water between 1976 and 1982, and divers discovered it from 1986 onwards. The lake measures roughly 1,300 x 500 m. Its maximum depth is given as about 60 m (water-level dependent) by the on-site dive base and 58 m by Taucher.Net. Numerous objects have been placed for divers - one source counts about 41 objects spread across the entry points. Diving is managed by the dive base on the western shore and requires a day dive permit.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Kreidesee, Hemmoor, Landkreis Cuxhaven, Lower Saxony
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 37 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Average 10-20 m at the entries; up to 40 m in optimal conditions, 5-10 m in poor conditions (best mid-week, Tuesday-Thursday)
- **Current**: None
- **Best Time**: Year-round with drysuit (the dive base operates all year); wetsuit diving April-October

## Underwater Features

Pro-Taucher lists an 8 m boat wreck at 15 m, a computer, caravans at 25 m and 37 m, a 'Lübeck' sign and a blue Mitsubishi at 20 m; Top Dive describes the plateaus at Entry 4 with ship and caravan wrecks. OSM maps a car-accident scene at 20 m, supply pipes (20-55 m), a Ford Mondeo car wreck, the 'Segelyacht Hemmoor' at 17 m, a lighthouse at 31 m and a caravan at 40 m around this entry. Sources disagree on the caravan depth (37 m per Pro-Taucher, 40 m per OSM).

## Marine Life

The dive base lists trout, roach, perch, stickleback, eel and whitefish, plus freshwater mussels, snails, sponges, crayfish and (seasonally) amphibians. Top Dive reports char (See-Saibling) and salmon trout reaching 60-80 cm. Pro-Taucher notes trout throughout the lake and abundant crayfish, especially around Entry 5.

## Dive Profile

Plateaus step down from the beach: the boat at about 15 m, cars around 20 m and caravans at 25 m and deeper.

## Entry and Exit

Access is a 3-5 minute walk to a sandy beach entry (Pro-Taucher). The dive base lists Entries 4A and 4B among the beginner-friendly entries with many shallow areas, and Pro-Taucher names Entries 1 and 4 as the only entries open to beginners. Every diver needs a day dive permit (Tauchgenehmigung) from the on-site dive base; published day-fee figures differ between sources (EUR 11 per Top Dive and Pro-Taucher, EUR 20 per a 2020 blog), so check current prices. Top Dive lists cold-water-suitable equipment, a dive computer and two independently closable valves/first stages as mandatory. Pro-Taucher reports that checks of divers and dive computers are not uncommon and that the permitted maximum depth depends on certification level. Taucher.Net describes the entries as equipped with floating pontoons and stairs.

## Tips and Recommendations

- OSM maps two entry points here (4A and 4B); both lead to the same plateau area
- Good choice for a relaxed wreck-object dive at moderate depth

## Safety Considerations

Water below the thermocline stays cold all year; a drysuit is needed outside the summer months (wetsuit diving is described as practical only April-October). Good visibility can make divers underestimate depth - the lake drops to about 60 m and several objects lie well below recreational limits. Stay within your certification and the depth limits set by the dive base.
