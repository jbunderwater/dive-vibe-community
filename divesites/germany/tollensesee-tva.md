## Tollensesee - TVA

The demolished remains of a Second World War torpedo test station and its sunken test torpedoes and target platforms in the north of the Tollensesee, dived by boat with local guides only.

## Overview

In 1941-42 the German navy built an outstation of its torpedo test facility (Torpedoversuchsanstalt, TVA) at Neubrandenburg; the firing station stood on an artificial island in the Tollensesee, and Wikipedia and 200bar both state that forced labourers or prisoners of war were used for the construction. Torpedoes were fired from the island along a test range. At the end of the war the facility was set on fire, and the firing station was later blown up on Soviet orders (Wikipedia gives April 1947). Today an overgrown rubble island remains, known locally as the Trümmerinsel, Torpedoinsel or TVA. Underwater, divers find demolished concrete and steel sections, an inverted platform about 30 m long, float chambers, test torpedoes lying on the lake bed and, further out, target or observation platforms at about 25-30 m. The Tollensesee itself was long charted at 31-34 m; independent measurements in 2023, reported by Wikipedia, found about 45.6 m.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Northern Tollensesee off Neubrandenburg, Mecklenburg-Vorpommern
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 25 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Often limited - 200bar reported 0.5-3 m in July 2016 and notes it depends on algae and wind; sharkneagle reports the best visibility in winter
- **Current**: None reported (lake)
- **Best Time**: Winter for visibility (sharkneagle); diveable year-round according to 200bar

## Underwater Features

Demolished concrete and steel structures, partly breaking the surface; an inverted platform about 30 m long and 7-8 m wide with intact float chambers and a propeller assembly (200bar); test torpedoes on the lake bed and a 'torpedo forest' at about 25 m (sharkneagle); separate target platforms at about 25-30 m away from the island (difomax, taucher.net forum). Taucher.net also mentions several boat wrecks connected by guide lines at about 8-9 m reached from the Wassersportzentrum.

## Marine Life

Sources say little about fish at the TVA: 200bar mentions only rust-coloured steel and mud, and one taucher.net review saw only a few small perch.

## Entry and Exit

Boat dive only. Dives at the TVA require accompaniment by the local dive bases or clubs (taucher.net, 200bar); boats leave from the Neubrandenburg side of the lake. The island is a bird sanctuary and landing is not permitted (200bar).

## Tips and Recommendations

- Book through a local base or club - independent diving at the TVA is not permitted
- Good buoyancy control matters: the silty bottom stirs up easily
- Winter dives tend to offer the clearest water

## Safety Considerations

Steel reinforcement is sharp enough to damage drysuits (sharkneagle). 200bar warns that munitions are present and that debris should not be touched; difomax states the test torpedoes contain no explosives - treat all objects as untouchable either way. Do not enter the collapsed structures. Heavy recreational boat traffic in good weather (200bar) requires a surface marker.
