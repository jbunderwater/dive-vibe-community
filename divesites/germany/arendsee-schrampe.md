## Arendsee - Schrampe

One of the officially designated dive entries of the Arendsee, on the west shore at Schrampe, where the bottom drops in terraces from about 13 m to around 40 m.

## Overview

The Arendsee in the Altmark is a collapse lake; 200bar gives its maximum depth as about 50 m and Taucher.Net as 52 m. Diving with breathing apparatus there is regulated by an ordinance of the Altmarkkreis Salzwedel (2014), which lists four permitted dive entries. One of them is "Schrampe, Regattaverein zwischen Steg Nr. 4 und 5", at the grounds of the Arendseer Regattaverein sailing club on the west shore. poseidon dive sports (2023) describes this entry as "Einstieg Westufer": a firm sandy bottom with a thin layer of silt at first, then from 13-15 m the bottom turns dark and drops quickly in terraces to 20 m, and continues to about 40 m.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Schrampe, west shore of the Arendsee, Altmarkkreis Salzwedel, Saxony-Anhalt
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: In the warm months only about 1 m above the thermocline, but often 4 m or more below it; in the colder months the pattern reverses (poseidon dive sports)
- **Current**: None reported
- **Best Time**: Not documented

## Underwater Features

- A terraced slope that drops quickly from 13-15 m to 20 m and continues to about 40 m (poseidon dive sports)
- A glacial boulder (Findling), reached by following the 15 m depth contour to the right from the entry (poseidon dive sports)

## Marine Life

poseidon dive sports mentions small perch or ruffe, reed beds, and suspended algae in season.

## Entry and Exit

Under the Altmarkkreis Salzwedel ordinance, diving is allowed only from the designated entries; entering elsewhere requires a permit from the authority. At Schrampe the entry is between jetties 4 and 5 of the Regattaverein. poseidon dive sports describes a paved path down to the water and a natural stone staircase to the right of the club's jetty.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Mark your descent and ascent area with a buoy: there is sailing traffic in the warm months (poseidon dive sports)
- poseidon recommends swimming about 100 m out from the shore before descending
- Follow the 15 m contour to the right to find the boulder

## Safety Considerations

Depth increases quickly below 13-15 m and the slope continues to about 40 m, so control your depth and plan gas and decompression for a deep dive. Visibility can be very poor above the thermocline in summer; use a compass and stay close to your buddy. Watch for sailing boats when surfacing.
