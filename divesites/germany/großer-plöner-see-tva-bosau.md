## Großer Plöner See - TVA Bosau

The ruins of a WWII torpedo and rocket propulsion test facility on the steep shore near Bosau, entered from the youth-centre grounds, with an installed object course nearby.

## Overview

The Großer Plöner See is the largest and deepest lake in Schleswig-Holstein. The tourism board gives a maximum depth of 58 m in the eastern Plön basin and 30 m in the shallower western Ascheberg basin, both formed by glaciers. It is a busy lake with scheduled passenger ships, sailing boats and canoes. During the Second World War, rocket and torpedo propulsion systems were tested at this site on the lake's south-eastern shore near Bosau; the facility was blown up after the war (7oceans; Taucher.Net also describes it as a WWII torpedo and rocket test facility). Today the remains form a jumble of concrete, piles and steel struts along the steeply sloping shore.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Stadtbek, Bosau, Kreis Ostholstein, Schleswig-Holstein
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 20 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Not documented for this site
- **Current**: None
- **Best Time**: Late summer for the sponge growth (7oceans)

## Underwater Features

The remains of the test site stretch down the steep shore to about 12 m. Concrete blocks, piles and steel struts rise from about 7 m to just below the surface, creating numerous overhangs (7oceans). Taucher.Net reviewers report debris from 1.5 m, concrete remains and steel beams in a 20-30 m debris field, and civilian waste such as old milk cans, bottles and logs.

The 'Drache' course, set up in 2022, starts from a beach access on the same youth-centre grounds (7oceans): a buoy at 270 degrees, a 'cow' at 8 m, a pedalo at 10 m, a sunken dinghy at 20 m, a free-floating dragon installation at about 14 m and a disused work platform at 10 m. The full course takes a little over half an hour.

## Marine Life

7oceans reports large schools of perch and pike in the overhangs and rich freshwater sponge growth in late summer. A Taucher.Net reviewer found no fish at all on their dive.

## Entry and Exit

Entry is from the car park of the djo youth centre on Stadtbeker Straße after registering. Parking there costs EUR 5 (7oceans; Taucher.Net mentions a EUR 5 fee paid to the site manager). The centre provides safe entries and kitting-up tables (7oceans). Taucher.Net reviewers note that the jetty area can be crowded with boats and mooring lines. The regional tourism board requires divers to leave their dive certification clearly visible in their car and to inform the holder of the fishing rights before diving; questions go to the state coastal agency (LKN). Swimming and diving are prohibited within 100 m of the passenger-ship landings while the boats are running (Wednesday-Sunday 11:00-18:00, plus Friday 19:00-21:00). Divers must follow the rules of the Schleswig-Holstein diving federation (TLV-SH), must not take any WWII munitions they find, and should report them. The diving club TUK e.V. (page last updated 2022) names the Plöner See as a lake that requires a personal TLV-SH authorisation card, issued only to TLV-SH members. The tourism board does not mention this, so check the current access rules before diving.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Combine a dive on the ruins with the nearby Drache course
- Visit in late summer for the sponges

## Safety Considerations

Heavy boat traffic in summer makes a surface marker buoy essential. WWII munitions have been dumped in Schleswig-Holstein lakes - never touch suspicious objects. The thermocline brings a sharp temperature drop, so dress for cold water. Taucher.Net reviewers warn of fishing nets, broken glass and protruding steel reinforcement that can snag equipment - keep clear of the structures and carry a cutting tool. The ruins are not safe to penetrate.
