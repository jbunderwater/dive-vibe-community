## Walchensee - Urfeld

An easy-entry site at the boat rental in Urfeld on Walchensee's north shore, known for its underwater art garden.

## Overview

Urfeld is the first site reached when arriving over the Kesselberg, located at the boat rental at the village entrance. besser-tauchen.de describes a large underwater art garden built up over many years by a local diver; ColdWater Films calls it a kind of underwater museum and Rabediver mentions collections of steel and wooden objects. Urfeld is also the starting point for the Pioniertafel and Fleckerlspitz sites. Walchensee is an alpine lake at about 800 m altitude in the Bavarian Prealps (Landkreis Bad Tölz-Wolfratshausen), with a maximum depth reported between about 192 m (Wikipedia) and 196-198 m (local dive sources). Diving is permitted here under a legal ordinance of the Landratsamt Bad Tölz-Wolfratshausen, but only at the released sites and times; several sites are closed from 15 October to 1 February. The lake feeds the Walchensee hydroelectric plant, so the water level is drawn down in winter (dive sources cite 4-5 m; Wikipedia gives a range between 795 m and 802 m), which changes entries noticeably.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Urfeld, north shore of Walchensee at the boat rental
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Not documented for this site; local dive bases describe 10-15 m as normal for the lake, varying with season and weather
- **Current**: None documented
- **Best Time**: Year-round (ColdWater Films, Rabediver)

## Underwater Features

Underwater installations and sculptures of an art garden, including a structure called the Mikado; besser-tauchen.de also mentions wrecks among the exhibits.

## Marine Life

For the lake as a whole, local dive bases list lake trout, char (Saibling), pike, whitefish (Renke), perch, burbot (Aalrutte) and bullhead (Koppe).

## Dive Profile

The bottom slopes gradually, with about 30 m reachable without difficulty (besser-tauchen.de).

## Entry and Exit

Enter directly at the boat rental; Rabediver describes a flat entry over a boat ramp. Parking is limited because the area is popular with walkers and holidaymakers.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Arrive early for parking near the boat rental
- Treat the art installations with care - they are the result of years of volunteer work
- Stay clear of the power-station intake in the shallows

## Safety Considerations

besser-tauchen.de warns that the outflow to the power station must be avoided in the shallow area; diving there is strictly prohibited. Walchensee lies at roughly 800 m altitude, so altitude (Bergsee) decompression procedures apply. Local sources describe the water as very cold (about 4-8 °C) and recommend a dry suit and two independent cold-water regulators on a twin-outlet valve; lights are also listed as mandatory kit by one local source. Night diving is only allowed from one hour before sunrise until one hour after dark.
