## Senftenberger See

The acidic, very clear southern basin of a former lignite mine south of Senftenberg, dived from two club grounds on the south shore.

## Overview

The Senftenberger See is the residual lake of a former lignite mine south-east of Senftenberg. An island divides it into two basins with no water exchange; divers use only the southern basin ("Südsee"), where the visibility is good and the diving clubs are based. The water here is acidic, so there are few or no fish, but the Tauchsport-Club Berlin describes the colour as greenish-turquoise and the visibility as very good. The southern basin reaches about 18 m.

## Site Information

- **Location**: South shore ("Südsee"), near Niemtsch, Senftenberg, Brandenburg
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 18 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Often good in the acidic southern basin (TCB reported the buddy clearly visible at 6 m in May 2021); a 200bar.de report from May 2018 gave only 1-2 m near the surface, improving below 8 m
- **Current**: None documented
- **Best Time**: Club bases open on weekends from May to October

## Underwater Features

A multi-level training platform, a fully furnished office at 14 m (table, chairs, PC, telephone), a small diving bell, sunken trees and a wreck placed for divers. Guide lines run from the entry to the platform and to the bell. The wreck is described by 200bar.de as a small passenger ship at 8-10 m and by another diver's site as an Elbe ferry lying at 11 m on a drop-off, so that divers can swim under the stern; the exact vessel type is not confirmed. It is reached after about ten minutes along the small spoil edge from the entry. The bottom is mostly muddy with a rust-brown colour from the high iron content.

## Marine Life

The acidic southern basin has few or no fish; the Tauchsport-Club Berlin saw none in May 2021 and only a few algae grow there. The northern basin holds pike, zander, carp and vendace according to 200bar.de, but it is not used for diving.

## Entry and Exit

Two diving clubs have grounds on the south shore, reached through Niemtsch and a forest track from the south-shore car parks: TSV Dino e.V., directly on the shore with an entry via beach or metal steps, and Tauchsportverein Senftenberg II e.V., set back about 100 m in the forest, with entry from the beach. Visiting divers register at the club bases, which charge a day fee and fill tanks. The clubs are open on weekends in season (Senftenberg II lists May-October 2026).

## Tips and Recommendations

- Follow the guide lines from the entry to find the platform and bell
- Larger groups should register with the club in advance
- Expect little fish life; the attractions are the clear water and the placed objects

## Safety Considerations

The iron-rich, acidic water has corroded the wreck; do not enter it without proper wreck training and equipment. Water below the thermocline is cold (around 8 °C at depth in May, per 200bar.de).
