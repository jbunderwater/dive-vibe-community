## Starnberger See - Allmannshausen Naturschutzgebiet

The southern Allmannshausen entry to the Starnberger See's steep east-shore wall, next to a nature reserve.

## Overview

The Naturschutzgebiet (nature reserve) entry is the southernmost of the Allmannshausen entries. ColdWater Films describes a beautiful steep wall running north-south that extends far to the south and becomes more jagged towards the north, starting at about 20 m throughout the entry zone. The designated entry area stretches from the "Naturschutzgebiet" sign north to a wooden fence.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Allmannshausen, east shore of the Starnberger See
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Not documented
- **Current**: Not documented
- **Best Time**: Closed 1 November to 15 March (winter diving ban)

## Underwater Features

- North-south steep wall starting at about 20 m, more jagged to the north (ColdWater Films)

## Entry and Exit

The road to the Allmannshausen entries requires an access permit (Zufahrtsgenehmigung); ColdWater Films lists this for all Allmannshausen spots. Enter between the "Naturschutzgebiet" sign and the wooden fence to the north (ColdWater Films). The Starnberger See travel guide states that the protected area requires a special permit and that diving is prohibited there from November to mid-March. Diving in the Starnberger See is regulated by a general order (Allgemeinverfügung) of the Landratsamt Starnberg. Its key rules: solo dives are prohibited; diving with compressed air is limited to 40 m; diving is allowed until one hour after sunset; divers must carry cold-water-rated equipment with two separate, independently shut-off regulators (EN 250), thermal protection and a hood (a dive computer is recommended). A safety distance of at least 100 m must be kept from harbours, shipping lanes and landing stages, and private boat facilities, fish spawning areas, fishing gear and net enclosures are off limits.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Stay within the marked entry area between the sign and the fence
- The wall is deep from the start, so plan depth before descending

## Safety Considerations

Part of the Allmannshauser Steilwand, the lake's accident hotspot (11 fatal accidents and 35 serious injuries since 1994 cited in the general order). Narcosis risk is significant; the 40 m air limit applies.
