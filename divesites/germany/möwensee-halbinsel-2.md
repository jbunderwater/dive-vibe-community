## Möwensee - Halbinsel 2

The rear entry of the Möwensee, from which a channel leads down to the lake's deepest point at about 45-46 m.

## Overview

The Möwensee is a disused gravel pit south-east of Nordhausen, about 1.2 km long and up to 700 m wide (Action Sport Nordhausen, Wikipedia). Action Sport's Tauchplatz 4 "Halbinsel 2" (justDiving's Einstieg II, in the southern part) is reached along the works road between the lake and the railway, and is where the lake's maximum depth is marked with a buoy. Action Sport says this entry is not recommended for beginners. Because the lake is young, there is little plant growth here and the bottom is covered with a thick layer of sediment.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Möwensee, Nordhausen (Bielen/Sundhausen), Thuringia
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 46 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Very clear according to Action Sport Nordhausen and unterwasserwelt.de; no figures published for this entry
- **Current**: None reported
- **Best Time**: Not documented

## Underwater Features

- A channel running north from the entry, ending at 45 m in a small basin (Action Sport Nordhausen)
- A yellow buoy whose anchor line leads to the 45 m deep (unterwasserwelt.de)
- A white, fine-sediment ridge at only 10-12 m running almost at a right angle out from the shore, with the bottom dropping steeply to well over 20 m on either side (unterwasserwelt.de)
- Large hill formations along the shore to the east (towards the motorway) and west (Action Sport Nordhausen)

## Marine Life

Action Sport Nordhausen calls the crayfish population the most remarkable feature of this site, with thousands of crayfish, some very large, especially on the east shore. unterwasserwelt.de describes countless crayfish burrows in the loamy slopes, and pike in the willows near the shore at 1-2 m.

## Dive Profile

Experienced divers can follow the channel north to 45 m. Action Sport Nordhausen recommends that less experienced divers stay along the shore to the east or west.

## Entry and Exit

Diving requires registration with one of the Sundhäuser See dive bases (justDiving; Taucher.Net says the Sundhäuser See day ticket also covers the Möwensee). The lake is behind a gate with a combination lock; you get the code when you register and must close the lock again after driving through (Action Sport Nordhausen). Follow the road between the lake and the railway to the rear third of the lake; the entry is signposted. A Taucher.Net reviewer (2012) notes you can drive right to the rear entry, where there is a kitting-up table.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Good buoyancy control is essential: the thick sediment layer is easily stirred up (Action Sport Nordhausen)
- A light is recommended for the deep channel, although it is rarely needed (Action Sport Nordhausen)

## Safety Considerations

This is a deep-diving site: the channel reaches 45 m, beyond recreational no-decompression limits for most divers. Plan depth, gas and decompression carefully, and expect cold water at depth. Sources disagree on the lake's maximum depth (35 m vs. 46 m); treat the buoyed deep point as at least 45 m.
