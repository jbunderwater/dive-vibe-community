## Tegernsee - Schweinebucht

A bay on the south-east shore of the Tegernsee that can be dived to both sides of the entry and that the local dive school recommends for night dives.

## Overview

The Schweinebucht is one of the shore entries listed by the Tegernsee dive school Seegeist, which says it can be dived to the left and right, is good for night dives, and requires consideration for bathers. The dive-site database divers-guide.com describes a beach entry, a shallow plant-covered plateau and a maximum depth of 35 m, and notes that the bay is also popular with boat owners. Seegeist regularly posts visibility reports for the site.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Schweinebucht (Kobelstraße), Tegernsee, Bavaria
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 35 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Highly variable. Seegeist reports for 2026: 7-10 m (April), 5-10 m (May), 5-6 m with a murky layer at 8 m (July), and 1-2 m with zero visibility from 6-12 m (August). divers-guide.com gives 5-10 m
- **Current**: None (divers-guide.com)
- **Best Time**: Not documented; Seegeist's spring reports show the best visibility

## Underwater Features

- A shallow plateau covered with plants (divers-guide.com)

## Entry and Exit

divers-guide.com says parking is limited, either in the bay or in the narrow street nearby, and the walk to the water is short; you enter from the beach. Diving in the Tegernsee is governed by a general order (Allgemeinverfügung) of the Landratsamt Miesbach (2004). Among other things it prohibits diving in the dotted nature-protection areas of the attached maps, in the hatched waterfowl areas from September to the end of March, in the seven buoyed and signed Tegernsee protection zones, in reed and aquatic-plant zones and their access routes, at fish spawning grounds and fishing gear (and within 100 m of set nets and traps), within the 100 m zones of the shipping piers and on the Bavarian lake shipping routes, in bathing areas and public bathing grounds during the bathing season (15 March - 31 October), and in harbours, buoy fields and boathouse areas. Ice dives are only allowed through naturally existing openings when the ice cover is not fully closed. Because Seegeist warns of bathers at this entry, check whether the bathing-season restriction applies before diving here in summer.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Check Seegeist's current visibility reports before you go - conditions changed from 7-10 m to near zero within a few months in 2026
- Use a surface marker buoy: the bay is popular with boat owners (divers-guide.com)
- Seegeist recommends the site for night dives

## Safety Considerations

The Tegernsee lies at 725 m above sea level, so use altitude settings on your dive computer and plan decompression accordingly. Visibility can drop to zero below the thermocline in late summer; carry a light and stay close to your buddy. Watch for boats when surfacing.
