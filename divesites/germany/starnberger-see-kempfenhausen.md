## Starnberger See - Kempfenhausen

A beginner-friendly shingle-beach entry at the northern end of the Starnberger See's east shore.

## Overview

Kempfenhausen, at the northern end of the Starnberger See's east shore, is one of the approved entry areas in the Allgemeinverfügung. The Starnberger See travel guide describes a long shingle beach in a recreation area, depths to about 20 m over a muddy and rocky bottom, and recommends it for beginner divers, heading south-east from the entry.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Kempfenhausen recreation area, north-east shore of the Starnberger See
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 20 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Not documented
- **Current**: Not documented
- **Best Time**: Not documented

## Entry and Exit

Entry is from the long shingle beach of the Kempfenhausen recreation area. Diving in the Starnberger See is regulated by a general order (Allgemeinverfügung) of the Landratsamt Starnberg. Its key rules: solo dives are prohibited; diving with compressed air is limited to 40 m; diving is allowed until one hour after sunset; divers must carry cold-water-rated equipment with two separate, independently shut-off regulators (EN 250), thermal protection and a hood (a dive computer is recommended). A safety distance of at least 100 m must be kept from harbours, shipping lanes and landing stages, and private boat facilities, fish spawning areas, fishing gear and net enclosures are off limits.

## Tips and Recommendations

- The travel guide recommends heading south-east from the entry
- Stay above the muddy bottom to preserve visibility

## Safety Considerations

A recreation area is busy with bathers and boats in summer; use an SMB. Cold water below the thermocline requires full cold-water equipment, as the general order requires.
