## Sorpesee - Wintertauchplatz

The winter dive site of the Sorpesee, entered from the campground, with a steep wall dropping toward the middle of the lake after a short swim.

## Overview

The Wintertauchplatz is the Sorpesee's cold-season dive site. According to MyDiveGuide, the entry from the campground is shallow, and after a short swim a steep wall drops toward the centre of the lake, reaching about 34 m at full water level. A local club lists the winter site as open from November to March, with prior registration mandatory.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Sorpesee, Sundern (Hochsauerlandkreis), at the campground
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 34 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Not documented
- **Current**: Not documented
- **Best Time**: November to March (winter site season per a local club)

## Underwater Features

A shallow entry zone followed by a steep wall ("wundervolle Steilwand") dropping toward the middle of the lake.

## Marine Life

A local club lists pike, perch, eels and crayfish for the Sorpesee.

## Entry and Exit

The entry is directly from the campground. Prior registration is mandatory, and registration covers both the summer and winter sites. Tank filling and rental are only available at the dive school's base in Sundern.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Register before diving
- Plan the wall dive with a set maximum depth and turn pressure; available depth changes with the reservoir level

## Safety Considerations

The wall gives fast access to depths beyond 30 m in winter water temperatures - a dry suit, cold-water regulators and good buoyancy control are essential. Bring filled tanks, as there is no filling on site.
