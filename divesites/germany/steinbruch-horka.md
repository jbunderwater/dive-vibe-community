## Steinbruch Horka

A clear-water quarry lake at Horka near Crostwitz in Upper Lusatia, entered by a steel stairway with steep walls on either side.

## Overview

Steinbruch Horka is a former quarry west of the village of Horka near Crostwitz, between Dresden and Bautzen. It is run as a dive lake with a dive base, air-fill station and accommodation on site. Depth figures have increased over time: a 2007 description gives 28 m, Taucher.Net and OpenStreetMap give 33 m, and the operator now states about 36 m because the water level has risen. The operator reports visibility of 10-15 m depending on the season, improving with depth.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Horka (Crostwitz), Landkreis Bautzen, Saxony
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 33 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 10-15 m depending on the season
- **Current**: None
- **Best Time**: April to October (operating season)

## Underwater Features

A 2007 description (Gerd Dietel) gives this layout, which may have changed since as the water level rose:

- From the stairway, a scree slope runs fairly flat to a ledge at 10 m and then steepens to the bottom at about 25 m
- Steep walls on both sides of the scree slope; a shallower bay with large boulders on the opposite side
- To the right of the entry: three shark figures, originally hung at 10 m and later lying on the bottom at about 15 m, next to a smooth vertical wall
- To the left: an old pump house at the foot of the wall
- The operator also mentions remains of former quarry operations

## Marine Life

The operator reports frog spawn and fish spawning in spring, pike and perch in summer, and noble crayfish on night dives.

## Entry and Exit

Entry is by stairs down to the water. In 2007 this was a steel stairway, as the water surface was about 10 m below ground level, with a pontoon next to it. Registration with the operator is required at least one day in advance. The operator's 2026 price list gives €15 per diver per day (older sources list lower fees). In 2026 the site is open from April to October, Thursday to Monday, with some closure periods.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Register a day ahead; walk-in diving is not accepted
- A night dive gives the best chance of seeing the crayfish

## Safety Considerations

The walls drop steeply beside the entry slope, so watch your depth. Quarry water is cold at depth; plan thermal protection accordingly.
