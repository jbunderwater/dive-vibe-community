## Sundhäuser See - Einstieg 3

The eastern entry at Sundhäuser See in Nordhausen, where the main attraction is the "Charlotte III", a fishing cutter sunk as a wreck-training object.

## Overview

Sundhäuser See lies on the southern edge of Nordhausen in northern Thuringia. According to the local dive base, the lake was created by bucket-chain excavation, which left a varied bottom with ridges and hollows. Plants grow down to about 20 m. Sources give the maximum depth as 30-35 m (Taucher.Net, unterwasserwelt.de), 31 m (Action Sport Nordhausen), 32 m (Ichthyo, for the Oasis area) and 38 m (Seen.de). Visibility of 10-15 m is widely reported. Two independent dive bases manage access: Action Sport Nordhausen ("Sundhäuser See Nord") with the numbered entries in the north-west corner, and the Oasis base, whose five entries are not covered by this entry. Entry 3 ("3N") is the newest entry of the Action Sport base, along the Uthleber Weg, with a metal ladder into the water and a coded gate (Action Sport Nordhausen). unterwasserwelt.de describes an entry 3 ("Clubwiese") about 100 m from the parking area, with stairs and a swim platform.

## Site Information

- **Location**: North shore of Sundhäuser See along the Uthleber Weg, Nordhausen, Thuringia
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 10-15 meters
- **Current**: None
- **Best Time**: Not documented

## Underwater Features

- The "Charlotte III", a cutter that the base describes as about 14 m long, 4.5 m wide and about 10 m high including the mast, sunk on 12 April 2008. The base gives its depth as 21 m and unterwasserwelt.de as about 13-20 m. Taucher.Net mentions an accessible wheelhouse, and the base uses the wreck for wreck-diver training
- Plant meadows down to about 20 m

## Marine Life

Action Sport Nordhausen lists pike, perch (in schools, up to 40 cm), carp including grass carp, eel, rainbow trout, roach, zander, tench and crustaceans for the lake. Taucher.Net reviewers (2020-2025) report frequent crustaceans, occasional perch, and rare mirror carp and pike.

## Entry and Exit

Entry by metal ladder (entry 3N) through a coded gate. Divers must register each day with the dive base that manages the entry and pay a day fee; each base charges separately (Seen.de). Seen.de gives €5 and unterwasserwelt.de €8, so check the current fee. At Action Sport Nordhausen, the gate code is given at check-in.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Leave everything on the wrecks as you found it, as the base asks
- Plan the Charlotte III dive as the main dive, with enough bottom time for its size

## Safety Considerations

The water is cold at depth: Taucher.Net reports 17-19°C at the surface and 9-10°C at 20 m in June. The varied bottom makes navigation difficult, so carry a compass. Entering any wreck or underwater structure requires proper training and equipment; the base does not allow beginners to enter the structures. Entering the wheelhouse or any other part of the Charlotte III requires wreck training and equipment.
