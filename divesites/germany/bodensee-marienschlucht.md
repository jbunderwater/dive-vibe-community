## Bodensee - Marienschlucht

A long, mussel-covered cliff wall on the Bodanrück shore below the Marienschlucht gorge, normally dived from a boat.

## Overview

The Marienschlucht and neighbouring Katharinenschlucht dive sites lie between Bodman and Wallhausen on the Überlinger See and are very difficult to reach on foot, so both 200bar.de and Tauchteam Bodensee recommend joining boat trips; Tauchteam Bodensee describes boat access from Wallhausen harbour. The cliff descends in stages to about 40 m, followed by a steep sand slope. Around 1.5 km of continuous wall between Katharinenbach and the Teufelstisch allows one-way dives. The gorge above water was closed after a 2015 landslide; Tauchteam Bodensee reports it reopened in March 2026 via a new steel walkway.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Bodanrück shore between Bodman and Wallhausen, Überlinger See, Baden-Württemberg
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 12-20 m reported, crystal clear below 20 m (200bar.de, Jan 2018); reduced significantly after rain
- **Current**: Not documented
- **Best Time**: Not documented

## Underwater Features

Numerous niches, crevices and small overhangs in mussel-covered rock along the wall. Tauchteam Bodensee notes that the Wulf-Werner caves in this area may only be dived with special permits.

## Marine Life

Tauchteam Bodensee lists eels and burbot among the inhabitants.

## Entry and Exit

Access is by boat. Diving along the wall between Katharinenbach and the Teufelstisch is permitted, but the Teufelstisch and the zone 300 m west/north-west and 500 m east/south-east of it are closed without a special permit, and violations are fined (Tauchteam Bodensee).

## Tips and Recommendations

- Join a boat dive run by a local base or club
- The roughly 1.5 km wall suits one-way dives; agree the pickup point with the boat crew
- Keep clear of the Teufelstisch ban zone at the eastern end

## Safety Considerations

Wall diving in cold, dark water; keep within your planned depth, as the slope continues below 40 m. 200bar.de notes that even in summer the Bodensee is only about 4-6 °C below roughly 10 m, so regulators can free-flow; cold-water regulators and cylinders with two separate outlets and two first stages are recommended, and first Bodensee dives should be made with a lake-experienced buddy. Under the Bodensee-Schifffahrts-Ordnung, shore divers must display a code flag 'A', and diving is prohibited within 100 m of harbour entrances and ferry landing areas; vessels always have right of way.
