## Schmaler Luzin - Carwitz

The easiest designated entry to the Schmaler Luzin, at the Carwitz bathing spot, with submerged trees along the banks and a pontoon dive boat operating from the village.

## Overview

The bathing spot at Carwitz, at the southern end of the Schmaler Luzin, is one of the lake's designated diving entries and, according to 200bar, the easiest one: a short walk from the car park down a small slope. Divers can follow the bank to the left or right, where bent trees in the water shelter fish (scubago). The Carwitz basin of the lake reaches about 33 m (Wikipedia); scubago gives about 30 m as the maximum depth for this site. The Tauchcenter Feldberg also runs dives from an electric pontoon from Carwitz, visiting the western shore between Carwitz and the Ziegenwiese, where unterwasserwelt.de reports depths to 24 m.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Badestelle Carwitz, south end of the Schmaler Luzin, Feldberger Seenlandschaft, Mecklenburg-Vorpommern
- **Entry Type**: Shore or boat entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Variable - 200bar reported 1-4 m in July 2018; the Feldberg dive base quotes 5-15 m for the lake
- **Current**: None reported (lake)
- **Best Time**: Diveable year-round according to 200bar

## Underwater Features

Submerged and bent trees along the banks, boulders, and freshwater sponges; unterwasserwelt.de describes antler sponges up to 1.5 m wide, old wooden fishing boats, sunken crockery and enamelware and a submerged carriage along the pontoon route, and an underwater ridge at 4-5 m dividing the lake's basins.

## Marine Life

Unterwasserwelt.de reports perch, pike, roach, bream, rudd and eels on the pontoon dives; seen.de mentions abundant mussels and fish in the vegetated shallows.

## Entry and Exit

Short walk from the official car park (roadside parking also possible, per scubago) down a small slope to the beach. The lake is a nature reserve with designated entries only; see the Luzinfähre page for the differing statements on registration and exemptions - register with a Feldberg base before diving. Pontoon dives run by the Feldberg base require buddy teams and use no anchoring (unterwasserwelt.de).

## Tips and Recommendations

- The easiest entry on the lake - a good choice for a first Luzin dive
- The most attractive diving is in the upper ten metres (unterwasserwelt.de)
- Consider the pontoon trip to reach the wooded western shore

## Safety Considerations

The bathing spot is busy with swimmers in summer. The banks slope steeply to more than 30 m. Cold water below the thermocline calls for appropriate thermal protection.
