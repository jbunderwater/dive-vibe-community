## Starnberger See - Allmannshausen Wintertauchplatz

A winter-only shore entry at Allmannshausen on the east shore of the Starnberger See, used while the nearby wall sites are closed.

## Overview

The Wintertauchplatz (also called Badeplatz) at Allmannshausen on the east shore of the Starnberger See is the northernmost of the Allmannshausen entries and the one used when the neighbouring wall sites are closed for the winter. ColdWater Films states that it may only be dived in winter, from 1 November to 15 March (until lake shipping resumes). TSC Starnberg describes a muddy bottom at around 19-22 m with an old hospital bed at about 19 m and old car tyres.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Allmannshausen, east shore of the Starnberger See, Bavaria
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 22 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Seasonal and temperature-dependent (TSC Starnberg); not quantified
- **Current**: Not documented
- **Best Time**: Winter only - diving here is permitted 1 November to 15 March

## Underwater Features

- Old hospital bed at about 19 m (TSC Starnberg)
- Old car tyres on a muddy bottom (TSC Starnberg)

## Marine Life

TSC Starnberg reports pike sightings at this site.

## Entry and Exit

The road to the Allmannshausen entries requires an access permit (Zufahrtsgenehmigung); ColdWater Films lists this for all Allmannshausen spots. TSC Starnberg notes the entry is at the fork in the access road and that parking at the entry is limited, so vehicles should be parked at the edge of the village. Diving in the Starnberger See is regulated by a general order (Allgemeinverfügung) of the Landratsamt Starnberg. Its key rules: solo dives are prohibited; diving with compressed air is limited to 40 m; diving is allowed until one hour after sunset; divers must carry cold-water-rated equipment with two separate, independently shut-off regulators (EN 250), thermal protection and a hood (a dive computer is recommended). A safety distance of at least 100 m must be kept from harbours, shipping lanes and landing stages, and private boat facilities, fish spawning areas, fishing gear and net enclosures are off limits.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Plan this site for the winter season, when the Wasserwacht and Naturschutzgebiet entries further south are closed
- Good buoyancy control helps on the muddy bottom

## Safety Considerations

Winter diving means very cold water; the general order explicitly requires two independent cold-water regulators and a hood. Keep within the 40 m air limit and never dive solo (both are legal requirements here).
