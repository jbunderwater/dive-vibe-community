## Walchensee - Walchenseebucht

A shallow year-round bay dive just outside the village of Walchensee, with spring pots that give locally clear water.

## Overview

Also called the Radl-Bucht, the bay is reached from a parking area on the left just after leaving the village of Walchensee, followed by a footpath to the shore (besser-tauchen.de). It is one of the sites open year-round. Walchensee is an alpine lake at about 800 m altitude in the Bavarian Prealps (Landkreis Bad Tölz-Wolfratshausen), with a maximum depth reported between about 192 m (Wikipedia) and 196-198 m (local dive sources). Diving is permitted here under a legal ordinance of the Landratsamt Bad Tölz-Wolfratshausen, but only at the released sites and times; several sites are closed from 15 October to 1 February. The lake feeds the Walchensee hydroelectric plant, so the water level is drawn down in winter (dive sources cite 4-5 m; Wikipedia gives a range between 795 m and 802 m), which changes entries noticeably.

## Site Information

- **Location**: West shore of Walchensee, just south of Walchensee village
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 15 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Not documented as a figure; several spring pots (Quelltöpfe) give good visibility at least locally (besser-tauchen.de, Rabediver)
- **Current**: None documented
- **Best Time**: Year-round (Rabediver)

## Underwater Features

Several spring pots, a fair amount of civilisation debris (hence the name Radl-Bucht), a boat wreck on the opposite side of the bay and some notable submerged trees (besser-tauchen.de). Rabediver adds an underwater forest and seagrass meadows.

## Marine Life

Rabediver describes the bay as perhaps the best place to explore the lake's species diversity and suggests it for night dives within the permitted hours. For the lake as a whole, local dive bases list lake trout, char (Saibling), pike, whitefish (Renke), perch, burbot (Aalrutte) and bullhead (Koppe).

## Entry and Exit

Shore entry via a footpath from the roadside parking area; Rabediver describes a flat shore entry.

## Tips and Recommendations

- A good option in winter when other sites are closed
- Visit the spring pots for the clearest water

## Safety Considerations

Although shallow, the site is at altitude with very cold water. Walchensee lies at roughly 800 m altitude, so altitude (Bergsee) decompression procedures apply. Local sources describe the water as very cold (about 4-8 °C) and recommend a dry suit and two independent cold-water regulators on a twin-outlet valve; lights are also listed as mandatory kit by one local source. Night diving is only allowed from one hour before sunrise until one hour after dark.
