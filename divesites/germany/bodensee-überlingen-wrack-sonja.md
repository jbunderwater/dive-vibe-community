## Bodensee - Überlingen Wrack Sonja

A deep winter-only shore dive off the Überlingen promenade to the silted-in wreck of the barge Sonja at 43-58 m.

## Overview

The Sonja is described by Tauchteam Bodensee and Primera Diving as a cargo barge (Lastenkahn) that was used during construction of the Überlingen lakeside promenade and sank in a storm. No build date, dimensions or sinking date are documented in the sources consulted (Trimix im Bodensee gives a length of about 30 m; this is a single source). The wreck lies at about 43-58 m, largely buried in sediment; Tauchteam Bodensee says only the keel protrudes, and Trimix im Bodensee describes it as lying upside down. All sources rate it as a site for experienced divers only.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Überlingen promenade (boat landing near the Zeughaus), Überlinger See, Baden-Württemberg
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 58 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Usually very clear at depth (Tauchteam Bodensee)
- **Current**: Not documented
- **Best Time**: Winter only: sources give 15 October to 31 March (Tauchteam Bodensee, Primera Diving) or 15 October to 15 March (Trimix im Bodensee)

## Underwater Features

The heavily silted hull of the Sonja; on the descent route Trimix im Bodensee mentions debris such as bicycles and tyres.

## Dive Profile

Trimix im Bodensee describes descending to about 45 m and then heading east to find the wreck. The depth means decompression obligations and high gas consumption.

## Entry and Exit

Entry is at the boat landing on the promenade, in front of the Zeughaus and an old cannon, heading straight out into the lake. Diving here is only permitted in the winter season (see Best Time). Primera Diving notes that the former parking area was privatised in 2017; divers now use Parkhaus West and walk along the promenade.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Check the current season dates before diving, as sources differ on the end date
- Plan decompression and gas carefully; consider trimix training for this depth
- Do not attempt penetration; the wreck is silted and only partly exposed, and any wreck penetration requires proper training and equipment

## Safety Considerations

At 43-58 m this dive is beyond recreational air limits, with serious narcosis and decompression risk in near-freezing water. 200bar.de notes that even in summer the Bodensee is only about 4-6 °C below roughly 10 m, so regulators can free-flow; cold-water regulators and cylinders with two separate outlets and two first stages are recommended, and first Bodensee dives should be made with a lake-experienced buddy. Under the Bodensee-Schifffahrts-Ordnung, shore divers must display a code flag 'A', and diving is prohibited within 100 m of harbour entrances and ferry landing areas; vessels always have right of way.
