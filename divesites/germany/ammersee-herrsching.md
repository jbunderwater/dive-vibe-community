## Ammersee - Herrsching

A shallow shore entry in the Herrschinger Bucht on the east side of the Ammersee, used by a local dive school for beginners' open-water dives.

## Overview

Taucher.Net lists an entry at Herrsching from a jetty near Summerstraße next to a bathing beach. The Herrschinger Bucht is a large, flat bay with a maximum depth of about 10 m (Ammerseepfad information board). The Bertl magazine reports that the Herrsching-based dive school takes beginners into the Ammersee after their pool session, but says the lake is fairly silty, probably because of the Ammer inflow, and that almost nothing can be seen below about 6 m. The only Taucher.Net review (March 2009) found 4-5 m visibility at 4°C, said you have to swim far out to reach any depth, saw no fish, and found a lot of rubbish.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Herrsching am Ammersee, east shore of the Ammersee, Bavaria
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 10 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 4-5 m in March 2009 (Taucher.Net); the local dive school says visibility is almost nil below about 6 m (Bertl magazine)
- **Current**: None reported
- **Best Time**: Not documented

## Marine Life

The 2009 Taucher.Net reviewer saw no fish. The Ammerseepfad board describes the bay as an important habitat for waterbirds, fish, amphibians and insects, and a resting and wintering area for great crested grebes and tufted ducks.

## Entry and Exit

The Taucher.Net entry is the jetty at Summerstraße next to a bathing beach, reached via the A96. The published coordinate lies next to the Herrsching steamer pier, so keep clear of ship traffic and check for local restrictions at the pier. Diving in the Ammersee is regulated by a Rechtsverordnung (listed for the Landkreis Landsberg am Lech on the Bavarian naturerlebnis.bayern.de list of waters released for diving). The Landkreis Landsberg am Lech publishes a map of no-dive zones for the whole lake (16.12.2020), with access bans under the "Ammersee-West" landscape protection ordinance and further no-dive strips along large parts of the shore. Check the current map before diving; Taucher.Net also points to the regulation published in the district's official gazette in 2020. On that map the town shore of Herrsching is not marked as a no-dive zone, but zones are marked along the shore to the north and south of the town.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Expect a long surface swim to reach deeper water; the reviewer suggested a scooter, or staying at about 5 m
- Keep away from reed areas: the Ammerseepfad board says any disturbance endangers nesting birds, especially in spring and early summer

## Safety Considerations

Visibility can drop to almost nothing below about 6 m, so stay close to your buddy and carry a light. The water is very cold in winter and spring (4°C in March 2009). Watch for boats and ships when surfacing near the piers.
