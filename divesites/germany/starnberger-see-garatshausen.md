## Starnberger See - Garatshausen

A beginner-friendly training site beside a public swimming area on the Starnberger See's west shore.

## Overview

Garatshausen, on the west shore of the Starnberger See, is described by TSC Starnberg as a beginner-friendly training site next to a public swimming area. Depths of about 25 m are reached only after swimming some distance from shore. Along the way are an old boat at about 10 m and a large pipeline at about 3 m.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Garatshausen, west shore of the Starnberger See
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 25 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Between 1 m and at best 10 m (TSC Starnberg)
- **Current**: Not documented
- **Best Time**: Not documented

## Underwater Features

- Old boat at about 10 m
- Large pipeline at about 3 m
- Algae zone at 3-5 m

## Entry and Exit

TSC Starnberg describes two access points near the public swimming area and parking for about 20-25 vehicles. Diving in the Starnberger See is regulated by a general order (Allgemeinverfügung) of the Landratsamt Starnberg. Its key rules: solo dives are prohibited; diving with compressed air is limited to 40 m; diving is allowed until one hour after sunset; divers must carry cold-water-rated equipment with two separate, independently shut-off regulators (EN 250), thermal protection and a hood (a dive computer is recommended). A safety distance of at least 100 m must be kept from harbours, shipping lanes and landing stages, and private boat facilities, fish spawning areas, fishing gear and net enclosures are off limits.

## Tips and Recommendations

- A good site for training dives according to TSC Starnberg
- Avoid the swimming area when bathers are present

## Safety Considerations

The depth is reached only well away from shore, so plan the return swim and gas accordingly, and use an SMB because of boat traffic on the lake.
