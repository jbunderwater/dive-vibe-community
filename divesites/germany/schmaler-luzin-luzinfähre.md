## Schmaler Luzin - Luzinfähre

A designated shore entry at the Luzin rope ferry below the Luzinhalle, reached by 104 steps, with submerged beech trees, freshwater sponges and small wrecks in a clear, protected lake.

## Overview

The Schmaler Luzin is a narrow glacial lake, 150-300 m wide and about 7 km long, running from Feldberg to Carwitz inside the Hullerbusch und Schmaler Luzin nature reserve. Its banks drop steeply both above and below the water. The ferry entry is one of the designated diving entries: a flight of 104 natural stone steps leads down from the Luzinhalle to the hand-operated rope ferry, and the OSM description notes a very stony entry from which divers can turn left or right along the shore. Unterwasserwelt.de gives about 18 m as the deepest part near the ferry crossing; the main basin south of the ferry reaches 33.5 m (Wikipedia). Combustion-engine boats are banned on the lake.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Luzinfähre below the Luzinhalle, Feldberg, Feldberger Seenlandschaft, Mecklenburg-Vorpommern
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 18 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Variable - 5-15 m quoted by the Feldberg dive base; 200bar reported 1-4 m in July 2018; taucher.net reviews range from 2 m to 6-9 m
- **Current**: None reported (lake)
- **Best Time**: Diveable year-round according to 200bar

## Underwater Features

Unterwasserwelt.de describes submerged beech trees ('Hechtbuchen') forming tunnels, large bright-green freshwater sponges, a steel motorboat wreck at about 6 m, a sailboat wreck below 10 m and the old ferry cable and pulleys on the bottom; 200bar mentions a small wreck in the shallows near the Luzinhalle entry. Boulders and submerged trees are common along the steep banks (seen.de).

## Marine Life

The OSM entry and 200bar report large pike and perch at this entry. Unterwasserwelt.de lists pike, perch, roach, ide, bream, crucian carp and relict shrimp for the Schmaler Luzin; Wikipedia lists bream, roach, pike, eel, perch, burbot, stickleback, tench, bleak and vendace as common species in the lake. Freshwater sponges are a highlight.

## Entry and Exit

Descend the 104 steps from the Luzinhalle to the ferry landing (200bar mentions a longer route without steps). The lake lies in a nature reserve and diving is only allowed at designated entries. Sources differ on the paperwork: seen.de states that an exemption from the environmental authority is required, issued via the local dive bases, with no night or training dives; 200bar (2018) says registration with a local base is mandatory to learn the current restrictions but no written permit is needed; taucher.net notes the lake is no longer fee-based. Register with a Feldberg base before diving.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Carrying kit down and back up 104 steps is strenuous - plan for it
- Turn left or right along the bank at the entry; most life is in the upper ten metres
- Check the current night-dive and training rules with a local base

## Safety Considerations

The entry is very stony (OSM). Steep banks lead quickly to depth. Keep clear of the ferry cable and ferry traffic at the crossing. Cold water below the thermocline calls for appropriate thermal protection.
