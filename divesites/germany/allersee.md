## Allersee

A small artificial lake in Wolfsburg's Allerpark, dived by the local club for training, with shallow water and limited visibility.

## Overview

The Allersee is an artificial lake created in 1971 in the Allerpark in Wolfsburg (about 30 ha according to Taucher.Net). It is used for sailing, rowing, canoeing and swimming. Tauchclub Wolfsburg lists it as one of its club waters with a maximum depth of 9 m, while a 2004 Taucher.Net review gives about 13 m and recommends it for training. The same reviews describe shallow areas near the shore with water plants, and mud with little or no visibility in the deeper parts.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Allerpark, Wolfsburg, Lower Saxony
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 9 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 0-8 m; about 1.5 m reported among the shallow plant beds, near zero over the muddy deeper bottom
- **Current**: None
- **Best Time**: Not documented

## Marine Life

Taucher.Net reviewers report aquatic plants but no fish seen on their dives. The local angling club lists pike, zander, carp, eel, trout, grass carp, perch, tench, burbot and wels catfish for the lake; these are angling records, not diver sightings.

## Entry and Exit

Tauchclub Wolfsburg states that no registration is required, but divers should carry a buoy because of boat traffic. The club describes access from the city centre towards the castle, taking the exit to the VfL arena, with parking at the end of that road. A Taucher.Net review describes a long shallow section to cross before reaching deeper water. The city requires a permit for boats on the lake and refers to its lake regulations (Allerseeordnung), held by the city's green-spaces department; check them before diving.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Best treated as a training site - reviewers found little to see
- Stay off the bottom; the deeper areas are muddy and silt up at once

## Safety Considerations

Visibility can drop to zero, so keep close contact with your buddy. Rowing, sailing and canoe traffic is common - always use a surface marker buoy.
