## Tegernsee - Holz

A versatile west-shore entry at Holz on the Tegernsee, with a basin, an underwater hill and a deeper canyon landscape.

## Overview

Holz, on the west shore of the Tegernsee, is a multi-option shore entry described in detail by Seegeist. The Seeseiten Tegernsee magazine calls the "Bayersäge in Holz" ideal for beginners because of its gradual entry into shallow water, with deeper areas further out. From the same entry, divers can reach a W-shaped basin (the "Kessel"), an underwater hill ("Seeberg") with a wall on its east side, and a deeper hill-and-canyon landscape.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Holz, west shore of the Tegernsee, Bavaria
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Seegeist reports for 2026 range from 0 m (mid-June) to about 5 m in July/August, often with a turbid layer at 6-8 m and clearer, colder water (about 12°C) below 10-12 m
- **Current**: Not documented
- **Best Time**: Not documented

## Underwater Features

- **Kessel**: from the bathing entry, swim about 10 minutes north-east at 6 m; the bottom then drops 3-5 m into a W-shaped basin (Seegeist)
- **Seeberg**: about 10 minutes due east (90°) from the bathing entry the bottom rises to 2-3 m; on the east side of this hill a wall drops to 20-25 m (Seegeist)
- **Hügelige Welt** ("hilly world"): follow the Kessel's southern wall east to a hill-and-canyon landscape at 25-40 m. Seegeist calls this a decompression dive, "not for sport divers"

## Entry and Exit

Seegeist says to park in the car park, which is also the entry point; you can dive to the left and right. Keep 50 m from the reed belt and stay at a minimum depth of 6-8 m because of spawning zones and a nature reserve. At the Seeberg, watch for shipping and set a buoy. Diving in the Tegernsee is governed by a general order (Allgemeinverfügung) of the Landratsamt Miesbach. Seegeist states that private diving needs no separate permit or dive-site card; people who train divers need an agreement with the Bavarian Palace and Lakes Administration. The general order prohibits diving in the seven buoyed and signed protection zones, in reed and aquatic plant areas and their access routes, at fish spawning grounds and fishing gear, within 100 m of fish traps, in shipping lanes and within 100 m of docks, and in private harbours and boathouse areas. In the bathing season (15 March - 31 October) diving is prohibited in bathing areas, and hatched waterfowl zones are closed from September to March.

## Tips and Recommendations

- The Kessel and Seeberg are good navigation dives from the same entry
- Only trained, properly equipped divers should attempt the Hügelige Welt, which requires planned decompression

## Safety Considerations

The Tegernsee lies at 725 m above sea level, so use altitude settings and altitude-adjusted decompression planning. The 25-40 m section requires decompression. The Seeberg rises to within 2-3 m of the surface in an area with boat traffic, so surface with an SMB.
