## Steinberger See

The single documented dive site of the Steinberger See, a flooded lignite mine, where a concrete channel leads down to 38 m.

## Overview

The Steinberger See near Schwandorf is a flooded former lignite mine (mining ended in 1982, according to 200bar.de) with acidic water. Its dive site "Zur Rutsch'n" lies on the south shore between a small bridge (inlet) and a pump house (outlet). From the bridge, a concrete channel (the "Rutsch'n") runs down to 38 m, with terraced ground on both sides. Taucher.Net and 200bar.de give about 40 m as the maximum depth of the site; 200bar.de puts the lake's maximum at about 60 m.

## Site Information

- **Location**: South shore of the Steinberger See, Steinberg am See, Landkreis Schwandorf, Bavaria
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 1-5 m depending on season and diver traffic (200bar.de); the local club reports clear water to 6 m, turbid at 7-15 m, about 5 m at 15-25 m and increasingly turbid below 25 m, and says visibility has declined noticeably since about 2012
- **Current**: Not documented
- **Best Time**: Not documented

## Underwater Features

- Concrete channel ("Rutsch'n") from the bridge down to 38 m
- Terraced slopes on both sides of the channel, with trees covered in algae, moss and sediment
- Three platforms: one at about 8 m to the left of the channel, and two near the pump house at about 10 m and 16 m, connected by guide lines (SV Schwandorf-Ettmannsdorf)
- Taucher.Net reviewers describe a "futuristic" moonscape with sparse vegetation

## Marine Life

Fish numbers are low because of the acidic water (200bar.de). Perch gather under the concrete channel near the entry (200bar.de, SV Schwandorf-Ettmannsdorf), and Taucher.Net reviewers report perch and pike at 1-8 m and once a shy group of about a dozen carp over a metre long. The local club notes that fish once common near the channel have become scarce.

## Entry and Exit

Oberpfälzer Seenland gives the entry at south-shore car park 5 (paid parking), a few metres from the water, with toilets. The club says the car park is about 50 m from the shore and has been paid since February 2021. Taucher.Net describes entry via the channel or a small bay between the pump house and the bridge. Diving is regulated by the Landkreis Schwandorf diving ordinance, which defines restricted and approved zones.

## Tips and Recommendations

- The bottom is very muddy - avoid touching it (200bar.de)
- Taucher.Net reviewers call the site suitable for training and night dives

## Safety Considerations

Windsurfers and sailors use the lake, so take care when surfacing (200bar.de). Following the channel leads quickly to 38 m, so watch your depth. Taucher.Net lists Regensburg as the nearest decompression chamber.
