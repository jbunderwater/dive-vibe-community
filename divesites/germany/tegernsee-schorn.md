## Tegernsee - Schorn

A shore entry at Schorn on the south-east shore of the Tegernsee, with a sandy and clay bottom that divers-guide.com says gets deeper than expected in places.

## Overview

Schorn is listed as a Tegernsee dive site by divers-guide.com, which places the entry directly next to a wooden boathouse on the waterfront and describes sand, fish and some vegetation. divers-guide.com also says an old transport ship should lie somewhere underwater; no other source found confirms this, so treat it as unverified. The local dive school Seegeist does not list Schorn as its own entry, but mentions it as the limit when diving to the left from the neighbouring "Bootshäuser Rottach (Panzer)" entry.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Schorn, south-east shore of the Tegernsee, Bavaria
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 35 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 5-10 m, average about 6 m (divers-guide.com)
- **Current**: None (divers-guide.com)
- **Best Time**: Not documented

## Underwater Features

- Clay and sand bottom with some vegetation (divers-guide.com)
- A possible old transport ship, reported only by divers-guide.com (unconfirmed)

## Entry and Exit

divers-guide.com says there is sufficient parking and a walk of about 30 m to the water, with the entry directly next to a wooden boathouse. Diving in the Tegernsee is governed by a general order (Allgemeinverfügung) of the Landratsamt Miesbach (2004). Among other things it prohibits diving in the dotted nature-protection areas of the attached maps, in the hatched waterfowl areas from September to the end of March, in the seven buoyed and signed Tegernsee protection zones, in reed and aquatic-plant zones and their access routes, at fish spawning grounds and fishing gear (and within 100 m of set nets and traps), within the 100 m zones of the shipping piers and on the Bavarian lake shipping routes, in bathing areas and public bathing grounds during the bathing season (15 March - 31 October), and in harbours, buoy fields and boathouse areas. Ice dives are only allowed through naturally existing openings when the ice cover is not fully closed. Because of the boathouse rule, check locally where exactly you may enter and dive here.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Watch your depth: divers-guide.com warns that the site can be deeper than expected in some areas
- Seegeist warns of the police boat at the neighbouring Rottach boathouse entry

## Safety Considerations

The Tegernsee lies at 725 m above sea level, so use altitude settings on your dive computer and plan decompression accordingly. Water below the thermocline is cold even in summer. Watch for boat traffic when surfacing.
