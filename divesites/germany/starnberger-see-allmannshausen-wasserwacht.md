## Starnberger See - Allmannshausen Wasserwacht

The classic entry to the Allmannshauser Steilwand, a stepped wall on the Starnberger See's east shore that is for experienced divers only.

## Overview

The Wasserwacht entry, north of the Wasserwacht (water rescue) station at Allmannshausen, is the classic entry to the Allmannshauser Steilwand. Tauchclub Bamberg describes a stepped rocky cliff with small vertical sections and terraces and estimates it continues to 80-100 m. Sources give different depths for the top of the wall at this entry - about 12 m (200bar.de), about 20 m (ColdWater Films) and about 25 m, rising to 13 m further south (Tauchclub Bamberg).

## Site Information

- **Location**: Allmannshausen, at the Wasserwacht station, east shore of the Starnberger See
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 8-10 m on average, up to 30 m at best (Tauchclub Bamberg); 200bar.de logged 4-10 m in May 2018
- **Current**: Not documented
- **Best Time**: Closed 1 November to 15 March (winter diving ban)

## Underwater Features

- Stepped steep wall with terraces (Tauchclub Bamberg)
- Deco stop / platform along the southern edge (ColdWater Films)
- Concrete slabs at about 3 m near the slipway (TSC Starnberg)
- A rowboat wreck at about 70 m (ColdWater Films) - beyond the 40 m air limit

## Marine Life

TSC Starnberg notes that eels and burbot may be seen here.

## Entry and Exit

The road to the Allmannshausen entries requires an access permit (Zufahrtsgenehmigung); ColdWater Films lists this for all Allmannshausen spots. TSC Starnberg describes the entry north of the Wasserwacht station, with limited street-side parking - unload and move the car. Tauchclub Bamberg describes entering directly at the wall via a buoy. Diving is prohibited here from 1 November to 15 March. Diving in the Starnberger See is regulated by a general order (Allgemeinverfügung) of the Landratsamt Starnberg. Its key rules: solo dives are prohibited; diving with compressed air is limited to 40 m; diving is allowed until one hour after sunset; divers must carry cold-water-rated equipment with two separate, independently shut-off regulators (EN 250), thermal protection and a hood (a dive computer is recommended). A safety distance of at least 100 m must be kept from harbours, shipping lanes and landing stages, and private boat facilities, fish spawning areas, fishing gear and net enclosures are off limits.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Tauchclub Bamberg and 200bar.de both describe this as a site for experienced divers only
- Use the platform along the southern edge for safety or deco stops

## Safety Considerations

This is the heart of the Allmannshauser Steilwand, where most of the lake's diving fatalities have occurred (the general order cites 11 fatal accidents and 35 serious injuries since 1994, mainly in this area). Nitrogen narcosis is a real risk; stay at or above 40 m on air, dive with a buddy, and carry the required redundant cold-water regulators.
