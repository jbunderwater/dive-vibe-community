## Steinbruch Ammelshain

A flooded quarry near Naunhof east of Leipzig, run as the training site of the Saxon diving federation, with car wrecks, mining relics and a training course.

## Overview

The Großer Ammelshainer Steinbruch is a former granite quarry at Ammelshain (Naunhof), east of Leipzig. It is run by the Saxon state diving federation (Landestauchsportverband Sachsen) as its training site (TANA), with overnight accommodation and an air-fill station on site. Depth figures range from 26 m (Taucher.Net) and 27 m (the federation) to 29 m (200bar, unterwasserwelt.de). The shallow entry area at 2-4 m is covered in vegetation such as water milfoil, and the terrain drops to about 20 m in places (200bar).

## Site Information

- **Location**: Ammelshain, Naunhof, Landkreis Leipzig, Saxony
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 29 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 3-10 m reported; poor near the bottom; under ice occasionally much better
- **Current**: None
- **Best Time**: Not documented

## Underwater Features

- A sunken motorboat at about 12 m (200bar, unterwasserwelt.de)
- Two Wartburg car wrecks at about 22-29 m (200bar gives 22 m and 26 m)
- A training obstacle course from 1990 with steel containers and crawl-through obstacles (unterwasserwelt.de)
- Quarry relics: narrow-gauge rails, ore carts, pumps and cables, mostly deteriorated (Landestauchsportverband Sachsen, Taucher.Net)
- Submerged tree stumps and wooden structures, a small cave formation, and a training platform and apnea course with guide tubes (unterwasserwelt.de, Taucher.Net)

## Marine Life

unterwasserwelt.de lists perch, pike, carp, bream, roach and tench; Taucher.Net reviewers also mention crayfish and schools of juvenile fish.

## Entry and Exit

Prior registration is required (Landestauchsportverband Sachsen). The site is open to members of VDST and other state diving federations, and to guests. A valid certification and dive medical are required (unterwasserwelt.de). According to the federation, the day fee is €7.50 (€4 for LVS members) plus a €2.50 service fee; older sources list lower fees. Vehicles are not allowed to drive to the water, so equipment is carried about 100 m from the parking area. 200bar notes that parking is only allowed at the dive centre because of nature-protection rules.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Do not dive the protected reed zone at the rear of the quarry; circling at about 5 m keeps you clear of it (unterwasserwelt.de)
- The car wrecks lie deep; plan them as a dedicated dive

## Safety Considerations

The water is cold at depth (Taucher.Net reported 20°C at the surface and 14°C at 10 m in July), and visibility near the bottom can be close to zero. Do not enter the cave formation or the training tubes without the appropriate training.
