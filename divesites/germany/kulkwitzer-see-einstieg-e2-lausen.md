## Kulkwitzer See - Einstieg E2 Lausen

The main east-shore entry of Kulkwitzer See at Lausen, where training platforms and a small aircraft fuselage are the main attractions.

## Overview

Kulkwitzer See is a flooded lignite opencast pit on the western edge of Leipzig, flooded from 1963 and opened in 1973 (Wikipedia). Entry E2 lies on the east shore in the Lausen district, next to a dive base with an air-fill station. It is the entry most associated with the lake's artificial attractions: training platforms at several depths and the fuselage of a small aircraft at about 20 m. Sources give the lake's maximum depth as 32 m (Just Leave Bubbles, club reports) up to 34-36 m (Seen.de, Wikipedia 36 m). The most attractive diving, according to Seen.de, is down to about 10 m, where stonewort (Characeae) meadows grow.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Lausen-Grünau, east shore of Kulkwitzer See, Leipzig, Saxony
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 32 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Often described as very clear; the lake is regularly cited among Germany's clearest dive lakes
- **Current**: None
- **Best Time**: Not documented

## Underwater Features

- Fuselage of a small Piper aircraft, about 6 m long, at about 19-20 m (Flotte Flosse; Just Leave Bubbles and Ichthyo give about 20 m)
- A large platform structure (reported as about 20 m tall and 9 m wide, starting at about 6 m) and a small platform at about 5 m (Flotte Flosse)
- A large metal framework colonised by freshwater mussels, reached from this entry (Just Leave Bubbles)
- Rails, barrels, stones and sandy formations, plus vegetation areas to the south (Padertaucher)
- A memorial plaque for a diver (Flotte Flosse)

## Marine Life

Club reports from this entry list perch, roach, tench, pike and carp, and repeated sightings of wels catfish (one group reported four fish of 1.5-2 m near the large platform). A large silver carp has also been reported. Seen.de mentions very large mirror carp in the shallows.

## Dive Profile

The gravelly shallows slope gradually, which makes the area popular for training (Just Leave Bubbles). The platforms and the aircraft are the usual targets; the aircraft lies at about 20 m.

## Entry and Exit

Diving at Kulkwitzer See is only permitted from the three designated entry points (E1 Göhrenz in the south, E2 and E3 on the east shore). A 2019 club report notes that diving in the shallow shore zone (under about 5 m) outside the entry points is not allowed to protect flora and fauna, and that divers snorkel out roughly 50 m before descending. According to Seen.de, neither dive base on the lake charges a diving fee; parking at the lake is paid. The Pro-Taucher description places this entry on the Lausen side at the naturist beach.

## Tips and Recommendations

- A compass helps to find the aircraft and the platforms
- Expect crowding at the platforms on summer weekends

## Safety Considerations

The water gets cold quickly below the thermocline (7°C at 23 m measured in July). The aircraft is at about 20 m, so manage gas and no-decompression limits accordingly.
