## Walchensee - Lehmsteilwand

A wall dive through clay formations shaped by ice-age glaciers on Walchensee's east shore toll road.

## Overview

The Lehmsteilwand (clay wall) is reached via the toll road on the east shore. Rabediver describes impressive clay formations formed by ice-age glaciers, a wall and a canyon landscape; ColdWater Films lists a wall, a canyon and an island with a sloping wall. Walchensee is an alpine lake at about 800 m altitude in the Bavarian Prealps (Landkreis Bad Tölz-Wolfratshausen), with a maximum depth reported between about 192 m (Wikipedia) and 196-198 m (local dive sources). Diving is permitted here under a legal ordinance of the Landratsamt Bad Tölz-Wolfratshausen, but only at the released sites and times; several sites are closed from 15 October to 1 February. The lake feeds the Walchensee hydroelectric plant, so the water level is drawn down in winter (dive sources cite 4-5 m; Wikipedia gives a range between 795 m and 802 m), which changes entries noticeably.

## Site Information

- **Location**: East shore of Walchensee on the toll road
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 35 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Not documented for this site; local dive bases describe 10-15 m as normal for the lake, varying with season and weather
- **Current**: None documented
- **Best Time**: Sources conflict: ColdWater Films lists it as diveable year-round, while Rabediver gives 1 February to 14 October - check the current ordinance

## Underwater Features

Clay wall, canyon landscape of clay formations and an island with a sloping wall.

## Marine Life

For the lake as a whole, local dive bases list lake trout, char (Saibling), pike, whitefish (Renke), perch, burbot (Aalrutte) and bullhead (Koppe).

## Dive Profile

ColdWater Films recommends a depth range between 18 and 35 m.

## Entry and Exit

Entry over a stone staircase (Rabediver). The access road is a toll road.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Confirm the seasonal status with the Landratsamt ordinance before a winter dive
- Good buoyancy is essential to avoid stirring up the clay

## Safety Considerations

Rabediver rates the site for experienced divers. Walchensee lies at roughly 800 m altitude, so altitude (Bergsee) decompression procedures apply. Local sources describe the water as very cold (about 4-8 °C) and recommend a dry suit and two independent cold-water regulators on a twin-outlet valve; lights are also listed as mandatory kit by one local source. Night diving is only allowed from one hour before sunrise until one hour after dark.
