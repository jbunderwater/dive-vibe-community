## Kulkwitzer See - Einstieg E3 Campingplatz

The northern east-shore entry of Kulkwitzer See at the campground, known for a sunken launch and a deep training structure.

## Overview

Kulkwitzer See is a flooded lignite pit on the western edge of Leipzig (flooded from 1963, opened 1973, per Wikipedia). Entry E3 is at the campground on the east shore. Ichthyo describes it as equipped with a practice tower and a metal platform for entering the water. A 2022 club report describes two training structures here, one at about 5 m with a platform and one at about 20 m with several platforms and netting, as well as a sunken launch ("Barkasse", described as an old pilot boat). Sources give the lake's maximum depth as 32-36 m.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Campground on the east shore of Kulkwitzer See, Leipzig, Saxony
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 32 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Good visibility reported in spring 2022; no figures documented
- **Current**: None
- **Best Time**: Not documented

## Underwater Features

- Sunken launch ("Barkasse") at about 21 m, with steel interior structures visible through the portholes (Ichthyo, Padertaucher)
- A training structure at about 20 m with several platforms and netting, and a platform at about 5 m (Padertaucher)
- Vegetation areas and rock formations

## Marine Life

The 2022 Padertaucher report mentions large carp near the shore, a large pike on a dusk dive, crayfish and small fish schools at this entry.

## Entry and Exit

Diving at Kulkwitzer See is only permitted from the three designated entry points (E1 Göhrenz in the south, E2 and E3 on the east shore). A 2019 club report notes that diving in the shallow shore zone (under about 5 m) outside the entry points is not allowed to protect flora and fauna, and that divers snorkel out roughly 50 m before descending. According to Seen.de, neither dive base on the lake charges a diving fee; parking at the lake is paid. Ichthyo notes that the campground charges an admission fee for this entry, which is waived for overnight guests.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Navigate by compass to the 20 m structure and the launch
- Look through the portholes rather than entering the wreck

## Safety Considerations

The launch lies at about 21 m in cold water. Entering any wreck requires proper wreck training and equipment; do not penetrate without it.
