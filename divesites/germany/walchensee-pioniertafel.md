## Walchensee - Pioniertafel

A wall and scree-slope dive on the north shore east of Urfeld, reachable only on foot with a handcart or by pedal boat.

## Overview

The site takes its name from a memorial plaque next to a bench about 1 km from Urfeld along the lakeside path towards Sachenbach/Jachenau (Tauchclub Bamberg describes it as a plaque for three nuns who died in an accident). The road is closed to car traffic. Walchensee is an alpine lake at about 800 m altitude in the Bavarian Prealps (Landkreis Bad Tölz-Wolfratshausen), with a maximum depth reported between about 192 m (Wikipedia) and 196-198 m (local dive sources). Diving is permitted here under a legal ordinance of the Landratsamt Bad Tölz-Wolfratshausen, but only at the released sites and times; several sites are closed from 15 October to 1 February. The lake feeds the Walchensee hydroelectric plant, so the water level is drawn down in winter (dive sources cite 4-5 m; Wikipedia gives a range between 795 m and 802 m), which changes entries noticeably.

## Site Information

- **Location**: North shore of Walchensee, about 1 km east of Urfeld on the path towards Sachenbach/Jachenau
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Not documented for this site; local dive bases describe 10-15 m as normal for the lake, varying with season and weather
- **Current**: None documented
- **Best Time**: Year-round (listed as diveable all year by ColdWater Films, Rabediver and Tauchclub Bamberg)

## Underwater Features

To one side lies a steep wall; besser-tauchen.de notes that its upper edge also drops away steeply, which matters if no free ascent is planned. In the other direction a scree slope holds sunken trees and the remains of a VW Beetle (described by besser-tauchen.de as a convertible). besser-tauchen.de also reports that remnants of munitions dumped by the Wehrmacht at the end of the war still lie here and must not be touched (single source).

## Marine Life

besser-tauchen.de reports char (Saibling) here in autumn and winter. For the lake as a whole, local dive bases list lake trout, char (Saibling), pike, whitefish (Renke), perch, burbot (Aalrutte) and bullhead (Koppe).

## Dive Profile

Tauchclub Bamberg recommends descending as desired to about 30 m and turning left into the wall, which is vertical only to about 20-25 m before running out into a scree slope. besser-tauchen.de notes that with enough stamina and gas the wall can be followed all the way to the Galerie.

## Entry and Exit

The site is not reachable by car. Park in Urfeld (limited spaces), then carry equipment about 1 km along the path with a handcart, or rent a pedal boat in Urfeld (Rabediver gives about a 20-minute walk). ColdWater Films notes that an access permit is needed for the road. The entry involves some scrambling over boulders, which besser-tauchen.de describes as strenuous with full kit, especially in snow.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Bring a handcart or rent a pedal boat in Urfeld to reach the site
- Plan the ascent route carefully because the top of the wall also slopes away
- Do not touch any munitions remnants

## Safety Considerations

Air limits are reached quickly on this wall, according to besser-tauchen.de. Old munitions are reported on the scree slope - leave them alone and report finds to the authorities. Walchensee lies at roughly 800 m altitude, so altitude (Bergsee) decompression procedures apply. Local sources describe the water as very cold (about 4-8 °C) and recommend a dry suit and two independent cold-water regulators on a twin-outlet valve; lights are also listed as mandatory kit by one local source. Night diving is only allowed from one hour before sunrise until one hour after dark.
