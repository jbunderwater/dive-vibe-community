## Rursee - Bank 2

The gentler of the Rursee's two designated dive sites, with a gradual descent along rock formations and overhangs, open to a wider range of buddy teams.

## Overview

Bank 2 is the second official dive site in the Eschauel bay of the Rursee. The Dürener Unterwasserclub describes a gradual descent to about 40 m, with long rock formations along the right-hand side creating overhangs and impressive structures. The Aachener Unterwasserclub mentions a metal sculpture called "Werner" used for training. Like Bank 1, the site is comparatively dark and cold because of the narrow valley.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Eschauel bay, Rursee (Schwammenauel reservoir), Nideggen-Schmidt
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Clubs describe clear water with generally good visibility
- **Current**: Not documented
- **Best Time**: April to October (only open season)

## Underwater Features

- Rock ridges along the right-hand side with overhangs
- "Werner", a metal sculpture for training exercises (AUC Aachen)

## Marine Life

Taucher.net lists pike, perch and eels for the Rursee. The DUC Düren mentions a turtle that roams the area.

## Entry and Exit

Entry is via concrete steps down the bank; usability depends on the reservoir level. Diving at the Rursee is permitted only at Bank 1 and Bank 2 in the Eschauel bay of the Schwammenauel reservoir. The TSV NRW meeting point is the large car park on the Eschaueler Weg in Nideggen(-Schmidt); on weekends local clubs run a small shuttle van from the car park to the banks. The season runs from the first Saturday in April to the last Sunday in October, with regular operation from 9:30 to 17:00 on weekends and holidays; opening is signalled by the Alpha flag at Bank 1. Advance online registration with the TSV NRW is required, a supervisor is present, and divers need a valid dive medical and diving accident insurance, shown on site. A daily fee (or annual pass) applies, and parking is charged at weekends. Commercial training is not allowed. Full thermal protection (hood and gloves), two regulators and an underwater light are mandatory. There is no fill station, dive shop, showers or toilets. Under the TSV NRW group rules, a CMAS**/CMAS* pair or a CMAS*** diver leading two CMAS* divers is limited to 20 m at Bank 2, and two CMAS* divers may not dive together.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Register online with the TSV NRW before the day
- Check the depth limit that applies to your buddy team
- Bring filled tanks; there is no filling at the lake

## Safety Considerations

Although the slope is gradual, the site reaches about 40 m in cold, dark water. Hood, gloves, redundant regulators and a light are mandatory. The steps can be hard to use at some reservoir levels.
