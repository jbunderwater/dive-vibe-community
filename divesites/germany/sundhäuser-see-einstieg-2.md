## Sundhäuser See - Einstieg 2

The western entry at Sundhäuser See in Nordhausen, closest to the small beginner-friendly wreck "Charlotte I".

## Overview

Sundhäuser See lies on the southern edge of Nordhausen in northern Thuringia. According to the local dive base, the lake was created by bucket-chain excavation, which left a varied bottom with ridges and hollows. Plants grow down to about 20 m. Sources give the maximum depth as 30-35 m (Taucher.Net, unterwasserwelt.de), 31 m (Action Sport Nordhausen), 32 m (Ichthyo, for the Oasis area) and 38 m (Seen.de). Visibility of 10-15 m is widely reported. Two independent dive bases manage access: Action Sport Nordhausen ("Sundhäuser See Nord") with the numbered entries in the north-west corner, and the Oasis base, whose five entries are not covered by this entry. Entry 2 lies west of the main pontoon and is reached via wooden stairs. The nearby entry 2a, on the western grounds near the B80 road, gives access to the same area.

## Site Information

- **Location**: North-west shore of Sundhäuser See, Nordhausen, Thuringia
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 10-15 meters
- **Current**: None
- **Best Time**: Not documented

## Underwater Features

- The "Charlotte I", a cabin cruiser of about 6 m at 12-15 m, which the base describes as suitable for beginners (Action Sport Nordhausen)
- The motorboat "Galileo" at about 9 m, between entries 1 and 2
- Plant meadows down to about 20 m

## Marine Life

Action Sport Nordhausen lists pike, perch (in schools, up to 40 cm), carp including grass carp, eel, rainbow trout, roach, zander, tench and crustaceans for the lake. Taucher.Net reviewers (2020-2025) report frequent crustaceans, occasional perch, and rare mirror carp and pike.

## Entry and Exit

Entry via wooden stairs (entry 2) or from the western grounds (entry 2a). Divers must register each day with the dive base that manages the entry and pay a day fee; each base charges separately (Seen.de). Seen.de gives €5 and unterwasserwelt.de €8, so check the current fee. At Action Sport Nordhausen, the gate code is given at check-in.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Charlotte I is a good first wreck dive in the lake
- Carry a compass; the bottom formations are easy to lose your way in

## Safety Considerations

The water is cold at depth: Taucher.Net reports 17-19°C at the surface and 9-10°C at 20 m in June. The varied bottom makes navigation difficult, so carry a compass. Entering any wreck or underwater structure requires proper training and equipment; the base does not allow beginners to enter the structures.
