## Bodensee - Bodman Alter Hafen

A winter-only shore dive in front of Bodman harbour among old wooden piles and beams at around 12 m.

## Overview

Bodman - Alter Hafen lies at the western end of the Überlinger See directly in front of the sailing and excursion harbour of Bodman. Because of boat traffic it is purely a winter dive site: diving is generally prohibited during the shipping season (200bar.de; Tauchteam Bodensee gives October-March, typically about 1 November to 15 March). The main attraction is historic wooden piles and beams of the old harbour at about 12 m on a gently sloping bottom that is accessible to about 20 m. Tauchteam Bodensee rates it as beginner-friendly and suitable for dry-suit training.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Bodman (Seestraße), Bodman-Ludwigshafen, Baden-Württemberg
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 20 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 5-10 m reported (200bar.de, Jan 2016); generally good
- **Current**: Not documented
- **Best Time**: Winter only, during the closed shipping season (see Overview)

## Underwater Features

Historic wooden piles and beams from the old harbour at about 12 m; Tauchteam Bodensee compares the experience to a wreck dive.

## Marine Life

Tauchteam Bodensee reports crustaceans and small fish among the structures.

## Entry and Exit

Entry is via the slipway at the right end of the harbour basin. Parking is along Seestraße at the harbour and is paid year-round according to Tauchteam Bodensee. Before diving, confirm that there are no vessel movements in or at the harbour.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Dive in the morning or bring a light: the Bodanrück shades the site in the afternoon and evening
- The slipway can be slippery in winter and step heights vary with the water level
- Check the Bodensee shipping timetable before diving

## Safety Considerations

Winter water is very cold; use cold-water-rated equipment. 200bar.de notes that even in summer the Bodensee is only about 4-6 °C below roughly 10 m, so regulators can free-flow; cold-water regulators and cylinders with two separate outlets and two first stages are recommended, and first Bodensee dives should be made with a lake-experienced buddy. Under the Bodensee-Schifffahrts-Ordnung, shore divers must display a code flag 'A', and diving is prohibited within 100 m of harbour entrances and ferry landing areas; vessels always have right of way.
