## Starnberger See - Berg

A winter shore dive at Berg on the Starnberger See with several sunken boats close to the steamer landing stage.

## Overview

The entry at Berg on the north-east shore of the Starnberger See lies close to the Berg steamer landing stage. Several sunken boats lie off the entry: Taucher.Net describes a 35 m² national-class sailing cruiser resting on its starboard side at about 8 m and an old pedal boat, and ColdWater Films lists a sailboat (9-10 m), a pedal boat (6-7 m), a kayak (about 29 m) and the "Schindelwrack" at 38 m. Sources differ slightly on the depths of the shallow boats.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Berg, north-east shore of the Starnberger See, next to the steamer landing stage
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 38 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 6-10 m in winter; 0.5-2 m in summer due to algal blooms (Taucher.Net)
- **Current**: Not documented
- **Best Time**: Winter (see access restrictions below)

## Underwater Features

- Sailing cruiser wreck on its starboard side at about 8-10 m
- Old pedal boat at about 6-8 m
- Kayak at about 29 m
- "Schindelwrack" at 38 m (ColdWater Films)

## Entry and Exit

Berg is one of the generally accessible shore areas listed in the Allgemeinverfügung. Because the entry is right next to the steamer landing, Taucher.Net says not to dive while the passenger steamers are operating, and ColdWater Films states that diving here is only tolerated when ship traffic has stopped (in winter). ColdWater Films also asks divers to be considerate of residents, especially in the morning, to keep the site open. Diving in the Starnberger See is regulated by a general order (Allgemeinverfügung) of the Landratsamt Starnberg. Its key rules: solo dives are prohibited; diving with compressed air is limited to 40 m; diving is allowed until one hour after sunset; divers must carry cold-water-rated equipment with two separate, independently shut-off regulators (EN 250), thermal protection and a hood (a dive computer is recommended). A safety distance of at least 100 m must be kept from harbours, shipping lanes and landing stages, and private boat facilities, fish spawning areas, fishing gear and net enclosures are off limits.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Excellent buoyancy is needed - the silty bottom stirs up easily (Taucher.Net, ColdWater Films)
- Taucher.Net advises against entering the sailboat's interior

## Safety Considerations

Keep well clear of the steamer landing and shipping traffic. The deeper Schindelwrack at 38 m is close to the 40 m air limit and much colder and darker than the shallow boats. Wreck penetration requires proper training and equipment.
