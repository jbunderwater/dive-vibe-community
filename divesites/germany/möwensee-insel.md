## Möwensee - Insel

The front (gate-side) entry to the clear Möwensee near Nordhausen, with a dive out to the lake's small island.

## Overview

The Möwensee is a disused gravel pit south-east of Nordhausen, about 1.2 km long and up to 700 m wide, and part of the lake landscape around the Sundhäuser See (Wikipedia). Action Sport Nordhausen describes its water as clear enough that a light is rarely needed even at depth, with remains of the former gravel extraction equipment in the water and gravel banks that run out flat before dropping off. This entry, Action Sport's Tauchplatz 5 "Insel" (justDiving's Einstieg I), is right behind the entrance gate and looks out at a small island about 350 m away.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Möwensee, Nordhausen (Bielen/Sundhausen), Thuringia
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 12 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Clear; Taucher.Net reported up to 10 m (September 2011)
- **Current**: None reported
- **Best Time**: Not documented

## Underwater Features

- The small island, about 350 m from the entry (justDiving); unterwasserwelt.de says circling the island makes an atmospheric dive
- Willows overhanging the water, whose red water roots are especially pronounced at this front entry (unterwasserwelt.de)
- Leftovers of the former gravel works (justDiving)

## Marine Life

Action Sport Nordhausen reports large schools of perch, rudd, carp, crayfish, freshwater sponges and pike among the tree roots, and says a catfish of about 1.5 m is sometimes seen at the island. justDiving mentions large perch, zander and many large crayfish at this entry. The catfish is reported by the bases and articles, but unterwasserwelt.de's authors did not meet it themselves.

## Dive Profile

justDiving suggests snorkelling to the island and then diving around it, or descending straight from the entry. Action Sport warns that air can run short on the way back from the island and suggests returning in the 5 m zone.

## Entry and Exit

Diving requires registration with one of the dive bases at the Sundhäuser See (justDiving; Taucher.Net says the Sundhäuser See day ticket also covers the Möwensee). Access is through a gate with a combination lock; you get the code when you register (Action Sport Nordhausen). The entry is immediately to the left behind the gate. There are no toilets or catering at the lake (justDiving).

## Tips and Recommendations

- A compass is strongly recommended for the island dive (Action Sport Nordhausen)
- Plan your gas for the return swim from the island

## Safety Considerations

The lake reaches 46 m elsewhere, so watch your depth when leaving the shore zone. The water is cold at depth outside summer; plan thermal protection accordingly.
