## Ammersee - Utting

A west-shore entry at Utting, which a Taucher.Net reviewer called the best area of the Ammersee for diving, especially in winter.

## Overview

The only Taucher.Net review of this site (2005) says the best diving in the Ammersee is near Utting, ideally in winter, and that the shallow east side is less suitable. After a shallow shore zone the bottom drops more steeply to 20 m. The Ammer flows through the lake and fine sediment drifts through it, so visibility ranges from 30 cm to 5 m.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Utting am Ammersee, west shore of the Ammersee, Bavaria
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 20 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 0.3-5 m (Taucher.Net review)
- **Current**: None reported
- **Best Time**: Winter, according to the Taucher.Net reviewer

## Marine Life

The Taucher.Net reviewer reports many perch among the plants in the shore zone and pike lying in wait near the shore.

## Entry and Exit

Taucher.Net does not publish an exact entry point; its map position is at the Utting bathing beach. Diving in the Ammersee is regulated by a Rechtsverordnung (listed for the Landkreis Landsberg am Lech on the Bavarian naturerlebnis.bayern.de list of waters released for diving). The Landkreis Landsberg am Lech publishes a map of no-dive zones for the whole lake (16.12.2020), with access bans under the "Ammersee-West" landscape protection ordinance and further no-dive strips along large parts of the shore. Check the current map before diving; Taucher.Net also points to the regulation published in the district's official gazette in 2020. On the map, no-dive strips lie directly north and south of the Utting bathing area, so choose your entry and route carefully.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Dive in winter for the best conditions (Taucher.Net)
- Avoid the plant and reed zones near the shore where they are protected

## Safety Considerations

Visibility can be very low because of suspended sediment; stay close to your buddy and carry a light. The bottom drops more steeply after the shallow zone, so watch your depth. Plan thermal protection for cold winter water.
