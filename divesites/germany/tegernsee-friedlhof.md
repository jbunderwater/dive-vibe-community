## Tegernsee - Friedlhof

A Tegernsee shore entry with wrecks at 18 m, one of the local dive school's regular sites.

## Overview

Friedlhof (also written Friedelhof or Friedl), on the Tegernsee, is one of the entries described by the local dive school Seegeist, and one of the sites for which it posts regular visibility reports. From the entry between the huts, a 90° heading leads to wrecks at 18 m; heading north, you can follow a power cable for about 10 minutes.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Friedlhof, Tegernsee, Bavaria
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 18 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Seegeist reports for 2026 range from under 1 m (June, improving below 12 m) to 5-10 m (May); late summer about 5 m above a thermocline at about 10 m and 8-10 m below it
- **Current**: Not documented
- **Best Time**: Not documented; Seegeist's spring and late-summer reports show the best visibility

## Underwater Features

- Wrecks at 18 m on a 90° heading from the entry (Seegeist)
- A power cable running north from the entry that can be followed for about 10 minutes (Seegeist)

## Entry and Exit

Seegeist says to park in the Hirtenweg and enter between the huts, with consideration for bathers and residents. Diving in the Tegernsee is governed by a general order (Allgemeinverfügung) of the Landratsamt Miesbach. Seegeist states that private diving needs no separate permit or dive-site card; people who train divers need an agreement with the Bavarian Palace and Lakes Administration. The general order prohibits diving in the seven buoyed and signed protection zones, in reed and aquatic plant areas and their access routes, at fish spawning grounds and fishing gear, within 100 m of fish traps, in shipping lanes and within 100 m of docks, and in private harbours and boathouse areas. In the bathing season (15 March - 31 October) diving is prohibited in bathing areas, and hatched waterfowl zones are closed from September to March.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Check Seegeist's current visibility reports before driving out - visibility varies strongly through the season
- Expect a thermocline at about 9-10 m in summer, with water around 12°C below it (Seegeist reports)

## Safety Considerations

The Tegernsee lies at 725 m above sea level, so use altitude-adjusted dive computer settings and decompression planning. Water below the thermocline is cold even in summer.
