## Möhnesee

The only area of the Möhne reservoir open to divers, a former quarry bay at Delecke with a platform, submerged trees and a steep quarry wall.

## Overview

The Taucherbucht at Möhnesee lies on the south shore west of the Delecke bridge, in the "Alter Steinbruch" (old quarry) at Delecke/Mühlenberg. A local dive school states it is the only place on the Möhnesee where diving is permitted. The site is commercially operated by a dive school, and the zone is bounded by white buoys. The bay has large stones and a small cliff, and beyond it a quarry wall drops almost vertically. Maximum depth is given as 28 m at full water level; other sources cite 20-25 m, and one shop quotes 15 m. Because the Möhnesee is a reservoir, depths fall with the water level.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Delecke, south shore west of the Delecke bridge, Möhnesee (Soest district)
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 25 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: About 3-6 m (taucher.net); 200bar logged 4-8 m in July 2018; visibility drops later in the day as sediment is stirred up
- **Current**: Not documented
- **Best Time**: Not documented; one shop notes clearer water in the colder months

## Underwater Features

- Training platform at about 7.4 m (taucher.net) - a local club gives ~7 m
- Stone statue at about 9 m and a small sailboat at about 11 m (at full water level)
- Submerged trees, including a large tree at around 15 m
- Traffic signs, metal pipes and other artificial objects
- Quarry wall dropping steeply to about 25-28 m

## Marine Life

Sources report eels, pike, perch, bream, zander and crayfish for this bay; one taucher.net review describes pike of about 80 cm near the trees, while a 200bar report from 2018 noted few fish.

## Entry and Exit

A wide path and stairs lead into the water. Diving is only allowed inside the buoyed zone and requires a day pass or 10-entry card from the operator or its authorised dive schools; opening hours depend on the season, and the 10-entry card is also valid on weekdays. Parking is paid. Toilets and a small snack bar are on site. The DLRG uses the eastern part of the zone for training under its own rules, which permanently exclude night and ice dives for DLRG groups.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Check current opening hours and pass conditions with the operator before travelling - they change by season
- Use the platform and marked objects for navigation; a local club mentions compass courses in the bay
- Watch out for fishing hooks and lures near the buoy line (taucher.net)

## Safety Considerations

The quarry wall leads quickly into deeper, darker water, and one taucher.net review recommends the site for experienced divers because visibility is low at depth. Stay inside the buoys as the lake carries boat and sailing traffic. Cold-water thermal protection is needed.
