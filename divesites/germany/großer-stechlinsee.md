## Großer Stechlinsee

A clear, nutrient-poor lake where diving is confined to the bathing bay at Neuglobsow, reached from the dive base on shore, with pike-rich weed beds in the shallows.

## Overview

The Großer Stechlinsee near Rheinsberg is known for its nutrient-poor, clear water. Diving is permitted only in the bathing bay at Neuglobsow, because the surrounding area is a nature reserve (seen.de); entry is directly from the dive base in the forest at the bay. The lake's greatest depth of about 65 m is found roughly 1 km from the entry, so it is out of range; seen.de gives 4-6 m as the typical depth in the permitted area, while 200bar puts the accessible depth at around 30 m. Beds of pondweed cover the bottom near shore down to about 8 m.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Bathing bay at Neuglobsow, Stechlin, Brandenburg
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Usually good - up to 10 m (dertaucherblog.de); 200bar reported 3-5 m in August 2018; summer weekends can be poorer because of the many swimmers (seen.de)
- **Current**: None reported (lake)
- **Best Time**: Year-round (ice-free status noted by 200bar); weekdays outside peak summer for visibility

## Underwater Features

A carpet of aquatic vegetation broken by sandy patches (dertaucherblog.de), with pondweed beds down to about 8 m and water lilies near shore (seen.de, 200bar).

## Marine Life

Sources consistently report large pike, which often accompany divers, together with perch and roach (seen.de), crayfish and snails (200bar), and tench, carp, eels and lampreys (dertaucherblog.de).

## Entry and Exit

Shore entry in the bathing bay directly at the dive base. Diving is allowed only after registering at the base, which issues the diving permit for a fee (NABU Naturschutztauchen); daily diver numbers are limited, so booking ahead is recommended, especially at weekends. 200bar states that boat and night dives are not allowed and that the vegetation must not be penetrated. Base rules require a valid certification or an instructor, a dive computer on every dive, buddy diving and signing out and back in.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Book ahead - diver numbers are capped
- Stay above the weed beds rather than pushing through them
- Respect swimmers and anglers in the bathing bay, as the base rules require

## Safety Considerations

Diving is restricted to the permitted bay; do not swim out into the nature reserve. Cold water at depth calls for appropriate thermal protection even in summer.
