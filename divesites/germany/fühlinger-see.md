## Fühlinger See

A former gravel pit lake system in northern Cologne where diving is allowed with a city diving card, centred on the entry at lake 5.

## Overview

The Fühlinger See is a chain of former excavation lakes with a regatta course north of Cologne. According to the City of Cologne, diving is allowed in lakes 4, 5 and 6, except for the swimming area, and only with a valid diving card; older club and database sources mention lake 5 as the only diving section, where the established entry is. Depth is given as about 15 m by most sources, 15-17 m depending on water level by a local club, and up to 18 m by the city.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Fühlinger See, Köln-Chorweiler, lake 5 entry
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 15 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Seasonal - the city quotes up to 15 m and the Förderverein up to 10 m; 200bar gives 2-6 m. In summer visibility drops sharply below about 8 m, while in winter the deeper layers are often clear
- **Current**: Not documented
- **Best Time**: Winter for clearer deep water (per a local club)

## Underwater Features

Aquatic vegetation along the shore down to about 4 m. The Förderverein mentions a barrier-free diving entry at lake 5.

## Marine Life

Sources report pike, zander, perch, sunfish, eels, carp, catfish and crayfish.

## Entry and Exit

Entry is at the foot of the ramp next to the crane, depending on water level; 200bar mentions a ladder, paved paths and parking close to the entry. A paid diving card (day or annual) is mandatory and is sold online by the Förderverein Fühlinger See; a limited number of cards is issued. Divers need at least an Open Water/CMAS one-star/Bronze or equivalent certification plus insurance covering recovery costs (or VDST membership), and must carry these documents and show them to security staff on request. Use can be restricted during sporting or cultural events. Ice diving is prohibited.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Buy the diving card online before the dive and carry your documents
- Use a compass when leaving the shore zone, as a local club recommends
- Check for events on the regatta course, which can restrict use

## Safety Considerations

Summer visibility can be poor below the thermocline. Diving without a card counts as a violation and can lead to a ban. Cold-water thermal protection is needed outside midsummer.
