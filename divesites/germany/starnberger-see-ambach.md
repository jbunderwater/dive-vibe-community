## Starnberger See - Ambach

A sandy, gradually sloping shore entry at Ambach on the Starnberger See's south-east shore.

## Overview

The Ambach recreation area on the south-east shore of the Starnberger See is an approved diving entry. The Starnberger See travel guide describes a sandy bottom that slopes gradually to 30-40 m and on to deeper water (up to 50 m), with objects at about 15 m around boat moorings. It notes that visibility varies from good to poor and that the site is only partly suitable in summer because of crowds.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Ambach recreation area, south-east shore of the Starnberger See
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Variable, good to poor (travel guide)
- **Current**: Not documented
- **Best Time**: Outside the summer bathing season (travel guide: only partly suitable in summer due to crowds)

## Underwater Features

- Objects around boat moorings at about 15 m
- Sandy, gradually sloping bottom to 30-40 m and beyond

## Entry and Exit

Ambach is listed as an approved entry area by the general order and by starnbergersee-info.de. Sources also describe a winter diving ban (1 November to 15 March) for part of the lake - south of the Höllgraben (starnbergersee-info.de) or in the Bad Tölz-Wolfratshausen boundary zone (Starnberger See travel guide). Check whether it applies at Ambach before a winter dive. Diving in the Starnberger See is regulated by a general order (Allgemeinverfügung) of the Landratsamt Starnberg. Its key rules: solo dives are prohibited; diving with compressed air is limited to 40 m; diving is allowed until one hour after sunset; divers must carry cold-water-rated equipment with two separate, independently shut-off regulators (EN 250), thermal protection and a hood (a dive computer is recommended). A safety distance of at least 100 m must be kept from harbours, shipping lanes and landing stages, and private boat facilities, fish spawning areas, fishing gear and net enclosures are off limits.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Avoid busy summer days
- The gradual slope makes it easy to control the descent, but it also makes it easy to go past the 40 m limit - watch your computer

## Safety Considerations

The bottom continues beyond the 40 m air limit; set depth alarms. Use an SMB for boat traffic around the moorings.
