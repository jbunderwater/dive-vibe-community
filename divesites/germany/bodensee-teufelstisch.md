## Bodensee - Teufelstisch

A free-standing rock pinnacle off the Bodanrück cliff, rising almost to the surface from over 90 m; diving only with a special permit.

## Overview

The Teufelstisch ('Devil's Table') is a rock needle in front of the Bodanrück cliff between Wallhausen and the Marienschlucht, marked by navigation mark 22. It reaches almost to the surface (occasionally breaking it at low water) and drops nearly vertically to about 90 m. Tauchteam Bodensee describes a plateau of about 22 x 10 m and a saddle at about 35 m connecting the pinnacle to the neighbouring cliff. After several diving accidents, a diving ban applies at and around the Teufelstisch; a time-limited special permit can be obtained from the Landratsamt Konstanz (both 200bar.de and Tauchteam Bodensee).

## Site Information

- **Location**: Between Wallhausen and Marienschlucht, Überlinger See, Baden-Württemberg (navigation mark 22)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Pinnacle/seamount
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 3-10 m reported, with a turbid layer between 5 and 20 m (200bar.de, Sep 2017); strongly dependent on weather and rain
- **Current**: Not documented
- **Best Time**: Not documented

## Underwater Features

Vertical walls, ledges and crevices around the pinnacle, the plateau near the surface, and the saddle at about 35 m linking the needle to the cliff. Sections of rock are covered with mussels (Tauchteam Bodensee).

## Entry and Exit

Diving is prohibited within 300 m north-west and 500 m south-east of mark 22 without an exceptional permit from the Landratsamt Konstanz, valid for two years. According to Tauchteam Bodensee, applicants need at least 40 dives in the past 12 months, including at least 10 Bodensee dives deeper than 20 m; permit conditions include a 40 m maximum depth, buddy teams only (no solo diving) and notifying the water police before and after the dive. 200bar.de also mentions a buddy line among the conditions. Violations incur substantial fines and the site is monitored by the water police.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Apply for the permit well in advance and carry it on the dive
- Access is realistically by boat; the shore route is closed
- Orientation between pinnacle and cliff wall can be confusing; agree a plan and turn point

## Safety Considerations

Tauchteam Bodensee lists accidents in 1977, 1979 and the early 1990s linked to deep diving, inadequate equipment and decompression errors. The depth drops away immediately to about 90 m. 200bar.de notes that even in summer the Bodensee is only about 4-6 °C below roughly 10 m, so regulators can free-flow; cold-water regulators and cylinders with two separate outlets and two first stages are recommended, and first Bodensee dives should be made with a lake-experienced buddy. Under the Bodensee-Schifffahrts-Ordnung, shore divers must display a code flag 'A', and diving is prohibited within 100 m of harbour entrances and ferry landing areas; vessels always have right of way.
