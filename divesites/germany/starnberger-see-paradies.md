## Starnberger See - Paradies

A gently sloping bathing-area entry at Possenhofen on the Starnberger See's west shore, known for night dives.

## Overview

Paradies is a bathing area on the west shore of the Starnberger See near Possenhofen and is listed as an approved diving entry. The Starnberger See travel guide describes a gently sloping shore and a roughly ten-metre-long wreck at 8 m. Taucher.Net reviewers describe a large, clean jetty for entry, a gradual slope to about 20 m at a buoy near the DLRG building, a large pipe near the buoy and some vegetation in the shallows, and call it suitable for beginners and especially lively on night dives.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Paradies bathing area, Possenhofen, west shore of the Starnberger See
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 20 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: About 8 m reported (Taucher.Net); thermocline at 10-12 m with water around 9°C below
- **Current**: Not documented
- **Best Time**: Not documented; the travel guide says diving is only possible outside ferry operating hours

## Underwater Features

- Wreck about ten metres long at 8 m (Starnberger See travel guide)
- Large pipe near the buoy by the DLRG building (Taucher.Net)
- Vegetation in the shallows (Taucher.Net)

## Marine Life

Taucher.Net reviewers report small and larger perch, pike and eels.

## Entry and Exit

Entry is from the jetty; Taucher.Net reports parking opposite the entry, and the travel guide mentions good parking. The travel guide states the site is only accessible outside the ferry operating hours. Diving in the Starnberger See is regulated by a general order (Allgemeinverfügung) of the Landratsamt Starnberg. Its key rules: solo dives are prohibited; diving with compressed air is limited to 40 m; diving is allowed until one hour after sunset; divers must carry cold-water-rated equipment with two separate, independently shut-off regulators (EN 250), thermal protection and a hood (a dive computer is recommended). A safety distance of at least 100 m must be kept from harbours, shipping lanes and landing stages, and private boat facilities, fish spawning areas, fishing gear and net enclosures are off limits.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Night dives are recommended by Taucher.Net reviewers for the fish activity (the general order allows diving until one hour after sunset)
- Deploy a surface marker buoy before ascending

## Safety Considerations

Taucher.Net reviewers flag watercraft traffic as the main hazard; surface with an SMB. Expect a cold thermocline at 10-12 m even in summer.
