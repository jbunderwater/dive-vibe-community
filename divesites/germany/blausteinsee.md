## Blausteinsee

A young former lignite pit lake near Eschweiler with a single official diver entry, training platforms and depths to about 40 m.

## Overview

The Blausteinsee is a flooded former brown coal opencast mine near Eschweiler. The lake operator states the current maximum depth is 40 m, rising to 42 m once filling is complete; the FAQ gives about 41 m. There is only one official diver entry, below the on-site dive station container, where an anchored entry platform helps with descents and ascents. From the entry the bottom slopes gradually - about 6 m after 15 m of swimming and about 12 m after 30 m. The ecosystem is still developing: sources mention early small fish schools and first aquatic plants along the shore.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Blausteinsee, Eschweiler (Städteregion Aachen)
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Described as relatively good by a local club; 200bar logged 0.5-1.5 m on 31 July 2017
- **Current**: None (OSM tags current = 0)
- **Best Time**: Year-round diving is permitted

## Underwater Features

- Training platforms at 2 m, 6 m, 9 m and 21 m and a truck cabin at 16 m (mapped in OpenStreetMap)
- Standing trees left from before flooding
- A pump station about 500 m offshore, which a local club says cannot be reached safely on standard tank configurations

## Marine Life

Sources describe the fauna as still developing, with small fish schools having settled in the lake; no species are documented.

## Entry and Exit

The single official entry is below the dive station container, with an anchored entry platform. Non-resident divers need a day or annual card from Blausteinsee GmbH, the Eschweiler town hall or the on-site sales booth, which also covers parking; no advance registration is needed. The minimum qualification for a buddy pair is four CMAS stars combined (2+2 or 3+1) or equivalent, and each group must carry a surface marker buoy. Divers must stay within the buoyed zone and surface only there; the zone is closed to surface water sports. VDST environmental rules apply. A fill station is operated on site.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Check your team meets the combined four-star rule before arriving
- Use the platforms for buoyancy and ascent training
- Carry the mandatory buoy for signalling an emergency ascent outside the zone

## Safety Considerations

Deep water is reachable within the zone, so plan depth and gas carefully. Surfacing outside the marked zone puts divers in the path of boats and windsurfers; emergency ascents outside must be signalled with a buoy. In an accident, call 112 and report a diving accident at the Blausteinsee.
