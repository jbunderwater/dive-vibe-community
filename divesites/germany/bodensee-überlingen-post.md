## Bodensee - Überlingen Post

A fish-rich Überlingen shore dive with a sand slope to about 20 m and a steep wall with a pronounced overhang from 20 to 40 m.

## Overview

The Überlingen - Post entry, named after the nearby Parkhaus Post car park, is a steep-wall site on the Überlinger See. A sand slope (Sandhalde) descends to about 18-20 m depending on water level, followed by a steep wall from about 20 to 40 m with a distinctive overhang starting around 35 m, and further drop-off beyond 40 m. 200bar.de calls it a fairly fish-rich wall site over 40 m deep that is not suitable for beginners or divers new to the Bodensee; Tauchteam Bodensee says beginners should stay on the sand slope to 18-20 m.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Überlingen town centre (near Parkhaus Post), Überlinger See, Baden-Württemberg
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Poor down to about 20 m, often best at depth (Tauchteam Bodensee); 6-10 m reported (200bar.de, Mar 2018)
- **Current**: Not documented
- **Best Time**: Not documented

## Underwater Features

Steep wall from 20 to 40 m with a marked overhang from about 35 m and partial ceiling formations in the deeper section.

## Marine Life

200bar.de reports many perch, some char and occasional eels.

## Entry and Exit

Entry is via steps from the shore, roughly 100-200 m from the Parkhaus Post car park; Tauchteam Bodensee recommends the lower deck of the garage for kitting up. Divers must head left from the entry; diving to the right is only allowed on 6 January (Dreikönigstauchen), according to Tauchteam Bodensee.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Keep to the left of the entry as required
- Stay on the sand slope unless experienced in deep wall diving
- Bring a light: the wall section is dark

## Safety Considerations

Tauchteam Bodensee lists depth, darkness, narcosis and the overhang as the specific risks of the wall section. 200bar.de notes that even in summer the Bodensee is only about 4-6 °C below roughly 10 m, so regulators can free-flow; cold-water regulators and cylinders with two separate outlets and two first stages are recommended, and first Bodensee dives should be made with a lake-experienced buddy. Under the Bodensee-Schifffahrts-Ordnung, shore divers must display a code flag 'A', and diving is prohibited within 100 m of harbour entrances and ferry landing areas; vessels always have right of way.
