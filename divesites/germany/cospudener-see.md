## Cospudener See

A shore dive at the Zöbigker harbour of Cospudener See, a flooded lignite pit south of Leipzig, with a buoyed parcours down to about 22 m.

## Overview

Cospudener See lies on the southern edge of Leipzig in Markkleeberg. The Cospuden opencast lignite mine opened in 1981 and production ceased in 1992; the pit was then converted into a recreational lake, which reached its final water level in spring 2000 (Wikipedia). Diving is based at the harbour in the Zöbigker Winkel on the south-east shore, where a dive school operates. Shore dives are confined to a rope-marked parcours with buoys, whose deepest point on the lake side is about 22 m (Taucher.Net). The lake itself is much deeper: 54 m according to Wikipedia, 55-60 m according to dive sites.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Zöbigker harbour, south-east shore of Cospudener See, Markkleeberg, Saxony
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 22 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Reported between 2-3 m in the colder months and 3-10 m otherwise, depending on source and season
- **Current**: None
- **Best Time**: Not documented

## Underwater Features

- Buoyed training parcours with placed objects, small wrecks and a practice platform (Taucher.Net)
- A small and a large submerged forest (Taucher.Net)
- A sailboat wreck at 19 m (200bar, Atlantis Berlin)
- Exposed coal seams from the mining past (TAZA-Tauchclub)
- By boat only: a steep wall to about 54 m and further sites named "Birke" and "Wäldchen" (200bar, Atlantis Berlin, Taucher.Net)

## Marine Life

Taucher.Net mentions schools of small fish. Atlantis Berlin notes that flora and fauna are less developed than at comparable older lakes.

## Entry and Exit

Shore entry from the designated area near the dive base in the harbour. Shore dives must stay within the rope-marked parcours. 200bar states that no registration with the base is required, while Atlantis Berlin lists requirements of the base (Open Water certification with at least six logged dives and a current dive medical). Check current conditions and fees with the base before diving; published fee figures differ between sources.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Stay inside the buoyed parcours on shore dives
- Book a boat dive for the steep wall or the forest sites

## Safety Considerations

The lake is heavily used for water sports in summer, so surface carefully (Taucher.Net). The steep wall reached by boat goes to about 54 m, beyond recreational limits.
