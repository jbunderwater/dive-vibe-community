## Bodensee - Überlingen Tennisplätze

A comparatively gentle Überlingen shore entry with a vegetated slope and an old slipway, and a steep wall from about 30 m for experienced divers.

## Overview

Named after the neighbouring tennis courts, this Überlingen entry is described by 200bar.de as well suited to divers new to the Bodensee because the bottom slopes relatively gently here. To the left of a disused slipway (ending at about 4-5 m) a sand slope descends moderately steeply to great depth but is forgiving of buoyancy errors. Tauchteam Bodensee describes a steep wall beginning about 50 m from the dock at around 30 m and continuing well beyond 100 m, and a sunken boat at 40 m. 200bar.de gives 40 m as the site depth.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Überlingen (Strandweg), Überlinger See, Baden-Württemberg
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 8-15 m reported (200bar.de, Jan 2018); weather-dependent
- **Current**: Not documented
- **Best Time**: Not documented

## Underwater Features

An old slipway leads from a shed into the water and ends at about 4 m, a good orientation point. The deeper wall starts at around 30 m to the right of the slipway (200bar.de) and a sunken boat lies at about 40 m (Tauchteam Bodensee).

## Marine Life

200bar.de notes more fish in the evening and fewer fish than at the Post entry, with occasional crayfish. Tauchteam Bodensee reports fish concentrations during safety stops at 3-6 m.

## Entry and Exit

Parking is at a small paid car park on Strandweg next to the tennis courts, a few metres from the water (fills quickly on summer weekends); further car parks lie beyond the underpass. Tauchteam Bodensee advises avoiding the area under the private boat dock, as boats usually approach from the left, and notes an emergency call point at the left entry.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Use the slipway rails as a reference for the start and end of the dive
- Good site for a first Bodensee dive if you stay on the slope
- Display the Alpha flag and watch for boat traffic at the dock

## Safety Considerations

The slope continues to great depth, so monitor depth closely. 200bar.de notes that even in summer the Bodensee is only about 4-6 °C below roughly 10 m, so regulators can free-flow; cold-water regulators and cylinders with two separate outlets and two first stages are recommended, and first Bodensee dives should be made with a lake-experienced buddy. Under the Bodensee-Schifffahrts-Ordnung, shore divers must display a code flag 'A', and diving is prohibited within 100 m of harbour entrances and ferry landing areas; vessels always have right of way.
