## Arendsee - Wrack Patrouillenboot

A former patrol boat sunk in 2006 as a tourist dive attraction, lying at about 16 m off the south-east shore of the Arendsee.

## Overview

In 2006 the local tourism association sank a former patrol boat in the Arendsee as a diving attraction, with LEADER funding, according to the Volksstimme. A 2011 taucher.net report describes it as a former NVA patrol boat lying at 16 m and gives its position. Access has been the main problem: the original entry at the Nagelweg became unusable because of a research field of the Leibniz Institute of Freshwater Ecology, and the county regulation does not list an entry near the wreck.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Off the south-east shore near the Nagelweg / Seepromenade, Arendsee (Altmark), Saxony-Anhalt
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 16 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Not documented for the wreck; lake visibility is often around 2 m
- **Current**: None documented
- **Best Time**: Not documented

## Underwater Features

The patrol boat itself; no further details of its size or condition have been published in the sources reviewed.

## Entry and Exit

According to the Volksstimme (December 2014), the nearest official entries (Kaskade and Tauchclub) are too far from the wreck to reach with normal recreational equipment, and boat use on the lake is tightly regulated. The frequently used entry at the jetty of the Hotel "Deutsches Haus" was only possible because the hotel had applied for an exemption from the county, which had been granted orally at the time. The ArendseeVO allows entries outside the designated points only with a county permit. Clarify the current access arrangement with the county or local operators before diving.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Confirm legal access in advance; unauthorised entries are fined
- Use the published coordinates with a surface float for navigation

## Safety Considerations

Do not penetrate the wreck without proper wreck training and equipment. Visibility in the Arendsee is often poor and can change from day to day; carry a light.
