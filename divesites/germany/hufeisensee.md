## Hufeisensee

A former lignite and gravel pit on the eastern edge of Halle with dense plant growth in the shallows, rich fish life and a deep coal hole to 28 m.

## Overview

The "Hufi", named after its horseshoe shape, is a former lignite pit on the eastern edge of Halle (Saale), mined until about 1940 and flooded since the 1950s. Divers describe the shallow zone as the highlight: down to about 9 m the bottom is covered with stoneworts, pondweed, water-milfoil and water-crowfoot up to 1.5 m tall. The deepest point, in the old coal hole, is about 28 m (Wikipedia gives 26 m). The lake also hosts a water-ski cable and boat traffic. The water is affected by chlorinated hydrocarbons from nearby industrial areas; see Safety Considerations.

## Site Information

- **Location**: East shore, Schkeuditzer Straße, Halle (Saale)-Kanena, Saxony-Anhalt
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 28 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: A 200bar.de report from July 2018 gave 3-6 m; a light is recommended from 15 m
- **Current**: None documented
- **Best Time**: Not documented

## Underwater Features

Mining remains such as pipes and pump houses, plus platforms, boats and a telephone booth sunk by the local diving club. Several steel cables and buoy lines run through the deeper area.

## Marine Life

Large pike hunting in the water-crowfoot, big tench, carp, schools of perch and thick eels are reported by 200bar.de, which also mentions a 2 m wels sighting. In 2005 divers discovered the swarming mysid shrimp Hemimysis anomala here, the first record in an inland lake in central Germany according to Wikipedia.

## Dive Profile

Most dives stay in the 6-7 m zone among the plants and fish. A deeper excursion into the old coal hole reaches 28 m, but sources consider the shallows more rewarding. One-way trips by boat are also possible.

## Entry and Exit

According to 200bar.de no registration is required to dive. The TC Orca Halle e.V. club has its grounds directly on the lake at Schkeuditzer Straße 70, with a compressor, changing room, showers and bunk accommodation; visitors can fill tanks there, mainly on weekends, and groups should announce themselves. Parking is available directly at the lake.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Set a surface marker buoy because of the water-ski station and boat traffic
- Take a light for dives below 15 m
- Stay in the plant-rich shallows for the best fish encounters

## Safety Considerations

Steel cables and buoy lines in the deeper area are an entanglement hazard in poor visibility. The lake is contaminated with carcinogenic chlorinated hydrocarbons (including vinyl chloride) from groundwater: in January 2025 the city said the bathing ban would stay, while Wikipedia reports that the ban was lifted in June 2025 after new tests, with the city still advising against bathing because pollution peaks can occur. Consider this exposure risk and minimise swallowing water.
