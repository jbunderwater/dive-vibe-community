## Bodensee - Ludwigshafen Lädine

A shore dive at Ludwigshafen to the wreck of a historic Lake Constance cargo sailing vessel (Lädine), part of the 'Museum unter Wasser'.

## Overview

At Ludwigshafen, at the western tip of the Überlinger See, lies the wreck of a Lädine, a type of cargo sailing vessel used on Lake Constance from the 14th to the 20th century (Tauchteam Bodensee). 200bar.de describes it as an archaeologically significant wreck of a former clay-carrying ship ('Lehmschiff'), about 11 m long (single source). It is the exhibit of the 'Museum unter Wasser', a project of the Tauchsportgruppe Konstanz e.V. with the underwater archaeology section of the Gesellschaft für Archäologie in Württemberg und Hohenzollern and the Landesdenkmalamt Baden-Württemberg, with an information panel above and under water on heritage-friendly diving (Wikipedia, 200bar.de). Sources give the wreck depth as 19-20 m (Tauchteam Bodensee) or about 22 m (Wikipedia, 200bar.de).

## Site Information

- **Location**: Ludwigshafen (near the railway station), Bodman-Ludwigshafen, Baden-Württemberg
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 22 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 3-7 m reported (200bar.de, Jun 2015); often murky in the first metres and better below about 6 m; reduced by Stockacher Aach sediment after heavy rain
- **Current**: Not documented
- **Best Time**: Not documented

## Underwater Features

The Lädine wreck with surrounding wooden piles and structural elements, and the underwater information panel of the Museum unter Wasser. Dense plant growth in the shallows in summer (Tauchteam Bodensee).

## Marine Life

Tauchteam Bodensee reports crustaceans and small fish among the structures.

## Entry and Exit

Access is from Bahnhofstraße over the railway crossing towards the lake and across a meadow; the entry is very flat with a gradual slope and can be muddy at low water. Parking is paid along Bahnhofstraße. The barrier at the railway crossing often causes waits. Heritage rules apply: do not touch or remove anything, do not enter the hull, keep your distance with good buoyancy, and report archaeological finds to the heritage authority.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Perfect buoyancy is essential; the wooden wreck is very sensitive
- Hover above the information panel to read it rather than kneeling on the bottom
- Boats should anchor well away from the wreck

## Safety Considerations

Visibility can be very poor after rain. 200bar.de notes that even in summer the Bodensee is only about 4-6 °C below roughly 10 m, so regulators can free-flow; cold-water regulators and cylinders with two separate outlets and two first stages are recommended, and first Bodensee dives should be made with a lake-experienced buddy. Under the Bodensee-Schifffahrts-Ordnung, shore divers must display a code flag 'A', and diving is prohibited within 100 m of harbour entrances and ferry landing areas; vessels always have right of way.
