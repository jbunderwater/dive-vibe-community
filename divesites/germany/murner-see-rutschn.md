## Murner See - Rutsch'n

The main diving entry of the Murner See, a former brown-coal pit near Wackersdorf, with an underwater forest at shallow depth.

## Overview

The Murner See near Wackersdorf in the Upper Palatinate is a former brown-coal mining pit, now a lake of about 90-94 hectares. Its water is acidic, which the VG Wackersdorf credits for its clear water. The "Rutsch'n" is the main diving entry on the Wackersdorf side, with its own access-controlled car park. PADI lists a maximum depth of about 37 m here and a small underwater forest at 5-12 m, and describes the site as easy to navigate. The lake as a whole reaches 48 m (Taucher.Net) to 50 m (Wiesenberger Diving).

## Site Information

- **Location**: Rutsch'n entry, Wackersdorf side of the Murner See, Landkreis Schwandorf, Bavaria
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 37 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: Around 10 m, varying by season (Taucher.Net)
- **Current**: None expected (Wiesenberger Diving describes it as a groundwater lake with no tributaries)
- **Best Time**: Not documented; Taucher.Net reports about 20°C at the surface and 8°C at depth in summer

## Underwater Features

- Small underwater forest at 5-12 m (PADI)
- An old slide from the mining era and an underwater forest, reported by Neptuns Taucherfreunde from a club dive in the lake (entry not specified)

## Marine Life

Taucher.Net reports pike (over 60 cm) and schools of perch. Wiesenberger Diving lists pike, perch and wels catfish, and notes that plant and fish populations are developing only slowly after the lake was restored.

## Entry and Exit

The car park at the Rutsch'n requires a free, permanently valid access card (Zufahrts-Berechtigungskarte) issued by the Wackersdorf town hall during office hours. On top of that, divers need a daily parking ticket from the machine on site (VG Wackersdorf; Taucher.Net cites 4 EUR/day). Parking spaces are limited. Diving is regulated by the diving ordinance of the Landkreis Schwandorf (Tauchverordnung), which defines permitted and prohibited zones; the VG Wackersdorf names two designated diving areas, the "Rutschn" (Wackersdorf side) and the observation-tower area (Schwarzenfeld side, north shore).

## Tips and Recommendations

- Get the access card from the town hall before your first visit - it is valid permanently
- The underwater forest is the highlight at shallow depth

## Safety Considerations

Taucher.Net rates the lake for advanced divers. Water below the thermocline is cold (about 8°C), and the lake is deeper than the 37 m documented for this entry. The nearest decompression chamber listed by Taucher.Net is in Regensburg.
