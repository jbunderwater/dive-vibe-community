## Sundhäuser See - Einstieg 1 Ponton

The main pontoon entry at Sundhäuser See in Nordhausen, the starting point for the underwater city "Nordhusia", a 30 m platform and several sunken boats.

## Overview

Sundhäuser See lies on the southern edge of Nordhausen in northern Thuringia. According to the local dive base, the lake was created by bucket-chain excavation, which left a varied bottom with ridges and hollows. Plants grow down to about 20 m. Sources give the maximum depth as 30-35 m (Taucher.Net, unterwasserwelt.de), 31 m (Action Sport Nordhausen), 32 m (Ichthyo, for the Oasis area) and 38 m (Seen.de). Visibility of 10-15 m is widely reported. Two independent dive bases manage access: Action Sport Nordhausen ("Sundhäuser See Nord") with the numbered entries in the north-west corner, and the Oasis base, whose five entries are not covered by this entry. Entry 1 is the main floating-pontoon entry at the Action Sport Nordhausen base, with fixed stairs. Buoyed attractions reached from here include marked platforms at 5, 8 and 30 m and the underwater city "Nordhusia".

## Site Information

- **Location**: North-west shore of Sundhäuser See, Nordhausen, Thuringia
- **Entry Type**: Shore entry
- **Site Type**: Beach dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters
- **Typical Visibility**: 10-15 meters
- **Current**: None
- **Best Time**: Not documented

## Underwater Features

- Marked platforms at 5, 8 and 30 m (Action Sport Nordhausen); the 30 m point has a green buoy
- The underwater city "Nordhusia" at about 12 m, at a bearing of 255° from the pontoon. Ichthyo describes it as including a city wall, a well, a church of about 10 x 10 m with a cemetery, and houses
- The "Charlotte II", a cabin cruiser of about 9 m at 23 m, sunk in September 2007, at a bearing of 195° from the pontoon (Action Sport Nordhausen)
- The motorboat "Galileo" at about 9 m, between entries 1 and 2 (Action Sport Nordhausen)
- Art installations placed since 2019 (Taucher.Net)

## Marine Life

Action Sport Nordhausen lists pike, perch (in schools, up to 40 cm), carp including grass carp, eel, rainbow trout, roach, zander, tench and crustaceans for the lake. Taucher.Net reviewers (2020-2025) report frequent crustaceans, occasional perch, and rare mirror carp and pike.

## Entry and Exit

Entry from the floating pontoon via fixed stairs. Divers must register each day with the dive base that manages the entry and pay a day fee; each base charges separately (Seen.de). Seen.de gives €5 and unterwasserwelt.de €8, so check the current fee. At Action Sport Nordhausen, the gate code is given at check-in.

## Tips and Recommendations

- Use the bearings given by the base (255° for Nordhusia, 195° for Charlotte II)
- Avoid the shallow bay so you don't stir up sediment (Action Sport Nordhausen)

## Safety Considerations

The water is cold at depth: Taucher.Net reports 17-19°C at the surface and 9-10°C at 20 m in June. The varied bottom makes navigation difficult, so carry a compass. Entering any wreck or underwater structure requires proper training and equipment; the base does not allow beginners to enter the structures. The 30 m platform is at the limit of recreational diving; plan gas and no-decompression limits accordingly.
