## Punta delle Cannelle

Headland on the north side of the Gulf of Porto Azzurro with a wall and an outer rock monolith covered in large red gorgonians.

## Overview

Elba CED describes the main dive as an ovoid rock mound ("panettone") parallel to the coast about 20 m from it, separated by a gravel channel about 12 m deep where large schools of sea bream and salema are frequent. The outer side descends in terraces to the maximum depth, where about 10 m away a second, outer rock is entirely covered in large red gorgonians. The suggested route starts at maximum depth, moves from the point outward toward Porto Azzurro and ends nearshore along the channel. Elba CED gives depths of 10-43 m with anchoring at 10-15 m near the point, and considers it suitable for all levels according to the depth reached. Riodiving places the dive in front of Capo d'Arco, on a buoy used by local centres, with a sandy channel facing a wall that reaches 38 m; it says the dive often goes beyond 40 m and up to 45 m, and that the gorgonian monolith is often swept by a sustained current from inside the Gulf of Porto Azzurro.

Infoelba describes a wall covered in white and yellow sea fans with a diagonal crack at 28-30 m and a granite slab forming a short tunnel between 37 and 40 m, and reports an object described as a Second World War mine, complete with its cart, on the sand at about 45 m. Any such object should be treated as potentially dangerous and not approached or touched. Elba CED also describes a second, harder dive on the same headland, "Cannelle esterne", 150 m north of the point: a vertical wall from 22 m to 48 m with a deep fissure among large Paramuricea clavata, suitable for advanced divers and demanding for depth and orientation. Bolle d'Azoto lists Punta delle Cannelle at 10-45 m with a minimum Open Water certification and describes a more demanding, decompression-level itinerary on the outer wall.

Reported marine life includes red gorgonians (Elba CED, Riodiving), lobsters and anthias (Riodiving), groupers, moray eels and octopus on the wall (Riodiving), conger eels and forkbeards in the crevices of the outer wall (Elba CED), groupers, sea breams and corvina (Infoelba), fan mussels (Pinna nobilis) on a wall at about 20 m, and dentex and barracuda in current (Bolle d'Azoto).

## Site Information

- **Location**: Elba, Europe (headland in front of Capo d'Arco, north side of the Gulf of Porto Azzurro)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wall dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 45 meters
