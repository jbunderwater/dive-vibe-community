## Colle d'Orano

Shallow, easy dive below the hamlet of Colle d'Orano on Elba's west coast, reached from the Le Buche cove or by boat.

## Overview

A tourism website run by a hotel at Fetovaia publishes a dive description for Colle d'Orano. It places the site about 6 miles from Marciana toward the west, halfway between Chiessi and Sant'Andrea. Access is from the small bay of Le Buche or by boat; the boat anchorage is 150 meters from the coast, near a small gulf with a large solitary pine tree above it, and the village of Colle d'Orano can be seen up the valley. Depth is given as 2 to 16 meters, difficulty as easy, current as low and visibility as good. Species listed as easy to see are European conger, Mediterranean moray, octopus and pen shell (Pinna nobilis), along with Posidonia.

Le Buche is a small cove at the end of a wild valley, below a high cliff that drops steeply into the sea, between Patresi and Chiessi. Regional tourism pages describe its water as clear with a seabed suited to snorkeling and diving. It is reached on foot by two trails from Colle d'Orano; the first, of medium difficulty, takes about 20 minutes.

No dive-centre listing for this site was found (the Elba CED, Diving in Elba and Il Careno site lists do not include it), and no source states the site type. "Reef" is inferred from the Posidonia and rocky-cove setting and should be treated as unconfirmed.
