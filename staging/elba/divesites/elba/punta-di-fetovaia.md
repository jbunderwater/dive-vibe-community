## Punta di Fetovaia

Punta di Fetovaia is a rocky headland dive at the end of Fetovaia bay on Elba's south-west coast, with red gorgonians on the rocks from about 15 m down past 40 m.

## Overview

Elba CED describes a dive around the point at the extremity of the bay. Divers move away from the coast over a Posidonia bottom, and at about 15 m meet the first rocks, completely covered with red gorgonians, which descend beyond 40 m; there is a lot of fish in burrows, conger and moray eels above all, and numerous dentex hunting in the blue. Diving in Elba describes rocky terraces from the surface to considerable depth, frequent pelagic fish, especially large shoals of dentex and barracuda, and, at depth, gorgonians and lairs of large groupers, moray eels and conger eels. Fetovaia.it gives a depth of 12-40 m and lists scorpionfish, grouper and pinna nobilis among the rocks, and a cave at about 40 m with sponges, lobsters and parazoanthus.

Difficulty is described differently. Fetovaia.it rates it high, with a strong stream flowing towards open sea and boat access; Dive Center Blu says it suits everyone, thanks to many burrows in the first 20 m and the gorgonian colours beyond 30 m; Diving in Elba calls it an excellent multilevel dive.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Elba, Europe (around Punta di Fetovaia, Campo nell'Elba)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters
