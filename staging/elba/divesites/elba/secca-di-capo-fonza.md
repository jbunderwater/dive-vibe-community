## Secca di Capo Fonza

Secca di Capo Fonza is a shoal at Capo di Fonza on Elba's south coast, topped at about 3-6 m, with a vertical east wall reported to 40-50 m and red coral in its cavities.

## Overview

Elba CED and Marina di Campo Diving (whose texts are identical) put the top of the secca at about 3-6 m, with a characteristic vertical wall on the east side dropping to about 40 m. Red coral twigs about 5-7 cm tall grow in almost every cavity, large yellow sponges cover the base of the wall, and encounters with "mustelle" and large crustaceans are described as almost certain. On the south side at about 25 m a plateau is rich in Eunicella gorgonians, with groupers and octopus. Nudibranchs of several species are abundant, and the intense colours are credited to the currents the shoal is exposed to. Other sources give the top at 4 m and the deepest point at 50 m (Dive Center Blu), or a depth range of 6-45 m (Aquanautic Elba's map, which also lists steep wall, red coral and fish shoals).

Suitability is described differently. Elba CED and Marina di Campo Diving call the dive open to everyone because multilevel profiles can be planned, and Dive Center Blu says it suits all levels. Diving in Elba says the shoal sometimes has sustained current and is a dive for advanced divers, although the top would also allow less experienced divers, and names barracuda and amberjack among the pelagic fish. Aquanautic Elba rates navigation as easy to demanding, with possible current. Diving in Elba also calls the shoal "Corallina", a name that overlaps with the separate Coralline dives listed by Elba CED and Dive Center Blu.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Elba, Europe (at Capo di Fonza, between the Gulfs of Marina di Campo and Lacona)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Pinnacle/seamount
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 45 meters
