## Relitto Aereo (Islander)

Wreck of a small twin-engine tourist aircraft, a Britten-Norman BN-2A Islander, lying at 12-16 m on sand and posidonia near Punta Nera, Capoliveri.

## Overview

Three independent sources identify the aircraft as a BN-2A Islander, a twin-engine, eight-seat type (Infoelba, Pesca Sub e Apnea; Aquanautic calls it a Britten-Norman Islander). Elba CED and Infoelba describe it as well preserved, leaning slightly on its right wing on a seabed of posidonia and sand, with the left engine detached and resting on the sand. Infoelba adds that the fuselage is covered in sponges and bryozoans, that moray eels and conger eels have burrows inside, and that the wreck is surrounded by dense shoals of damselfish and bream; Bolle d'Azoto, by contrast, says the wildlife interest is minimal and notes a few scattered boulders nearby.

Sources give depths from 12 m (Elba CED, Infoelba) through 13 m (Pesca Sub e Apnea) to 14-16 m (Bolle d'Azoto, Aquanautic). The dive is described as very easy and short (Elba CED, Infoelba) and suitable for beginners (Aquanautic); Bolle d'Azoto lists a minimum Open Water certification. Elba CED describes anchoring near the point, going to the 12 m contour and heading east to the wreck. Infoelba reminds divers that it is still a wreck dive and should be approached with care.

Infoelba and Pesca Sub e Apnea both say fishing nets later moved the aircraft to its present position; Pesca Sub e Apnea puts the distance at about three miles. That article alone also reports a Transavio aircraft that ditched on 27 June 1980 after engine failure, with the pilot and two passengers swimming ashore; this account could not be confirmed in a second independent source. The location is given variously as Punta Nera, about three miles south-east of Porto Azzurro between the islet of Liscoli and Capo Calvo (Elba CED, Infoelba), or 200 m south-east of Punta Nera in the Straccoligno area of Capoliveri (Pesca Sub e Apnea). Note that other operators use the name "Relitto dell'Aereo" for a different wreck, the Junkers 52 off Portoferraio.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Elba, Europe (off Punta Nera / Straccoligno, Capoliveri, near Porto Azzurro)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Wreck dive
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 16 meters
