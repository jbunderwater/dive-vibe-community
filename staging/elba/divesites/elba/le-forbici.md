## Le Forbici

A rocky reef and slope dive off the Capoliveri coast of south-east Elba, between Punta Galera and Punta Bianca and below the old Ginevro mine.

## Overview

Infoelba describes large rocks from about 15 m depth covered with sea spirographs, sea urchins, anemones and a large field of red gorgonians. The varied rocky bottom continues to about 40 m before it smooths out toward open water. Daily Nautica describes two scenarios: a shoal rich in life that descends to 25 m, then a slope covered with red gorgonians, sea daisies (margherite di mare) and sponges; the article says divers can go down to 42 m but that the most interesting part is in the first 20 m or so. Sources therefore give a maximum of about 40-42 m.

Infoelba reports conger eels, moray eels, groupers and octopus among the large rocks, and larger pelagic fish passing by. It also warns that currents can be very strong. A Capoliveri mooring buoy named "Le Forbici" is listed in a 2011 news report, placed over sand at 20 m depth about 35 m from the coast; this points to boat diving, but the sources reviewed do not state the entry type explicitly. The site is not among the 41 sites listed by the Elba CED consortium and is not described on the operator pages reviewed (Stefano Sub, Biodivers, Omnisub), so there is less detail here than for neighbouring sites.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Elba, Europe (Capoliveri coast, between Punta Galera and Punta Bianca, south-east Elba)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters
