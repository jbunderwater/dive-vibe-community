## Capo Stella Nord

Northern point of the Capo Stella promontory, listed by one Morcone-based operator as a dive with a tunnel, 0-30 m.

## Overview

Capo Stella is a narrow peninsula, about 2.5 km long according to the park's trail description, that separates the gulfs of Lacona and Stella. Aquanautic Elba's dive-site map lists "Capo Stella Nord" with a tunnel as the special feature, a depth range of 0-30 m, easy navigation and rare currents. No other site-specific description, entry method or marine life for this point was found.

Other sources describe dives around Capo Stella and its Corbella islet (VirtualElba: a boulder rock-fall with dens and narrow passages, a sponge-covered slope and red gorgonians beyond 35 m; Elba CED: a demanding north wall and an easier south side with red coral); it is not shown that these are the same as the north point, so they are not attributed to it. Whether the tunnel is an overhead passage, and what training it needs, is not documented in the sources read.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Elba, Europe (Capo Stella, between Golfo della Lacona and Golfo Stella, Capoliveri)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate
- **Maximum Depth**: 30 meters
