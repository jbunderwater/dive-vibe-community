## Scoglietto di Portoferraio

Lighthouse islet just north of Portoferraio, inside a fishing-free biological protection zone since 1971, with boulder slopes, walls and groupers used to divers.

## Overview

Lo Scoglietto di Portoferraio is a small islet with a lighthouse in front of Ghiaie beach, about 1.5 km north-east of Capo Bianco. The surrounding sea has been a biological protection zone since 1971, where all fishing, including spearfishing, is prohibited. I Love Elba describes a mostly rocky bottom from about 5 m to 40 m with a light, continuous upward current, and PADI lists a maximum depth of 38 m. The islet supports several separate dives, listed here by Diving in Elba with its depth ranges:

- Franata delle Cernie (0-25 m, max about 24 m), a boulder slope on the east side. Elba CED describes a slope from shallow water to sand at 30 m below the lighthouse, and Infoelba puts the slope at 7-8 m to 25 m, with large groupers used to divers.
- Franata Nord (0-17 m, easy, no current), a boulder slope on the north side.
- Grottoni (15-40 m, medium). At about 30 m there are three consecutive caverns that divers can enter for a few meters; entering them requires appropriate training and equipment.
- Parete NE (20-45 m, medium), starting where the Franata ends, past an Admiralty-type anchor with a plaque (I Love Elba puts it at 27 m); the edge drops from 22 m to 35-40 m.
- Statua del Cristo (20-47 m), reaching a Christ statue at about 39 m (I Love Elba: 39 m; Bolle d'Azoto: about 40 m).
- Panettone delle Gorgonie (40-52 m), reserved for technical divers.

Named for the site are groupers, moray and conger eels, scorpionfish, "mustelle" (as named by the sources), corvina, sea bream, barracuda, large dentex, amberjack, bonito and gilthead bream. The deeper outer promontory has red gorgonians (Paramuricea clavata), lobsters and eagle rays, and the Panettone has large spiny lobsters, catsharks and a large spider crab. PADI rates the site as suitable for all levels; Bolle d'Azoto asks for a minimum of Open Water for La Franata.

Protection rules: Elba's waters are outside the Arcipelago Toscano National Park perimeter, and the biological protection zone (Ministerial Decree of 10 August 1971, between Punta Falcone and Capo Bianco) is managed by the Comune di Portoferraio. The Park's guided-diving and authorization rules on islepark.it apply to Pianosa's buoy field, not to this site. On land, the islet and the Enfola promontory are Natura 2000 sites. No legal certification requirement for diving here and no Portoferraio diving ordinance were found; check with local operators and the Comune before diving.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Elba, Europe (islet off Ghiaie beach, north of Portoferraio)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
