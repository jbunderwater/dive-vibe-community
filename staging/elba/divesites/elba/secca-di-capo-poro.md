## Secca di Capo Poro

Secca di Capo Poro is a deep offshore ridge about 500 m to half a mile off Capo Poro on Elba's south coast, with its top at roughly 30-39 m and its base beyond 50 m.

## Overview

Elba CED and Marina di Campo Diving describe a descent in blue water, surrounded by shoals of castagnole (damselfish) and occhiate, to the top of the secca at about 34 m, where large moray eels and many colourful encrusting sponges are seen. Below, large dense fans of red gorgonians cover the walls, and the deep crevices hold large lobsters and scorpionfish. A second Marina di Campo Diving text puts the top at 30 m with the bottom at the sand border beyond 50 m, with sheer walls covered by paramuricee fans carrying sea lilies and red coral in the cracks, and mentions a nearby rock on the seaward ridge with more coral, lobsters, big moray and conger eels, melon and crown urchins, sponges and dense clouds of anthias. Dive Center Blu gives the top at 39 m and the base beyond 50 m, with a sheer wall on the east side and a gentler slope on the west. Fetovaia.it lists a depth of 36-56 m.

All four sources recommend the dive for experienced divers only. Elba CED cites the depth, the descent in the blue and probable current as difficulties, while Fetovaia.it rates the stream as low. The secca lies about half a mile offshore (Elba CED); Fetovaia.it's anchoring instructions start 500 m from the cape and advise an echosounder.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Elba, Europe (off Capo Poro, south of Marina di Campo)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Pinnacle/seamount
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 50 meters
