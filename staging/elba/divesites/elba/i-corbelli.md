## I Corbelli

Small group of rocks off Golfo Stella, south-west of the Gemini islets, with boulders, a Parazoanthus-lined canyon and a wall dropping to about 40 m.

## Overview

I Corbelli (also called Scoglio dei Corbelli) are small rocks emerging off Golfo Stella, south-west of the Gemini islets. Elba CED describes anchoring north of the largest rock, sheltered from south-easterly winds, on a stony bottom at 10 m. The dive has a rock-fall of boulders to the north and rocky walls and crevices to the south; swimming anticlockwise with the rock on your left leads to a sandy bottom where, at about 35 m, Elba CED reports a corroded old mine (which it says dates from the Second World War). The dive then continues from hole to hole with conger eels, moray eels and octopus, with large sea breams and dentex in the blue, and ends by passing through a canyon formed by the rock walls at about 10 m, completely covered with Parazoanthus. Divers should not approach or touch any ordnance; this report comes from a single source.

Verona Sub Diving Club, which lists Mandel Diving at Morcone as its support centre, describes a boat dive with a boulder bottom down to 15-20 m sheltering groupers and eels, then a wall to the south-west down to 40 m with lobsters, yellow gorgonians, sponges and nudibranchs; dentex and barracuda are frequent in open water. It gives a depth of 10/40 m and light to medium current. Sub Now describes a beginner-friendly circumnavigation of the rocks (boulders in the first 20 m with groupers, morays, congers and corvina), a more demanding deeper southern zone of scattered rocks on sand, sponges and gorgonians on the main islet's walls, dentex and amberjack in open water, and at times a rather intense current; it lists 38 m and a medium difficulty. Infoelba describes a tunnel formed by two leaning boulders on the north side, with a Parazoanthus-covered roof, a descent to about 27 m along a wall with yellow gorgonians, and, continuing to 40-45 m, red gorgonian fans, lobsters and groupers; it warns that current is often present.

Aquanautic Elba's dive-site map lists "Corbelli" with a depth range of 0-18 m, demanding navigation and possible currents. This site is not the Corbella rock at Capo Stella, which Elba CED lists as a separate dive. Entry is by boat in all the descriptions read.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Elba, Europe (off Golfo Stella, south-west of the Gemini islets, Capoliveri)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Advanced
- **Maximum Depth**: 40 meters
