## Gemini Rock

Rock just south of the Gemini islets off Capo Calamita, known for a large passage, a narrow swim-through, a canyon and a small cave.

## Overview

Divers Guide describes Gemini Rock as lying south of the Gemini rock formation, known for its rocks and steep walls, with large congers, octopuses and barracudas. The dive starts on the east side of the rock, which is kept on your right; over the seagrass on your left, schools of barracuda are often seen. You dive through a large passage in front of you and then a narrow swim-through at the end to reach open water again, then return to the boat. Divers Guide lists a maximum depth of 20 m, visibility of 15-25 m, limited current, a bottom of sand and rocks, and warns of a main route for pleasure craft nearby, so a diving flag and buoy should be used on the ascent. Aquanautic Elba lists "Gemini Rock" with a canyon and small cave, 2-15 m, easy navigation and possible currents. The passages and cave are overhead environments and should only be entered by divers trained and equipped for them.

Elba CED describes La Focacciola, a small rock that emerges a few minutes from Innamorata beach, south of the Gemini islets, suitable for all divers, from 0 to 20 m. The boat moors in the channel between the shoal and the Gemini; a clockwise circuit passes a colourful Parazoanthus-covered wall, tongues of rock forming crevices, an obliquely cut end forming a long canyon, and a shallow cave that is easy to enter. Elba CED reports a sculpture in memory of Jacques Mayol at 15-18 m, and Divers Guide places a memorial stone for him on the east side of Gemini Rock. These overlaps suggest that Gemini Rock and La Focacciola may be the same dive, but no source states this.

Divers Guide classes it as a boat dive with free access. No protection rule specific to this site was found; the park's rules page states that the waters of Elba are not included in the perimeter of the Park.

## Site Information

- **Location**: Elba, Europe (rock south of the Gemini islets, off Capo Calamita, Capoliveri)
- **Entry Type**: Boat dive
- **Site Type**: Reef
- **Difficulty Level**: Beginner
- **Maximum Depth**: 20 meters
